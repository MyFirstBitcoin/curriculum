# Introduction to Bitcoin

Introduction to Bitcoin is a short, linear path from money basics to what Bitcoin is and how people use it.

It is designed to give learners a clear picture of:

- why money matters,
- what money is (and what can go wrong with it),
- what Bitcoin is at a high level,
- how use and next steps fit into a beginner’s journey.

Educators and self-directed learners can use it as a compact on-ramp before deeper programs (such as the [Bitcoin Diploma](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/curriculum/tree/main/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en)).

## 🎯 Educational objectives

By completing Introduction to Bitcoin, learners should be able to:

- explain why money is a shared idea that affects daily choices,
- describe money in simple terms and recognize common failure modes of fiat systems,
- summarize what Bitcoin is for and how it differs from “just another app,”
- outline how people typically get started with Bitcoin responsibly,
- identify sensible questions to ask next as they keep learning.

The focus is on clarity and curiosity, not on trading, price prediction, or speculation.

## 👥 Target audience

Introduction to Bitcoin is designed for:

- newcomers who want a short, structured overview,
- study groups, classrooms, and community sessions with limited time,
- anyone who wants a single narrative arc before diving into longer curricula.

No technical or financial background is required.
