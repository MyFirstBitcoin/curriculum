# Kutheni Imali Ibalulekile?

Uninzi lwabantu abaze bacinge ngemali.

Abacingi ngokuyifumana, okanye ukuyisebenzisa, koko ngokwenene bacinga ukuba yintoni na imali kwaye isebenza njani.

Oko kunokubonakala ngathi yinto engaqhelekanga ukuyithetha, kodwa kuyinyaniso.

**Sichitha ubomi bethu bonke sisebenzisa imali singaze siyibuze.**

UHenry Ford wakha wathi yinto entle ukuba uninzi lwabantu aluqondi inkqubo yeebhanki nemali, kuba ukuba babeya kuyazi, kungabakho uvukelo.

Nokuba uyavuma okanye awuvumelani noFord, lo mbuzo kufanelekile ukuwucamngca: yintoni esingayaziyo uninzi lwethu ngemali esiyisebenzisa yonke imihla?

Esi sifundo sakhiwe malunga nalo mbuzo.

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, uya kuba nokuqonda okucacileyo malunga nokuba yintoni na imali ngokwenene, kutheni inkqubo yangoku inengxaki ezinzulu, yintoni iBitcoin, kwaye ungayiqala njani ukuyisebenzisa.
