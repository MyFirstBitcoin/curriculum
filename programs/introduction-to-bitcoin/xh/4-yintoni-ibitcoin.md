# Yintoni iBitcoin?

I-Bitcoin yimali yedijithali engaphakathi kwelawulo, inemida, kwaye isebenza ngokupheleleyo ngaphandle kolawulo lukarhulumente neebhanki. Zonke ezo mpawu zintathu zibalulekile.

Ukuba **ngaphandle kolawulo oluphakathi** kuthetha ukuba akukho gunya liphakathi, akukho rhulumente, akukho shishini likhulu, akukho ndawo enye yolawulo. Umnatha ugcinwa ngabasebenzisi bawo, kwaye nabani na naphi na angathatha inxaxheba. Xa kuthelekiswa nenkqubo yemali yangoku, apho ukuprinta imali okanye ukuvula ibhanki kunzima kakhulu kubantu abaqhelekileyo.

Ukuba **inomda** kuthetha ukuba akusoze kubekho ngaphezu kwe-21 yezigidi ze-bitcoin ezikhoyo. Elo nani lifakwe ngqo kwinkqubo kwaye alinakutshintshwa ngaphandle kwemvume yomnatha wehlabathi uphela. Njengoko imali ye-fiat isanda ingenamda, i-bitcoin nganye iba nexabiso elingakumbi xa ithelekiswa. Yile ngxaki yokunqongophala imali ye-fiat engenayo.

Ukuba **izimeleyo** kuthetha ukuba akukho mntu unokuthintela iintengiselwano zakho, abambe imali yakho, okanye atshintshe imithetho. I-Bitcoin ayinakuthintelwa ngendlela engazukwenzeka kwiakhawunti yebhanki.

Olunye uluvo olungachanekanga kukuba i-Bitcoin ayifikeleleki kubantu abaninzi ngenxa yexabiso layo. Kodwa akufuneki uthenge i-bitcoin epheleleyo. Njengoko i-euro yahlulwe ibe zii-senti ezili-100, i-bitcoin yahlulwe ibe zii-satoshi ezizigidi ezili-100. Unganako ukuba ne-bitcoin enexabiso elibalulekileyo ngee-euro ezimbalwa kuphela.

Umnatha usebenza ngabathathi-nxaxheba abathathu: abasebenzisi abathumela nabamkela i-bitcoin, ii-node eziqinisekisa iintengiselwano kwaye ziqinisekise imithetho, kunye nabavukuzi abongeza iibhloko ezintsha kwirekhodi. Elo rekhodi, i-blockchain, likawonkewonke, lihlala ngonaphakade, kwaye liyabonakala kuye nabani na. Akukho magosa aphakathi, akukho bavali-mnyango, kukhanya ngokupheleleyo.
