# Yintoni imali?

**Imali ayinaxabiso elingaphakathi.**

Oko kunokubonakala kungaqhelekanga, kodwa cinga ngako, akukho mntu uyifunayo imali ngenxa yayo ngokwayo. Siyifuna ngenxa yezinto esinokuzithenga ngayo. Ibhili ye-R200 yiphepha nje kuphela. Okwenza ibe luncedo kukuba abanye abantu bayayamkela xa betshintshiselana ngezinto esizifunayo ngokwenene.

Le nto yahlula imali ekutshintshiselaneni ngezinto.

Kwinkqubo yokutshintshiselana ngezinto, urhwebo lusebenza kuphela xa bobabini abantu befuna into enayo omnye. Ukuba ukhulisela i-apile mna ndikhulisela iibhanana, sinokutshintshiselana, kodwa kuphela xa ufuna iibhanana ngokwenene. Imali isombulula oku ngokunika wonke umntu isixhobo esiqhelekileyo abanokulisebenzisa nabani na, nangaliphi na ixesha.

**Into ukuze isebenze kakuhle njengemali, kufuneka yenze izinto ezintathu.**

Kufuneka yamkelwe ngokubanzi njenge **sixhobo sokutshintshiselana.** Kufuneka isebenze njenge **iyunithi yokubala**, isinika indlela esabelana ngayo yokulinganisa nokuthelekisa ixabiso lezinto ezahlukeneyo. Kwaye kufuneka **igcine ixabiso**, oko kuthetha ukuba kufuneka igcine amandla okuthenga ngokuhamba kwexesha.

Kwiimbali, izinto ezininzi ziye zasetyenziswa njengemali: amagobolondo, ityuwa, amaso, izinyithi ezixabisekileyo. Igolide nesilivere ekugqibeleni zaba ngaphezulu kuba zazinqabile, zihlala ixesha elide, kwaye zamkelwa kwihlabathi lonke. Kodwa ukuthwala igolide akusebenzi lula, ngoko iibhanki zaqala ukukhupha amanqaku ephepha njengemvume, ngalinye limele igolide egcinwe kwindawo ekhuselekileyo. Eli xesha laziwa njengegold standard.

Kuhamba kwexesha, oorhulumente baqala ukuprinta amaphepha emali angaphezulu kunegolide ababenayo yokuwaxhasa. Emva koko ngo-1971, i-US yaphelisa ngokusesikweni ukuguqulwa kweedola zayo zibe yigolide, isiganeko esaziwa njengeNixon Shock. Ukusukela ngelo xesha, imali yaba yinto abaziingcali kwezoqoqosho abayibiza ngokuba yimali ye-fiat. Igama elithi fiat lithetha "ngomthetho." Imali yethu ayixhaswanga sisixhobo esibonakalayo kwakhona. Ikhona kuphela kuba urhulumente uthi ikhona.

**Sasuka kwimali exhaswa yinto ethile, saya kwimali exhaswa ngumntu.**
