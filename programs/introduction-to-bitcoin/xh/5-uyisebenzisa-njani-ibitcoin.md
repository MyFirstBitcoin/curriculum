# Uyisebenzisa njani iBitcoin?

I-wallet yeBitcoin yindlela nje yesicelo esikuvumela ukuba uthumele, wamkele, kwaye ulawule i-bitcoin yakho. Eyona nto ibalulekileyo ekufuneka uyazi phambi kokukhetha enye kukuba ngubani ogcina imali yakho.

###### Ngubani ngokwenene ogcina i-bitcoin yakho?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/0b3a21664386a27441afee165ab5b3078d5627ed-1377x768.svg)

Nge **self-custodial wallet**, ulawula ngokupheleleyo.

Xa uyimisa, ivelisa i-**seed phrase:** uluhlu lwamagama ali-12 okanye ali-24 aqhelekileyo alandelelana ngendlela ethile. La magama asisigcino sakho, apho izitshixo zabucala ze-wallet yakho ziveliswa khona. Nabani na onawo angabuyisela i-wallet yakho aze alawule imali yakho.



Yibhale phantsi ephepheni uze uyigcine kwindawo ekhuselekileyo, ngaphandle kwe-intanethi. Ungaze uyithathe ifoto, uyithumele nge-imeyile, okanye uyigcine kwi-digital. Khumbula: nabani na onala magama unokufikelela kwi-bitcoin yakho, kwaye ukuba uwalahlekelwa ngaphandle kwesisigcino, i-bitcoin yakho ilahlekile ngonaphakade.

Nge **custodial wallet**, inkampani igcina i-bitcoin yakho egameni lakho, njengasebhankini.

Ukumisa kulula kwaye awudingi ukulawula i-seed phrase, nto leyo eyenza ezi wallets zilungele imali encinci kunye nokusebenzisa imihla ngemihla. Ingxaki kukuba uthembele kule nkampani ngemali yakho, kwaye ukuba bayayivala i-akhawunti, bahlaselwa, okanye bayavalwa, unokungakwazi ukufikelela kwiimali zakho.


---


Isivakalisi esiluncedo kwihlabathi leBitcoin: **akukho zitshixo zakho, akukho mali yakho.**

Nazi ezinye iindlela ze-wallets onokuzizama ngokwakho. Njengesiqhelo, phanda ngokwakho kwaye uqonde into oyenzayo phambi kokufaka imali ongazimisele ukuyilahlekelwa.

Ukuqala, nayiphi na kwezi self-custodial wallets zikhankanyiweyo ziindlela ezaziwayo ezikukhokelela kwinkqubo yokudala i-seed phrase.

Kwintlawulo encinci yemihla ngemihla, iBlink kunye neWallet of Satoshi zi-wallets zeLightning ze-custody. Zikhawuleza, zixabisa kancinci, kwaye zilungele abaqalayo.

I-**Lightning Network** linqanaba elakhiwe ngaphezulu kwe-blockchain ye-Bitcoin eyenzelwe ukuthengiselana okukhawulezayo, okungabizi kakhulu, kufanelekile ukusetyenziswa kwemihla ngemihla.
