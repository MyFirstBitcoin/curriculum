# Yintoni elandelayo?

###### Imali ye-fiat yonakele ngokuyilwa kwayo

Inkqubo yakhiwe ngendlela ethe chu etshintshela amandla okuthenga kude kubagcini-mali abaqhelekileyo iye kwabo balawula unikezelo lwemali. Oku akusiyongqokelela-mfihlo, kuyabonakala kwidatha kwaye kuqinisekisiwe yimbali.

###### I-Bitcoin yindlela yokwenene enye

Ukusukela ngo-2009, bekukho uhlobo lwemali olunqabileyo, olungathathi hlangothi, kwaye luvulelekile kuye nabani na, olunika kwakhona imisebenzi ephambili yemali ekufuneka iyenzile ngaphandle kokufuna ukuthembela nakwaziphi na iziko okanye igunya.

###### Ukuqala kulula kunokuba ucinga

Khuphela i-wallet, funda indlela yokuyisebenzisa, uze uqale kancinci. Akufuneki uqonde yonke into phambi kokuthatha inyathelo lokuqala. Ukuqonda kuza ngokwenza.

###### Oku kusekuqaleni nje

I-[Bitcoin Diploma](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/bitcoin-diploma-course/) liyinyathelo elilandelayo, elakhelwe phezu koko ukufundileyo apha. Hlala unomdla, uqhubeke ubuza imibuzo, kwaye wamkelekile emngxunyeni wempuku. Funda ngakumbi kwi-[www.myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org)
