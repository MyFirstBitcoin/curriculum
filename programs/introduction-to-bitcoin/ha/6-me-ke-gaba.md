# Me ke gaba?

###### Kudin fiat ya lalace tun daga tushe

An gina tsarin ne ta yadda zai rika kwasar ikon sayayya daga hannun masu ajiya na yau da kullum zuwa ga wadanda ke da iko da samar da kudi. Wannan ba wata makirci ba ce, ana iya gani a cikin bayanai kuma tarihi ya tabbatar da hakan.

###### Bitcoin wata sahihiyar madogara ce

Tun daga shekarar 2009, akwai wani nau'in kudi da yake da kankanta, adalci, kuma kowa na iya amfani da shi, wanda ke dawo da muhimman ayyukan da kudi ya kamata ya rika yi ba tare da bukatar dogaro da kowace hukuma ko wani iko ba.

###### Fara amfani da shi ya fi sauki fiye da yadda ake zato

Zazzage walat, koyi yadda ake amfani da shi, kuma fara da kadan. Ba sai ka fahimci komai ba kafin ka dauki mataki na farko. Fahimta tana zuwa ne da yin aiki.

###### Wannan shi ne farkon tafiya kawai

The [Bitcoin Diploma](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/bitcoin-diploma-course/) shi ne mataki na gaba, yana gina bisa abin da ka koya a nan. Ka ci gaba da sha'awa, ka rika tambaya, kuma barka da zuwa cikin zurfin bincike. Kara karantawa a [www.myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org)
