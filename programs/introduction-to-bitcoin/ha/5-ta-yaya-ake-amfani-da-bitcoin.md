# Ta yaya ake amfani da Bitcoin?

Wallet ɗin Bitcoin kawai wata manhaja ce da ke ba ka damar aika, karɓa, da sarrafa bitcoin ɗinka. Abu mafi muhimmanci da ya kamata ka fahimta kafin zaɓar ɗaya shi ne batun riƙon amana.

###### Wa ne ainihin ke riƙe da bitcoin ɗinka?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/0b3a21664386a27441afee165ab5b3078d5627ed-1377x768.svg)

Tare da **wallet ɗin kai tsaye (self-custodial wallet)**, kai ne ke da cikakken iko.

Lokacin da ka saita shi, yana samar da **jimlar kalmomi (seed phrase):** jerin kalmomi 12 ko 24 na yau da kullum a cikin tsari na musamman. Wannan jimlar ita ce ajiyar ka, daga gare ta ne wallet ɗinka ke samo mabuɗan sirri. Duk wanda ya mallaki wannan jimlar zai iya dawo da wallet ɗinka kuma ya sarrafa kuɗinka.



Rubuta ta a takarda ka adana ta a wuri mai aminci, gaba ɗaya a layi (offline). Kada ka ɗauki hotonta, aika ta ta imel, ko adana ta a na’ura. Ka tuna: duk wanda ke da waɗannan kalmomi yana da damar shiga bitcoin ɗinka, kuma idan ka rasa su ba tare da ajiyar baya ba, bitcoin ɗinka ya ɓace har abada.

Tare da **wallet ɗin riƙon amana (custodial wallet)**, kamfani ne ke riƙe da bitcoin ɗinka a madadinka, kamar yadda banki ke yi.

Saita wallet ɗin yana da sauƙi kuma ba sai ka kula da jimlar kalmomi ba, wanda hakan ke sa waɗannan wallet ɗin su dace da ƙananan kuɗi da kashe kuɗi na yau da kullum. Amma fa, kana dogaro da wannan kamfani da kuɗinka, kuma idan sun toshe asusu, an yi musu kutse, ko sun rufe, za ka iya rasa damar shiga.


---


Wani muhimmin karin magana a duniyar Bitcoin: **ba kai ke da mabuɗi ba, ba kai ke da kuɗi ba.**

Ga wasu zaɓuɓɓuka na wallet da za ka iya gwadawa da kanka. Kamar koyaushe, ka bincika da kanka kuma ka fahimci abin da kake yi kafin ka ajiye kuɗin da ba ka shirya rasa ba.

##### Wallet ɗin riƙon amana (Custodial wallets)


| Suna | Bayani | Tushe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blink | Wallet ɗin Bitcoin na matakin farko don amfani na yau da kullum tare da biyan kuɗi na Lightning na riƙon amana nan take, da kuma taswirar 'yan kasuwa a ciki don nemo shagunan da ke karɓar Bitcoin a yankinka. | blink.sv |
| Wallet of Satoshi | Ɗaya daga cikin wallet ɗin Lightning mafi sauƙi a duniya, tare da saiti na riƙon amana nan take, sauƙaƙƙen fuskar Scan-to-Pay, da adireshin Lightning na kanka don sauƙin mu'amala na yau da kullum. | walletofsatoshi.com |


Don farawa, kowanne daga cikin wallet ɗin kai tsaye da aka ambata zaɓi ne mai kyau da zai ja hankalinka cikin tsarin ƙirƙirar jimlar kalmomi.

Don ƙananan biyan kuɗi na yau da kullum, Blink da Wallet of Satoshi wallet ɗin Lightning ne na riƙon amana. Sauri ne, arha, kuma masu sauƙin amfani ga masu farawa.

The **Lightning Network** wata mataki ce da aka gina a saman sarkar Bitcoin domin yin mu'amaloli cikin sauri da araha, wanda ya dace da amfani na yau da kullum.
