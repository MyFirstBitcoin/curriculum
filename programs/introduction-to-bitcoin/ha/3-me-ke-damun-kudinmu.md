# Me ke damun kudinmu?

Idan mutane suka ji kalmar hauhawar farashi, yawanci suna tunanin yana nufin farashin kaya yana tashi.

Hakan gaskiya ne, amma wannan alama ce kawai.

Asalin dalilin kuwa abu ne mafi zurfi: yanzu ana iya ƙirƙirar kuɗi ba tare da iyaka ba, kuma ƙananan mutane ne kawai ke da ikon yin hakan.

Ka yi tunani game da abin da ke sa wani abu ya zama mai daraja.

Asalin Mona Lisa ya fi kowanne kwafinsa daraja, ba don yana da kyau fiye da sauran ba, amma saboda shi kaɗai ne. Rashin yawa yana ƙirƙirar daraja. Wannan tunanin yana aiki da kuɗi ma.

**Idan adadin wani abu ya ƙaru, kowanne daga cikinsu yana rasa daraja.**

Dalar Amurka ta rasa fiye da kashi 96% na ƙarfin sayenta tun daga shekarar 1913.

Cheeseburger da ake sayarwa kan kobo 19 a shekarar 1940, yanzu yana kusan fiye da $2.50. Ba don cheeseburger ya fi kyau ba ne, sai dai saboda kowace dala yanzu ta fi ƙarancin daraja fiye da da.

Ajiya naka, albashinka, da ƙarfin sayenka duk suna raguwa a hankali tsawon lokaci, ba don wani abu da ka yi ba, amma saboda yadda tsarin yake.

**Wannan shi ne matsalar da Bitcoin aka gina domin magancewa.**
