# Zovuta Zomwe Zili Ndi Ndalama Zathu

Anthu akamva mawu oti kukwera kwa mitengo (inflation), nthawi zambiri amaganiza kuti zikutanthauza kuti mitengo ikukwera.

Ndizoona, koma ndi chizindikiro chokha.

Chifukwa chenicheni ndi chachikulu kwambiri: ndalama tsopano zitha kulengedwa mopanda malire, ndipo anthu ochepa kwambiri ndi amene amalamulira ndondomekoyi.

Ganizirani za chomwe chimapangitsa chinthu kukhala chamtengo wapatali.

Mona Lisa yoyambirira ili ndi mtengo woposa makope ake onse, osati chifukwa imawoneka bwino, koma chifukwa ndi imodzi yokha. Kuchuluka kwa zinthu kumapangitsa mtengo. Zimenezi ndizofanana ndi ndalama.

**Chinthu chikakhala chambiri, mtengo wa chilichonse umachepa.**

Dola la ku US lataya mphamvu yogulira zinthu oposa 96% kuyambira 1913.

Cheeseburger yomwe inali ndi mtengo wa 19 tambala mu 1940 tsopano imafika pa K2,500. Izi sizichitika chifukwa cheeseburger yasintha, koma chifukwa ndalama iliyonse ili ndi mphamvu yochepa kuposa kale.

Ndalama zanu zosungira, malipiro anu, ndi mphamvu yanu yogulira zinthu zonse zikungochepa pang'onopang'ono, osati chifukwa cha zomwe mwachita, koma chifukwa cha mmene dongosololi limapangidwira.

**Ichi ndi vuto limene Bitcoin inamangidwa kuti ithetse.**
