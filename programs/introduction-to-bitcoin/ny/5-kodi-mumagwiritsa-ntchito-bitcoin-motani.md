# Kodi Mumagwiritsa Ntchito Bitcoin Motani?

Chikwama cha Bitcoin ndi pulogalamu yomwe imakupatsani mwayi wotumiza, kulandira, ndi kusamalira bitcoin yanu. Chofunika kwambiri chomwe muyenera kumvetsa musanasankhe ndi za umwini wa ndalama.

###### Ndani ali ndi bitcoin yanu kwenikweni?

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/0b3a21664386a27441afee165ab5b3078d5627ed-1377x768.svg)

Ndi **chikwama chaumwini**, inu muli ndi ulamuliro wonse.

Mukamakhazikitsa, chimapanga **mawu a chinsinsi:** ndondomeko ya mawu 12 kapena 24 wamba omwe ayenera kulembedwa motsatira dongosolo. Mawu amenewa ndi chinsinsi chanu chobwezeretsera, kuchokera pomwe makiyi achinsinsi a chikwama chanu amachokera. Aliyense amene ali nawo akhoza kubwezeretsa chikwama chanu ndi kuwongolera ndalama zanu.



Lembani pansi pa pepala ndikusunga pamalo otetezeka, osalumikizidwa ndi intaneti. Musajambule chithunzi chake, musatumize pa imelo, kapena kusunga pa digito. Kumbukirani: aliyense amene ali ndi mawu amenewa ali ndi mwayi pa bitcoin yanu, ndipo ngati mutawataya popanda kubwezeretsa, bitcoin yanu imatha kosatha.

Ndi **chikwama cha kampani**, kampani imasunga bitcoin yanu m'malo mwanu, ngati banki.

Kukhazikitsa kwake ndi kosavuta ndipo simuyenera kusamalira mawu a chinsinsi, zomwe zimapangitsa kuti zikhale zabwino kugwiritsa ntchito ndalama zochepa komanso zolipira tsiku ndi tsiku. Koma, mumadalira kampaniyo ndi ndalama zanu, ndipo ngati atatseka maakaunti, kuba, kapena kutseka, mungataye mwayi wanu.

Mawu ofunikira m'dziko la Bitcoin: **osakhala ndi makiyi, osakhala ndi ndalama.**

Nawa ena mwa ma chikwama omwe mungayesere nokha. Monga nthawi zonse, funsani zambiri nokha ndikumvetsetsa zomwe mukuchita musanapereke ndalama zomwe simukufuna kutaya.

Poyambira, chikwama chilichonse chaumwini chomwe tatchulachi ndi chisankho chabwino chomwe chimakutsogolerani pa njira yopanga mawu a chinsinsi.

Kwa malipiro ang'onoang'ono a tsiku ndi tsiku, Blink ndi Wallet of Satoshi ndi ma chikwama a Lightning a kampani. Ndi achangu, otsika mtengo, komanso osavuta kwa oyamba.

The **Lightning Network** ndi gawo lomwe limamangidwa pamwamba pa blockchain ya Bitcoin lomwe lapangidwa kuti lizichita malonda mwachangu komanso mwotsika mtengo, loyenera kugwiritsidwa ntchito tsiku ndi tsiku.
