# Chotsatira ndi chiyani?

###### Ndalama za fiat zawonongeka mwadala

Dongosololi limamangidwa m'njira yomwe pang'onopang'ono limachotsa mphamvu yogulira kwa osunga ndalama wamba ndikupereka kwa amene amalamulira ndalama. Izi si chiwembu, zikuonekera mu deta ndipo mbiri imatsimikizira.

###### Bitcoin ndi njira yeniyeni ina

Kuyambira mu 2009, pakhala mtundu wa ndalama womwe ndi wosowa, wopanda tsankho, komanso wotseguka kwa aliyense, womwe ukubwezeretsa ntchito zazikulu zomwe ndalama ziyenera kuchita popanda kufunikira kokhulupirira bungwe kapena ulamuliro uliwonse.

###### Kuyamba ndi kosavuta kuposa momwe zimawonekera

Tsitsani wallet, phunzirani momwe mungagwiritsire ntchito, ndipo yambani ndi zochepa. Simuyenera kumvetsa zonse musanayambe. Kumvetsa kumabwera ndi kuchita.

###### Ichi ndi chiyambi chabe

The [Bitcoin Diploma](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/bitcoin-diploma-course/) ndi sitepe yotsatira, yomanga pa zomwe mwaphunzira pano. Pitirizani kukhala ndi chidwi, pitirizani kufunsa mafunso, ndipo takulandirani mu chitsime cha mbuzi. Dziwani zambiri pa [www.myfirstbitcoin.org](https://myfirstbitcoin.org)
