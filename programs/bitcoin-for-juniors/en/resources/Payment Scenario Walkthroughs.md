# Payment Scenario Walkthroughs

Step-by-step guides for common Bitcoin and Lightning payment scenarios.


---

## **Scenario 1: Send Bitcoin to a Friend (On-Chain)**

**Situation:** You want to send Bitcoin to your friend's wallet address.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **Open your wallet** (Blue Wallet, Muun, etc.)
2. **Tap "Send"**
3. **Enter amount** (how much Bitcoin)
4. **Paste recipient's address** (copy-paste, don't type manually)
5. **Review fee** (if network is congested, fee will be higher)
6. **Confirm and send**
7. **Wait for confirmations** (\~10 min for 1st, \~60 min for 6)
8. **Check blockchain explorer** to verify transaction

**Time:** \~10 minutes to first confirmation\n**Cost:** $2-10 depending on network congestion


---

## **Scenario 2: Send Bitcoin via Lightning (Daily Spending)**

**Situation:** You're paying for coffee using Lightning Network.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **Open Lightning wallet** (Muun, Breez, or Blue Wallet with Lightning enabled)
2. **Go to merchant's payment page** (QR code displayed at register)
3. **Scan QR code** with your wallet
4. **Invoice appears** (amount in satoshis)
5. **Confirm payment**
6. **Transaction settles instantly** (< 1 second)
7. **Receipt generated**

**Time:** < 1 second\n**Cost:** < $0.01


---

## **Scenario 3: Receive Bitcoin Directly (Self-Employed)**

**Situation:** You're a freelancer and want to accept Bitcoin payment from client.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **Open your wallet**
2. **Tap "Receive"**
3. **Display receiving address** (or QR code)
4. **Share with client** (address or QR code)
5. **Client sends Bitcoin** to your address
6. **After 1-6 confirmations**, Bitcoin appears in your wallet
7. **Check blockchain explorer** to verify (optional but good practice)

**Time to receive:** \~10 minutes (1st confirmation)\n**Cost:** Free to receive (no fee for you)


---

## **Scenario 4: Receive Remittance from Family Abroad**

**Situation:** Your family in another country is sending you money.

### **Traditional Bank Wire**

* Time: 2-3 days
* Cost: 8-10% ($80-100 if receiving $1000)
* Process: Bank → Intermediary → Your bank → Your account

### **Bitcoin Solution**

**Step-by-Step**


1. **Your family downloads wallet** (Blue Wallet, Muun)
2. **You share your receiving address** (via WhatsApp, email, etc.)
3. **Family goes to Bitcoin exchange** (LocalBitcoins, Kraken)
4. **Family buys Bitcoin** with local currency ($1000 → \~0.03 BTC)
5. **Family sends Bitcoin** to your address
6. **You receive in \~10 minutes** (1st confirmation)
7. **You convert to local currency** (optional; or hold Bitcoin)
8. **Withdraw to bank account** if needed

**Time:** \~30 minutes (including exchange account creation)\n**Cost:** < $1 (exchange fee + network fee)

**Savings:** $80-100 vs. traditional wire!


---

## **Scenario 5: Earn Bitcoin (Freelancer Getting Paid)**

**Situation:** You're getting paid for work in Bitcoin.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **Send invoice** with your Bitcoin address (or create invoice using BTCPay)
2. **Client sends Bitcoin** to your address
3. **Bitcoin arrives** in \~10 minutes
4. **You check address** on blockchain explorer to confirm
5. **You hold Bitcoin** (store of value) OR
6. **You convert to fiat** (through exchange) if needed for bills

**Advantages:**

* No middleman; no chargebacks
* Censorship-resistant (no bank can freeze account)
* Cross-border friendly (same payment system globally)


---

## **Scenario 6: Request Payment (Using QR Code)**

**Situation:** You want to get paid and want to make it easy.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **Open wallet**
2. **Tap "Request"**
3. **Enter amount** you want to receive
4. **Generate QR code** (displayed on screen)
5. **Customer scans QR code** with their wallet
6. **Customer confirms payment**
7. **Bitcoin sent** to your address
8. **You're notified** (wallet shows incoming transaction)

**Advantage:** Customer doesn't have to copy-paste your address (reduces errors)


---

## **Scenario 7: Send Bitcoin Cross-Border**

**Situation:** You're sending $1000 to a business partner in another country.

### **Step-by-Step**


1. **No permission needed** (Bitcoin doesn't ask who you send to)
2. **No bank approval** required
3. **Get recipient's address** (email, message, etc.)
4. **Open your wallet**
5. **Send Bitcoin** (\~0.03 BTC for $1000)
6. **Bitcoin arrives** in \~10 minutes
7. **Recipient receives** (same global system)
8. **Recipient converts** to local currency (or holds)

**Advantages:**

* No borders recognized by Bitcoin
* No government can freeze transaction mid-way
* No 3-5 day delays
* Lower fees than international wire


---

## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

* ❌ **Sending to wrong address** (irreversible; use copy-paste)
* ❌ **Not waiting for confirmations** (assume 0 confirmations = unconfirmed)
* ❌ **Setting fee too low** (transaction will be stuck; takes days)
* ❌ **Sharing private key or seed phrase** (funds will be stolen)
* ❌ **Trusting "customer support"** via email (it's a scam)
* ❌ **Not backing up seed phrase** (losing device = losing Bitcoin)
