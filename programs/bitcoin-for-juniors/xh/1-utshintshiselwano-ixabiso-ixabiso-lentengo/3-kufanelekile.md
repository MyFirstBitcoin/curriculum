# 1.3 Kufanelekile

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukuhlelwa • Ukuhlukaniswa ngokubonwayo • Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 45 imizuzu

**Ukuhlulwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke, ngamnye efumana ithuba lakhe

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga intsingiselo yexabiso ngokusika izinto zemihla ngemihla baze bazibeke kumgca omnye wokuxabisa ukusuka kwezisezantsi ukuya kwezibizayo. Ngokuthelekisa, ingxoxo, kunye nokukhokelwa ngutitshala, abafundi bafumana ukuqonda okucacileyo kokuba ixabiso lithetha imali ekufuneka ihlawulwe into ethile kwaye amaxabiso angahluka kakhulu phakathi kwezinto.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya kuba:

* Beqonda ukuba ixabiso lithetha ukuba into ithenga malini ngemali
* Beyakwazi ukubona umahluko phakathi kwezinto ezisezantsi nexabiso eliphezulu
* Beyakwazi ukuthelekisa amaxabiso kwiindidi ezahlukeneyo zezinto
* Beyakwazi ukuthatha inxaxheba kwincoko yeqela kunye nokuxhasa izimvo zabo

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iikhatala ezindala, iimagazini, okanye amaphepha eemveliso ashicilelweyo
* Iglue stick okanye iteyiphu
* Iimaki
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** Amanqaku ancangathi okanye izitikha zokuvota

##### **Izixhobo zombane**

* Iisikere ezikhuselekileyo kubantwana
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** Iprojektha, Izixhobo Zokubonisa (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

_10 imizuzu_

1. Ukhetho: Khetha kwangaphambili uze usike izinto zokubonisa umsebenzi
1. Qinisekisa ukuba yonke imixholo yekhatala ilungele ubudala babafundi.
1. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, zama ukufihla amaxabiso ezinto usebenzisa imaki.



##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Buza abafundi: “Ngubani owaziyo ukuba igama elithi _ixabiso_ lithetha ntoni?”
1. Bhala ngokucacileyo ebhodini:
  1. **Ixabiso = imali ekufuneka ihlawulwe into ethile**
1. Bonisa imizekelo emibini engafaniyo (umzekelo, iapile xa ithelekiswa nebhayisekile) uze ubuze ukuba yeyiphi ebiza kakhulu.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Funda ukuba izinto zibiza malini kwaye ukwazi umahluko phakathi kwezisezantsi nezibizayo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_25 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Yenza umgca omde othe tye eludongeni okanye ebhodini usebenzisa iteyiphu okanye ipeni yokucima. (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
1. Phawula:
  1. Ekhohlo: “Ixabiso Eliphantsi”
  1. Ekunene: “Ixabiso Eliphezulu”

**Umzekelo**

1. Ukuba awukayenzi, khetha uze usike into kwizinto ezikhoyo
1. Beka into enye ngokwakho kumgca othe tye uze uchaze indlela ocinga ngayo ngokuvakalayo:
  1. “Le into ndiyibeka apha kuba inexabiso eliphantsi – ihlala ixabisa imali encinci.”
1. Buza abafundi ukuba bayavuma okanye bayaphikisa.

**Qhuba**

1. Abafundi bathathana ngokulandelelana beza phambili bebekela izinto zabo kumgca wexabiso.
1. Imibuzo yomfundisi:
  1. “Ngaba eli xabiso liphantsi okanye liphezulu kunelokugqibela?” okanye “Ngaba ifuna imali eninzi ukuyithenga?”
  1. “Ngaba kufuneka ibe ngasekhohlo okanye ekunene?”
1. Khuthaza intshukumo kunye nokulungisa njengoko iimbono zitshintsha.

**Ingqwalasela**

* Hamba kunye nabafundi kumgca wexabiso.
* Buza:
  * “Ucinga ukuba le nto kufuneka ihambe?”
  * “Yeyiphi into ekumangalisileyo?”
* Vula okanye uqikelele amaxabiso okwenyani kwezinye izinto uze uthelekise neengcinga zabafundi.
* Phawula:
  * Izinto ezincinci zinokubiza kakhulu
  * Izinto ezinkulu ngamanye amaxesha zinokubiza kancinci

##### **LANDISELA**

_imizuzu emi-2_



Shwankathela:

* Ixabiso lisinceda xa sithelekisa izinto
* Amaxabiso ahluka-hluka kwizinto ezahlukeneyo
* Singaphikisana, kodwa imali isinceda sigqibe

##### **VALA**

1. Ncoma abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba kwabo.
1. Thatha umzuzu wokubulela ngezinto ezikwiimpilo zabo.
1. Cela abafundi babuyise zonke izinto nezixhobo.
1. Iprojekthi ingaboniswa okanye igcinwe kwaye abafundi banokongeza kuyo kamva.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Biza abafundi ngomnye ngomnye
* Khuthaza ukuphikisana ngentlonipho

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Yongeza iindidi zamaxabiso (Ngaphantsi kwe-R200, R200–R1000, Ngaphezulu kwe-R2000, Ngaphezulu kwe-R20,000)
* Phinda wenze umgca usebenzisa ukutya, amava, okanye iinkonzo
* Thelekisa amaxabiso aqikelelweyo namaxabiso okwenyani
* Umdlalo othi Phezulu okanye Phantsi
* Ngenisa umxholo wokunqongophala ngokubuza ukuba kutheni ezinye izinto zibiza kakhulu

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* **Abafundi abancinane:** izinto ezisikiweyo sele zilungile
* **ELL/Ukufikeleleka:** vumela ukubonisa ngeminwe endaweni yokuthetha
* **Ukhuseleko:**qinisekisa ukongamela ukusetyenziswa kwezikere
