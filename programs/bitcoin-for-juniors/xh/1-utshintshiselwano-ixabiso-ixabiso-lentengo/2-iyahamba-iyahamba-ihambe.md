# 1.2 Iyahamba, Iyahamba, Ihambe!

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukulinganisa indima • Ukuthengisa ngephenyane

**Ixesha** imizuzu engama-45

**Ukwahlulwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke

**Inkcazo**

Kulo msebenzi, abafundi bathatha inxaxheba kuthengiso lweklasi besebenzisa “amaqokobhe” (okanye iithokheni) njengemali elula. Ikilasi liqhuba ukuthengiswa kwezinto apho izinto zemihla ngemihla zithengiswa enye ngexesha kumthengi ophakamileyo. Njengoko amaxabiso evela ngexesha lokwenyani, abafundi babona indlela ukuswela (izinto ezilinganiselweyo nemali encinci) kunye nemfuno (bangaphi abafuna into enye) kuchaphazela ukuba into “ixabisa” malini. Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo idibanisa iziphumo zokuthengiswa kwabo neengcinga eziphambili zexabiso, utshintshiselwano, kunye nendlela amaxabiso enziwa ngayo, kugxininiswa ukuba amaxabiso awasimiyo kwaye abantu abohlukeneyo baxabisa into enye ngendlela eyahlukileyo.

**Iziphumo zokufunda**

* Abafundi bafumana amava okuswela kunye nendlela amaxabiso enziwa ngayo ngokuthatha inxaxheba kuthengiso oluphila.
* Bafunda ukuba izinto zifumana ixabiso ngokuxhomekeka kubantu abaninzi abafuna zona, kunye nokuba zilinganiselwe kangakanani.
* Abafundi bayaqonda ukuba:
  * Ukuswela kwenza izinto zixabiseke ngakumbi.
  * Amaxabiso awasimiyo; axhomekeke kwimfuno.
  * Abantu baxabisa izinto ezahlukeneyo ngendlela eyahlukileyo.

**Izixhobo**

* Izinto ezincinci ezili-10 zeklasi (ezonwabisayo okanye eziluncedo: izitikha, iipensile, izimuncumuncu, iirabha, iminqwazi ehlekisayo, iithoyi, njl.)
* Amaqokobhe (okanye iithokheni/ii-coin zephepha ukuba akukho maqokobhe) — ubuncinane ama-3–5 kumfundi ngamnye

**Izixhobo zokusebenza**

* Ipensile & nephepha lokubhala amanani okuthenga
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Ibhodi emhlophe kunye neepeni, intsimbi yokuthengisa okanye isandi/umqondiso

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

imizuzu emi-2

* Cwangcisa kwangaphambili ukuba umfundi ngamnye uza kufumana iithokheni zingaphi.
* Hlela izinto ezili-10 phezu kwetafile phambili (i “ndlu yokuthengisa”)

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

* Sebenzisa amaqokobhe ukuthenga kuthengiso kwaye ubone indlela amaxabiso atshintsha ngayo xa abantu abaninzi befuna into enye kwaye zimbalwa.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

imizuzu emi-5

1. **Phonononga** izinto eziphezu kwetafile kunye nabafundi. Xoxa amagama kunye nemisebenzi yezinto, ukuba kuyimfuneko.
1. **Bhengeza**: “La maqokobhe yimali yenu. Niza kuwasebenzisa ukuthenga izinto kumdlalo wanamhlanje.
1. **Sasaza** amaqokobhe kwaye wenze abafundi babale ukuba banawo mangaphi.

##### **UMSEBENZI**

imizuzu engama-30

**Ulungiselelo**

1. **Cacisa** ukuba izinto eziphezu kwetafile ziza kuthengiswa enye ngexesha.
1. Abafundi baza **thenga** besebenzisa amaqokobhe — lowo uthenga kakhulu ufumana into.

**Bonisa**

1. **Khetha umfundi omnye** njengomthengi. Phakamisa into, uyinike igama ngokucacileyo, uqalise ukuthenga ngo-1 qokobhe.
1. **Bonisa indlela yokuthenga**: cela umfundi aphakamise isandla sakhe, abize ixabiso lakhe ngokuvakalayo, aze abeke amaqokobhe apho wonke umntu angawabona khona. Phinda kube kanye ngokumgqitha (okanye usebenzise omnye umfundi) ukuze ubonise indlela amaxabiso anyuka ngayo.
1. **Vala intengiso**: yenza ukubala okucacileyo (“Okokuqala, okwesibini, kuthengisiwe”), khwaza/uqhwabe, chaza ixabiso lokugqibela, nikezela ngento, kwaye ubhale ixabiso apho wonke umntu angalibona khona.

**Intengiso Ebukhoma**

1. Utitshala uphakamisa into.
1. Qala ngo-1 iqokobhe, vumela abafundi banyuse amaxabiso.
1. Bhala ixabiso lokugqibela kwisitiki uze usincamathisele kwinto (ibonakale kubo bonke).
1. Phinda oku kuyo yonke into kwezi-10.

**Iindawo Zokujonga**

* Ngexesha lentengiso, khumbuza abafundi ukuba baqaphele amaqokobhe abasalayo.
* Cacisa ukuba xa sele bengenaso nasinye iqokobhe, abanakuphinda bathenge kwenye into.
* Abafundi kufuneka 'balawule' indlela abasebenzisa ngayo izinto zabo.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

1. Beka zonke izinto kunye namaxabiso azo okugqibela ebhodini/eludongeni.
1. Buza:
  1. Zeziphi izinto ebezibiza kakhulu? Kutheni?
  1. Zikhona izinto ebezingabizi? Kutheni?
  1. Ngaba wonke umntu ebefuna izinto ezifanayo?
  1. Ngaba kukho umntu ophelelwe ngamaqokobhe ngokukhawuleza?
1. Gxininisa: Ixabiso livela kwixabiso _kunye_ nokunqongophala.
1. Chaza la magama uze uwabhale ebhodini.

##### **VALA**

* Nika izinto kubafundi abaphumelele kwintengiso uze ubanqwenelele impumelelo

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Abanye abafundi banokufumanisa ukuba le ngcamango ayilunganga. Bangahle bagqithwe kwizinto abazifunayo. Qaphela ukuba oku kungaba ngamava angalunganga okufunda. Nikezela ithuba lokurhweba okanye ukutshintshiselana (jonga _Umahluko_ ngezantsi)

##### **Umahluko**

* Ukwahlula indlela yokwabiwa kwamaqokobhe kungatshintsha iziphumo zomsebenzi. Eyona ndlela ilula, nelungileyo kukuwabela ngokulinganayo. Kodwa, le ayisosimo sobomi bokwenyani. Ukwabiwa okungalinganiyo kungafaneleka kubafundi abaphambili ukuze kuxoxwe ngemfuduko kwezezimali kunye nokungalingani.
* Abafundi abancinci: Ukuba ukuthenga akulula kubafundi, kunokwenzeka ukujikeleza iqela nganye nganye.
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: Abantwana banokubonisa ixabiso labo ngokubeka amaqokobhe embindini wetafile, ngendlela ye-poker.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi Yongezelelekileyo**

* Ukuba akukho nto inikelwayo: endaweni yokunika izinto, abafundi babhale kwiziposti ukuba bacinga ukuba into nganye _kufuneka_ ibize malini. Thelekisa 'uqikelelo' lwabo neziphumo zokwenyani zentengiso.
* Oku kwenza umsebenzi ube mthethweni ngakumbi kwaye kuvumela ukuphinda-phinda ngaphandle kwesidingo samabhaso.
* Ukuba ixesha livumela, intengiso yokurhweba ingavulwa kubafundi abangonelisekanga yintengiso. Abafundi banokukhuthazwa ukuba batshintshe okanye basebenzise amaqokobhe abasalayo ukuze batshintshe phakathi kwabo. Oku kungalawulwa ngutitshala, okanye kushiywe kubafundi ukuba bazilawule.
