# 1.4 Yintoni ixabiso?

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ingxoxo • Iprojekthi yobugcisa yokusebenzisana

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 45 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke kunye negalelo lomntu ngamnye

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga ingcamango yexabiso ngokuchonga izinto zemihla ngemihla ebomini babo baze bazibeke kwitshathi ebonakalayo ekwabelwana ngayo. Ngengxoxo ekhokelwayo kunye nomsebenzi wobugcisa odityanisiweyo odityaniswe eludongeni, abafundi bathelekisa ixabiso lomntu kunye nexabiso lentengo baze bacinge ngendlela elinokutshintsha ngayo ixabiso ngokuxhomekeka kumbono womntu. Lo msebenzi wakha ukuqonda okusekwe kwisiseko sexabiso, ixabiso lentengo, kunye nokunqongophala ngokusebenzisa ingxoxo, intshukumo, kunye nokuqonda okubonakalayo.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni kwalo msebenzi, abafundi baya:

* Baqukumbela ukuba _ixabiso_ lisekelwe kwiimfuno zomntu kunye nezentlalo.
* Baqubisise ubudlelwane phakathi kwe _ixabiso lentengo_ kunye ne _ixabiso_.
* Baqonde ukuba ixabiso alisoloko lihlala linjalo kodwa litshintsha ngokuxhomekeka kumbono womntu.
* Baqhelise ukusebenzisana ngengxoxo ekwabelwana ngayo kunye nomsebenzi wobugcisa weqela.

**Izixhobo**

* Amanqaku ancangathi okanye amakhadi e-index
* Iimaki, iipeni okanye iipensile
* Iteyiphu okanye iglue encangathi
* Iteyiphu engomeleziyo (iteyiphu yokugquma, washi tape, itephu yabapeyinta njl.)
* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako:** Ukhetho lwezinto ezivela ekhaya

**Izixhobo**

* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako**: Ibhodi emhlophe & iimaki

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

_10 imizuzu_

1. Fumana indawo ekufanelekileyo kwigumbi lokufundela yokubonisa umsebenzi wobugcisa omkhulu. Kufuneka ibe ngumgangatho ogudileyo, othe tye.
1. Coca udonga kuyo nayiphi na into kwaye uhlambe udonga ukuze amanqaku ancangathi akwazi ukunamathela eludongeni.
1. Beka naziphi na izinto ezivela ekhaya phezu kwetafile phambili kwigumbi phambi kokuba abafundi bangene.

**Qaphela:** Ukusebenzisa ibhodi emhlophe phambili kwigumbi lokufundela kunokufaneleka kulo msebenzi. Nangona kunjalo, ukuze kufezekiswe impembelelo enkulu, kungcono ukwenza lo msebenzi kwenye indawo kwigumbi ukuze iprojekthi yobugcisa ikwazi ukuboniswa ixesha elide.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Vumela abafundi bachonge izinto ozibeke phambi kwabo.
1. Xoxa ngexabiso lazo lentengo kunye nexabiso lazo.
1. Yazisa ngezimvo ezimbini:
  1. **Ixabiso lentengo**: ukuba into ixabisa malini
  1. **Ixabiso**: ukuba into iluncedo okanye ibaluleke kangakanani
1. Cela abafundi banike uluvo lwabo malunga nento kwaye ukuba bayavuma okanye bayaphikisa.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

* “Fumanisa ukuba yintoni egqiba ixabiso lezinto.”
* “Funda ukuba yintoni enexabiso kuthi kwaye kutheni kungavisisani wonke umntu.”
* “Thetha ngelokuba kutheni izinto ezibizayo zingasoloko zibalulekile, kwaye izinto ezixabisa kancinci zisengaba nentsingiselo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-30-40_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Cela abafundi ukuba badwelise izinto ezininzi abanokuzicinga ezikwiigumbi zabo zokulala okanye ezindlwini zabo (iithoyi, iimpahla, izixhobo, iincwadi, njl.).
1.  Umfundi ngamnye ubhala into enye kwisitiki esinye.
  1. Imibuzo yokukhuthaza:
      1. “Zeziphi izinto ezibalulekileyo kuwe?”
    1. “Zeziphi izinto ezingabalulekanga?”
1. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, umfundi ngamnye angaba nombala wakhe wesitiki ukuze kube lula ukwahlula izinto kamva emsebenzini.
1. Ncamathisela umgca omde othe tye ebhodini (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_).Beka igama kule axis:
  1. Ekhohlo = “Ixabiso eliphantsi”
  1. Ekunene = “Ixabiso eliphezulu”
1. Emva koko yongeza umgca othe nkqo ophakathi. Beka igama kule axis:
  1. Ezantsi: “Ixabiso eliphantsi”
  1. Phezulu: “Ixabiso eliphezulu”

**Bonisa**

1. Thatha enye yezinto ezisuka ekhaya ubhale igama lento kwisitiki.
1. Bonisa apho kufuneka ibekwe khona phakathi kwezikwere ezine.

**Qhuba**

1. Abafundi babeka izitiki zabo kumgca apho becinga ukuba into yabo ifanele khona.
1. Xhasa abafundi ngokubakhuthaza ukuba bongeze izinto ezininzi kangangoko. Ukuba abafundi bagqibile izitiki zabo, babakhuthaze ukuba bacinge ngezinye izinto ezintsha.

**Ingqwalasela**

* Imibuzo yokukhokela:
  * “Le into ixabisa kancinci okanye ibiza kakhulu?”
  * “Ngaba abantu abaninzi bangavumelana ngexabiso layo?”
  * “Isebenza kangakanani le nto?”
  * “Ngaba wonke umntu angayixabisa ngendlela efanayo?”
* Khuthaza ingxoxo:
  * “Zeziphi izinto ezikumangalisileyo?”
  * “Kutheni ezinye izinto ezixabisa kancinci zibalulekile?”
  * “Kutheni ezinye izinto ezibizayo zinganzwanga zibalulekile?”
  * Ukutshintsha kweembono ngokuzikhethela:
* Ukutshintsha kweembono ngokuzikhethela:
  * Yenza abafundi bajonge izinto zomnye umfundi baze baxoxe ngomehluko.
* Eyona nto ibalulekileyo:
  * Ixabiso yinqaku
  * Ixabiso kukugweba

##### **LANDISELA**

* Phonononga:
  * Ixabiso kunye nexabiso lokubaluleka azifani
  * Abantu abavumelani ngexabiso
  * Amaxabiso anceda abantu barhwebe nangona bengavumelani
* Qhagamshela nemali:
  * Imali kunye namaxabiso kunceda abantu barhwebe xa ixabiso lingumntu ngamnye

**VALA**

1. Chonga naziphi na iindlela okanye imikhwa.
1. Bongela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba kwabo.
1. Thatha umzuzu wokubulela abantu, amaxesha kunye nezinto ebomini babo.
1. Cela abafundi babuyise zonke izinto kunye nezixhobo.
1. Iprojekthi ingaboniswa okanye igcinwe kwaye abafundi banokongeza kuyo kamva.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Khuthaza ukungavumelani ngentlonipho
* Qinisekisa ukuba akukho mpendulo ingalunganga
* Kungaba nomdla ngakumbi ukuncamathisela kwaye uphawule iiaxisi ngexesha lomsebenzi. Oku nako kungaphazamisa ingqwalasela kwaye kuphazamise ingqondo xa inkqubo inzima.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Bonisa kwaye Uxelele
* Yongeza umaleko wesithathu: “Ngubani ogqiba ixabiso?”
* Phinda wazise imidlalo yokuthengisa okanye yokurhweba
