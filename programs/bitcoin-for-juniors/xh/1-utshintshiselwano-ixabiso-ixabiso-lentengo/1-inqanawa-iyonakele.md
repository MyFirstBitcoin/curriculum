**Inkcazo**

Kulo mdlalo wokulinganisa weklasi yonke othathela imizuzu engama-60, abafundi bazakulingisa njengabantu abasindileyo abaphuma enqanaweni abamele ukufumana izixhobo ezibalulekileyo ukuze 'baphile' iveki kwisiqithi esingumqwebedu. Besebenzisa amakhadi okudlala ukumela izixhobo kunye nezinto zokusebenzisa, abafundi baqala ngokutshintshiselana izinto ngokuthe ngqo, beziqhelanisa nokuxoxisana ngexabiso ngelixa bejamelene nemiqobo eqhelekileyo yokurhweba efana nokugqithiseka kwezinto, ukunqongophala, kunye nokungafani kweminqweno. Umsebenzi obizwa ngokuba yi 'Ukukhangela Iqokobhe' obunexesha, uvelisa imali yezinto (commodity money), njengoko abafundi beqokelela iimpawu abanokuzisebenzisa ukwenza utshintshiselwano lube lula. Xa amaqokobhe esesebenzisweni, iklasi ingena kwimarike ekhokelwayo apho abafundi bemisa amaxabiso, bephendula kubonelelo kunye nemfuno, kwaye bafumane amava okufumanisa amaxabiso. Umsebenzi uphetha ngokwenza uhlahlo-lwabiwo mali kunye nomjikelo wokuthengisa okanye utshintshiselwano olucwangcisiweyo lwezinto ezikwi 'uluhlu lweminqweno yobutyebi', oku kudibanisa isizathu sokuba imali ikhona.

**Iziphumo Zokufunda**

* Abafundi bafumana amava okutshintshiselana izinto ngokuthe ngqo, 'ukungafani kweminqweno', ukunqongophala, unikezelo/imfuno, ukufumanisa amaxabiso, uhlahlo-lwabiwo mali, kunye nokuthengisa ngephenyane.
* Unxibelelwano nemali: imali yezinto (amaqokobhe) kunye nesizathu sokuba imali iluncedo kurhwebo.

**Izixhobo**

* Amakhadi okudlala
* 'Amaqokobhe' (iimpawu ezibonakalayo: amaphepha ajikelezileyo, amaqhosha, amaqokobhe obugcisa)
* Ibhokisi yobutyebi (ibhokisi encinci/envelopu)
* Ikhadi lesipho (ikhadi lesipho eliphelelwe lixesha okanye elingeyonyani le-R100)
* Amakhadi e-flash angenanto (okanye amaphepha amancinci)
* **Izinto eziluncedo ukuba zikhona:** Imifanekiso echazayo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A & B_), Imaphu yobutyebi, Iisitika zemivuzo

**Izixhobo zobuchwepheshe**

* Isibali-xesha/iwotshi yeselfowuni
* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye isikrini
* Ipeni yokubhala
* **Iluncedo ukuba ikhona**: Ibhodi emhlophe/iipen, iprojektha, intsimbi/umqondiso wexesha, umculo (ukhetho xa kukhangelwa ubutyebi)

**USUKU 1 - UKUTSHINTSHISANA**

imizuzu eli-10

**Lungiselela**

1. **Sasaza** amakhadi amaninzi kumfundi ngamnye, amakhadi amaninzi anohlobo olufanayo.

* _Umfundi A = Amadayimani, Umfundi B = Iintliziyo njl._

* _Idayimani = Iimachete, Iintliziyo = Iintonga zokuloba, Iiklabhu = Iintente, Iispedi = Iitshisi_

1. **Buza** umfundi ngamnye ukuba angayisebenzisa njani loo nto. Xa bechaza indlela abaza kuyisebenzisa ngayo into yabo, **thatha ikhadi** kumfundi kwaye ulifake kwindawo ethi ‘isetyenzisiwe’ embindini weqela.
1. Emva kokuba umfundi ngamnye esebenzise enye yezinto zakhe, **buza** ukuba baza kwenza ntoni ngezinto ezishiyekileyo - **khuthaza** ukuba kuya kufuneka batshintshe izinto ABANAZO ngezinto ABAZIDINGAYO (Jonga _Isihlomelo B_)

**Bonisa umzekelo**

* Utitshala ubonisa indlela yokutshintshiselana nomfundi (nikela ↔ phendula ↔ sivumelane). Bhala izakhelo zemigca ebhodini, ukuba kuyimfuneko: “Ndine ____. Ndiyafuna ____.” / “Ndingatshintsha ____ ngo ____.”

**Tshintshiselana**

* Cacisa igridi yokutshintshiselana (Jonga _Isihlomelo B_)
* Abafundi banemizuzu **emi-5** yokutshintshiselana ngezinto ABANAZO ukuze bafumane izinto ABAZIDINGAYO.
* Xa ixesha liphelile, sebenzisa umnxeba wokuphelisa.

**Inqaku lokujonga & Ingxoxo yokugqibela**

* Phakamisa umnwe: zonke iimfuno zifezekisiwe / phantse / akukabikho
* Xoxa: Yintoni ebe lula/inzima? Ngubani onento eninzi kakhulu? Encinane kakhulu? Yazisa **ukunqongophala** kunye ne-**unikezelo & imfuno**.

**Ingcinga**

**USUKU 3 - UKUNQONGOPHA & UMZAMO: “Ukukhangela Iqokobhe”**

**Bala & Bhala**

* Amaqela/ababini babala amaqokobhe. Umzobo omfutshane webha ebhodini (ngubani onezininzi/ambalwa).
* Xoxa **ngokunqongophala** kunye **nomzamo**.

**Isifundo Esincinci**

* Chaza **imali yezinto ezithengiswayo** (into abantu abayisebenzisayo njengemali).

* **Buza**: “Ngaba amaqokobhe angaba yimali apha?”

**Ingqwalasela**

* “Ukuba amaqokobhe aba yimali, **ixabiso** lezinto linokuba njani? Kutheni?”

**USUKU 6 - ULuhlu Lweminqweno**

Kulo msebenzi wesibini wokugqibela kukho iindlela ezimbini onokukhetha kuzo:

**UKHETHO 1 – Ukwabiwa Ngokungacwangciswanga kunye noRhwebo (KULULA)**

Izinto ezikwiwishlist zinokunikezelwa kubafundi ngokungacwangciswanga. Oku kungachazwa ngebali eliyilwe. Umzekelo, akukho ndawo yokumisa inqwelo-moya kwesi siqithi, ngoko izinto ziye zaphoswa ngeparashuti zaza zasasazeka kwisiqithi. Ukusuka apha, abafundi banokurhweba ngento yobunewunewu abanayo ukuze bafumane le bayifunayo. Banokusebenzisa amaqokobhe abashiyekileyo ukufumana abayifunayo.

**Ukulungiselela uRhwebo**

* Cacisela abafundi, njengoko baberhweba ngamakhadi okudlala, banokumisela ixabiso kwaye barhwebe ngento abanayo ukuze bafumane abayidingayo.

**Urhwebo**

* Abafundi bane **imizuzu emi-5** yokurhweba ngento abanayo ukuze bafumane abayidingayo.
* Injongo kukufumana into yobunewunewu abayicelileyo.
* Amaqokobhe nezinye izinto (amakhadi okudlala) zinokubandakanywa kurhwebo.
* Xa ixesha liphelile, sebenzisa umnxeba wokuphelisa.

**UKHETHO 2 – Ithengiso (INZIMA)**

* Umntu ofumene ikhadi lesipho uthumele izinto kwindawo yakhe yokuhlala kwisiqithi. Ngoku ungumnini wezo zinto zonke kwaye angazithengisa kumntu onikela ngexabiso eliphezulu.
* Utitshala ngoku uthengisa izinto ngokusebenzisa **ithengiso**. Abafundi basebenzisa amaqokobhe abo ukukhuphisana kwithengiso baze bazame ukuphumelela into yabo yobunewunewu.

**Ukulungiselela ithengiso (5 min)**

* Cacisa nge **ithengiso yesiNgesi** (amaxabiso anyuka; isandla sokugqibela siyaphumelela). Qhelisela ukuphakamisa/ukuhlisa iphedali.
* Umgaqo webhajethi: ungaze unikele ngamaqokobhe amaninzi kunalawo unawo; cwangcisa kwangaphambili.

**Iithengiso (10 min)**

* Thengisa izinto nganye nganye kubafundi.
* Abafundi banikela ngamaqokobhe kwizinto abazifunayo.

**Ingxoxo emva kwethengiso (5 min)**

* Yintoni **ibhajethi**? Amaxabiso abonise njani **imfuno**? Bekufanelekile? Imithetho ingatshintsha njani iziphumo?

**UKULANDELWA (imizuzu emi-2)**
