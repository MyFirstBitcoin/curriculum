##### **Inkcazo**

Money Matters ngumdlalo wokulingisa uqoqosho lwaseklasini apho umfundi ngamnye eqala kwisiseko sakhe sasekhaya. Umfundi ngamnye uqala ne-HOME tile yakhe enegama lakhe, ikhadi lomngeni, kunye nenjongo yezemali. Abafundi bahamba kwinkqubo yezoqoqosho esetyenziswa ngumntu wonke, besebenzisa imithetho yokuhamba ngeedayisi ukuze bafumane, bagcine, batyale, baboleke, kwaye barhwebe. Uqoqosho luziswa kancinci kancinci phambi kokuba kuvulwe ukudlala indima ngokuzimeleyo, apho abafundi balandela iinjongo zabo phantsi kwemithetho efanayo, ixesha elilinganiselweyo, kunye nemida yokufikelela. Iindlela zokwandisa ngokuzikhethela zivumela inkqubo ukuba ikhule ibe noqoqosho olutyebileyo olunamashishini, iindleko zengqesho, kunye nemfundo.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baza:

* Bachonge iindlela ezahlukeneyo abantu abafumana ngazo imali
* Bachaze ukugcina imali, utyalo-mali, ukuboleka, kunye norhwebo
* Baqonde indlela imithetho, ithamsanqa, kunye nokufikelela kuchaphazela ngayo iziphumo
* Balandele inkqubela phambili ukuya kwiinjongo zabo zemali ezibonakalayo
* Bacinge ngomzamo, umngcipheko, ubulungisa, kunye nokwenza izigqibo

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yeSongezo**

*  **Amashishini**

Yongeza izikhululo zamashishini amatsha ezifana neBhekri, iMili, okanye iWorkshop. Amashishini athenga izinto ezingekavuthwa (ingqolowa okanye umgubo), ongeze ixabiso, aze athengise iimveliso ezigqityiweyo eMariketini. Abanini bamashishini bafumana inzuzo emva kweendleko, oku kuzisa umxholo wobunini bamashishini kunye nemida yenzuzo.

* **Iindlela ezihlawulelwayo** Yongeza iindawo zokuhlawula phakathi kweTown Square kunye nezikhululo eziphambili. Abafundi kufuneka bahlawule igolide e-1 okanye e-2 ukuze badlule, okanye balinde baze bazame enye indlela. Oku kubonisa iindleko zokwakha iziseko, imiqobo yokufikelela, kunye nokutshintshiselana ngexesha.
* **Amashishini amaFama amaninzi** Yongeza izikhululo zeFama ezimbini okanye nangaphezulu ezinemithetho eyahlukeneyo. Imizekelo ibandakanya ifama ekhawulezayo enentlawulo ephantsi, ifama ecothayo enentlawulo ephezulu, okanye ifama enobungozi enotshintsho olukhulu lwemivuzo. Abafundi bakhetha ngokusekelwe kumngcipheko kunye nexesha.
* **Izikolo** Yongeza isikhululo seSikolo apho abafundi banokuchitha ixesha okanye igolide ukuze bafumane izibonelelo zexeshana ezifana nokuphosa idayisi enye eyongezelelweyo, ukusebenza ngokukhawuleza kwifektri, okanye ukufumana imivuzo engcono ebhankini. Izibonelelo zilinganiselwe ngexesha, oku kugxininisa utyalo-mali lwexesha elide.
