# 6.1 Ukusuka Kwingxaki Ukuya KwiProtocol

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukubalisa amabali – Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo – Ukuzicingela

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 45-60 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke / Amaqabane / Ngamnye

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi bamamela ibali likaSatoshi kunye nesiqalo seBitcoin. Ngexesha lokuma okukhokelelwayo kunye nengxoxo, badibanisa ibali nezifundo zangaphambili malunga nexabiso, ukunqongophala, ixesha namandla, iinkqubo zedijithali nezingedijithali, ukhuseleko kunye nemfihlo. Utitshala usebenzisa iphepha eliprintiweyo “white paper” njengomfanekiso wokwenza umdla xa uSatoshi ebeka ingcamango yakhe kwihlabathi. Abafundi bacinga ngendlela iBitcoin eqale ngayo njengengcebiso, efune umsebenzi onzima ukuyakha, kwaye ixhomekeke kubathathi-nxaxheba ukuze inethiwekhi ihlale iphila.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Bachaze ukuba ngubani uSatoshi ngendlela elula.
* Bachaze ukuba yintoni i-white paper.
* Bachonge iGenesis Block njengesiqalo seBitcoin.
* Bachaze ukuba kutheni iBitcoin ifuna ixesha, amandla, kunye nemithetho esabelwana ngayo.
* Baqaphele ukuba iinethiwekhi zihlala ziphila xa abantu bethatha inxaxheba.

##### **Izixhobo**

* Ikopi eprintiweyo yebali (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Umfanekiso oprintiweyo we “White Paper” (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Amakhadi amagama aphambili amathathu: IXABISO - IXESHA & AMANDLA - UKUTHEMBANA & IMFIHLO (_Jonga Isihlomelo C_)
* Ibhodi emhlophe kunye neepeni zokubhala
* Amashiti amancinci okanye amaphepha amancinci
* **Okulungileyo ukuba unako:** Umfanekiso oprintiweyo womxokelelwane wamabhulokhi, Iphakheji encinci enelebheli ethi ‘BTC’

##### **Izixhobo zobuchwepheshe**

* **Okulungileyo ukuba unako**: Ibhodi emhlophe okanye iProjektha (ukhetho)

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

* Printa ibali kwaye ubeke umfanekiso we “White Paper” ngaphakathi kwephakheji enelebheli ethi BTC ukuze wenze umdla xa uyityhila.
* Bhala ebhodini: Kwenzeka ntoni xa iinkqubo zemali zisilela?
* Lungiselela amakhadi amagama aphambili amathathu: IXABISO • IXESHA & AMANDLA • UKUTHEMBANA & IMFIHLO.
* Zoba uphawu olulula oluthi “IBHANKI – IVALIWE” ebhodini ukuze uvuse umdla.
* Khetha kwangaphambili kwaye uphawule iindawo ezi-3–4 zokuma kwibali ukuze kukhokelwe ingxoxo.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1.  Buza abafundi: “Kwenzeka ntoni ukuba ibhanki ivalwe abantu bangakwazi ukufumana imali yabo?”
1. Bhala iimpendulo ebhodini. Dibanisa ngokufutshane nezahluko zangaphambili (Ixabiso, Ukunqongophala, Ukuthembana)
1. Ungachazi iBitcoin okwangoku. Yakha umdla.
1. Bonisa abafundi iphakheji evaliweyo enelebheli ethi BTC.
1. Yithi: “Ngaphakathi kule phakheji kukho ingcamango etshintshe imali ngonaphakade.”

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Siza kufunda ukuba iBitcoin yaqala njani kwaye kutheni abantu bekhetha ukuyigcina iphila.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-20–25_

**Ukulungiselela**

1. Abafundi bahlala emjikweni okanye ngendlela yokuphulaphula ngononophelo.
1. Ubambe ibali eliprintiweyo kunye nephepha elimhlophe elimele iprop.

**Qhuba**

1. Funda ibali ngokuvakalayo ngesantya sendalo.
1. Yima kancinci kumaxesha abalulekileyo:
  * Xa ibhanki ivaliwe – Yintoni ingxaki?
  * Xa uSatoshi esebenza ebusuku – Kutheni kubalulekile ukuzama?
  * Xa iphepha elimhlophe liboniswa – Kuthetha ntoni ukwabelana ngengcamango nehlabathi?
  * Xa iGenesis Block ikhankanyiweyo – Kuthetha ntoni ukuqala?
  * Xa inethiwekhi kufuneka ihlale iphila – Ngubani ogcina iphila?
1. Gcina izithuba zifutshane ukuze ugcine umdla.

**Ingcinga**

* Ingxoxo yeklasi yonke:
  * Kutheni iBitcoin ifuna abathathi-nxaxheba?
  * Kwenzeka ntoni xa abantu bengasakhathali ngemithetho?
* Qhagamshela kwiSahluko esidlulileyo:
  * Ixabiso – Kutheni iBitcoin kufuneka ibe yinqabileyo?
  * Ixesha & Amandla – Kutheni iProof of Work ibalulekile?
  * Analogu vs Digital – Yintoni eyahlula iBitcoin nemali yephepha?
  * Ukhuseleko & Ubumfihlo – Kutheni kufuneka sikhusele izitshixo?

##### **LANDISELA**

_imizuzu emi-2_

* Abafundi bagqiba isivakalisi sokuphuma esinye:
  * I-Bitcoin yaqala xa ______.
  * USatoshi wazisa ______ kwihlabathi.
  * Inethiwekhi ihlala iphila xa ______.
* Qokelela iimpendulo.
* Lungiselela umsebenzi olandelayo ngokukhankanya ukuba siza kuphanda apho uSatoshi wanyamalala khona.

##### **VALA**

* Qokelela amaphepha ophumayo kunye namaphepha engcinga.
* Cela abafundi babuyise iikopi zebali eliprintiweyo, amakhadi omyalelo, kunye nezixhobo kwindawo ebekiweyo.
* Yenza abafundi babeke izitulo kakuhle okanye babuyisele indlela yokuhlala ukuba yatshintshwa ngexesha lokubalisa ibali.
* Qinisekisa ukuba iprop yephepha elimhlophe kunye nezixhobo ezikwi-appendix zigcinwe ngokukhuselekileyo ukuze zisetyenziswe kwixesha elizayo.
* Lungiselela igumbi leklasi elilandelayo okanye ukhokelele abafundi ngokuzolileyo kumsebenzi olandelayo.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Cacisa okulindelweyo ekuphulaphuleni.
* Sebenzisa imiqondiso yokubuyisela ingqalelo xa kutshintshwa umsebenzi.
* Gcina ingxoxo ijolise kwinjongo kwaye ingathathi xesha lide.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Bhala ileta kuSatoshi uchaza indlela oza kukhusela ikamva lemali.
* Zoba iGenesis Block usebenzisa iimpawu ezintathu: ikhonkco, iwotshi, isitshixo.
* Dlala indima kaSatoshi ebonisa iphepha elimhlophe.

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinci: Nciphisa iziqendu zokufunda kwaye ugxile kulwakhiwo lwengxaki–ingcamango–isisombululo.
* Abafundi abangengabo abathetha isiXhosa/Ukufikeleleka: Fundisa amagama aphambili kwangaphambili: isindululo, genesis, inethiwekhi, umzamo, umthathi-nxaxheba.
* Ukhuseleko: Nika iziqalo zezivakalisi zemisebenzi yokuphuma.
