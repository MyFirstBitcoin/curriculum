# 6.3 Yeyam!

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ulingiso • Umdlalo Wenkumbulo • Ukhuphiswano Lweqela

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 45 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kweqela** Amaqela amancinci (abafundi aba-3–4) + amaxesha onke eklasi

##### **Inkcazo**

Kulo msebenzi, abafundi baya kulingisa ukumba iBitcoin besebenzisa amakhadi okudlala njengee-hash kunye namagama esigama njengezithengiselwano. Amaqela ajika amakhadi ukuze afumane amakhadi afanayo. Xa befumana amakhadi amabini afanayo, bathi “Yeyam.” Isibini esisebenzayo sibanika ukufikelela kwi-mempool, apho baqokelela igama elinye lesigama seBitcoin. Amagama adityaniswa abe zii-blocks kwaye abalwa kuphela emva kokuqinisekiswa. Inani lamakhadi kwi-mining pool limele ubunzima kwaye lihlengahlengiswa phakathi kwemijikelo. Amanqaku okugqibela abalwa ngokubala inani lweeleta kumagama aqinisekisiweyo agcinwe kwi-wallet yeqela ngalinye.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baya:

* Ukuchaza ukuba ukumba kufuna ukuzama amaxesha amaninzi
* Ukuchaza indlela ubunzima obunokunyuka okanye behle ngayo
* Ukuqonda ukuba izithengiselwano zidityaniswa zibe zii-blocks
* Ukuqonda ukuba ukuqinisekiswa kuza phambi kobunini
* Ukwenza unxibelelwano phakathi komzamo nomvuzo

##### **Izixhobo**

* 1–2 iidekhi zamakhadi okudlala
* 40–60 amakhadi amagama esigama seBitcoin (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* 1 i-envelopu yeqela ngalinye enesihloko esithi WALLET
* 1 i-envelopu enkulu enesihloko esithi BLOCK
* Iimpawu ezinesihloko esithi MEMPOOL kunye neMINING POOL
* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako:** Izitikha okanye iitempu zokuqinisekisa, Ibhodi emhlophe yokubhala amanqaku

##### **Izixhobo zombane**

* Isibali-xesha
* Indawo etafileni okanye emhlabeni yokubeka amakhadi
* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako**: Ibhele okanye isandi sesiginali sokugqitywa kwe-block

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1.  Phonononga amagama aphambili ngokufutshane: hash, mine, block, wallet, difficulty.
1. Gcina iinkcazo zifutshane kwaye zicace.
1. Chaza ukuba namhlanje kufuneka 'ubungqine umsebenzi' ngaphambi kokufumana amagama.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Beka amakhadi angama-20 etafileni ebekwe phantsi kwigridi. Chaza: Amakhadi amabini afanayo = i-hash esebenzayo.
1. Xa ufumana isibini esifanayo, thetha ngokucacileyo: “Yeyam.”
1. Bonisa ukujika amakhadi amabini kunye nokubuyisela xa engafani.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Ndifumana isibini esifanayo ndize ndithethe ‘Yeyam’ ukuze ndifumane igama kwi-wallet yam.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_30 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Yahlula abafundi abe ngamaqela aba-3–4.
1. Nikeza iqela ngalinye i-envelopu ye-WALLET.
1. Beka onke amakhadi amagama phantsi kwindawo ye-MEMPOOL.
1. Beka i-envelopu ye-BLOCK phambili.

**Qhuba**

1. Sebenzisa amakhadi angama-20 kwipuli yemigodi.
1. Amaqela atshintshana ngokuguqula amakhadi amabini.
1. Ukuba ayafana, bathi “Yeyam.”
1. Baquka igama elinye kwi-mempool.
1. Kufuneka balifunde ngokuchanekileyo ukuze baligcine okwethutyana.
1. Emva kwemizuzu emi-6–8, yima umjikelo.
1. Ukuba amagama amaninzi agodwe kakhulu, nyusa ubunzima ngokongeza amakhadi angakumbi. Ukuba amagama ambalwa agodwe, yehlisa ubunzima ngokususa amakhadi.
1. Chaza ngokulula: “Xa imigodi ikhawuleza kakhulu, siyenza inzima. Xa imigodi icotha kakhulu, siyenza lula.”

**Qhuba**

1. Ekupheleni komjikelo ngamnye, qokelela onke amagama afunyenweyo uwabeke kwi BLOCK envelope.
1. Chaza “Block igqityiwe.”
1. Buyisela amagama aqinisekisiweyo kwi-wallet yeqela ngalinye.
1. Kuphela amagama aqinisekisiweyo abalwa.

##### **VALA**

* Qokelela uze ubale onke amakhadi okudlala uze uwabuyisele kwiidekhi ezigcweleyo.
* Qokelela onke amakhadi amagama, khupha ii-wallets kunye ne-block envelope, uze uwabeke kakuhle.
* Coca indawo yemigodi, cima ibhodi, uze ujonge amakhadi angaphantsi kweetafile nezitulo.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Iqela elinye kuphela eliguqula amakhadi ngexesha
* Akukho kuchukumisa amakhadi ngaphandle komjikelo wakho
* Amagama kufuneka afundwe ngokuvakalayo nangokucacileyo
* Amakhadi kufuneka abuyiselwe kakuhle ukuba awafani

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yeSongezelelo**

* Ngenisa isiganeko sokunciphisa: emva kwexesha elimisiweyo, i-hash nganye esebenzayo ifumana kuphela igama ngalinye kwesiBini.
* Funa izibini zamakhadi abomvu kuphela ukuze i-hash ibe semthethweni
* Yongeza ibhonasi ukuba iqela ligodla izibini ezithathu kumjikelo omnye

##### **Ukwahlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: Ukusebenzisa amakhadi amagama ngokuthe ngqo kwipuli yemigodi kunokwenza kube lula.
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: Yongeza inkxaso yemifanekiso kumakhadi amagama. Vumela uncedo lokufunda phakathi kwabafundi.
* Ukhuseleko: Coca indawo ejikeleze ipuli yemigodi. Akukho kugijima
