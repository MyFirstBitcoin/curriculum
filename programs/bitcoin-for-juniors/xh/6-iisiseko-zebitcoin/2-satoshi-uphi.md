# 6.2 Satoshi, Uphi?

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukubhala Ngokuyilayo – Ukufunda Ngokusebenzisana – Ubugcisa Bomdlalo

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 60 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Ngamnye / AmaQela Amancinci

##### **Inkcazo**

Kwesifundo, abafundi bakha ibali besebenzisa izimvo ezine ezizimeleyo ezibhaliweyo kwiincam ezincamathelayo. Baqala ngokukhumbula ibali langaphambili apho uSatoshi wanyamalala baze baqikelele ukuba angaba uye phi. Emva koko, abafundi bavelisa izimvo ezine besebenzisa imibuzo ekhokelela baze bazilungelelanise zibe sisingeniso, isiqalo, nesisiphelo. Ngokwabelana okuququzelelweyo kunye nokutshintshiselana ngeencam ezincamathelayo, baphinda bahlengahlengise amabali abo ukuze bafake imiqathango emitsha. Isifundo siphela ngokuba abafundi benze umqulu wokugqibela obhaliweyo okanye otyatyekiweyo, okanye badlale indima emfutshane besebenzisa ibali elenziwe yiklasi.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baya kuba:

* Bahlula izimvo zibe sisiseko, isiqalo, nesisiphelo
* Bakhe ibali elinengqiqo besebenzisa amacandelo azimeleyo
* Bahlengahlengise ibali xa kufakwa imiqathango emitsha
* Bathathe inxaxheba ekubaleni amabali ngokwamaqela
* Basebenzisane kumdlalo omfutshane wokulingisa

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iincam ezincamathelayo (4 kumfundi ngamnye)
* Iimaki okanye iipensile
* Iphepha elineemigca ezintathu: Isingeniso / Isiqalo / Isiphelo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Ibhodi emhlophe
* Isibali-xesha
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** Iincam ezinemibala, Isibali-xesha

##### **Izixhobo**

* Ibhodi emhlophe
* Iimaki
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Ibhele okanye uphawu lokutshintsha imisebenzi

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

* Lungiselela indawo yebhodi emhlophe ukuze kamva kube nemigca emithathu.
* Qinisekisa ukuba iimaki zifumaneka lula.
* Qinisekisa ukuba uphawu lwexesha kunye namaqela alungile.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_8 imizuzu_

1. Vusa ulwazi lwangaphambili ngokubhekisa kwibali langaphambili apho uSatoshi wanyamalala.
1. Buza:
  * Ucinga ukuba uSatoshi uye phi?
  * Kutheni enokuthi ahambe?
  * Kwenzeke ntoni emva koko?
1. Yamkela iimpendulo ezimfutshane.
1. Cacisa ukuba namhlanje abafundi baza kwakha amabali abo besebenzisa izimvo ezincinci ezidibanayo.
1. Cela umfundi omnye achaze umsebenzi ngamagama akhe.

**Injongo Ngamagama Omfundi**

*  “Siza kutshintsha izimvo ezincinci zibe libali elipheleleyo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_30 imizuzu_

**Qhuba**

1. Sasaza Izixhobo - inike abafundi nganye amanqaku anamathelayo amane kunye neziphawuli. Cacisa umthetho: umbono omnye omfutshane kwiqhekeza ngalinye lamanqaku anamathelayo.
1. Ukuvelisa Iingcinga - Abafundi baphendula kwimibuzo emine:
  * Yeyiphi indawo ophupha ukuyindwendwela?
  * Ukuba unayo i-bitcoin enye, ungayenza ntoni?
  * Yintoni enokuguqula usuku oluhle lube mbi?
  * Ngubani iqhawe lakho?
1. Hamba ujikeleze uqinisekise ukuba iimpendulo zifutshane kwaye zifundeka kakuhle.
1. Sasaza iphepha elinemigca emithathu. Abafundi mababeke amanqaku anamathelayo amane kwiSiqalo, Embindini, okanye Ekupheleni. Bangabeka amanqaku amaninzi kwikholam enye.
1. Bhala Ibali - Abafundi bakhe ibali elifutshane besebenzisa onke amanqaku anamathelayo amane. Iimfuno:
  * Isiqalo esicacileyo
  * Utshintsho okanye ingxaki embindini
  * Isiphelo esibonisa okwenzekileyo
1. Abafundi bahluleka babe ngamaqela ama-3–4. Umfundi ngamnye uxelela ibali lakhe. Abaphulaphuli banika ingxelo emfutshane malunga nokucaca okanye umdla.
1. Umfundi ngamnye ususa iqhekeza elinye lamanqaku anamathelayo alidlulisele ekhohlo. Abafundi balungiselele kwakhona ixesha labo ukuze bafake umbono omtsha. Balungise kwaye baxele kwakhona ibali labo.

Ukhetho: Phinda utshintsho kube kanye kwakhona ukuba ixesha likuvumela.

**Inqanaba lokujonga**

1. Yintoni etshintshe kwibali lakho?
1. Bekulula okanye kunzima ukufaka umbono omtsha?

**Imveliso**

Ukhetho 1Abafundi babhale umqulu wokugqibela (abafundi abadala) okanye bazobe inguqulelo yamacandelo amathathu enezichazi (abancinci).

Ukhetho 2Qokelela amanqaku anamathelayo wakhe ibali leklasi elingahleliwe ebhodini. Kwamaqela amancinci, abafundi benze umdlalo wemizuzu emi-2 besebenzisa zonke izinto ezibhodini. Emva kokudlala, chaza ngokufutshane indawo yokutshintsha kunye nesiphelo kwibali ngalinye.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_imizuzu emi-2_

1. Phonononga kwaye uqinisekise ukufunda.
1. Buza:
  * Kutheni ulungelelwaniso lubalulekile kwibali?
  * Yintoni eyenza embindini ube namandla?
  * Yintoni etshintshe ukusuka kwinguqulelo yakho yokuqala ukuya kweyokugqibela?

##### **VALA**

* Qokelela amanqaku anamathelayo kunye namaphepha.
* Coca ibhodi kunye nezixhobo.
* Buyisela iziphawuli.
* Lungisa indawo yeklasi kwakhona.
* Gcina okanye ungenise imisebenzi yabafundi ngendlela efanelekileyo.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Sasaza amanqaku anamathelayo kuphela ngexesha lokubhala.
* Beka imida yexesha ebonakalayo kwisigaba ngasinye.
* Gcina amaqela emincinci ukuze wonke umntu athathe inxaxheba.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Funa incoko kwinguqulelo yokugqibela.
* Yongeza into efihlakeleyo ekufuneka ivele embindini.
* Ngenisa umthetho wokuba iqhawe alinakubonakala ekuqaleni.

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinci: Vumela ukuzoba phambi kokubhala, Nika iziqalo zemigca.
* Abafundi abangengomntu wasekhaya/Ukufikeleleka: Nika uncedo lwamagama, Vumela ukubalisa ngomlomo phambi kokubhala.
* Ukhuseleko: Gcina intshukumo ilawulwa ngexesha lomdlalo.
