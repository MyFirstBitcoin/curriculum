# 2.2 Uqoqosho lwaMandla

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukuziphendulela okusebenzayo • Umsebenzi wokuzoba igrafu

**Ixesha elithathayo** 35 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Ingxoxo yeklasi yonke, emva koko umsebenzi wokuzoba igrafu ngabanye

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga indlela amandla abo atshintsha ngayo emini ngokuzoba imisebenzi eqhelekileyo kwigrafu yeklasi kunye ne-Energy Tracker yabo. Ngokubonisa phezulu nasezantsi kwamandla ngexesha, abafundi baqala ukuqonda indlela abasebenzisa ngayo amandla, ukuphumla, kunye nendlela amandla achithwa ngayo. Lo msebenzi ubeka isiseko sezifundo ezizayo malunga nokukhetha ixesha, ixabiso, kunye nokwenza izigqibo ngenjongo.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baya kuba:

* Beqonda ukuba amanqanaba amandla ayatshintsha emini
* Bechonga imisebenzi enika okanye esebenzisa amandla
* Bezoba imizobo yemigca yamandla abo yemihla ngemihla
* Beqala ukuziphendulela ngendlela abasebenzisa ngayo ixesha namandla

##### **Izixhobo**

* Amakhadi omfanekiso okanye amakhadi omsebenzi wemihla ngemihla (ukutya isidlo sakusasa, ukuhlamba amazinyo, ukuhamba uye esikolweni, ukwenza umsebenzi wasekhaya, ukudlala phandle, ukubukela iTV, ukulala, njl.)
* Iincwadana ze-Energy Tracker zabanye (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Iikhrayoni, iipensile, iimaki
* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako:** Imifanekiso emincinci eprintiweyo yabangakwaziyo ukubhala, Izixhobo zokubonisa (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)

##### **Izixhobo ezinkulu**

* Ibhodi emhlophe enkulu okanye iposta yokuzoba igrafu ye-Energy Tracker yeklasi
* Iimaki zebhodi emhlophe
* Iteyiphu okanye iimagneti zamakhadi omfanekiso
* **Okulungileyo ukuba nako**: Iprojektha okanye isibali-xesha esibonakalayo

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Umqolo othe tye: Ixesha losuku (Ukuphuma kwelanga → Ukutshona kwelanga)

1. Beka amakhadi omfanekiso ukuze abonakale kwaye afikeleleke lula.
1. Lungiselela iincwadana zabanye kunye nezixhobo zokubhala.
1. Qinisekisa indawo ecacileyo ukuze abafundi bakwazi ukusondela ebhodini ngokukhuselekileyo.

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_30 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Ukuba kunokwenzeka, sebenzisa izixhobo zokubonisa ukuchaza ixesha kunye nokulandelela amandla (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
1. Buyela kumzobo we-Energy Tracker owawuzotywe ngaphambili.
1. Cacisa ukuba umgca othe tye ubonisa usuku ukusuka ekuphakameni kwelanga ukuya ekutshoneni kwalo.
1. Cacisa ukuba umgca othe nkqo ubonisa amandla ukusuka ezantsi (amancinci) ukuya phezulu (amakhulu).
1. Cela abafundi bakhankanye imizekelo yemisebenzi abayenzayo kusasa, emini, nasebusuku.

**Umzekelo**

1. Khetha ikhadi lomsebenzi elinye njengokutya isidlo sakusasa.
1. Buza: "Uqhele ukwenza oku nini?" kunye no "Oku kukunika amandla okanye kuyawathatha amandla?"
1. Beka ikhadi kumzobo usebenzisa ingcebiso yabafundi.

##### **1. Ukuzoba Iqela**

_Imizuzu eli-15_

1. Bonisa ikhadi lomsebenzi elinye ngexesha.
1. Buza: "Uqhele ukwenza oku nini?" kunye no "Oku kunika amandla okanye kuyawathatha amandla?"
1. Sebenzisa imvume yeklasi ukubeka ikhadi ukusuka ekhohlo ukuya ekunene ngokwexesha losuku, kwaye phezulu okanye ezantsi ngokwamanqanaba wamandla.
1. Phinda oku ngee khadi zomsebenzi ezi-8 ukuya kwezi-10.
1. Khuthaza ingxoxo kunye nokungavumelani ngokukhumbuza abafundi ukuba imisebenzi ingaziva yahlukile kubantu abohlukeneyo.
1. Hlehlisa uze uphonononge umzobo ogqityiweyo.
1. Buza: "Uqaphele ntoni?" "Amandla aphezulu nini?" "Kwenzeka ntoni emva kwemini?"

##### **2. Ukuzoba Ngokuzimeleyo**

_Imizuzu eli-10–15_

1. **Sasaza** amaphepha e-Energy Tracker. (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
1. **Yalela** abafundi ukuba bacinge ngomhla oqhelekileyo, bakhethe imisebenzi emi-4 ukuya kwe-6 abayenzayo, baze bayizobe kumzobo wabo besebenzisa amagama okanye imizobo.
1. Xhasa abo bangakwaziyo ukubhala ngokubonelela ngemifanekiso eqatywayo okanye encinci, ukuvumela umzobo endaweni yokubhala, kunye nokubadibanisa nomlingane xa kuyimfuneko.

**Ingqwalasela**

_Imizuzu emi-2_

1. Buza: "Yeyiphi ixesha losuku onamandla amaninzi?" "Yeyiphi imisebenzi esebenzisa amandla amaninzi?" "Yintoni ekunceda uphumle ngakumbi?" "Ungawasebenzisa njani amandla akho ngcono?"
1. Kwabafundi abadala buza: "Kwenzeka ntoni xa wonke amandla akho esetyenziswa kusasa?" kunye no "Singawagcina njani amandla ezinto ezibalulekileyo?"

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_Imizuzu emi-3_

Phonononga umxholo kunye nabafundi

1. Phinda ukuba amandla ayatshintsha emini, imisebenzi ichaphazela amandla ngendlela eyahlukileyo, kwaye abantu baneendlela ezahlukeneyo zamandla.
1. Qhagamshela phambili ngokucacisa ukuba ezi ndlela zichaphazela indlela esisebenza ngayo, siphumla, sidlala, kwaye sithatha izigqibo.

##### **VALA**

_Imizuzu emi-5_

1. Qokelela amaphepha nemathiriyeli.
1. Susa okanye ucime umzobo weklasi xa kuyimfuneko.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba kwaye uqinisekise umyalezo wokuba amandla alinganiselwe kwaye indlela esiwasebenzisa ngayo ibalulekile.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Sebenzisa imithetho ecacileyo yokutshintshana, nciphisa izigqibo zokubeka kumzuzu omnye okanye emibini, khuthaza ukuva kunye nokungavumelani ngentlonipho.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Thelekisa imizobo yamandla yosuku lwesikolo xa ithelekiswa neempelaveki, uthelekiso lwamaqela amancinci, umzekelo wamandla ngokwasemzimbeni usebenzisa ukugoba xa amandla ephantsi kunye nokuma nkqo xa amandla ephezulu.
* Sebenzisa imigaqo efanayo ukujonga amandla kwiixesha ezide (iveki, amaxesha onyaka, ubomi njl.)

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: Inkxaso yokusebenza kunye nomlingane, umzobo kunye namakhadi emifanekiso.
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: imifanekiso, umzekelo, inkxaso yomlingane.
* Ukhuseleko: qapha intshukumo kufutshane nebhodi.
