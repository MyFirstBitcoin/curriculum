# 2.4 Akulunganga Loo nto (Ixesha Lebali)

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ixesha lebalisa – Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo – Ukucinga ngokuziphatha

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 30–40 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke

**Inkcazo**

Abafundi bamamela ibali elifundwa ngutitshala apho abalinganiswa befumana imivuzo enokungahambelani nomgudu wabo. Kwiindawo ezibalulekileyo, utitshala uyama kwibali ukuze avule ingxoxo kunye nokugweba. Abafundi bacinga ngobulungisa, umzamo, ithamsanqa, kunye neziphumo ezingalinganiyo, besakha isakhono sokucinga ngokuziphatha kunye nesigama esiza kunceda kwiifundo ezizayo malunga nomsebenzi, umvuzo, kunye neProof of Work.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Bachonge iimeko ezibonakala zilungile okanye zingalunganga
* Badibanise umzamo nomvuzo ngokusebenzisa iziganeko zebali
* Baqhelise ukuveza izimvo neemvakalelo ngendlela enentlonipho
* Baphuhlise ukuqonda kwangethuba ngokuziphatha besebenzisa imizekelo yokwenyani

##### **Izixhobo**

* Incwadi yebali ekhethwe ngutitshala equlathe umzamo kunye nomvuzo
* Iphepha lokuzoba kunye neepensile okanye iikhrayoni
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** imifanekiso epapashiweyo yabalinganiswa bebali, amanqaku ancangathi ukumela iindawo zokuma kwibali

##### **Izixhobo zokusebenza**

* Indawo yokufunda etofotofo (ikhaphethi, izitulo, ukuhlala kwisangqa)
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Isibali-xesha esibonakalayo ukulawula amaxesha engxoxo

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Khetha ibali elibonisa ngokucacileyo abalinganiswa abasebenza nzima, abanye abangasebenzi, kunye nemivuzo enokuphikiswa (Jonga iSihlomelo A & B).
1. Funda ibali kwangaphambili kwaye uphawule iindawo ezi-3 ukuya kwezi-5 apho kungaxoxwa ngobulungisa.
1. Hlela izitulo ukuze bonke abafundi bakwazi ukubona nokuphulaphula kakuhle.
1. Lungiselela izixhobo zokuzoba zomsebenzi wokucamngca.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_2 imizuzu_

1. Xelela abafundi: “Namhlanje ndiza kunifundela ibali, kodwa siza kuma size sicinge ngexesha sifunda.”
1. Buza: “Kuthetha ukuthini ukuba into ilungile?”
1. Buza: “Ngaba into ingaziva ingalunganga nangona ilandela imithetho?”
1. Cacisa umbuzo ophambili wesifundo: “Njengoko sifunda, siya kuhlala sibuza umbuzo omnye:_Ngaba oko kulungile?_”

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Mamela ibali uze ugqibe ukuba abalinganiswa bafumene into abayifaneleyo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_20-25 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Bonisa isiciko sencwadi kwaye wazise ngokufutshane abalinganiswa.
1. Xelela abafundi ukuba uza kuma ngexesha lebali ukuze nicinge kunye.

**Bonisa umzekelo**

1. Phambi kokufunda, bonisa umgwebo wobulungisa usebenzisa umzekelo olula: “Ukuba umntu usebenza nzima aze angafumani nto, oko kungaziva kungalunganga.”
1. Khumbuza abafundi ukuba akukho mpendulo ichanekileyo okanye engachanekanga, zikhona izizathu kuphela.

**Qhuba**

1. Qalisa ukufunda ibali ngokuvakalayo.
1. Kwindawo yokuqala yokuphumla, yima uze ubuze: “Kwenzeke ntoni kanye kanye?”
1. Buza: “Ngaba lo mlinganiswa usebenzele loo mvuzo?”
1. Buza: “Ngaba oku kuvakala kufanelekile? Kutheni okanye kutheni kungenjalo?”
1. Qhubeka ufunda uze uphinde le nkqubo kwindawo nganye yokuphumla ephawulweyo.
1. Khuthaza abafundi ukuba baxhase iimpendulo zabo ngezizathu, hayi nje ngezimvo.

**Inqaku lokujonga**

1. Phakathi nendawo kwibali, yima uze ubuze: “Ukuza kuthi ga ngoku, ngubani ocinga ukuba uphathwa ngokufanelekileyo?”
1. Buza: “Ngubani ongaphathwanga kakuhle?”
1. Buza: “Ucinga ukuba oku kuya kutshintsha ekupheleni?”

**Ingqwalasela**

* Emva kokugqiba ibali, buza: “Yeyiphi inxalenye ebivakala ingalunganga kakhulu?”
* Buza: “Ngubani umlinganiswa osebenze nzima kakhulu?”
* Buza: “Ngaba umzamo uhlala ukhokelela kumvuzo?”

##### **LANDISELA**

_Imizuzu eli-10_

1. Phonononga iingcamango eziphambili: umzamo, umvuzo, ukufaneleka.
1. Buza: “Yintoni eyenza umvuzo ube mfaneleko?”
1. Buza: “Ngaba wonke umntu kufuneka afumane umvuzo ofanayo, okanye umzamo kufuneka ube nentsingiselo?”
1. Qhagamshela phambili: “Kwiifundo ezizayo, siza kuphonononga iinkqubo apho umzamo uchaza umvuzo.”

##### **VALA**

1. Qokelela izixhobo zokuzoba uze uhlambulule indawo yokufunda.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuphulaphula nokwabelana ngezimvo zabo.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Ukufaneleka akuphelelanga kwiziphumo kuphela, kukwajolise kumzamo nethuba.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Qhelisela iimvakalelo kunye nokungavumelani
* Qinisekisa ukuba wonke umntu uyavakala
* Gcina iindawo zokuphumla zifutshane kwaye zixhaswe kakuhle

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Abafundi bazobe isiqendu esinye baze babonise ukuba besilungile okanye besingalunganga.
* Bhala kwakhona isiphelo ukuze sibe mfaneleko ngakumbi
* Dlala isiqendu esinye baze bavotele ukufaneleka kwaso

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: ukuvota ngokufaneleka usebenzisa iminwe
* ELL/Ufikeleleko: inkxaso ebonakalayo kunye nemibuzo elula
* Ukhuseleko: umsebenzi owenziwa phantsi, kungabikho kushukuma okuninzi
