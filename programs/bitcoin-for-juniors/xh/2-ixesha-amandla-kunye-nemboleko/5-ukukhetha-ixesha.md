# 2.5 Ukukhetha ixesha

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ukubalisa amabali - Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo - Umsebenzi wokuthelekisa

**Ixesha elithathwayo** imizuzu engama-20

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Uthisha ukhokele, iklasi yonke

##### **Inkcazelo**

Abafundi baphonononga ingcamango yokukhetha ixesha ngokusebenzisa ibali abalisekileyo, _The Three Little Pigs_. Ngokumamela ibali, bexoxa ngezinto ezikhethwe ngabalinganiswa, kwaye bethelekisa izinto ezakhiweyo, abafundi bacinga ngokulula kwexesha elifutshane xa kuthelekiswa namandla exesha elide. Lo msebenzi unceda abantwana baqonde ukuba umzamo nomonde unokuzisa iziphumo ezingcono kamva.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baya:

* Baqubisane nokuqonda ukuba izigqibo ezikhawulezayo zinokuba neziphumo kamva
* Baqubisane nokuqonda ukuba umzamo nomonde unokuzisa iziphumo ezomeleleyo
* Baqubisane nokuqonda ingcamango yokukhetha ixesha ngendlela elula
* Baqubisane nokuthelekisa iinketho kunye nokuchaza izizathu zabo

##### **Izixhobo**

* Incwadi yebali okanye uhlobo olubonakalayo olupapashiweyo lwe _The Three Little Pigs_
* Imifanekiso ebonisa izindlu ezenziwe ngengca, imithi, kunye nemigangatho (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Iikhrayoni okanye iimaki
* **Kulungile ukuba kukho:** Oopopayi okanye abalinganiswa abaqatywe

##### **Izixhobo**

* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye iphosta yokubhala amanqaku engxoxo yeqela
* Iimaki
* **Kulungile ukuba kukho**: Iprojektha okanye isikrini sokubonisa imifanekiso

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Lungiselela incwadi yebali kunye nemifanekiso ukuze kube lula ukuyibonisa ngexesha lokufunda.
1. Lungiselela ibhodi okanye indawo yeposta yokubhala izimvo zabafundi.
1. Beka amaphepha kunye neekhrayoni apho zinokuhanjiswa ngokukhawuleza.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu emi-3_

1. Buza: “Ngubani owaziyo ibali le _The Three Little Pigs_?”
1. Buza: “Nikhumbula ntoni ngeendawo abazakha kuzo?”
1. Cacisa ukuba iklasi iza kumamela ibali kwaye icinge ngezinto ezikhethwe yinkunzi nganye.

**Injongo Ngokuthethwa Ngabafundi**

*  “Mamela ibali uze ugqibe ukuba zeziphi izigqibo ezazilumkileyo kwaye kutheni.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu eli-15_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Cacisa ukuba abafundi baza kumamela ibali, bajonge imifanekiso, baze baxoxe ngezinto ezazilumkileyo okanye ezingazange zibe luncedo.
1. Khumbuza abafundi ukuba bamamele ngentlonipho baze bathethe ngokulandelelana.

**Umzekelo**

1. Phambi kokufunda, nikezela umzekelo olula: “Ngamanye amaxesha indlela elula iziva ilungile ngoku, kodwa indlela eyomeleleyo inceda kamva.”
1. Cacisa ukuba ibali liza kubanceda babone umahluko.

**Qhuba**

1. Funda Ibali
  1. Funda _Iihagu Ezintathu Ezincinane_ ngokuvakalisa amazwi ngendlela ebonisa iimvakalelo.
  1. Bonisa imifanekiso yendlu nganye xa ivele.
  1. Yima ngamaxesha athile ubuze: “Ucinga ukuba kuzakwenzeka ntoni emva koko?”
  1. Gxininisa indlela ekhawuleza ngayo ukwakhiwa kwendlu nganye kunye nendlela emelana ngayo nengcuka.
1. Ingxoxo Yeqela
  1. Buza: “Yeyiphi indlu eyomeleleyo kakhulu?”
  1. Buza: “Yeyiphi indlu eyakhiwe ngokukhawuleza?”
  1. Buza: “Yeyiphi ihagu echithe ixesha elininzi yakha?”
  1. Buza: “Kwenzeke ntoni xa ingcuka ifikile?”
  1. Ngenisa ingcinga yokukhetha kunye neziphumo ngokubuza: “Ngaba kwakuluvakalelo oluhle ukuthatha indlela elula?” kunye “Kutheni indlu yamatye ihlale ixesha elide?”
  1. Ngenisa igama elithi **ukhetho lwexesha** ngolwimi olulula: “Ukhetho lwexesha lithetha ukukhetha into elula ngoku okanye into engcono kamva.”
1. Umsebenzi Wothelekiso
  1. Nikezela umfundi ngamnye ngephepha lokuthelekisa okanye ubonise itshathi ebhodini (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_).
  1. Phonononga imathiriyeli nganye kunye: ingca, imithi, amatye.
  1. Buza abafundi ukuba bakhangele iibhokisi abacinga ukuba zifanele imathiriyeli nganye.
  1. Buza: “Yeyiphi indlu onokuyakha?” kunye “Kutheni?”

**Ukuzicingela**

* Buza abafundi ukuba bachaze ukuba yeyiphi imathiriyeli ehlala ixesha elide.
* Buza abafundi ukuba bachaze ukuba yeyiphi imathiriyeli ekhawuleza ukwakhiwa.
* Phulaphula izizathu ezidibanisa umzamo nesiphumo.
* Buza: “Ungakhumbula ixesha apho ukulinda kwakuncede?”

##### **QHUBEKA**

_3-5 imizuzu_

* Phonononga ingcamango ephambili: ukhetho lwexesha elifutshane lunokubangela iingxaki zexesha elide.
* Sebenzisa ukhetho lwexesha kwizigqibo zemihla ngemihla ezifana nokugcina izimuncumuncu okanye ukugqiba umsebenzi phambi kokudlala.
* Qinisa ukuba umonde nomzamo kungakhokelela kwiziphumo ezingcono.

##### **VALA**

_2 imizuzu_

1. Qokelela amaphepha omsebenzi kunye neepeni.
1. Coca indawo yengxoxo.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela: “Ukusebenza nzima ngoku kunokwenza ubomi bukhuseleke kwaye bube lula kamva.”

#### **AMANQAKU**



Ukuba ootitshala bakhetha, amanye amabali angakhethwa ukufundwa. Injongo yale misebenzi kukuxoxa ngokubaluleka kokuba ukhetho lwexesha lunokuchaphazela iziphumo.

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Gcina ukubaliswa kwamabali kubandakanya ngokuqikelela kunye nemiqondiso
* Sebenzisa izikhumbuzo ukulawula ingqalelo nokuzibandakanya
*

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Sebenzisa izikhumbuzo ukulawula ingqalelo nokuzibandakanya
* Thetha ngezinto ezisetyenziswa namhlanje ezifana neplastiki, umthi, isikhumba, okanye ilitye

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinci: ukubonisa, imifanekiso, imibuzo ethi ewe/hayi
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: ukuphindaphinda, imifanekiso, ulwimi olulula
* Ukhuseleko: umsebenzi owenziwa uhleli, intshukumo encinci
