# 2.1 Umsebenzi, Ukuphumla kunye Nokudlala

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Umsebenzi weqela onxibelelanayo - Ukuzicingela komntu

**Ixesha elithathayo** 45 imizuzu

**Ukwahlulwa kweqela** Iklasi yonke xa kusenziwa imisebenzi ngokudibeneyo, emva koko umntu ngamnye okanye ababini xa becamngca

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphanda indlela imisebenzi yemihla ngemihla enokwahlulwa ngayo ibe ngumsebenzi, ukuphumla, kunye nokudlala. Ngokusebenzisa umsebenzi wokuhlela ngokudibeneyo kunye nokuzoba imephu yomsebenzi wemihla ngemihla, abafundi bacinga ngendlela abasebenzisa ngayo ixesha labo ngosuku. Lo msebenzi wakha ukuqonda kwangethuba ngokulinganisela, amandla, kunye nokusebenzisa ixesha ngenjongo.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baza:

* Bachonge kwaye bahlule imisebenzi njengomsebenzi, ukuphumla, okanye ukudlala
* Bacinge ngendlela ixesha labo lisasazwa ngayo kubomi bemihla ngemihla
* Baqaphele ukubaluleka kokulinganisela phakathi komsebenzi, ukuphumla, kunye nokudlala
* Baqubule ukuqala ukudibanisa ukusetyenziswa kwexesha namandla kunye nempilo entle

##### **Izixhobo**

* Amakhadi anemisebenzi eqhelekileyo yemihla ngemihla (umz. ukuhlamba amazinyo, umsebenzi wesikolo, ibhola ekhatywayo, imidlalo yevidiyo, ukulala, ukupheka, ukufunda)
* Amaphepha angenanto omsebenzi wemihla ngemihla:
* Iipensile, iikhrayoni, iimaki
* **Okulungileyo ukuba kukho:** Amakhadi angekho nto ongezelelweyo ukuze abafundi benze imisebenzi yabo, Imifanekiso ebonakalayo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)

##### **Izixhobo ezifunekayo**

* Iphepha elikhulu okanye ibhodi eyahlulwe kwiMSEBENZI – UKUPHUMLA – UKUDLALA (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Indawo yebhodi emhlophe okanye udonga
* Iteyiphu okanye iimagnethi zokuncamathelisa amakhadi
* **Okulungileyo ukuba kukho**: Isibali-xesha okanye iwotshi ebonakalayo

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5-10 imizuzu_

1. Buza abafundi:

* "Yintoni umsebenzi?"
* "Yintoni ukuphumla?"
* "Yintoni ukudlala?"

1. Qokelela iimpendulo ezimbalwa ungabaphikisi. Cacisa ukuba eminye imisebenzi inokuba kwindidi ezingaphezulu kwenye. Imifanekiso ebonakalayo ingasetyenziswa ukuxhasa eli nyathelo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
1. Cacisa iindidi ezintathu ezibhodi:

* Umsebenzi: izinto ekufuneka sizenze
* Ukuphumla: izinto ezisinceda siphumle
* Ukudlala: izinto esizikhethayo ukuze sonwabe

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

* "Ndiphanda ukuba ndenza ntoni njengomsebenzi, ukuphumla, nokudlala, kwaye ndijonge ukuba ndinobalansi olungileyo na."

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_30 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Lungisa ipowusta okanye ibhodi enamacandelo amathathu acacileyo adibana (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_):

* UMSEBENZI
* UKUPHUMULA
* UKUDLALA

1. Beka amakhadi emisebenzi apho bonke abafundi banokuwabona.

**Bonisa Umzekelo**

1. Khetha ikhadi elinye (umz. "ukulala" okanye "umsebenzi wasekhaya") ucinge ngokuvakalayo:

* "Ndingalibeka apha ku 'UKUPHUMULA' kuba likunceda umzimba wam aphumle."

1. Beka ikhadi
1. Buza abafundi ukuba bayavuma okanye bangalibeka kwenye indawo.

**Umjikelo Wokuqala: Ukuhlelwa Ngokudibeneyo**

1. Abafundi batshintshana ngokukhetha ikhadi baze balibeke phantsi koMSEBENZI, UKUPHUMULA, okanye UKUDLALA.
1. Imibuzo yomfundisi:

* "Kutheni ukhethe eli candelo?"
* "Ngaba lingangena kwenye indawo?"

1. Uguqulelo olukhethiweyo:

* Abafundi bavota phambi kokubeka ikhadi
* Ukudibanisa ngendlela yememori phambi kokuhlela

**Inqaku Lokujonga**

1. Phonononga ipowusta egqityiweyo kunye.
1. Buza:

* "Ngaba kukho into enokungena kumaqela angaphezu kwelinye?"
* "Ngaba siyavumelana sonke?"

1. Qinisekisa ukuba amacandelo anokudibana.

**Umjikelo Wesibini: Imihla Yam (imizuzu engama-20)**

1. Abafundi bagqiba enye okanye ezingaphezulu zeefom zokwenza imihla:

* Imihla ngemihla
* Iveki ngeveki
* Unyaka nonyaka

1. Bazalisa imisebenzi ephuma kubomi babo.
1. Imibuzo yengxoxo:

* "Ulibona phi umsebenzi kwimihla yakho?"
* "Uphumla nini?"
* "Yeyiphi imidlalo eyenzeka yonke imihla, iveki, okanye unyaka?"

**Ukuzicingela**

Ingxoxo enzulu ekhethiweyo:

* "Kwenzeka ntoni xa kukho umsebenzi omninzi kakhulu?"
* "Kwenzeka ntoni xa kungekho kuphumla okanye ukudlala?"

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_imizuzu emi-5_

1. Shwankathela iingcamango eziphambili:

* Wonke umntu uyawufuna umsebenzi, ukuphumla, nokudlala
* Ibhalansi ibalulekile
* Abantu abohlukeneyo baneendlela ezahlukeneyo zemihla

1. Qhagamshela phambili:

* Ezi zikhetho zichaphazela amandla, impilo, nokufunda.

1. Ukwandiswa ekhaya okukhethiweyo: Abafundi babuze umzali okanye umnakekeli ukuba umsebenzi, ukuphumla, nokudlala kwakunjani xa babenobudala obufanayo.

##### **VALA**

1. Qokelela amakhadi okukhumbula kunye neencwadana ezisandleni
1. Susa okanye ucoce ipowusta ukuba kuyimfuneko
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: "Imihla ngemihla yomntu wonke iyahluka, kodwa ibhalansi ibalulekile."

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Khuthaza ukungavumelani ngentlonipho
* Vumela abafundi ukuba baguqule iingcinga zabo

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Phinda lo msebenzi ngeempelaveki xa kuthelekiswa neentsuku zesikolo
* Thelekisa imihla ngemihla kwiixesha ezahlukeneyo zonyaka kunye neeholide

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abadala: Abafundi banokubhala amakhadi abo besebenzisa amanqaku ancangathi.
* ELL/Ufikeleleko: sebenzisa iimpawu kunye nemiqondiso yesandla
* Ukhuseleko: qapha intshukumo ejikeleze ibhodi
