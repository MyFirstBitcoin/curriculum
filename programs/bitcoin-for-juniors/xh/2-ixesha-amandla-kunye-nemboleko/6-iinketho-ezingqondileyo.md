# 2.6 Iinketho Ezingqondileyo

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ingxoxo - Umboniso obonakalayo - Ubugcisa bokuyila - Umdlalo olula

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 60 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Ikilasi lonke kwinxoxo, umntu ngamnye kubugcisa, ababini okanye amaqela amancinci kumdlalo kunye nokuzicingela

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga indlela izigqibo zemihla ngemihla ezichaphazela ngayo iziphumo zexesha elide ngokudibanisa ukukhetha ixesha nokutya, impilo, umsebenzi, ukudlala, kunye nemfundo. Ngengxoxo, umgca wexesha obonakalayo, uyilo lweqhawe eliyilwe ngokuyila, kunye nomdlalo wokuthelekisa okonwabisayo, abafundi bacinga ngendlela izigqibo ezincinci zemihla ngemihla ezakha amandla, ingqwalasela, kunye nempumelelo yexesha elizayo.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Baqulunqe ukuqonda ukuba ukucinga ixesha elide kukhokelela kwiziphumo ezomeleleyo
* Basebenzise ukukhetha ixesha kwizigqibo zemihla ngemihla
* Bacinge ngeendlela zabo ezinxulumene nempilo, ukutya, ukufunda, kunye nomsebenzi
* Baqulunqe ukuqonda ukuba izigqibo ezincinci ziyanda ngokuhamba kwexesha

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iitemplate zeqhawe (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Ukukhetha Ixesha (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Iipensile zokuzoba, iikhrayoni, okanye iimaki
* Iibhidi okanye iithokheni zemivuzo
* Iiswiti okanye izimuncumuncu ezincinci (ukhetho)
* Isikhokelo sokubala amanqaku eqhawe (_Jonga Isihlomelo C_)

##### **Izixhobo ezifunekayo**

* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye indawo enkulu yeposta
* Iimaki
* Iglu kunye neesikere (ukhetho)
* **Izinto eziluncedo**: Isibali-xesha okanye iwotshi yokubala ixesha ebonakalayo

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Lungiselela ibhodi emhlophe ngendawo yomgca wokukhetha ixesha (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_).
1. Lungiselela iitemplate zeqhawe, izixhobo zokuzoba, kunye nezixhobo zomdlalo.
1. Lungiselela iibhidi kunye neeswiti ozikhethileyo.
1. Qinisekisa indawo ecacileyo yengxoxo yeqela kunye nomdlalo kamva.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_10 imizuzu_

1. Phinda uxoxe ngesifundo esidlulileyo usebenzisa _Oonogwaja Abathathu Abancinane_.
1. Buza: “Yeyiphi indlu ehlala ixesha elide?” kunye “Kutheni ibomelele?”
1. Zoba umgca othe tye ebhodini ukumela ukukhetha ixesha.
1. Bhala kwicala lasekhohlo: Ukukhetha Ixesha Eliphezulu - ufuna iziphumo ngoku.
1. Bhala kwicala lasekunene: Ukukhetha ixesha elide - ukulungele ukulinda iziphumo ezingcono. Beka umququ, imithi, kunye nezitena ecaleni komgca.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

* “Funda indlela ezikhethwa ngayo izinto endizenzayo namhlanje zinokundenza ndomelele kwixesha elizayo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-45_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Cacisa ukuba ukhetho lwemihla ngemihla lufana nezinto zokwakha.
1. Cacisa ukuba ezinye iinketho zikhawuleza kwaye ziyonwabisa kodwa zibuthathaka, ngelixa ezinye zifuna umzamo kodwa zihlala ixesha elide.

**Bonisa**

1. Nika umzekelo ofana neswiti xa ithelekiswa nesidlo esisempilweni.
1. Beka umzekelo ngamnye kumgca wokukhetha ixesha kwaye uchaze isizathu.

**Qhuba**

##### **1. Ingxoxo: Ukhetho Lwemihla Ngemihla**

_imizuzu engama-20_

1. Yazisa iindidi: ukutya, impilo, umsebenzi, ukudlala, imfundo.
1. Nika imizekelo kwindidi nganye njengokutya iswiti xa ithelekiswa nemifuno okanye ukugxalathelwa umsebenzi wesikolo xa kuthelekiswa nokuwenza kakuhle.
1. Buza abafundi ukuba loluphi ukhetho olufanele ukuba likuphi kumgca.
1. Mema abafundi ukuba baxhase izizathu zabo.

##### **2. Yila Iqhawe Lakho**

_imizuzu engama-25_

1. Nika abafundi itemplate yeqhawe.
1. Yalela abafundi ukuba banike igama iqhawe labo, bapende umfanekiso, kwaye bakhethe izinto abazithandayo kwindidi nganye. Khuthaza ubuchule kunye nokuqamba.
1. Phonononga ukhetho lwabafundi kwaye unike amanqaku amandla kwiqhawe ngalinye ngokusekelwe kukhetho lwexesha elide.

##### **3. Idabi LamaQhawe kunye noVavanyo**

_imizuzu eli-15_

1. Yenza amaqela amancinci okanye abelane abafundi ababini.
1. Yenza abafundi bathelekise amanqaku amaqhawe ngendlela yeTop-Trumps.
1. Gxininisa ukuba injongo kukufunda, hayi ukuphumelela.

**Inqaku lokujonga**

1. Jonga ukuba abafundi bayakwazi na ukuchaza ukuba kutheni ezinye iinketho zenza amaqhawe omelele.
1. Phulaphula unxibelelwano phakathi komzamo kunye neziphumo zexesha elide.

**Ingqwalasela**

* Buza: “Zeziphi iinketho ezenze iqhawe lakho laba namandla kakhulu?”
* Buza: “Ngaba kukho ukhetho olunye ofuna ukuluphucula?”
* Mema abafundi ukuba bakhankanye isenzo esinye sokukhetha ixesha elide abangazama kule veki.

##### **LANDISELA**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Phonononga ingcamango yokuba ukhetho oluncinane lwemihla ngemihla luyadibanisa ngokuhamba kwexesha.
1. Qinisekisa ukuba umzamo ngoku uhlala ukhokelela kwiziphumo ezingcono kwixesha elizayo.
1. Qhagamshela ebomini bokwenyani ngokubuza abafundi ukuba bakhankanye umkhwa omnye abanokuwenza namhlanje.

##### **VALA**

_imizuzu emi-5_

1. Qokelela izinto kwaye ucoce indawo yokwenza ubugcisa.
1. Bulela abafundi ngobuchule babo kunye nokunyaniseka.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela: “Ikamva elomeleleyo lakhiwa ngokukhetha okuncinci ngexesha elinye.”



#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Gcina ithoni ikhuthaza kwaye ingagwebi
* Gxininisa ukuba imikhwa iyaphandwa, ayivavanywanga

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Yila kwakhona i-hero emva kweveki enye yokwenza izigqibo ezingcono
* Umngeni wokulibazisa ukwaneliseka usebenzisa iswekile encinci kunye nomvuzo owongezelelweyo xa ulinde

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinci: iinketho ezimbalwa kunye nokukhokelwa ekukhetheni
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: imifanekiso, ukubonisa, inkxaso yomhlobo
* Ukhuseleko: qapha ukusetyenziswa kwezikere kunye nokuhamba
