# 2.3 Akulunganga!

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Uphando olusekelwe kumdlalo – Ingxoxo elawulwa – Ukufunda ngokwenza

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 60 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kweqela** Umfundisi ubonisa, wonke umfundi uyathatha inxaxheba, wonke umgangatho uxoxa, abanye bangasebenza ngababini okanye kumaqela amancinci

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga umbono wobulungisa ngokuthatha inxaxheba kumidlalo emifutshane elandelelanayo apho amabhaso asasazwa ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo. Ngokufunda ngokuthe ngqo, abafundi bathelekisa iinkqubo ezingalunganga, ezingenamthetho, ezisekelwe kumgudu, kunye neProof of Work. Iimvakalelo zamkelwa kwaye zisetyenziswa ukuxhasa ingxoxo elawulwayo. Lo msebenzi wakha ukuqonda okusekelwe kubulungisa, ibhaso, kunye namathuba alinganayo.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baya:

* Bafumane amava kwaye bachonge iinkqubo ezingalunganga, ezingenamthetho, ezisekelwe kumgudu, kunye neenkqubo ezifanelekileyo
* Baqulunqe ukuqonda ukuba ayizizo zonke iimbasa ezifumaneka ngendlela efanayo
* Baqonde ukuba ubulungisa bufuna imithetho elinganayo namathuba alinganayo
* Bakhe amagama kunye nokuqonda iimvakalelo malunga nomgudu, ibhaso, nobulungisa
* Bakulungele ukuqonda ingcamango yeProof of Work

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iibhidi okanye iimpawu ezincinci zokumela amabhaso (“My Stack”)
* Amakhadi omfanekiso okanye imifanekiso ebonisa iimpawu (umbala weenwele, impahla, imihla yokuzalwa, njl.)
* Iphepha lokuzoba kunye neepensile
* Idayisi
* Ikhadi yomdlalo
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** Inkxaso ebonakalayo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)**,** Iilebhile zomjikelo ngamnye (Engalunganga, Engahleliwe, Umsebenzi, Proof of Work)

##### **Izixhobo**

* Isibali-xesha okanye iwotshi yokumisa
* Itafile esembindini okanye isitya esiza kusebenza njenge “ibhanki”
* Indawo evulekileyo yemidlalo yokuhamba

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Coca indawo eyaneleyo yemidlalo yomzimba.
1. Lungiselela “ibhanki” esembindini enazo zonke iibhidi.
1. Lungiselela indawo yokuhamba kwaye uqinisekise ukhuseleko.
1. Lungiselela iilebhile zomjikelo ngamnye womdlalo.
1. Beka izixhobo ukuze utshintsho phakathi kwemijikelo lube lula.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Wamkela abafundi kwaye uqale ngomoya omhle.
1. **Cacisa**: “Namhlanje siza kudlala imidlalo, kodwa ayizizo zonke eziza kuba zilungile.”
1. Ngenisa umbono ka **My Stack**: abafundi bazama ukufumana iibhidi, kodwa bagcina ezivela kwimidlalo eqondwa njengeyobulungisa yiklasi.
1. **Buza** abafundi: “Uthetha ukuthini ukuba into ilungile kuwe?” “Ungakhumbula ixesha apho into ibingalunganga?” “Yenza njani ukuba uzive?”
1. **Misela okulindelweyo**cacacile: “Siza kudlala imidlalo emine emfutshane. Emva komdlalo ngamnye, niya kugqiba ukuba ibifanelekile na.”

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

* “Vavanya ukuba yimidlalo yiphi efanelekileyo kwaye yeyiphi engafanelekanga, kunye nezizathu zoko.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-45_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Cacisa ukuba injongo kukukhulisa ‘My Stack’ ngokufumana amaso.
1. Cacisa ukuba emva komjikelo ngamnye, iklasi iya kuvota ukuba umdlalo ubufanelekile na.
1. Cacisa ukuba amaso afunyenwe kwimidlalo engafanelekanga kufuneka abuyiselwe ebhankini.
1. Cacisa ukuba xa umdlalo ofanelekileyo uchongiwe, unokuphindwa ukuze abafundi bagcine amabhaso abo.

**Umzekelo**

1. Bonisa indlela amaso afunyanwa ngayo kwaye abuyiselwa ebhankini.
1. Bonisa indlela yokuziphatha ngentlonipho xa uphumelela, uphulukene, okanye ungavumelani.

##### **Umjikelo 1: Awufanelekanga**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Khetha indlela engafanelekanga njengokhuphiswano lokuzoba olugwetywa ngumfundisi ngokuthanda kwakhe, umdlalo onobuhlobo obukhethekileyo okanye ukusasazwa okungalinganiyo ngokusekelwe kwiimpawu ezifana nobude okanye ulungelelwaniso lwamagama.
1. Nikezela amaso ngokwe ndlela ekhethiweyo.
1. Buza: “Ibekho ifanelekile?” “Wonke umntu ube nethuba elifanayo?” “Ngaba umzamo ubalulekile?”
1. Buyisela onke amaso ebhankini.

##### **Umjikelo 2: Ithamsanqa**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Khetha indlela yokuwina engahleliwe njengokuphosa idayisi okanye ukurhola amakhadi.
1. Sasaza amaso ngokwe thamsanqa.
1. Buza: “Ngaba oku kufanelekile?” “Ngaba umzamo uchaze ophumeleleyo?”
1. Ukuba iklasi ivumelana ukuba bekungafanelekanga, buyisela onke amaso ebhankini.

##### **Umjikelo 3: Umsebenzi**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Nika imingeni yomzimba okanye yengqondo njengokwenza i-jumping jacks, push-ups, ukhuphiswano lokujonga, okanye arm wrestling.
1. Nikezela amaso kwabo bagqibe okanye baphumelele imisebenzi.
1. Buza: “Ngaba oku kufanelekile kubo bonke?” “Ngaba wonke umntu unako ukuthatha inxaxheba ngokulinganayo?”
1. Buyisela amaso ngaphandle kokuba iklasi ivumelana ukuba inkqubo ibifanelekile.

##### **Umjikelo 4: Ubufakazi Bomsebenzi**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Nika umfundi ngamnye iidayisi ezintlanu.
1. Misela injongo ecacileyo njengokuphosa amanani amathathu afanayo.
1. Abafundi baphosa rhoqo de baphumelele, bephosa kwakhona xa bengaphumeleli.
1. Buza: “Ngaba wonke umntu angaphumelela lo mdlalo?” “Ngaba wonke umntu ube nethuba elifanayo?” “Ngaba oku kuvakala kufanelekile ngakumbi kuneminye?”
1. Ukuba kuvunyelwene ukuba kufanelekile, vumela abafundi bagcine amaso kwaye bawongeze kwi**My Stack**.

Ukhetho: misa umfundi oza kujikeleza ukuze aqinisekise iziphumo phambi kokuba amabhaso aqinisekiswe.

#### **Inqaku Lokujonga**

1. Yima kancinci emva komjikelo ngamnye ukuqokelela izigqibo zabafundi.
1. Rekhoda ukuba zeziphi iinkqubo ezalahlwayo kwaye zeziphi ezamkelweyo njengezifanelekileyo.

#### **Ingqwalasela**

* Buza abafundi ukuba bazive njani ngomdlalo ngamnye.
* Buza ukuba yintoni eyenze umdlalo wokugqibela wahluke.
* Qinisekisa ukuba ukufanelekela kumalunga nemithetho, amathuba, kunye nokuqinisekiswa.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_imizuzu eli-10_

1. Phonononga iinkqubo ezine ezamava: engalunganga, engahleliyo, esekwe kumsebenzi, Proof of Work.
1. Xoxa ukuba kutheni iProof of Work inike wonke umntu ithuba elifanayo.
1. Qhagamshela amava kwiinkqubo zobomi bokwenyani apho amabhaso afunyanwa ngomsebenzi.

**Ibali ongakhetha ukulibalisayo okanye ingcinga yokuyila**: abafundi bazobe okanye babhale malunga nexesha abavakalelwa ukuba into ibingalunganga okanye inkqubo abayicinga ukuba ilungile.

##### **VALA**

_Imizuzu emi-5_

1. Qokelela amaso kunye nezixhobo.
1. Coca indawo yomsebenzi.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuziphatha ngentlonipho.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Iinkqubo ezifanelekileyo zinika wonke umntu imithetho efanayo kunye nethuba elifanayo.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Vuma iimvakalelo ngokucacileyo kwaye wenze oku kwesiqhelo xa kukho ukudana.
* Gcina ulawulo oluzolileyo ekwabelaneni ngamaso.
* Jikelezisa indima ukuze wonke umntu abandakanyeke.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Phinda uxelele umsebenzi njengentsomi okanye ibali.
* Thelekisa ubulungisa kwimidlalo xa kuthelekiswa nobulungisa ebomini bokwenyani.

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinane: sebenzisa iidayi ezimbalwa okanye wabelane ngazo.
* ELL/Ufikeleleko: sebenzisa imiqondiso ebonakalayo, umzekelo, okanye inkqubo yokuncedisana.
* Ukhuseleko: jonga imingeni yomzimba kwaye unike iindlela ezizezinye.
