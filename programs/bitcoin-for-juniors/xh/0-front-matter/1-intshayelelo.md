# Intshayelelo

Ihlabathi abantwana bethu baza kulilawula liyakhe ngoku. Imali, ulwazi, kunye nezixhobo zokusebenzisana ziyatshintsha ngokukhawuleza kunokuba iinkqubo zemfundo zinokulandela. Nokuba isizukulwana esilandelayo siya kujongana nelo hlabathi ngokuqonda nangokuzithemba, okanye ngengxaki nangokuxhomekeka, kuxhomekeke kakhulu koko sibafundisa kona namhlanje.

Imfundo isoloko ingaphezulu kokudluliselwa kolwazi. Injongo yayo kukuphuhlisa abacinga ngokuzimeleyo abanakho ukubuza imibuzo, bahlalutye, baze bafikelele kwizigqibo zabo. Loo nto ibaluleke ngakumbi ngoku kunangaphambili.

My First Bitcoin ikhona ukuze yakhe olo hlobo lwemfundo. Sikholelwa ukuba inkqubo yemali yangoku ayisebenzi kakuhle kwaye iBitcoin imele imali ezinzileyo kwixesha ledijithali. Sikholelwa ukuba imfundo kufuneka ibe yohlobo oluvulekileyo kwaye ingaxhomekekanga kwinzuzo yemali. Ukunika abafundi amandla okucinga ngokwabo, hayi ukubaxelela ukuba bacinge ntoni, ngumsebenzi obaluleke kakhulu.

Bitcoin for Juniors ibeka umgangatho omtsha kwimfundo yeBitcoin yabantwana. Ayisiyo inguqulelo elula yeBitcoin Diploma yethu, kodwa yicingwe ngokutsha. Ngokufunda okudlalayo, okufanelekileyo kwiminyaka yabo, nokusebenzisanayo, abantwana abaneminyaka eli-8 ukuya kweli-12 baphonononga iingcamango ezisisiseko ezifana nexabiso, ukunqongophala, utshintshiselwano, ubulungisa, ubumfihlo, kunye noxanduva. Ayisiyo khosi yezobugcisa kwaye ayikhuthazi naluphi na ukholo. Inika iziseko zokuba abantwana bacinge baze bafikelele kwizigqibo zabo.

Ootitshala ngabo bongeza ixabiso. Le nkqubo inika ootitshala nabafundisi boluntu izixhobo ezisebenzayo zokufundisa ngendlela enomdla, efanelekileyo kwiminyaka yabantwana. Injongo ayikokudala abakholelwa kwiBitcoin, kodwa abacinga ngokucacileyo abanokubuza imibuzo kwiinkqubo kwaye bathathe inxaxheba kwihlabathi elivulekileyo, elingaphantsi kolawulo olunye.

Isizukulwana esilandelayo asidingi ukuxelelwa ukuba sicinge ntoni ngemali. Sifuna isiseko sokuzifumanela impendulo. Bitcoin for Juniors inika eso siseko.
