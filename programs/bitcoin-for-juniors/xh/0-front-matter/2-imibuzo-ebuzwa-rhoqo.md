# Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

#### 1. Yintoni iBitcoin for Juniors?

Bitcoin for Juniors yinkqubo esisiseko yabantwana abaneminyaka eyi-8 ukuya kweyi-12. Yazisa ngeengcamango eziphambili eziyimfuneko ukuze kuqondwe iBitcoin, ngaphandle kokuba abantwana kufuneka bayisebenzise ngqo.

Le nkqubo inikezelwa ngezahlulo ezimbini:

* Isikhokelo soTitshala esibanzi kwiPDF esinecebo lezifundo, amanqaku emfundo, kunye neengcebiso zokukhokela isifundo
* Amaphepha omsebenzi abantwana anokushicilelwa kunye nezixhobo zemisebenzi yokusebenzisana egumbini lokufundela

Yikhosi yokulungiselela, hayi ikhosi yezobugcisa.

#### 2. Ngubani oya kufundelwa yiBitcoin for Juniors?

Bitcoin for Juniors yenzelwe:

* Ootitshala begumbi lokufundela
* Abafundisi basekhaya
* Iinkokeli zoluntu kunye nabacebisi babantwana
* Abazali abafuna ukwazisa abantwana babo ngolwazi lwezezimali kwangoko

Yenzelwe abantwana abaneminyaka eyi-8 ukuya kweyi-12, kwaye iyakwazi ukulungelelaniswa kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo zemfundo nakwiimeko zenkcubeko ezahlukeneyo.

#### 3. Kutheni kufuneka abantwana bafunde ngeBitcoin?

Abantwana abadingi ukusebenzisa iiwallet zeBitcoin okanye balawule iikhi zabucala. Into abayidingayo ziziseko zokuba baqonde iBitcoin kwixesha elizayo.

I-B4J yenziwe ngokukodwa ukuba ingasebenzisi itekhnoloji ephezulu — izifundo zisebenzisa izinto ezibonakalayo, hayi izixhobo zombane — nto leyo eyenza ukuba ifikeleleke kuyo nayiphi na iklasi kwaye ilungele abafundi abancinci. Injongo kukulungela ukuqonda, hayi ubuchule bobugcisa.

#### 4. Yintoni efundiswa yile nkqubo?

Bitcoin for Juniors ifundisa iingcamango eziphambili eziyimfuneko ukuze kuqondwe iBitcoin, kubandakanya:

* Yintoni imali kwaye kutheni uluntu luyisebenzisa
* Umahluko phakathi kwexabiso kunye nexabiso lentengo
* Ixesha, amandla, umzamo, kunye nomvuzo
* Ukufumaneka okuncinci kunye nokuninzi kwezinto
* Ulwazi loluntu xa luthelekiswa nolwabucala
* Iingcamango ezisisiseko malunga nokufihla ulwazi kunye neekhowudi
* Imbali yotshintsho lwemali
* Imvelaphi kunye nenjongo yeBitcoin

Ezi ngcamango zihlolisiswa ngemidlalo, amabali, intshukumo, kunye nemisebenzi yokusebenzisana. Abantwana abaxelelwa ukuba bakholelwe ntoni ngeBitcoin — banikwa izixhobo zokucinga ngemali, ixabiso, kunye nobulungisa, baze bafikelele kwizigqibo zabo.

#### 5. Ingaba le yikhosi yokutyala imali, ukurhweba, okanye ukusetyenziswa kwemali yokwenene?

Hayi. Le nkqubo ayifundisi abantwana ukutyala imali, ukurhweba, okanye ukuqikelela. Ayibandakanyi mali yokwenene. Akukho ngozi yezezimali kwaye akukho kukhuthazwa kokuziphatha kokutyala imali.

#### 6. IBitcoin ichazwa njani ngendlela efanelekileyo kwiminyaka yabo?

Bitcoin yaziswa ngofuzisela, umdlalo, kunye nengxoxo ekhokelwayo. Iingcamango zobugcisa ezinzima ezifana nokufihla ulwazi okanye iinethiwekhi ezisasazekileyo zenza lula zibe ziingcamango zokuthembana, ubumfihlo, ubulungisa, kunye noxanduva. Ingqwalasela ikwisicwangciso sokuqonda imigaqo, hayi ukufunda iinkcukacha zobugcisa.

#### 7. Ndingayifundisa njani le nkqubo egumbini lam lokufundela okanye kwindawo yokufunda?

Le nkqubo yenzelwe ukuba ingasebenzisi itekhnoloji ephezulu kwaye ibe nokusebenzisana okukhulu. Izifundo zinokufundiswa kusetyenziswa:

* Amaphepha omsebenzi ashicilelweyo
* Imidlalo yokulingisa indima
* Iimpawu ezibonakalayo, amakhadi, okanye izinto ezilula zegumbi lokufundela
* Ingxoxo eqhutywa liqela

Isikhokelo soTitshala sibonelela ngemiyalelo inyathelo ngenyathelo, iingcebiso zolawulo lwegumbi lokufundela, imibuzo yokucamngca, kunye neengcebiso zemisebenzi eyongezelelweyo. Akufuneki ulwazi lobugcisa obuphambili malunga neBitcoin.

#### 8. Kwenzeka ntoni emva kokuba abafundi begqibe le nkqubo?

Abafundi baphuma benolwazi olungcono ngezezimali, izakhono zokuqonda izinto nzulu, kunye nokuqonda ngendlela elungelelanisiweyo indlela imali etshintsha ngayo. Abangabasebenzisi beBitcoin. Bangabafundi abalungiselelwe. Xa beya kudibana neBitcoin kwixesha elizayo, baya kuba nesiseko sokuyiqonda ngokuzimela nangoxanduva.
