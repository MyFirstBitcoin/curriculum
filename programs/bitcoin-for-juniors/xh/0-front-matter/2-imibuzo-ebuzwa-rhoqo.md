# Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

#### 1. Yintoni iBitcoin for Juniors?

Bitcoin for Juniors yinkqubo esisiseko yabantwana abaneminyaka eli-8 ukuya kweli-12. Yazisa ngeengcamango eziphambili eziyimfuneko ukuze kuqondwe iBitcoin, ngaphandle kokuba abantwana kufuneka bayisebenzise ngqo.

Le nkqubo inikezelwa ngezahlulo ezimbini:

* Isikhokelo soTitshala esibanzi sePDF esinecebo lezifundo elilungelelanisiweyo, amanqaku okufundisa, kunye neengcebiso zokukhokela isifundo
* Amaphepha okusebenza abantwana anokushicilelwa kunye nezixhobo zemisebenzi yokusebenzisana egumbini lokufundela

Yikhosi yokulungiselela, hayi ikhosi yezobugcisa.

#### 2. Ngubani oya kufundelwa yiBitcoin for Juniors?

Bitcoin for Juniors yenzelwe:

* Ootitshala begumbi lokufundela
* Abafundisi basekhaya
* Iinkokeli zoluntu kunye nabacebisi babantwana
* Abazali abafuna ukwazisa abantwana babo ngolwazi lwezezimali kwangoko

Yenzelwe abantwana abaneminyaka eli-8 ukuya kweli-12, kwaye iyakwazi ukulungelelaniswa kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo zemfundo nakwiimeko zenkcubeko ezahlukeneyo.

#### 3. Kutheni kufuneka abantwana bafunde ngeBitcoin?

Abantwana abafuni ukusebenzisa iiwallet zeBitcoin okanye ukuphatha izitshixo zabucala. Into abayifunayo ziziseko zokuba baqonde iBitcoin kwixesha elizayo.

B4J yenziwe ngokukodwa ukuba ingasebenzisi itekhnoloji ephezulu — izifundo zisebenzisa izinto ezibonakalayo, hayi izixhobo zombane — nto leyo eyenza ukuba ifikeleleke nakweyiphi na imeko yegumbi lokufundela kwaye ilungele abafundi abancinci. Injongo kukulungela ukuqonda, hayi ubuchule bobugcisa.

#### 4. Yintoni efundiswa yile nkqubo?

Bitcoin for Juniors ifundisa iingcamango eziphambili eziyimfuneko ukuze kuqondwe iBitcoin, kuquka:

* Yintoni imali kwaye kutheni uluntu luyisebenzisa
* Umahluko phakathi kwexabiso kunye nexabiso lentengo
* Ixesha, amandla, umzamo, kunye nomvuzo
* Ukufumaneka okuncinci kunye nokuninzi kwezinto
* Ulwazi loluntu xa kuthelekiswa nolwazi lwabucala
* Iingcamango ezisisiseko malunga nokufihla ulwazi kunye neekhowudi
* Imbali yotshintsho lwemali
* Imvelaphi kunye nenjongo yeBitcoin

Ezi ngcamango zihlolisiswa ngokusebenzisa imidlalo, amabali, intshukumo, kunye nemisebenzi yokusebenzisana. Abantwana abaxelelwa ukuba bakholelwe ntoni ngeBitcoin — banikwa izixhobo zokucinga ngemali, ixabiso, kunye nobulungisa, baze bafikelele kwizigqibo zabo.

#### 5. Ingaba le yikhosi yokutyala imali, ukurhweba, okanye ukusetyenziswa kwemali yokwenene?

Hayi. Le nkqubo ayifundisi abantwana ukutyala imali, ukurhweba, okanye ukuqikelela. Ayibandakanyi mali yokwenene. Akukho ngozi yezezimali kwaye akukho kukhuthazwa kokuziphatha kokutyala imali.

#### 6. IBitcoin ichazwa njani ngendlela efanelekileyo kwiminyaka yabo?

IBitcoin yaziswa ngokuthelekisa, ukudlala, kunye nengxoxo ekhokelwayo. Iingcamango zobugcisa ezinzima ezifana nokufihla ulwazi okanye iinethiwekhi ezisasazekileyo zenza lula zibe ziingcamango zokuthembana, ubumfihlo, ubulungisa, kunye noxanduva. Ingqwalasela ikwisicwangciso sokuqonda imigaqo, hayi ukufunda iinkcukacha zobugcisa.

#### 7. Ndingayifundisa njani le nkqubo egumbini lam lokufundela okanye kwindawo yokufunda?

Le nkqubo yenzelwe ukuba ingasebenzisi itekhnoloji ephezulu kwaye ibe nokusebenzisana okukhulu. Izifundo zinokufundiswa kusetyenziswa:

* Amaphepha omsebenzi ashicilelweyo
* Imidlalo yokulinganisa indima
* Iimpawu ezibonakalayo, amakhadi, okanye izinto ezilula zegumbi lokufundela
* Ingxoxo eqhutywa liqela

Isikhokelo soTitshala sibonelela ngemiyalelo inyathelo ngenyathelo, iingcebiso zolawulo lwegumbi lokufundela, imibuzo yokucinga, kunye neengcebiso zemisebenzi eyongezelelweyo. Akufuneki ulwazi lobugcisa oluphezulu malunga neBitcoin.

#### 8. Kwenzeka ntoni emva kokuba abafundi begqibe le nkqubo?

Abafundi baphuma benolwazi olungcono lwezezimali, izakhono zokucinga nzulu, kunye nokuqonda okucwangcisiweyo ngotshintsho lwemali. Abangabo "abasebenzisi beBitcoin." Bangabafundi abalungiselelwe. Xa beya kudibana neBitcoin kwixesha elizayo, baya kuba nesiseko sokuyiqonda ngokuzimeleyo nangoxanduva.
