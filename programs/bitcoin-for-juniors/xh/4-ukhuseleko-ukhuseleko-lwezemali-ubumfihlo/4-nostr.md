# 4.4 Nostr

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi**Umdlalo wokulinganisa – Ukubhala okusekelwe ekwenzeni izinto – Umngeni wokhuphiswano

**Ixesha elithathwayo**imizuzu engama-50-60

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela**Amaqela amancinci abantwana abathathu

##### **Inkcazo**

Ukuseka kwimisebenzi yangaphambili malunga nokuthembana, ukudluliselwa kwemiyalezo, kunye nokuphazamiseka, abafundi ngoku bathelekisa ukudluliselwa kolwazi okungafihliyo nokunxibelelana okubhaliweyo. Abafundi baqala beva indlela yokuba ukudluliselwa kolwazi kufuna ukuthembela kubadlulisi bolwazi kwaye imiyalezo ingavakala okanye iguqulwe. Emva koko basebenzisa ivili lokubhala ukufihla imiyalezo, befumana ukuba ulwazi lungahlala luyimfihlo naxa ludlula kwiindlela ezingathembekiyo okanye ezivulekileyo.

**Izinto ezifunekayo ngaphambili**

* 4.2 - Umnxeba
* 4.3 - AmaCypherpunks Abhala Ikhowudi

##### **Izinto abafundi abazifundayo**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Phinda uphonononge indlela yokuba ukudluliselwa kolwazi kufuna ukuthembela kubadlulisi bolwazi
* Qaphela indlela imiyalezo enokuvuza okanye iguquke ngayo xa idluliselwa ngokuvulekileyo;
* Qonda ukuba ukubhala kufihla ulwazi naxa abadlulisi bolwazi bengathembekanga
* Bhale kwaye bafunde amagama besebenzisa ivili lokubhala.

##### **Izixhobo**

* Amakhadi amagama okanye imisho alungiselelwe ukudluliselwa
* Iitemplate zevili lokubhala zomfundi ngamnye (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Amaphepha omsebenzi okanye amaphepha angenanto okubhala
* Iipensile okanye iimaki
* **Kulungile ukuba kukho:** Imizekelo yemiyalezo elula kunye neyinzima (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)

##### **Izixhobo zokusebenza**

* Isikere
* Iipini zokwahlula okanye iibrads zevili lokubhala
* Indawo evulekileyo yeklasi yokuhamba-hamba
* **Kulungile ukuba kukho**: Isibali-xesha

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Hlela igumbi ube neetafile ezimbini kwiqela ngalinye zibekwe kude ukuze kubekho umgama.
1. Beka amakhadi emiyalezo evulekileyo kwitafile yomthumeli nganye.
1. Lungiselela izixhobo zevili lokubhala kodwa uzigcine zifihlakele kude kube sisiqingatha sesibini.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu emi-5_

1. Cela abafundi bakhumbule umsebenzi odlulileyo apho imiyalezo yayithethwa kancinci okanye idluliselwa.
1. Buza ukuba zeziphi iingxaki abazibonileyo malunga nokuthembana, iimpazamo, okanye ukuva kwabanye.
1. Khumbuza abafundi ukuba okokugqibela abakwazanga ukugcina imiyalezo iyimfihlo.
1. Cacisa ukuba namhlanje baza kuvavanya ukuba ingxaki leyo ingasombululeka na.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Fumana indlela engcono yokuthumela imiyalezo ngaphandle kokuba wonke umntu kufuneka athembeke.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-40–45_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Yahlula abafundi babe ngamaqela amathathu.
1. Nika indima: Umfundi 1 nguMthumeli, Umfundi 2 nguMqhubi, Umfundi 3 nguMamkeli.
1. Cacisa ukuba iindima ziza kuhlala zingatshintshi kuyo yonke imisebenzi.

**Umzekelo**

1. Bonisa umjikelo omfutshane wokubaleka nokudikitha usebenzisa umyalezo ovulekileyo.
1. Khangela ukuba umqhubi kufuneka akhumbule aze aphinde umyalezo ngokuchanekileyo.
1. Khumbuza abafundi ukuba nabani na oseduze angakuva umyalezo.

**Isigaba 1 – Uthumelo Oluvulekileyo (Kufuneka Ukuthembana)**

1. Umfundi 1 ufunda umyalezo etafileni yomthumeli ngokuzolileyo.
1. Umfundi 1 ufunda umyalezo ngokuthuleyo kuMthumeli 2 etafileni yomthumeli.
1. Umfundi 2 ubaleka aye kuMthumeli 3 aze adlulise umyalezo ngomlomo.
1. Umfundi 3 ubhala phantsi oko akuva.
1. Umfundi 2 angabuyela emva nangaphambili xa kufuneka.
1. Iqela lokuqala elidlulisa umyalezo ngempumelelo liyaphumelela.

**Ingxoxo Yokuqinisekisa**

* Buza ukuba umyalezo wokugqibela uyahambelana na nowokuqala.
* Buza ukuba abaqhubi beve umyalezo wamaqela amanye.
* Buza ukuba umqhubi wayenamandla okutshintsha umyalezo.
* Qinisa ukuba le nkqubo isebenza kuphela xa umqhubi ethembekile.

**Isigaba 2 – Uthumelo Olufihliweyo (Ukuthembana Kwehlisiwe)**

1. Nika izinto zokwenza ivili lokufihla kwaye uvumele abafundi bazihlanganise.
1. Umthumeli noMamkeli bavumelana ngotshintsho lweeleta ngasese besebenzisa ivili.
1. Umfundi 1 ufihla umyalezo omtsha besebenzisa indlela abavumelene ngayo.
1. Umfundi 2 udlulisa kuphela umyalezo ofihliweyo engazi intsingiselo yawo.
1. Umfundi 3 ubhala aze achaze umyalezo esebenzisa ivili lokufihla.

**Imithetho Yokhuphiswano**

* Amaqela afumana amanqaku ngokuchaza umyalezo ngokuchanekileyo.
* Isantya nokuchaneka kubalulekile zombini.
* Abaqhubi abavumelekanga ukufunda indlela yokufihla.

**Ingcamango**

1. Buza ukuba kwatshintsha ntoni phakathi kothumelo oluvulekileyo nolufihliweyo.
1. Buza ukuba ukuthembana kumqhubi kusemfuneko na.
1. Buza ukuba ngubani olawula ukufikelela kumyalezo kwisigaba ngasinye.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_imizuzu emi-5-10_

1. Phinda uchaze ukuba imisebenzi yangaphambili ibonise indlela umyalezo unokuphazanyiswa okanye utshintshwe ngayo.
1. Phinda uchaze ukuba ukufihla kuvumela imiyalezo ukuba ihambe kwindawo kawonke-wonke ngokukhuselekileyo.
1. Qhagamshela oku nombono wokukhetha ukuba loluphi ulwazi olusoloko luyimfihlo okanye loluntu.

##### **VALA**

imizuzu emi-5

1. Qokelela izinto ezisetyenziswa kunye kwaye ulungise igumbi.
1. Vumela abafundi bagcine amavili abo okufihla.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Akufuneki uthembe wonke umntu xa umyalezo wakho ukhuselekile.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lwegumbi lokufundela**

* Abafundi abadala banokukhetha indima yabo ekubalekeni.
* Lawula iindlela zokubaleka kwaye uqinisekise ukuba kuyahamba xa kufuneka.
* Gxininisa ukuba iimpazamo zilindelekile kwaye ziyinxalenye yokufunda.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi Yezongezelelo**

* Jikisa iindima kwaye uphinde usebenzise isifihlo esitsha.
* Yandisa ubude bomyalezo okanye ungeze imiyalezo yokukhohlisa.

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinci: amagama alula kunye nokushenxisa iileta ngendlela efanayo.
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: iitshathi zealfabhethi kunye nesantya esicothayo.
* Ukhuseleko: imithetho ecacileyo yokuhamba kunye nokusetyenziswa kwezixhobo phantsi kolawulo.
