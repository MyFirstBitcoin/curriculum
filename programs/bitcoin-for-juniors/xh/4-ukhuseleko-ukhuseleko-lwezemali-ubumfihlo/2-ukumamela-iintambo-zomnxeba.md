# 4.2 Ukumamela iintambo zomnxeba

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi**Umdlalo wokulingisa - Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo - Ukufunda ngamava

**Ixesha elithathwayo**imizuzu engama-40 ukuya kwengama-50

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela**Amaqela amancinci, ingxoxo yeklasi yonke

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga indlela ulwazi olutshintsha ngayo kuxhomekeke ekubeni ngubani oluthwalayo kwaye ngubani ovumelekileyo ukuliva. Ngothotho lwemidlalo emfutshane yokudlulisa imiyalezo eneemithetho ezitshintshayo, abafundi bafumana amava okuthembana, iimpazamo, kunye nokuphazamiseka ngenjongo. Ingqwalasela yokucamngca ikwisimo sokuba imiyalezo ingatshintsha, ivuze, okanye ikhuseleke njani, kwaye ngubani omele agqibe ukuba ngubani ofanele ukufumana ulwazi.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Bafumane amava okuba ulwazi lungatshintsha xa ludluliselwa phakathi kwabantu
* Baqubisane nomahluko phakathi kweempazamo kunye notshintsho ngenjongo
* Baqubisane nokuqonda ukuba akusoloko kufuneka wonke umntu eve yonke imiyalezo
* Baqubisane nokuqalisa ukucinga ngokunzulu malunga nokuthembana, ubumfihlo, kunye nolawulo lolwazi

##### **Izixhobo**

* Amakhadi emiyalezo emfutshane nemide alungiselelwe kwangaphambili (ukuba titshala angabhekisa kuwo) (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Iphepha kunye nepensile yohlobo lomdlalo wokubaleka nokudikitha
* **Okungathandekiyo ukuba kukhona:** Iphepha elineempawu ezilula okanye ikhowudi yokutshintsha amagama yemiyalezo evaliweyo (ukhetho) (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)

##### **Izixhobo ezifunekayo**

* Indawo evulekileyo yeklasi ukuze abafundi bahambe ngokukhululekileyo
* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye iphosta yokubhala amanqaku okucamngca
* **Okungathandekiyo ukuba kukhona**: Isibali-xesha

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Lungiselela imiyalezo elula kunye neminzima kwangaphambili.
1. Gqiba amaqela abafundi aba-6 ukuya kwabasi-8.
1. Coca indawo ukuze abafundi bakwazi ukuma emgqeni okanye bahambe bekhuselekile.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu emi-5_

1. Buza abafundi ukuba bakhe badlala umdlalo wefowuni (telephone).
1. Buza ukuba ingaba umyalezo uhlale ufanayo na.
1. Cacisa ukuba namhlanje baza kuvavanya ukuba ngubani onokuthembeka ngomlayezo.
1. Gwema ukusebenzisa amagama obuchwephesha okanye angacacanga.

**Injongo Ngokweengcinga Zabafundi**

*  “Jonga ukuba kwenzeka ntoni kumyalezo xa uthwalwa ngabantu abohlukeneyo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-30 ukuya kwengama-35_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Yahlula iklasi ibe ngamaqela amancinci.
1. Cacisa ukuba iqela ngalinye liza kudlala imijikelo emifutshane emininzi enesakhiwo esifanayo kodwa imithetho eyahlukeneyo.
1. Gxininisa ukuba akukho mntu uza kugxothwa ngenxa yeempazamo.

**Bonisa**

1. Bonisa imithetho yokuncokola ngokuthulileyo usebenzisa umzekelo omfutshane kakhulu.
1. Qinisekisa imithetho icacile: thetha kancinci kuphela, ungaphindi, ungazilungisi iimpazamo.

**Qhuba**

**Umjikelo 1 – Umlayezo Olula (Ikhonkco Elithembekileyo)**

1. Thetha kancinci isivakalisi esilula kumfundi wokuqala.
1. Abafundi badlulisa umyalezo ngokuthetha kancinci kumgca.
1. Umfundi wokugqibela uyawuthetha umyalezo ngokuvakalayo.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Ngaba umyalezo ubuchanekile, ngaba kukho umntu owutshintshileyo ngamabomu, ngaba bekulula ukuthemba iqela?

**Umjikelo 2 – Umlayezo Onzima (Ulwazi Oluntsonkothileyo)**

1. Sebenzisa isicwangciso esifanayo ngomhlathi omde, ochanileyo.
1. Phinda ikhonkco lokuthetha kancinci.
1. Umfundi wokugqibela uyawuthetha umyalezo ngokuvakalayo.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Yintoni etshintshileyo, uphulukene phi, ngaba kukho umntu ozenze kakubi okanye bekunzima nje?

**Umjikelo 3 – Umthetho Ofihliweyo (Utshintsho Ngamabomu)**

1. Sebenzisa umyalezo onzima ofanayo.
1. Fihla uxelele umfundi omnye ophakathi ukuba angatshintsha igama elinye.
1. Ungabhengezi ukuba lo mthetho ukhona.
1. Phinda ikhonkco lokuthetha kancinci.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Ngaba oku kuzivele kwahlukile kwiimpazamo, singayiqonda njani ukuba kukho into engalunganga, yintoni ebingalunganga?

**Olunye Uhlobo – Ukubaleka Nokubhala (Ulawulo xa kuthelekiswa nokuthembana)**

**Umjikelo 4 – Umqhubi Othembekileyo**

1. Beka umyalezo obhalwe ephepheni kwelinye icala legumbi.
1. Umfundi omnye uyabaleka ayokuwufunda aze abuye axelele umbhali.
1. Umbhali ubhala phantsi umyalezo.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Ngaba oku bekucacile kunokuthetha kancinci kwaye kutheni?

**Umjikelo 5 – Indawo Engathembekanga**

1. Yongeza umphulaphuli kufutshane nomzila womqhubi.
1. Umphulaphuli angamva kodwa akavumelekanga ukubona umyalezo.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Ngubani ofunde into angafanele ayazi kwaye ngaba umyalezo usakhuselekile?

**Olunye Uguquko Lokugqibela – Umlayezo Otywiniweyo**

1. Phinda ukubaleka nokubhala nomyalezo obhalwe ngekhowudi yeempawu elula (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_).
1. Cela abafundi ukuba babone ukuba ngubani onokukuqonda.
1. Inqaku lokujonga: Ngubani onokufunda kwaye ngubani omva kodwa akafundi nto?

**Ingcinga**

1. Buza ukuba ngubani ofanele ukuva yonke into.
1. Buza ukuba ngubani ofanele angave nto.
1. Buza ukuba ngubani ofanele agqibe.
1. Vumela abafundi babize ingxaki ngeendlela zabo.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_5-10 imizuzu_

1. Phonononga ukuba imiyalezo ingatshintsha, ivuze, okanye iguqulwe.
1. Qinisekisa ukuba ukuthembana kuxhomekeke kwimigaqo nemida.
1. Qhagamshela kwizifundo zangaphambili malunga nolwazi loluntu xa kuthelekiswa nolwabucala.

##### **VALA**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Bulela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba ngokunyaniseka.
1. Buyisela igumbi lokufundela kwimeko yesiqhelo.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: Ukulondoloza ulwazi kunceda ukukhusela abantu.

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Qhelisela iimpazamo kunye neemvakalelo ezivelayo ngelixa ugcina ulawulo oluzolileyo.
* Ungamtyhili okanye umsole umphuli womthetho ofihlakeleyo ngaphandle kokuba abafundi bacela ngokucacileyo.

##### **Imisebenzi yokwandisa & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Bhala umthetho weklasi wokukhusela imiyalezo okanye yila umdlalo kwakhona ukuze ube nobulungisa.

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula abafundi**

* Abafundi abancinci: imiyalezo emfutshane kunye nemijikelo embalwa.
* Abafundi abangengomthonyama/Ukufikeleleka: imiqondiso ebonakalayo, isantya esicothayo, inkxaso yomlingane.
* Ukhuseleko: intshukumo elawulwayo kunye nemithetho ecacileyo yokuncokola ngelixa kuncwina.
