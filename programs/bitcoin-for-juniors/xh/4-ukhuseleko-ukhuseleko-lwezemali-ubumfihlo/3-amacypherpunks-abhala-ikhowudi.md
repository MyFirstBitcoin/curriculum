# 4.3 AmaCypherpunks abhala ikhowudi

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ubugcisa - Umsebenzi wokuchaza amagama ngokwakho - Ukuqeqeshwa okukhokelelwayo

**Ixesha elithathayo** 45-60 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Umsebenzi ngamnye nomzekelo phambi kweklasi yonke

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baziswa kumbono wekhodi njengendlela yokufihla nokukhusela ulwazi. Ngokusebenzisa umsebenzi wezandla, abafundi bakha isixhobo esilula sokuguqula iileta (cipher wheel) kwaye basisebenzise ukufihla nokuchaza amagama anxulumene ne-Bitcoin. Lo msebenzi udibanisa izakhono zokusebenza ngezandla, ukucinga ngengqiqo, kunye neengcamango zokuqala zobugcisa bokufihla ulwazi, ngelixa uqinisekisa amagama amatsha ngendlela edlalayo necacileyo.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baza:

* Baqukumbela intsingiselo yokufihla nokuchaza amagama; baqonde ukuba ukutshintsha ikhowudi kutshintsha impendulo; basebenzise isixhobo sokuguqula iileta ukufihla nokuchaza amagama alula; bachaze ngokuchanekileyo amagama athile anxulumene ne-Bitcoin.

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iitemplate zoshicilelo zesixhobo sokuguqula iileta ezineeringi zangaphakathi nezangaphandle zealfabhethi (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Iphepha lomsebenzi elishicilelweyo elinegama le-Bitcoin elifihlakeleyo (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Iipensile, iikhrayoni, okanye iimaki
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho:** Isixhobo sokuguqula iileta esele senziwe ukuze siboniswe, iibrads okanye iipini zokudibanisa amavili, amaphepha omsebenzi ongezelelweyo

##### **Izixhobo zombane**

* Isikere
* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye ibhodi yeposta
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Umboniso ngeprojektha, isikere sabasekhohlo

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

**Ukulungiselela kotitshala**

1. Shicilela kwaye ulungiselele iitemplate zesixhobo sokuguqula iileta namaphepha omsebenzi.
1. Sika kwangaphambili isixhobo esikhulu sokubonisa okanye ulungiselele umfanekiso kwibhodi.
1. Qinisekisa ukuba izikere kunye neepini zisasazwe ngokukhuselekileyo.



##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Buza abafundi ukuba bayazi na into ebizwa ngokuba yikhowudi yemfihlo.
1. Buza apho bakhe babona khona iikhowudi ngaphambili njengemidlalo, imiyalezo efihliweyo, okanye izitshixo.
1. Yazisa amagama athi 'encode' kunye 'decode' usebenzisa ulwimi olulula.
1. Cacisa ukuba namhlanje baza kufunda indlela yokufihla nokutyhila amagama besebenzisa ikhowudi.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Yenza isixhobo sekhowudi yemfihlo kwaye usisebenzise ukufihla nokufunda amagama.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_35-45 imizuzu_

**Ukulungiselela**

1. Cacisa ukuba iileta zingatshintshwa ukwenza imiyalezo yemfihlo.
1. Zoba i-alfabhethi kwibhodi ubonise utshintsho olulula lweleta njengokuba A iba nguD.

**Bonisa**

1. Bonisa indlela yokufihla igama elifutshane ngokutshintsha iileta.
1. Bonisa indlela yokuchaza igama elifanayo ukubuyela kwelokuqala.
1. Yazisa isixhobo sokuguqula iileta ubonise ukuba ukusijikeleza kutshintsha iileta eziphumayo.
1. Cacisa ukuba inani lamanyathelo ajikwe ivili litshintsha isiphumo.

**Qhuba**

**Inxalenye 1 – Ukwenza Isondo leCipher**

1. Abafundi basika imijikelo yealfabhethi yangaphakathi nangaphandle.
1. Abafundi babeka umjikelo omncinci ngaphezulu komkhulu.
1. Abafundi babopha imijikelo kunye ngepini ukuze isondo likwazi ukujika ngokukhululekileyo.
1. Abafundi bavavanya isondo ngokulungelelanisa iileta ezahlukeneyo.

#### **Inqaku Lokujonga**

1. Buza abafundi ukuba bachaze igama elinye kunye njengeklasi.
1. Qinisekisa ukuba abafundi bayayiqonda indlela utshintsho lwesondo olutshintsha ngayo isiphumo.

**Inxalenye 2 – Ukuziqhelanisa nokufihla nokuchaza amagama**

1. Abafundi basebenzisa isondo labo le-cipher ukuchaza amagama asephepha lomsebenzi.
1. Abafundi bafihla amagama anikwa iMy First Bitcoin besebenzisa utshintsho lweeleta abakhethileyo.
1. Ijikeleza ukuxhasa abafundi nokujonga ukuqonda.

**Ingqwalasela**

* Buza abafundi ukuba kwenzeke ntoni xa betshintshe indawo yesondo.
* Buza ukuba igama elifanayo lihlala libonakala lifana na xa ikhowudi itshintsha.
* Buza ukuba kutheni umntu angafuna ukufihla ulwazi ngale ndlela.

##### **UKULANDELWA**

_Imizuzu emi-2_

* Phonononga amagama aphambili: fihla, chaza, ikhowudi, umyalezo.

##### **VALA**

* Qokelela izikere nezixhobo.
* Vumela abafundi bagcine iisondo zabo ze-cipher.
* Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Ukutshintsha ikhowudi kutshintsha umyalezo, kodwa intsingiselo ihlala injalo.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Jonga ngononophelo ukusetyenziswa kwezikere nepini.
* Khuthaza umonde ngexesha lokwenza ubugcisa.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Umngeni wokuchaza amagama ngokukhawuleza usebenzisa amagama amatsha afihliweyo.
* Dlala umngeni wokuchaza amagama njengomdlalo we-cipher hangman apho igama elifihliweyo libhalwe ebhodini.

##### **Umahluko**

* Abafundi abancinane: Amagama amafutshane kunye notshintsho olusisigxina lweeleta, Sebenzisa amagama angengawo e-My First Bitcoin
* Abafundi abadala: Bakhethe kwaye babhale utshintsho lwabo lweeleta ukuze abanye bakwazi ukusombulula.
* Abafundi abaneengxaki zolwimi/ukufikeleleka: izikhokelo zealfabhethi ezibonakalayo kunye noncedo lwezikhwetha.
* Ukhuseleko: izikere ezingacacanga kunye nokusetyenziswa kwepini okujongiweyo.
