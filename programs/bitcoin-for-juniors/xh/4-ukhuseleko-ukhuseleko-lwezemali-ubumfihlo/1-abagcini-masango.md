# 4.1 Abagcini-masango

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ingxoxo ekhokelwayo - Ukuhlukaniswa ngokubonwayo - Ukuzicingela komntu siqu

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 30 imizuzu

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Iqela lonke / Amaqabane / Umntu ngamnye

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi baphonononga umahluko phakathi kweendawo zoluntu nezasese kunye nolwazi ngokusebenzisa ingxoxo ekhokelwayo nemizekelo ebonakalayo. Besebenzisa iimeko zemihla ngemihla abazaziyo, abafundi baqhelisela ukuhlula ukuba yintoni eqheleke ukuba yoluntu okanye iyimfihlo baze bacinge ngombono wokuba umntu ngamnye unelungelo lokukhetha ukuba loluphi ulwazi agcina luyimfihlo okanye alwabelane ngalo. Lo msebenzi wakha ukuqonda kwangethuba ngobumfihlo, imida, kunye noxanduva lomntu siqu.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni kolu qeqesho, abafundi baya:

* Bahlule phakathi kweendawo zoluntu nezasese kunye nolwazi
* Bachaze ukuba kutheni ezinye izinto kufuneka zigcinwe ziyimfihlo
* Baqukumbela ukuba abantu banokukhetha into abayabelana ngayo
* Baqulunqe ingxoxo enentlonipho kunye nokwenza izigqibo zomntu siqu

##### **Izixhobo**

* Izixhobo zokubonisa okanye amakhadi abonisa imizekelo yeendawo nolwazi (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Iphepha lomsebenzi lomfundi elineebhokisi zokukhangela zeLuntu / Zasese (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)
* Iipensile okanye iikhrayoni

##### **Izixhobo**

* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye ibhodi yeposta
* Iimaki
* **Kulungile ukuba unako:** Iprojektha okanye isikrini semifanekiso

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Lungiselela izixhobo zokubonisa okanye iislayidi ezibonisa imizekelo efana nepakhi, ibhasi, igumbi lokuhlambela, imoto, umbala wamehlo, iphasiwedi, iPIN yasebhankini.
1. Lungiselela ibhodi enezihloko ezimbini: ULUNTU kunye NEZASESE.
1. Beka amaphepha omsebenzi kunye nezixhobo zokubhala apho zinokufikeleleka lula.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_5 imizuzu_

1. Buza abafundi: “Kuthetha ukuthini ‘uluntu’?” kunye “Kuthetha ukuthini ‘zasese’?”
1. Yamkela iimpendulo ezininzi kwaye ungakaphenduli okwangoku.
1. Cacisa ukuba namhlanje baza kujonga imizekelo yemihla ngemihla baze bagqibe kunye.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Vavanya ukuba yintoni yoluntu, yintoni eyimfihlo, nento endifuna ukwabelana ngayo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_20 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Cacisa ukuba ezinye izinto zihlala zisesidlangalaleni, ezinye zihlala ziyimfihlo, kwaye ezinye zixhomekeke kukhetho.
1. Yazisa umbono wokuba akusoloko kukho impendulo enye echanileyo.

**Bonisa**

1. Bonisa umzekelo omnye njengendawo yokudlala (ipakhi).
1. Cinga ngokuvakalayo: “Abantu abaninzi banokungena apho baze bakubone, ngoko sihlala sisithi le yindawo yoluntu.”
1. Bonisa umzekelo wesibini njengalapho kusetyenziswa iphasiwedi.
1. Cinga ngokuvakalayo: “Le yeyomntu omnye kuphela, ngoko sihlala siyigcina iyimfihlo.”

**Qhuba**

1. Bonisa umzekelo omnye ngexesha.
1. Cela abafundi bavote okanye baphakamise izandla zabo ukuba 'sesidlangalaleni' okanye 'siyimfihlo'.
1. Beka umzekelo phantsi kwesihloko esifanelekileyo ebhodini.
1. Buza imibuzo elandela njengokuthi “Kutheni?” okanye “Ngaba oku kungatshintsha nanini na?”
1. Sebenzisa imizekelo enjengendawo yokudlala, ibhasi, igumbi lokuhlambela, imoto, umbala wamehlo, iphasiwedi, iPIN yasebhankini.

**Inqaku lokujonga**

1. Cela abafundi bachaze umahluko phakathi kokuba sesidlangalaleni nokuba yimfihlo ngeendlela zabo.
1. Phulaphula ukuqonda hayi iinkcazo ezifundwe ngentloko.

**Ingqwalasela**

* Buza: “Ngaba umbala wamehlo uhlala uyimfihlo?”
* Buza: “Ngaba into ingaba yimfihlo nokuba abanye abantu bayayibona?”
* Khokela abafundi ukuba baqonde ukuba abantu bakhetha ulwazi abalwabelana ngalo.

##### **LANDISELA**

_Imizuzu emi-5_

1. Sasaza iphepha lomsebenzi oneebhokisi zokukhangela.
1. Abafundi bagqiba kwelokuba into nganye bayigcina isesidlangalaleni okanye iyimfihlo.
1. Gxininisa ukuba iimpendulo zingahluka kumntu nomntu.

##### **VALA**

1. Qokelela amaphepha omsebenzi nezixhobo.
1. Shwankathela ingcamango ephambili: “Ungakhetha ulwazi ofuna ukwabelana ngalo.”
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Ukucinga ngobulumko malunga nemfihlo kukunceda ukhuseleke.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Khuthaza ukuphulaphula ngentlonipho kunye neembono ezahlukeneyo
* Kuphephe ukucela abafundi ukuba babelane ngolwazi lwabo lobuqu olubuthathaka

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Yongeza imizekelo yedijithali enjengamagama omsebenzisi okanye iifoto
* Yenza ipowusta yomthetho weklasi wokwabelana ngolwazi
* Jonga amagorha nabakhohlakeleyo - kutheni bekhusela isazisi sabo?
* Yenza umzobo wolwazi abafundi abafuna ukwabelana ngalo/nokugcina iyimfihlo

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: sebenzisa imizekelo yendawo kuphela
* Abafundi abadala: yiya kwizifundo ezinzima ngakumbi (iDNA, Ukuqondwa kobuso, Indawo yeGPS, Igama)
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: imifanekiso, imiqondiso, ulwimi olulula
* Ukhuseleko: kuphephe ukucela iipasiwedi zokwenyani okanye idatha yobuqu
