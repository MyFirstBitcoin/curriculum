# 3.1 Ngexesha lam elidlulileyo

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi**Ukudibanisa izibini – Ukuzoba ngemibala – Ingxoxo yeqela

**Ixesha elithathwayo**imizuzu engama-20

**Ukuhlulwa kweqela**Umsebenzi ngamnye nomfundi, kodwa banokusebenza bebini okanye bonke bexoxa njengeklasi

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi badibanisa itekhnoloji yakudala ye-analogue nezixhobo zale mihla zedijithali. Ngokudibanisa imifanekiso, ukuzoba ngemibala, kunye nengxoxo, abafundi bafunda indlela izixhobo eziguquka ngayo ngokuhamba kwexesha kunye nendlela izinto ezintsha ezitshintsha ngayo indlela abantu abaphila, abasebenza, nabathetha ngayo. Lo msebenzi wakha ukuqonda kwangethuba ngenkqubela yetekhnoloji kunye notshintsho olusekelwe kwixesha.

##### **Iziphumo Zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi lo, abafundi baya:

* Bachonge imizekelo yeetekhnoloji ze-analogue nezidijithali
* Baqukumbela ukuba itekhnoloji iyatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha
* Baqaphele ukuba izinto ezintsha zizisa ukuphuculwa kunye nokuzivumelanisa
* Bacinge ngendlela itekhnoloji etshintsha ngayo ubomi bemihla ngemihla

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iphepha lomsebenzi wokudibanisa eliprintiweyo (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
* Iipensile zemibala okanye iikhrayoni
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Imifanekiso ebonakalayo (_Jonga Isihlomelo B_)

##### **Izixhobo zombane**

* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye ibhodi yesichoko
* Imifanekiso ebonakalayo okanye isilayidi
* **Okungcono ukuba kukho**: Isibali-xesha

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Qinisekisa ukuba umfundi ngamnye unendawo ecocekileyo yokusebenza.
1. Lungiselela ibhodi okanye imifanekiso ebonakalayo enemifanekiso yeetekhnoloji zakudala.
1. Sasaza izixhobo zokuzoba ngemibala kwangaphambili.
1. Qalisa umsebenzi ngokuthi: “Namhlanje siza kujonga indlela itekhnoloji etshintsha ngayo ukusuka kwixesha elidlulileyo ukuya kwanamhlanje.”
1. Bonisa imizekelo efana nomatshini wokuchwetheza, iWalkman, isibane seoyile, okanye iteyiphu yeVHS.
1. Buza: “Uyakhe wayibona le nto?” “Sisebenzisa ntoni endaweni yayo namhlanje?”

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu emi-2-3_

1. Cacisa ukuba abafundi baza kuzibamba njengee-detektivhi zetekhnoloji.
1. Yazisa amagama amatsha: analogue, digital, technology, innovation, evolution.
1. Cacisa intsingiselo usebenzisa ingcaciso elula okanye imifanekiso.
1. Cacisa ukuba injongo kukudibanisa izixhobo zakudala nezanamhlanje.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Dibanisa itekhnoloji yakudala netekhnoloji entsha uze ubone indlela izinto ezitshintshileyo ngayo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu eli-10_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Sasaza iphepha lomsebenzi wokudibanisa kumfundi ngamnye (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)
1. Bhala imiyalelo elula ebhodini.

**Bonisa**

1. Bonisa umzekelo omnye wokudibanisa izinto ebhodini.
1. Cacisa ukuba kutheni ezi zinto zimbini zihambelana.

**Qhuba**

1. Abafundi badibanisa into nganye yendabuko ekho ngasekhohlo kunye nenguqulelo yayo yedijithali ekho ngasekunene.
1. Abafundi bapenda izinto abazisebenzisileyo ngaphambili.
1. Abafundi bajikeleza izinto abazinekhaya.
1. Utitshala ujikeleza ukunceda abafundi abangazaziyo ezinye iitekhnoloji.

**Inqanaba lokujonga**

1. Qaphela ukuba abafundi bayakwazi na ukudibanisa uninzi lwezibini ngokuchanekileyo.
1. Yima kancinci ukuba abafundi abaninzi banengxaki kwaye ucacise usebenzisa imifanekiso.

##### **LANDISELA**

_Imizuzu emi-3 ukuya kwemi-5_

1. Biza iklasi yonke ukuze nixoxe.
1. Buza: “Yeyiphi itekhnoloji yakudala oyifumene inomdla kakhulu?”
1. Buza: “Yeyiphi itekhnoloji entsha oyisebenzisa kakhulu?”
1. Buza: “Ngaba itekhnoloji entsha isoloko ingcono?”
1. Ukhetho: abafundi baxelele ummelwane wabo omnye umdlalo abamangaliswe ngawo abawufundileyo.

##### **VALA**

1. Qokelela amaphepha omsebenzi kunye nezixhobo.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokugxila kwabo nomzamo.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela: “Itekhnoloji iyatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha, kodwa isixhobo ngasinye sinceda abantu ukusombulula iingxaki ngexesha labo.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lweklasi**

* Lungiselela izixhobo kwangaphambili ukuze ungaphazamiseki
* Khuthaza abagqiba ngokukhawuleza ukuba bapende ngononophelo baze bacinge

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Udliwanondlebe ekhaya nabazali okanye ootatomkhulu noomakhulu malunga netekhnoloji yakudala
* Yila uze uzobe inguqulelo yexesha elizayo yesixhobo esinye
* Jonga iividiyo ezimfutshane zokubonisa iitekhnoloji ezindala

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: ukudibanisa okukhokelelwayo usebenzisa imifanekiso ebhodini
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: imifanekiso, ulwimi olulula, uncedo loontanga
* Ukhuseleko: umsebenzi wokuhlala phantsi, intshukumo encinci
