# 3.3 Yila Ikamva

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi**Ukwenza izinto ezintsha - Intetho yokwazisa - Ingxoxo elungiselelwe kakuhle

**Ixesha elithathwayo**imizuzu engama-45 ukuya kwengama-60

**Ukuhlelwa kwamaqela** Umntu ngamnye okanye amaqela amancinci abantwana aba-2 ukuya kwabayi-4

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi bacinga ngekamva lobuchwepheshe ngokwenza imveliso okanye inkqubo entsha baze bayazise ngendlela ye-“Dragons’ Den”. Abafundi bacinga ngendlela itekhnoloji etshintsha ngayo, bachonge iingxaki zokwenene ekufuneka zisombululwe, baze baqeqeshe ukuchaza izimvo zabo ngokucacileyo. Lo msebenzi ukhuthaza ubuchule, ukucinga nzulu, kunye nonxibelelwano ngelixa uqinisekisa ukuba izinto ezintsha ziveliswa ngenxa yeemfuno ezitshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baza kukwazi:

* Uqikelelo ngendlela itekhnoloji enokutshintsha ngayo kwixesha elizayo
* Uyilo lwento entsha elula yokusombulula ingxaki
* Chaza indlela kunye nesizathu sokusebenza kwento abayenzileyo
* Qeqesha indlela yokwazisa nokukhusela izimvo zabo.

##### **Izixhobo**

* Iphepha elingenanto lokuzoba kunye nokubhala amanqaku
* Iipensile, iikhrayoni, okanye iimaki
* **Kulungile ukuba unako:** Itemplate yePitch (_Jonga Isihlomelo A_)

##### **Izixhobo zokusebenza**

* Ibhodi emhlophe okanye indawo yokubeka iposta
* **Kulungile ukuba unako**: Isibali-xesha seepitch

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Hlela izitulo ukuze amaqela akwazi ukusebenza kunye aze kamva azise phambili.
1. Lungiselela indawo yebhodi ukubhala imizekelo yetekhnoloji yakudala, yangoku, kunye neyatsha.
1. Gqiba indlela ezakulinganiswa ngayo iipitch kunye nendlela ezakulinganiswa ngayo.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu emi-5 ukuya kweli-10_

1. Bonisa okanye chaza imizekelo yezinto ezintsha ezaphumelela kunye nezo ezingaphumelelanga.
1. Xoxa kancinci ukuba kutheni ezinye iitekhnoloji zisebenze ezinye zingasebenzi.
1. Yazisa ngeetekhnoloji ezitsha ezimbalwa uze ubuze abafundi ukuba bacinga ukuba kuzakwenzeka ntoni ngokulandelayo.
1. Chaza ukuba namhlanje abafundi baza kuba ngabaqambi bezinto ezintsha, beqikelela ikamva.

**Injongo Ngokutsho Komfundi**

*  “Qamba itekhnoloji entsha uze uqinisekise abanye ukuba yinto elungileyo.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_imizuzu engama-30 ukuya kwengama-40_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Chaza indlela yeDragons’ Den: abafundi baqamba, baze bazise.
1. Chaza okulindelweyo kwiPitch: yintoni, isombulula yiphi ingxaki, kwaye kutheni ibalulekile.
1. Yabela abafundi ukuba basebenze bodwa okanye kumaqela amancinci.

**Umzekelo**

1. Nika umzekelo olula wokuqamba, njengengxowa yomqolo ehlawulisa izixhobo xa uhamba.
1. Bonisa umzekelo omfutshane wePitch ochaza ingxaki, isisombululo, kunye nenzuzo.

**Qhuba**

1. Abafundi bacinga ngeengxaki abafuna ukuzisombulula.
1. Abafundi bayila into entsha ejolise ukusombulula ingxaki.
1. Abafundi bazoba umfanekiso wento abayilileyo kwaye babhale amanqaku aphambili okucacisa ingcamango yabo.
1. Amaqela azilolonga indlela aza kuchaza ngayo ingcamango yawo.
1. Abafundi bazisa into abayilileyo kutitshala okanye eklasini ngeentetho ezimfutshane.

**Inqaku lokujonga**

1. Hamba ujikeleze ngexesha lokucwangcisa ukuqinisekisa ukuba iingcamango zicacile kwaye zinokwenzeka.
1. Buza imibuzo ekhokelela njengokuthi “Ngubani oza kuyisebenzisa le nto?” kunye “Kutheni le nto ingcono kunale ikhoyo ngoku?”

**Ingqwalaselo**

* Emva kwezentetho, buza abafundi ukuba zeziphi izinto ezintsha abazifumene zinomdla kakhulu.
* Buza ukuba zeziphi iingxaki ezibonakale zibalulekile ukusombulula.
* Buza ukuba yintoni eyenze intetho yakholeleka.

##### **UKULANDELA**

_Imizuzu emi-5 ukuya kweli-10_

1. Phonononga ukuba itekhnoloji iyaguquka ukusombulula iingxaki.
1. Xoxa ngendlela uqikelelo olunokuthi lube luchanekile okanye lungachanekanga ngokuhamba kwexesha.
1. Qhagamshela kwiintsingiselo zobuchule bokwenyani kunye nendlela iingcamango eziphuculwa ngayo ngempendulo.

##### **VALA**

1. Qokelela imizobo kunye nezixhobo.
1. Cwangcisa igumbi lokufundela kwakhona.
1. Bulela abafundi ngobuchule babo nomzamo abawubekileyo.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela: “Yonke into entsha iqala njengengcamango umntu owaba nesibindi sokuyabelana ngayo.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

##### **Ulawulo lwegumbi lokufundela**

* Gcina ixesha lentetho lifane.
* Khuthaza ukuva ngentlonipho kunye nemibuzo.

##### **Ulwandiso & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Guqula intetho ibe ngumsebenzi wokubhala.
* Yongeza iindidi zokuvota ezifana neyona iluncedo, eyona inomtsalane, okanye eyona inokwenzeka.

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: gxila ekuzobeni nasekuchazeni lula.
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: inkxaso ebonakalayo, intetho ngababini.
* Ukhuseleko: umsebenzi owenziwa uhleli, akukho msebenzi wokwakha ngokwasemzimbeni ofunekayo.
