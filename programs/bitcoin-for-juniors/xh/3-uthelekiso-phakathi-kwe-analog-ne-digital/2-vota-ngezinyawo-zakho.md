# 3.2 Vota Ngezinyawo Zakho

**Uhlobo lomsebenzi** Ingxoxo-mpikiswano - Ukwenza izigqibo ngokuhamba

**Ixesha elithathwayo** 20-25 imizuzu

**Ukuhlulwa kweqela** Ikilasi lonke, likhokelwa ngutitshala, ukwahlulwa kwamaqela kuyazikhethela xa kufunwa ukwandisa umsebenzi

##### **Inkcazo**

Abafundi bazibeka ngokwasemzimbeni kwigumbi lokufundela ukuze babonise ukuba bakhetha itekhnoloji endala okanye entsha. Ngokuhamba, ukuxoxa, kunye nokuchaza izizathu zabo, abafundi bathelekisa izixhobo zedijithali neze-analogue, bacinga ngezinto ezilungileyo nezimbi, baze baqhelise indlela yokungavumelani ngentlonipho. Lo msebenzi uqinisekisa ukufunda kwangaphambili malunga nendlela itekhnoloji etshintsha ngayo, ngelixa unyusa umdla ngokuhamba.

##### **Iziphumo zokufunda**

Ekupheleni komsebenzi, abafundi baza:

* Bathelekise itekhnoloji yakudala kunye nentsha baze bachaze izinto abazikhethayo
* Bachonge izinto eziluncedo nezimbi kwizixhobo ezahlukeneyo
* Baqulunqe izimvo zabo baze bamamele abanye
* Bathethelele ingxoxo ngokuhamba kunye nengxoxo-mpikiswano

##### **Izixhobo**

* Amaphepha omsebenzi agqityiweyo othelekiso lwe-Analogue vs Digital (_Jonga 3.1 - Utshintsho lweTekhnoloji_)
* Iilebhile okanye iimpawu ozikhethayo ezithi OLD, CENTER, NEW
* Iibhidi okanye iinkwenkwezi zokuvuza abaxoxayo (ozikhethayo)
* Iphepha lephepha elikhulu kunye neepeni zokubhala (ozikhethayo)

##### **Izixhobo ezifunekayo**

* Indawo ecacileyo kwigumbi lokufundela yokuhamba
* **Izinto eziluncedo ukuba zikhona**: Isibali-xesha okanye intsimbi yokutshintsha imisebenzi

#### **INKQUBO**

##### **ISIQALO**

1. Qinisekisa ukuba abafundi bagqibe umsebenzi wokuthelekisa i-Analogue vs Digital.
1. Cela abafundi bakhuphe baze bajonge ngokufutshane izinto abazidibanisileyo.
1. Cacisa injongo yomsebenzi: “Ngoku siza kwabelana ngezimvo zethu malunga netekhnoloji yakudala kunye nentsha.”
1. Gxininisa ukuba akukho mpendulo ichanekileyo okanye engachanekanga.

##### **PHAMBI KOMSEBENZI**

_3-5 imizuzu_

1. Qhuba ingxoxo emfutshane yeklasi yonke malunga nokuba itekhnoloji entsha isoloko ingcono na.
1. Buza ukuba yintoni itekhnoloji yakudala eyenzayo kakuhle kwaye yintoni enokulahleka xa kusetyenziswa entsha.
1. Khetha isibini seetekhnoloji ezifana nomatshini wokubhala xa kuthelekiswa nelaptop okanye ihashe kunye nenqwelo xa kuthelekiswa nemoto.
1. Mema abafundi ukuba bachaze izinto ezilungileyo nezimbi kwindlela nganye.
1. Cela bonke ukuba baphakamise izandla ukubonisa into abayikhethayo ekuqaleni.

**Injongo Ngamazwi Omfundi**

*  “Bonisa ukuba yeyiphi itekhnoloji endiyithandayo kwaye ndichaze isizathu.”

##### **UMSEBENZI**

_10-15 imizuzu_

**Ulungiselelo**

1. Chonga iindawo ezintathu kwigumbi lokufundela ezineelebhile ezithi OLD, CENTER, NEW.
1. Qinisekisa ukuba imida icacile kwaye imithetho yokuhamba iyaziwa.

**Bonisa**

1. Bonisa indlela yokwenza usebenzisa umzekelo olula njengencwadi xa ithelekiswa ne-imeyile.
1. Bonisa indlela yokuhamba uye kwicala elithile uze unike ingcaciso emfutshane.
1. Qinisa umthetho wokumamela ngentlonipho nokungavumelani.

**Phumeza**

1. Funda ngokuvakalayo isibini sobuchwepheshe.
1. Bala phantsi ngokucacileyo uze uthi “QALA.”
1. Abafundi bahamba baye kwi-OLD, NEW, okanye CENTER ngokokuthanda kwabo.
1. Mema umfundi omnye okanye ababini kwicala ngalinye ukuba bachaze isizathu sokukhetha kwabo.
1. Khuthaza ukungavumelani ngentlonipho kunye nokunika izizathu.
1. Cela bonke abafundi babuyele kwi-CENTER.
1. Phinda usebenzise izibini ezintsha zobuchwepheshe imijikelo emine ukuya kwesithandathu okanye de umdla uqhubeke uphezulu.

**Inqaku lokujonga**

1. Qaphela ukuba abafundi bayakwazi na ukunika izizathu ezingaphezulu kokuthi bathanda nje.
1. Yima ukuze ucacise ukungaqondani ngobuchwepheshe xa kuyimfuneko.



**Ingqwalasela**

* Buza ukuba ngaba kukho umntu otshintshe ingqondo emva kokuphulaphula abanye.
* Buza ukuba yeyiphi isibini sobuchwepheshe esinzima kakhulu ukukhetha.
* Qinisekisa ukuba nokuba abantu baneminqweno eyahlukeneyo, konke kusemthethweni.

##### **LANDISELA**

_Imizuzu emi-3-5_

* Phonononga ukuba itekhnoloji iyatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha kwaye isebenza kwiimfuno ezahlukeneyo.
* Gxininisa ukuba zombini izixhobo ezindala nezintsha zineenzuzo.
* Qhagamshela ingxoxo kwiisifundo ezizayo malunga nokuqamba izinto ezintsha kunye nokuzivumelanisa.

Olu lwandiso lunokubandakanya iingxoxo ezimfutshane, ukunika abafundi izimvo zokuxhasa, okanye ukwenza itshathi yokuvota yeklasi.

##### **VALA**

_Imizuzu emi-2_

1. Cela abafundi babuyele ezihlalweni zabo.
1. Susa iimpawu uze ulungise igumbi lokufundela kwakhona.
1. Bulela abafundi ngokuthatha inxaxheba ngentlonipho.
1. Umyalezo wokugqibela kubafundi: “Itekhnoloji nganye inexesha nendawo yayo, kwaye okubalulekileyo kukuba siyiqonde kwaye siyisebenzise njani izixhobo zethu.”

#### **AMANQAKU**

* Sebenzisa esi sifundo ukukhuthaza ukucinga okunzulu _ngaphandle kokugweba_
* Linganisela umdla kunye nokugxila - khuthaza intshukumo yomzimba ngelixa ugcina ulandelelwano kwigumbi lokufundela
* Cinga ngokusebenzisa imifanekiso yokwenyani okanye eprintiweyo yezinto ukunceda abafundi abafunda ngamehlo
* Lo msebenzi unokukhokelela kwisifundo esizayo malunga _nokuqamba izinto ezintsha_, _utshintsho ngokuhamba kwexesha_, okanye _indlela abantu abazivumelanisa ngayo nezixhobo ezintsha_

##### **Ulawulo lwegumbi lokufundela**

* Qinisekisa intshukumo ekhuselekileyo kunye nemida ecacileyo
* Qwalasela abafundi abathe cwaka uze ubameme ukuba bathethe

##### **Ukwandiswa & Imisebenzi yokuzalisa ixesha**

* Ibar graph ebonakalayo yokuthanda kweklasi
* Ingqwalasela kwidayari malunga netekhnoloji abayithandayo
* Endaweni yokukhetha, nikezela abafundi itekhnoloji yokuyixhasa _ngayo_
* Yenza igrafu ye-bar yeklasi okanye iposta

##### **Ukwahlula-hlula**

* Abafundi abancinane: imijikelo embalwa kunye neengcaciso ezilula.
* Abafundi abadala: Mema amavolontiya ayi-1–2 kwicala ngalinye ukuba “bakhethe” isizathu sokhetho lwabo
* ELL/Ukufikeleleka: imiqondiso ebonakalayo, umzekelo, kunye nesiqalo semigca.
* Ukhuseleko: Ukhuseleko: akukho kugijima kunye nemithetho ecacileyo yokuhamba.
