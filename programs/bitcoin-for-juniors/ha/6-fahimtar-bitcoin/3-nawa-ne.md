# 6.3 Nawa ne!

**Nau'in Ayyuka** Kwaikwayo • Wasan Tuna • Gasar Ƙungiya

**Tsawon Lokaci** Minti 45

**Rarrabawa** Ƙananan Ƙungiyoyi (dalibai 3–4) + Lokutan Gaba ɗaya Aji

##### **Bayani**

A cikin wannan aikin, masu koyo za su kwaikwayi hakar Bitcoin ta amfani da katunan wasa a matsayin hash da kalmomin ƙamus a matsayin mu'amaloli. Ƙungiyoyi za su juya katuna don nemo katuna biyu masu daidaito. Idan sun sami katuna biyu iri ɗaya, za su ce “It’s mine.” Idan sun sami daidaitaccen nau'i, za su sami damar shiga mempool, inda za su ɗauki kalmar ƙamus ɗaya daga Bitcoin. Ana haɗa kalmomi cikin blocks kuma za su ƙidaya ne bayan an tabbatar da su. Yawan katuna a cikin mining pool yana nuna wahala kuma ana daidaita shi tsakanin zagaye. Ana ƙididdige maki na ƙarshe ta ƙirga jimillar haruffa a cikin kalmomin da aka tabbatar da aka adana a cikin wallet na kowace ƙungiya.

##### **Abubuwan da Za a Koya**

A ƙarshen wannan aikin, masu koyo za su:

* Bayyana cewa hakar na buƙatar ƙoƙari da yawa
* Bayyana yadda wahala za ta iya ƙaruwa ko raguwa
* Fahimta cewa ana haɗa mu'amaloli cikin blocks
* Gane cewa tabbatarwa tana zuwa kafin mallaka
* Hada ƙoƙari da lada

##### **Kayan Aiki**

* Katunan wasa 1–2
* Katunan kalmomin ƙamus na Bitcoin guda 40–60 (_Duba Ƙari A_)
* Envelope ɗaya ga kowace ƙungiya mai lakabin WALLET
* Babban envelope ɗaya mai lakabin BLOCK
* Alamomi masu lakabi MEMPOOL da MINING POOL
* **Abubuwan da za su ƙara daɗi:** Stickers ko stamps don tabbatarwa, Allon rubutu don rubuta maki

##### **Kayan Aiki**

* Agogo mai ƙidayar lokaci
* Tebur ko fili a ƙasa don shimfiɗa katuna
* **Abubuwan da za su ƙara daɗi**: Ƙarar kararrawa ko sauti don kammala block

#### **HANYAR AIKI**

##### **BUDEWA**

1.  Duba muhimman kalmomi a taƙaice: hash, mine, block, wallet, difficulty.
1. Ka takaita bayanai kuma ka bayyana su a fili.
1. Bayyana cewa yau dole ne su “nuna aiki” kafin su sami kalmomi.

##### **KAFIN AIKI**

_Minti 5_

1. Shimfiɗa katunan wasa 20 a fuska ƙasa a cikin grid. Bayyana: Katuna biyu iri ɗaya = daidaitaccen hash.
1. Idan ka sami daidaituwa, ka faɗi a fili: “It’s mine.”
1. Nuna yadda ake juya katuna biyu da mayar da su idan ba su dace ba.

**Manufa a Cikin Maganar Dalibi**

*  “Na samo katuna biyu masu daidaito kuma na ce ‘It’s mine’ don samun kalma ga wallet ɗina.”

##### **AIKI**

_Minti 30_

**Shiri**

1. Raba dalibai zuwa ƙungiyoyi na 3–4.
1. Ba wa kowace ƙungiya envelope ɗaya mai WALLET.
1. Shimfiɗa duk katunan kalmomi a fuska ƙasa a yankin MEMPOOL.
1. Ajiye envelope ɗin BLOCK a gaba.

**Aiwana**

1. Yi amfani da kati 20 a cikin tafkin hakar ma'adinai.
1. Kungiyoyi suna juyawa suna juya kati biyu-biyu.
1. Idan sun dace, suna cewa “Na samu.”
1. Suna tattara kalma daya daga mempool.
1. Dole ne su karanta ta daidai kafin su rike ta na ɗan lokaci.
1. Bayan mintuna 6–8, dakatar da zagaye.
1. Idan an hako kalmomi da yawa, ƙara wahala ta hanyar ƙara ƙarin kati. Idan an hako kaɗan, rage wahala ta hanyar cire kati.
1. Fada a saukake: “Idan hakar ma'adinai ya yi sauri, muna ƙara wahala. Idan ya yi jinkiri, muna sauƙaƙa shi.”

**Aiwana**

1. A ƙarshen kowanne zagaye, tattara duk kalmomin da aka samu a cikin kwali na BLOCK.
1. Bayyana “Block ya cika.”
1. Mayar da kalmomin da aka tabbatar ga walat ɗin kowace ƙungiya.
1. Kalmomin da aka tabbatar kawai ake lissafawa.

##### **BINCIKE**

_Mintuna 4_

* Me ya faru lokacin da muka ƙara kati?
* Shin ya fi wahala samun ma'aurata?
* Shin kun samu ƙasa da kalmomi?
* Shin dogayen kalmomi sun ba da maki mafi yawa?
* Shin kowa ya bi doka ɗaya?

##### **RUFEWA**

* Tattara kuma ƙirga duk katunan wasa kuma mayar da su cikin cikakkun dekuna.
* Tattara duk katunan kalmomi, fitar da walat, da kwali na block, sannan a sake jera su da kyau.
* Tsaftace wurin hakar ma'adinai, goge allo, kuma duba ko akwai katunan da suka zube a ƙarƙashin tebura da kujeru.

#### **LURA**

##### **Gudanar da aji**

* Ƙungiya ɗaya kawai ke juyawa a lokaci guda
* Kada a taɓa kati ba tare da izini ba
* Dole ne a karanta kalmomi a fili da kyau
* Dole ne a mayar da katunan da ba su dace ba da kyau

##### **Karin bayani & Ayyukan ƙari**

* Gabatar da taron rabi: bayan wani lokaci, kowanne hash mai inganci yana samun kowane kalma ta biyu kawai
* Nemi ma'auratan jan kati kawai don hash mai inganci
* Ƙara kari idan ƙungiya ta hako ma'aurata uku a zagaye guda

##### **Bambanci**

* Ƴan yara: Amfani da katunan kalmomi kai tsaye a cikin tafkin hakar ma'adinai na iya sauƙaƙa abubuwa.
* ELL/Na musamman: Ƙara hotuna a katunan kalmomi. Bada damar taimakon aboki wajen karantawa.
* Tsaro: Tabbatar da sarari a kusa da tafkin hakar ma'adinai. Kada a yi gudu
