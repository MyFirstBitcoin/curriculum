In this chapter, we will take a closer look at the technical side of Bitcoin. We will explain in simple terms the cryptography that underlies and secures the protocol and how transactions really work through the so-called UTXO model. We are going to cover some challenging and technical concepts in this cchapter, but don't worry! It's important to remember that many people don’t understand how the internet works, yet they are able to use it every day to send emails, contact friends on social media, and even pay their bills.

Learning the technical side Bitcoin is a long journey that not everyone may want to take, even if they decide to use it as money. While we do encourage you to keep learning about the technical aspects of Bitcoin, we will keep this chapter focused on basic key concepts.

As you saw in the video, the Bitcoin network is simply a record of transactions stored on multiple computers called nodes. The Bitcoin ledger is pseudonymous, meaning it doesn't have personal details such as name, age, etc. — only transaction and address information. The ledger shows every bitcoin and its movements since the blockchain started on January 3rd, 2009.
