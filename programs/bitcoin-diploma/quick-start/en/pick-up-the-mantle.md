# Pick up the mantle

This redesign gives educators more structure where consistency matters and more room where local wisdom matters. Your careful facilitation turns the Diploma from a book into a shared learning experience: one that helps people think clearly about money, Bitcoin, responsibility, and their own community.

Keep learning with the [Educator Training Program](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/educator-training-program/), connect through the [My First Bitcoin Education Network](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about/network), and contribute corrections, translation improvements, or local learning resources through the [official contribution form](https://forms.myfirstbitcoin.org/). The open-source [curriculum repository](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/mfb-curriculum) is the place to check current editions and participate in improving the work.
