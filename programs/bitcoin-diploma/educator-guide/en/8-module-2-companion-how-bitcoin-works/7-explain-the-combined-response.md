# 8.7 Explain the combined response

Use a five-part map:

1. A wallet uses a private key to authorize a transaction with a digital signature.
1. The transaction is broadcast through a peer-to-peer network.
1. Miners collect transactions and use proof of work to propose blocks.
1. Nodes independently check transactions, blocks, proof of work, and issuance rules.
1. Participants converge on the valid history with the most accumulated work.

No single part creates Bitcoin's credible scarcity alone. Avoid saying “the blockchain solves everything.”
