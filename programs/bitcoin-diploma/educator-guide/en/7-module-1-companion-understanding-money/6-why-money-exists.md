# 7.6 Why money exists

Keep the explanation grounded in coordination:

* people specialize;
* direct exchange requires matching wants;
* commonly accepted money separates selling from buying;
* a shared unit helps people compare and record value; and
* money can move purchasing power across time.

Ask:

> Why did money come into existence? Which exchange or coordination problem does it help solve?

A strong beginner answer does not need a universal theory of monetary origins. It should explain why a commonly accepted medium makes exchange easier.

Avoid saying all money began through one historical process. The course presents a useful pattern, not one identical global sequence.
