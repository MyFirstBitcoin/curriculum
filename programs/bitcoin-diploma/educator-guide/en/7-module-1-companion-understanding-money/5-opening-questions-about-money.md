# 7.5 Opening questions about money

Ask learners to answer the five questions in the Student Guide before you explain the course position. The answers create a baseline, not a test.

Facilitate in this order:

1. Give quiet writing time.
1. Let learners choose one answer to discuss in pairs.
1. Ask the group which statements are factual and which are judgments.
1. Record questions and contradictions without resolving all of them.
1. Tell learners they will return to the same questions after Module 4.

Listen for hidden definitions. If a learner says money is “anything valuable,” ask whether every valuable object is normally accepted, used for pricing, or carried into the future.

Do not ask learners to disclose income, debt, savings, banking status, or holdings.
