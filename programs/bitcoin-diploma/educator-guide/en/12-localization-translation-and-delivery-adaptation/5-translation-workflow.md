# 12.5 Translation workflow

Use this sequence:

1. Draft and approve the English learning meaning.
1. Edit for beginner clarity, short sentences, direct voice, consistent terminology, and low ambiguity.
1. Identify terms requiring a translator note or local replacement.
1. Translate the meaning, not English word order.
1. Localize examples only where authorized.
1. Review the translation with a language specialist and a subject-matter educator.
1. Test difficult safety instructions with learners.
1. Confirm that objectives, task difficulty, rubrics, and stop conditions remain equivalent.
1. Approve the language version before publication or certification use.

Avoid idioms and metaphors that cannot travel cleanly. If using an analogy, always state what it explains and what it does not.
