# 12.3 Legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory context

You may help learners recognize that these areas exist and identify where reliable local guidance is needed. You may explain a checked public rule at beginner level when competent to do so.

Do not:

* advise a learner how to structure a transaction for tax or legal advantage;
* tell a learner that a path is legal without jurisdiction-specific support;
* interpret an individual learner's obligations;
* encourage evasion or concealment;
* present a community practice as proof of compliance; or
* allow a guest to use the class as professional solicitation.

Use wording such as:

> Local rules may affect this path. The current public guidance we checked on [date] says ________. This is an educational summary, not advice for an individual situation. A qualified local professional or authority should answer case-specific questions.
