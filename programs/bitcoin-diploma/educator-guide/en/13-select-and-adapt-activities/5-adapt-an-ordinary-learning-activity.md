# 13.5 Adapt an ordinary learning activity

You may change:

* names, places, objects, currency units, and local payment methods;
* group size and roles;
* oral, written, visual, or tactile response format;
* live tool versus prepared screen;
* timing and scaffolding; and
* the debrief example.

Preserve:

* the concept being learned;
* the safety boundary;
* the learner action;
* the critical comparison or decision; and
* the connection to the module objective.
