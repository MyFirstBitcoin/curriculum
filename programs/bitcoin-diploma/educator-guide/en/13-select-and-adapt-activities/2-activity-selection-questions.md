# 13.2 Activity selection questions

Before choosing an activity, ask:

1. Which stable objective ID does it serve?
1. What will learners think, say, decide, or do?
1. Does the experience reveal the concept or distract from it?
1. How much setup and debrief time is needed?
1. What misconception could the activity create?
1. Can every learner participate accessibly?
1. What private or financial information could appear?
1. What happens without internet, video, or a personal device?
1. Has the activity been piloted for this learner context?
1. Is it approved for the intended assessment use?
