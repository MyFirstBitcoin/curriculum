# 3.2 Teach how to think, not what to think

Use one recurring reasoning sequence throughout the Diploma:

1. What is the claim or question?
1. Which terms need a shared definition?
1. What evidence can be checked?
1. What is opinion, preference, or inference?
1. What alternative should be compared?
1. What benefit and trade-off appear?
1. What remains uncertain or locally variable?
1. What would change your conclusion?

Do not reward slogans without understanding. “Fiat is broken,” “Bitcoin fixes this,” “not your keys, not your coins,” and “don't trust, verify” may open a discussion, but each must be unpacked into a specific claim, mechanism, condition, and trade-off.
