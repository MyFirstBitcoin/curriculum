# 3.3 Welcome disagreement and correct error

When a learner disagrees:

1. Ask them to state the claim precisely.
1. Ask what evidence or experience informs it.
1. Identify the shared factual question.
1. Compare the claim with the course definition or a checked source.
1. Separate what can be resolved from what remains a value judgment.
1. Invite the learner to revise, qualify, or retain their conclusion.

Correct factual errors clearly and respectfully. Do not avoid correction in the name of neutrality. Neutral facilitation means applying fair standards of evidence, not treating every statement as equally accurate.
