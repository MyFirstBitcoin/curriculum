# 9.13 Receiving bitcoin

Receiving still requires verification even though it does not authorize funds to leave.

Demonstrate:

1. Open the intended wallet.
1. Select **Receive**.
1. Confirm on-chain versus Lightning and the intended network.
1. Display or copy only the payment request.
1. Confirm the amount and any relevant fee arrangement.
1. Verify the pending payment in the receiving wallet or trusted view.
1. Interpret confirmation or payment status appropriately.

Explain that a message or screenshot saying “sent” is not proof of receipt. The receiver should verify within their own wallet or trusted view.

#### Practice: receive-screen walkthrough

Use printed screens or a training wallet. Ask learners to:

* locate the receive function;
* identify whether the request is on-chain or Lightning;
* point to what can be shared;
* explain what must remain secret;
* check the amount if present; and
* state how receipt will be verified.

For formal evidence, observe the learner individually under the approved task conditions.
