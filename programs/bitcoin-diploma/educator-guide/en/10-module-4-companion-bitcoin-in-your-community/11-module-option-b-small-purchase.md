# 10.11 Module option B: small purchase

**Purpose:** Practise a point-of-sale payment and compare on-chain and Lightning where locally appropriate.

**Presentation:** The merchant creates the correct request; the customer verifies recipient, payment type, amount, unit, and fee; both parties confirm the result.

**Practice:** Use a low-value item, test environment, or simulation. Include one failed or expired request.

**Production:** The learner completes the approved payment role and explains:

* why the selected rail fits the amount and timing;
* who controls the wallet;
* how receipt is confirmed;
* what happens if sender and merchant displays disagree; and
* when to stop.

**Debrief:** A fast payment is not automatically private, self-custodial, final in the same way, or suitable for higher value.
