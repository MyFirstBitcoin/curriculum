# 10.12 Module option C: payment for work or family remittance

**Purpose:** Map a payment from the payer's starting value to the recipient's usable outcome.

**Learner decisions:**

* Is the obligation priced in local currency, bitcoin, or sats?
* When is the exchange rate fixed?
* Who pays fees and spreads?
* Does the recipient want bitcoin or local currency?
* Who controls keys at each stage?
* Which path provides liquidity or conversion?
* Is either fiat payment reversible?
* What records or tax treatment may apply?
* What happens if a service delays or prevents withdrawal?
* Does the complete path improve cost, time, access, or control?

**Production:** Demonstrate or simulate the receive step and verify the final amount and status. Explain the main benefit, risk, and failure point.

**Safety:** Use an invented family or work case. Do not ask learners about their own wages, migration status, relatives, transfers, or tax affairs.
