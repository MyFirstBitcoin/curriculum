# 10.3 Begin with a need, not a tool

Ask first:

1. What real local problem or opportunity are we examining?
1. How is it handled now?
1. Which function of money is involved?
1. Could Bitcoin improve the complete path?
1. What new cost, custody, privacy, price, legal, operational, or safety risk appears?
1. Can learners examine or practise the path safely?
1. What evidence would show understanding and competence?

A valid conclusion may be that Bitcoin is not the best option for the case. The module assesses reasoning and safe performance, not adoption.
