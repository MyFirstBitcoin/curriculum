# 10.18 Strong Module 4 discussion questions

* What local need are we trying to solve?
* Which current option is the strongest comparison?
* Which function of money is involved?
* Is Bitcoin better after considering the complete route?
* Who controls the keys, accounts, and records at each stage?
* Who pays fees and bears exchange-rate risk?
* Which identity or transaction information becomes visible?
* Can the payment be reversed, disputed, or refunded?
* What happens when internet, electricity, wallet, service, or recipient fails?
* Which claim is globally stable and which is locally specific?
* Which fact requires a current authoritative source?
* Is participation genuinely voluntary?
* Does the design create dependence on one service or organizer?
* What would count as success besides transaction volume?
* What is the smallest safe next step?
