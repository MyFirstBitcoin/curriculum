# 10.14 Module option E: community donation

**Purpose:** Design a safe donation route for a nonprofit, classroom project, or simulated community group.

Learners decide:

* how donors verify the genuine payment request;
* whether receipt is on-chain, Lightning, or both;
* who controls the wallet and backup;
* who can authorize spending;
* what transparency is useful;
* which donor information should remain private;
* what records may be required;
* how a mistaken or fraudulent request is handled; and
* what happens if the custodian, device, or service fails.

**Production:** Demonstrate a verified sample donation request and explain custody, transparency, privacy, and the main failure point.

**Safety:** Do not collect real donations during an assessment unless an approved organizational process, consent, records, and legal review already exist. A simulation usually serves the learning objective better.
