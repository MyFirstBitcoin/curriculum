# 6.2 Demonstration safety protocol

Before a live tool demonstration:

1. Confirm the exact tool, version, source, network, custody model, and recovery method.
1. Run the full process privately on the same device and connection.
1. Decide whether funds are required. Prefer no funds; otherwise use an amount whose complete loss would remain a lesson, not a crisis.
1. Prepare the expected state and a recovery or reset process.
1. Define stop conditions.
1. Tell learners what information is public, private, or fictional.
1. Position screens so no unrelated notifications, contacts, balances, or accounts are displayed.
1. Prepare an offline or printed equivalent.
1. Stop if the interface, address, invoice, amount, network, or result differs materially from the checked process.
1. Clear temporary learner data after the session.
