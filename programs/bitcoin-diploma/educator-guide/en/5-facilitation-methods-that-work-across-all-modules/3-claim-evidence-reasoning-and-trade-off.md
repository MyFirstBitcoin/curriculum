# 5.3 Claim, evidence, reasoning, and trade-off

Write a claim where everyone can see it. Ask teams to complete:

* **Claim:** What exactly is being asserted?
* **Evidence:** What observation or reliable source would support or weaken it?
* **Reasoning:** How does the evidence connect to the claim?
* **Trade-off:** Which cost, limitation, or alternative must be included?
* **Uncertainty:** What is unknown, changing, or locally dependent?

This pattern works for claims about inflation, decentralization, energy, payment speed, privacy, adoption, CBDCs, and local tools.
