# 5.8 Debrief every activity

An activity does not teach itself. Debrief with four questions:

1. What happened?
1. Which course concept explains it?
1. What did the activity simplify or fail to show?
1. Where would this matter in a real or local situation?

Correct misleading effects created by the activity. A token bank-run game, for example, does not reproduce a real bank balance sheet or local regulation. A miner-and-node role-play does not simulate the network's full technical operation.
