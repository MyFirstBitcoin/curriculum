# 4.5 What to protect when time is tight

Protect, in this order:

1. safety boundaries;
1. required objectives and evidence;
1. learner explanation and questions;
1. supported practice;
1. review and connection across modules;
1. local context needed for accurate use; and
1. optional examples and technical depth.

Move optional history, extra analogies, extended debates, detailed mechanics, and Advanced questions to follow-up resources.
