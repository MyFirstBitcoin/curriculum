# 6.3 Saita Walat ɗin Wayar Hannu

#### Ayyuka: Kafa & Mayar da Wallet

Yanzu da mun fi fahimtar wallet na Bitcoin da bambance-bambancen su, za mu ga yadda ake amfani da daya a aikace. A wannan misalin, za mu ƙirƙiri wallet na wayar hannu kai tsaye a kan wayar mu. Idan ɗalibai ba su da wayar hannu, malam zai samar da daya don ɗalibai su yi amfani da ita. Akwai zaɓi biyu don wannan aikin.

##### Zaɓi na 1: Sauke Sabon Wallet

**Yadda ake ƙirƙira da amfani da wallet na Bitcoin:**

1. Nemo manhajar a cikin App Store (iOS) ko Google Play Store (Android)
1. Buɗe manhajar kuma zaɓi "Create a new wallet". Manhajarka za ta ƙirƙiri mabuɗin sirri ta atomatik.
1. Za a tambaye ka ka rubuta jerin kalmomi 12 zuwa 24 kuma ka adana su a wuri mai aminci. Wannan shine **recovery phrase** (wanda ake kira seed phrase ma): yana ba ka damar dawo da cikakken damar shiga kuɗinka idan ya zama dole.**Ka tuna cewa idan ka rasa ko ka manta da wannan jerin kalmomin, ba za ka iya samun damar shiga bitcoin ɗinka ba idan ka rasa wallet ɗinka. Haka kuma, idan wani ya samu recovery phrase ɗinka, zai iya samun damar shiga bitcoin ɗinka!**
1. Dole ne ka tabbatar cewa ka adana recovery phrase ɗinka. Don yin haka, dole ne ka shigar, a daidai wannan tsari, kalmomin seed phrase ɗinka.
1. A matsayin ƙarin matakin tsaro, wasu wallet suna ba ka damar zaɓar kalmar sirri mai ƙarfi.
1. Da zarar ka adana recovery phrase ɗinka, shiga wallet ɗin. Nema zaɓin "Receive": wallet ɗinka zai ƙirƙiri mabuɗin jama'a don karɓar bitcoin.
1. Canja wurin bitcoin zuwa wallet ɗinka. Da wallet mai zaman kansa, ba za ka iya siyan bitcoin kai tsaye da kuɗin fiat ba a kowane lokaci, don haka wataƙila sai ka fara siya daga kasuwa sannan ka canja wurin zuwa wallet ɗinka.

Ka ɗauki **public key** kamar **adireshin imel ɗinka**: kana iya raba wannan da wasu domin su turo maka bitcoin (ko, a misalin adireshin imel, su turo maka imel).

Ka ɗauki **private key** a matsayin **kalmar sirri** ta imel ɗinka: ba za ka raba wannan da kowa ba, domin zai ba su damar shiga imel ɗinka.

##### Zaɓi na 2: Mayar da Wallet

**Sauke wallet na Bitcoin kuma ƙara wasu sats ga kowane ɗalibi.**

Ba wa kowane ɗalibi takarda da **recovery phrase** don dawo da wallet.

**Jagoranci ɗalibai mataki-mataki:**

1. Da zarar ka fara wallet ɗinka, za ka ga hanyoyi uku na ƙirƙirar wallet, danna **[Import an existing wallet]**. Za ka ga allon gabatarwa, danna **[Restore with recovery phrase]**.
1. Shigar da recovery phrase ɗinka ɗaya bayan ɗaya, a daidai tsari.
1. Za ka ga saƙon tabbatarwa da zarar an shigo da wallet ɗinka cikin nasara. Kuɗin da aka dawo da su sun shirya don amfani!
