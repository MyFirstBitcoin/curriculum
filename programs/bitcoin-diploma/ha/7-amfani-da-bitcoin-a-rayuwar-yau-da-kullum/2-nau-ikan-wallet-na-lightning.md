# 7.2 Nau'ikan Wallet na Lightning

Wallet ɗin Lightning yana kama da wallet ɗin Bitcoin saboda yana ba ka damar aika da karɓar bitcoin. Babban bambanci shi ne yana aiki ne a kan Lightning Network, wani mataki na biyu da aka gina a saman Bitcoin. Kamar wallet ɗin Bitcoin, wallet ɗin Lightning suna da siffofi daban-daban da ya kamata a duba kafin zaɓar ɗaya.

#### Mallakar Kai da Mallakar Wani

Ana iya rarraba wallet ɗin Lightning ta hanyoyi da dama, amma don sauƙi, muna raba su zuwa nau'i biyu: mallakar kai da mallakar wani.  
Mallakar kai na wallet yana nufin kai kake da iko da makullanka. Mallakar wani na wallet yana nufin wani ne ke da ikon makullanka.  
Da wallet ɗin mallakar wani, za ka iya aika da karɓar kuɗi, amma kana dogara da wani kuma ka mika cikakken iko da kuɗinka don samun sauƙi. Wannan zai iya dacewa da ƙananan kuɗi, amma ana ba da shawarar amfani da wallet ɗin mallakar kai idan ka fahimci yadda yake aiki. Sauran wannan sashe zai mayar da hankali ne kawai kan wallet ɗin Lightning mallakar kai.

#### Buɗaɗɗen Tushen Lamba da Rufaffen Tushen Lamba

Wallet ɗin Lightning na iya zama buɗaɗɗen tushen lamba ko rufaffen tushen lamba. Ana fi son wallet ɗin buɗaɗɗen tushen lamba saboda lambarsu a fili take, kowa na iya duba ta, kuma al'umma na iya inganta ta.
