# 10.0 Gabatarwa

> Bitcoin ya fi zama kudi kawai; juyin juya hali ne da ke dawo da iko ga mutane, yana ba da ɗanɗano na zaman lafiya da 'yanci a cikin duniya mai ƙishin ƙarfafa mutane.  
_My First Bitcoin_

A cikin wannan babin na ƙarshe, za mu taƙaita darussan da muka koya a duk tafiyarmu, mu tambaya kuma mu tattauna wasu muhimman tambayoyi, sannan mu duba makomar Bitcoin.

Bitcoin ba fasaha kawai ba ce; hanyar sadarwa ce da ke samar da sabon nau'in kudi wanda babu wani mutum guda da zai iya canza adadinsa. Bil'adama ba ta taɓa samun nau'in kudi da adadinsa ke daure kuma babu wani cibiyar iko ba. Idan aka rungume shi sosai, Bitcoin kayan aiki ne da zai buɗe motsi don canji mai kyau wanda zai iya sauya rayuwar mutane a ko'ina cikin duniya. Yana wakiltar juyin juya hali cikin lumana zuwa ga 'yanci da adalci na kowa, yana buɗe sabbin damar ga bil'adama ta hanyar samar da tsarin kudi na bai daya, na duniya.

A matsayin tsarin duniya mara tsakiya, Bitcoin yana ba da damar 'yancin kudi mafi girma, yana sauya iko daga hannun ƙalilan zuwa ga jama'a da yawa. Yana samar da dandalin da ba a iya tsoma baki ko toshewa ba don adanawa da aika kudi, yana ƙarfafa mutane su ɗauki iko da dukiyarsu da kare ƙarfin sayensu. Wannan yana da matuƙar muhimmanci a wannan yanayin tattalin arziki mai cike da rashin tabbas, inda tsarin kudi na gargajiya ke fuskantar ƙalubale da ba a taɓa gani ba.

Bayan haka, za mu duba wani nau'in kudin dijital mai suna Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) kuma mu tantance yadda yake kama da Bitcoin da kuma bambance-bambancen da ke tsakaninsu.
