# 3.1 Tarihin Takaitaccen Kudin Fiat

| 1815-1933 | 1913 | 1933 | 1934 | 1944 | 1971 | 1980 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tsarin Zinariya | Kirkirar “Federal Reserve” | Dokar Shugaba 6102 | Dokar Adana Zinariya | Yarjejeniyar Bretton Woods | Nixon Shock | Kudinmu ya rasa kashi 96% na ƙimar sa |


A ƙarni na 19, al'ummomi da dama sun yi amfani da tsarin kuɗi mai ƙarfi wanda ya dogara da ƙarafa masu daraja kamar zinariya da azurfa. Ana daraja waɗannan ƙarafa saboda ƙarancinsu, ɗorewarsu, da kuma amincewa da su a ko'ina. Yayin da kasuwanci ya ƙaru, ɗaukar manyan adadin ƙarafa ya zama abin wahala. Bankuna suka fara adana zinariya da azurfa ga mutane, suna ba da takardun shaida na takarda da ke wakiltar adadin ƙarafa da aka ajiye.

A hankali, bankuna suka fara fitar da takardun shaida fiye da zinariya da suke da ita a ajiya. Wannan dabi'a, wadda aka fi sani da **bankin ajiya na rabo**, ta haifar da haɗarin **gudun banki**, inda mutane da dama ke ƙoƙarin cire zinariyarsu a lokaci guda. Don daidaita tsarin banki, gwamnatoci suka shiga cikin lamarin sosai.

A shekarar 1913, Amurka ta ƙirƙiri **Federal Reserve**, babban banki da zai iya fitar da sabon kuɗi da tallafawa bankunan da ke cikin matsala.

A shekarun 1930s, gwamnatin Amurka ta tilasta wa 'yan ƙasa su mika zinariyarsu a musanya da takardun kuɗi na takarda. Ba da daɗewa ba, gwamnati ta rage ƙimar dala idan aka kwatanta da zinariya, wanda hakan ya rage ƙarfin siyan da adana mutane ke da shi.

Bayan Yaƙin Duniya na Biyu, tsarin Bretton Woods ya ɗaure kuɗaɗen duniya da dala ta Amurka, kuma har yanzu ana iya musanya dala da zinariya. Wannan tsarin ya ƙare a 1971, lokacin da Amurka ta daina barin a musanya dala da zinariya. Tun daga lokacin, mafi yawan ƙasashe suna amfani da kuɗin fiat.


> **Definition – Ma'anar Kuɗin Fiat**
>
> **Kuɗin fiat** shine kuɗi wanda ba ya da tushe a kan wani abu na zahiri kamar zinariya. Maimakon haka, yana da ƙima ne saboda gwamnati ta ayyana shi a matsayin kuɗin doka kuma mutane suna yarda da karɓarsa wajen biyan kuɗi.
