# 3.0 Gabatarwa

> Tarihin ɗan adam tarihin kuɗi ne da ke rasa ƙima.  
_Milton Friedman_

Mun gani a cikin darasin da ya gabata yadda kuɗi suka samo asali a tsawon lokaci da yadda tsarin kuɗinmu ya canza daga kuɗi masu ƙarfi zuwa marasa ƙarfi, wanda hakan ya tsara duniyar da muke rayuwa a ciki yau. Wannan darasin zai zurfafa cikin yadda waɗannan abubuwa suka haifar da tsarin fiat na yau da yadda wannan tsarin fiat ke aiki.

###### To, ta yaya tsarin fiat da muke amfani da shi a yau ya samo asali?

Don fahimta, dole ne mu kalli dalar Amurka. Yau, dalar tana zama babban kuɗin ajiya na duniya, ma’ana ƙasashe da dama suna dogara da ita wajen kasuwanci, ajiya, da kwanciyar hankalin kuɗi. Saboda haka, duk wani yanke shawara game da dalar Amurka yana shafar tattalin arzikin ƙasashe a fadin duniya.

Don fahimtar yadda kuɗi ke aiki a ƙasarka, yana da amfani ka fahimci yadda tsarin fiat na zamani ya samo asali a Amurka.
