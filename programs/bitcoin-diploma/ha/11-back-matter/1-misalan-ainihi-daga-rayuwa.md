# Misalan ainihi daga rayuwa

#### Cibiyar Ilimi

A My First Bitcoin, mun gaskata da karfin al'umma da hadin kai don kawo ilimin Bitcoin zuwa kowane lungu da sako na duniya. Wannan ne yasa muka kirkiro da My First Bitcoin Education Network, wata sabuwar hanya da ke kokarin watsa ilimi ba tare da takura ba kuma ta karfafa al'ummomi na gida. Zuwa watan Afrilu 2026, tana da al'ummomi 80 daga kasashe fiye da 40.

Mu saurari labarinsu kuma mu koyi yadda wannan Bitcoin Diploma ta canza al'umma daga sassa daban-daban na duniya!

#### Nazari: Bitcoin Indonesia

![Bitcoin Indonesia](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/6acdaf25baed66b59fc2e5215a65aedf51301295-171x57.svg)

**Tun daga shekarar 2023, Bitcoin Indonesia tana sauya kasar a hankali. Diana, daya daga cikin masu kafa, ta bayyana yadda ilimin Bitcoin mai zaman kansa ke shafar yankin Asiya.**

A watan Satumba 2023, mun shirya taron Bitcoin Indonesia na farko. Bayan bako na karshe ya tafi kuma an tattara kujeru, mun kalli juna muka gane: babu abin da ya rage a zahiri. Babu wani abu mai dorewa, babu ci gaba da ilimi. Saboda haka wasu daga cikinmu, 'yan Bitcoin na gida da abokai, muka yanke shawarar muna so mu samu taro irin wannan kullum. Haka aka haifi Bitcoin Indonesia.

A watan Mayu 2024, mun bude cibiyar zahiri: Bitcoin House Bali. Anan muka fara koyar da al'ummarmu game da Bitcoin. Bayan kusan rabin shekara, muka hadu da tawagar My First Bitcoin lokacin da wani dan al'umma ya fassara manhajar. Da farko, mun yi shakku, mun yi tunanin za mu iya gina shirin da ya fi wannan kyau: Diploma din ya yi tsawo. Yanzu, mun yarda cewa Bitcoin Diploma hanya ce mai sauki a ilimin Bitcoin. Ta zama kamar man fetur ne. Bayan amfana da manhajar, shirin horar da malamai tare da Full Node da kuma Online School don ajin yanar gizo sun taimaka matuka! Mun shiga Node Network, sauran kuma tarihi ne.

Yanzu mun yaye dalibai fiye da 200 a Bali, Bandung, da Surabaya, kuma muna fatan fadada zuwa Jakarta, Karawang, jami’o’in gida biyu da sauran wurare nan gaba. Kuma wadanda suka kammala ba kawai 'dalibai' ba ne. Wasu daga cikinsu suna da kasuwancinsu kuma ya zama mai sauki a shigar da dalibai bayan yin aji kai tsaye. Wani mai gyaran babur yanzu yana karbar Bitcoin, da kuma wata kasuwar iyali a titin da ke sayar da Jamu, wani shayi na gargajiya na Bali. Haka kuma, wadanda suka kammala suna shirya nasu ajin ko kuma suna dawowa a matsayin malamai baƙi.

Mun mayar da manhajar tamu: mun fassara ta zuwa Bahasa, mun kara labarai game da faduwar kudin gida, Rupiah, kuma mun bayyana satoshis ta hanyar tattalin arzikin durian. Rupiah ba ta da kason cent ko penny, don haka muna kwatanta Bitcoin da durian: daya ne, amma yana da yanyan ciki da tsaba da yawa. A karshen kowanne aji, muna murnar kammala karatu a matsayin wani abu na musamman: da takardar shaidar kammala, jawabi, baloons, lambobin yabo, da barbecue. Domin idan wani ya zabi ya dauki makonni 10 yana koyon 'yancin kudi, hakan ya cancanci lada.

Ajinmu sun kunshi kowa: daga yara masu shekaru 15 zuwa dattijai, daga masu fasaha zuwa wadanda ba su taba mallakar kwamfuta ba. Wasu dalibai sun taba kokarin kashe kansu saboda damfarar shitcoin, amma bayan shiga shirin sun zama bangare na tawagarmu kuma suna yada labari. Wasu kuma sun rasa duk kudinsu a damfarar banki. Yanzu sun zama bangare na tattalin arzikin da ke zagaye da kansa. Abin mamaki ne ganin yadda dalibai, wadanda suka taso a kasa mai tsarin kwaminisanci ba tare da tunani mai zurfi ba, suka fara tattaunawa da juna game da abin da suke koya – maimakon karanta abin da ke cikin littafi kawai.

Karona na farko a rayuwata da nake jin aikina yana da tasiri na gaske! Wannan ne ke sa ni tashi kowace rana. Za ka ga haske a idanunsu, za ka ga sun sake samun fata na kyakkyawar makoma.

Babban abin da na koya da zan raba da sauran malamai shi ne a sanya darussa su zama masu dadi, su tayar da sha'awar dalibai. Ka fara da sauki, kada ka rikita abubuwa. Nuna dalibai dalilin da yasa Bitcoin ke da muhimmanci a gare su, kuma ka yi magana daga zuciya. Kuma kafin koyar da ajin yanar gizo, lallai ka fara da aji kai tsaye. Na koyi abubuwa da yawa daga koyarwa a zahiri da farko!

Muna yawan cewa shirin My First Bitcoin ya fi yadda kake zato. Mun yi matukar shakku a farko kuma mun yi tunanin ba za mu taba yi ba. Amma ya zama daya daga cikin mafi kyawun shawarwari ga aikin.

#### Nazari: The Core

![The Core](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/2d0906b2a4b51b46f0565a0536c8cf3ac6b4031a-1738x1511.png)

**Abin da ya fara a matsayin karamin rukuni na WhatsApp a Kenya ya zama gagarumin motsi na ilimin Bitcoin a duniya, yana shafar rayuka fiye da 300 a nahiyoyi daban-daban. Shirin ilimin Bitcoin mai suna The Core ya tabbatar da canza rayuwa. Wanda ya kafa, Felix Mukungu, ya raba tafiyarsa.**

Komai ya fara ne da wata mummunar kwarewa: wata damfara ta crypto da ta zama hanyar da ta bude min fahimtar Bitcoin. Wannan kwarewar ta nuna min cewa fasahar tana da karfi, amma an gabatar min da ita ta hanyar da ba daidai ba. Da farko, ban yi niyyar gina dandalin ilimi ba. Ina so ne kawai in fahimta, amma dukkan kayan karatu, labarai, podcasts, da bidiyo sun watse. Babu hanya daya, babu manhaja da za ta jagoranci mutane daga tarihin kudi zuwa makomar Bitcoin. Don haka na rubuta tawa: babi tara da batutuwa masu sauki, na tura ta sakon rubutu a WhatsApp inda kowa zai iya tambaya. Abin ya kasance mai sauki, amma mai karfi. Haka The Core ta fara: daga burin nuna wa mutane hanya madaidaiciya.

Daga baya, muka koma Google Classroom, muna gudanar da kiran waya sau biyu a mako ga karamin al'umma na dalibai 10. A wannan lokaci, na gano My First Bitcoin ta hannun wani dan Bitcoin daga Holland. Manhajarsu ita ce abin da nake nema: tsari, bayani mai sauki, kuma daidai da burina. Na shiga Node Network a Mayu 2023, na fara koyar da ajin Bitcoin Diploma na farko. Muna da masu rajista 30 da wadanda suka kammala 12. Daga baya na samu taimako daga malamai baƙi, misali Glenn daga Bitcoin Ubuntu.

A matsayin dandalin yanar gizo, mun ga Bitcoin Diploma da Online School sun taimaka matuka. Learning Management System (LMS) yana bamu damar saka bidiyo, slides, tambayoyi, aiki, da tattaunawa. Tsari ne na kwararru, dalibai kuma suna dauka da muhimmanci. Suna samun sanarwa a matsayin tunatarwa don kammala aiki. Bayan haka, General Assemblies tare da sauran nodes sun kasance masu karfafa gwiwa. Kana shiga ka ga abin da ke faruwa a sauran al'umma. Bayan taron wata-wata, kana fita da kwarin gwiwa. Hanyar sadarwa ta masu gini ta karfafa ci gaba.

Fiye da dalibai 500 sun bi ta cikin ajinmu tara. Muna baiwa dalibai sats, muna raba takardar shaidar kammala, kuma muna murnar kowanne mataki na tafiya da Bitcoin Pathfinders threads a X.

The Core ta zama ginshiki ga wasu ayyuka. Daga ajinmu na farko Rikto Xonghoti a India ya fito, tun daga lokacin fiye da ayyuka 10 sun taso, ciki har da Yes Bitcoin Haiti, Bitcoin School Kenya, da Kabul Bitcoin. A kowane hali: dalibai suna zama malamai! Wasu suna fara tattalin arzikin zagaye kamar Bitcoin Githurai, wanda wadanda suka koyi tare da mu a yanar gizo ke tallafawa.

Yawancin dalibai sun fito daga sassa daban-daban na Afirka—Najeriya, Kenya, Ghana, Zambia, da sauransu. Ajin farko sun cika da masu fasaha, amma yanzu, tasirin yana bayyana. Wadanda suka kammala suna kawo iyalansu, abokansu, makwabtansu. Na samu sakon daga wata 'ya mace da mahaifiyarta suna sayen Bitcoin tare. Ko labarin wata iyali gaba daya da suka shiga aji tare. Mahaifin da bai kammala aji ba a farko, amma ya sake shiga aji na gaba.

Babban abin da muka fi alfahari da shi? Kammala karatu a zahiri, wani taron gefe a lokacin African Bitcoin Conference, inda dalibai suka hadu da 'yan Bitcoin na duniya kai tsaye. Kuma samun tallafinmu na farko daga Human Rights Foundation babban ci gaba ne. Abin da ya fara da sakon WhatsApp, yanzu ya zama tawaga hudu da ke koyar da mutane daga ko'ina cikin nahiyar ta hanya madaidaiciya.

Shawara ta ga duk wanda ke koyar da Bitcoin Diploma? Ka fara da sauki. Ka zabi jinkirin lokaci. Ka nuna aikin da ka yi. Ka gina da farko, ka nema tallafi daga baya.
