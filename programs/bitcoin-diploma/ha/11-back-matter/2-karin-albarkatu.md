# Karin Albarkatu

#### 1. Me yasa a yi amfani da Bitcoin?

* **“The Bullish Case for Bitcoin” daga Vijay Boyapati**: Wannan labarin yana bayyana dalilan da yasa Bitcoin ke da muhimmanci a matsayin dukiya kuma me yasa zai iya zama kudin duniya na gaba. Marubucin ya tattauna fannoni na fasaha da tattalin arziki na Bitcoin da ke sa ya zama wata dama mai kyau don zuba jari.
* **“Why Bitcoin Matters” daga Aleks Svetski (1 awa)**: Wannan bidiyon yana bayyana muhimmancin Bitcoin a matsayin dukiyar dijital mai zaman kanta da yadda zai iya shafar tsarin kudi na yanzu. Mai magana yana bincika damar da Bitcoin ke da ita wajen kawo 'yancin kudi ga mutane a fadin duniya.
* **“Why Bitcoin” daga Wiz**: Wannan labarin yana ba da taƙaitaccen bayani game da amfanin amfani da Bitcoin a matsayin kudi da kuma ajiya na daraja. Yana haskaka yadda Bitcoin ke zaman kanta da yadda hakan ke ba da damar samun 'yancin kudi da tsaro.

#### 2. Menene Bitcoin?

* **“How Bitcoin Works under the Hood” daga CuriousInventor**: Wannan bidiyon yana ba da cikakken bayani game da yadda Bitcoin ke aiki daga bangaren fasaha.
* **“What Is Bitcoin” daga Greg Walker**: Wannan labarin yana ba da cikakken bayani game da menene bitcoin, tarihin sa, fasahar sa, da yadda yake bambanta da kudaden gargajiya.
* **“Bitcoin - The Genesis” daga RT (30 minti)**: Wannan bidiyon yana bayyana yadda aka kirkiro Bitcoin da kuma farkon kwanakin sa. Yana bincika dalilan da suka sa mahaliccin sirri, Satoshi Nakamoto, ya kirkiro Bitcoin da yadda ra'ayin ya samo asali.

#### 3. Karin Koyo

* **“The Bitcoin Standard” (1 awa 40 minti)**: Wannan littafin mai jiwuwa yana bincika yanayin tattalin arziki da tarihi da ya haifar da kirkirar Bitcoin. Yana bayyana amfanin kudin da ba shi da shugabanci daya da kuma damar da Bitcoin ke da ita wajen zama ma'aunin duniya.
* **“Intro to Bitcoin Austrian Thought” (1 awa)**: Wannan darasin mai jiwuwa yana bayyana makarantar tattalin arzikin Austria da yadda take da alaka da ra'ayin Bitcoin. Yana ba da zurfin fahimta game da ka'idojin tattalin arziki da ke bayan Bitcoin da yadda yake daidai da tunanin Austria.
* **“Bitcoin Babies”** daga Naomi Wambui - Twitter: @btcbabies - @ngachanaomi1 Wani PDF kyauta da ke nufin karfafa mata da ilimi mai mahimmanci game da abinci mai gina jiki, Bitcoin, da kuma lafiyar kwakwalwa gaba daya.
* **BTC Sessions**: Tashar YouTube mai koyarwa kan Bitcoin kawai tare da darussa da jagorori masu amfani.

#### 7. Sauran Kayan Aiki:

* **Bitcoin.org**: Shafin yanar gizo na hukuma na tsarin Bitcoin.
* **Bitcointalk.org**: Bitcointalk wata dandalin tattaunawa ce inda masu amfani ke tattauna batutuwan da suka shafi Bitcoin, tambaya, da raba bayanai. Wuri ne mai kyau don koyo daga sauran masoya da kwararrun Bitcoin.
* **Bitcoincore.org**: Wannan shi ne asalin manhajar Bitcoin kuma har yanzu ana amfani da ita sosai daga masu amfani da masu haɓakawa. Yana samar da kayan aiki masu ƙarfi don hulɗa da hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin da gina aikace-aikacen Bitcoin.
* **Bitcoinwiki.org**: Wannan wata hanyar al'umma ce da ke samar da cikakken jagora game da duk abin da ya shafi Bitcoin. Yana rufe komai, daga fasahar Bitcoin zuwa tarihinsa da hanyoyin amfani da shi.
* **Bitcoinmagazine.com**: Wannan wata mujallar yanar gizo ce da ke kawo labarai da fahimta game da Bitcoin da sauran kudaden dijital. Hanya ce mai kyau don kasancewa da sabbin abubuwa a duniyar Bitcoin.
* **Bitcoin.Design**: Wani kundin fayilolin zane-zanen Bitcoin na buda-tushen don zane, shafukan yanar gizo, samfuri, da gumaka.
* **Yzer**: Sauki, wayar hannu, ilimin Bitcoin. Koyi game da Bitcoin, kudi, tattalin arziki, kuma sami Sats.
* **NOSTR**: Kafafen sada zumunta inda kai kake mallakar bayananka.
* **Simple X**: Wata manhaja mai zaman kanta, mai zaman kanta ta hanyar sadarwa.
* **Saita Node na Bitcoin**: Raspberry Pi DIY daga Keith Mukai
* **Yadda ake zaɓar walat ɗin Bitcoin**: Yi amfani da sabuwar iliminka don zaɓar walat ɗin da ya dace da kai.
* **BitcoinIcons.com**: Tarin gumakan Bitcoin kyauta.
* **Bitcoin Ga Kasuwancin Gida**: Kundin takardun talla don taimaka maka yada amfanin Bitcoin ga kasuwancin gida da kake so.
* **Mempool.Space**: Wani aikin buda-tushen Mempool wanda kuma ke nuna bayanai da zane-zanen hanyar sadarwar Lightning.
