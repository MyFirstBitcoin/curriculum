# Muhimman Ka'idoji

#### Module 1

* **Gabatarwar Kwasa-kwasan**: Bincika manufofi da tsammanin wannan kwas din My First Bitcoin Diploma.
* **Tattaunawar Aji — Tambayoyi Biyar Akan Kudi**: Shiga cikin wani aikin tunani ta hanyar amsa tambayoyi biyar masu muhimmanci game da kudi.
* **Fahimtar Kudi**
* **Ayyuka, Halaye, da Nau'ikan Kudi**
* **Tattaunawar Aji — Dalilin Da Yasa Muke Bukatar Kudi**
* **Hankalin Dan Adam Game da Kudi**

#### Module 2

* **Gabatarwa ga Tarihi da Ci gaban Kudi**: Bincika tarihin da ci gaban kudi. Fahimci yadda tsoffin hanyoyin kasuwanci suka kai ga kirkirar kudin da muke amfani da shi a yau.
* **Aikin Wasan Musanya**: Shiga cikin wani wasan musanya kai tsaye don fahimtar kalubalen musayar kai tsaye da kuma gane bukatar tsarin da ya fi dacewa.
* **Ci gaban Kudi**: Bincika yadda aka koma daga tsoffin nau'ikan kudi kamar kwallaye da zobba zuwa fitowar tsabar kudi da takardun kudi. Bi tafiyar daga takarda zuwa filastik, don fahimtar yadda kudi ya canza a tarihi.
* **Juyin Juya Halin Kudin Dijital**

#### Module 3

* **Asalin Kudin Fiat**: Bincika asalin kudin fiat ta hanyar duba tarihin sa a takaice, da fahimtar yadda ya zama mafi rinjaye a matsayin kudi.
* **Aikin Bankin Ajiyar Kashi**: Shiga cikin aikin Bankin Ajiyar Kashi don fahimtar yadda wannan tsari ke aiki, tare da haskaka dogararsa ga bashi da tasirinsa ga tattalin arziki gaba daya.
* **Tsarin Fiat**: Fahimci muhimman abubuwan tsarin fiat, ciki har da yadda yake a matsayin tsarin kudi na doka, rawar da bankin ajiyar kashi ke takawa, da manyan masu iko a wannan tsari.
* **Kudin Dijital na Babban Banki (CBDCs)**: Bincika yadda Kudin Dijital na Babban Banki (CBDCs) ke sauya yanayin kudi da kuma yiwuwar tasirinsu a makomar kudi.

#### Module 4

* **Raguwar Karfin Siyan Kudi**: Fahimci ma'anar hauhawar farashi da tasirinta ga karfin siyan kudi. Shiga cikin Aikin Gasa: Tasirin Hauhawar Farashi don ganin tasirin kai tsaye.
* **Aikin Tasirin Tsarin Fiat**: Shiga cikin aikin Tasirin Tsarin Fiat, don fahimtar illolin tsarin kudi na yanzu gaba daya.
* **Tarin Bashin Duniya da Bambancin Zamani**: Bincika tasirin tarin bashin duniya da bambancin zamantakewa. Gane illolin da ke shafar mutum da al'umma, musamman raguwar karfin siyan kudi da karuwar gibin arziki.
* **Cypherpunks da Tsarin Raba-gida**: Koyi labarin Cypherpunks da dalilansu na neman kudin da ba a karkashin iko daya. Bambanta tsakanin tsarin da aka tara iko da tsarin da aka raba iko, tare da fahimta daga tarihin kudin dijital a takaice.

#### Module 5

* **Satoshi Nakamoto da Kirkirar Bitcoin**: Bincika mutumin Satoshi Nakamoto da labarin asalin Bitcoin, tare da fahimtar dalilan farko na kirkirarsa.
* **Aikin Aji — Gina Matsaya Daya**: Shiga cikin aikin Gina Matsaya Daya a cikin hanyar sadarwa ta Peer-to-Peer don fahimtar yadda ake cimma matsaya daya a cikin hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin.
* **Bitcoin a Matsayin Kudi Mai Karfi na Dijital**: Duba rawar da Bitcoin ke takawa a matsayin kudi mai karfi na dijital, tattauna ci gabansa, ayyuka da halayensa, sannan ku shiga tattaunawar aji kan ko Bitcoin na da halayen kudi mai karfi.
* **Yadda Bitcoin Ke Aiki**: Dubi yadda Bitcoin ke aiki, ciki har da tsarin matsaya na Nakamoto. Gane manyan masu ruwa da tsaki a hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin, kamar masu hakar ma'adinai, nodes, masu amfani, masu haɓakawa, da ayyuka, da fahimtar yadda suke aiki tare.
* **Karbar Nauyin Kai**: Jaddada ma'anar daukar nauyin kai a cikin tsarin Bitcoin, tare da karfafa fahimtar rawar mutum da alhakin kowa a cikin tsarin da aka raba iko.

#### Module 6

* **Samun Bitcoin**: Bincika hanyoyi kamar mu'amala kai tsaye da musayar kudi, tare da tattauna damuwar sirri da ke tattare da tsarin KYC.
* **Mu'amala Kai Tsaye (Peer-to-Peer)**: Yi hulɗa da ma'amaloli marasa tsakiya don fahimtar ainihin ka'idojin musayar Bitcoin.
* **Saita Wallet na Bitcoin**: Koyi matakan da suka wajaba don sauke, ƙirƙira makullai, da yin ajiyar wallet na Bitcoin don tabbatar da amintattun ma'amaloli.
* **Irin Wallet na Bitcoin**: Bambanta tsakanin wallet na buda tushen lamba, rufe tushen lamba, masu riƙe da kuɗi, da marasa riƙe da kuɗi, tare da fahimtar rawar da makullai ke takawa wajen tsaro.

#### Module 7

* **Gabatarwa zuwa Lightning Network**: Gane ci gaban Bitcoin ta hanyar fasahohi kamar Lightning Network, wanda ke ƙara masa ƙwarewa.
* **Saita Wallet na Lightning**: Koyi matakan da suka wajaba don saita wallet na Bitcoin Lightning, wanda ke sauƙaƙa ma'amaloli masu sauri da masu faɗi.
* **Aikin Hannu**: Yi hulɗa da gasar relay na wallet na Lightning a aikace, don ƙarfafa fahimta mai zurfi game da ma'amaloli a Lightning Network.
* **Irin Wallet na Lightning**: Bambanta tsakanin wallet na buda tushen lamba, rufe tushen lamba, masu riƙe da kuɗi, da marasa riƙe da kuɗi na Lightning don dacewa da buƙatun masu amfani daban-daban.
* **Ma'amaloli na Lightning**: Bincika yadda ake aikawa da karɓar ma'amaloli ta Lightning, tare da jaddada sauri da ingancin Lightning Network.

#### Module 8

* **Littafin Lissafin Bitcoin**: Fahimci ma'anar littafin lissafi mara tsakiya da nodes da miners ke kula da shi, wanda ke tabbatar da gaskiya da tsaro.
* **Makullai Jama'a da Masu Zaman Kansu**: Bincika muhimmancin tsaron lissafi a cikin ma'amalolin Bitcoin ta hanyar amfani da makullai jama'a da masu zaman kansu, tare da aiki da ke nuna SHA 256 hashing.
* **Samfurin UTXO**: Fahimci samfurin Unspent Transaction Output a matsayin ginshiƙi na tsarin ma'amalolin Bitcoin.

#### Module 9

* **Nodes da Miners na Bitcoin**: Duba rawar nodes da miners wajen kula da hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin, ciki har da batutuwa kamar fitarwa, ƙaranci, rabi, da wahala.
* **Mempool**: Bincika blockchain na Bitcoin ta hanyar mempool kuma sami ƙwarewa a aikace ta hanyar aikin mempool.space.
* **Yadda Ma'amalolin Bitcoin ke Aiki**: Samu fahimta game da dukkan matakan rayuwar ma'amalar Bitcoin, daga mai aikawa, mai karɓa, nodes, miners, da mempool.

#### Module 10

* **Makomar Bitcoin**: Shiga zurfi cikin yiwuwar ci gaban Bitcoin a nan gaba a matsayin kudin dijital mai sauyi.
* **Kudaden Dijital na Babban Banki**: Kwatanta tsarin maras tsakiya na Bitcoin da na CBDCs don fahimtar rawar Bitcoin wajen ƙarfafa ɗan adam.
* **Tushen Falsafar Bitcoin**: Bincika falsafar da ke bayan Bitcoin, fahimtar yadda ya samo asali a matsayin amsa ga ƙalubalen tattalin arziki, tare da mayar da hankali kan tasirinsa ga 'yancin kuɗi da yadda ya bambanta da kudade na gargajiya.
