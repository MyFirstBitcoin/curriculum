# 10.4 Ikamva Elinamandla

Imali inceda abantu ukuba banxibelelane malunga nezona zenzo, iimpahla, kunye neenkonzo zibalulekileyo kuluntu. Xa imali ilawulwa ngamagunya aphakathi, ngokuqinisekileyo iya kuphathwa gwenxa.

Enye yeempazamo abantu abasoloko beziphinda kwimbali kukungenelela kwimarike yasimahla, njengokuphathwa gwenxa kwemali, nto leyo echaphazela kakubi abantu ngabanye, iintsapho, amashishini, kwaye ekugqibeleni nempumelelo yoluntu kwihlabathi liphela.

Ngokuthabatha ulawulo lwemali ezandleni zamagunya aphakathi kwaye sisebenzise imali enobuninzi obusisigxina engenakutshintshwa naliliphi iqela, sinokudala ihlabathi elahlukileyo ngokupheleleyo — apho asisafuni ukuthembela kwizopolitiko ukuba benze into elungileyo, kuba ukwenza into engalunganga sele kubiza kakhulu kwaye kunzima.

Eli lihlaba elitsha ngokupheleleyo. Kwaye unako ukuba yinxalenye yokudala le nyani. Ngokusebenzisa iBitcoin, uqhuba eyakho i-node, kwaye unceda abanye abantu bafunde ngakumbi ngekamva lemali, uvota kwelinye ihlabathi.
