# 10.0 Intshayelelo

> I-Bitcoin ayisiyongemali kuphela; yinguqu enkulu ebuyisela amandla ebantwini, inika incasa yoxolo nenkululeko kwihlabathi elilambileyo lamandla.  
_My First Bitcoin_

Kule modyuli yokugqibela, siza kushwankathela izifundo esizifundileyo kulo hambo lwethu, sibuze kwaye sixoxe ngemibuzo emininzi ebalulekileyo, kwaye siphonononge ikamva le-Bitcoin.

I-Bitcoin ayisiyotekhnoloji kuphela; yinkqubo edibanisa abantu enika uhlobo olutsha lwemali apho unikezelo lwayo alunakutshintshwa nangubani na. Uluntu alukaze lube nemali enikezelo olungatshintshiyo kwaye ingenalo ulawulo oluphakathi. Ukuba yamkelwe ngokubanzi, i-Bitcoin sisixhobo esinokuvula intshukumo yotshintsho oluhle olunokuguqula ubomi babantu kwihlabathi liphela. Imela uguquko oluxolileyo olukhokelela kwinkululeko kunye nokulingana, ivula amathuba amatsha kuluntu ngokudala inkqubo yemali esetyenziswa kwihlabathi liphela.

Njengenkqubo yehlabathi engaphantsi kolawulo lwabantu abaninzi, i-Bitcoin ivumela inkululeko enkulu kwezemali, iguqula amandla isuka kubambalwa iye kubaninzi. Inika iqonga elikhuselekileyo, elingavinjwanga lokugcina nokuthumela ixabiso, inika abantu amandla okulawula ubutyebi babo nokukhusela amandla okuthenga. Oku kubaluleke kakhulu kwimeko yezoqoqosho engazinzanga yanamhlanje, apho inkqubo yemali yesintu ijongene nemingeni engazange ibonwe ngaphambili.

Okulandelayo, siza kujonga olunye uhlobo lwemali yedijithali olubizwa ngokuba yi-Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) kwaye sihlalutye indlela efana ngayo ne-Bitcoin kunye nendlela eyahlukileyo ngayo.
