# 10.3 Iinzuzo zeBitcoin

**Hyperbitcoinization** ibhekisa kwikamva elicingelwayo apho iBitcoin iye yaba yinkqubo yemali ephambili kwihlabathi lonke. Oku kuthetha ukuba iBitcoin isetyenziswa ngumntu wonke, naphi na, nakuzo zonke izinto — ukusuka ekuthengeni ikofu ukuya ekuhlawuleni iibhili, kwanokuthenga ipropati. Umdla okhulayo kwiBitcoin phakathi kwabantu ngabanye, amashishini, kwanurhulumente ugqamisa impembelelo enokubakho yokwamkelwa kwayo ngokubanzi kuqoqosho lwehlabathi noluntu.

Nazi ezinye zeenzuzo zehlabathi elisebenza ngokupheleleyo ngeBitcoin:

###### Ikamva Lokuzimela Ngokwakho

Ikamva lokuzimela ngokwakho lithetha ukuba abantu kwihlabathi lonke banolawulo olupheleleyo kwisazisi sabo sedijithali kunye neempahla zabo. Oku kunokukhokelela ekubeni abantu abaninzi bafumane ithuba lokungena kwinkqubo yezezimali, inkululeko, ubumfihlo, nokhuseleko, ngaloo ndlela kuncedisa ekuphuculeni impilo yoluntu, ubutyebi, nolonwabo ngokubanzi.

###### Indlela Ethembekileyo Yokugcina Ixabiso

Ukungabikho kweBitcoin ngokwedijithali kuyenza ibe yeyona ndlela ithembekileyo yokugcina ixabiso ixesha elide, nto leyo ekhuthaza abantu abaninzi ukuba bayisebenzise njengendlela yokugcina imali yabo yekamva.

###### Utshintsho Kwinqununu-mali

Ukuba iBitcoin ingamkelwa ngokubanzi, inokususa amandla oorhulumente okwandisa inani lemali esetyenziswa ngokusebenzisa izixhobo zemali zemveli. Ukwamkelwa kweBitcoin ngokubanzi kunokunyusa amandla okuthenga kwabantu kwaye kukhuthaze uluntu ukuba lujolise ekucingeni ixesha elide kunokukhawuleza.

###### Ukucaca Nokulandeleka Okuphuculweyo

Irekhodi elingaguqukiyo nelingenakutshintshwa leentengiselwano zonke kwi-blockchain liza kunyusa ukucaca nokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo kumashishini nakwimimandla eyahlukeneyo. Okwangoku, amaziko anamandla anako ukuhambisa izigidi zeerandi kwihlabathi lonke ngaphandle kokubonakala ngokucacileyo ukuba ezi mali ziya phi okanye zisetyenziswa njani. Ngokubonelela ngerekhodi evulekileyo nethembekileyo yeentengiselwano zezezimali, iBitcoin ingaqinisekisa ukuba ukuhamba kwemali kuba kukhangeleka ngakumbi kuluntu.

###### Uguquko Kwimarike Yothumelo-mali

Imarike yothumelo-mali ibandakanya ukudluliselwa kwemali ukusuka kumntu omnye ukuya komnye, rhoqo phakathi kwamazwe. Nangona iindleko ziye zehla, ukuthumela imali kusebiza kakhulu xa kuthelekiswa nothutho lwemali lwasekhaya, ngakumbi kwiimali ezincinci. I-Lightning Network inika ukuthengiselana okukhawulezayo, okungabizi kakhulu kwihlabathi lonke, nto leyo eyenza ilungele imarike yothumelo-mali kwaye isombulule iingxaki ezinxulumene neendleko eziphezulu, ixesha elide lokuhlala kwemali, kunye nemida yexesha lokusebenza kwamashishini.

###### Amandla Amaninzi

Xa kukho amandla amaninzi angabizi, uluntu luyaphumelela, kwaye amashishini noluntu banako ukuhlangabezana nemfuno ekhula rhoqo yamandla ezindlu, amashishini, kunye netekhnoloji entsha. Ukumba iBitcoin kukhuthaza abavukuzi ukuba basebenzise amandla angasetyenziswanga awayeza kulahleka, avela kwimithombo yamandla ehlaziyekayo efana nelanga, umoya, namanzi. Abavukuzi beBitcoin banokusebenzisa la mandla angabizi ukwenza iBitcoin entsha ngokumba, bakhusele inethiwekhi, baze babuyisele amandla angaphezulu abawavelisayo kwigridi yamandla xa efuneka luluntu.
