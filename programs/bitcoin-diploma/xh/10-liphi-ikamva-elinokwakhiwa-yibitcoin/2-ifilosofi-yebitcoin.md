# 10.2 Ifilosofi yeBitcoin

KwiZifundo 5 no-8, safumanisa ukuba abantu abaqhuba i-node banegalelo ekukhuseleni imithetho ye-Bitcoin. Oku kubalulekile kuba, okokuqala ngqa, abantu abafana nathi banokuthatha inxaxheba kwinethiwekhi eqinisekisa ukuba imithetho yenkqubo yethu yemali ikhuselekile. Le mithetho ibandakanya umda wokubakho kwemali, kwaye akukho qela linokutshintsha le mithetho lodwa. Lithuba elikhethekileyo kubantu abaqhelekileyo ukuba bathathe inxaxheba ekukhuseleni imali yethu ukuba ikhuselekile kwaye ithembekile.

Ifilosofi ye-Bitcoin imele amandla, inkululeko, ukuzimela kwezezimali, ukucinga okunzulu, kunye nokuzimela — umbono wokuba sonke sifanele sibe nelizwi kwimigaqo yenkqubo esiyikhethayo. Ngokungafaniyo nenkqubo ye-fiat elawulwa ngamaqela anamandla aphakathi, i-Bitcoin isekelwe kwinethiwekhi apho akukho qela linolawulo olupheleleyo. Oku kuthetha ukuba akukho mntu unokuthatha impahla yakho okanye akuthintele ekusebenziseni imali yakho ngendlela oyifunayo. Ngokuchaseneyo, i-CBDC's zisenza oku kanye.

Kwihlabathi le-fiat, ukuba nemali eninzi kuthetha ukuba unempembelelo enkulu nolawulo. Ngokwahlukileyo, i-Bitcoin isasaza amandla ngokulinganayo kubantu. Yinzame yeqela apho wonke umntu, nokuba unemali engakanani, udlala indima ebalulekileyo kwinkqubo. Yicinge njengamandla adibeneyo, apho amandla akho ezemali awathethi ukuba unempembelelo enkulu ngokuzenzekelayo. I-Bitcoin yakhiwe kwimigaqo engatshintshiyo, kwaye kule ntsebenziswano, ngathi uluntu ngokwalo lulawula inkqubo. Akukho babalulekileyo abambalwa abenza izigqibo — sonke sisebenza kunye njengoluntu olomeleleyo, silawula indlela ye-Bitcoin ngaphandle kokuba iguqulwe ligunya elinye.

Ngelixa kwinkqubo ye-fiat abanamandla bechaza imithetho, kwi-Bitcoin yigalelo elongeziweyo labantu ngamnye eligcina inethiwekhi isebenza. Akukho sishishini, nokuba sinemali okanye sinamandla kangakanani, sinokuchaza indlela i-Bitcoin eya kuyithatha. Le yindlela entsha yokwabiwa kwamandla, apho amandla enkqubo akusezandleni zabambalwa kodwa asezandleni zamandla adibeneyo wabo bonke abathathi-nxaxheba.

Ingcamango ephambili kukudala inkqubo ekhuselekileyo, ecacileyo, nefanelekileyo apho wonke umntu anokufikelela, anokuyilawula, kwaye asebenzise imali yehlabathi, kungakhathaliseki ukuba ungubani okanye uphi.

1. Kutheni ukurhweba ngezinto kwaphela ukusetyenziswa? Yintoni ingxaki yokufuna izinto ezimbini ngaxeshanye?
1. Kutheni kubalulekile ukuqonda i-Nixon shock kunye nendlela esisasebenza ngayo nanamhlanje?
1. Yiyiphi enye into yembali esiyifundileyo echaphazele kakhulu kuwe?
1. Imali enomda wokubakho yahluke njani kwiimali zemveli ze-fiat?
1. I-Bitcoin yadalwa nini, ngubani, kwaye yadalelwa ntoni? Ingcamango yenkqubo esekwe kuluntu inegalelo njani kule njongo?
