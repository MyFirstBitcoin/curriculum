# 10.1 Yintoni ii-Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)?

Ii-Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) yimali yedijithali ekhutshelwa kwaye ilawulwa ngumbutho ophambili welizwe, oko kukuthi, ibhanki ephambili. Ziyindlela yedijithali yemali esemthethweni yelizwe, efana nemali ephepheni okanye iinkozo zemali, kodwa zikhona kuphela kwifomathi yedijithali. I-CBDC ijolise ekuphuculeni ukhuseleko, ukunciphisa iindleko zokusebenza kwemali, kunye nokwenza lula ukuthengiselana phakathi kwabantu, amashishini, kunye norhulumente. Ngokungafaniyo nezinye iimali zedijithali ezifana neBitcoin, i-CBDC ilawulwa ngokupheleleyo yibhanki ephambili kwaye ayinayo inkululeko okanye ubumfihlo obufumaneka kwiimali zedijithali ezizimeleyo.

Iimali zeBhanki Ephakathi zeDijithali, okanye iiCBDC, ziinguqulelo zedijithali zemali eqhelekileyo ye-fiat. IiCBDC zilandela imigaqo efanayo nemali ye-fiat, apho igunya eliphakathi (njengurhulumente) linokwandisa inani lemali ngokuzimeleyo, nto leyo enciphisa amandla abantu okuthenga. Nangona kunjalo, iiCBDC zikwanika oorhulumente izixhobo ezintsha nezomeleleyo zokulawula indlela le mali isetyenziswa ngayo ngabantu kwihlabathi lonke.

Ngokophando lweHuman Rights Foundation (HRF), amazwe angama-134 kunye nemibutho yemali, emele iipesenti ezingama-98 zeGDP yehlabathi, aphanda malunga neCBDC. NgoMeyi ka-2020 elo nani lalingama-35 kuphela. Okwangoku, amazwe angama-66 asekwinqanaba eliphambili lophando—uphuhliso, uvavanyo, okanye ukumiliselwa. Wonke ilizwe leG20 liphanda ngeCBDC, kwaye ali-19 kuwo asekwinqanaba eliphambili lophando lweCBDC.

Ke, ngaphandle kokuba zedijithali, yintoni eyenza iiCBDC zahluke kwimali ye-fiat eqhelekileyo? Kubalulekile ukuqonda ukuba, ngokungafaniyo nemali ye-fiat eqhelekileyo efana nephepha okanye iinkozo zemali, iiCBDC zivumela urhulumente ukuba abukele aze alawule zonke iintengiselwano kwihlabathi lonke ngendlela yedijithali. Oku kuthetha ukuba urhulumente angayimisa intengiselwano ethile okanye ade avale iakhawunti yakho yonke ukuba engakuthandi okanye engayithandi indlela osebenzisa ngayo imali yakho.

Umzekelo, khawucinge ukuba ufuna ukuthumela imali kumntu osapho lwakho kwilizwe elifuna uncedo, kodwa urhulumente wakho uyala intengiselwano yakho kuba engavumelani neenkokeli zaloo lizwe. Okanye ucinge uhamba uye evenkileni ukuthenga into oyithandayo, kodwa awukwazi kuba uthethe uluvo lwakho ngokukhululekileyo kwimidiya yoluntu.

Okwangoku, uninzi lwemali ye-fiat esetyenziswa ngoku sele iyidijithali — umahluko kukuba ziibhanki ezahlukeneyo ezinokubukela kwaye zivimbe iintengiselwano, kwaye kuphela ezo zidlula kwiakhawunti zabo. Sekunjalo, imizekelo yokuvinjelwa kweentengiselwano efana nale ingentla sele yenzekile kwaye iyaqhubeka kwilizwe ezininzi, yenziwa ziibhanki kodwa rhoqo phantsi komyalelo worhulumente ophakathi.

IiCBDC, nangona kunjalo, zinika oorhulumente ulawulo olukhulu ngendlela imali esetyenziswa ngayo kummandla wabo, nto leyo enciphisa amandla abantu okuchitha ngokukhululekileyo. Abanye bathi iiCBDC zinokwenza oorhulumente bakwazi ukuphoqelela imigaqo yabo kwangoko, ngaphandle kokuba kubekho umntu ophumeza oko. Endaweni yokusebenzisana neebhanki ezininzi, banokulandelela baze bavimbe ukuhamba kwemali kwilizwe labo ngokukhawuleza.

Zombini iiCBDC kunye neBitcoin zedijithali kodwa, ngaphandle kolu faneko, zimele iindlela ezahlukeneyo zemali kunye neefilosofi ezahlukeneyo, nto leyo ekhokelela kwiziphumo ezahlukeneyo.
