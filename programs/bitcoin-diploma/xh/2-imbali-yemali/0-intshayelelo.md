# 2.0 Intshayelelo

> Imali ayizange yadalwa ngokuyilwa, kodwa yavela kwinkqubo yemarike. Ayizange yenziwe ngurhulumente. Yavela ngokwendalo ngokuhamba kwexesha njengolungelelwaniso oluzivelelayo.  
_Murray Rothbard_

![The History of Money](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/348d96d705b7a753b2dc71f1e598ea9b4b4b86c0-504x76.svg)

Zama ukucinga ngexesha elidlulileyo apho abantu babengenazo iinkozo zemali okanye amaphepha-mali esiwasebenzisayo namhlanje. Ngelo xesha, babesebenzisa indlela eyahlukileyo yokurhweba—besebenzisa izinto ezifana neengqokoma okanye izinyithi ezixabisekileyo ezifana negolide njengohlobo lwemali olukhethekileyo. Oku kunokubonakala kungaqhelekanga, kodwa yayiyindlela yabo yemali, into wonke umntu avumelana ngayo ukuba inexabiso.

Kule modyuli, siza kuqalisa uhambo lwexesha, sifunde ngokutshintsha kwemali ngokwethu. Siza kulandela imvelaphi yayo kwaye sibone indlela etshintshe ngayo kwaye yalungelelaniswa kwimbali.

#### Umsebenzi: Umdlalo Wokutshintshiselana Ngezinto

https://qr.myfirstbitcoin.org/barter.pdf

Lo ngumsebenzi weklasi oza kukunceda uqonde ngcono ingcamango zokutshintshiselana ngezinto. Ukuthatha inxaxheba kuya kukunceda ubone indlela imali evela ngayo ngokwendalo kwiimarike ezikhululekileyo.

###### Key Points

1. **Ukutshintshiselana ngokuthe ngqo** kunokwenzeka xa kukho ukufana okugqibeleleyo kwezinto ezifunwayo.
1. **Ukutshintshiselana ngokungathanga ngqo** kuyanceda kodwa kwandisa ubunzima bokurhweba.
1. Ukufumanisa eyona nto ithengiseka lula (okanye ithengiseka kakhulu) yinkqubo yendalo kwaye ikhokelela kwimali.

###### Ingcebiso Kubafundi

Lo ngumdlalo wokuthatha inxaxheba. Okukhona uzibandakanya ngamandla kunye nobuchule, kokukhona uza konwaba kwaye ufumane iziphumo ezilungileyo.
