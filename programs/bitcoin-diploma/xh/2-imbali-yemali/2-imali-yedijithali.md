# 2.2 Imali yedijithali

Ngokungafaniyo nemali yesintu, imali yedijithali ikhona kuphela ngendlela ye-elektroniki. Igcinwa kwaye itshintshiselwana kusetyenziswa iikhompyutha kunye nesoftware ekhethekileyo.

Njengokuba i-imeyile isivumela ukuba sithumele kwaye samkele imiyalezo kwangoko kwaye ngaphandle kweendleko zokuthumela, imali yedijithali yenza ukuba sithumele kwaye samkele ixabiso kwangoko nangexabiso eliphantsi kakhulu.

Iimali esizisebenzisayo ziya zisiba zedijithali ngakumbi; namhlanje, kuphela inxenye encinci yemali eseleyo ikhona ngendlela yeemali ezityhidiweyo kunye neebhili zephepha. Iibhanki kunye neenkonzo zebhanki zibonelela abathengi bazo ngezicelo zokutshintshiselana ngemali ngokulula kwi-intanethi. Kodwa imali ivela phi?
