# Izixhobo Ezongezelelweyo

#### 1. Kutheni usebenzisa iBitcoin?

* **“The Bullish Case for Bitcoin” ka Vijay Boyapati**: Eli nqaku lichaza isizathu sokuba iBitcoin iyinto exabisekileyo kwaye inamandla okuba yimalini yehlabathi. Umbhali uchaza izinto zobugcisa kunye nezoqoqosho ezenza iBitcoin ibe lithuba elihle lotyalo-mali.
* **“Why Bitcoin Matters” ka Aleks Svetski (iyure e-1)**: Le vidiyo ichaza ukubaluleka kweBitcoin njengemali yedijithali ezimeleyo kunye nendlela enokuchaphazela ngayo inkqubo yemali yangoku. Umthethi uphonononga amandla eBitcoin okuzisa inkululeko yezemali ebantwini kwihlabathi lonke.
* **“Why Bitcoin” ka Wiz**: Eli nqaku linika isishwankathelo seenzuzo zokusebenzisa iBitcoin njengemali kunye nendawo yokugcina ixabiso. Ligxininisa indlela iBitcoin ezimele ngayo kwaye indlela enika ngayo inkululeko kunye nokhuseleko lwezemali.

#### 2. Yintoni iBitcoin?

* **“How Bitcoin Works under the Hood” ka CuriousInventor**: Le vidiyo ichaza ngokweenkcukacha indlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo ngokobugcisa.
* **“What Is Bitcoin” ka Greg Walker**: Eli nqaku lichaza ngokubanzi ukuba yintoni iBitcoin, imbali yayo, itekhnoloji, kunye nendlela eyahlukileyo ngayo kwiimali zemveli.
* **“Bitcoin - The Genesis” ka RT (imizuzu engama-30)**: Le vidiyo ibalisa ngokudalwa kunye neentsuku zokuqala zeBitcoin. Iphanda izizathu zomdali ongaziwayo, uSatoshi Nakamoto, kunye nendlela ingcamango yeBitcoin eyakhula ngayo.

#### 3. Funda Ngaphezulu

* **“The Bitcoin Standard” (iyure e-1 imizuzu engama-40)**: Le ncwadi yomsindo iphonononga imeko yezoqoqosho kunye nembali ekhokelele ekudalweni kweBitcoin. Ichaza izibonelelo zemali ezimeleyo kunye namandla eBitcoin okuba ngumgangatho wehlabathi.
* **“Intro to Bitcoin Austrian Thought” (iyure e-1)**: Le ntetho yomsindo ichaza iSikolo saseAustria kwezoqoqosho kunye nendlela enxulumene ngayo nengcamango yeBitcoin. Inika umbono onzulu wemigaqo yezoqoqosho engasemva kweBitcoin kunye nendlela ehambelana ngayo neengcinga zaseAustria.
* **“Bitcoin Babies”** ka Naomi Wambui - Twitter: @btcbabies - @ngachanaomi1 Imithombo yasimahla yePDF ejolise ekuxhobiseni oomama ngolwazi oluyimfuneko olubandakanya ukutya, iBitcoin, kunye nempilo-ntle yengqondo iyonke.
* **BTC Sessions**: Ijelo leYouTube lemfundo yeBitcoin kuphela elineziqeqesho kunye nezikhokelo eziluncedo.

#### 7. Ezinye iiNzuzo:

* **Bitcoin.org**: Iwebhusayithi esemthethweni yeprotokholi yeBitcoin.
* **Bitcointalk.org**: IBitcointalk yiforum apho abasebenzisi banokuxoxa ngezihloko ezinxulumene neBitcoin, babuze imibuzo, kwaye babelane ngolwazi. Yindawo entle yokufunda kwabanye abathandi beBitcoin kunye neengcali.
* **Bitcoincore.org**: Le yisoftware yoqobo yeBitcoin kwaye isasetyenziswa kakhulu ngabasebenzisi abaninzi kunye nabaphuhlisi. Inika iseti enamandla yezixhobo zokusebenzisana nenethiwekhi yeBitcoin kunye nokwakha usetyenziso lweBitcoin.
* **Bitcoinwiki.org**: Le yindawo yoluntu enika isikhokelo esibanzi kuyo yonke into enxulumene neBitcoin. Igubungela yonke into, ukusuka kwicala lobugcisa leBitcoin ukuya kwimbali yayo kunye neendlela ezisetyenziswa ngazo.
* **Bitcoinmagazine.com**: Le lupapasho lwe-intanethi olugubungela iindaba kunye neengcamango ezinxulumene neBitcoin kunye nezinye ii-cryptocurrencies. Inika indlela entle yokuhlala usesikhathini ngophuhliso lwamva nje kwindawo yeBitcoin.
* **Bitcoin.Design**: Ingqokelela evulekileyo yeefayile zoyilo ezinxulumene neBitcoin zokuzoba, iiwebhusayithi, iitemplate, kunye neempawu.
* **Yzer**: Imfundo elula, ephathwayo, yeBitcoin. Funda ngeBitcoin, ezemali, uqoqosho, kwaye ufumane iiSats.
* **NOSTR**: Imidiya yoluntu apho unelungelo lokuba wedwa kwidatha yakho.
* **Simple X**: Iprotocol yesicelo yabucala, esekwe kulawulo olusasazekileyo.
* **Misela iNode yeBitcoin**: Raspberry Pi DIY nguKeith Mukai
* **Indlela yokukhetha i-wallet yeBitcoin**: Sebenzisa ulwazi olusandula ukufunyanwa ukukhetha i-wallet efanelekileyo kuwe.
* **BitcoinIcons.com**: Ingqokelela yeempawu zasimahla zeBitcoin.
* **Bitcoin Kwishishini Lasekuhlaleni**: Ingqokelela yeeflaya eziza kukunceda wabelane ngexabiso leBitcoin kumashishini owathandayo asekuhlaleni.
* **Mempool.Space**: Iprojekthi yeMempool evulekileyo equka nedatha kunye nemizobo yeLightning Network.
