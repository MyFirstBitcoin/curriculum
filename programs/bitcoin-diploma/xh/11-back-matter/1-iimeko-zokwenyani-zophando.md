# Iimeko zokwenyani zophando

#### Inethiwekhi yeMfundo

Kwa My First Bitcoin, sikholelwa kumandla oluntu kunye nentsebenziswano ekuziseni imfundo yeBitcoin kuwo onke amacala ehlabathi. Yiyo loo nto sidale iMy First Bitcoin Education Network, inyathelo elitsha elijolise ekusasazeni imfundo kunye nokomeleza uluntu lwasekuhlaleni. Ukusukela ngo-Epreli 2026, iquka uluntu olungama-80 kumazwe angaphezu kwama-40.

Masive amabali abo kwaye sifunde indlela le Diploma yeBitcoin etshintshe ngayo uluntu ukusuka kulo lonke ihlabathi!

#### Uphando lweMeko: Bitcoin Indonesia

![Bitcoin Indonesia](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/6acdaf25baed66b59fc2e5215a65aedf51301295-171x57.svg)

**Ukusukela ngo-2023, iBitcoin Indonesia ibitshintsha buthule ilizwe layo. Umseki-mphathi uDiana uchaza ngakumbi ngempembelelo yemfundo ezimeleyo yeBitcoin eAsia.**

NgoSeptemba 2023, sabamba iNkomfa yokuqala yeBitcoin Indonesia. Xa undwendwe lokugqibela lwaluhamba kwaye izitulo ziqokelelwe, sajonga macala onke saqonda: akukho nto ibonakalayo isalayo. Akukho bume buhlala, akukho mfundo iqhubekayo. Ke thina bambalwa, amaBitcoiner asekuhlaleni nabahlobo, sagqiba ekubeni sifuna inkomfa yonke imihla. Yiloo ndlela iBitcoin Indonesia yazalwa ngayo.

NgoMeyi 2024, savula indawo yokusebenza ebonakalayo: iBitcoin House Bali. Kulapho saqala khona ukufundisa uluntu lwethu lwasekuhlaleni ngeBitcoin. Emva kweenyanga ezintandathu, sadibana neqela leMy First Bitcoin xa inkqubo yemfundo iguqulelwa lilungu loluntu. Ekuqaleni, sasithandabuza, sicinga ukuba singakwazi ukwenza inkqubo engcono sodwa: iDiploma yayide kakhulu. Ngoku, sikholelwa ukuba iBitcoin Diploma yindlela emfutshane kwimfundo yeBitcoin. Yayiyifutha le-rocket. Ngaphandle kokuxhamla kwinkqubo yemfundo, inkqubo yoqeqesho lootitshala eneFull Node kunye neSikolo se-Intanethi seemfundo zedijithali zibe luncedo kakhulu! Sijoyine iNode Network, kwaye imbali iqhubekile.

Ngoku sele siphumeze abafundi abangaphezu kwama-200 eBali, eBandung, naseSurabaya, kwaye sinethemba lokwandisa eJakarta, eKarawang, kwiiyunivesithi ezimbini zasekuhlaleni nakwezinye iindawo ezininzi kungekudala. Kwaye abafundi abaphumeleyo abango 'bafundi' kuphela. Abanye babo baqhuba amashishini abo kwaye kube lula kakhulu ukufaka abafundi emva kokwenza iiklasi zobuso ngobuso. Umsebenzi wokulungisa iiskuta ngoku wamkela iBitcoin, kunye nosapho oluthengisa iJamu, isiselo semifuno saseBali. Ukongeza, abafundi abaphumeleyo babamba iicohort zabo okanye babuyele njengootitshala abamenyiweyo.

Siyenze eyethu inkqubo yemfundo: sayiguqulela kwiBahasa, songeza amabali ngokuwela kweRupiah, imali yasekuhlaleni, sachaza ii-satoshis sisebenzisa uqoqosho lweedurian. IRupiah ayinakwahlulwa ibe zii-cents okanye ii-pennies, ngoko siyithelekisa iBitcoin nedurian: njengesiqhamo esinye, esineengqatyana ezininzi kunye neembewu ngaphakathi. Ekupheleni kweecohort, sibhiyozela yonke imfundo njengento ekhethekileyo: ngeziqinisekiso, iintetho, iibhaluni, iimbasa, kunye neebarbecue. Kuba xa umntu ekhetha ukuchitha iiveki ezili-10 efunda ngenkululeko yezezimali, kufanelekile ukuba avuzwe.

Iiklasi zethu zahlukeneyo: ukusuka kubantwana abaneminyaka eli-15 ukuya kubadala, ukusuka kubatyali-mali abaqondayo itekhnoloji ukuya kubantu abangazange babe ne-laptop ebomini babo. Abanye abafundi babefuna ukuzibulala ngenxa yeenkohliso ze-shitcoin, kwaye emva kokujoyina inkqubo baba yinxalenye yeqela lethu kwaye basasaza igama. Okanye abanye balahlekelwa yimali yabo yonke kwiinkohliso zebhanki. Ngoku bayinxalenye yoqoqosho olujikelezayo oluluvaliweyo. Kuyamangalisa ukubona indlela abafundi, abakhulele kwilizwe lobukhomanisi ngaphandle kokucinga ngokuzimeleyo, baqala ukuthetha omnye komnye malunga nezinto abafunda zona okokuqala – endaweni yokufunda nje okubhalwe kwiincwadi.

Lixesha lokuqala ebomini bam ndiziva ukuba umsebenzi wam wenza umahluko wokwenene! Kundigcina ndisuka embhedeni yonke imihla. Uyabona ukukhanya emehlweni abo, uyabona ukuba bathemba kwakhona ikamva elingcono.

Eyona nto ndifunde kakhulu endinokuyabelana nabanye ootitshala kukwenza izifundo zibe mnandi, kwaye uvuse umdla wabo. Qala lula, ungayenzi ibe nzima kakhulu. Bonisa ukuba kutheni iBitcoin ibalulekile kumfundi ngamnye, kwaye uthethe usebenzisa intliziyo. Kwaye phambi kokufundisa iicohort ezikwi-intanethi, ngokuqinisekileyo yenza iicohort zobuso ngobuso kuqala. Ndakhula kakhulu ngokufundisa ndingekho kwi-intanethi kuqala!

Sisoloko sisithi inkqubo yeMy First Bitcoin ingcono kakhulu kunokuba unokucinga. Sasithandabuza kakhulu ekuqaleni kwaye sacinga ukuba asisoze siyenze. Kwavela ukuba yenye yezona zigqibo zingcono kwiprojekthi.

#### Uphando lweMeko: The Core

![The Core](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/2d0906b2a4b51b46f0565a0536c8cf3ac6b4031a-1738x1511.png)

**Okwathi kwaqala njengeqela elilula leWhatsApp eKenya kutshintshe laba yintshukumo yemfundo yeBitcoin yehlabathi, echaphazela ubomi abangaphezu kwama-300 kumazwekazi ahlukeneyo. Inyathelo lemfundo yeBitcoin iThe Core ibonakalise ukuba litshintsha ubomi. Umseki uFelix Mukungu wabelana ngohambo lwakhe.**

Konke kwaqala ngamava angalunganga: inkohliso ye-crypto eyaba yindlela yam yokungena ekuqondeni iBitcoin. Elo phupha landibonisa ukuba itekhnoloji inamandla, kodwa ndaziswa kuyo ngendlela engalunganga. Ekuqaleni, bendifuna ukuqonda yonke into, kodwa zonke izixhobo, amanqaku, iipodcast, kunye nevidiyo zazahlulwe-hluliwe. Kwakungekho ndlela ecwangcisiweyo, akukho nkqubo yemfundo yokukhokela abantu ukusuka kwimbali yemali ukuya kwikamva leBitcoin. Ke ndabhala eyam: izahluko ezilithoba ngezihloko ezisisiseko, ndazithumela ngeSMS kwiWhatsApp group apho nabani na wayenokubuza imibuzo. Yayiyinto eqhelekileyo, kodwa inamandla. Yiloo ndlela iThe Core yaqala ngayo: ukusuka kumnqweno wokubonisa abantu indlela elungileyo.

Ekugqibeleni, safudukela kwiGoogle Classroom, sibamba iifowuni kabini ngeveki kuluntu olusondeleyo lwabafundi abalishumi. Ngelo xesha, ndafumanisa neMy First Bitcoin ngethuba ndandifunda kwiBitcoiner waseDatshi. Inkqubo yabo yemfundo yayiyile nto bendiyikhangela: icwangciswe kakuhle, icacile, kwaye ihambelana kakhulu nomnqweno wam. Ndajoyina iNode Network ngoMeyi 2023, ndaqala ukufundisa iicohort zam zokuqala zeBitcoin Diploma. Sasinabantu abangama-30 ababhalisile kunye nabaphumeleyo abali-12. Ngokuhamba kwexesha ndafumana uncedo olungakumbi kootitshala abamenyiweyo, umzekelo uGlenn waseBitcoin Ubuntu.

Njengeqonga le-intanethi, sifumene iBitcoin Diploma kunye neSikolo se-Intanethi zisebenza kakuhle kakhulu. Inkqubo yoLawulo lweMfundo (LMS) isivumela ukuba sifake iividiyo, iislayidi, iimibuzo, imisebenzi, kunye neengxoxo. Yindlela yobungcali, kwaye abafundi bayithatha nzulu. Bafumana izaziso zokubakhumbuza ukuba bagqibe imisebenzi. Ngaphandle koko, iNdibano Jikelele nezinye ii-node ziye zaba yinkuthazo enkulu. Ujoyina kwaye ubone okwenzekayo kwezinye iiluntu. Uphuma kwezi ntlanganiso zenyanga uziva ukhuthazekile. Inethiwekhi yabakhi ikhuthaza ukuqhubeka nokwakha.

Abafundi abangaphezu kwama-500 badlule kwiicohort zethu ezilithoba. Sipha abafundi ii-sats, sinike iziqinisekiso, kwaye sibhiyozela onke amanyathelo ohambo lwabo ngeBitcoin Pathfinders threads ku-X.

IThe Core ibonakalise ukuba sisiseko kwezinye iiprojekthi. Kwi-cohort yethu yokuqala kwavela uRikto Xonghoti eIndiya, kwaye ukusukela ngoko kuphume iiprojekthi ezingaphezu kwe-10, kubandakanya iYes Bitcoin Haiti, iBitcoin School Kenya, kunye neKabul Bitcoin. Kuzo zonke iimeko: abafundi baba ngootitshala! Abanye baqala uqoqosho olujikelezayo njengeBitcoin Githurai, exhaswa ngabantu abafunde nathi kwi-intanethi.

Uninzi lwabafundi luvela kulo lonke elaseAfrika—eNigeria, eKenya, eGhana, eZambia, nangaphezulu. Iicohort zokuqala zazizele ngabafundi abaqondayo itekhnoloji, kodwa ngoku, ifuthe lokusasazeka liyabonakala. Abaphumeleyo bazisa iintsapho zabo, abahlobo babo, abamelwane babo. Ndifumene imiyalezo kumntwana nomama wakhe bethenga iBitcoin kunye. Okanye ibali losapho lonke elijoyine i-cohort. Utata ongaphumelelanga ekuqaleni, kodwa wabhalisa kwakhona kwi-cohort elandelayo.

Eyona nto siyibhiyozelayo kakhulu? Imfundo ebonakalayo, umsitho osecaleni ngexesha leAfrican Bitcoin Conference, apho abafundi badibana namaBitcoiner ehlabathi ubuso ngobuso. Kwaye ukufumana inkxaso-mali yethu yokuqala kwiHuman Rights Foundation yayiyeyona nto inkulu. Okwathi kwaqala njengemiyalezo yeSMS ngeWhatsApp, ngoku sekuyiqela labane elifundisa abantu abavela kulo lonke eli lizwe ngendlela elungileyo.

Ingcebiso yam kuye nabani na ofundisa iBitcoin Diploma? Qala lula. Khetha ukulinda ixesha elide. Yenza ubungqina bomsebenzi. Yakha kuqala, cela inkxaso-mali kamva.
