# Iingcamango eziphambili

#### Isahluko 1

* **Intshayelelo Yekhosi**: Phonononga iinjongo zekhosi kunye nolindelo lweDiploma yeBitcoin.
* **Ingxoxo Yeklasi — Imibuzo Emihlanu NgeMali**: Zibandakanye kumsebenzi wokuzicingela ngokuphendula imibuzo emihlanu ebalulekileyo malunga nemali.
* **Ukuqonda Imali**
* **Imisebenzi, Iimpawu, kunye Neentlobo**
* **Ingxoxo Yeklasi — Kutheni Sifuna Imali**
* **I-Psychology yeMali**

#### Isahluko 2

* **Intshayelelo Kwimbali Ne-Evolution yeMali**: Phonononga imbali kunye nendlela imali etshintshe ngayo. Qonda indlela urhwebo lwakudala olukhokelele ngayo kuphuhliso lwemali esiyisebenzisayo namhlanje.
* **Umsebenzi Womdlalo Wotshintshiselwano**: Zibandakanye kumava omdlalo wokutshintshiselana ukuze uqonde imingeni yokutshintshiselana ngokuthe ngqo kwaye uxabise isidingo senkqubo esebenza ngcono.
* **Utshintsho Lwemali**: Phonononga utshintsho ukusuka kwiindlela zakudala ezifana neengqokama kunye neebhidi ukuya ekuveleni kwemali yeqhekeza kunye nemali yephepha. Landela uhambo ukusuka ephepheni ukuya kwiplastiki, uqonde indlela imali etshintshe ngayo kwimbali.
* **Uguquko Lwemali Yedijithali**

#### Isahluko 3

* **Imvelaphi Yemali yeFiat**: Phonononga imvelaphi yemali ye-fiat ngokubanzi kwimbali, uqonde indlela eyaba yindlela ephambili yemali.
* **Umsebenzi WeBhanki Yegcinwe Ngokwahlukeneyo**: Zibandakanye kumsebenzi weBhanki Yegcinwe Ngokwahlukeneyo ukuze ufumane ukuqonda ngendlela le nkqubo isebenza ngayo, ugxininise ukuxhomekeka kwayo kwityala kunye neziphumo zayo kuqoqosho ngokubanzi.
* **Inkqubo yeFiat**: Qonda izinto ezisisiseko zenkqubo ye-fiat, kubandakanya indalo yayo njengenkqubo yemali ngokomthetho, indima yeBhanki Yegcinwe Ngokwahlukeneyo, kunye nabadlali abaphambili abalawula le nkqubo.
* **Iimali Yedijithali YeBhanki Ephakathi (CBDCs)**: Phonononga imeko eguqukayo ye-Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) kunye nefuthe lazo elinokubakho kwikamva lemali.

#### Isahluko 4

* **Ukwehla Kwamandla Okuthenga**: Qonda ingcamango yokunyuka kwamaxabiso kunye nefuthe layo kumandla okuthenga. Zibandakanye kumsebenzi we-Auction: Iziphumo Zokunyuka Kwamaxabiso ukuze uzivele ngokwakho.
* **Umsebenzi: Iziphumo Zenqaku leFiat**: Thatha inxaxheba kumsebenzi weziphumo zenqaku leFiat, uveze iziphumo ezibanzi zenkqubo yemali yangoku.
* **Umthwalo Wetyala Lomhlaba Jikelele kunye Nokungalingani Kwezentlalo**: Phonononga iziphumo ezimbini zomthwalo wetyala lomhlaba kunye nokungalingani kwezentlalo. Qaphela iziphumo kumntu ngamnye nakuluntu, ugxininise ukulahleka kwamandla okuthenga kunye nokwanda komahluko wobutyebi.
* **AmaCypherpunks kunye Nokuzimela**: Funda ibali lamaCypherpunks kunye nezizathu zabo zokufuna imali ezimeleyo. Yahlula phakathi kweenkqubo eziphakathi kunye nezingazimeleyo, ufumane ukuqonda kwimbali emfutshane yeemali zedijithali.

#### Isahluko 5

* **USatoshi Nakamoto kunye Nendalo yeBitcoin**: Phonononga umlingiswa ongaziwayo uSatoshi Nakamoto kunye nembali yokuqala yeBitcoin, uqonde izizathu zokuqala zokuphuhliswa kwayo.
* **Umsebenzi Weklasi — Ukwakha Imvumelwano**: Zibandakanye kumsebenzi wokwakha imvumelwano kwinethiwekhi yePeer-to-Peer ukuze ufumane ukuqonda okusebenzayo ngendlela imvumelwano ifezekiswa ngayo kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin.
* **I-Bitcoin Njengemali Yedijithali Ethembekileyo**: Hlola indima yeBitcoin njengemali yedijithali ethembekileyo, uxoxe ngotshintsho lwayo, imisebenzi, kunye neempawu zayo, kwaye uthathe inxaxheba kwincoko yeklasi malunga nokuba iBitcoin ingaba yimali ethembekileyo na.
* **Indlela I-Bitcoin Esebenza Ngayo**: Jonga indlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo, kubandakanya iNakamoto Consensus Mechanism. Chaza abadlali abaphambili kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin, abafana nabavukuzi, iinodi, abasebenzisi, abaphuhlisi, kunye neeprojekthi, kwaye uqonde indlela abasebenzisana ngayo.
* **Ukwamkela Uxanduva Lomntu Ngamnye**: Gxininisa ingcamango yoxanduva lomntu ngamnye kumxholo weBitcoin, ukhuthaze ukuqonda indima yomntu kunye noxanduva kwinkqubo ezimeleyo.

#### Isahluko 6

* **Ukufumana iBitcoin**: Phonononga iindlela ezifana norhwebo lwe-peer-to-peer kunye neendawo zokutshintshiselana, uxoxe ngemiba yabucala enxulumene neenkqubo ze-KYC.
* **Urhwebo lwePeer-to-Peer**: Yenza ukuthengiselana okungaphakathi kwinethiwekhi ukuze ufumane amava kwiimigaqo ezisisiseko zotshintshiselwano lweBitcoin.
* **Ukuseta Iwallet yeBitcoin**: Funda amanyathelo abalulekileyo okukhuphela, ukwenza izitshixo, kunye nokugcina i-backup ye-wallet yeBitcoin ukuze ukhusele ukuthengiselana kwakho.
* **Iindidi zeeWallet zeBitcoin**: Yahlula phakathi kweewallet ezivulekileyo (open source), ezivaliweyo (closed source), ezibanjwe ngabanye (custodial), kunye nezingabanjwanga ngabanye (noncustodial), uqonde indima yezitshixo ekhuselekweni.

#### Isifundo 7

* **Intshayelelo kwiLightning Network**: Qaphela indlela iBitcoin ephucuka ngayo ngezobuchwepheshe ezifana neLightning Network, eyandisa amandla ayo.
* **Ukuseta Iwallet yeLightning**: Funda amanyathelo abalulekileyo okuseta iwallet yeBitcoin Lightning, ukuze ukwazi ukuthengiselana ngokukhawuleza nangokwandisa umthamo.
* **Umsebenzi Osebenzayo**: Thatha inxaxheba kumdyarho wokudlulisa iiwallet zeLightning, ukuze ufumane ukuqonda okusebenzayo kwendlela yokusebenza kweLightning Network.
* **Iindidi zeeWallet zeLightning**: Yahlula phakathi kweewallet zeLightning ezivulekileyo, ezivaliweyo, ezibanjwe ngabanye, kunye nezingabanjwanga ngabanye, ngokweemfuno zomsebenzisi.
* **Uthengiselwano lweLightning**: Phanda indlela yokuthumela nokwamkela uthengiselwano lweLightning, ugxininise isantya kunye nokusebenza kakuhle kweLightning Network.

#### Isifundo 8

* **I-Ledger yeBitcoin**: Qonda umxholo we-ledger engaphakathi kwinethiwekhi, eqhutywa zi-node kunye nabavukuzi (miners), ukuqinisekisa ukucaca kunye nokhuseleko.
* **Iitshixo Zoluntu Nezabucala**: Phanda ukubaluleka kokhuseleko lwe-cryptography kwiintengiselwano zeBitcoin usebenzisa iitshixo zoluntu nezabucala, kunye nomsebenzi wokubonisa i-SHA 256 hashing.
* **Imodeli ye-UTXO**: Qonda imodeli ye-Unspent Transaction Output njengesiseko sentengiselwano kwiBitcoin.

#### Isifundo 9

* **IiNode zeBitcoin kunye nabaVukuzi**: Jonga indima yeenode kunye nabavukuzi ekugcineni inethiwekhi yeBitcoin, kubandakanya imiba enjengekuveliswa, ukunqongophala, i-halving, kunye nobunzima.
* **IMempool**: Phanda i-blockchain yeBitcoin usebenzisa i-mempool kwaye ufumane amava asebenzayo ngomsebenzi we-mempool.space.
* **Indlela Uthengiselwano lweBitcoin Olusebenza Ngayo**: Fumana ukuqonda kwenkqubo epheleleyo yothengiselwano lweBitcoin, kubandakanya umthumeli, umamkeli, ii-node, abavukuzi, kunye ne-mempool.

#### Isifundo 10

* **Ikamva leBitcoin**: Ngena nzulu kwindlela iBitcoin enokuthi ikhule ngayo kunye nophuhliso oluzayo njengemali yedijithali etshintsha umhlaba.
* **Iimali Zedijithali ZeBhanki Ephakathi**: Thelekisa umgaqo-siseko wokuzimela kweBitcoin nalowo we-CBDC ukuze uqonde ngcono indima yeBitcoin ekomelezeni uluntu.
* **Iziseko Zengcinga zeBitcoin**: Phanda ifilosofi esisiseko emva kweBitcoin, uqonde indlela eyavela ngayo njengempendulo kwimingeni yezoqoqosho, ugxininise kwimpembelelo yayo kwinkululeko yezezimali kunye nendlela eyahlukile ngayo kwiimali zemveli.
* **Ingqwalasela yeDiploma**
