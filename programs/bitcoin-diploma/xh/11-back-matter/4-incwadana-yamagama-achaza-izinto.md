# Incwadana yamagama achaza izinto

**Uhlaselo lwe-51%:** Uhlobo lohlaselo kwinethiwekhi ye-blockchain apho iqela elinye okanye iqela labantu lilawula uninzi lwamandla okusebenza kwenethiwekhi, nto leyo ebavumela ukuba balawule iintengiselwano baze baphazamise inethiwekhi.

**Utshintshiselwano lwe-Atomic:** Utshintshiselwano phakathi kwabantu ababini lwemali ye-crypto enye ngenye ngaphandle kwesidingo sokusebenzisa indawo yokutshintshiselana ephakathi okanye umthengisi.

**Indibano yokuthengisa:** Inkqubo apho iimpahla okanye izixhobo zithengiswa kumthengi ophakamileyo.

**Utshintshiselwano:** Utshintshiselwano ngqo lweempahla kunye neenkonzo, ngaphandle kokusebenzisa izinto eziphakathi, ezifana nemali.

**Ibhaskithi yeeMpahla:** Ingqokelela yeempahla okanye iinkonzo ezisetyenziswa ukulinganisa utshintsho kwindleko yokuphila.

**Bitcoin:** Yinkqubo esekwe kwinethiwekhi engaphantsi kolawulo lwabantu abaninzi evumela abantu ukuba bathumele ixabiso omnye komnye ngaphandle kwabalamli. Igama elifanayo lisetyenziswa kwinethiwekhi yabasebenzisi kunye nemali yemveli yale nkqubo.

**Umkhangeli weBlock:** Isixhobo esisetyenziswa ukujonga nokuhlola i-blockchain, sivumela abasebenzisi ukuba babone iibhloko ezithile, iintengiselwano, kunye nedilesi ze-wallet.

**Umvuzo weBlock:** Inani le-bitcoin entsha enikwa abavukuzi (miners) xa bephosa ibhloko entsha kwi-blockchain.

**Blockchain:** Ingxelo kawonke-wonke yazo zonke iintengiselwano ze-Bitcoin ezenzekileyo, ngendlela yomxokelelwane weebhloko.

**Ulawulo lweMali lwamazwe ngamazwe:** Izithintelo ekufudusweni kwemali phakathi kwamazwe.

**Ibhanki Ephakathi:** Iziko elilawulwa ngurhulumente elilawula umgaqo-nkqubo wemali welizwe.

**Ulawulo oluphakathi:** Ukuqokelelwa kwamandla okanye ulawulo kwiqela elinye.

**Ukugcinwa okubandayo (Cold Storage):** Indlela yokugcina i-bitcoin ngaphandle kwe-intanethi, kude nomngcipheko wabaphuli-mthetho be-intanethi okanye ezinye izoyikiso ezikwi-intanethi.

**Imali yeMpahla:** Izinto ezisetyenziswa njengendlela yokutshintshiselana ezingeloxanduva lomnye umntu okanye ityala, ezifana negolide nesilivere.

**Uqinisekiso:** Inkqubo yokuba intengiselwano ye-Bitcoin yongezwa ngumvukuzi kwi-blockchain. Ibhloko nganye entsha evunyiweyo yongeza uqinisekiso kule ntengiselwano, nto leyo eyenza kube nzima (kungenakwenzeka phantse) ukuyiguqula.

**Indlela yoVumelwano:** Indlela esetyenziswa kwitekhnoloji ye-blockchain ukuqinisekisa iintengiselwano kunye nokuqinisekisa ukuthembeka kwe-blockchain.

**Utyikityo lweKhripthografi:** Ikhowudi yedijithali eyenziwe kusetyenziswa isitshixo sabucala ukuqinisekisa ubunyani kunye nokuthembeka komyalezo okanye idatha ethile, kwaye, kwimeko ye-Bitcoin, ubunini be-UTXO ethile.

**Ikhripthografi:** Icandelo lemathematika elinceda ekudaleni iinkqubo ezikhuselekileyo.

**Ukuncitshiswa kwexabiso lemali:** Ukwehla kwexabiso lemali, rhoqo ngokunciphisa isixa sesinyithi esixabisekileyo kwi-coin.

**Ityala:** Imali etyala komnye umntu.

**Ukusasazwa kolawulo:** Ukwabiwa kwamandla nolawulo kulo lonke uthungelwano.

**Imali yezeMali engaphantsi kolawulo lwabantu abaninzi (DeFi):** Intshukumo ngaphakathi kwishishini le-cryptocurrency yokudala iimveliso kunye neenkonzo zezezimali ezisebenza kwi-blockchain ngaphandle kolawulo lwabalamli.

**Ixabiso leDijithali:** Umfanekiso wedijithali wexabiso onokutshintshiselwa okanye usetyenziswe njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso, njengaleyo ye-bitcoin.

**Ingxelo esasazekileyo:** Idatha egcinwe kwinethiwekhi yeekhompyutha endaweni yokugcinwa kwindawo enye ephakathi.

**Ukungahambelani kweemfuno:**  Ingxaki esisiseko kwinkqubo yokutshintshiselana, efuna ukuba amaqela omabini atshintshiselanayo afune oko kunikwa elinye iqela, kwelo nani lichanekileyo.

**Ukuphindaphinda Ukuchitha:** Ukuzama ukusebenzisa imali esele isetyenzisiwe ngendlela yobuqhetseba. Oku kulungiswa ngolawulo oluphakathi noluvunyelweyo kwiibhanki zemveli, kwaye ngokusebenzisa ikhowudi kwiBitcoin.

**Intengiselwano ye-Dust:** Intengiselwano ethumela isixa esincinci kakhulu se-bitcoin esingekho kwinqanaba elinengeniso ngokwezoqoqosho.

**Utshintshiselwano:** Iqonga apho abasebenzisi banokuthenga, bathengise, kwaye batshintshe iimali ze-crypto ngezinye iiasethi ezifana nemali yesintu okanye ezinye iimali ze-crypto.

**Ixabiso Lotshintshiselwano:** Ixabiso lemali enye xa ithelekiswa nenye.

**FOMO:** Ukoyika ukuphosa ithuba, ligama elisetyenziswa ukuchaza imvakalelo yoxinzelelo okanye ukuzisola ngokuphosa ithuba elinengeniso.

**FUD:** Uloyiko, ukungaqiniseki, kunye nokuthandabuza, ligama elisetyenziswa ukuchaza amarhe amabi okanye ulwazi olungakhokelela kwipanikhi okanye ukuhla kwemarike.

**GDP:** Imveliso elishishiniweyo yelizwe, ixabiso elipheleleyo leempahla neenkonzo ezenziwe kwilizwe ngexesha elithile.

**Hard Fork:** Utshintsho olungahambelaniyo neenguqulelo zangaphambili kwinkqubo yeBitcoin oludala imithetho emitsha efuna imvumelwano entsha, kwaye oku kubangela ukuba kubekho iibhulokhi ezimbini ezahlukeneyo.

**IHardware Wallet:** Isixhobo somzimba esisetyenziselwa ukuvelisa, ukugcina, nokulawula izitshixo zabucala, sibonelela ukhuseleko olungaphezulu kuneesoftware wallets.

**Umsebenzi weHash:** Umsebenzi wezibalo othatha idatha engenayo yobungakanani obuthile aze aphumeze umtya oneempawu zobude obusisigxina, oqhele ukusetyenziswa kwi-cryptography nakwitekhnoloji ye-blockchain.

**Izinga leHash:** Indlela yokulinganisa amandla okusebenza kwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin.

**HODL:** Igama elisetyenziswa kuluntu lweBitcoin ukuchaza ukubamba iBitcoin ixesha elide, endaweni yokuyithengisa okanye ukuyitshintsha.

**IHot Wallet:** Iwallet yeBitcoin edityaniswe kwi-intanethi, ivumela ukufikelela lula kwiBitcoin.

**Ukwangeniswa:**  Iimpahla neenkonzo ezenziwe kwelinye ilizwe kwaye zithengiswa kwimarike yasekhaya.

**Ukwanda kwamaxabiso (Inflation):** Ukonyuka kwexabiso ngokubanzi leempahla neenkonzo kuqoqosho ngenxa yokwehla kwamandla emali.

**Umgaqo-siseko weLayer-1:** Umphantsi womgaqo-siseko weBitcoin ophatha izinto ezisisiseko zemvumelwano, ukuqinisekiswa kwentengiselwano, kunye nokugcinwa kwedatha.

**Umgaqo-siseko weLayer-2:** Umphantsi wesibini owakhiwe ngaphezulu kwe-layer-1, oqhele ukusetyenziswa ukuphucula ukwandiswa, isantya, kunye nokusebenza.

**I-Ledger:** Irekhodi leentengiselwano zemali.

**Lightning Network:** Umgaqo-siseko wokuhlawula we-layer-2 weentengiselwano ezincinci ovumela iintengiselwano zeBitcoin ezikhawulezayo nezixabisa kancinci ngokusebenzisa imijelo engaphandle kwekhonkco.

**Isixhobo sotshintshiselwano:** Izinto ezamkelwa ngokubanzi njengotshintshiselwano lweempahla neenkonzo.

**Umthi weMerkle:** Ulwakhiwo lwedatha olufana nomthi olusetyenziswa kwi-blockchain yeBitcoin ukuqinisekisa ngokufanelekileyo ukuthembeka kweeseti ezinkulu zedatha.

**Mining Pool:** Iqela labavukuzi abasebenzisana ukonyusa amathuba okufumana iibhulokhi ezintsha nokufumana i-bitcoin.

**Mining:** Ukusebenzisa amandla ekhompyutha ukwenza izibalo ezilungiselelwe ukuqinisekisa iintengiselwano nokunyusa ukhuseleko lwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin.

**Umgaqo-nkqubo wezeMali nowezeRhafu:** Imigaqo-nkqubo yebhanki ephakathi kunye norhulumente, ngokwahlukeneyo, echaphazela inani lemali esebenzayo kunye namanqanaba emivuzo kwiqoqosho.

**Imali:** Isixhobo sotshintshiselwano esamkelwa ngokubanzi, esikwasebenza njengesixhobo sokugcina ixabiso kunye nomlinganiselo wokubala.

**Unikelelo-mali:** Isixa semali esikwiqoqo-mali elijikeleza kuqoqosho.

**I-Wallet ye-Multi-Signature (Multisig):** I-wallet efuna utyikityo oluninzi okanye imvume ezininzi phambi kokuba intengiselwano yenziwe, inika ukhuseleko olongezelelweyo nolawulo.

**I-Node:** Ikhompyutha okanye isixhobo esigcina ikopi ezimeleyo ye-ledger ye-Bitcoin kwaye sixhunyiwe kwi-network ye-Bitcoin, sithatha inxaxheba ekuqinisekiseni nasekudluliseni iintengiselwano.

**Inethiwekhi yeeNode:** Inethiwekhi yeekhompyutha okanye izixhobo ezixhunyiwe ezixhasa kwaye zigcina inethiwekhi ye-Bitcoin.

**Nonce:** Inani elingahleliwe elongezwa kwi-header ye-block ukwenza i-hash edibana nenjongo yobunzima.

**I-Orphan Block:** I-block engafakwanga kwikhonkco eliphambili le-blockchain ngenxa yokuba ingasebenzi ngenxa yekhonkco elide elikhuphisana nalo.

**I-Paper Wallet:** Ikopi eprintiweyo yezitshixo zabucala nezoluntu zomsebenzisi ezisetyenziselwa ukugcina nokulawula i-Bitcoin ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.

**Peer-to-Peer (P2P):** Ithetha naluphi na unxibelelwano ngqo phakathi kwamaqela amabini, ngaphandle kokungenelela kwabalamli okanye amaqela esithathu.

**Peg:** Umlinganiselo wokutshintshiselana ozinzileyo phakathi kweemali ezimbini apho enye ixhomekeke kwixabiso lezinye.

**Isitshixo Sabucala:** Umtya wemibhalo enamanani angahleliwe efihliweyo evumela umsebenzisi ukuba atyikitye imiyalezo kunye neentengiselwano, ngaloo ndlela eqinisekisa ilungelo lomntu lokusebenzisa i-bitcoin evela kwi-UTXO ethile.

**Proof of Work (PoW):** Indlela yokuvumelana efuna abasebenzisi ukuba benze umsebenzi othile wokubala ukuze bongeze iibhulokhi kwi-Bitcoin blockchain.

**Idilesi Yoluntu:** Isazisi esikhethekileyo esiveliswe kwisitshixo sabucala somsebenzisi ngenkqubo yezibalo eyaziwa njenge-one-way function kwaye isetyenziselwa ukufumana i-bitcoin.

**Amandla Okuthenga:** Ubungakanani beempahla neenkonzo ezithengwa yinye yemali, ebonisa ixabiso lemali ngokwento enokuthengwa ngayo.

**Recover (Seed) Phrase:** Uluhlu lwamagama ayi-12, 18, okanye 24 afundekayo ngabantu aphuma kwisitshixo sabucala esingafundekiyo ngabantu, enza kube lula ukukhusela. Ingasetyenziswa ukubuyisela i-wallet ye-Bitcoin.

**Umlinganiselo Wogcino:** Inxalenye yeediphozithi ekufuneka ibhanki igcine njengegcinwe.

**Ububanzi Bokubhanga Obunqongopheleyo:** Izithintelo okanye imida kwiinkonzo zebhanki okanye ukufikelela kwiinkonzo zebhanki.

**satoshi (sat):** Eyona yunithi incinci ye-Bitcoin, ilingana ne-1/100,000,000 ye-bitcoin. Iqanjwe ngumsunguli we-Bitcoin, uSatoshi Nakamoto.

**Satoshi Nakamoto:** Igama elisetyenziswa ngumsunguli ongaziwayo we-Bitcoin.

**Satoshis ngeByte (sat/b):** Iyunithi esetyenziselwa ukulinganisa umrhumo wentengiselwano ye-Bitcoin ohlawulwa nge-byte yedatha yentengiselwano.

**SegWit (Segregated Witness):** Uhlaziyo lweprotocol ye-Bitcoin olutshintsha indlela idatha egcinwa ngayo kwi-blockchain, luvumela umthamo omkhulu kunye nemirhumo ephantsi yeentengiselwano.

**Sidechain:** I-blockchain ehamba ecaleni exhunyiwe kwi-blockchain ephambili, evumela ukudluliselwa kweempahla okanye ulwazi phakathi kwezi khonkco zimbini.

**Smart Contract:** Isivumelwano esizisebenzelayo esibhaliweyo kwikhowudi.

**Soft Fork:** Utshintsho olungasemva oluvumelanayo kwi-protocol ye-Bitcoin oluvumela ii-node ezihlaziyiweyo ukuba zisasebenzisane nee-node ezingahlaziywanga, kugcinwa ukuvumelana.

**Stablecoin:** Uhlobo lwe-cryptocurrency oluxhomekeke kwimali ye-fiat okanye kwenye impahla.

**Indawo yokugcina ixabiso:** Impahla engaphulukani nexabiso ngokuhamba kwexesha, ivumela ukugcina ixabiso kwixesha elizayo (ukonga).

**Unikezelo kunye Nemfuno:** Umgaqo-qoqosho othi ixabiso leempahla okanye iinkonzo limiselwa yintsebenziswano phakathi kobuninzi beempahla okanye iinkonzo ezikhoyo kunye nobuninzi obufunwayo.

**Ixabiso Lexesha Lemali:** Umgaqo othi imali inexabiso elingakumbi ngoku kunexesha elizayo.

**Isibini Sokurhweba:** Isibini seemali ezimbini okanye iiasethi ezinokurhweba ngazo omnye komnye kwindawo yokutshintshiselana (umzekelo, BTC/ZAR).

**Umrhumo Wotshintshiselwano:** Isixa esincinci se-bitcoin esihlawulwa ngumthumeli wotshintshiselwano, ukukhuthaza abavukuzi ukuba bafake utshintshiselwano kwibhloko kwaye balongeze kwi-blockchain.

**Isazisi Sotshintshiselwano (TXID):** Umtya owodwa weenombolo kunye neencwadi obonisa iinkcukacha zotshintshiselwano lweBitcoin (njengesixa esithunyelweyo, iidilesi zomthumeli nomamkeli, kunye nomhla wotshintshiselwano) kwi-blockchain yeBitcoin.

**Utshintshiselwano:** Ukudluliselwa kwe-bitcoin ukusuka kwidilesi enye ukuya kwenye kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin.

**Ayifuni Kuthembela:** Inkqubo okanye inkqubo engafuni kuthembela nakubani na wesithathu okanye umphakathi. Endaweni yoko, ixhomekeke kukhuseleko kunye nokucaca kobuchwepheshe obusisiseko.

**Uqinisekiso Lamanqanaba Mabini (2FA):** Umanyathelo lokhuseleko elifuna iindlela ezimbini zokuqinisekisa, (ngokuqhelekileyo igama lokugqitha kunye nekhowudi eyahlukileyo okanye isixhobo) ukufikelela kwiakhawunti okanye ukugqibezela utshintshiselwano.

**Abangenazo iiAkhawunti Zasebhankini:** Abantu okanye uluntu olungenakho ukufikelela kwiinkonzo zebhanki zemveli.

**Iyunithi Yexabiso:** Iyunithi esemgangathweni yokulinganisa esetyenziswa ukubonisa ixabiso leempahla neenkonzo.

**Imveliso Engasetyenziswanga YoTshintshiselwano:** Isixa esithile se-bitcoin esivela kutshintshiselwano lwangaphambili kwaye siphethwe sisitshixo sabucala somamkeli, esenza ukuba umsebenzisi lowo akwazi ukusisebenzisa.

**Isixhobo se-UTXO:** Ingqokelela yazo zonke ii-UTXO zeBitcoin ezikhoyo ukuze zisetyenziswe ngabanini bazo besebenzisa isitshixo sabo sabucala.

**Ukuguquguquka:** Inqanaba lotshintsho kwixabiso le-asethi ngokuhamba kwexesha.

**Ugcino lweWallet:** Ikopi yefreyizi yokubuyisela ye-wallet yeBitcoin, enokusetyenziswa ukubuyisela ukufikelela kwi-wallet xa elona qela liphambili lilahlekile okanye libiwe.

**Wallet:** Isikhongozeli esibonakalayo se-bitcoin esifana ne-wallet yomzimba eyenziwe ngesitshixo sabucala somsebenzisi kunye nee-UTXO eziphethwe sesi sitshixo.

**Whale:** Umntu okanye umbutho ophethe isixa esikhulu se-asethi ethile, kwaye ke unako ukuchaphazela amaxabiso emarike ngokurhweba okukhulu.

**Umphangi Omhlophe:** Umphangi okhaliphileyo owenza umsebenzi wakhe ngokuziphatha kakuhle, esebenzisa izakhono zakhe ukufumanisa nokulungisa izikhewu zokhuseleko kwiinkqubo zekhompyutha kunye neenethiwekhi.

**Iphepha EliMhlophe:** Ingxelo echaza umbono weprojekthi, iinjongo, itekhnoloji, kunye neenkcukacha zokuphunyezwa. Kwimeko yeBitcoin, lixwebhu elisisiseko elabhalwa nguSatoshi Nakamoto elenza ukuba le protocol yaziwe esidlangalaleni okokuqala.

**XBT kunye neBTC:** Izifinyezo ze-bitcoin.
