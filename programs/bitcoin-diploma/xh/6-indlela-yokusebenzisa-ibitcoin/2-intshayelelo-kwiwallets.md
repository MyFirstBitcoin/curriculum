# 6.2 Intshayelelo kwiWallets

Ngokungafaniyo nemali ebonakalayo, i-bitcoin ayigcinwanga ngokwenene kwi-wallet ye-Bitcoin. Endaweni yoko, zihlala kwi-ledger esasazekileyo eqinisekiswa kwaye ikhuseleke rhoqo yi-network ye-Bitcoin. Ke, ungayiba njani i-bitcoin?

Unobunini be-bitcoin yakho kuphela xa ulawula izitshixo zabucala ezikuvumela ukuba usayine iintengiselwano kwaye udlulisele ubunini be-bitcoin yakho komnye umntu. Le yindlela yokuthumela i-bitcoin.

Masijonge kwiingcamango ezimbini esizisebenzisayo xa sithetha igama elithi **wallet**:

* Isitshixo esikhulu sabucala, njengaloo password, apho izitshixo zakho zikawonke-wonke, ezifana nedilesi ye-imeyile, ziveliswa khona. Ungabelana ngedilesi yakho kawonke-wonke nabanye ukuze wamkele kwaye uthumele i-bitcoin, kodwa akufanele uze wabelane ngesitshixo sakho sabucala!
* Ujongano lweselfowuni okanye lwekhompyutha olusetyenziselwa ukunxibelelana ne-network ye-Bitcoin, ukujonga ibhalansi yakho ye-bitcoin, ukuthumela nokwamkela iintengiselwano, kunye nokuzisasaza kwi-network. Iindidi ezahlukeneyo zeewallet, kunye neenzuzo kunye neengxaki zazo, ziya kuchazwa kwiindawo ezilandelayo.

#### Iiwallet Zokuzigcina vs Iiwallet Zogcino-mali

Phambi kokuba sichaze iindidi ezahlukeneyo zee-wallet ze-Bitcoin kunye neempawu zazo, masenze umahluko obalulekileyo phakathi kweewallet zokuzigcina kunye neewallet zogcino-mali. Uhlobo ngalunye luneenzuzo zalo, imingcipheko, kunye nenqanaba lolawulo kwi-bitcoin. Ukuzigcina kuthetha ukuba umsebenzisi ugcina izitshixo zabucala kwaye ulawula ngokwenene i-bitcoin yakhe; kwi-wallet yogcino-mali, umntu wesithathu ugcina i-bitcoin egameni lomsebenzisi.


| Uhlobo | Ulawulo | Iinzuzo | Imingcipheko |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ukuzigcina | Umsebenzisi | Ulawulo olupheleleyo kwiimali kunye neentengiselwano, akukho nkqubo yokuvuma okanye ukuvalwa kweakhawunti, akukho lawulo lwenkampani okanye urhulumente, ukhuseleko ekuthathweni kwemali. | Akukho kubuyiselwa xa ilahleka iphrase yokubuyisela, uxanduva olupheleleyo lusemsebenzini. |
| Ugcino-mali | Umnikezeli wesithathu | Kulula ukubuyisela xa ulahlekelwe ukufikelela, inkxaso yabathengi elula. | Iimali zixhunyiwe kwi-Intanethi, zixhaphake ngakumbi ekugengeni. Umgcini-mali angavalela iiakhawunti. |


Kwi-wallet yokuzigcina (ekwabizwa ngokuba yi-non-custodial wallet), nguwe kuphela onazo izitshixo ze-wallet kwaye unolawulo olupheleleyo kwinto engenayo nophumayo. Kwelinye icala, kwi-wallet yogcino-mali omnye umntu ugcina isitshixo sabucala, emnika ulawulo olupheleleyo lokuhambisa nayiphi na i-bitcoin leyo umnikezeli ayilawulayo egameni lakho.

* Ukuzigcina kufana nokuba yibhanki yakho. Iintengiselwano aziphantsi kophicotho-zincwadi nolawulo
* Ukuzigcina kuqinisekisa ukuba abantu besithathu abanakuthatha i-bitcoin yakho.
* Ukuzigcina kunika uxolo lwengqondo ngexesha lokungaqiniseki, kuba uyazi ukuba i-bitcoin yakho ikhuselekile.

Kubalulekile ukukhetha uhlobo lwe-wallet olufanelekileyo kwiimfuno zomntu ngamnye. Ngamanye amaxesha, abantu bafumana kunzima ukwahlula ukuba bafaka i-wallet yokuzigcina okanye yogcino-mali. Le theyibhile ibonisa umahluko kwinkqubo yokufaka.


| Uhlobo | Inyathelo 1: Khetha | Inyathelo 2: Faka | Inyathelo 3: Yenza | Inyathelo 4: Khusela |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ukuzigcina | Khetha i-wallet yokuzigcina | Landela imiyalelo ye-wallet | Velisa iphrase yokubuyisela | Gcina iphrase yokubuyisela kwindawo ekhuselekileyo |
| Ugcino-mali | Khetha i-wallet yogcino-mali | Landela imiyalelo ye-wallet | Yenza iakhawunti | Akusebenzi |


“**Not your keys, not your coins**” sisaga esidumileyo phakathi kwabanini be-bitcoin. Sithetha ukuba ukuba awulawuli ngokuthe ngqo izitshixo zabucala ezinxulumene ne-wallet yakho ye-Bitcoin, awunabunini bokwenene beemali.

Nabani na ofumana izitshixo zakho zabucala unobunini be-bitcoin yakho. Kungenxa yoko le nto kubaluleke kakhulu ukuzikhusela ngokuzigcina kude namehlo angafunekiyo! Siza kubona iindlela ezimbalwa onokwenza ngazo oku kamva encwadini.

Kwicandelo elilandelayo, siza kuthetha ngeewallet zokuzigcina kuphela, apho umsebenzisi enezitshixo zakhe kwaye unolawulo olupheleleyo kwi-bitcoin yakhe.

Ungakhathazeki ukuba kubonakala kunzima okanye awuqondi yonke into — le luhambo, kwaye uya kuqonda ngcono xa uqala ukusebenzisa i-Bitcoin!

#### Iindidi Ezohlukeneyo Zee-Wallet ze-Bitcoin

Indawo apho isitshixo sakho sabucala senziwa kwaye sigcinwa ichaza indlela esichaza ngayo ii-wallet ze-Bitcoin. Ukuba izitshixo zikwiselfowuni yakho, yiyo **mobile wallet**. Ukuba zigcinwe ngokhuseleko kwisixhobo esizinikeleyo, yinto ebizwa ngokuba yi-**hardware wallet**.


| Uhlobo | Inkcazo | Izinto eziluncedo | Izinto ezingalunganga | Umzekelo womsebenzisi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I-Wallet ye-Intanethi | Ifumaneka ngewebhu | Ifikeleleka nakwesiphi na isixhobo esinokufikelela kwi-intanethi | Ayikhuselekanga kakhulu kuba inokugenca okanye ukuphazamiseka | Ufuna ukufikelela kwi-wallet yakhe rhoqo kwaye akanazo imali eninzi azigcinayo |
| I-Wallet yeselula | Ifakwe kwisixhobo seselula | Kulula ukuyisebenzisa | Inokulahleka ukuba isixhobo sibiwa okanye sigencwa | Ufuna ukwenza iintengiselwano ephatheni kwaye akanazo imali eninzi azigcinayo |
| I-Wallet ye-Desktop | Ifakwe kwikhompyutha ye-desktop | Iluncedo kwaye ifikeleleka naphi na | Inokugencwa ukuba ikhompyutha inesoftware enobungozi | Ufuna ukugcina i-bitcoin eninzi kwaye uziva ekhululekile ukusebenzisa ikhompyutha ye-desktop |
| Hardware Wallet | Isixhobo esibonakalayo esigcina i-bitcoin ngaphandle kwe-intanethi | Ikhuselekile ngakumbi kuneewallet ze-intanethi kwaye ingasetyenziswa ngaphandle kwe-intanethi | Imali inokungabuyiseki | Ufuna ukugcina i-bitcoin eninzi kwaye ukulungele ukuhlawula ukhuseleko olongezelelweyo |


Ngenxa yokuba izitshixo zinokuhanjiswa ukusuka kwesinye isixhobo ukuya kwesinye, “imeko” ye-wallet yakho ye-Bitcoin ayisoloko isisigxina. Umzekelo, ukuba ndenza izitshixo ze-wallet yam kwikhompyutha ndize ndizihambise kwifowuni yam kamva, i-“desktop wallet” iba yi-“mobile wallet.”

Xa kufikwa ekugcineni i-bitcoin yakho, akuphelelanga nje ekubeni ngubani onolawulo kwizitshixo — zininzi nezinye iingozi ekufuneka uziqwalasele. Yiyo loo nto kubalulekile ukufumana indlela yokugcina ekhuselekileyo kwaye elula. Xa uhlalutya izinto ezahlukeneyo zeewallet, uza kufunda ukuba akukho wallet igqibeleleyo enokuhlangabezana nazo zonke iimfuno.

##### Yintoni omawuyiqwalasele xa ukhetha i-wallet

* **Ukhuseleko**: Qinisekisa ukuba i-wallet inemigaqo-nkqubo yokhuseleko eqinileyo.
* **Ubumfihlo**: Qwalasela ukuba i-wallet ifuna ulwazi lomntu siqu.
* **Ukulula ukuyisebenzisa**: Khetha i-wallet ekulula ukuyisebenzisa nokuyijonga.
* **Ukuhambelana**: Qinisekisa ukuba i-wallet iyahambelana nesixhobo sakho.
* **Imirhumo**: Thelekisa imirhumo ehlawuliswa zi-wallet ezahlukeneyo.
* **Igama elihle**: Jonga igama labaphuhlisi ukuqinisekisa ukuba bayathembeka.
* **Ulawulo**: Ezinye ii-wallet zikunika ulawulo olungakumbi kwizitshixo zakho zabucala.

##### Open Source vs Closed Source

Enye into ebalulekileyo ekufuneka uyigcine engqondweni xa ukhetha i-wallet ye-Bitcoin kukuqonda ukuba usetyenziso okanye isoftware ivulelekile (open-source) na. Oku kubalulekile kuba iiprojekthi ezivulekileyo zivumela uluntu ukuba luhlolisise ikhowudi kwaye luqhubeke neprojekthi xa iqela liphumelele ukuyisebenza. Njengokuba ikhowudi ye-Bitcoin ivulekile ngokupheleleyo ukuze wonke umntu ayijonge, ayisebenzise, kwaye ayiguqule, kunjalo kufuneka ikhowudi ye-wallet oyisebenzisayo ukulawula i-bitcoin yakho ibe njalo.
