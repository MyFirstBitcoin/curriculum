# 6.3 Ukuseta Iwalethi Yeselula

#### Umsebenzi: Ukuseta kunye nokuBuyisela iWallet

Ngoku ukuba siqonda ngcono ii-wallet zeBitcoin kunye neendlela ezahlukeneyo phakathi kwazo, siza kubona indlela yokusebenzisa enye kwindlela esebenzayo. Kulo mzekelo, siza kwenza i-wallet yeselfowuni ngqo kwi-smartphone yethu. Ukuba abafundi abanazo ii-smartphone, utitshala uya kubanika enye ukuze bayisebenzise. Kukho iinketho ezimbini kulo msebenzi.

##### Ukhetho 1: Khuphela iWallet Entsha

**Indlela yokwenza nokusebenzisa i-wallet yeBitcoin:**

1. Khangela usetyenziso kwi-App Store (iOS) okanye kwi-Google Play Store (Android)
1. Vula usetyenziso uze ukhethe "Yenza i-wallet entsha". Isitshixo sakho sabucala senziwa ngokuzenzekelayo lusetyenziso lwakho.
1. Uya kucelwa ukuba ubhale phantsi uluhlu lwamagama ali-12 ukuya kwangama-24 uze uwagcine kwindawo ekhuselekileyo. Le yindlela yakho yokubuyisela i-wallet, **recovery phrase** (ekwabizwa ngokuba yi-seed phrase): ikuvumela ukuba ufumane ukufikelela ngokupheleleyo kwiimali zakho xa kukho imfuneko.**Khumbula ukuba ukuba ulahleka okanye ulibale olu luhlu lwamagama, awuyi kukwazi ukufikelela kwi-bitcoin yakho ukuba ulahlekelwa yi-wallet yakho. Kwakhona, ukuba nabani na ufumana i-recovery phrase yakho, baya kufumana ukufikelela kwi-bitcoin yakho!**
1. Kuya kufuneka uqinisekise ukuba ugcine i-recovery phrase yakho. Ukwenza oku, kufuneka ufake, ngokulandelelana okufanayo, amagama e-seed phrase yakho.
1. Njengenye indlela yokhuseleko, ezinye ii-wallet zikuvumela ukuba ukhethe igama lokugqitha elikhuselekileyo.
1. Emva kokuba ugcine i-recovery phrase yakho, ngenisa kwi-wallet. Khangela ukhetho oluthi "Yamkela": i-wallet yakho iya kuvelisa isitshixo sikawonke-wonke ukuze wamkele i-bitcoin.
1. Dlulisa i-bitcoin kwi-wallet yakho. Nge-wallet ozilawulayo, awusoloko unako ukuthenga i-bitcoin ngqo ngemali yesintu, ngoko unokufuna ukuyithenga uze uyidlulisele kwi-exchange kuqala.

Cinga ngesitshixo sakho **public key** njengedilesi yakho ye-imeyile: le uyabelana ngayo nabanye ukuze bakuthumele i-bitcoin (okanye, kwimeko ye-imeyile, i-imeyile).

Cinga ngesitshixo sakho **private key** njengelithi **igama lokugqitha** kwi-imeyile yakho: awuzukwabelana ngale nto nabani na, kuba ingabavumela ukufikelela kwi-imeyile yakho.

##### Ukhetho 2: Buyisela iWallet

**Khuphela i-wallet yeBitcoin uze ungeze ii-sats kubafundi ngamnye.**

Nikeza umfundi ngamnye iphepha eline **recovery phrase** ukuze abuyise i-wallet.

**Khokela abafundi inyathelo ngenyathelo:**

1. Xa uqala i-wallet yakho okokuqala, uya kubona iindlela ezintathu zokwenza i-wallet, cofa **[Ngenisa i-wallet ekhoyo]**. Uya kubona isikrini sesingeniso, cofa **[Buyisela nge-recovery phrase]**.
1. Faka amagama e-recovery phrase yakho nganye nganye, ngokulandelelana okufanelekileyo.
1. Uya kubona umyalezo wokuqinisekisa xa i-wallet yakho ingeniswe ngempumelelo. Imali yakho ebuyiselweyo ilungele ukusetyenziswa!
