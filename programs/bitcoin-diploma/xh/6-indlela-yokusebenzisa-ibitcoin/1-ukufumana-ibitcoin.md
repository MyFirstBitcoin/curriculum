# 6.1 Ukufumana iBitcoin

Zininzi iindlela zokufumana nokutshintshiselana nge-bitcoin. Umzekelo, unako:

* Uhlawulwe nge-bitcoin njengentlawulo yomsebenzi wakho kwaye uhlawule iimveliso kunye neenkonzo zabanye abantu usebenzisa i-bitcoin (siza kuthetha ngakumbi ngale nto kwiSahluko 7)
* Umgodi we-bitcoin (siza kuthetha ngakumbi ngale nto kwiSahluko 9)
* Tshintshiselana ngemali yakho yesiqhelo (i-Rand) nge-bitcoin okanye utshintshe i-bitcoin yakho ngemali yesiqhelo ubuso ngobuso.
* Tshintshiselana ngemali yakho yesiqhelo (i-Rand) nge-bitcoin okanye utshintshe i-bitcoin yakho ngemali yesiqhelo kwi-intanethi.

Ngezantsi, siza kuphonononga indlela yokutshintshiselana ngemali yesiqhelo ne-bitcoin nangokuphindaphindiweyo, zombini ngendlela yobuso ngobuso nakwi-intanethi, kuba ezi zezona ndlela zixhaphakileyo.

#### Peer-to-Peer: Ubuso ngobuso

Ukuthatha inxaxheba kwiintengiselwano phakathi kwabantu ababini (P2P) ukuthenga nokuthengisa i-bitcoin ubuso ngobuso kuthetha ukutshintshiselana ngokuthe ngqo ngemali yesiqhelo (okanye ezinye iimpahla okanye iinkonzo) nge-bitcoin nomnye umntu, ngaphandle kwesidingo sebhanki okanye omnye umntu ophakathi.

Zombini amaqela ayavumelana ngesixa semali kunye nemali yokutshintshiselana. Umthengi unika imali, umthengisi uthumela i-bitcoin, kwaye intengiselwano igqityiwe xa iqinisekisiwe kwi-blockchain. Ukutshintshiselana nge-bitcoin ngemali yesiqhelo kusebenza ngendlela efanayo ngokuphindaphindiweyo.

#### Peer-to-Peer: Kwi-Intanethi

Nangona kulula ukwenza utshintshiselwano phakathi kwabantu ababini ubuso ngobuso ngokudibana nomnye umntu kwihlabathi lokwenyani, oku kuza nomngcipheko othile — njengazo zonke ezinye iintengiselwano zemali ezibanjwa ubuso ngobuso. Yiyo loo nto abanye abantu bekhetha ukutshintshiselana nge-bitcoin kwi-intanethi, naphi na bakhoyo ngenxa ye-intanethi.

Nazi iindawo zokutshintshiselana phakathi kwabantu ababini kwi-intanethi, apho abathengi nabathengisi be-Bitcoin badibana khona kwi-cyberspace ukwenza iintengiselwano ngaphandle kwabalamli, ngokuthe ngqo kwi-intanethi.

Kwiindawo ezinjalo, akudingeki ukuba uthembe nabani na ngolwazi lwakho okanye ngemali yakho; udibana nabanye abantu kwaye nithengiselana ngokuthe ngqo.


> **Light**
>
> Kwiindawo ezininzi zokutshintshiselana phakathi kwabantu ababini kwi-intanethi, abantu kufuneka babeke imali ethile kwi-escrow ukuqinisekisa ukuba amaqela omabini aya kwenza oko avumelene ngako. I-escrow ithetha ukubeka imali kwindawo ekhuselekileyo phantsi kolawulo lweqonga de amaqela omabini enze oko athembisileyo. Kufana nomhlobo othembekileyo ogcina izinto zakho de wonke umntu aneliseke ngentengiselwano.


#### Iindawo zokutshintshiselana eziphakathi (Centralized Exchanges)

Iindawo zokutshintshiselana eziphakathi ziinkampani ezivumela abathengi ukuba bathenge kwaye bathengise i-bitcoin ngokuthe ngqo ngazo. Zezona ndlela zilula zokufumana nokuthengisa i-bitcoin, kodwa le lula iza nemingeni emikhulu.

##### Imingeni yeZiko lokuTshintshiselana (Centralized Exchanges Trade-Offs)

Kubalulekile ukuqaphela ukuba xa uthenga i-bitcoin kwiindawo zokutshintshiselana eziphakathi, rhoqo kufuneka unikeze ulwazi lwakho lomntu siqu kwaye uqinisekise isazisi sakho. Oku kudala umngcipheko wokuba ulwazi lwakho lungabi khuselekile kwaye lube semngciphekweni wokuba luphangeleke. Ukongeza, ezi ndawo zigcina i-bitcoin yakho egameni lakho, okuthetha ukuba awulawuli imali yakho de uyirhoxise.

Ukongeza kwezi ngxaki, iindawo zokutshintshiselana eziphakathi zinokusebenzisa kakubi imali yomsebenzisi okanye zithengise i-bitcoin engaphezulu kunaleyo zinayo de ziwile — Ewe, kanye njengeebhanki! Ngaphandle kokuba, kwihlabathi le-Bitcoin, akukho bhanki ephakathi yokusindisa iibhanki ezikhohlakeleyo ngokushicilela imali engakumbi, kuba awukwazi ukuprinta i-bitcoin engakumbi!
