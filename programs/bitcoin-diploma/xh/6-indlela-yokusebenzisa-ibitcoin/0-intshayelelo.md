# 6.0 Intshayelelo

> Kutheni umntu angathembela imali yama-nerd xa kuthelekiswa nemali yebhanki ephakathi? Ama-nerd akuzisele i-intanethi. Iibhanki zikuzisele uQoqosho oluKhulu lweHlabathi.  
_Satoshi Nakamoto_

Ngoku sele siqonda ngcono ukuba yintoni i-bitcoin kunye nenjongo yayo, lixesha lokufunda indlela yokuyisebenzisa ngokwenene. Kolu candelo, siza kukukhokela inyathelo ngenyathelo kwinkqubo yokufumana i-bitcoin, siphonononge iintlobo ezahlukeneyo zeewallets ezikhoyo, sikuncede usete eyakho iBitcoin wallet, kwaye siqhelise ukuthumela nokulandelela intengiselwano yeBitcoin kwinethiwekhi. Lixesha lokusebenzisa ulwazi lwakho kwisenzo!
