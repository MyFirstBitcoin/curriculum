# 6.4 Ukufumana kunye nokuthumela iintengiselwano

Intengiselwano yeBitcoin litshintshelo lobunini bebitcoin kumntu omtsha. Qaphela ukuba akusosimali uqobo oludluliselwa, koko bubunini baso: ngamanye amazwi, ilungelo lokulusebenzisa. Rhoqo xa intengiselwano yamkelwa kwibhloko, zonke i-node kwinethiwekhi zihlaziya ikopi yazo yencwadi yoluntu ukubonisa utshintsho lobunini. Kule ndlela, intengiselwano yeBitcoin ifana ngakumbi nentengiselwano yezindlu (okanye enye impahla) kunentengiselwano yemali.

Ukuze "uthumele" i-bitcoin, umthumeli usayina umyalezo ngeyona nto yakhe yabucala, ebonisa inethiwekhi ukuba umnini wokwenene we-bitcoin udlulisele ubunini kumamkeli.

I-bitcoin ngoku iya kudityaniswa nedilesi yomamkeli, imnika ubunini be-bitcoin, ukuze kuphela umnini omtsha angayisebenzisa esebenzisa isitshixo sakhe sabucala.

Iintengiselwano ezintsha zeBitcoin ziqalelwa kwi-wallets kwihlabathi lonke, kodwa akukho msebenzi ophakathi wokuhlawula. Endaweni yoko, abavukuzi bayakhuphisana ukurekhoda iintengiselwano kwincwadi.

Masithi uSipho uboleke uNoluthando i-0.5 BTC kwaye ulungele ukumhlawula. Bobabini bane-wallets zedijithali.

1. UNoluthando wabelana ngedilesi yakhe noSipho.
1. USipho usebenzisa isoftware ye-wallet yakhe ukwenza intengiselwano, equka idilesi kaNoluthando, isixa esiza kudluliselwa (0.5 BTC), kunye nemali yomvukuzi. Imali ephezulu yenza kube lula ukuba umvukuzi ayifake intengiselwano kwibhloko elandelayo.
1. Emva kokusayina intengiselwano, iyasakazwa kwinethiwekhi, apho iqinisekiswa khona zi-node. Zijonga ukuba uSipho unayo na imali eyaneleyo kwaye ungumnini wokwenene weemali azimisele ukuzisebenzisa. Ukuba akunjalo, bayayikhaba intengiselwano kwangoko.
1. Xa intengiselwano iqinisekisiwe, abavukuzi bakhetha ukuba bayifake na intengiselwano kwibhloko elandelayo, ngokuqhelekileyo ngokusekelwe kwimali ekhethiweyo. Xa intengiselwano ingene kwibhloko, yongezwa kwi-blockchain kwaye imali idluliselwa kwidilesi kaNoluthando.
1. Ubunini budluliselwe kuNoluthando. Ngoku angasebenzisa isitshixo sakhe sabucala ukusebenzisa imali.

_Kubalulekile ukuqaphela ukuba xa intengiselwano igqityiwe, ayinakuguqulwa._


> **Note – Indlela Esisebenza Ngayo Intengiselwano yeBitcoin**
>
> 1. Umntu ucelile intengiselwano
> 1. Intengiselwano isakazwa kwiikhompyutha zeP2P (ii-node)
> 1. Abavukuzi baqinisekisa intengiselwano
> 1. Iintengiselwano zidibaniswa ukwenza ibhloko yedatha
> 1. Ibhloko entsha yongezwa kwi-blockchain ekhoyo
> 1. Intengiselwano igqityiwe



> **Note – Ukufumana Iintengiselwano zeBitcoin**
>
> Ukuze ufumane i-bitcoin, kuya kufuneka unike umthumeli idilesi yakho kawonke-wonke yeBitcoin. Le yintambo eyodwa yeencwadi kunye namanani amele i-wallet yakho kwaye isetyenziselwa ukuyichonga kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin.
>
> Ungayifumana idilesi yakho kawonke-wonke ngokuvula i-wallet yakho yeBitcoin kwaye ukhangele ukhetho oluthi “Receive” okanye “Deposit” bitcoin.
>
> Ungabelana ngedilesi yakho yeBitcoin ngeendlela ezininzi:
>
> 1. **Kopa kwaye uncamathisele idilesi**: Ungakopa idilesi ngokuyigqamisa uze ucofe "Copy", emva koko uyincamathisele kwi-imeyile okanye umyalezo.
> 1. **Yabelana ngekhonkco kwi-wallet yakho yeBitcoin**: Ezinye ii-wallets zeBitcoin zikuvumela ukuba wenze ikhonkco kwi-wallet yakho onokuthi wabelane ngalo nomthumeli. Bangacofoza kwikhonkco ukuze bafikelele kwi-wallet yakho kwaye bathumele i-bitcoin.
> 1. **Yabelana ngeQR code**: Ukuba umthumeli une-smartphone enohlelo lwe-wallet yeBitcoin, angaskena i-QR code ukuze afumane idilesi yakho yeBitcoin.


Xa umthumeli sele enedilesi yakho, angakuthumelela i-bitcoin ngokufaka idilesi yakho kunye nesixa afuna ukusithumela. I-bitcoin emva koko ithunyelwa ukusuka kwi-wallet yakhe iye kwi-wallet yakho.

Intengiselwano iqinisekiswa yiNethiwekhi yeBitcoin kwaye idla ngokuthatha malunga nemizuzu eli-10. Ukuze ukhuseleke ngakumbi, kucetyiswa ukuba ulinde iziqinisekiso ezimbini, ezithatha malunga nemizuzu engama-20.


> **Note – Ukuthumela Iintengiselwano zeBitcoin**
>
> Ukuze uthumele i-bitcoin, uya kufuna izinto ezimbalwa: i-wallet yeBitcoin, idilesi kawonke-wonke yomamkeli, kunye nesixa se-bitcoin ofuna ukusithumela.
>
> 1. Vula i-wallet yakho yeBitcoin.
> 1. Yiya kwi “Send” uze uncamathisele idilesi yomamkeli kwindawo ethi "To". Okanye, unokuskene i-QR code ukuba umamkeli uyinike.
> 1. Faka isixa se-bitcoin ofuna ukusithumela kwindawo ethi “Amount”.
> 1. Qinisekisa kabini idilesi yomamkeli kunye nesixa esiza kuthunyelwa. Khumbula iintengiselwano azinakuguqulwa!
> 1. Phambi kokucofa “Confirm and Send”, sicebisa ukuba uqinisekise iinkcukacha zentengiselwano kwakhona ukuze uqiniseke ukuba uthumela isixa esifanelekileyo se-bitcoin kwidilesi efanelekileyo.
> 1. Sakaza intengiselwano uze ulinde inethiwekhi iqinisekise intengiselwano.
>
> Ngoku uyazi indlela yokuvavanya, ukukhetha, kunye nokuseta i-wallet yeBitcoin ozilawulayo. Ukuthumela nokufumana i-bitcoin kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin kubizwa ngokuba ziintengiselwano “on-chain”. Oku kungenxa yokuba iintengiselwano zenzeka kwinethiwekhi ephambili yeBitcoin kwaye zirekhodwa kwi-blockchain.
>
> Iintengiselwano ezise-on-chain zezona zikhuselekileyo zokwenza intengiselwano nge-bitcoin ngenxa yokuqinisekiswa okwenziwa yile nethiwekhi engaphantsi kolawulo.
>
> Nangona kunjalo, iintengiselwano ezise-on-chain zicotha kwaye zinokubiza kakhulu kunezinye iindlela (esiza kuxoxa ngazo kwiSifundo 7) ngenxa yemali yomvukuzi.

###### Amanqaku Aphambili

1. Kukho iindidi ezine zabathathi-nxaxheba kuyo yonke intengiselwano ye-bitcoin: umthumeli, umamkeli, abavukuzi, kunye nabaqhubi bee-node.
1. Umthumeli kufuneka avume (asayine ngokwe-cryptography) **isixa se-bitcoin** sokuthumela KWAYE **idilesi ethile** yokuthumela kuyo.
1. Umamkeli kufuneka anikeze nge-**idilesi esebenzayo** kumthumli KWAYE aqinisekise ukuba intengiselwano iqinisekiswe ngempumelelo kwi-blockchain.
1. Abavukuzi baqinisekisa ukuba zonke iimfuno ziyasebenza phambi kokuba bongeze iintengiselwano kwiibhloko ezizayo.
1. Abasebenzisi beenodi baqinisekisa ukuba iibhloko ezivunyiweyo ziyasebenza phambi kokuhlaziya inguqulelo yabo ye-blockchain (incwadi yemali).

###### Ingcebiso kubafundi

Jikeleza kuzo zonke izikhundla ezine ukuze ufumane amava omsebenzi womntu ngamnye.
