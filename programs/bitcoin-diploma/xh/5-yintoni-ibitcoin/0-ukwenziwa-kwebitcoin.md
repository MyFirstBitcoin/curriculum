# 5.0 Ukwenziwa kweBitcoin

> Abantu abaninzi bayayilahla ngokuzenzekelayo imali yedijithali njengento engenakwenzeka ngenxa yeenkampani ezininzi ezasile ukusukela ngeminyaka yoo-1990. Ndiyathemba kuyacaca ukuba yindlela yokulawulwa embindini kwezo nkqubo eyabangela ukuba ziphumelele. Njengoko sabona kwisifundo esidlulileyo, amaCypherpunks amaninzi azama ukudala enye indlela yemali. Esi sifundo siqhubekeka nembali yomnye wabo: umbono obalaseleyo owaziwa njenge “Satoshi Nakamoto”. Lo mntu ongaziwayo (okanye iqela), kudala phambi kweBitcoin, wayethatha inxaxheba kwiingxoxo ezikwi-intanethi malunga ne-cryptography kunye nekhompyutha ukuze kufunyanwe iindlela ezisebenzayo zokutshintsha inkqubo yemali ye-fiat.

Ngo-Okthobha ka-2008, uNakamoto wabhengeza uxwebhu olutsha olubalulekileyo olubizwa ngokuba yi “Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System” kuluhlu lwe-imeyile lwe-cryptography. Olu xwebhu lwabeka isiseko senkqubo ye-peer-to-peer engaxhomekekanga, eyenzelwe ukuququzelela ukuthengiselana okukhuselekileyo kwi-intanethi ngaphandle kwesidingo sabalamli. Umbono kaNakamoto wawucacile: ukudala uhlobo lwemali yedijithali olusekelwe kubantu bodwa, lukhululekile kulawulo lwemimandla enamandla kunye neenkampani zezezimali.

Nangona ubuwena bukaNakamoto bungaziwa, injongo yakhe yayingacacanga: ukuthabatha amandla kwabambalwa aze ayibuyisele ebantwini abaninzi ngokudala inkqubo yemali evulekileyo, ecacileyo, engenaziko, ezimeleyo kurhulumente.  
Bitcoin yayisisimanga kwimicimbi yezezimali ka-2008, eyabetha abantu abaqhelekileyo ngelixa izizityebi zixhamla. Yanika enye indlela kwinkohlakalo kunye nobuthathaka benkqubo ye-fiat. UNakamoto wabeka isiseko sento entsha, waza wakhetha ukungazithathi izidima.

NgoJanuwari 3, 2009, uNakamoto wagrumba ibhloko yokuqala yeBitcoin, eyaziwa njenge genesis block. Oku kwabhengeza ukuqaliswa kwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin, inkqubo engaxhomekekanga kuthembeko, ekhuselekileyo ngebhuku-mali elisasazekileyo.

Ngo-2011, emva kokubonisa ukuba inethiwekhi ingaqhuba ngaphandle kwakhe, uNakamoto wasuka, eshiya iBitcoin ezandleni zabanye ababelana nombono wakhe.

Kwiminyaka elandelayo, abantu abaninzi bajoyina baza banegalelo. IBitcoin yakhula yaba luphawu lwethemba kunye namandla, inika indlela ekhuselekileyo, engenakuthintelwa yokuthengiselana. Njengenkqubo evulekileyo, akukho mntu unokuyilawula, kwaye nabani na angathatha inxaxheba.

Namhlanje, umbono kaNakamoto wenkqubo yezezimali engenamda necacileyo uyaqhubeka. Abantu kwihlabathi liphela bakhetha iBitcoin, kwaye uqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin luyavela kwiindawo ezininzi.
