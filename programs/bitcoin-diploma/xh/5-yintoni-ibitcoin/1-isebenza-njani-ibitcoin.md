# 5.1 Isebenza Njani iBitcoin?

#### Indlela Yokuvumelana kaNakamoto

Ke, iBitcoin isebenza njani? IBitcoin inezinto ezininzi ezahlukeneyo, kwaye xa uqala ukuyifunda, ungafumanisa ukuba inzulu kakhulu. Ngethamsanqa, ukuba ungumntu omtsha kwiBitcoin, akufuneki uqonde yonke into ngokupheleleyo ukuze uqale ukuyisebenzisa.

Kufana nendlela abasebenzisi abaninzi bengayazi indlela i-intanethi isebenza ngayo: abantu abaninzi abazi ukuba i-TCP/IP protocol isebenza njani, kodwa basathumela ii-imeyile nemiyalezo, kwaye bapapasha umxholo kwiintanethi zabo zentlalo yonke imihla. Kufana nokuqhuba imoto — abantu abaninzi abazi ukuba imoto isebenza njani ngokweenkcukacha, kodwa bayakwazi ukuqhuba.


> **Callout – IBitcoin ayikagqitywa ukwamkelwa ngokubanzi okwangoku**
>
> IBitcoin ayikagqitywa ukwamkelwa ngokubanzi okwangoku. Sisixhobo esitsha, njengendlela i-intanethi yayinjalo kwiminyaka yee-90s. Ngenxa yoku, kunokuba luncedo ukugxila kwiziseko zeBitcoin, endaweni yokugxila kwiinkcukacha zobugcisa bayo.


Ingcamango ephambili ngendlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo ingacatshulwa ngomhlathi omnye: IBitcoin yimithetho eqhelekileyo evunyelwene ngayo ngabo bonke abathathi-nxaxheba kwinethiwekhi. Ungayicinga njengomdlalo webhodi odlalwa nabahlobo. Kumdlalo onjengeMonopoly, niyavumelana nabanye abadlali ngemithetho ethile. Enye yemithetho yeMonopoly kukuba kufuneka kusetyenziswe kuphela "iibhili zeMonopoly". Ukuba uSipho (omnye wabadlali) angathatha isigqibo sokusebenzisa iphepha langasese endaweni yeebhili zeMonopoly ukuthenga indlu, abanye abadlali bangamxelela ukuba uyakhohlisa baze bayeke ukudlala naye. Ngamafutshane, ukuze udlale umdlalo, kufuneka kube nokuvumelana kwimigaqo kwaye nize nivumelane ukuba ningaphambuki kuyo, kungenjalo niya kugxothwa.

Le yindlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo. IBitcoin yindawo yabantu abavumelana ngemithetho efanayo. Le mithetho ibhalwe ngeendlela ezinzulu zobalo, ibhalwe kwikhompyutha, kwaye yamkelwa ngqo ngabo bonke abasebenzisa isoftware yeBitcoin. Imithetho yeBitcoin isebenza kubo bonke abathathi-nxaxheba ngokulinganayo, oko kuthetha ukuba wonke umdlali kufuneka alandele imithetho yomdlalo okanye angakwazi ukudlala kuba inethiwekhi iza kumgxotha.

Umzekelo, enye yemithetho yeBitcoin ithi "Akusoze kubekho iBitcoin ezingaphezu kwezigidi ezingama-21." Ukuba umntu angazama ukwenza iBitcoin ezizigidi ezongezelelweyo, ngeke zibe luncedo kuye, kuba bonke abanye baya kumqaphela baze bamgxothe. Le nto yenza iBitcoin yomelele kangaka.


> **Dark**
>
> Akhonto ibalulekileyo ukuba ungubani okanye uvela phi: ukuba ungene kwihlabathi leBitcoin, kufuneka udlale ngemithetho efanayo nabanye bonke.


Oku kusebenza nakubantu kunye nemibutho enempembelelo enkulu kwihlabathi lefiat. KwiBitcoin, akukho ndawo yokukhohlisa okanye ukonakalisa — wonke umntu uphathwa ngokulinganayo, kwaye akukho mntu unokutshintsha loo nto.


> **Callout**
>
> Uyazi ukuba, ukusukela ngo-2009, iBitcoin iye yamelana nemizamo yamawaka yokuyihacka, ukuyitshintsha, okanye ukuyonakalisa? IBitcoin ibonakalise rhoqo ukuba akukho mntu unokuyimisa, ukuyilawula, okanye ukuyiphazamisa.


#### Abadlali Bomdlalo

Ukuze siqonde ngcono indlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo ngokungaphakathi, kufuneka singene nzulu kwiindima ezahlukeneyo kwinethiwekhi. KwiBitcoin, abathathi-nxaxheba abohlukeneyo badlala indima ezahlukeneyo kodwa ezihambelanayo, bephuhlisa ukusebenza kakuhle kweprotocol.

##### 1. Abavukuzi: Abakhi Bokhuseleko

Abavukuzi bangumqolo weBitcoin. Basebenza ngasemva ukuqinisekisa nokukhusela inethiwekhi besebenzisa indlela ebizwa ngokuba yiProof-of-Work (PoW).

Abadlali aba banomatshini abakhethekileyo abanamandla amakhulu okusebenza. Bafaka la mandla kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin, bekhuphisana omnye nomnye kumdyarho wehlabathi wonke wokongeza iibhloko ezintsha zotshintshiselwano kwincwadi yeBitcoin (i-blockchain). Ukuzinikela kwabo kuqinisekisa ukuba incwadi ayinakutshintshwa kwaye ikhuseleke kwiintshutshiso ezimbi.

Indalo yokuzimela kwabavukuzi ithetha ukuba nabani na angathatha inxaxheba — kodwa enyanisweni, ukhuphiswano lukhulu. Njengomvuzo wokunceda kwabo, umvukuzi wokuqala osombulula iphazili ufumana iBitcoin entsha, umvuzo obizwa ngokuba yi-block reward.

Abavukuzi beBitcoin basasazeke kwihlabathi lonke, begcina inethiwekhi ikhuselekile kwaye ingagxilwanga, beqinisekisa ukuba ukhuseleko lweBitcoin luhlala lomelele kwaye lusasazekile.

##### 2. Iinodi: Abagcini Bokuvavanya

Iinodi zeBitcoin ziqhutywa ngabantu abaqhelekileyo kwihlabathi lonke. Aba bathathi-nxaxheba basebenza njengabagcini benethiwekhi yeBitcoin ngokusebenzisa isoftware yeBitcoin kwiikhompyutha zabo apho bagcina ikopi yonke yencwadi. Iinodi zivavanya utshintshiselwano kwaye ziqinisekisa ukuba bonke abathathi-nxaxheba balandela imithetho yokuvumelana.

Ngokusasaza uxanduva lokuvavanya phakathi kwenethiwekhi yeenodi, iBitcoin ihlala yomelele kwiintshutshiso kwaye igcina ukungathembani. Iinodi zidlala indima ebalulekileyo ekugcineni ukuthembeka kwencwadi, zize zibe yinxalenye yenkcubeko yokuzimela yeBitcoin.

##### 3. Abasebenzisi: Abathathi-nxaxheba Abagunyazisiweyo

Abasebenzisi — umphefumlo wenethiwekhi yeBitcoin — ngabantu abenza utshintshiselwano. Ungabacinga njengabantu abaqhelekileyo abaye bazigunyazisa ngokusebenzisa iBitcoin ebomini babo. Umzekelo, abanye abasebenzisi bagcina imali yabo kwiBitcoin ngelixa abanye beyisebenzisa ukuthenga ukutya okanye ukufumana umvuzo wabo.

IBitcoin inika abasebenzisi amandla ngokususa isidingo sabalamli abafana neebhanki noorhulumente, ivumela utshintshiselwano oluthe ngqo phakathi kwabantu. Oku kuthetha ukuba abasebenzisi banolawulo olupheleleyo kwimali yabo nakwiintshukumo zabo.

##### 4. Abaphuhlisi neeProjekthi: Abakhi boPhuhliso

Inkqubo yemali yekamva ayizukuzakhela yona, kwaye ayizukwamkelwa kwihlabathi lonke ngendlela efanelekileyo ngaphandle komzamo. Apho ke bangena khona abaphuhlisi beBitcoin kunye neeprojekthi. Abaphuhlisi basebenzisa ubuchule babo bobugcisa ukuphucula nokwenza izinto ezintsha kwiBitcoin protocol. Aba bantu bafaka ikhowudi, bacebisa ukuphuculwa, kwaye bajongana nezisilelo, beqinisekisa ukuba inethiwekhi iyakhula xa ijongene nemingeni eyahlukeneyo. Uhlobo lweBitcoin oluvulekileyo luvumela intsebenziswano, apho abaphuhlisi kwihlabathi lonke banokunceda ekukhuleni kwayo.

Ubuhle bophuhliso olusasazekileyo kukuba akukho mntu mnye unokulawula ngokupheleleyo i-protocol. Oku kwenzeka ngenkqubo yokuvumelana. Abaphuhlisi bacebisa izimvo notshintsho, kwaye kuphela abo banemibono engcono ehambelana nombono omkhulu wehlabathi elingcono bafumana inkxaso kuluntu, bephakamisa ukukhula kweBitcoin ngendlela evulekileyo nedemokhrasi njengoko ikhula ukuya kubantu abazizigidi ezisibhozo.

Iiprojekthi zeBitcoin zibandakanya amaqela ahlukeneyo, ukusuka kwiingqungquthela ezingenzi nzuzo, iinkampani, ukuya kumaqela nabantu abavelisa umxholo onexabiso. Aba bantu basebenzisana kwiinjongo ezithile ngaphakathi komsebenzi omkhulu weBitcoin wokukhulula uluntu. Iiprojekthi zeBitcoin zidlala indima ebalulekileyo ekwakheni nasekukhuthazeni ukwamkelwa kweBitcoin, zisebenza kwikamva elijolise ekomelezeni nasekukhululeni uluntu.

#### Ingoma Entle

Ukuzimela kweBitcoin kunokuthelekiswa nomculo odlalwa ngoomatshini abaninzi, apho wonke umculi udlala indima yakhe ukwenza umculo omhle. Akukho mphathi kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin: abavukuzi, iinodi, abasebenzisi, abaphuhlisi, kunye neeprojekthi basebenza ngokuzimela nangentsebenziswano.

Incwadi yokubhalisa esasazekileyo, egcinwa ziindawo zeenodi, iqinisekisa ukucaca, ngelixa indlela ye-proof-of-work inika ukhuseleko kwaye ithintele ukugxila kwamandla kubavukuzi; abasebenzisi bafumana inkululeko yemali kunye namandla, bekhululekile kulawulo lwenkqubo yemali yesiqhelo; abaphuhlisi, bekhokelwa kukuvumelana, baqinisekisa ukuba i-protocol iyazivumelanisa neemfuno zoluntu eziguqukayo; iiprojekthi zeBitcoin, ngendlela yazo eyahlukileyo, zenza igalelo kwinjongo enkulu yokukhulula uluntu.

Umntu ngamnye kolu lwandiso lomculo oluzimeleyo udlala indima ebalulekileyo ekwakheni ukwamkelwa kweBitcoin nasekomelezeni uluntu, enegalelo ekomelezeni nasekuphila ixesha elide kweBitcoin, esenza indawo engenamda, engenakuthembana, nenika amandla.

_Activity: Consensus_


Lo ngumsebenzi weklasi apho abathathi-nxaxheba bafunda ngokwabo ukuba kunzima kangakanani ukuvumelanisa intshukumo kwiqela elingenamphathi ocacileyo. Injongo kukuba abathathi-nxaxheba baqonde indlela ukuvumelana (consensus) kufezekiswa ngayo kwiBitcoin.

###### Amanqaku Aphambili

1. **Ukuvumelana = ukuvumelana**
1. Umahluko omkhulu phakathi kweqela elilawulwa ngumntu omnye kunye nelingalawulwa kukuba kukho umbuzo wokuthembana. Amaqela azimeleyo afana neenethiwekhi ezisebenza phakathi kwabantu abaninzi awanampathi kwaye abathathi-nxaxheba abathembani. Afuna indlela eyahlukileyo yokusebenzisana.
1. Kubaphuhlisi beenethiwekhi ezisebenza phakathi kwabantu abaninzi, oku kwaziwa njengeByzantine Generals Problem. IBitcoin isombulula le ngxaki ngobalo kunye nokumba nge-proof-of-work.
1. Ukuzimela kweBitcoin kubalulekile kwixabiso layo. Kwimbali, iinkokeli zoluntu zisoloko ziwonakalisa amaxabiso emali ekuhambeni kwexesha.
1. Ukuvumelana kukaNakamoto kuthiywe ngegama lomdali weBitcoin, uSatoshi Nakamoto. Le ndlela yokuvumelana yindlela amawaka abantu angazaniyo nabangathembaniyo agcine ngayo incwadi yeBitcoin ukusukela ngo-2009.
