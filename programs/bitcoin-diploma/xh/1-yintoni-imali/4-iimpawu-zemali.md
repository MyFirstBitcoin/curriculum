# 1.4 Iimpawu zeMali

Imali isebenza kakuhle kuphela ukuba inezinto ezithile ezibalulekileyo. Kufuneka ilungele ukuthenga nokuthengisa (indlela yokutshintshiselana), ukugcina imali ukuze isetyenziswe kamva (indlela yokugcina ixabiso), kunye nokulinganisa amaxabiso (iyunithi yokubala). Ezi mpawu zenza imali ithembeke kwaye ibe nentsingiselo.

* **Ukuhlala ixesha elide** ibhekisa kumandla emali ukumelana nokonakala ngokwasemzimbeni kwaye ihlale ixesha elide. Oku kuqinisekisa ukuba imali inokujikeleza kuqoqosho ikwimeko eyamkelekileyo kwaye ichongiwe lula. Igolide yinto ehlala ixesha elide enokumelana nokunxitywa, nto leyo eyenza ibe ngumzekelo olungileyo wokuhlala ixesha elide kwemali.
* **Ukuhlulahlula** ibhekisa kumandla emali ukuhlulwa ibe ziinxalenye ezincinci ukuze abantu bakwazi ukuyisebenzisa ukuthenga izinto ezixabisa imali eyahlukeneyo. Amaphepha emali anokwahlulwa lula abe ziimali ezincinci, nto leyo eyenza abe ngumzekelo olungileyo wokuhlulahlula kwemali.
* **Ukuthwaleka** ibhekisa ekubeni lula ukuthwala imali nokuyiphatha naphi na. Oku kuvumela abantu ukuba basebenzise imali ukuthenga nokuthengisa iimpahla neenkonzo ngaphandle kobunzima. Amakhadi etyala ayathwaleka, kuba anokuthwalwa lula ephaketheni okanye ebhegini, nto leyo eyenza abe ngumzekelo olungileyo wokuthwaleka kwemali.
* **Ukwamkelwa** ibhekisa ekwamkelweni ngokubanzi kwemali njengendlela yokuhlawula ukuze abantu bakwazi ukuyisebenzisa ukuthenga nokuthengisa iimpahla neenkonzo ngokuzithemba. Idola yaseMelika yamkelwa ngokubanzi njengendlela yokuhlawula, nto leyo eyenza ibe ngumzekelo olungileyo wokwamkelwa kwemali.
* **Ukungabikho ngokwaneleyo** ibhekisa ekubeni nzima ukwenza ezinye iiyunithi zemali, nto leyo egcina ixabiso layo kwaye ithintele ukuba kufuneka sichithe imali eninzi ukuthenga izinto ezifanayo. Iimpawu zokuqokelela, ngakumbi ezo zinqabileyo nezixabisekileyo, zinokuba yindlela elungileyo yemali kuba azikho zininzi kwaye zinokunyuka ngexabiso ngokuhamba kwexesha. Abaqokeleli beempawu bahlala bezisebenzisa njengeyona ndlela yokutyala ubutyebi babo kunye nokwahlula iindlela zabo zotyalo-mali.
* **Ukufana** ibhekisa ekutshintshiselaneni kwemali ukuze iyunithi enye yemali ilingane nenye iyunithi exabisa okufanayo. Imali kufuneka ifane. Iinkozo zobhedu zifana ngobukhulu nobunzima, nto leyo eyenza zibe ngumzekelo olungileyo wokufana kwemali. Iisenthi enye ihlala iyisenthi enye.

#### Umsebenzi

Iimpahla ezahlukeneyo zineempawu ezahlukeneyo kwaye zenza imisebenzi yemali ngendlela eyahlukeneyo. Uluntu ekugqibeleni luthatha isigqibo sokuba yeyiphi impahla esetyenziswa njengemali ngokusekelwe ekubeni le mpahla isebenza kakuhle kangakanani kule misebenzi.

Ukuze kugqitywe ukuba izinto ezahlukeneyo zihlangabezana kakuhle kangakanani neempawu zemali, unokunika inqaku kwinto nganye ngezantsi ukusuka ku-1 ukuya ku-5 kwimpawu nganye (0 = Imbi kakhulu; 3 = Kulungile; 5 = Kugqwesile).

Ngokudibanisa amanqaku ezinto nganye, unokufumanisa ukuba yeyiphi efaneleka kakhulu ukuba ibe yimali.

**Sebenzisa le mibuzo ilandelayo** ukukunceda ugqibe ukuba izinto ezahlukeneyo ezikwi theyibhile zihlangabezana kakuhle kangakanani neempawu zemali.

* **Ukuhlala ixesha elide:** Ngaba imali inokumelana nokonakala ngokuhamba kwexesha?
* **Ukuthwaleka:** Ngaba imali inokuthwalwa lula kwaye isetyenziswe kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo?
* **Ukufana:** Ngaba imali ingatshintshiselwa kwezinye iindlela zemali?
* **Ukwamkelwa:** Ngaba imali yamkelwa ngokubanzi njengendlela yokuhlawula?
* **Ukungabikho ngokwaneleyo:** Ngaba imali ayikho ngokwaneleyo kwaye kunzima ukwenza enye?
* **Ukuhlulahlula:** Ngaba imali inokwahlulwa ibe ziinxalenye ezincinci?
