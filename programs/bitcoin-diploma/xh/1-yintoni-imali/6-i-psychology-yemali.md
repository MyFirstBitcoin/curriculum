# 1.6 I-Psychology yeMali

Zicinga ukuba uvaleleke entlango kwaye unebhotile enye kuphela yamanzi eseleyo. Unxaniwe kwaye ufuna amanzi kakhulu, kodwa uyazi ukuba uza kuwadinga la manzi ukuze usinde de ufumane eminye. Le yimeko eqhelekileyo yokunqongophala kwezinto: unexabiso elilinganiselweyo lomthombo (amanzi) kwaye kufuneka wenze izigqibo malunga nendlela ongawasebenzisa ngayo kakuhle.

Kule meko, unokugqiba ekubeni uyawagcina la manzi kwaye usele kancinci kancinci ixesha elide ukuze akwane ixesha elide kangangoko kunokwenzeka. Okanye, unokukhetha ukusela kangangoko unako ngexesha elinye, ukwanelisa ukunxanwa kwakho okwethutyana, kodwa le nto inokunganeli ukukunika amandla okufuna amanye amanzi kwixesha elizayo. Nokuba yeyiphi na into oyikhethayo, ujongene nesigqibo esinzima.

Ukunqongophala kwezinto kusebenza kuzo zonke izinto, hayi amanzi kuphela. Nokuba yimali, lixesha okanye uthando kunye nenkathalelo, sihlala sijongene nezinto ekufuneka sizikhethe malunga nendlela yokwabela izixhobo zethu ezilinganiselweyo.


> **Callout**
>
> **Ukunqongophala kwezinto** kusinyanzela ukuba silinganise izibonelelo kunye neengxaki ngendlela esisebenzisa ngayo izixhobo zethu kwaye senze utshintsho.


##### Kukho iintlobo ezimbini zokunqongophala kwezinto

* **Ukunqongophala kwezinto okwenziweyo**, okwaziwa njengekunqongophala okuphakathi, kubandakanya izinto ezifana neebhegi zoyilo ezikhutshwa kancinci, amakhadi emidlalo angafumanekiyo, kunye nemisebenzi yobugcisa enamanani athile. Ezi zinto kulula ukuziphinda-phinda okanye ukuzilungisa
* **Ukunqongophala kwezinto kwendalo**, okwaziwa njengekunqongophala okungaphathwa mntu, kubandakanya izinto ezifana nomhlaba osecaleni kolwandle kunye nezinto ezixabisekileyo ezifana negolide. Ezi zinto kunzima ukuziphinda-phinda okanye ukuzenza mbumbulu.

Umahluko ophambili phakathi kwezi zimbini lulawulo.

Ukunqongophala kwezinto okuphakathi kulawulwa yinkampani enye okanye urhulumente, ngelixa ukunqongophala kwezinto kwendalo kungalawulwa mntu. Umzekelo wokunqongophala kwezinto okuphakathi yimpahla yefashoni ebizayo, ekhutshelwa kancinci, njengeebhegi okanye izihlangu: inkampani inokwenza ezinye iiyunithi eziyi-1,000 ngaphandle kweendleko ezinkulu, kodwa ixabiso eliphezulu lilawulwa kukunqongophala okwenziweyo. Kulawulo lwenkampani kwinani leemveliso elichaza ixabiso lazo. Ngokwahlukileyo, indlela yokufumana nokusebenzisa ityuwa, amagobolondo okanye igolide kukusebenzisa amandla kunye nomzamo omkhulu (okanye 'umsebenzi'). Kwezi zixhobo zendalo, le ndleko inengqiqo kuphela ukuba imveliso ephumayo ixabisekile kakhulu.

Akukho mntu okanye iqela elilawula ixabiso lomthombo wendalo ukuze lichaphazele ixabiso lawo, koko kwenzeka ngokuchaseneyo: yimfuno yento leyo kwimarike echaza ukuba kufuneka kusetyenziswe amandla ukufumana eminye.

Ukunqongophala kwezinto kuchaphazela izigqibo zethu. Ukuqonda oku kunokuphucula indlela esenza ngayo izigqibo: sihlala sijongene nokukhetha phakathi kwenzuzo ekhawulezayo kunye nengeniso yexesha elide, kwaye olu tshintsho lwenza indlela yethu yokufikelela kwiinjongo zethu.

##### Umzekelo wokukhetha ixesha

Unenketho yokufumana i-R1,800 namhlanje okanye i-R1,980 kunyaka ozayo. Ukuba unomdla omkhulu kwangoko, unokukhetha ukufumana i-R1,800 namhlanje kuba ubaluleke ngakumbi ukufumana imali ngoku kunenzuzo yokulinda unyaka ufumane i-R180 engaphezulu. Kwelinye icala, ukuba unomdla omncinci kwangoko, uya kukhetha ukulinda ufumane umvuzo omkhulu kuba ugxile ngakumbi kulungiselelo lwexesha elide kwaye awukhathali kangako ngolonwabo olukhawulezayo.


> **Definition – Inkcazo yokukhetha ixesha**
>
> **Ukukhetha ixesha** kubhekisa kumbono wokuba abantu bahlala bekhetha ukufumana into NGOKU kunokuba balinde.


Ukubuyela kumzekelo wangaphambili webhotile yamanzi entlango, ukusela onke amanzi ngoko nangoko, nokuba kuthetha ukuba awuzukuba nawo kamva, kubonisa umdla omkhulu kwangoko: ukunxanwa okukhoyo ngelo xesha kubaluleke kakhulu kangangokuba awukhathali nokunxanwa onokukufumana kwixesha elizayo ukuze uhlangule ukunxanwa kwakho ngoku. Oku kuyinto eqhelekileyo: sihlala sikhetha ulonwabo lwangoku kunokuzithintela ngoku ukuze sifumane inzuzo kwixesha elizayo, kuba ikamva lihlala lingaqinisekanga.

Ukuzama ukugcina amanzi ngokusela kancinci kancinci, kwelinye icala, kubonisa umdla omncinci kwangoko kunye nokwabela izixhobo ezinqongopheleyo ngendlela enengqiqo. Oku kuthetha ukuba ukulungele ukulibazisa ukwaneliseka kwangoku ukuze uphucule amathuba akho okusinda kwixesha elide. Oku akufiki ngokwendalo kwaye kufuna umzamo omkhulu, ukuzilawula kunye nokujolisa kwikamva.


> **Definition – Inkcazo yexabiso elilahlekileyo**
>
> **Ixabiso elilahlekileyo** libhekisa kwixabiso lonyulo olulandelayo olungcono kakhulu olulahlekileyo xa wenza isigqibo. Zonke izigqibo ziquka utshintsho, ngoko ke zonke izigqibo zineendleko zexabiso elilahlekileyo.


Kumzekelo wentlango, ixabiso elilahlekileyo lokusela onke amanzi ngoko nangoko yinzuzo yokusinda ongenokuyifumana ngokugcina amanzi kwaye uwasebenzise ixesha elide.

Masithi ugqiba ekubeni ugcine amanzi kwaye usele kancinci kancinci ixesha elide. Ngenxa yoko, unamandla kunye nokufuma okwaneleyo ukukhangela amanye amanzi. Xa ukhangele, ufumana ikhakasi equlethe amanzi ambalwa. Ayininzi, kodwa yanele ukunqanda ukunxanwa kwakho okwethutyana. Ukuba ubusesele onke amanzi akho ngexesha elinye, ungenokube unamandla okufuna amanye amanzi kwaye ufumane le khakasi.

Kule meko, ixabiso elilahlekileyo lokusela onke amanzi akho ngexesha elinye ibingumthuba wokufumana ikhakasi kwaye ufumane amanzi angakumbi.

Lo mzekelo ubonisa ukuba ixabiso elilahlekileyo alibandakanyi kuphela utshintsho olukhawulezayo phakathi kweendlela ezimbini, kodwa namathuba anokuthi afunyanwe okanye alahleke kwixesha elizayo ngenxa yezigqibo zethu.

Ukulungelelanisa ukulahla umvuzo omkhulu kwixesha elizayo ngenxa yomvuzo omncinci ngoku kuchaphazeleka kukhetho lwethu lwexesha, okanye indlela esixabisa ngayo ulonwabo olukhawulezayo xa kuthelekiswa nolungiselelo lwexesha elide.

#### Umsebenzi: Ukukhetha ixesha

1. Mamela inkcazo kathisha malunga nokukhetha iswiti.
1. Gqiba ukuba ungathanda ukufumana iswiti encinci okanye i-marshmallow ngoku okanye ulinde de kube sekupheleni kweklasi ukuze ufumane iiswiti ezimbini okanye iswiti enkulu, enqwenelekayo ngakumbi.
1. Zibophezele kwisigqibo sakho kwaye wazise utitshala ngento oyikhethileyo. Fumana iswiti yakho ngoko nangoko okanye ekupheleni kweklasi, ngokuxhomekeke kwisigqibo sakho.
1. Thatha inxaxheba kwingxoxo yeklasi malunga nomsebenzi, ucinga ngendlela othathe ngayo isigqibo kunye nombono wokukhetha ixesha.
