# 1.5 Iintlobo zeMali

Imali inokuwohlulwa ibe ziindidi ezimbini eziphambili: imali ebonakalayo kunye neyedijithali.

##### Imali ebonakalayo

* **Imali yezinto ezixabisekileyo**, ethi ibe yinto ebonakalayo exatyiswa kakhulu kwaye yamkelwe njengendlela yokutshintshiselana.
  * Imizekelo: Igolide, isilivere, kwanegunya lomlilo zaye zakhonza njengemali yezinto ezixabisekileyo.
* **Imali emele into**, ethi imele ilungelo lokufumana into ebonakalayo.
  * Umzekelo: Izatifikethi zesilivere zazinokutshintshwa ngesilivere.
* **Imali ye-fiat**, ethi ibe ngamaphepha-mali kunye neengqekembe ezikhutshwe ngurhulumente kwaye zamkelwa njengendlela yokutshintshiselana.
  * Umzekelo: Amanqaku eFederal Reserve yimali ye-fiat, echazwe ngurhulumente wesizwe njengendlela yamkelekileyo yokuhlawula amatyala.

##### Iimali zedijithali

Iimali zedijithali zingasetyenziswa kwiintengiselwano ezikwi-intanethi kwaye ziquka imali yombane, ii-stablecoin, kunye ne-cryptocurrency.

Iimali zombane ziinguqulelo zedijithali zeemali zemveli ezifana ne-euro. Zisetyenziswa kwiintlawulo ezikwi-intanethi ngokusebenzisa iinkqubo ezibizwa ngokuba ziindlela zokuhlawula, ezihambisa imali ukusuka kwenye indawo ukuya kwenye.

Kwinkqubo yemali yesintu, ezi ndlela zokuhlawula zixhomekeke kubalamli abafana neebhanki. Aba balamli batyala iintlawulo kwaye banamandla okuvuma, ukulibazisa, okanye ukurhoxisa intengiselwano.

Imizekelo eqhelekileyo yale nkqubo ziquka iinethiwekhi zamakhadi, ezilawula iintlawulo zamakhadi okutsala kunye namakhadi etyala, kunye neewallet zedijithali, ezigcina imali kwaye zivumela abasebenzisi ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele iintlawulo.

* **Iimali zedijithali zeBhanki Ephakathi (CBDCs)**: Iinguqulelo zedijithali zemali yelizwe, ezikhutshwe kwaye zilawulwa yibhanki ephakathi.
* **Ii-stablecoin**: Iimali zedijithali ezilungiselelwe ukugcina ixabiso elizinzileyo, zihlala zixhomekeke kwinto exabisekileyo njenge-euro.
* **Ii-cryptocurrency**: Uhlobo lwemali yedijithali. Ezinye azilawulwa ngulo naluphi na uluntu olunye, oko kuthetha ukuba azinagunya lunye oluzilawulayo, ngelixa ezinye zilawulwa ngakumbi.

Ingcamango ephambili kwezinye zeemali zedijithali kukususa abalamli. Oku kunokwenza iintengiselwano zisebenze ngokukhawuleza kwaye kunciphise ugxininiso lwamandla. Injongo kukwakha inkqubo esebenza njengendlela ye-intanethi, apho ulawulo lwabelwana ngalo endaweni yokuba lube sesandleni sogunya olunye.
