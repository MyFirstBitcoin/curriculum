# 1.3 Imisebenzi yeMali

> Imali isebenza kuphela njengomsebenzi wexeshana kutshintshiselwano; kwaye xa urhwebo lugqityiwe ekugqibeleni, kuhlala kufumaneka ukuba uhlobo olunye lwempahla lutshintshiselwe olunye.  
_Jean-Baptiste Say_

Xa kufikwa ekuthengeni nasekuthengiseni iimpahla neenkonzo, imali yeyona iphambili. Imali inemisebenzi emininzi ebalulekileyo ehlabathini, ezifana nale:

##### Indawo Yokugcina Ixabiso

Imali kufuneka igcine ixabiso layo ngokuhamba kwexesha, nto leyo eyenza ukuba iluncedo njengendlela yokugcina nokutyala ixabiso lomsebenzi womntu. Oku kuvumela abantu ukuba basebenzise imali ukuceba ikamva labo. Ngoko ke, ixesha elizayo xa ugcina imali ukuze uthenge into ekhethekileyo, khumbula ukuba imali ayisosixhobo sokuhlawula izinto kuphela — sisixhobo sokukunceda ucebe kwaye utyale kwikamva lakho.

##### Indlela Yokutshintshiselana

Ngemali, akufuneki ufumane umntu ofuna kanye le nto onayo ukuze nitshintshe. Endaweni yoko, ungasebenzisa imali ukuthenga nokuthengisa nantoni na oyifunayo. Oku kwenza urhwebo kunye norhwebo lube lula kwaye lusebenze kakuhle.

##### Umgangatho Wexabiso

Imali ibonelela ngomgangatho oqhelekileyo wokulinganisa ixabiso, ovumela abantu ukuba baxele kwaye bathelekise amaxabiso ezinto ezahlukeneyo kunye neenkonzo. Oku kwenza ukuba imarike isebenze kakuhle kwaye icace, apho abantu banokwenza izigqibo ezilumkileyo malunga nokuthenga nokuthengisa.

Ukuba ubufuna ukuthenga imoto entsha, unokuthelekisa amaxabiso kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo zokuthengisa kwaye wenze isigqibo esilumkileyo sokuba uza kuthenga eyiphi ngokusekelwe kwixabiso le-Randi. Ngaphandle komgangatho wexabiso, kuya kufuneka uzame ukuthelekisa ixabiso lemoto enye kwenye usebenzisa enye into, njengenani leenkomo okanye ixesha elithathayo ukuyenza.

Ezi misebenzi mithathu zezinto ezenza uqoqosho lube nzima kwaye luguquguquke. Ngaphandle kwemali, kuya kuba nzima kakhulu ukuthenga nokuthengisa iimpahla neenkonzo, kwaye uqoqosho lwethu beluya kuba lungaphuhliswanga kakhulu.
