# 1.2 Inkcazo yeMali

> Imali liqinisekiso lokuba sinokufumana into esiyifunayo kwixesha elizayo. Nangona singadingi nto ngoku, iqinisekisa ukuba kunokwenzeka ukwanelisa umnqweno omtsha xa uvela.  
_Aristotle_

Ukhe wema wacinga ukuba imali yintoni na ngokwenene? Okanye waze wabuza ukuba yintoni eyenza imali… ibe yimali? Uninzi lwethu siyayisebenzisa, kodwa bambalwa abaqondayo ukuba ivela phi okanye isebenza njani. Imali sisixhobo sokutshintshiselana ngeempahla neenkonzo. Imela ixabiso lezi zinto ngendlela elula yokutshintshiselana. Imali inokuba neendlela ezininzi, ezifana namaphepha-mali, iinkozo zemali, kunye neentlawulo zedijithali. Urhulumente okanye amagunya athile aqhele ukushicilela nokulawula imali, kodwa imali ingaphezulu kokuba sisixhobo somzimba okanye sedijithali sokutshintshiselana; ifana nolwimi lwehlabathi olusivumela ukuba sithengiselane nabantu abavela kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo, nokuba asithethi ulwimi olufanayo okanye asabelani nenkcubeko efanayo. Ungaba usecaleni lehlabathi kwaye usakwazi 'ukuthetha' imali ngokubeka imveliso kwikhawuntara kwaye uyitshintshise ngemali yendawo okanye usebenzise ikhadi letyala.

Imali ifana nesivumelwano sentlalo esivumela ukuba sitshintshe izinto ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka ekutshintshiselaneni ngokuthe ngqo okanye ukufumana umntu ofuna kanye le nto sinayo. Ukuba iqela labantu liqalile ukwamkela itshokolethi njengentlawulo yeempahla neenkonzo ezininzi, itshokolethi ingaba yimali (nangona, kuba inganyibilika kwezinye iindawo zehlabathi, sinokuthi ibe yimali embi).

Ngamanye amazwi, imali ngokwayo ayinawo amandla okwanelisa iimfuno zabantu; sisixhobo nje esisivumela ukuba sithengiselane ngokukhawuleza nangcono.


> **Callout**
>
> Imali imela ixabiso ESI tshintshiselwana ngalo iimpahla. Imali ASIYO ixabiso ESI tshintshiselwana ngaso iimpahla.



> **Definition – Inkcazo yentengiselwano**
>
> I **intengiselwano** lutshintshiselwano okanye ukudluliselwa kweempahla neenkonzo. Yindlela yokutshintshiselana ngexabiso phakathi kwamaqela amabini nangaphezulu.


Kukho iintlobo ezininzi zeentengiselwano, ukusuka kutshintshiselwano olulula (njengokuthenga isandwichi kwivenkile yokutya) ukuya kutshintshiselwano lwezezimali olunzima ngakumbi (njengokuthenga indlu okanye utyalo-mali kwi-

Iintengiselwano zinokwenziwa ubuso ngobuso, ngomnxeba, kwi-intanethi, okanye ngezinye iindlela, kwaye zinokubandakanya amaqela ahlukeneyo, kubandakanya abantu ngabanye, amashishini, kunye neenkampani zezezimali.


> **Callout – Isishwankathelo**
>
> Imali yenza ukuba urhwebo lube lula kuba wonke umntu uyayamkela njengentlawulo yokugqibela. Ikwasivumela ukuba silinganise ixabiso kwaye siqwalasele iimpahla neenkonzo ezahlukeneyo.
