#### Ingxoxo: Kutheni Sifuna Imali?

Yahlulani ngamaqela kwaye:

* Yabelanani kwaye nixoxe ngeempendulo zemibuzo emine yokuqala. Bhala phantsi iimpendulo ezithandwayo.
* Yabelanani ngeempendulo zemibuzo yokugqibela, nize nivotele eyona mibuzo ithandwayo. Bhala phantsi isiphumo.
* Buyelani kwiimpendulo nemibuzo ekupheleni kwesifundo.

Ngoku ukuba sele unokuqonda okucacileyo malunga nesizathu sokuba imali ibalulekile, izifundo ezilandelayo ziza kuphonononga ukuba yintoni imali, indlela etshintshe ngayo ngokuhamba kwexesha, ngubani ochaphazela imali, kunye nohlobo lwayo olutsha. Qhubeka ubhekisa kuluhlu lwakho losuku lokuqala eklasini ukuze uqhagamshele iingcinga zakho nendlela imali eyadalwa ngayo, ichazwa, kwaye isetyenziswa ngokuhamba kwexesha.
