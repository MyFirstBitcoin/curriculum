# 7.1 Inethiwekhi yeLightning

I-Lightning Network yinkqubo yokuhlawula evumela abasebenzisi ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele i-bitcoin ngokukhawuleza nangexabiso eliphantsi. Isebenza ngokumisela i-wallet esabelwana ngayo apho omabini amaqela agcina khona i-bitcoin yabo. Emva koko banokwenza iintengiselwano ezingapheliyo phakathi kwabo ngaphandle kokuba kufuneka baxelele nganye nganye kwi-blockchain ephambili. Ngokwenza njalo, bayayigqitha imfuneko yokuqinisekisa nokufaka yonke intengiselwano kwi-block, nto leyo eyenza inkqubo ikhawuleze kwaye ibe nexabiso eliphantsi. Imirhumo ephantsi ithetha ukuba i-Lightning Network ingasetyenziswa kwiintlawulo ezincinci ezingasoloko zisebenza kwi-chain. Xa amaqela egqiba ukugqiba intsebenziswano yabo, kuphela ibhalansi yokugqibela ebhalwa kwi-blockchain.

Zicingele usuku usebenza kwikhefi. Uceba ukuhlala ixesha elide, uvula ithebhu kwaye uhlawule kwangaphambili endaweni yokuhlawula nganye nganye iodolo. Ekupheleni kosuku, wena nomnini wevenkile nijonga ithebhu ukuze nihlengahlengise ityala. Ukuba idiphozithi yakho ingaphezulu kunoko uchitheyo, ufumana umahluko; ukuba uchithe ngaphezulu, uhlawula osele uswele.

Le ndlela ingandiswa ukuze ibandakanye abathathi-nxaxheba abaninzi. Umzekelo, kwenye yeendwendwe zakho kwikhefi, uzisa umhlobo wakho ongazaziyo umphathi kwaye akakwazi ukuvula ithebhu. Unika umhlobo wakho ithebhu yakho ekhoyo ukuze ihlawule iindleko zabo, kwaye niyavumelana ukuba baya kukubuyisela imali ngokuzimeleyo. Ngoku khawucinge ngamawaka abantu benza into efanayo ngaxeshanye, bevumela abanye ukuba basebenzise iithebhu ezikhoyo ukudibanisa nabanye abantu abaninzi — yile ndlela i-Lightning Network isebenza ngayo!

Nge-Lightning, unokwenza iintlawulo kuye nabani na okwi-network, hayi kuphela kumntu onethebhu naye ngqo — ukuba kuphela kukho indlela phakathi kwamaqela omabini. Intlawulo yakho ingahamba kwi-network ide ifike kwindawo yayo, nokuba awunalo ijelo elivulekileyo ngqo kumamkeli.

Masiqwalasele umahluko phakathi kweentengiselwano ezikwikhonkco (on-chain) nezingekho kwikhonkco (off-chain).

##### Iintengiselwano Ezikwikhonkco

Ezi ziintengiselwano ezenzeka ngqo kwi-Bitcoin blockchain. Zithatha malunga nemizuzu eli-10 ukuqinisekiswa, kwaye imirhumo ixhomekeke kubungakanani bentengiselwano kwi-virtual bytes. Zikhuselekile ngakumbi kodwa zicotha, kuba zifuna imvumelwano yenethiwekhi.

##### Iintengiselwano ze-Lightning Network

Ezi ntengiselwano zenzeka kwinethiwekhi eyahlukileyo eyakhiwe ngaphezulu kwe-Bitcoin blockchain. Zihlawulwa ngokukhawuleza nangemirhumo ephantsi. Zisetyenziswa rhoqo apho izinto ezifana nesantya kunye neendleko zentengiselwano zibalulekile. Xa kuthelekiswa neentengiselwano ezikwikhonkco, azikhuselekanga kangako.
