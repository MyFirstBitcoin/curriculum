# 7.4 Ukuthumela nokuFumana iiNtengiselwano zeLightning

Nge-wallet ye-Lightning, ukusebenzisa iBitcoin kuyakhawuleza, kuxabisa kancinci, kwaye kuyimfihlo, nto leyo eyenza ukuba ukuthengiselana phakathi kwabantu ababini kube lula. Ungathumela kwaye wamkele i-bitcoin ngokukhawuleza kwizinto zemihla ngemihla ezifana nokuthenga ikofu.

Makhe sijonge imizekelo embalwa ye-Lightning Network isebenza.

###### Umzekelo 1

UMarcia no-Eve ngamnye baneeyunithi ezi-5 zemali. UMarcia ufuna ukuthumela ii-yunithi ezi-2 ku-Eve. Intlawulo idlula kuJeff, onceda ukudlulisela intlawulo kwi-Lightning Network. Emva kokuba intlawulo igqityiwe, u-Eve uneeyunithi ezi-7 kwaye u-Marcia unezi-3.

UJeff unceda ekudluliseni intlawulo, kodwa akakwazi ukweba imali. I-Lightning Network isebenzisa i-cryptography ukuqinisekisa ukuba kuphela lowo kufanelekile afumane intlawulo. UJeff nje unceda intlawulo ukuba ihambe kwi-network.

Oku kubonisa inzuzo ephambili ye-Lightning Network: abantu banokuthumela iintlawulo ngokukhawuleza ngaphandle kokuthembela kubalamli abafana neebhanki.

Abasebenzisi be-node abafana noJeff banokufumana imirhumo emincinci ngokunceda ukudlulisela iintlawulo. Ngokwenza oku, banegalelo ekugcineni inethiwekhi ingaphakathi kolawulo lwabantu abaninzi kwaye isebenza kakuhle.

Xa kuthelekiswa nokuthengiselana okuqhelekileyo kweBitcoin:

* **Iintengiselwano ezikwibhulokhwe (on-chain)** zenzeka ngqo kwi-blockchain yeBitcoin. Zikhuselekile kakhulu kodwa zinokucotha kwaye zibize.
* **Iintengiselwano ze-Lightning** zenzeka ngaphandle kwe-blockchain (off-chain) kwaye zivumela iintlawulo ukuba zihambe ngokukhawuleza kakhulu nangexabiso eliphantsi kakhulu.

Ngenxa yoku, i-Lightning iluncedo kwiintlawulo ezincinci zemihla ngemihla, ngelixa iintengiselwano ezikwibhulokhwe zisetyenziswa rhoqo kwiintlawulo ezinkulu okanye ukugcina imali ixesha elide.

###### Umzekelo 2

UMina uyathanda ukutya ngaphandle kwaye uhlala etyelela ikhefi yakhe ayithandayo. Ngenxa yokuba zininzi iindlela zokuhlawula, akaziqinisekanga ukuba yeyiphi engcono. Ngethamsanqa, sele efunde kancinci malunga neBitcoin kunye ne-Lightning Network. Emva kokujonga iinketho zakhe, uMina uqonda ukuba ukusebenzisa indlela yokuhlawula ye-Lightning yeyona ilungileyo.

UMina ufuna ukuthenga ikofu, kodwa ukuhlawula ngeBitcoin eqhelekileyo kungathatha ixesha kwaye kufune imirhumo ephezulu. Endaweni yoko, ugqiba ekubeni asebenzise i-Lightning Network.

I-Lightning Network ivumela abantu ukuba bathumele i-bitcoin kwangoko nangemirhumo ephantsi kakhulu. Oku kuyenza ifaneleke kakhulu kwiintengiselwano ezincinci zemihla ngemihla ezifana nekofu.

Ukuqala ukusebenzisa i-Lightning, uMina ukhuphela i-wallet ye-Lightning kwifowuni yakhe. Emva koko uthumela i-bitcoin encinane esuka kwi-wallet yakhe eqhelekileyo yeBitcoin ukuya kwi-wallet yakhe ye-Lightning. Eli nyathelo lisebenzisa intengiselwano eqhelekileyo yeBitcoin kwi-blockchain. Xa imali sele ikwi-wallet yakhe ye-Lightning, ingasetyenziswa kwi-Lightning Network.

Ngoku uMina angahlawula ikhefi kwangoko esebenzisa i-Lightning. Intlawulo yenzeka ngaphandle kwe-blockchain ephambili yeBitcoin, yiyo loo nto ikhawuleza kwaye ixabisa kancinci kunentengiselwano eqhelekileyo ye-on-chain.

Iintengiselwano ezikwikhonkco (on-chain) zenzeka ngqo kwi-blockchain yeBitcoin kwaye zingathatha ixesha elide kunye nemirhumo ephezulu. Iintengiselwano zeLightning zenzeka ngaphandle kwekhonkco (off-chain), okuvumela iintlawulo ezikhawulezayo nezixabisa kancinci ngelixa zisasebenzisa i-bitcoin.

###### Amanqaku Aphambili

1. Ukusebenzisa i-wallet yeLightning kuya kwakha ukuzithemba kwakho ekwamkeleni nasekuthumeleni ii-sats zokwenene.
1. Qaphela iiyunithi. Ezinye ii-wallet zivumela abasebenzisi ukuba bathumele i-bitcoin OKANYE ii-sats (1/100,000,000 ye-bitcoin).
1. Iintlawulo zeLightning ngamanye amaxesha zinokunqunyanyiswa ekuthumeleni, ngakumbi xa intlawulo inkulu. Nangona kunjalo, olu hlobo lwamava lomsebenzisi luyancipha njengoko inethiwekhi ikhula.

###### Icebiso Labafundi

Qinisekisa nomfundisi wakho ukuba imirhumo yangoku yeentengiselwano zeBitcoin ezikwikhonkco iya kuchaphazela njani/ukuba ichaphazela i-wallet yeLightning oyisebenzisayo.
