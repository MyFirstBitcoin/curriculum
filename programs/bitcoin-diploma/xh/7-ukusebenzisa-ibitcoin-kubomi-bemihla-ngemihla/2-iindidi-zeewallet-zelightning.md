# 7.2 Iindidi zeeWallet zeLightning

Iwallet ye-Lightning ifana newallet ye-Bitcoin kuba ikuvumela ukuba uthumele kwaye wamkele i-bitcoin. Umahluko ophambili kukuba isebenza kwi-Lightning Network, umaleko wesibini owakhiwe ngaphezulu kwe-Bitcoin. Njengeewallet ze-Bitcoin, ii-wallet ze-Lightning zineempawu ezahlukeneyo ekufuneka uziqwalasele phambi kokukhetha enye.

#### Ukuzigcina wena vs Ukugcinwa ngabanye

Ii-wallet ze-Lightning zinokwahlulwa ngeendlela ezininzi, kodwa ukuze kube lula, sizahlula zibe ziindidi ezimbini: ezizigcinayo wena kunye nezigcinwa ngabanye.  
Iwallet ezigcinayo wena ithetha ukuba ulawula izitshixo zakho. Iwallet egcinwa ngabanye ithetha ukuba omnye umntu ulawula izitshixo zakho.  
Ngewallet egcinwa ngabanye, unokuthumela kwaye wamkele iintlawulo, kodwa uxhomekeke kumntu wesithathu kwaye uphulukana nolawulo olupheleleyo lweemali zakho ngenxa yokulula. Oku kungalunga kwiimali ezincinci, kodwa kusekho kungcono ukusebenzisa iwallet ezigcinayo wena xa sele uqonda indlela esebenza ngayo. Kule nxalenye isalayo, siza kugxila kuphela kwi-wallet ze-Lightning ezizigcinayo wena.

#### Umthombo Ovulekileyo vs Umthombo Ovaliwe

Ii-wallet ze-Lightning zinokuthi zibe ngumthombo ovulekileyo okanye umthombo ovaliweyo. Iiwallet zomthombo ovulekileyo zithandwa kuba ikhowudi yazo ivulekile, ingajongwa nangubani na, kwaye ingaphuculwa luluntu.
