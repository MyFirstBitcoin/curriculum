# 7.3 Ukuseta Iwallet yeBitcoin Lightning

Ukuseta i-wallet ye-Lightning ozigcinelayo kufana kakhulu nokuseta i-wallet ye-Bitcoin ozigcinelayo.

1. Khangela usetyenziso kwi-App Store (iOS) okanye kwi-Google Play Store (Android).
1. Vula usetyenziso uze wenze i-wallet entsha. Uza kucelwa ukuba ugcine uluhlu lwamagama ali-12 ukuya kwangama-24: le yinkcazo yakho yokubuyisela (ekwabizwa ngokuba yi-seed phrase). **Qiniseka ukuba uyayibhala phantsi kwaye uyigcine kwindawo ekhuselekileyo!** Le nkcazo yokubuyisela ikuvumela ukuba ufumane ukufikelela ngokupheleleyo kwiimali zakho xa kuyimfuneko. **Ukuba ulahleka okanye ulibale olu luhlu lwamagama, awuzukukwazi ukufikelela kwi-bitcoin yakho ukuba ulahlekelwa ukufikelela kwi-wallet yakho.**
1. Kuya kufuneka uqinisekise ukuba ugcine inkcazo yakho yokubuyisela. Ukwenza oku, ngenisa amagama e-seed phrase yakho ngendlela efanayo.
1. Ukukhusela ngakumbi, ezinye ii-wallet zikuvumela ukuba usete i-PIN okanye iphasiwedi.
1. Ngoku unokuqalisa ukuthumela nokwamkela i-bitcoin kwi-Lightning.

Qaphela: Ukuba usebenzisa i-wallet egcinwa ngomnye umntu (custodial wallet), kusenokwenzeka ukuba akufuneki ulandele onke la manyathelo. Nangona kunjalo, ii-wallet ezinjalo zineengozi kuba awulawuli isitshixo sakho sabucala, oko kuthetha ukuba awulawuli ngokupheleleyo imali yakho.
