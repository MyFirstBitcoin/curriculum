# 9.0 Intshayelelo

Kule candelo, siza kujonga ngokusondeleyo kwiindawo ezimbini ezibaluleke kakhulu (kunye nabathathi-nxaxheba) kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin ezazaziswa okokuqala kwiSifundo 5:

* **Iinodi zeBitcoin**: Abagcini-masango bokuvavanya abano msebenzi ophambili wokugcina ikopi yencwadi yeBitcoin, kunye nokuqinisekisa ukuba zonke iintengiselwano zivumelekile kwaye wonke umntu ulandela imigaqo efanayo. Ngokuhambisa lo msebenzi kubantu abaninzi kwihlabathi lonke, iBitcoin ihlala yomelele ngokuchasene nobuqhetseba obunokubakho ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kugunyaziwe ophakathi. Iinodi zinceda ukugcina inkqubo ithembekile kwaye ihambelana nomgaqo wokuzimela, apho akukho mntu okanye iqela linamandla agqithisileyo kwabanye.
* **Abavukuzi beBitcoin**: Abaqulunqi bokhuseleko abasisombulula imingeni ye-cryptography besebenzisa iikhompyutha ezinamandla nombane. Bahlola kwaye baqinisekise iintengiselwano, beqinisekisa ukuba yonke into ikhuselekile. Umsebenzi wabo wokubala unceda ukwenza incwadi (okanye ibhlokhcheyini) inganyangeki kubantu ababi ngokwenza utshintsho kwi-blockchain kubize kakhulu kumandla ombane.

Ngokudibeneyo, iinodi zeBitcoin kunye nabavukuzi basebenza njengeqela ukugcina inkqubo ezimeleyo, ikhuselekile, kwaye yomelele — inkqubo yemali entsha enokuthembeka ngabantu kwihlabathi liphela. Masihlole ezi ndima ngokubanzi ukuze siqonde indlela abanegalelo ngayo kwi protocol yeBitcoin.
