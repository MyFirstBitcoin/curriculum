# 9.2 Yintoni iMempool?

I-**mempool**, igama elifutshane elithetha “memory pool,” ifana negumbi lokulinda leentengiselwano zeBitcoin. Xa uthumela i-bitcoin, intengiselwano yakho iqala ngokusasazwa kwinethiwekhi kwaye ibekwe kwi-mempool.

Ungayicinga njengokulinda emgqeni kwindawo yokutyela. Igama lakho libhalwa kuluhlu, uze ulinde ide kufumaneke itafile. Ngokufanayo, intengiselwano yakho ilinda kwi-mempool ide umgodi wayo ayifake kwibhloko.

Iinodi zeBitcoin zihlola intengiselwano nganye entsha ukuqinisekisa ukuba ivumelekile kwaye i-bitcoin esetyenziswayo ayikaze isetyenziswe ngaphambili. Ukuba intengiselwano ivumelekile, ihlala kwi-mempool ide iqinisekiswe.

Abagodi bakhetha iintengiselwano kwi-mempool baze bazifake kwiibhloko ezintsha. Ngokuqhelekileyo, iintengiselwano ezineemali zenkonzo eziphezulu zikhethwa kuqala.

Xa intengiselwano ifakwe kwibhloko, iqinisekisiwe kwaye igcinwa ngonaphakade kwi-blockchain yeBitcoin.

###### Amanqaku Aphambili

1. **Mempool** ibhekisa kuluhlu lweetengiselwano ezingekaqinisekiswa ezigcinwa yiyo yonke i-node yeBitcoin, hayi inkonzo ethile okanye iqonga elithile.
1. Akukho mempool enye, jikelele. I-Mempool.space yenye yezininzi.
1. [Mempool.space](https://mempool.space) ivulekile kwaye yaziwa ngokuba ngumkhangeli webhloko olula ukuyisebenzisa. Inika idatha ngexesha langempela yeentengiselwano ezingekaqinisekiswa, amaxabiso eemali zenkonzo, kunye neminye imisebenzi yenethiwekhi.

###### Icebiso Labafundi

I-Mempool.space yenza okungakumbi kunokubonisa iibhloko. Hlola nezinye iindawo zehlabathi leBitcoin: umzekelo, i-Lightning, imigodi, i-hash rate, iipuli, kunye ne "goggles" zesithuba sebhloko.
