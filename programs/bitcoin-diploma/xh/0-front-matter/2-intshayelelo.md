# Intshayelelo

Uphando ngeBitcoin luqala ngombuzo onzulu: yintoni imali, kwaye kutheni ibalulekile?

Phambi kokuba sihlolisise iBitcoin ngokwayo, kubalulekile ukuqonda iziseko ezisekelwe kuyo. Imali iye yaguquka kumawaka eminyaka, yenziwa ngabantu abasebenzisana, inkqubela phambili yetekhnoloji, kunye neemfuno eziguqukayo zoluntu. Ukusuka kwiinkqubo zokutshintshiselana iimpahla ukuya kwizinyithi ezixabisekileyo, ukusuka kwiimali zamaphepha ukuya kumanethiwekhi emali edijithali esizisebenzisayo namhlanje, inqanaba ngalinye kule nkqubela libalisa ibali malunga nokuthembana, amandla, kunye nokuqhuba uqoqosho.

Ngokufunda imbali kunye nolwakhiwo lweenkqubo zemali zale mihla, sifumana umbono osisigxina wokuba kutheni iBitcoin ivele kwaye zeziphi iingxaki ezizama ukuzisombulula. IBitcoin ayizange ivele ingenamvelaphi. Yimpendulo kwimingeni emide ekho kwiinkqubo zemali, kunye nomzamo wokuphinda sicinge ngendlela ixabiso elinokugcinwa kwaye lidluliselwe ngayo kwixesha ledijithali.

Le diploma yenzelwe ukukukhokela kuloo hambo inyathelo ngenyathelo. Njengoko uqhubeka, uya kwakha ulwazi oluyimfuneko ukuze uhlalutye ngokunzulu zombini inkqubo yemali yangoku kunye nezinto ezintsha eziziswe yiBitcoin.

Sondela kule nkqubo yokufunda ngomdla kunye nomonde. Iimbono ezihlolwayo apha zinamandla okuguqula indlela esiqonda ngayo imali, itekhnoloji, kunye nenkululeko yezoqoqosho. Ngokwakha isiseko esiluqilima, uya kuba ulungele kakuhle ukujongana nophuhliso lwezezimali kunye netekhnoloji oluphambili kwixesha lethu.
