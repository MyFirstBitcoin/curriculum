# Ibali leDiploma yeBitcoin

Akukho nto inamandla kunengcinga efikelele kwixesha layo.  
Eli bali liqala e-El Salvador ngo-2022, xa saqalisa iDiploma yeBitcoin yokuqala emhlabeni ngaphakathi kwenkqubo yesikolo sikarhulumente. Asizange sicela imvume kurhulumente. Isikolo sasimemile, kwaye abafundi abangama-38 bagqiba izifundo ngoJuni waloo nyaka.

Abanye basigxeka ngokungalandeli imijelo esemthethweni. Xa abafundi bokuqala bewela eqongeni, samemezela ukwandisa kwisikolo sesibini ngengomso.

Kunyaka olandelayo, urhulumente ofanayo wacela uncedo ekwaziseni imfundo ye-bitcoin. Ukuze sikhusele ukuzimela kwethu, salala ukuhlawulwa kwaye endaweni yoko saqeqesha amakhulu ootitshala bezikolo zikarhulumente ukuze baqhube inkqubo ngokwabo.

NgoSeptemba 2022 senza yonke inkqubo yemfundo yafumaneka ngokukhululekileyo (open source). Oko kwaqala njengovavanyo lwengingqi kwakhawuleza kwaba yintshukumo yehlabathi.

Namhlanje inkqubo yemfundo ihlaziywa kabini ngonyaka kwaye ilungiswa ngootitshala kwihlabathi lonke.  
Ngoku ibandakanya intshayelelo yehora enye kunye nenkqubo yabancinci.

NgoMatshi 2023 saqalisa iNethiwekhi yeMfundo, uluntu lwehlabathi olwakhiwe kwiimigaqo ezifanayo: ukuzimela, ukungakhethi, ulawulo loluntu, i-bitcoin kuphela, imfundo esemgangathweni ophezulu. Ukusukela ngoko, ootitshala batolika inkqubo yemfundo kwaye bayise kwiilizwe ezingama-41 kumazwekazi amathandathu.

Ngenxa yokuba umsebenzi uvulelekile (open source), abaninzi bafundisa ngokuzimeleyo ngaphandle kolwazi lwethu okanye imvume yethu.

I-Bitcoin Diploma iyaphumelela kuba ibonisa oko ikufundisayo: amandla ngokutsha okungafuni mvume. Ikhula ngokusebenzisana, hayi ukhuphiswano. Ngokuchaphazela, hayi inzuzo.

Imfundo ye-bitcoin ezimeleyo, ekhokelwa luluntu, iza kutshintsha ihlabathi. Sele ikwenze oko.
