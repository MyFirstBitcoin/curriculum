#### 4.4.0 Intshayelelo

Icandelo lobuchwepheshe likwicala eliphambili lokwamkelwa kweBitcoin. Njengoko kusoloko kusithiwa, kwaye kuchazwe kwisiqendu esingentla - idibanisa yonke into abantu abangayiqondiyo malunga neekhompyutha kunye nayo yonke into abangayiqondiyo ngemali. Isiqendu sikwachaza ezinye zezinto eziphambili malunga neBitcoin ekufuneka ziqondwe ukuze kubonakale ixabiso layo lokwenene. Oku kubeka icandelo lobuchwepheshe kwindawo entle yokuxhamla ekwamkelweni kwayo, kodwa kwakhona kwicala eliphambili lotshintsho oluzakwenzeka kulo lonke ixesha leshishini.

#### 4.4.1 Isicwangciso seShishini – njani na ishishini elinokuthi lilungelelanise?

I-Bitcoin imele itekhnoloji entsha ekwisigaba sokwamkelwa kwihlabathi liphela. Nangona ekuqaleni ingabonakali njalo, inokuchaphazela onke amacandelo eshishini naliphi na, kuquka nalawo agxile kubuchwepheshe.

Yeyona asethi yedijithali inqabileyo yokuqala kwaye idale imarike entsha ye-‘crypto-coins’ okanye ‘alt-coins’ ezizama ukulinganisa okanye ukwakha into entsha kusetyenziswa itekhnoloji esisiseko. Enye yezona zibonakalayo kwezi yi-blockchain, ebonelela ngesakhono esibalulekileyo kulawulo lwemali yedijithali, kodwa ngenye indlela ikakhulu isisombululo esikhangela ingxaki yokusombulula.

Indalo yonke yendalo esingqongileyo iyakhiwa ejikeleze iBitcoin ukuze ingabi nje imali ezinzileyo kwixesha ledijithali, kodwa neesivumelwano ezihlakaniphileyo, iinkqubo zokuhlawula kunye nezinye izisombululo ezisekelwe kule protocol.

* I-Blockchain trilemma kunye notshintsho olufunekayo
* Imiba yokhuseleko
* Ulawulo lwesoftware evulekileyo
* Imiba yenethiwekhi
* I-Cryptography
* Ukusetyenziswa kunye nefuthe kubathengi – ukuhlawula njl.

##### Ukhuseleko kunye nobumfihlo - Ukubethela kunye ne-cryptography

Ukhuseleko lwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin lunamanqanaba amaninzi. Xa uthumela iBitcoin, intengiselwano ibethelwa kusetyenziswa i-SHA-256 hashing. Umsebenzi wokumba nawo usebenzisa i-hashing ukwenza ibhloko evumelekileyo rhoqo emva kwemizuzu elishumi.

Iintengiselwano zeBitcoin zingagcinwa ziyimfihlo, kodwa oku kufuna ukuqonda nokusebenzisa izakhono ezifana nalezi:

* Ukungaphindi kusetyenziswe iidilesi
* Ukuphepha ukusetyenziswa kwe-KYC kunye nokungasebenzisi iidilesi ze-imeyile zobuqu
* Ukusebenzisa i-wallet edityaniswe kwinode yakho
* Ukusasaza unxibelelwano lwe-on-chain ngeTor
* Ukusetyenziswa kwe-Lightning
* Ukusebenzisa imisebenzi ye-Coinjoin
* Ukusetyenziswa kwesoftware ekhuselekileyo efana neewallets

Iinkampani zobuchwepheshe ezigxile kukhuseleko zikwindawo entle yokuqonda nokusebenzisa ezi zakhono ukugcina abathengi kunye neBitcoin yabo bekhuselekile.

##### I-blockchain trilemma kunye nokutshintsha phakathi kweendlela

![Blockchain trilemma: Decentralization, Scale and Security.](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/ce9935b08072ea42cb5697781d77e1c3c863c83d-250x249.svg)

I-blockchain trilemma ibhekisa kumceli mngeni wokufezekisa izinto ezintathu ezibalulekileyo kwitekhnoloji ye-blockchain: ukhuseleko, ukwandiswa, kunye nokusasazwa kwamandla.

I-trilemma icebisa ukuba ukuphucula elinye icala kuhlala kuthetha ukunciphisa amanye, nto leyo eyenza kube nzima ukufezekisa zonke ezintathu ngaxeshanye. I-Bitcoin iphucula ukhuseleko, apho i-hash rate ekhusele inethiwekhi iqhubeka ikhula, kunye nokusasazwa kwamandla, okuvumela inethiwekhi yehlabathi lonke engenagunya eliphakathi elilawula. Oku kwenzeka ngexabiso lokwandiswa, inani leentengiselwano ezixhaswayo ngomzuzwana liphantsi. Le ngxaki ishiywe ukuba isonjululwe kumaleko aphezulu afana ne-Lightning, ngendlela efana nendlela ye-TCP/IP enamaleko amaninzi.



##### Ulawulo lwesoftware evulekileyo (open source)

I-open source ithetha okungakumbi kunokusetyenziswa simahla. Ithetha nokuba i-bitcoin iphuhliswe luluntu oluvulekileyo lwabazithandayo abazinikeleyo.

Olo 'luntu oluvulekileyo lwabazithandayo abazinikeleyo' lusebenza ngokubambisana kumaqonga abaphuhlisi afana ne-GitHub. Ngamarekhodi oluntu, balungiselela uphuhliso lwesoftware ngokucacileyo. Ikhowudi kunye nembali yayo zihlala zikhona. Lonke ulungelelwaniso lunempawu ezifanayo ne-blockchain, i-ledger evulekileyo esembindini wenethiwekhi ye-Bitcoin.

Umoya we-open source ukhoyo kuyo yonke i-ecosystem ye-Bitcoin; ukusuka kubaxhasi abafana ne-Bitcoin Core kunye ne-Bitcoin Knots, ukuya kubavuni abenza izinto ngokwabo abafana ne-BitAxe, ukuya kwi-wallets ezifana ne-Wasabi, Green Wallet, okanye i-Blink.

Nabani na onobuchule kwitekhnoloji okanye inkampani angazibandakanya kwezi projekthi, ongeze ixabiso kwaye akhe izisombululo ngaphezulu kwayo.

##### Imiba yenethiwekhi

Inethiwekhi ye-Bitcoin iquka ii-node ezinxibelelana kwinethiwekhi ye-mesh enobume 'obuthe tye'. Akukho server, akukho nkonzo egxile embindini, kwaye akukho manyathelo aphezulu ngaphakathi kwenethiwekhi. I-node yikhompyutha edityaniswe nezinye ezilandelayo imithetho kwaye yabelana ngolwazi. 'I-full node' yikhompyutha kwinethiwekhi ye-peer-to-peer ye-Bitcoin, egcina kwaye ihlaziya ikopi epheleleyo ye-blockchain ye-Bitcoin. Ii-node zibalulekile ekugcineni inethiwekhi ye-cryptocurrency isebenza, ikhowudi yayo ifumaneka kwi-software ye-Bitcoin Core. Abavuni (miners) bayinxalenye ekhethekileyo yee-node ezenza umsebenzi wokuhashisha kwaye zenze iibhloko. Njengoko umzobo we-Lightning ubonisa ngasentla, kukho i-ecosystem yamashishini anikezela ngehardware kunye/okanye isoftware ukubonelela ngezi nkonzo, apho nayiphi na inkampani yetekhnoloji ingazibandakanya.



##### Ukusetyenziswa kunye nefuthe kubathengi

Njengoko umzobo ubonisa, inxalenye ye-ecosystem iquka iinkampani ezinikezela ngengqalasizinda yeentlawulo, ii-wallets, amaqela abavuni (mining pools) kunye neenkqubo ezinika ixabiso kumthengi ezifana neePodcasts kunye nee-Exchanges. Nayiphi na kwezi ndawo zinokuthi zibe ngamathuba okufunyanwa yinkampani yetekhnoloji, nokuba kukuqonda amathuba anokubakho okanye imingcipheko kwishishini elikhoyo.

* **Zeziphi iinkqubo zolawulo ezifunekayo?** - litshintsha njani ishishini kunye neenkqubo zangaphakathi xa imali/ithrezari itshintsha ifom?

Ukwenza uhlalutyo olufanayo kusetyenziswa le okanye ezinye iindlela kuya kunceda ukuqonda indlela i-Bitcoin enokuchaphazela ngayo ishishini, kunye nezinto ekufuneka zitshintshwe.

#### 4.4.3 Oko unokuthengisa- Zeziphi iimveliso okanye iinkonzo ezinokwenziwa okanye zilungiswe ukuze zilungele le modeli yeshishini intsha?

Eminye imizekelo yeenkampani ezisungula izinto ezintsha kweli candelo zezi:

**Fountain** yiPodcast App enikezela ngezinto ezintsha ezininzi kwiPodcasting.

I-App idityaniswe ne-Lightning wallet eqhutywa yiCashApp okanye iStrike, evumela abasebenzisi be-app ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele 'sats' - imali encinci ye-bitcoin.

Abaphulaphuli banokukhetha ukuhambisa iisats kumvelisi ngomzuzu ngamnye ngexesha belalele. Banokuthi baphakamise ii-podcast abazithandayo ngokuthumela iisats kumenzi wepodcast kunye nomyalezo. Ezi Boosts ziboniswa njengezimvo ukuze abanye abaphulaphuli bafunde baze baphendule kuzo. Xa uthumela iBoost iya kubonakala kwiphepha lomboniso kunye ne-episode phantsi kwemicimbi. Okukhona uphakamisa, kokukhona ibonakala phezulu kwiphepha le-episode. Iya kubonwa nangabanye abasebenzisi abakulandela kwiFountain kwiDiscover feed yabo.

Abenzi beepodcast ke ngoko banokufumana iisats nge-lightning ngenxa yokuzibandakanya kwabaphulaphuli, kwaye banokukhetha ukwabelana ngokuzenzekelayo nabanye abancedisi kumboniso.

Kumsebenzisi, umzuzu ngamnye ochithwe ekusebenziseni umxholo, ekudaleni umxholo, okanye ekubukeni iintengiso, wongeza ixabiso leqonga elisetyenziswa... Cinga ngezo zisetyenziswa yonke imihla. Youtube, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok - onke la maqonga angalahlekelwa lixabiso ngaphandle kwexesha nengqwalasela enikelwa kuwo, kodwa akukho nanye ehlawulayo umsebenzisi ngokuhlala. Kwi-app yeFountain, abasebenzisi banokufumana imivuzo ngokuthanda, ukulalela umxholo okhuthazwayo okanye ngokulula ngokuphulaphula ipodcast.

Iinkampani zinokucinga ngokujika imali yentengiso – eyaziwayo ukuba i-50% yayo idla ngokuchithwa kodwa kunzima ukwazi ukuba yeyiphi i-50% – ukuze zamkele imodeli efanayo.

I-MicroStrategy yenye yeenkampani eziphambili kuphuhliso nasekuphunyezweni kwezisombululo kweli cala, kwaye ngo-2023, iMicroStrategy yabhengeza inani leminikelo kweli candelo:

#### 4.4.4 Indlela yokuphumelela – isicwangciso sokungena kwimarike kunye nentengiso

Isicwangciso sentengiso yedijithali kufuneka sibandakanye isicwangciso sokusebenzisa amajelo e-intanethi ukwakha ubukho kwi-intanethi kunye nokufezekisa iinjongo ezithile zentengiso. Ekugqibeleni, injongo kukunyusa ukubonakala kweshishini kwaye kutsalwe abathengi abatsha kusetyenziswa amajelo ahlukeneyo. Njengoko kubonisiwe kwimizekelo engentla, iintlawulo ezincinci eziphazamisa imarike zinokwenza iimodeli ezintsha zentengiso zibe semgangathweni.

Isicwangciso sentengiso kunye nesicwangciso kufuneka silungiswe ukuze sibandakanye amanyathelo anxulumene neBitcoin avunyelwene ngawo, kunye neenjongo ezicacileyo nezichaziweyo.

Iinjongo zinokuba zezi:

* Ukuseka ubunkokeli bokucinga ngeBitcoin kwicandelo letekhnoloji elinxulumene neshishini
* Ukutsala abathengi abatsha ngeemveliso kunye neenkonzo ezijolise kwiBitcoin
* Ukonyusa ukuqonda kwegama lophawu kwindawo yeBitcoin

Isicwangciso sentengiso sinokubandakanya amanyathelo anje:

* Yakha iphepha elitsha lewebhusayithi elijolise kwiBitcoin kunye/okanye iLightning
* Faka imiyalezo yeBitcoin kwiiphulo zentengiso ezikhoyo
* Ukwazisa iimveliso okanye iinkonzo ezinxulumene neBitcoin
* Khulisa abalandeli kumajelo onxibelelwano ajolise kumxholo weBitcoin
* Ukuncedisa kwisoftware evulekileyo yomthombo
* Sebenzisa ithuba lokuhambisa iisats ukukhuthaza ukusetyenziswa komxholo wentengiso okanye wemfundo



#### 4.4.5 Ukhuseleko – naziphi na iimpembelelo kuLawulo, imigaqo kunye neemfuno zokuthotyelwa

Ngaba ukusetyenziswa kweBitcoin kunye nezinye iiprothokholi zomthombo ovulekileyo kuchaphazela indlela esicinga ngayo ngomngcipheko we-IT kunye nokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo?

Ukhuseleko kunye nokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo-mthetho kubalulekile kwimpumelelo ye-Bitcoin, ethembele kwi-hashing, i-cryptography kunye nokufihla ulwazi. Njengomgaqo-nkqubo ovulekileyo, ikhowudi iyafumaneka kuye nabani na ofuna ukuyijonga aze enze igalelo kuyo. Iinkampani zobuchwepheshe zinokubakho kwindawo efanelekileyo yokwenza igalelo kule nkqubo.

Kuxhomekeke kwizisombululo ezikhethiweyo ezijolise kuyo inkampani, kusenokubakho neminye imiba yokhuseleko kunye nokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo-mthetho ekufuneka ithathelwe ingqalelo, njengemigaqo-nkqubo yezemali okanye ubumfihlo babathengi.



#### 4.4.6 Ulawulo lwetalente – ziziphi izakhono ezintsha eziza kufuneka

Uninzi lwabantu bokuqala abasebenzisa i-Bitcoin bavela kwishishini le-IT, mhlawumbi ngenxa yokuba abo baqala basebenzisa le nkqubo babefuna ukuba nenqanaba eliphezulu lolwazi lobuchwepheshe ukuze baqonde indlela i-Bitcoin ekhupha ngayo ukhuseleko kunye nokunqongophala.

Njengoko kuchaziwe kwintshayelelo, kusekho umbono wokuba i-Bitcoin idibanisa yonke into ongayiqondiyo ngekhompyutha kunye nayo yonke into ongayiqondiyo ngemali. Le yokugqibela inokuba yinto efuna uqeqesho olungakumbi lwangaphakathi, kodwa ishishini lobuchwepheshe libekwe kakuhle xa kuthelekiswa namanye amashishini ekuchongeni nasekunikezeleni izakhono ezifanelekileyo ezifunekayo. Kuxhomekeke kwindawo ethile egxilwe kuyo, izakhono zobuchwepheshe ezintsha zinokufuneka zichongwe malunga nonxibelelwano, ukufihla ulwazi okanye i-cryptography ngaphakathi kwenkampani.

Ukuqonda imiba yezobuchwepheshe ye-Bitcoin nako kusweleka ngokubanzi, kwaye iinkampani zobuchwepheshe zibekwe kakuhle ukunceda ukufundisa imarike.

Ukuze bakwazi ukuhambisa uqeqesho olusebenzayo nolufanelekileyo kwinkqubela yangaphakathi kunye nenkxaso kwakunye nemarike, imiba yezemali ye-Bitcoin nayo kufuneka iqondwe. Oku kusenokungangeni kuluhlu lwezinto ezilula kwinkampani egxile kubuchwepheshe, kodwa kuyakufuneka ukuze kunikwe uqeqesho olusebenzayo kubathengi abanokubakho malunga nokuba kutheni kubalulekile kwaye kutheni iimveliso kunye neenkonzo ezintsha ezinikwa yinkampani zixabisekile.



#### 4.4.7 Ulawulo lwabathengi – ukunxibelelana, ukufakwa kunye nokugcinwa

Injongo ephambili yazo zonke iinkampani kukuzisa iziphumo ezilungileyo kubathengi abajolise kubo baze baguqulele oko kwinzuzo. Iinkampani zobuchwepheshe zihlala ziseka 'Iqela lempumelelo yomthengi' eligxile kulawulo lomthengi ngamnye kulo lonke ixesha lobomi, ukusuka ekwakheni ulwazi ukuya kukhetho, ukufakwa kunye nolawulo lwabathengi, ekugqibeleni nokunyaniseka.

Nokuba lo msebenzi ulawulwa njani, ngoku kuya kufuneka udibanise i-Bitcoin. Oku kungabandakanya:

* Iindibano zoqeqesho lwabathengi malunga ne-Bitcoin
* Ugalelo kuphuhliso lweenkonzo oluvela kubathengi
* Ukuthengisa iinkonzo ezintsha ezinxulumene ne-Bitcoin kubathengi abakhoyo
* Ulawulo lwaneliseko lwabathengi oluhlobene neenkonzo ezintsha
* Ukuqokelela izifundo zamatyala kunye nobungqina obuhlobene nezisombululo ze-Bitcoin

Oku kuya kufuna izakhono ezintsha kwaye mhlawumbi iinkqubo ezintsha kufuneka zisekwe.



#### 4.4.8 Uqambi-zinto ezintsha

I-Bitcoin isesekuqaleni kokwamkelwa kwayo kunye nophuhliso, kwaye ngenxa yoko ikwiqonga elikhawulezayo lotshintsho oluqhubekayo kwiindawo ezininzi zeshishini. Kubalulekile ke ngoko kwinkampani yobuchwepheshe ukuba ihlale ingaphezulu kolu tshintsho ukuze ihlale ifanelekileyo kwishishini. Njengoko ishishini lobuchwepheshe sele likhula ngesantya esiphezulu, inkcubeko yokuqamba izinto ezintsha rhoqo kufuneka sele ikhona, kwaye i-Bitcoin kufuneka idityaniswe kule nkqubo.



#### 4.4.9 Isishwankathelo

I-Intanethi yanyusa 'isebe le-IT' ukuya kwi-C-Suite ngokusebenzisa indima ye-CTO njengoko kwacaca ukuba i-IT ayisiyonto 'yokusebenza kuphela' kodwa itshintsha (okanye iyaphelelisa) iindlela zokusebenza. I-Bitcoin iyakha oku ngakumbi njengoko itshintsha icala lezemali leshishini. Yonke inkampani enkulu/ephezulu ngoku yishishini lesoftware kwaye, kwiminyaka eli-10 ukuya kweyi-15 ezayo, yonke inkampani inokuba yishishini le-Bitcoin nayo.

Iinkampani zobuchwepheshe zibekwe kakuhle ukuze ziqonde kwaye zixhamle kolu kwamkelwa kwe-Bitcoin. Oku kungathatha iindlela ezininzi ngokuxhomekeke kwiprofayili yenkampani kunye nemarike ejolise kuyo, kodwa kuya kufuna inqanaba lokuqonda kunye notyalo-mali kwiindawo ezininzi zenkampani ukuze iphumelele.
