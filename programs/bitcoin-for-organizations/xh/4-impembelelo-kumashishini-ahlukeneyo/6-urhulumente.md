# 4.6 Urhulumente

> Ukuba asibali ubuntu obungelulo ukuzingca, imali yinto ekhuthaza yonke intshukumo yomntu eyenziwa ngokuzithandela. I-Bitcoin yimali engathathi cala, yehlabathi enemiqathango, kodwa engenabaphathi. Ukuba ayinakunyanzelwa, kwaye ayiphumeleli, iziphumo kwezopolitiko zehlabathi azinakuthelekiswa.  
_James Dewar_



#### 4.6.0 Intshayelelo

> Xa itekhnoloji entsha ikugqugqisa, ukuba awuyinxalenye yomgqugqisi, uyinxalenye yomgaqo wendlela.  
_Stewart Brand_

Injongo yesahluko sesi kukubonisa oorhulumente, amasebe karhulumente, kunye nabasebenzi bakarhulumente ukuba ukungayiqwali i-Bitcoin kufana nokufaka intloko emhlabeni uthembe ukuba inganyanzelwa okanye iya kusilela. Ukuba akukho nanye kwezi ziphumo zenzeka, iya kutshintsha kakhulu indlela abantu abenza ngayo izigqibo. Abantu abahlala kummandla welizwe elikhoyo apho urhulumente wabo uqonda kuqala aze enze umgaqo-nkqubo ofanelekileyo banokufumana inzuzo enkulu.

Kusenokwenzeka ukuba i-Bitcoin iyanyanzelwa okanye iyasilela, kodwa ngaphandle kokuba urhulumente ufunda malunga netekhnoloji aze afikelele kweso sigqibo esekelwe kulwazi olungenakukhethi, uthatha umngcipheko omkhulu, iziphumo zawo ziya kuthwalwa ngabemi abamele ukuba bayabakhathalela. Emva kokufumana isaziso ngokufunda esi sahluko, oko kuya kuba kukungakhathali okukhulu kunye nokungafezekisi uxanduva ngoorhulumente banamhlanje, amasebe abo kunye nabasebenzi babo. Ukuba awufuni olo xanduva, yeka ukufunda ngoku, kwaye ushiye umsebenzi.

Oorhulumente banokuchazwa njengezinto ezisebenzisa, okanye ezifuna ukusebenzisa, igunya lokusebenzisa ugonyamelo kummandla wazo. Olu lungiselelo lufikelelwe ngenxa yokuzikhusela kwizingozi zangaphandle, kunye nokugcina uxolo ngaphakathi. Njengoko uluntu kunye noqoqosho luphuhlile, oorhulumente baye bandisa indima yabo ngaphakathi. Enye yeendima ezavela, kancinci kancinci, ukusukela ngasekupheleni kwenkulungwane ye-19 yayikukulawula imali ngokusebenzisa iibhanki eziphakathi. Injongo yesahluko sesi ayikokwenza ityala elichasene okanye elixhasa iibhanki eziphakathi kunye nolawulo lwemali ngurhulumente, koko kukubonisa ukuba kunokwenzeka ukuba olu lawulo lungabi nako kwixesha elizayo. Nabani na oqonda ukubaluleka kolawulo lwemali ekusebenziseni amanye amandla uyaqonda ukuba ukulahleka kolu lawulo lwemali kungaba ngumoya onamandla wokusasaza amandla ngaphakathi kumazwe.

Imbali isifundisa ukuba ngaphandle kwemfazwe okanye izifo, utshintsho olukhulu kuluntu luhlala lukhuthazwa yitekhnoloji kunomthetho kuba olo tshintsho lwalungasoloko lufunwa kwangaphambili ngabantu okanye ziinkokeli zabo. Enyanisweni, nokuba lwalufunwa, umthetho awunako ukufumanisa okanye ukuvelisa itekhnoloji.

Uninzi lwamandla oorhulumente abawafumene kule minyaka ilishumi elineshumi idlulileyo ngenxa yokulawula imali (into engazange yenzeke kwimbali) kusenokufuneka bayeke. Oku asisongelo kubantu, kodwa kunokuba yingozi kwisakhiwo sikarhulumente kunye nemibutho. Iinkokeli zemibutho yethu kufuneka zifunde malunga ne-Bitcoin kunye neziphumo zayo ukuze ziqonde indlela umdla wommandla kunye nabantu abenza ilizwe labo unokuchaphazeleka ngayo.

Kuphela ngale ndlela baya kukwazi ukukhetha imigaqo-nkqubo eya kuthi iphucule ikamva lommandla wabo kunye nabantu babo. Imfundo kunye nokuzithoba kuyakufuneka kubo bonke ukuze kwenziwe izigqibo ezifanelekileyo. Akukho ndawo yokuzimela koku, utshintsho luya kusinyanzela ngelinye ixesha, kwaye iziphumo ezingcono zinokufezekiswa ngokulindela utshintsho. Ithiyori yomdlalo esebenzayo ingatsho ukuba yamkela kwangoko okanye uphoswe.

Oku kunguvavanyo lwabapolitiki bethu kunye neenkokeli zemibutho. Ngaba bakhuthazwa ikakhulu ngumdla wokuphucula ikamva lommandla kunye nabantu abenza ilizwe labo ngoku? Okanye, ngaba izinto abazikhethayo zikhuthazwa zezinye iinjongo, nokuba zezakho, zeshishini okanye zezimvo?

Ngokufunda malunga ne-Bitcoin oorhulumente banokucwangcisa indlela yokuphucula ubomi babemi babo bangoku nabazayo. Esi sahluko sijolise ekuboniseni imiba ethile enokuthi ithathelwe ingqalelo ukuqala inkqubo yokucwangcisa.



#### 4.6.1 Itekhnoloji vs Umthetho

> Ezona zizathu zibalulekileyo zotshintsho azifumaneki kwiinkqubo zopolitiko okanye kwizimvo zoqoqosho zabafileyo, koko zifumaneka kwizinto ezifihlakeleyo ezitshintsha imida apho amandla asetyenziswa khona. Rhoqo, utshintsho oluncinane kwimozulu, kwindawo, kwiintsholongwane, nakwitekhnoloji lutshintsha indlela amandla asetyenziswa ngayo.  
_James Dale Davidson_

Imbali izele yimizekelo yokuba itekhnoloji entsha itshintshe indlela uluntu lwabantu olukhula ngayo nolawulwa ngayo. Incwadi ethi The Sovereign Individual, eyapapashwa ngo-1997 nguJames Dale Davidson noWilliam Rees-Mogg, iphonononga indlela utshintsho kwitekhnoloji olukhokelele ngayo kutshintsho eNtshona ukusuka kwihlabathi elalilawulwa yiCawa ukuya kwilizwe leentlanga esihlala kulo namhlanje. Bakhomba ezona tekhnoloji zibalulekileyo njengokuvela kwephephandaba kunye nokusetyenziswa kwepowuda yezibhamu njengamandla okusebenzisa ugonyamelo, nto leyo eyatshintsha indlela amandla esetyenziswa ngayo ngobuninzi.

Eyona nto ibalulekileyo kukuba akukho bungqina bokuba iCawa okanye uluntu ngokubanzi lwalufuna okanye lwaluqalise utshintsho olwenzeka. Xa sijonga emva, kubonakala ngathi amandla aphuma kulawulo lweCawa kulwazi ayehla njengoko uqoqosho olutsha lwasusa ilungelo lwayo lokupapasha izinto ezibhaliweyo.

Iphephandaba lehlisa iindleko zokuphinda uphinde ulwazi, ngaloo ndlela lonyusa ukusasazeka kwemveliso yezinto ezibhaliweyo.

Imbali ibonisa ukuba itekhnoloji eyabangela utshintsho olukhulu ayizange iqalwe ngurhulumente, imibutho, iinkokeli okanye uluntu ngokuvota. Rhoqo sibona ukuba abantu kunye nemibutho bazama ukuchasa, bathintele kwaye balibazise ukwamkelwa kwayo. Kwinkulungwane yama-20 oku sibona kwindlela abantu abaphendula ngayo ekuqaleni ekusetyenzisweni kweemoto, umbane, i-cryptography, i-imeyile kunye ne-intanethi.

Ukwamkelwa kweetekhnoloji ezintsha ziimarike kwakhokelela kutshintsho kwindawo abantu abahlala kuyo, indlela abasebenza ngayo kwaye ngamanye amaxesha kwasusa nesakhiwo sabo njengoluntu, ilizwe okanye umbutho wokhokelo. Kweminye imizekelo yatshintsha ubungakanani kunye nolwakhiwo lwaloo mbutho ngokwawo. Eminye imizekelo yetekhnoloji etshintshe uluntu ngokubanzi ibandakanya ukuvela kombane, iimoto kunye ne-intanethi.

Irhafu kunye nemboleko nazo zilawulwa ngumthetho kwaye ke zixhomekeke kwitekhnoloji. Oku kungabonwa kwimbali. Kwixesha elidlulileyo irhafu yayilinganiselwe kwizinto ezifana nerhafu yamaphepha kunye nerhafu yokungenisa ngaphandle kuba itekhnoloji eyayifunekayo ukuxhasa irhafu yengeniso kunye nezinye yayingenzeki.

Obu bungqina buchazwa ukuxhasa isiphelo sokuba itekhnoloji ime ngaphezu komthetho. Umthetho unokulandela emva koko, kodwa indlela izinto ezenzeka ngayo ibonisa ukuba umthetho awunakumisa utshintsho ngokwenene okanye ngokusisigxina, kwaye awunakuwenza utshintsho. Utshintsho kumthetho luyimiphumo yetekhnoloji entsha, hayi imbangela, kwaye itekhnoloji entsha ayinakuvotelwa, ayinakumiselwa ngukumkani okanye ithintelwe ngaphandle kokuzilimaza.

> Inyaniso ayinakuphikwa. Ubugwenxa bunokuyihlasela kwaye ubungazi bunokuyigculela, kodwa ekugqibeleni, isekhona.  
_Winston Churchill_

Iitekhnoloji eziphumeleleyo zifana "nenyaniso". Njengoko kunjalo, umthetho awukwazanga ukuthintela inkqubela phambili yeetekhnoloji ezininzi ezathi ngexesha lazo uluntu lwalungafuni ukuba ziqhubeke. Xa oku kwenzeka, rhoqo kwanciphisa ubutyebi belizwe elo kwixesha eliphakathi ukuya kwelide.



#### 4.6.2 Ukuhlulwa kwamandla

> Akukho mathandabuzo ukuba amandla oorhulumente aphakathi okusebenzisa olu hlobo lwemali yenye yezinto ezikhokelele ekwandeni kokugxila kwamandla kurhulumente ophakathi.  
_Friedrich A. Hayek_

UHayek wathi ukugxila kwemali okwenzeka kwinkulungwane yama-20 kwaba sesona sizathu sokugxila kwamandla kumazwe. Ukuvela kwe-Bitcoin kunokubuyisela oku umva, into abantu abaninzi abayixhasayo kwaye abapolitiki bayithetha, kodwa abakwazi ukuyifezekisa.

Ngaphambi kwenkulungwane yama-20 ookumkani, ookumkanikazi, oorhulumente babenolawulo olulinganiselweyo kwimithombo yemali. Ubutyebi obulinganiswa yimali abuphumi phezulu, koko buvela phantsi, buhlulwe-hlulwe. Kude kube yinkulungwane yama-20 imali yayibonisa le nyaniso kwaye nayo yayiyinyani. Ifomathi yale mali, itekhnoloji yayo, yayahluka ngokweendawo nangexesha njengoko uluntu lwabantu lwalukhula.

Ukusukela ekuqaleni kwenkulungwane yama-20 kwenziwa iimvavanyo zokukhupha "inyani" yemali, kwagqitywa ngokwahlula ngokupheleleyo ngo-1961 xa ukuguqulwa kwedola ibe yigolide "kwamiswa okwethutyana".

> Ndiyalele uNobhala uConnally ukuba amise okwethutyana ukuguqulwa kwedola yaseMelika ngaphandle kwemigaqo nemiqathango eza kugqitywa ngokwenzuzo yokuzinza kwemali kunye nomdla waseUnited States.  
_Richard Nixon_

Imali yitekhnoloji kwaye i-Bitcoin, njengemali yedijithali, inokuba yenye yezona tekhnoloji zinkulu kwimbali yoluntu.

> ...imali, kwisiseko sayo, yitekhnoloji njengazo zonke ezinye izixhobo ezisisiseko ezifana nesando, isile okanye ivili.  
_Business Insider_

Mhlawumbi imali inokuqhathaniswa nobukhulu bokufumaneka kokulima, iphephandaba okanye ipowuda yezibhamu. Ukuba asibali ubuntu obungelulo ukuzingca, imali ikhuthaza yonke intshukumo yomntu eyenziwa ngokuzithandela kwaye ke kunokwenzeka ukuba isisombululo esitsha kule teknoloji sabantu abazizigidi ezisibhozo sinokuba yeyona teknoloji inempembelelo enkulu kwimbali yoluntu.

Imali ngamandla, yitekhnoloji, kwaye ke ime ngaphezu komthetho, kwaye ke ngokulandela, ime ngaphezu kwemibutho eyenza imithetho. Ukuhlulwa kwemali kuya kukhokelela ekuhlulweni kwamandla.

> Enye yezohlwayo ezihlawulelwayo ngenxa yezibonelelo zokuzinza kwemali kukuba umthetho ufumaneka ungakulungelanga utshintsho olungalindelekanga noluguqukayo kwinkqubo yemali.  
Phanor _J. Eder_

Namhlanje amazwe achaza imali enye okanye ezingaphezulu njengemali esemthethweni. Ukuba imarike ingakhetha enye imali njengemali ekhethwayo apha okanye phesheya, oorhulumente banokufumana imeko apho imfuno yemali yasekhaya idlula yimfuno yemali yehlabathi, engathathi cala, 'yinyani'. Siza kuphinda sibone ukuba imali yokwenene yinto emiselweyo yimarike enokubangela utshintsho kumthetho, kwaye ayidalwa ngumthetho. Amazwe angasoloko ekhetha ukugcina imithetho yemali esemthethweni echaza imali engafunwayo, kodwa kungangcono ukuba acinge ngeziphumo ezimbi zokwenza olo khetho:

> Imbali ibonisa ukuba akunakwenzeka ukuzikhusela kwiziphumo zokuba abanye begcina imali enzima kune yakho.  
_Saifedean Ammous_

Ingxoxo engentla ibonisa indlela imali ehlulwe-hlulweyo, yehlabathi, engenabuphathi enokukhokelela ekuhlulweni kwamandla. Ngoku sijonga indlela i-Bitcoin, njengetekhnoloji yonxibelelwano yemali ehlulwe-hlulweyo, inokuba ngumqondiso wokuqala wokuhlulwa konxibelelwano kunye nemithombo yeendaba engengomali.



##### Ukuhlulwa konxibelelwano

I-Bitcoin yitekhnoloji yonxibelelwano ehlulwe-hlulweyo, evulekileyo, engenabuphathi, kwaye imarike ibonakala iyixabisa ngakumbi njengemali.

Zikhona iimpawu zale protocol ezinokuthi zivelise umgangatho omtsha kwiindlela zonxibelelwano zabantu ezingaphaya kwemali. Siqala ukubona izisombululo zonxibelelwano kunye nemidiya yoluntu ziqala ukuvela kwaye zifumana ukuthandwa ezisebenzisa ezinye zezi mpawu, umzekelo iNostr kunye neKeet. Oku kunokuba sisiqalo sokwahlulwa okukhulu kweenkonzo xa kuthelekiswa nendlela i-intanethi esetyenziswa ngayo namhlanje. Ezi nkonzo zemidiya nazo kulindeleke ukuba zibe nokwenzeka kokwenza iintengiselwano ngeBitcoin ngokwendalo.

Ngokujonga iingxoxo zamva nje, ngakumbi kumazwe aphuhlileyo, malunga nenkululeko yokuthetha kunye nokucinywa kolwazi, ukwahlulwa kweendlela zonxibelelwano akufanele kungahoywa ngurhulumente, kwaye kufuneka kube yinxalenye yomgaqo-nkqubo namhlanje nokuba iBitcoin uqobo ayithathelwa ngqalelo.

Kusenokwenzeka ukuba akulunganga kurhulumente ukuba abe ngqongqo kakhulu ezama ukuvala, ukuvala ngokupheleleyo, ukucima okanye ukuphazamisa ezinye izisombululo eziphakathi. Okukhona urhulumente esenza nzima, kokukhona izisombululo ezizezinye zikhula ngokukhawuleza. Kumazwe aphuhlileyo anenkululeko, ezi zisombululo zizezinye ziya kuba nzima kakhulu ukulandelwa, kwaye ke phantse akunakwenzeka ukulandela uluntu olupheleleyo.

Ngoko ke indlela efanelekileyo inokuba kukusebenzisana nezisombululo eziphakathi ngokuzithandela kugxilwe kuphela kumonakalo omkhulu ovunyelweyo kwaye kungagxilwa kwimibono engavumelekanga ngokubanzi okanye kubemi ngabanye. Ukuzibophelela kwehlabathi kwinkululeko epheleleyo yokuthetha kumaqonga akhoyo kuya kunciphisa uxinzelelo lokusuka kwiinkonzo ezisandul' ukuvela ezahlulweyo.



#### 4.6.3 Ukwenziwa kwehlabathi liphela

##### Ukwenziwa kwehlabathi liphela ngokusebenzisa iimpawu

I-Bitcoin yimpahla yedijithali kwaye ke ayithathi ndawo yenyani kwaye ayinawo umkhondo wendawo. Ukongeza, iBitcoin ayisiyo sakhiwo somthetho. Ngoko ke iBitcoin yinto entsha eyahlukileyo ethe yaba yimpahla exabisekileyo kodwa engenalo ikhaya ngokwasemzimbeni okanye ngokwasemthethweni.

Ihlabathi lidlule kwixesha elidlulileyo apho lalisebenzisa umgangatho omnye wemali - umgangatho wegolide. Nangona amazwe ayenayo imali yawo, amaxabiso otshintshiselwano ayengatshintshi rhoqo kuba ayexabiswa ngokobunzima begolide eyayimele yona. Nangona kunjalo, ubunzima kunye nokungakhawulezi kokuhanjiswa kwegolide kwaphelisa eli xesha. Kwakhona, oku kwathintela ukwenziwa kwehlabathi liphela okwakunokwenziwa yegolide ngaphandle kokuba ngumlinganiselo ofanayo wexabiso. Ukusinda kwegolide njengomgangatho wemali wehlabathi kwakulinganiselwe kwaye ekugqibeleni kwaphazanyiswa kukuba ithatha indawo yenyani.

##### Ukwenziwa kwehlabathi liphela ngokusebenzisa inethiwekhi yoluntu

Uluntu lweBitcoin lukhule njengothungelwano lwehlabathi lonke. Indawo yokuhlala ingachaphazela inkululeko abantu abanayo ngokweendawo, umzekelo ukufikelela kwi-intanethi evulekileyo, okanye uluhlu lwezicelo ezivunyelweyo kwi-Apple App Store, okanye kwi-Google Play Store. Indawo yokuhlala ingachaphazela nemeko yezomthetho kunye nemigaqo yeBitcoin njengempahla kunye nendlela yokutshintshiselana. Nangona kunjalo, inkcubeko kunye nolwazi oluqhelekileyo olukhulileyo nolukhulayo kuluntu lweBitcoin ngamava ahlangeneyo ehlabathi.

#### 4.6.4 Imigaqo yokuziphatha

Ezona ngxaki zibalulekileyo zokuziphatha ziyahluka ngokwepolitiki yehlabathi. Sijonga iindlela ezimbini: Indlela yehlabathi eliphuhlileyo kunye nendlela yeGlobal South. Ukuba inkqubo yemali ekhoyo inemiba eyahlukeneyo kubemi ngokuxhomekeke kwindawo abahlala kuyo nako kungathathwa njengengxaki yokuziphatha enxulumene nokulingana kwabantu.

##### Indlela yehlabathi eliphuhlileyo - ukungalingani kobutyebi kunye nokusingqongileyo

I-Cantillon Effect ithiywe ngegama likaRichard Cantillon, ingcali yezoqoqosho yaseIreland-France yenkulungwane ye-18 eyabhala okokuqala ngale ngxelo. Le mpembelelo ichaza indlela engalinganiyo umphumo wokunyuka kwemali kwimpahla kunye neempahla kuqoqosho. Xa imali entsha ye-fiat ingeniswa kuqoqosho, imiphumo yayo ivakala ngabantu kunye namashishini ahlukeneyo ngexesha elahlukileyo. Ngokuqhelekileyo iimpahla zonyuka ixabiso, zixhamlisa abatyebi, ngelixa abo bengenampahla bejamelene nokunyuka kwamaxabiso ngokubanzi.

Inkqubo yemali ekhoyo iyimbangela ephambili yokwanda komahluko wobutyebi phakathi kwabatyebi nabasweleyo. Amandla abatyebi kunye nabo banxulumene nezopolitiko okusebenzisa inkqubo ekhoyo ukuze baxhamle, ngexabiso labo bangaphantsi, yimpawu yendlela inkqubo yemali esebenza ngayo. Le ndlela ikwakhokelela ekunciphiseni kwiklasi ephakathi, abambalwa baceba, abaninzi baswela. Ukubuyela kwimali engathathi cala enokufumaneka kuphela ngomsebenzi, kungakhathaliseki ubutyebi bomntu, kuya kuyeka ukwanda kwala mahluko wobutyebi kwaye kuqalise ukuwanciphisa, kukhokelela kuluntu olungalinganiyo kangako.

Kwihlabathi apho imali ingenziwa nje ukukhuthaza ukusetyenziswa kwezixhobo, izixhobo ezikhoyo ziya kusetyenziswa ngendlela esebenzayo kwaye engachithwanga. Kulindeleke ukuba sibone utshintsho kwibhalansi yezikhuthazo zoqoqosho phakathi kwemveliso entsha kunye 'nokulungisa nokuphinda kusetyenziswe' apho oku kokugqibela kuya kunyuka. EMazantsi eHlabathi sibona kwindawo apho iinkqubo zemali zilawulwa kakubi, kukho ukunyuka kwamaxabiso aphezulu, ukungakwazi ukutshintsha imali kwamanye amazwe kunye namanqanaba aphantsi okufikelela kwiinkonzo zezezimali. Kumazwe amaninzi amakhulu isiqingatha okanye nangaphezulu kwabemi abanakho iakhawunti yasebhankini. Imali yehlabathi engathathi cala, engafuni mvume kwaye inikezelwa ngokusisigxina inezinto ezininzi ezinokunceda olu luntu.

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso aphezulu kwenza kube nzima kubemi ukuba bagcine imali. Ukugcina imali yinto ebalulekileyo ekwakheni ikapitali kuqoqosho kwaye ukuswela oko kuthintela ukukhula okwenyani kunye nokuqina. Elo tyala lidla ngokuba nemigaqo ethile echaza uhlobo lophuhliso loqoqosho olufunwa ngumbolekisi kwaye enokuba ayisiyo leyo imarike yendawo ebeya kuyiphuhlisa ngokwendalo. Ukuba elo tyala lilawulwa embindini likwanika ithuba kubalawuli abangathembekiyo ukuba baphange inxalenye yalo ukuze bazuze.

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso aphezulu kukhokelela ekunyukeni kokubaluleka kwexesha elifutshane njengoko abantu bengasayiboni imfuno yekamva. Ukunciphisa ukubaluleka kwexesha elifutshane kubalulekile ekukhuleni kwenkcubeko kunye noqoqosho. Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso okuphantsi, kunye nokunciphisa ukubaluleka kwexesha elifutshane kwenza kube lula kubantu ukucwangcisa kunye nokutyala imali kwikamva.

Ukungabandakanywa kwenani elikhulu labemi behlabathi ekufikeleleni kutshintshiselwano lwemali yehlabathi kukwabangela ukuba bangabandakanyeki kwiimarike zehlabathi. Oku kubenzakalisa ekubeni bakwazi ukuziphilisa ngokubonelela ngeempahla kunye neenkonzo kubathengi abavumayo. Ukungabandakanywa kwabo kukwabangela umonakalo kubathengi abanokuxhamla kwizibonelelo zokhuphiswano abanokuthi abo bangabandakanyekanga babanike; sonke siyalahlekelwa, abanye ngakumbi kunabanye.



#### 4.6.5 Ukuvavanywa kweenketho zomgaqo-nkqubo

> Utshintsho ngumthetho wobomi. Kwaye abo bajonga kuphela kwixesha elidlulileyo okanye langoku ngokuqinisekileyo baza kuphoswa likamva  
_John F. Kennedy_

Nokuba oorhulumente bathatha siphi isisombululo kule ngxaki, ubunzulu nobubanzi beempembelelo zayo kuthetha ukuba ukunyuka kweBitcoin kufuneka kongezwe kuluhlu lomngcipheko welizwe.



##### 4.6.5.1 Ukuchasa - Umlo

Ezinye izinto zomgaqo-nkqubo karhulumente, kodwa ngakumbi ezabaphathi-mali, zibonakala ngathi zithathe le ndlela ixesha elininzi ukusukela oko iBitcoin yaba nexabiso elitsalayo. Xa imigaqo-nkqubo ivele, ihlala isabela kuphela, icotha kwaye inefuthe lokuthintela ukukhula. Nangona kunjalo, oku kusenokuba kungakhange kujoliswe kuko kwaye kusenokuba sisiphumo sokungabikho kwendlela ecwangcisiweyo evela kurhulumente, kwaye ke abaphathi-mali abangaphantsi.

Ukuba iBitcoin iyaqhubeka nokunyuka, iincwadi zembali ziya kubona ukuba ukutyhala amathuba kwabanye kuya kuba yinkohlakalo enkulu kubemi abahlala kumazwe athathe le ndlela. Ukunyuka kwe-intanethi kwachasa kancinci, ekuqaleni kakhulu, kumazwe aphuhlileyo, kodwa ubulumko baphumelela ngokukhawuleza kwaye uninzi lwezibonelelo zorhwebo nezobuchwepheshe zaphuhliswa, zakhiwa kwaye zafunyanwa ngala mazwe awamkela xa yayisencinci.



##### 4.6.5.2 Sukuwukhathalela - Umngcakazi

Le ndlela ibonakala ngathi yeyona ithathwe ngurhulumente abaninzi ukusukela ngo-2009 ukuya ku-2024 ngaphandle kwamazwe ambalwa afana ne-El Salvador ne-Bhutan. Nangona kukho imizamo yeendawo zomgaqo-nkqubo ezifana neBitcoin Policy Institute e-US kunye neBitcoin Policy UK, urhulumente abaninzi bahlala bengazi kakhulu ngemiba kwaye ke baphoswa zizinyathelo zokunciphisa umngcipheko namathuba avela kuKunyuka kweBitcoin. Xa imigaqo-nkqubo ibikhona, ihlala isabela kuphela endaweni yokucwangciswa kakuhle.

Ukungazi ngenjongo akufanele kube yindlela yamkelekileyo kuba kukudlala ngethamsanqa nekamva labantu abahlala kwiindawo urhulumente anoxanduva kuzo. Ukuxhasa ulwazi olungelulo kunye nokusebenzisa intetho yezopolitiko ukunciphisa itekhnoloji ebalulekileyo akusiyo indlela yokuziphatha kakuhle okanye yobungcali ekwenzeni ezopolitiko. Ukuhleka iBitcoin njenge 'imali yomlingo ye-intanethi' kusenokuba kwakufanelekile kwiminyaka yokuqala xa yayiyinto yabathandi bobuchwepheshe okanye inexabiso eliphantsi kakhulu, kodwa ngoku akusisiso isikhundla esamkelekileyo.



##### 4.6.5.3 Libazisa - Umphambili ongacothayo

Ukuzama ukulibazisa ukunyuka kweBitcoin yindlela enokwenziwa. Nangona kunjalo, oku kuya kuza ngexabiso lesiphumo esibi kubantu abahlala ngoku nakwixesha elizayo kwiindawo urhulumente anoxanduva kuzo. Ngenxa yalo mngcipheko, le ndlela kufuneka ilandelwe kuphela xa kukho ulwazi kunye nobuchule obufanelekileyo.

Kusenokubakho iindawo apho ukongeza ukulibazisa okuthile okuquka umxholo othile kwaye okuququzelelwa ngamazwe ngamazwe kungaba luncedo ekuthomalaliseni utshintsho olungathintelwa. Umzekelo omnye kukuba ngokunciphisa imizamo yokucinezela okanye ukubeka imithwalo engafanelekanga kwiinkampani zemidiya yoluntu eziphakathi, intshukumo eya kwizisombululo ezingacinezelwayo ingalibaziseka. Oku kunganciphisa iindleko zolawulo lolwaphulo-mthetho lwezezimali lwamazwe ngamazwe kunye nolunye ulwaphulo-mthetho olukhulu ixesha elide.
