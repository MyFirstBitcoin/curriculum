# 4.7 Imibutho yeNceba kunye neMibutho engenzi-Nzuzo

> Kuyingozi ngakumbi eluntwini ukuba usozigidi achithe ubutyebi bakhe obungaphezulu kwinto encedo kunokuba achithe kwinto yobunewunewu. Kuba xa echitha kubunewunewu, ubangela umonakalo ikakhulu kuye kunye nabantu abakufutshane naye, kodwa xa echitha kwinto encedo, ubangela umonakalo omkhulu ngakumbi eluntwini.  
_John A. Hobson_



#### 4.7.0 Intshayelelo



> Oorhulumente abayi kuphelelwa kuphela ngamandla okuqokelela irhafu kwiindidi ezininzi zemivuzo kunye nempahla; baphinde bajongane nokuphulukana namandla okuqobela abantu kwimicimbi yemali.  
_James Dale Davidson_



Njengoko kuchaziwe kwicandelo elijolise koorhulumente, ukunyuka kweBitcoin kulindeleke ukuba kubeke uxinzelelo olukhulu kwiimali zikarhulumente zokuxhasa iinkonzo zentlalontle. Olu xinzelelo longezelelweyo kulindeleke ukuba luvele ngexesha elifanayo namazwe amaninzi aseNtshona ajongene nabemi abadala abaninzi.



Ukususwa kukarhulumente njengomxhasi okanye umhambisi weenkonzo, kudityaniswe nemfuno enkulu evela kubemi babo, kuya kukhokelela kwimfuno enkulu kakhulu yemisebenzi yenceba kunye nephilanthropi yokuxhasa loo misebenzi. Kwihlabathi apho iiasethi zemali zinokugcinwa ngokuzimeleyo kwaye zingabonakali ngokwasemzimbeni, amazinga aphezulu erhafu eqobayo anokuhlala kuphela, nokuba kumazwe anenkululeko yokhetho, ngokunyanzeliswa kwemida engamkelekanga kwinkululeko yokuhamba. Bona isicatshulwa esingentla esivela kwiThe Sovereign Individual, okanye uyifunde yonke.



Nangona kunjalo, zikhona amathuba kwimisebenzi yenceba kunye nemibutho engenzi nzuzo. Akusosiphelo semfuno yeenkonzo zabo, koko kukuchasene noko. Kwaye abaninzi kwabo bafunda kwaye babona kwangaphambili baza kuba nempumelelo ngeli xesha lotshintsho baza kulandela umkhondo wabo bangaphambili (umzekelo uRockefeller noCarnegie) baze babe ngabaphilanthropi njengoko bebona imfuno kwaye benamandla okunceda.



#### 4.7.1 Imingcipheko



> Ndinyanzelekile ukuba ndihlawule imali eninzi kubahluphekileyo ngokomthetho, kangangokuba andinakukwazi ukunikela ngokuzithandela... oku kuthintela kwaye kubuthathachisa umoya wenceba ngaphakathi; kwaye lo moya, xa ungasebenzi... uba buthathaka ngakumbi nangakumbi, kwaye ekugqibeleni, mhlawumbi, uyaphela ngokupheleleyo  
_Thomas Alcock_



Imingcipheko ejongene nemibutho yenceba ivela kwezi ngqwalasela zilandelayo:

* Ukwanda kwemfuno yoluntu kwiinkonzo zempilo nentlalontle ngenxa yotshintsho lwabemi (kumazwe aphuhlileyo)
* Ukuncipha kwamandla karhulumente okuqhubeka nokubonelela ngeenkonzo ezikhoyo
* Ukuncipha kwamandla karhulumente okuqhubeka nokuxhasa ngemali imibutho yenceba ukuze iqhubeke neenkonzo ezikhoyo



Oku kungasentla kukhokelela kwimngcipheko wokuba imibutho yenceba kunye neengenzinzuzo zinokujongana nemfuno enkulu yeenkonzo ngexesha elifanayo nemithombo ephambili yemali isehla.



Ngexesha lekhulu lama-20 urhulumente waba ngumboneleli ophambili weenkonzo zempilo nentlalontle kumazwe amaninzi aphuhlileyo, imimandla eyayikade iphantsi kwemibutho yenceba, amaqela oluntu kunye neendawo zonqulo. Ukuvela kwenkqubo yentlalontle ukusukela emva kwemfazwe yesibini yehlabathi kusekelwe ekunyukeni kwamandla koorhulumente bamazwe. Ukuba oku kuya kutshintsha, kuya kuvela imingxunya kwinkonzo yoluntu kwaye abantu baza kuzama ukuzilungiselela ukuzalisa ezo mingxunya.





> Kuyanyanyisa kuluntu oluphucukileyo ukuba izibhedlele zixhomekeke kwimisebenzi yenceba yabucala.  
_Aneurin Bevan_





Nangona abantu abaninzi, kuquka nabapolitiki, benze amabango okuziphatha malunga nokuxhomekeka kwimibutho yenceba njengento "enyanyisayo" okanye efanayo, kunokwenzeka ukuba ukugcina ezo ngcinga kwakuyilungelo lexeshana. Kuyo nayiphi na imeko, ezo ngcinga ziye zaphikiswa ngokwengqondo. Ukubuyela kulindelo lokuba abo banamandla makhulu baza kuthwala umthwalo omkhulu ngokuzithandela kunokuba kuxhatshazwa, kunokuthi kube luncedo ngokuziphatha nangokwezentlalo.



Inkqubela yangoku kunye neyesikhathi esizayo kwitekhnoloji yehlabathi kunye nemveliso ithetha ukuba olu tshintsho aluyi kuba kukubuyela emva kwihlabathi langaphambi kwekhulu lama-20. Izixhobo zokubonelela ngeziseko zokhuseleko ezifunwayo namhlanje zigqithile kakhulu kwezo zikhoyo ngaphambili. Eneneni iBitcoin, ngokukhuthaza izigqibo ezingcono zokwabiwa kwezixhobo, ukukhula kwemali kunye nokunciphisa ukulibazisa, ngokwayo iya kubonelela ngesiseko esitsha sokwandisa izixhobo ezinokuveliswa luqoqosho lwehlabathi.





#### 4.7.2 Izoyikiso



> Utshintsho ngumthetho wobomi. Kwaye abo bajonga kuphela kwixesha elidlulileyo okanye langoku ngokuqinisekileyo baza kuphoswa lixesha elizayo  
_John F. Kennedy_



##### **Imali yokuxhasa**



Imibutho yenceba okanye engenzi nzuzo efumana inkxaso-mali kurhulumente ophakathi okanye wengingqi inokuba semngciphekweni wokuncitshiswa kwezi mali ngenxa yokusasazeka kwamandla emali kude nezi ziko zopolitiko.



Umngcipheko ofanayo usebenza nakwimibutho efumana inkxaso-mali enkulu kwiinkampani ezinkulu ezinokuthi zona zibe semngciphekweni ngenxa yokunyuka kweBitcoin. Ngokukodwa, siza kugxininisa ezo nkampani namhlanje zisebenza kwicandelo leenkonzo zemali. Ukuba ezi nkampani zixhasayo azizilungiselele kwaye aziphenduli kule teknoloji intsha, amandla azo okuqhubeka nokuxhasa imisebenzi yenceba anokuncipha.



Imibutho yenceba efumana inxenye yemali yayo kutyalo-mali, ilifa okanye izibonelelo, inokuba semngciphekweni wokuba ingeniso yokwenene kwezi mali inciphe njengoko iBitcoin inciphisa ixabiso lemali elikhoyo kwezinye iiasethi. Ngokukodwa oku kunokuchaphazela umhlaba, igolide kunye nezitokhwe. Amabond akwanokuphulukana nexabiso lokwenene hayi ngenxa yokuxhaphaza karhulumente kuphela, kodwa nangenxa yokhuphiswano oluvela kwiasethi yemali entsha.



Ubume babaxhasi bemali buhlala butshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha kwaye oku akusiyo into entsha. Nangona kunjalo, into enokuthi yenzeke ngenxa yokunyuka kweBitcoin kukuba umahluko kwiingqikelelo kunye neembono zezizukulwana ezitsha unokwahluka kakhulu kunoko bekubonwa ngaphambili.







##### Ukwanda kwemfuno



Njengoko oorhulumente besiba namandla amancinci okubonelela ngezinye iinkonzo zempilo nentlalontle, kuya kubakho imfuno engagqitywanga eluntwini. Abantu baza kujonga ngakumbi kwimibutho emincinci, yengingqi ukuze bancede ukuhlangabezana nale mfuno. Imibutho emitsha iza kuvela, ixhaswa ngabaxhasi abatsha abazinikeleyo.



Ilizwe eliphuhlileyo lijongene nengxaki enkulu yabemi ngenxa yokuguga kwabantu kunye nokwehla kwamazinga okuzala. Le mikhwa ityhala oorhulumente ekubeni bangene kwiingxaki zemali kwaye kwezinye iimeko yandisa umthwalo wezikweletu zexesha elizayo ezingaxhaswanga. Imfuno emininzi yangoku ayihlangatyezwanga, kwaye izithembiso ezingenakwenzeka ukuba zifezekiswe ziyaqhubeka zenziwa. Oku kukhuthazwa zizizathu zexesha elifutshane zopolitiko, kodwa kunemiphumo embi yokunyusa ulindelo olungaphezulu kakhulu kokunokunikezelwa. Olu lindelo luyimvelaphi yemfuno yesikhathi esizayo kuba xa zingafezekiswanga, abantu banokungazilungiselele ngokwabo, nangona babenokukwazi ukuba bebengazifumani ezo zithembiso ekuqaleni.



#### 4.7.3 Amathuba



Kule ndima sigxininisa amanye amathuba anokubakho kwimibutho yenceba kunye neengenzinzuzo anokubonelelwa yiBitcoin. Awagqitywanga, kodwa ngamathuba afumanekayo kwimibutho emininzi kwaye ajolise ekukhuthazeni ingcinga.



Enye ingcamango ephambili kukuba ukuba iBitcoin iyaqhubeka nokuba yiasethi kunye nemali exabisekileyo kwihlabathi, kukho isizukulwana esele sibandakanyekile namhlanje apho abaninzi babaphilanthropi bekamso baza kuvela khona. Bangamaqela anomdla kwaye azinikeleyo abaninzi sele beqonda ukuba ubutyebi obukhulu buza nomthwalo onzima. Kulindeleke ukuba bazimisele ngakumbi ukusebenzisana nokuxhasa imibutho yenceba ezibeka ngokuziphatha ukunceda abasweleyo, nokuba imfuno ikhona ngoku, okanye iza kuvela njengoko oorhulumente besiba namandla amancinci okuzalisekisa izithembiso zangaphambili.



Ngemfuno entsha kunye nesizukulwana esitsha sabaxhasi, kungafanelekile ukucinga ukuqala isebe elitsha lemibutho yenceba okanye engenzi nzuzo. Isebe elitsha elizimeleyo linokufumana ingqwalasela engcono kwaye likwazi ukuvelisa izinto ezintsha ngokukhawuleza ekunikezeleni nasekufumaneni inkxaso-mali. Unokufumana nabasebenzi abathanda iBitcoin abakulungele ukunikela ngexesha labo ukukunceda, kunye nokubonelela ngezixhobo zokuxhasa iphilanthropi ngemali, kuba ukuba nobutyebi kuthetha ukuba abantu bangabanikazi bexesha labo.





##### Yamkela kwaye ujonge iminikelo yeBitcoin



Ukwamkela iBitcoin yindlela elula, enexabiso eliphantsi yokunxibelelana nesizukulwana sabantu abathanda iBitcoin. Zininzi iinkampani kwihlabathi jikelele ezenza oku ukuze zifumane ingeniso eyongezelelweyo, zisebenzisa amajelo onxibelelwano oluntu kunye neenkonzo ezikwi-intanethi ukuzazisa, rhoqo simahla. Ngokwamkela iBitcoin, imibutho ivulela iminyango kwimarike yehlabathi ekhula ngokukhawuleza yabantu abatyebileyo ngokuhamba kwexesha. Le marike kulula ukuyijolisa okwangoku kuba idibene kakhulu kumaqonga onxibelelwano oluntu nakwi-intanethi.



Kwezinye iindawo zerhafu, irhafu yenzuzo iyafuneka xa kusetyenziswa iBitcoin kumashishini. Nangona kunjalo, iminikelo kwimibutho yenceba inokuba ayirhafiswa nto leyo enciphisa imiqobo ngakumbi kunakwishishini.



Ukwakha igama elihle kolu lwalamano lwehlabathi okwangoku kuya kuba lula kakhulu kuneminyaka embalwa ezayo xa sele likhulu kwaye umbutho wakho ungomnye kwamakhulu amkela iBitcoin. Ukongeza, abantu obadibanisa nabo namhlanje kulindeleke ukuba babe yinxalenye enkulu yabaphilanthropi bekamso kunabo baza kwamkela iBitcoin kamva. Ukunxibelelana nolu lwalamano luvula amathuba ehlabathi ukuba uthathelwe ingqalelo kwiilifa zeBitcoin kunye nolunye uncedo olungengomali njengokuzinikela.







##### Ulawulo lweMali



Kwimibutho yenceba kunye neengenzinzuzo ezifumana ingeniso ethile kutyalo-mali okanye/izibonelelo, ukufaka iBitcoin kwindibaniselwano yotyalo-mali kunokuzisa iziphumo ezingcono kumngcipheko othile ngokunyusa iSharpe ratio. Bona iSahluko 7.3 'Ulawulo lweMali' ukufumana ulwazi olungakumbi ngaloku.





##### Ukwenziwa kwezinto ezintsha ekunikezeleni



Amashishini amaninzi asebenzisana neBitcoin azisa iimveliso kunye neenkonzo ezijolise kolu lwalamano ukukhuthaza ingeniso kunye nokuzinikela kwabathengi. Kukho ithuba lemibutho yenceba lokujonga indlela itekhnoloji yeBitcoin enokubonelela ngayo iqonga lokuphucula indlela abanikela ngayo iinkonzo zabo.



Sinikezela umzekelo omnye apha ukukunceda uqonde le ngcamango kwaye sikuncede kwinkqubo yakho yokwenza izinto ezintsha.



‘Bhanka’ abangenaso isikhwama: Amakhadi okuhlawula eBitcoin, anikezelwa kwaye agcwaliswa yimibutho yenceba, anokunceda abantu abangenazo iiakhawunti zebhanki, nokuba ngenxa yemiba yesazisi okanye ingeniso encinci, ukufikelela kwiinkonzo ezifana neebhanki zokutya ngelixa begcina imfihlo kunye nesidima. Esi sisombululo sinokukhuthaza izakhono ezinxulumene nokuphila ngokuzimela njengokwenza izigqibo ezisekwe kubhajethi evenkileni. Oku kunokubonelela ngesiseko sokuba abafumani-mali bafumane amava kulawulo lwezemali nangona bengafikeleli kwiibhanki. Kunganceda nokuqinisekisa ukuba iintlawulo zifika kwiindawo ezifunekayo ngokukhawuleza nangexabiso eliphantsi.





##### Ulwakhiwo lokuNikela



Cinga ngokusasaza ulwakhiwo ukuze ubandakanye uluntu lwendawo ngakumbi. Ulwakhiwo olusasazekileyo lunokukhuthaza ukubandakanyeka okuvela ezantsi kwaye lunokubonakalisa ngcono intshukumo eya kwihlabathi olusasazekileyo.





##### Ukwanda kwemfuno



Chonga indawo apho iinjongo zothando zombutho wakho zinqamlezana khona neendawo apho kukho iimfuno ezingekagcwaliswa, okanye apho urhulumente ngoku egcwalisa ezinye iimfuno kodwa anokurhoxa okanye anciphise unikezelo xa ejongene nemingeni yezezimali kwixesha elizayo.



Beka phambili kwaye wenze izicwangciso zokwandisa iinkonzo zakho kwezi ndawo.
