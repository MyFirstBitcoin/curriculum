#### 4.3.0 Intshayelelo

> Xa itekhnoloji entsha ikugqugqisa, ukuba awuyinxalenye yelori, uyinxalenye yendlela.  
_Stewart Brand_

Ububambiswano phakathi kwamashishini ezebhanki kunye nenkonzo zokuhlawula buye baba namandla ukusukela oko kwavela iibhanki. Amaziko amaninzi abandakanyeka kwinqanaba elithile kuzo zombini ezi zenzo. Kwiminyaka yoo-2020, ezona bhanki zinkulu zikwangaphakathi kuluhlu lweenkampani ezinkulu zenkonzo zokuhlawula kwaye zihlala zinobunini obukhulu bokwabelana. Umzekelo, ngoDisemba 2023, iBank of America, eyaseka iVisa ngo-1958 njengeBankAmericard, yayinesabelo esingu-1.53% kwiVisa Inc. Morgan Stanley yayinesabelo esingu-3.26% (umthombo: Wikipedia).

Kude kube sekupheleni kwenkulungwane ye-19, ukubandakanyeka kweekhanki kwiintlawulo, ngaphandle kokuba ngabapapashi bemali yephepha exhaswa yigolide, kwakuphantsi kakhulu kuba uninzi lweentengiselwano lwalwenziwa ngemali. Phakathi kwenkulungwane, amacheki ephepha axhaswa ziibhanki aqala ukuvela kwaye aba yeyona ndlela iphambili yokutshintshiselana eUnited States kwiintengiselwano ezinkulu endaweni yemali. Ukuvela kwe-telegraph kwenza ukuba iWestern Union ibe ngowokuqala ukusungula inkonzo yokuthumela imali ngo-1871. Kwimbali, indima ephambili yeebhanki yayikukugcina imali ngokubonelela ngee-akhawunti zebhanki, kunye nokubonelela ngetyala kubantu nakumashishini.

Nangona kunjalo, kude kube kufika i-stable coins kwi-blockchain ledgers, inkqubo yeebhanki yayisengaphantsi kwazo zonke izisombululo zokuhlawula. USDt, eyaziwa njengeTether, ngoku ivumela ukuba inkqubo yeebhanki isuswe phakathi kwiintlawulo ezibhalwe kwaye zixabiswe yimarike njenge-US Dollars. Ukususwa kweebhanki phakathi kwentlawulo yinxalenye yetekhnoloji enokutshintsha iibhanki kunye nentlawulo. Olunye utshintsho olukhulu kwindima yeebhanki lunokukhuthazwa kukuvela nokukhula kokutshintshiselana ngexabiso okubhalwe kweyona mali yahlukileyo ngokupheleleyo - bitcoin.

Injongo yale sahluko kukujonga ukuba ukukhula kweBitcoin kungathetha ntoni kumashishini eebhanki kunye neenkonzo zokuhlawula. Kwakhona ijolise ekuboneleleni ngesiseko seebhanki kunye neenkampani zeenkonzo zokuhlawula ukuze zikwazi ukuqaphela izoyikiso namathuba anokuziswa le teknoloji yokutshintshiselana ngexabiso engathathi cala, engenamvume kwaye yehlabathi lonke.



#### 4.3.1 Umngcipheko

> Ezona zizathu zibalulekileyo zotshintsho azifumaneki kwiimanifesto zopolitiko okanye kwizimemezelo zabaqoqeshi bezomnotho abathe basweleka, kodwa kwizinto ezifihlakeleyo ezitshintsha imida apho amandla esetyenziswa khona. Rhoqo, iinguqu ezincinane kwimozulu, kwindawo, kwiintsholongwane, nakwitekhnoloji zitshintsha indlela yokucinga ngobundlobongela.  
_James Dale Davidson_

Asixhalabeli apha ngemingcipheko eqhelekileyo yezemali okanye yokusebenza amashishini eebhanki, iinkonzo zokuhlawula kunye nezinye iinkampani ekufuneka zihlale ziqaphela kwaye zilawule. Kule ndima sijonga imingcipheko yesicwangciso seshishini enokuphuma kwaye indlela ukukhula kweBitcoin kunokukhokelela kuguquko olukhulu kwishishini kube kanye kwinkulungwane. Ukuba besiza kulinganisa amashishini ngokwethuba kunye nefuthe ngokubhekiselele ekuchaphazelekeni kukukhula kwale teknoloji, iibhanki kunye nentlawulo zingabekwa phezulu kuluhlu.

Imbali igcwele imizekelo yetekhnoloji entsha etshintsha indlela uluntu lwabantu olukhula ngayo nolulawulwa ngayo. Incwadi ethi The Sovereign Individual, ebhalwe ngo-1997 nguJames Dale Davidson noWilliam Rees-Mogg, iphonononga indlela utshintsho lwetekhnoloji olukhokelele kutshintsho eNtshona ukusuka kwihlabathi elalilawulwa yiCawa ukuya kwilizwe leentshaba esihlala kulo namhlanje. Bakhomba izinto eziphambili zitekhnoloji njengokuvela kwephephandaba kunye nokusetyenziswa kwepowuda yezibhamu njengamandla obundlobongela atshintsha indlela yokufumana inzuzo ngobundlobongela kwinqanaba elikhulu.

Eyona nto ibalulekileyo kukuba akukho bungqina bokuba iCawa okanye uluntu ngokubanzi lwalufuna okanye lwalukhokele utshintsho olwenzeka. Xa sijonga emva, kubonakala ngathi amandla aphuma kulawulo lweCawa kulwazi ayehla njengoko uqoqosho olutsha lususa ilungelo lwayo lokupapasha izinto ezibhaliweyo.

Iphephandaba lehlisa iindleko zokuphinda kwenziwe ulwazi, ngaloo ndlela lonyusa ukuhambelana kokwenziwa kwezinto ezibhaliweyo.

Imbali ibonisa ukuba itekhnoloji ekhokelele kutshintsho olukhulu ayizange iqale kwinkqubo karhulumente, kumaziko, kubalawuli okanye ebantwini ngokusebenzisa iinkqubo zedemokhrasi. Rhoqo sibona abantu namaziko bezama ukumelana, ukuvala indlela nokulibazisa ukwamkelwa kwayo. Kwinkulungwane yama-20 oku sikubona kwiimpendulo zokuqala ekusetyenzisweni kweemoto, umbane, i-cryptography, i-imeyile kunye ne-intanethi.

Ukwamkelwa kweetekhnoloji ezintsha ziimarike kwakhokelela ekutshintsheni indlela abantu abahlala ngayo, indlela abasebenza ngayo kwaye ngamanye amaxesha kwakutshintsha nesakhiwo sento abayithathela ingqalelo njengenkcubeko yabo, ilizwe labo okanye umbutho wabo wokukhokela. Kwiimeko ezininzi kwatshintsha ubungakanani kunye nolwakhiwo lwaloo mbutho ngokwawo. Eminye imizekelo yetekhnoloji etshintsha uluntu ngokubanzi ibandakanya ukuvela kokulima, umbane, iimoto kunye ne-intanethi.

Obu bungqina buchazwa ukuxhasa isiphelo sokuba itekhnoloji kufuneka ibe ngaphezulu komthetho. Umthetho unokulandela emva koko, kodwa indlela yokuhamba kweziganeko ibonisa ukuba imithetho ayinakuyeka okanye itshintshe utshintsho ngokwenene nangokuzinzileyo, kwaye ayinakuyibangela. Utshintsho lwemithetho luyimpembelelo yetekhnoloji entsha, hayi imbangela, kwaye izinto ezintsha zitekhnoloji azinakuvotelwa, zixelwe ngukumkani okanye zithintelwe ngaphandle kokuzilimaza.

Umthetho awukwazanga ukuthintela ukuqhubela phambili kweetekhnoloji ezininzi ezathi ngexesha lokuqala kwazo uluntu lwalufuna ukuzithintela. Xa oku kufezekisiwe, kuhlala kunciphisa ubutyebi belizwe elo ixesha eliphakathi ukuya kwelide.

#### 4.3.3 Ukususwa kwabalamli kwiintlawulo

> Ukuba uzama ukurhoxisa iqhwitha letyala kodwa [iibhanki] zibalulekile ngokupheleleyo kwiintlawulo kwaye ngenxa yoko kurhwebo... akukho sizathu sangaphambili... sokuba oko kufuneka [kube njalo]  
_Allen Farrington_

Uyilo lweBitcoin, zombini kwinqanaba layo elisezantsi leblockchain, ezinye iilayer zesibini ezivulekileyo, kunye ne-apps kwinqanaba eliphezulu, luvumela abantu ukuba bathengiselane ngexabiso ledijithali ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kubantu besithathu. Bekungekho nako ngaphambili ukuba kube neentlawulo zedijithali ezingenabalamli. Nangona kunjalo, ukuyila amandla okwenza iintlawulo phakathi kwabantu akuthethi ukuba zonke okanye uninzi lweentlawulo ziya kugqibela zisenjalo. Ubungakanani apho iintlawulo ziba phakathi kwabantu njengoko iBitcoin ikhula buya kuvela kwimakethi. Imakethi, ngokuyinxenye, yenziwa ngabo banikezela ngeenkonzo, ezinokubandakanya amaziko asebhankini kunye neenkonzo zokuhlawula ezikhoyo. Ukuba la maziko awathathi nxaxheba, imakethi iya kuvela nabadlali abatsha bekhula ukuze banikezele ngeenkonzo ezifuneka.

Amandla okuthengiselana ngemali ehlulwe-hluliweyo ngedijithali ngendlela ephakathi kwabantu aza kusebenza njengolawulo ngokuchasene nokusetyenziswa kwamandla okuba nomonopoly okanye oligopoly ekuzameni ukugxila imali kunye neentlawulo. Oku kuthetha ukuba ukuthatha inxaxheba kweekhanki ezikhoyo kunye neenkonzo zokuhlawula ukunikezela ngeenkonzo akubeki semngciphekweni umxholo weBitcoin iyonke.

Ukusukela oko iindawo zokutshintshiselana ngeBitcoin zokuqala zavulwa, ziye zaba ngabagcini beemali kubaninimali abaninzi. Ngenxa yoko, naziphi na iintlawulo zeBitcoin ezenziwa kwezi ndawo zokutshintshiselana nokuba zisiya kwiwallet yomntu yokuzigcina okanye ngqo kumrhwebi ziye zaphakathi kwabalamli. Nangona kunjalo, xa sele ikwiwallet yokuzigcina, iintlawulo zinokwenziwa phakathi kwabantu kwaye abaninzi abanikazi beBitcoin bathengiselana ngale ndlela.

Uphuhliso lweLightning Network njengesisombululo sentlawulo esingabizi, esikhawulezayo nesigqityiweyo kwinqanaba lesibini lukhokelele kwiqela elitsha nelahlukeneyo labalamli beentlawulo. Banikezela ngokulula kwemali egcinwe kwiLightning enokufikelelwa ngee-apps zefowuni ukuze kwenziwe iintlawulo ezikhawulezayo nezilula ebusweni okanye kwi-intanethi ngeBitcoin. Kunokwenzeka ukuba umntu azisebenzise eyakhe i-node yeLightning yokuzigcina, kodwa leyo ifuna ulwazi lobuchwephesha olungaphezulu kunokugcina iBitcoin kwi-chain. Iiwallet zeLightning zilungele ngakumbi ukusetyenziswa kwemihla ngemihla kuneemali ezikwi-chain, kwaye zifana neakhawunti yangoku/ yokujonga ebhankini kuneakhawunti yokonga okanye utyalo-mali. Njengeeakhawunti zangoku, nangona izizathu zifanayo, ezi wallet zihlala zigcina kuphela imali encinci efunekayo kwixesha elifutshane. Abaninzi abanikazi beBitcoin babonakalise ukuba bakhululekile ukutshintsha ukuzigcina ngokulula xa besebenzisa iLightning kuba bathatha umngcipheko omncinci kuphela.

##### Ukunciphisa umngcipheko wenkqubo

Ukugqitywa kweentlawulo ezininzi zedijithali namhlanje, nokuba intlawulo ibonakala njani, kufuna iibhanki ukuba zisebenze ngaphakathi okanye nezinye iibhanki. Oku kungenxa yendlela inkqubo yezemali ekufuneka yakhiwe ngayo njengephiramidi enebhanki ephambili phezulu, phantsi kwayo iibhanki zilawula iintlawulo zedijithali ngemali apho ibhanki ephambili inoxanduva. Oku kuthetha ukuba xa kukho ingxaki ebhankini, iziphumo zokuvumela ibhanki ukuba isilele zibeka umngcipheko kwinkqubo yeentlawulo.

**Ukwenziwa kwentlawulo ngekhadi letyala, ngexesha lokuthengisa:**

1. Umthengi ubonisa ikhadi ukuze ahlawule
1. Isicelo sentlawulo sithunyelwa kumphathi wentlawulo
1. Umphathi wentlawulo uthumela isicelo kumbutho wekhadi (Visa, Mastercard)
1. Umbutho wekhadi uthumela isicelo kwibhanki ekhuphe ikhadi yomthengi
1. Ibhanki ekhuphe ikhadi iyavuma okanye iyala intlawulo
1. Umbutho wekhadi uthumela impendulo kumphathi wentlawulo
1. Umphathi wentlawulo uthumela impendulo kwiterminal yomrhwebi evuma okanye iyala intlawulo

**Ukugqitywa kwentlawulo, ngexesha elizayo:**

1. Umphathi wentlawulo uyalela ibhanki yakhe ukuba yenze intlawulo (engenayo) kwibhanki yomrhwebi
1. Umthengi uyalela ibhanki yakhe ukuba yenze intlawulo (engenayo) kwibhanki ekhuphe ikhadi

Ukudityaniswa kwenkqubo yeebhanki kunye neentlawulo kudala umngcipheko ongafunekiyo ovela kwimfuno yezopolitiko yokusindisa iibhanki xa kukho ingxaki yetyala okanye kungenjalo uqoqosho lonke lungawa njengoko iinkqubo zentlawulo zisilela. Oku kudala umngcipheko wokuziphatha kakubi kwinkqubo yokudala ityala ngokwayo.

##### Ukwenziwa kwentlawulo ngeLightning Network

1. Iterminal yomrhwebi ibonisa i-QR code ye-invoyisi yeLightning
1. Umthengi uskena i-QR code ye-invoyisi kwi-wallet yakhe yeLightning kwifowuni kwaye uthumele imali
1. Uthengiselwano lugqitywa ngexesha langempela zi-node kwiLightning Network ezidlulisela iBitcoin ngothotho lwamanzi

**Ukugqitywa kwentlawulo kwenzeka ngexesha lokuthengisa**

* Umrhwebi ufumana imali kwangoko xa kuthelekiswa neentsuku okanye iiveki usebenzisa iindlela ezikhoyo
* Iibhanki azifuneki
* Akukho mngcipheko wetyala
* Akukho mngcipheko wenkqubo

Ngokuvumela ukwahlulwa kwenyani phakathi komsebenzi wemali kunye nenkqubo yeentlawulo zedijithali, iBitcoin inika ithuba lokunciphisa kakhulu, ukuba kungabi kukususa ngokupheleleyo, uxhomekeko phakathi kweentlawulo zedijithali kunye neebhanki, okwenza inkqubo yezemali iyonke yomelele ngakumbi. Oku kungathetha ukuba uluntu alusayi kuphinda lujongane nengxaki yokufuneka ukwabelana ngokulahleka okwenziwe kwicandelo leebhanki okanye ukujongana nokuphuka kwenkqubo yeentlawulo zoqoqosho. Akukho qela linoxanduva lweBitcoin, nangona abantu abaninzi, iinkampani kunye nemibutho engenzi nzuzo benegalelo ekukhuleni nasekuphuhliseni kwayo. Ixabiso leBitcoin aliveli kwizakhiwo zamaziko, kodwa livela kwimakethi.



#### 4.3.4 Imingcipheko

##### Iintlawulo

Kukho inzuzo ecacileyo kuluntu nakuzinzisweni koqoqosho ekubeni sikwazi ukwahlula iinkqubo zeentlawulo kwinkqubo yeebhanki ngokubanzi. Ukuba iibhanki bezingasayi kuba seziko kwinkqubo yeentlawulo yehlabathi, imfuno yokusindisa ezo ziba zityala ngenxa yokudala ityala elibi ingancitshiswa kakhulu. Ukuze baphumelele, iibhanki zinokuthi zibe noxanduva ngakumbi kwimisebenzi yazo yokubolekisa njengoko inkxaso ekhoyo ngoku ingancitshiswa kakhulu okanye isuswe.

Ayisiyongxaki kuphela kwingeniso ethile iibhanki eziyifumana kwiintlawulo, kodwa inefuthe elibanzi kwishishini lazo lokonga kunye nemali-mboleko.

Kusenokwenzeka ukuba kuya kubakho iqela elahlukeneyo labanikezeli beentlawulo, kubandakanya abathengi abathile abaya kuba ngabanikezeli babo ngokusebenzisa iinkonzo zeBitcoin Lightning zabantu okanye zoluntu. Ngokungena kwabangenayo abaninzi, iibhanki kunye nabanikezeli beenkonzo zokuhlawula ezikhoyo kuya kufuneka baphuhlise iinkonzo ezinomtsalane ukuze bagcine ingeniso yabo.

##### Ugcino kunye nemali-mboleko

Abathengi abasenyanzelekanga ukuba bagcine ixabiso kwiibhanki ukuze bakwazi ukuyisebenzisa kwiintlawulo. Oku asikokosongela kuphela indawo yeebhanki 'ezinkulu kakhulu ukuba zingawa' njengoko kuchaziwe ngasentla kwi-3.4.1, kodwa kunokuchaphazela namandla azo okudala ityala ngendlela yemali-mboleko.

Ukuncipha kobungakanani bamatyala iibhanki ezinokuwadala kunokunciphisa kakhulu ingeniso kwiibhanki zabathengi nakwiibhanki zehlabathi.



#### 4.3.5 Amathuba

Nangona iBitcoin yenza ukuba ugcino kunye nentlawulo engenamlamli zibe nokwenzeka, abanye abantu banokubona ixabiso ekuthengiseni umngcipheko womntu wesithathu ngenxa yokulula, nokuba ngokupheleleyo okanye ngokuyinxenye. Kukho ithuba elikhulu kwiibhanki nakwiinkonzo zentlawulo ukuba basebenzise ubukho babo kwimarike ukuze bavelise iimveliso eziphumelelayo ezitsala abathengi abanjalo.

##### Intlawulo

Indawo yentlawulo yeBitcoin ikhula ngokukhawuleza ukusuka kwicala labathengisi nakwicala labathengi. Kukho amathuba amakhulu kwiibhanki nakwiinkonzo zentlawulo ukuba bavelise izinto ezintsha kumaqonga abo akhoyo ukuze bazise izisombululo zentlawulo ze-bitcoin ngendlela engenazithintelo. Kuyimfuneko ukuba oku kuya kwenzeka ekugqibeleni, kodwa umngcipheko wokulibazisa oku uvula amathuba okuba isabelo semarike esizayo siye kubadlali abatsha abasesemva sele beqhuba kwaye besakha izisombululo ezinjalo. Ezinye zezisombululo zivulelekile (open source), ezinye zizezabucala, kwaye ezinye ziphakathi apho ikhowudi ivuliwe, kodwa abasebenzisi banokukhetha ukuyisebenzisa njengesiseko sezisombululo zabo zabucala.

Ezi zisombululo zinokuthi zibe nemida ngokweendawo okanye zifumaneke kwihlabathi lonke. Zikwabandakanya iindleko eziphantsi zentlawulo kunye nexesha elikhawulezayo lokugqitywa kwentlawulo - rhoqo ngaphantsi komzuzwana. Zisusa abalamli kwaye zigqitha kwiibhanki eziphakathi nezamazwe ukuze zinikeze iinkonzo zentlawulo ezingcono nezisebenzayo kubathengi nabathengisi.

Iintlawulo ezincinci kakhulu kunye neentlawulo ezihamba ngokuqhubekayo azinakwenziwa ngokwezoqoqosho kusetyenziswa iinkqubo zemali zangoku. Imigangatho ephezulu yeBitcoin efana neLightning Network inokuzisa ezi zakhono ngexabiso elifanelekileyo. Kukho iindawo ezininzi apho uphuhliso lwezisombululo ezinjalo lunokuthi lube nefuthe elikhulu elihle ekukhuleni. Enye yezo ndawo kukuhlawula ukufunda inqaku elinye leendaba okanye ukuhlawula ngokubukela imithombo yevidiyo. Oku kunokuphucula amava omthengi ngokunciphisa intengiso engafunekiyo kwaye kukhokelele ekukhuleni kwengeniso ethe ngqo kwiinkampani zemithombo yeendaba. Kusenokwenzeka ukuba zininzi ezinye iindawo apho uphuhliso lweentlawulo ezincinci luza kongeza ixabiso kwiimveliso nakwiimarike ezikhoyo kubathengi kunye nenzuzo kubaboneleli. Zisenokwenza ukuba kuphuhliswe iimveliso kunye neemarike ezintsha ngokupheleleyo.

Oonobuchule bokufakelwa kobukrelekrele (AI agents) abakwazi ukwenza okanye ukufumana iintlawulo kwinkqubo ekhoyo kuba abanazazisi zabantu okanye zomthetho. Kukho ithuba lokukhokela nokwakha iimveliso eziza kunceda oonobuchule be-AI ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele iintlawulo. Iibhanki ezikhoyo kunye nababoneleli beenkonzo zentlawulo banokuphuhlisa itekhnoloji enokuthi iphucule oku. Kuba le marike ayikabikho, ayisosongelo, kodwa inokuba lithuba eliphuthelweyo. IBitcoin ayifuni zazi zabantu ukuze isebenze, ngoko ke kusenokwenzeka ukuba oonobuchule be-AI baza kusebenzisa itekhnoloji eyakhelwe kwiBitcoin ukuze bafumane amandla okuthumela nokufumana iintlawulo ngexesha elizayo.

##### Imali ethunyelwa kwamanye amazwe kunye nokutshintshiselana ngemali

Kukho ithuba kwiibhanki nakwiinkonzo zentlawulo ukuba basebenzise izisombululo zeBitcoin kunye ne-stable coin ukuze benze iinkonzo zabo zibe lula kubathengi nakumashishini, banciphise iindleko, baphucule ukuthembeka kwaye banciphise ukulibaziseka kwentlawulo. Okukhawulezayo ukuba bayasebenza, kokukhona benokunciphisa 'ukulingana kwamathuba' okuziswa zezi teknoloji zintsha kwaye bagcine isabelo esihle semarike yabo yangoku. Ukuba abenzi njalo ngexesha, kunokukhokelela ekunciphiseni kwengeniso yabo kwezi marike.

##### Ugcino

Iibhanki zinethuba lokuphuhlisa nokubonelela ngeeakhawunti ezintsha ezinokuthi zisuke kwelula ziye kweluntsonkothileyo ngokuxhomekeke kwiingenelo zokugcina imali kubathengi. Kuya kubakho izinto abathengi abaya kuzithathela ingqalelo phakathi kokuzigcinela imali ngokwabo kunye nokulula, kwaye iibhanki zinokuphuhlisa iimveliso ezahlukeneyo ukwanelisa abathengi abohlukeneyo. Abathengi abaninzi banokonwaba ukuzigcinela i-bitcoin yabo ngokwabo ngelixa beneeakhawunti kwiibhanki kwezinye zeemali zabo.

Iintlobo zeeakhawunti zinokubonelela ngeengenelo ezahlukeneyo kumthengi nakumthengisi, nazi ezinye iimeko ezinokwenzeka:

* Ugcino olwabelwana ngalo ngemali yokuphatha, kodwa umboneleli akanalo ilungelo lokusebenzisa iiasethi zomthengi ekubolekiseni
* Iiakhawunti zogcino zasimahla apho umboneleli enelungelo lokusebenzisa imali ngokupheleleyo ekubolekiseni egciniweyo
* Iiakhawunti zogcino ezinengeniso kwi-bitcoin ukuba umboneleli ugunyazisiwe ukubolekisa ngokuchasene neereservi ezincinci okanye ukubonelela ngolwelo kwiimarike zorhwebo okanye kwiindlela zentlawulo.

##### Ityala

Kusenokubakho amathuba okwandisa iintlobo ezintsha zamatyala asekelwe kwi-bitcoin egcinwe njengesiqinisekiso. Nazi ezinye iimeko ezinokucetyiswa:

* Ityala lemali eqhelekileyo (umz. R) elisekelwe kwi-bitcoin egcinwe ngokwabelwana okanye ubungqina bolawulo
* Ityala leBitcoin elifana nelo lingasentla
* Ityala leBitcoin elingenasiqinisekiso.
