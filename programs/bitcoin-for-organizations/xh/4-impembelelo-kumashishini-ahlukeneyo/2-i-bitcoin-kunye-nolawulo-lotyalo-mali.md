# 4.2 I-Bitcoin kunye noLawulo loTyalo-mali

#### 4.2.0 Umngeni woLawulo-mali lwamaTyalo-mali

I-Bitcoin ayisiyonto intsha kuphela kwicandelo lotyalo-mali lwezikhungo, inezinto ezizodwa ezenza kube nzima kwiimali zemveli ukuba ziyichaze kwaye ziyihlalutye. I-Bitcoin ineminyaka eli-15 kuphela kwaye, uninzi lwelo xesha, ibiyinto encinci kwaye ingekho lula ukuthengiswa, ilawulwa ngabatyali-mali ngabanye. Ukuthatha inxaxheba kwimarike ye-bitcoin ngabatyali-mali bezikhungo, ukuza kuthi ga ngoku, bekulinganiselwe. Iminyaka emininzi, ukuswela imigaqo-nkqubo ecacileyo yezomthetho kunye nolawulo bekuyingxaki ecacileyo kwaye mhlawumbi ibe sisizathu esilula sokungangeni.

Ukwaziswa kweemarike ze-futures kwi-bitcoin malunga nokuphela kuka-2017 kwaba linyathelo lokuqala ekwenzeni le asethi yamkelwe ngabatyali-mali abaphantsi kolawulo. Nangona kunjalo, yayikukuvunywa kolawulo kwee-ETF zeBitcoin, ngoJanuwari 2024, okokugqibela okubonise kwicandelo lolawulo-mali lobuchwephesha ukuba i-bitcoin ngoku iyiklasi yeasethi esekiweyo efuna ingqwalasela.

Ngoku i-bitcoin isiba yiklasi yeasethi eqwalaselwa ngakumbi, umngeni ophambili kubatyali-mali bezikhungo kukuba bahlalutye kwaye baphuhlise isicwangciso sotyalo-mali esijikeleze yona. Abalawuli-mali baphantsi koxinzelelo olukhulayo oluvela kubathengi ukuba banganiki nje uluvo, kodwa banike isakhelo esithembekileyo kunye neemveliso ezivumela abathengi ukuba bathathe inxaxheba kule klasi yeasethi intsha. Kungenjalo, basengozini yokuphulukana neshishini elikhulu kubakhuphisana nabo abanendlela evulekileyo ngakumbi yokwakha ubutyebi.

Kude kube kutshanje, bekunzima kubalawuli-mali ukuba baxhase ukuchitha ixesha befundisisa i-bitcoin, ingakumbi ukwahlula-mali kuyo. Nangona kunjalo, njengoko umdla wabathengi ukhula, sinokufikelela kwinqanaba apho abalawuli-mali abangenaso isicwangciso sotyalo-mali lwe-bitcoin kufuneka banike isizathu esivakalayo.

Le module ijonga ezinye iindlela abalawuli-mali bemveli abanokuthi bacinge ngayo i-bitcoin njengeasethi, amathuba ayo, imilinganiselo yoxabiso kunye nefuthe kwiiphothifoliyo. Ayijoliswanga ekubeni ngumthetho ongaguqukiyo - uluntu lolawulo-mali luthe gabalala ngokubanzi nangendlela, kwaye umphathi ngamnye uya kuba nesakhelo sakhe sotyalo-mali kunye nenkqubo.



#### 4.2.1 Ukuqala Ukuqonda i-bitcoin

Abatyali-mali bezikhungo banokwahlulwa babe ziiqela ezimbini. Iqela lokuqala liquka abatyali-mali abacebileyo abanokuthatha inxaxheba ngeyona ndlela yokutyala-mali okanye i-ofisi yosapho. Eli qela liquka nabatyali-mali abakhethekileyo okanye ii-hedge funds ezikwaziyo ukusebenzisa imali ngokukhawuleza. Banokuba nabantu abambalwa abanomdla, nto leyo eyenza bangabi nemida kwaye bakhawuleze xa kufikwa kwizigqibo zotyalo-mali. Banokuba nemida encinci ngokwemigaqo-nkqubo kwaye babe nomngcipheko ophezulu. Eli qela sele likhulu amathuba okuba sele linothile kwi-bitcoin.

Iqela lesibini linokuthathwa njengecandelo lotyalo-mali lwemveli. Liquka abacebisi botyalo-mali abaphantsi kolawulo, abalawuli-mali abakhulu, iibhanki, abaphathi beemali ezidibeneyo, iipenshini, iimali zokuxhasa kunye neefandesi zelizwe. Lihamba kancinci kakhulu kwaye linomngcipheko ophantsi.

Eli qela likwangqongqo kakhulu. Amalungu alo aya kuba neenkqubo ezinzulu nezicwangcisiweyo zotyalo-mali. Abalawuli-mali banokubanjwa yimigaqo-nkqubo eneenkcukacha echaza iiasethi okanye iimarike ezikhoyo. Izigqibo zokwahlula-mali zihlala zenziwa ziikomiti ezakhiwe ngabalawuli bephothifoliyo, abahlalutyi kunye nabasebenzi bokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo apho izimvo zihlolisiswa nzulu kwaye zibhaliwe ngokwemigangatho engqongqo, phantsi kweliso elibukhali lomlawuli-mali.

Kulula ukuva uvelwano neli qela kunye nemingeni elijongene nayo xa kufikwa kwi-bitcoin. Ngokwendalo eli qela linomgaqo wokugcina izinto zikhuselekile, alithandi utshintsho kwaye, ngokubanzi, le ndlela ibisebenza kakuhle. Kule meko, xa itekhnoloji entsha okanye ephazamisa kakhulu iza, impendulo yendalo kukuba uqaphele, ungathembi okanye uyikhuphe ngokupheleleyo, ngakumbi xa uninzi lweshishini lwenza okufanayo. Ke ngoko, kule meko ingqongqo, akumangalisi ukuba eli qela lesibini lihambe kancinci xa lifaka imali kwi-asethi entsha njenge-bitcoin.

Abafundi beenkcukacha ezininzi zemithombo yeendaba zemali zemveli malunga nenethiwekhi yeBitcoin kunye neprotocol baye baqaphela ukuba abaninzi abachaza iziganeko bayasokola ukubona ukuba ingena phi kwinkqubo yemali yakudala. Oku kuyacaca. I-Bitcoin yavela ngaphandle kwenkqubo yemali ekhoyo. Kwaye, njengetekhnoloji ephinda icinge ngemali ngokwayo, ukwamkelwa kwayo kunokwenza uninzi lwezemali zemveli lungasasebenzi. Ewe kunjalo, oku kufana nendlela yetekhnoloji ephazamisa kwamanye amashishini.

Nangona kunjalo, imfuno ekhulayo yokufumana i-bitcoin evela kubathengi inyanzelisa abalawuli-mali ukuba bacinge ngokufaka i-bitcoin kwiiphothifoliyo okanye baphulukane nabathengi. Le mfuno ibiyinto ephambili ekwazisweni kwee-ETF zeBitcoin ngabaphathi be-asethi abakhulu abafana neBlackRock, Fidelity nabanye. Oku kunike abalawuli-mali imveliso evunyiweyo ngumlawuli, evumela ukuba banelise imfuno yabatyali-mali yokufumana i-bitcoin ngendlela ehambelana nemigaqo-nkqubo ekhohlo.

Emva kweminyaka eli-15 yokugqunywa kakubi kwimithombo yeendaba ephambili kunye neyoshishino, iBitcoin isaqhubeka nokuphikisa uqikelelo lwabaninzi abangathembe. Inethiwekhi iyaqhubeka nokukhula kwaye imilinganiselo efana nenani leedilesi zikawonke-wonke, i-hash rate yenethiwekhi kunye nexabiso leedola elithengiselwana, konke kubonisa itekhnoloji eqhubeka ikhula ngokukhawuleza.

Ngoko ke, kuya kusiba nzima kubalawuli-mali ukuba baxhase ukungabinayo i-bitcoin kwiiphothifoliyo zabo.



#### 4.2.2 IBitcoin: Ingaba Iyindlela Yamkelekileyo yeAsethi?

Kuxhomekeke kumyalelo wabo (umzekelo, iasethi enye, isicwangciso esinye okanye iifandesi ezininzi ezilinganisiweyo ngomngcipheko njl.), abalawuli-mali abakhulu bemveli baya kuba nemigangatho kunye nesakhelo esicwangcisiweyo sokuhlalutya utyalo-mali olunokubakho. Ezi nkqubo zingqongqo kwaye zihlolisiswa, njengoko kufanelekile. Ke ngoko, kunokuba ngumngeni kumphathi-mali ukubona ukuba ukugcina i-bitcoin kwi-phothifoliyo kungangena njani kwinkqubo ekhohlo, kuba ayinakuxabiswa kusetyenziswa imilinganiselo yemveli esetyenziswa kwezinye iiklasi zeasethi. Ke ngoko, inxalenye yohlalutyo lwe-bitcoin njengotyalo-mali kufuneka ihambe kunye nokuphononongwa kweenkqubo zotyalo-mali ezikhoyo.

Ukusuka kwindawo yezifundo, injongo yolawulo-mali olusebenzayo kukuphuhlisa iiphothifoliyo ezigqwesileyo 'kumda osebenzayo'. Oko kukuthi, iiphothifoliyo ezinikezela ngembuyekezo ephezulu elindelekileyo kwinqanaba lomngcipheko ochaziweyo, okanye umlinganiselo ophantsi womngcipheko kwimbuyekezo elindelekileyo echaziweyo. Umbuzo ophambili kubalawuli-mali kukuba ingaba ukwahlula-mali kwi-bitcoin kunokunceda ukufikelela kule njongo.

Izifundo zibonisa ukuba uhlalutyo oluphumelelayo lomngcipheko xa kuthelekiswa nembuyekezo kufuneka lubeke iphothifoliyo kufutshane okanye kumda osebenzayo. Umlinganiselo oqhelekileyo osetyenziselwa oku yiSharpe Ratio. Yahlula imbuyekezo egqithisileyo yephothifoliyo ngomlinganiselo wokungazinzi kwephothifoliyo, njengokuphambuka okuqhelekileyo kwembuyekezo egqithisileyo. ISharpe ephezulu ingcono xa kuthelekiswa iiphothifoliyo.

Kufanelekile ukuqaphela ukuba iiphothifoliyo ezikumda osebenzayo zihlala zibonisa inqanaba eliphezulu lokwahluka. Oko kukuthi, ziqulathe uluhlu olubanzi lweasethi ezingenalo unxulumano olusondeleyo kwindlela amaxabiso azo ahamba ngayo. Iiasethi ezizezinye azihlali zinxulumene kakhulu neeklasi zeasethi eziphambili (izitokhwe kunye neebhondi) kwaye ke zinokuba lithuba elinomtsalane kubalawuli-mali abafuna ukwandisa ukwahluka kwi-phothifoliyo. 'Ezizezinye', ezifana ne-equity yabucala, izindlu, amasimi, okanye nemisebenzi yobugcisa kunye nezinto eziqokelelwayo zihlala zikwiluhlu lokujongwa ngabaphathi bephothifoliyo bezikhungo njengendlela yokwahlula, ngakumbi ngenxa yokungasebenzi kakuhle kwemarike yeebhondi kwiminyaka yakutshanje. Abalawuli bephothifoliyo eqhelekileyo '60/40' (60% izitokhwe, 40% iibhondi) baphantsi koxinzelelo lokuphucula ukusebenza kotyalo-mali ngokubanzi.

Nangona kunjalo, iiasethi ezizezinye zihlala ziza nomqobo omkhulu. Zinokuhlala kwiimarike ezingenalo ulwelo olunzulu olufumaneka kwiimarike ezinkulu zezabelo okanye iibhondi. Kunzima ukufumana abathengi okanye abathengisi abanevolumu eyaneleyo. Nokuba ukuxabisa iasethi ngeenjongo zephothifoliyo kunokuba ngumngeni kuba 'ukuyiphawula kwimarike' kunokungathembeki kwimarike engenalo ulwelo.

I-Bitcoin yiasethi ezizezinye engenalo olu ngxaki. Yimarike exabisa ngaphezulu kwe-$1 trillion kwaye ithengiswa iiyure ezingama-24 ngosuku, iintsuku ezisi-7 ngeveki kwihlabathi liphela. Ivolumu ethengiswayo ibalwa kwiishumi zeebhiliyoni zeedola ngosuku. Kwakhona, ngokungafaniyo nezinye iiasethi ezizezinye, i-bitcoin ifana, iyahlolwa ngokuzimeleyo, yahlulwe lula kwaye ayinangxaki yomntu wesithathu, ukuba igcinwe ngqo.



#### 4.2.3 Ukuhlalutya kunye noXabiso lweBitcoin

Abalawuli-mali abaqeqeshiweyo sele benenkqubo engqongqo yokuhlalutya utyalo-mali olunokubakho. Ngo-2017, umhlalutyi wenkqubo yoshishino uBrian Leemoon [waphuhlisa isakhelo](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/321831572_Bitcoin_Valuation_Framework_Pub) sokuvavanya itekhnoloji yeBitcoin kusetyenziswa iindlela ezintathu ezaziwayo zohlalutyo lweshishini: SWOT, PESTLE kunye nePorter's Five Forces. Ukusuka kwindawo yomphathi-mali, umzekelo omfutshane we-SWOT analysis yeBitcoin unokubonakala ngolu hlobo:

##### Amandla

* Eyona digital asset yaziwayo, inethiwekhi/protocol yokuhlawula evulekileyo enembali ende
* Iprotocol ezinzileyo kakhulu engenazo iinguqu ezinkulu kwinkqubo yemali
* Inethiwekhi ezinzileyo kakhulu ene-99.99% uptime ukusukela oko yaqalwa
* Eyona nethiwekhi ye-digital asset ikhuselekileyo kwaye isasazeke kakhulu
* Eyona marike ye-digital asset ixabisekileyo kwaye inolwelo oluninzi kunye nabasebenzisi abaninzi
* Inethiwekhi ye-digital asset ekhula ngokukhawuleza - inani leedilesi, i-hash rate, ixabiso elithengiselwana
* Ivumela ukuthatha inxaxheba kwezoqoqosho kumazwe asakhulayo
* Ubudlelwane obuhle kunye namandla ahlaziyekayo kunye nezinye izibonelelo ze-ESG
* Iqala ukwamkelwa njengeasethi ye-treasury kwiinkampani, iqinisa iibhilanxi
* Imeko yolawulo iyaphucuka. Umzekelo, ukuvunywa kwee-ETF e-US

##### Ubuthathaka

* Ixabiso le-asethi eliguquguqukayo kakhulu kunye nokwehla okukhulu kwexabiso
* Iindleko zokuthengiselana kwi-chain ziyahluka kakhulu
* Ukuswela imfundo yemarike malunga nokusebenza kunye neempawu
* Kunzima ukuhlalutya ixabiso ngabatyali-mali
* Ukuswela imigaqo-nkqubo ecacileyo yolawulo kwiimarike eziphambili
* Imibono emibi yemarike kunye nokusabela okukhulu ekusetyenzisweni okungekho mthethweni okanye okungekho semthethweni
* Umngcipheko womsebenzi/igama elihle onxulumene nokuxhasa iBitcoin kwicandelo lezemali zemveli
* Inokungabi nolwelo ukuba iimvakalelo zabatyali-mali zijika ngokuchasene neasethi
* Abagcini beasethi bomntu wesithathu basengozini yokuhlaselwa kwe-cyber okanye ukubiwa

##### Amathuba

* Iba sisixhobo esaziwayo sokugcina ixabiso kunye neasethi ye-treasury yehlabathi
* Iyaqatshelwa ngakumbi njengendlela yokuhlawula iimpahla kunye neenkonzo
* Impumelelo yeemigaqo yesicelo somaleko iya kutsala abasebenzisi abaninzi kakhulu
* Ukukhula kwabasebenzisi ngesantya esifana nese-intanethi phakathi kwiminyaka yoo-1990
* Amathuba okuphucula imbuyekezo kutyalo-mali ngokwahlula-hlula (ukungahambelani)
* Iimeko zemarike zilungele imfuno - ukwehla kwamandla emali ye-fiat kulindeleke ukuba kuqhubeke
* Amathuba okuba ngowokuqala kutyalo-mali kubaphathi kuthelekiswa nabakhuphisana nabo

##### Iingozi

* Ukonyuka kwexabiso elikhulu okubangela umngcipheko wokwehla okukhulu kwexabiso
* Imithetho kwiimarike eziphambili ijika ichasene netekhnoloji
* Inethiwekhi ifumana ingxaki yobugcisa, echaphazela indlela abajonga ngayo ixabiso
* ‘Uhlaziyo’ lwenethiwekhi lubangela iingxaki zobugcisa ezingalindelekanga
* Igwetywa yenye i-asethi yedijithali enenkxaso enkulu kubasebenzisi okanye kwimithetho

Olu alululuhlu olupheleleyo kwaye kufuneka silindele ukuba abatyalomali babe neembono ezahlukeneyo ngamandla kunye nobuthathaka njl. Kodwa, lo msebenzi ukhuthaza abahlalutyi ukuba baqonde ngakumbi inethiwekhi yeBitcoin, umgaqo-nkqubo kunye ne-asethi kwaye bacinge ukuba yintoni eyenza ukuba kufanelekile kutyalo-mali. Kubalulekile kwakhona ukuphinda uphonononge uhlalutyo rhoqo kuba izinto eziphambili kwinkqubo ziyaqhubeka ziphuhliswa.

Uhlalutyo lwe-SWOT lukhuthaza abahlalutyi ukuba bathelekise nenkqubo yemali ye-fiat. Ukungamkelwa ngokubanzi kweengxaki ezinxulumene nemali ye-fiat kunokuba ngumqobo ekuqondeni iBitcoin. Iminyaka emininzi, bekungekho ngxoxo ingako ngezithintelo zemali ye-fiat phakathi kwabatyalomali okanye kwiindawo zophando, ikakhulu kuba kuyamkelwa ukuba akukho enye indlela ethembekileyo ukusukela oko kugqityiwe umgangatho wegolide, kwavela inkqubo yemali ye-fiat ezimeleyo. Oko kwatshintsha xa kwavela iBitcoin.

I-Bitcoin inezinto ezintsha nezizodwa. Ngenxa yoku, kube nengxoxo eninzi malunga nendlela yokuyixabisa njengotyalo-mali. Ingxelo eqhelekileyo kukuba ayinazinzuzo zemali ezifana nezitokhwe, amatyala okanye izindlu nomhlaba, ngoko ke, 'ayifanelekanga kutyalo-mali'. Kodwa oku kungabonisa ukungaguquguquki kwenkqubo yokutyala-mali efuna uhlaziyo ukuze ilungele iingcinga ezintsha. Kunceda abanye abatyalomali ukuba bacinge ngebitcoin (i-asethi) njenge 'igolide yedijithali' enezinto ezingcono kune golide yenyani. IGolide nayo ayinazinzuzo zemali kwaye inembali ende isetyenziswa njengemali. Ukwenza uthelekiso lweempawu zemali zegolide, i-fiat kunye nebitcoin kunokuba luncedo, njengoko kuchazwe kwinqaku lika-2018 'The Bullish Case for Bitcoin' likaVijay Boyapati.

Xa singenazo iindlela zemveli zokuxabisa, kufuneka siqwalasele njani ixabiso elinokubakho lebitcoin kwixesha elizayo?

Umhlalutyi uJesse Myers4 e-OnRamp ucebisa ukuba kuba i-bitcoin ingacingelwa njengemali entsha esisiseko enokuphinda ixabise zonke iintlobo zee-asethi, ke iMarike ebanzi (TAM) ye-bitcoin yiyo yonke ubutyebi behlabathi (malunga ne-R17Q), oko kukuthi, yonke ixabiso eligcinwe kwizitokhwe, amatyala, izindlu nomhlaba, iimarike zemali njl.

Kodwa ke, ukulindela ukuba iBitcoin ithathe uninzi okanye inxenye enkulu yobutyebi behlabathi akulindelekanga ukuba kwenzeke kungekudala. Le yimbali yeminyaka emininzi. Ngoko, okwangoku, singalandelela njani ukuba inethiwekhi yeBitcoin ihamba ngendlela efanelekileyo, ukuze le ngxelo yotyalo-mali ye-bitcoin (ibhale ngoonobumba abancinci xa kuthethwa nge-asethi) ihlale isemi?

KwiBitcoin network zininzi iindlela5 esinokuzilandelela ukubona umsebenzi kwinethiwekhi. Nantsi eminye imizekelo. Olu alululuhlu olupheleleyo kwaye inkcazelo epheleleyo nganye ingaphaya komda wale module.

* I-Hashrate yenethiwekhi (yonke amandla ekhompyutha adityanisiweyo asetyenziswa ukumba kunye nokucubungula iintengiselwano kwiBitcoin ledger yeProof-of-Work)
* Ixabiso le-randi elithengiselwe kwinethiwekhi
* Inani le-bitcoin ezikwi-exchange (umlinganiso wokunqongophala kwe-bitcoin ekhoyo yokurhweba)
* Inani labasebenzisi, iiwallet ezisebenzayo kwinethiwekhi (umaleko osisiseko & umaleko 2 njenge-Lightning). Kufanelekile ukuqaphela ukuba uqikelelo lwale nto lungahluka kakhulu, ngoko kubalulekile ukukhetha indlela efanayo ukuze ukwazi ukulandelela iindlela ngokuthembekileyo.
* Inani lama-node asebenzayo kwinethiwekhi
* I-HODL waves (ukwabiwa kwabanini be-bitcoin ngokusekelwe kwixesha abaye bagcina ngalo iinkozo zabo)
* Umkhwa weemveliso zemidiya ezinxulumene neBitcoin, ezifana neefilimu, iincwadi okanye amanqaku eendaba eziphambili.
* Umkhwa wenani labathengisi abamkela iintlawulo ngebitcoin kumazwe aphambili. Oku kungalandelelwa kwi-btcmap.org

Ukulandelela iqela leemetriki zenethiwekhi kunceda umphathi ukuba abone ukuba ingxelo yotyalo-mali isemi. Ukongeza, ukulandelela inkqubela phambili yezitokhwe ezithengiswa esidlangalaleni (ezifana nabavukuzi okanye iinkampani ezinika ukufikelela kwi-bitcoin) kunokunika idatha eyongezelelweyo. Ezi zitokhwe zinokuxabiswa kusetyenziswa iindlela zemveli abaphathi abaqeqeshiweyo sele beziqhelile, ezifana nemodeli yokuhla kwemali (discounted cash flow) okanye imilinganiselo efana ne-price/earnings, price/sales, price/earnings growth, price/free cash flow, okanye price/book value.



#### 4.2.4 Impembelelo yeBitcoin kwiipotifoliyo

Nangona ineminyaka eli-15 kuphela, ngo-Agasti 2025 i-bitcoin yayisele izinzile njengesixhobo sesixhenxe esixabiseke kakhulu emhlabeni. Ngelixa kubalulekile kubaphathi botyalo-mali ukuqonda ubume beBitcoin kunye nefuthe elinokubakho kuqoqosho nakuluntu, umbuzo ophambili kwicala lobuchwephesha kukuba yintoni impembelelo enokubakho kwiipotifoliyo.

Ukuba sikholelwa ukuba iBitcoin ingathatha inxenye enkulu yobutyebi behlabathi kwixesha elide, kubonakala kufanelekile ukuba sibe nesabelo kuyo njengendlela yokunyusa imbuyekezo. Kodwa, kubalulekile ukuqwalasela ukuba imbuyekezo iyafaneleka na umngcipheko. Oku kuboniswa yiSharpe Ratio ekhankanywe ngaphambili. Umphathi kufuneka aqwalasele nokwahlula-hlula. Oko kukuthi, indlela imbuyekezo yotyalo-mali lwe-asethi ehambelana ngayo nezinye ii-asethi kwipotifoliyo, ngelixa eqwalasela nemingcipheko enxulumene nokuguquguquka kwexabiso kunye nokwehla okukhulu.

Kukho ulwazi olufumanekayo esidlangalaleni malunga nefuthe lebitcoin kwiipotifoliyo kwiFidelity Investments okanye iNakamoto Portfolio evela kwiSwan Research. Kuphando oluhlaziywe ngoJuni 20248, iFidelity yenze uhlalutyo lwembuyekezo, ukuguquguquka kunye nokuhambelana kwebitcoin xa ithelekiswa nezitokhwe, amatyala kunye negolide ngaphakathi kwepotifoliyo ye-60/40 izitokhwe/amatyala. Kufunyenwe ukuba i-bitcoin inika inzuzo yokwahlula-hlula, nangona ukuhambelana kungahluka.

Umzobo ongezantsi ubonisa ukuhambelana okuqhubekayo kweminyaka emi-3 kwebitcoin xa ithelekiswa nezitokhwe kunye namatyala, ukusuka ngoJulayi 2013 ukuya kuMatshi 2024. Ubonisa ukuba, ngelixa ukuhambelana phakathi kwezitokhwe namatyala kunyuka. Ukuhambelana okuqhubekayo kweminyaka emi-3 nezitokhwe kwe-0.53 kunye namatyala kwe-0.26 ukuya kuMatshi 2024 kubonisa amathuba amakhulu okwahlula-hlula kwipotifoliyo enee-asethi ezininzi.

Ixabiso elipheleleyo lemarike ye-bitcoin alizange lidlule kwi-R1.9T de kwaba phakathi ku-2016. Ngoko ke, ukulinganisa ukuhambelana kwangethuba kunokuba kungathethi nto kakhulu kuba imarike ye-bitcoin yayingenabunzulu, ulwelo kunye nokusebenza kakuhle kwexabiso kwiminyaka yokuqala, ngakumbi phambi kukaJanuwari 2018 xa kwaqala ukuthengiswa iikontrakthi zokuqala ze-bitcoin futures.

Ukusuka kwicala lembuyekezo, iFidelity yafumanisa ukuba ukusukela ngo-Agasti 1, 2010 ukuya kuMatshi 31, 2024, i-bitcoin ifumene imbuyekezo yonyaka ye-178%, ngelixa iqaphela ukuba ukusukela oko kwaqalwa ukuthengiswa kwe-futures, imbuyekezo yonyaka imalunga ne-29.6%. Kunjalo, elidlulileyo alisiqinisekiso sekamva. Kodwa, kuyacaca ukuba i-bitcoin inamandla okuphucula kakhulu imbuyekezo yeepotifoliyo.

Ukuguquguquka kwexabiso leBitcoin kudla ngokukhankanywa njengesizathu sokuba abaphathi botyalo-mali bangayifaki iBitcoin kwiipotifoliyo zabo. Le yingxelo engaqhelekanga, kuba ulawulo lokuguquguquka kwexabiso lee-asethi ezithile kwipotifoliyo luphawu oluqhelekileyo kumphathi weepotifoliyo onobuchule. Ukongeza, iFidelity yafumanisa ukuba ukuguquguquka kwexabiso leBitcoin akwahlukanga kakhulu kwiqela 'magnificent seven' lezitokhwe zetekhnoloji ezisebenza kakuhle. Ekupheleni kuka-2023, bekukho izitokhwe ze-S&P ezingama-92 eziguquguqukayo ngakumbi kuneBitcoin.

I-Fidelity yafumanisa kwakhona ukuba ukuguquguquka kwe-bitcoin kuyehla ngokuhamba kwexesha. Oku kulindelekile njengoko imarike ikhula. Imarike enzulu kunye nolwelo olungakumbi luvumela ukungena kwemali okukhulu ngaphandle kokuchaphazela kakhulu ixabiso lemarike.

Ukuguquguquka okuphezulu kwexabiso, okulinganiswa ngokuphambuka okuqhelekileyo kwembuyekezo, kwaziwa kakuhle ngabatyali-mali njengempembelelo ye-asethi ezisebenza kakuhle. Njengoko kubonisiwe kwitheyibhile engezantsi, kwixesha ukusuka ngo-2020 ukuya ekuqaleni kuka-2024, abatyalomali bebitcoin bahlawulwe kakuhle ngokuguquguquka kwayo, bebonisa iSharpe ratio ye-0.96 xa ithelekiswa ne-0.65 ye-S&P 500. I-Sortino ratio inokuba luncedo ngakumbi, kuba ijonga kuphela ukuphambuka okuqhelekileyo kweembuyekezo ezisezantsi okanye 'ukuguquguquka okubi'. Ixabiso layo le-1.86 libonisa ukuba uninzi lokuguquguquka kule minyaka mine belusisiya phezulu. Oku kungenxa yokuba bekukho ukuhla okukhulu kwexabiso ngeli xesha.

Eyona nto ibalulekileyo kukuba i-bitcoin yimpahla enexabiso eliguquguqukayo kakhulu. Oku kulindelekile kuba iseyintsha kwaye iseluhambeni olude lokufumanisa ixabiso layo kunye nokwamkelwa ngabantu. Abatyali-mali abangengcali banamava okusingatha olu guquguquko, kwaye, ngenxa yamathuba okuba nenzuzo enkulu, bayakwazi ukulawula oku ngokufanelekileyo. Bangakwazi ukumisela isabelo esifanelekileyo, ngokuhambelana nesicwangciso sabo sokutyala imali kunye nomngcipheko abakulungeleyo ukuwuthwala. Kwaye, ukuze kuncitshiswe uguquguquko lwexesha elifutshane, ukugcina i-bitcoin ubuncinane kwisithuba esinye, somjikelo wokwahlula kabini weminyaka emi-4, kunokuba lixesha elincinci elifanelekileyo lokuyigcina.


> **Light – Ngaba iBitcoin yindlela yokuzikhusela kwiNtlupheko yeXabiso leMali?**
>
> Ngenxa yokuba i-bitcoin inenani eliqingqiweyo, abaxhasi bayo sele bexoxa ixesha elide ukuba isebenza njengesixhobo sokuzikhusela ekwehleni kwamandla okuthenga kwiimali ze-fiat. Kuyacaca ukuba, kwixesha elide kakhulu, i-bitcoin isebenze kakuhle kakhulu ekukhuseleni ukwehla kwamandla okuthenga kwiimali ze-fiat. Nangona kunjalo, abagxeki bakhomba kunyuko olukhulu kwinqanaba le-CPI minyaka le ukusukela ekuqaleni kuka-2021 ukuya kwinqanaba eliphezulu kwiminyaka engama-40 phakathi kuka-2022 kwaye babuza ukuba kutheni ixabiso le-bitcoin lingakhange lenyuke kakhulu ngeli xesha. Ngaba ke i-bitcoin ayiphumelelanga njengendlela yokuzikhusela kwi-ntlupeko ye-xabiso le-mali?
>
> Kubalulekile ukuqaphela ukuba sele sibonile kuphela olu nyuko lukhulu lwe-ntlupeko kube kanye kwimbali yonke ye-bitcoin, ngoko kufuneka silumke xa sithatha izigqibo. Nangona kunjalo, kuyaziwa ukuba i-CPI sisilinganiso esijonge emva. Oko kukuthi, siphendula kunyuko lwamaxabiso ezimpahla neenkonzo sele zenzekile. Ezo nyuko zamaxabiso zihlala zisisiphumo sokwanda kwemali ekhoyo kuqoqosho ngokubanzi.
>
> Ubuninzi bemali kuqoqosho lwehlabathi bonyuke kakhulu ngo-2020 ngenxa yemeko ye-COVID. Amazwe amaninzi amakhulu athatha isigqibo ekupheleni kwekota yokuqala ka-2020 sokuvala amacandelo amakhulu oqoqosho lwawo, kwaye, ukuze kugqunywe umsantsa kwingeniso yerhafu kunye nokuxhasa izigidi zabantu abangasebenziyo, benza izigidigidi zemali entsha ye-fiat.
>
> Ixabiso le-Bitcoin nalo lanyuka kakhulu ukusuka phakathi kuka-2020 ukuya ekupheleni kwekota yokuqala ka-2021, linyuka ngaphezulu kwamaxesha amathandathu. Ngoko ke, kubonakala ngathi ibingumqondiso wangaphambili wokunyuka kwamaxabiso ezimpahla kunye nenqanaba le-CPI. Ngoko ke, isebenze kakuhle njengexilongo elilumkisayo kunyuko olukhulu lwemali ekhoyo, ekhokelela kunyuko lwe-CPI kwixesha elizayo.




#### 4.2.5 Ukutshintsha Kwembali Yokubalisa

Ukusukela ekuqaleni kuka-2024, kubekho utshintsho olukhulu kwindlela abantu abathetha ngayo nge-bitcoin kwiimali zemveli. Imarike ingene ku-2023 isaxhathiswa kukuwa kwe-FTX. Ngenxa yoko, amacandelo amakhulu oshishino ayengayithathi i-bitcoin njengempahla enokutyalwa imali ngokwenene.

Ukusuka apho, emva konyaka omnye, indlela yokubalisa itshintshile kakhulu. Ukuvunywa kwe-SEC yase-US kweemveliso ze-Bitcoin ETF kubonisa utshintsho olukhulu kwindawo yolawulo. Le nkqubela ivula umnyango kubatyali-mali abangengcali, ibavumela ukuba bangene kweli candelo lempahla ngeemveliso ezilawulwayo, ezikhutshwe ngabalawuli beempahla abahlonitshwa kakhulu nabadala.

Emva nje kweenyanga ezilithoba zorhwebo, ii-Bitcoin ETF zathathwa njengezona zimpumelelo zinkulu kwezinye zonke ezake zakhutshwa. Emva kweekota ezimbini zorhwebo, sele zaphula iirekhodi zokutsala imali eninzi kunyaka wokuqala kunye nenani eliphezulu labatyali-mali bezoshishino.12 Emva kokuvunywa kwe-ETF kunye nempumelelo yazo kurhwebo, iinkampani ezinkulu zotyalo-mali (ezifana neBlackrock, Fidelity kunye neCantor Fitzgerald) zenza amagqabantshintshi amaninzi alungileyo malunga nekamva le-bitcoin. Oku kubeka uxinzelelo kubalawuli botyalo-mali abangekayenzi isicwangciso esicacileyo se-bitcoin.

Kuyanda ukuba kube nzima kubatyali-mali abangengcali ukungachazi ukuba kutheni bengafuni nokuba nencinci i-bitcoin, nokuba kukwizinga lokucinga. Ukongeza, utshintsho kwindlela yokubalisa luncede ekutyhileni ngakumbi inkqubo yemali ye-fiat, luvula ngakumbi ingxaki yayo ephambili - yokuba ixabiso layo lehla ngokuhamba kwexesha.

Ekugqibeleni, indima yeengcali zotyalo-mali kukulawula umngcipheko. Ngoko ke, kufanelekile ukucinga ukuba 'ukungabikho kwaphela' kwe-bitcoin kuthetha ntoni ngokwembono yolawulo lomngcipheko.

Ngaba kufana nokubheja, ngokuqinisekileyo nge-100%, ukuba inethiwekhi ye-Bitcoin kunye nomgaqo-nkqubo wayo iya kusilela? Ukuba kunjalo, ngaba olo lulawulo lomngcipheko olufanelekileyo?

Kwihlabathi lezezimali zemveli, abathandabuzi be-bitcoin basesele baninzi. Oku akumangalisi. Eli candelo ngokwendalo lithanda ukugcina izinto zikhuselekile, lisebenza kwindawo elawulwayo kakhulu. Kule meko, kwaye phakathi kwabantu abathanda ukufana, akukho nzuzo inkulu yokuba ngowokuqala ukwamkela itekhnoloji entsha nephazamisa izinto. Enyanisweni, kunokubakho umngcipheko omkhulu womsebenzi xa uzama ukukhokelela phambili.

Nangona kunjalo, njengamanye amacandelo afana ne-inshurensi okanye ubugcisa bokwakha apho ulawulo lomngcipheko lubalulekile, imfuneko yokusebenza kakuhle kufuneka idlule iminqweno yokuba 'uchanekile'. Ngoko ke, abalawuli botyalo-mali abathandabuza i-bitcoin kufuneka bacinge nzulu ngokuba nencinci i-bitcoin, ngenxa yomngcipheko wokungaphumeleli ngokupheleleyo ukuba i-bitcoin iqhubeka nokusebenza kakuhle kwixesha elide. Umngcipheko wokungaphumeleli ngenxa yokungabikho kwaphela kwe-bitcoin akufanele ungahoywa.



#### 4.2.6 Impembelelo yeBitcoin kwikamva loLawulo loTyalo-mali

Kufanelekile kwakhona ukucinga ukuba yintoni impembelelo yexesha elide yokunyuka kwe-bitcoin kwiipotifoliyo enokuba nayo kushishino lolawulo lotyalo-mali.

Ingxaki yemali ye-fiat yokugcina ixabiso ibe sesona sizathu siphambili sokukhula kweshishini lolawulo lotyalo-mali ukusukela ngeminyaka yoo-1970. Ukungakwazi kwemali ye-fiat ukugcina amandla okuthenga xa igcinwe kwi-akhawunti yokonga elula, kukhokelele ekwandeni kweemveliso zotyalo-mali, ezijolise kubathengi abafuna ukukhulisa okanye ukugcina amandla okuthenga.

Isiphumo esingekho ngqo sale meko kukuba iindidi eziphambili zeempahla - izitokhwe, amatyala kunye nezindlu, zitsale amaxabiso aphezulu ngenxa yokukwazi kwazo ukugcina ixabiso kwixesha elide. Ukongeza, njengoko ukwehla kwemali ye-fiat kukhawulezile, imali iye yaya kwezi mpahla ngakumbi, ekhokelela kutyalo-mali olungelolungileyo kunye namaxabiso aphazamisekileyo, anokubangela amaqamza eempahla kunye neziphumo ezingalunganga kubatyali-mali. Kuthiwa esi siphumo sikwabonakala nakwiimpahla ezizezinye ezifana nobugcisa, iimoto zakudala kunye nezinye izinto eziqokelelwayo.

Kwihlabathi elizayo apho i-bitcoin yamkelwa ngakumbi njengendawo egqwesileyo yokugcina ixabiso xa ithelekiswa nezinye iindidi zeempahla kwaye izinzisa isikhundla sayo njenge-asethi yokugcina imali yeenkampani, yintoni impembelelo enokuba nayo kumaxabiso kwezinye iindidi zeempahla?

Impembelelo yexesha elide inokuba nkulu: i-bitcoin inokuba itsale 'ixabiso lemali' kwezinye iindidi zeempahla, kancinci ekuqaleni, emva koko ngokukhawuleza njengoko iimpawu zayo ziqondwa ngakumbi. Kule meko, uninzi lweemveliso zotyalo-mali lungaphelelwa sisidingo, nto leyo enokubangela ukuba ishishini lolawulo lotyalo-mali linciphe ngobukhulu. Ngokuhamba kwexesha, isabelo salo kuqoqosho lukhulu sinokuncipha size sifane nesabelo esasinalo kwiminyaka engama-50 eyadlulayo.

Kodwa ke, abaninzi abachazi bezemali abakulindelanga ukuba le meko yenzeke kungekudala - ukwamkelwa kwe-bitcoin kwihlabathi liphela yibali leminyaka emininzi. Nangona kunjalo, iinkampani zolawulo lotyalo-mali ezilungiselelwe sele zinegalelo elikhulu kunabo bangalungiselelwanga. Ngoko ke, kubalulekile kuzo zonke iinkampani zolawulo lotyalo-mali ukuba zibe 'noNyuko lweBitcoin' kwiirejista zabo zomngcipheko. Kwaye, nabalawuli abathandabuza kakhulu kufuneka bazibuze:

Kuthekani ukuba, njengokunyuka kwe-intanethi, asikwazi ukukhetha ukuphuma nje?


> **Dark – Isilumkiso**
>
> Qaphela ukuba impumelelo edlulileyo ayisiqinisekiso sempumelelo kwixesha elizayo. I-Bitcoin yimpahla enomngcipheko ophezulu. Abatyali-mali kufuneka bakhumbule ukuba ixabiso layo linokunyuka kakhulu okanye lehle kakhulu kwixesha elizayo, njengoko kubonisiwe kwimbali yayo.
>
> I-Bitcoin ayikabi nembali ende njengemakethi yezitokhwe kunye namatyala, oko kuthetha ukuba kunzima ukuqikelela ngokuqinisekileyo ingeniso yayo. Qaphela ukuba i-bitcoin iseyimpahla entsha, kwaye impembelelo yayo kwiipotifoliyo ingatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha.
>
> Qaphela ukuba i-bitcoin iguquguquka kakhulu, kwaye inokuba semngciphekweni wokuphathwa kakubi yimakethi kunezokhuseleko. Imeko yolawulo lwexesha elizayo ye-bitcoin ayikacaci okwangoku.
