# 4.5 Iinkonzo zobungcali

#### 4.5.0 Intshayelelo

> Ndicinga ukuba i-Intanethi iza kuba yenye yezinto eziphambili eziza kunciphisa indima karhulumente. Into enye esele ingekho, kodwa ezakuphuhliswa kungekudala, yimali ethembekileyo ye-elektroniki, indlela apho kwi-Intanethi unokuthumela imali ukusuka ku-A ukuya ku-B ngaphandle kokuba u-A uyamazi u-B okanye u-B uyamazi u-A. Njengendlela endinokuthatha ngayo ibhili ye-R400, ndiyinike kuwe, kwaye akukho ngxelo yokuba ivela phi. Unokuyifumana loo mali ungazi ukuba ndingubani.  
_Milton Friedman_

Iinkampani ezininzi kunye nabantu abaninzi abaqondanga ukubaluleka kwe-intanethi xa yaqala, kwaye baqhubeka bengayiqondi njengoko yaphuhliswa ukuze inikeze uluhlu olubanzi lwezakhono esizithatha lula namhlanje, eziguqule kakhulu imiba emininzi yobomi bethu. Abasebenzisi bokuqala - ngexesha lale quote - ababelwa ukuthumela ii-imeyile kwi-modem ecothayo ye-dial-up babeya kuba nzima ukucinga behamba nesixhobo esiphathwayo esinika ukufikelela ngokukhawuleza kwiinkqubo ezininzi ezifumanekayo namhlanje.

Itekhnoloji yeLifu nayo yaphazamisa ngendlela efanayo, yaqala ngokwaziswa kweenkonzo zokugcina ulwazi ezisisiseko kwiminyaka embalwa phambi kokuba kuqalwe iBitcoin, ekuqaleni yayijongwa njengohlobo olwahlukileyo lokugcina ulwazi ngaphandle kwendawo, kodwa yaba liqonga eliphambili lokuhanjiswa kweenkonzo ze-IT kwaye iyaqhubeka nokuphuhlisa izakhono ezintsha kunye neempawu ngesantya esikhawulezayo esinzima ukusilandela.

Ikhonkco elilahlekileyo lokuvula inqanaba elilandelayo lexabiso kwi-intanethi, njengoko uFriedman wayekhe wakutsho kwangethuba, yimali ethembekileyo yedijithali efana nemali esetyenziswayo ukuhlawula ixabiso elihanjiswe kwi-intanethi, into eyenziwa yiBitcoin. Ikhule ngokwendalo, ngaphandle kwephulo lentengiso okanye iinkampani ezinkulu ezisemva kwayo, ngesantya sokwamkelwa esikhawulezayo kune-intanethi ukuze ibe 'yi-e-cash' uFriedman wayeyibona njengelikhonkco elilahlekileyo. Ivumela abantu kunye neenkampani ukuba bathengiselane kwihlabathi liphela ngaphandle kwemvume okanye iinkqubo ezivela kuRhulumente. Iza kuvula igagasi elitsha leentshukumo ezintsha kunye nentsebenziswano kwihlabathi liphela, kwaye iinkampani eziqonda oku kwaye zikwamkele kwangoko ziya kuba ngabaxhamli abakhulu.



#### 4.5.1 IBitcoin kubanikezeli beenkonzo zobungcali

Iinkonzo zobungcali zihlala zichaza amashishini anikezela ngeenkonzo kunye nobuchule endaweni yemveliso eyenziweyo. Ukuba ungomnye wabo, le sahluko ichaza impembelelo enokubakho kweli shishini, indlela yokuba nokuba nemali yedijithali ethembekileyo ekhoyo kwihlabathi liphela kunokuchaphazela indlela oshishina ngayo, kunye neentlobo zeenkonzo ezinokunikwa ukuze kufumaneke amathuba kule nkqubela.

##### Kutheni ucinga ngeBitcoin?

IBitcoin inokuvula iindlela ezintsha zeenkonzo ezikhoyo, ichaphazele indlela iinkonzo zangoku ezimele zilungelelanise ngayo, kwaye inike ithuba lokwakha iinkonzo ezintsha ezisondeleyo ezisekelwe kubuchule bakho bangoku kunye nolwazi lweshishini.

##### Ukongeza iBitcoin kwingxoxo nabathengi bakho abakhoyo

Kwimeko yangoku, iBitcoin isasebenza ngakumbi kwi-B2C. Inkampani yeenkonzo zobungcali enabathengi abafana neehotele okanye amatyathanga eerestyu ingacebisa abo bathengi ukuba bafake iintlawulo zeBitcoin kubathengi babo bokugqibela.

Njengoko imarike ikhula, ukongeza iBitcoin njengendlela yokuhlawula kwiinkonzo zabo kunokubonisa izakhono zabo kweli candelo. Abanini beBitcoin badla ngokufuna kwaye basebenzise iinkonzo ezivela kwiinkampani abanokuhlawula kuzo ngeBitcoin.

##### Indlela iBitcoin enokuchaphazela ngayo iinkonzo ezikhoyo

Kumashishini eenkonzo zobungcali, ukunyuka koqoqosho lweBitcoin kunokufuna indlela yokusebenzisana ngakumbi apho abathengisi, ababoneleli, abathengi kwanabo bakhuphisana nabo baya kuba notshintshiselwano olucacileyo lwedatha kunye nolwazi kwinkqubo ababelana ngayo. IBitcoin imalunga nenkqubo esebenzayo, ilungelelanisa amathuba kwaye iphinde isasaze amandla kunye noxanduva kwabo basebenzisana ngaphakathi kwayo. Umzekelo, iNostr (Amanqaku kunye nezinye izinto ezithunyelwa nge-relay) liqonga elitsha lemidlalo yoluntu elinika unxibelelwano ngqo phakathi koluntu lomsebenzisi ukuze kutshintshiselwane ngolwazi kunye nexabiso kusetyenziswa iBitcoin.

Njengendlela iLifu evumele ngayo intsebenziswano yehlabathi kwaye yanika ukufikelela okukhulu kwizixhobo ze-IT kubathengi abaninzi, iBitcoin inokuvumela ukufikelela kwimali evulekileyo necacileyo kubantu behlabathi liphela. Amaziko eenkonzo zobungcali aza kufuneka ajonge izisombululo abanazo ukuze acinge ngendlela olu tshintsho lunokuchaphazela ngayo indlela abanikezela ngayo ngeenkonzo kunye neemveliso zabo kubathengi.

##### Iinkonzo ezintsha ezisekelwe kwinkqubo yeBitcoin

Ukongeza ekuchaphazeleni iinkonzo ezikhoyo, iBitcoin kunye nenkqubo eyakhiwe phezu kwayo inika ithuba lokudala iinkonzo ezintsha njengemithombo yengeniso eyongezelelweyo okanye entsha ngokupheleleyo kumashishini eenkonzo zobungcali. Eminye imizekelo yale nto inikwe ngezantsi:



#### Ubugcisa bokubalwa kwezimali

##### Imvelaphi

Nangona iBitcoin ingengomxholo oqhelekileyo kubagcini-mali bamashishini amakhulu, ukuqonda okungakumbi ngeBitcoin kunokunceda ukuchaza ukuba kutheni abagcini-mali bamashishini amakhulu, abathengiswa esidlangalaleni, beqalile ukuyamkela kwiminyaka yakutshanje. Iimpawu ezininzi zeBitcoin, ezifana nokubakho kwe-21 yezigidi kuphela kunye nokunqongophala okuqinisekisiweyo kwi-blockchain kawonke-wonke, kunokuyenza ibe yindawo yokugcina ixabiso enqwenelekayo. Eli candelo libalulekileyo lephothifoliyo linokusebenza njengesikhuseli esixabisekileyo ngokuchasene nokunyuka kwemali mboleko karhulumente, ukwehla kwamandla emali, kunye nemingcipheko yezopolitiko. Njengoko abagcini-mali bamashishini bejamelene nemimoya emitsha yezoqoqosho, iimpawu ezizodwa zeBitcoin ziye zaba luncedo.

Ngokwesiko, amagumbi ezimali amashishini aye alawula imali ngokukhuselekileyo ngokuyabela uninzi lwemali kwizinto ezithathwa njengezisezantsi emngciphekweni (umzekelo, iidiphozithi zebhanki, iimali zemarike yemali, amaphepha-mali karhulumente, amaphepha orhwebo, kunye nezivumelwano zokuthenga nokuthengisa). Nangona kunjalo, izinto ezingaqinisekanga zoqoqosho ezifana nokunyuka kwamaxabiso, amanqanaba enzala, kunye nemingcipheko yezopolitiko eninzi zinokubangela ukuba amashishini aqwalasele ukuba ezi ndlela zisasebenza na.

Umhlalutyi nothatha inxaxheba kwezemali uLyn Alden uchaza iintlobo ezintathu zokunyuka kwamaxabiso: ukunyuka kwemali, ukunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto, kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto zabathengi (CPI). Ukunyuka kwemali (ukwanda kwesixa semali esikhulu njengoko kulinganiswa yi-M2) akusiqinisekisi kodwa kusisikhokelo sokunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto (ukwanda kwexabiso kunye nokuxabiseka kwezinto ezityalwayo) kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto zabathengi (ukwanda kwamanqanaba amaxabiso ezinto ezingezizo ezemali kunye neenkonzo).

Kuxhomekeke kuhlobo lweshishini, amashishini anokuchaphazeleka kukunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto zabathengi. Umzekelo, ukunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto kunokukhokelela ekwandeni kwexabiso lempahla ishishini elinokufuna ukuyityala okanye ukuyithenga, kwaye ukunyuka kwamaxabiso ezinto zabathengi kunokukhokelela ekwandeni kweendleko zesitokhwe xa kuthelekiswa namandla okuthenga kwemali.

##### Kutheni Abagcini-mali Bamashishini Banokufuna Ukutyala Imali kwiBitcoin

Iinkampani zeenkonzo zobungcali kufuneka ziqaphele inani elikhulayo lamashishini afaka iBitcoin kwiibhile zawo zemali, kwaye kutheni, umzekelo iMicroStrategy, kunye neMetaplanet. Ukuqonda izibonelelo zokusebenzisa ibhalansi yemali yakho njengendlela yokunyusa ixabiso labanini zabelo kongeza ixabiso kwicebiso elinokunikwa yinkampani yeenkonzo zobungcali kubathengi bayo.

##### Imigaqo

Uphuhliso oluninzi lwemigaqo-nkqubo kwihlabathi liphela malunga neempahla zedijithali lwenze abatyali mali bazive bekhuselekile ngakumbi xa betyala kwiBitcoin. Ngokwanda kwedatha yemarike kunye nembali yamaxabiso, imigaqo-nkqubo enobuhlobo kwiimpahla zedijithali efana ne-European Union's (E.U.) Markets in Crypto Assets (MiCA) legal framework, kunye nemvume ye-U.S. SEC yokuthengisa imveliso yeBitcoin etsalwe ngexabiso lentengiso ngoJanuwari 2024 inike abatyali mali kunye neenkampani ukuqinisekisa kunye nokuqonda abebekufuna.

##### Utshintsho Lomthetho weFASB kwiAccounting

NgoDisemba 2023, iBhodi yeMigangatho yeAccounting yezeMali (FASB) yahlaziya imigaqo yayo malunga nendlela iinkampani ekufuneka zibale ngayo kwaye zixele ngeBitcoin kunye nezinye iimpahla zedijithali kwiibhile zazo zemali. Le migaqo mitsha ixhamla iinkampani ezibambe iBitcoin ngokubavumela ukuba basebenzise i-accounting yexabiso lentengiso, ekugqibeleni ivumela iinkampani ukuba zonyuse ixabiso lempahla ngokwemarike. Ngaphambili, iinkampani zazivunyelwe kuphela ukwehlisa ixabiso lempahla zedijithali. Imigaqo emitsha inokuveza umfanekiso ongcono weengxelo zezezimali zenkampani kunye nempilo yezezimali ngokubonisa umfanekiso ochanileyo wexabiso leBitcoin elibanjwe.

##### Ukusebenza Kwexabiso leBitcoin Ngokuhamba Kwexesha

Njengomsebenzi wengcinga, cinga ukuba ibhalansi yemali yenkampani ephakathi kwi-S&P 500 ibinokujonga njani ukuba batyale nje i-1% yebhalansi yabo kwiBitcoin kwiminyaka emihlanu edlulileyo. Sicinga ukuba ubungakanani bebhilansi yemali yi-R180 billion, cinga ukuba i-1% (R1.8 billion) yatyalwa kwiBitcoin ngoJuni 2019 xa ixabiso lingu-R180,000. Nangona bekukho ukwehla kokuqala kunye namaxesha okungazinzi, isikhundla seBitcoin besiya kuphinda siphile kwaye sikhule sibe malunga ne-R12.6 billion ngoJuni 2024. Nangona inkampani inokuba neengxaki zexesha elifutshane kwinzuzo, ukusebenza kwezezimali kwexesha elide kwenkampani kuya kuphucuka kakhulu, ngakumbi ngexesha lokunyuka kwamaxabiso elandelwe yi-COVID-19.

##### Amathuba

Imeko yoqoqosho engazinzanga kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso okukhulu okwangoku kuye kwatyhala abagcini-mali bamashishini abacinga phambili ukuba baqwalasele ukufaka iBitcoin kwiibhile zabo zemali. Uthotho lokwabelana ngebhalansi yemali kwiBitcoin oluvela kwiBlock Inc., MicroStrategy, Stone Ridge Holdings Group, nabanye lubonisa umkhwa onokuthi uqhubeke ukhule njengoko amashishini ecinga umngcipheko wokuncipha kwemali ngenxa yokunyuka kwamanqanaba enzala kunye nelahleko enokwenzeka yamandla okuthenga kwemali ngenxa yezenzo zeebhanki eziphambili zemali kunye nezezimali.

Iinkampani ezikhetha ukwabela kwiBitcoin zinokuxhamla kukunyuka kwexabiso ngenxa yokuba ixabiso leBitcoin linyuka ngokuhamba kwexesha, kwaye oonobugcisa bokubalwa kwezimali abaqonda izibonelelo ezinokubakho kunye neziphumo zale ndlela banokuxhamla.



#### Iinkonzo zezezimali

##### Imvelaphi

Ukudibana kweshishini leenkonzo zezezimali kunye netekhnoloji kudale inqanaba elithile lokungaqinisekanga, kunye nezinto ezintsha ezifana netekhnoloji yokuhlawula phakathi kwabantu eziphazamisa iziseko, ukusebenza kunye neemodeli zeshishini.

Nangona ezi zinto zintsha zikhokelela kwizisombululo ezijolise ngakumbi kubathengi ngexabiso eliphantsi, zikwabeka uxinzelelo olungazange lubonwe ngaphambili kumaziko ezemali ukuba aphendule ngokukhawuleza kwaye abe nekhono lokutshintsha.

Ishishini leTekhnoloji yezeMali (Fintech) liquka uluhlu olubanzi lweenkampani ezisebenzisa itekhnoloji ukuhambisa iinkonzo ezintsha ezifana neentlawulo ezikwi-intanethi, usetyenziso lweselula, rhoqo zisebenza ngemali yesintu kunye neenkqubo zokuhlawula.

Iimali zedijithali zisebenzisa i-blockchain kunye ne-cryptography ukuhambisa iimali zedijithali ezizezinye, ezilungiselelwe ukubonelela ngokucaca, ukhuseleko kunye notshintshiselwano olungenamda. Le marike iye yaphuhliswa kwiminyaka eliqela ukudala izisombululo ezahlukeneyo kwaye isisiseko seMali yeDijithali yeBhanki Ephambili (CBDCs) ephuhliswa ngooRhulumente kwihlabathi liphela. Oku kukhokelele ekudidekeni kwimarike kuba izisombululo ezahlukeneyo zikhuphisana ngengqwalasela yamashishini noRhulumente. Akukho nanye kwezi zisombululo inika izibonelelo ezinqwenelekayo zemali engaphantsi kolawulo, evulekileyo, kwaye ingafuni mvume ngaphandle kweBitcoin.

##### Amathuba

* **Uthumelo lwemali lwamazwe ngamazwe (FX)**: Iindlela zemali ezikhoyo ngoku zokuthumela imali kwihlabathi jikelele zibiza kakhulu, zinzima kwaye zicotha, kwaye azifumaneki kubo bonke abanokuthi bazisebenzise. Oku kuvula amathuba okunikela ngotshintshiselwano lwemali olungabizi, olucacileyo nolusebenzayo phakathi kwamaqela amabini naphi na emhlabeni. Oku kungase kusekelwe kwi-US Dollar kusetyenziswa izisombululo ezifana ne-USDT (Tether), okanye kusetyenziswa i-bitcoin ngokuthe ngqo nge-Lightning Network.
* **Iinkonzo zokubonisana**: Ukwamkelwa kweBitcoin njengesona sisombululo silungileyo sokuhlangabezana neemfuno zabathengi – ukuqonda iimpawu eziphambili zeBitcoin njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso kunye nenkqubo ejikeleze yona – kuvula amathuba okunika iinkonzo zokubonisana kubathengi ngendlela abanokuxhamla ngayo ekwamkeleni i-bitcoin, kunye nokuyithelekisa nezinye iindlela.



#### Inkonzo yokuhlola kunye nokuqinisekisa

##### Imvelaphi

Itekhnoloji ye-blockchain inamandla okuchaphazela yonke inkqubo yokugcina iirekhodi, kubandakanya nendlela iintengiselwano eziqalwa, ziqhutywa, zigunyaziswa, zigcinwa kwaye zixelwa ngayo. Oku kungachaphazela imisebenzi efana nokuxelwa kwezemali kunye nokuxelwa kwerhafu. Oogqirha beeakhawunti kufuneka baqonde itekhnoloji esetyenziswa ukudala nokuqinisekisa iintengiselwano zeBitcoin kunye nendlela ezi zigcinwa ngayo kwi-ledger ye-blockchain xa kuthelekiswa ne-ledger zemveli.

Nangona ingaphandle komda wale module, ezinye iindlela ezisekelwe kumbono we-blockchain zikhona, ezifana ne-CBDC, 'stablecoins' kunye nezinye izisombululo ezigxile kwi-smart contract. Ukuqonda ezi zisombululo kunye nefuthe elinokubakho xa umxhasi ekhethe ukuzisebenzisa kuya kufuna nokuqonda indlela ezisebenza ngayo. Olu lwazi luya kuba lubalulekile ekuphenduleni imibuzo enjengokuba zisebenza ngendlela ezimeleyo nezivulelekileyo okanye 'ezinemvume' kwaye zilawulwa liqela labanini beprotocol, njengomzekelo kulawulo lwekhonkco lonikezelo. Oku kuya kuchaphazela ukuba zeziphi iindlela zokuqinisekisa ezifunekayo ukuze kufumaneke umkhondo wokuhlola osebenzayo.

* **Izikript kontrakthi (Smart contracts)**: I-smart contracts yindlela yokwenza ngokuzenzekelayo inkqubo yokwenza izivumelwano kunye nokuvumela ukongamela kunye nokunyanzeliswa kwezithembiso zesivumelwano ngaphandle kongenelelo oluninzi lomntu. Uzenzekelayo lunokuphucula ukusebenza kakuhle, lunciphise ixesha lokuhlawula kunye neempazamo zokusebenza. Kuba ukusetyenziswa kwetekhnoloji ye-smart contract kufuna uguqulelo lwazo zonke iimeko zesivumelwano zibe yilogic, kunokuphucula ukuthotyelwa kwesivumelwano ngokunciphisa ukungaqondakali kwezinye iimeko. Nangona kunjalo, kunokufuna inqanaba lolwazi lobuchwephesha ukuze kufakwe kwaye kugcinwe, elingekho kwiqela ngoku. Umhloli we-CPA angafuneka ukuqinisekisa ujongano phakathi kwe-smart contracts kunye nemithombo yedatha yangaphandle eqhuba iziganeko zeshishini. Ngaphandle kovavanyo oluzimeleyo, abasebenzisi bajongene nomngcipheko weempazamo ezingachongwanga okanye ubuthathaka.
* **Ingxelo ye-ESG**: Ukusebenza ne-bitcoin kungavumela amashishini ukuba afumane inzuzo entle kwiingxelo zabo ze-ESG, ezisele ziyimfuneko kwaye ziyinxalenye enkulu yeengxelo zabo zokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo. Iphepha le-KPMG elabhalwa ngo-2022 lichaza indlela iBitcoin mining enokukhuthaza ngayo ukusetyenziswa kwamandla ahlaziyekayo, ilungelelanise iigridi zamandla ezisebenzisa imithombo yamandla ahlaziyekayo angazinzanga, inciphise ukukhutshwa kwe-methane kwaye incede ekuphindaphindeni ukusetyenziswa kobushushu obuveliswa ziziko ledatha.

##### Amathuba

Ukuze athathe indima entsha, umhloli angafuna iseti entsha yezakhono:

* Uluvo ngeelwimi zokubhala iinkqubo zobuchwephesha
* Iindlela ezisebenzayo zokuqokelela idatha kwiBitcoin blockchain ukuze isetyenziswe ekuhlolweni
* Umahluko wokuphunyezwa phakathi kwezisombululo kunye nefuthe elinalo ekuthembekeni nasekubeni ngumnini weprotocol
* Ukuhlola i-smart contract ukuthotyelwa kwemigaqo kunye nolawulo lomntu wesithathu
* Ugxininiso kwimpikiswano ye-smart contracts ngokwemigaqo yomthetho esebenzayo
* Uluvo ngeziphumo ze-ESG zokwamkelwa kwe-bitcoin



#### Abacebisi bezeMali

##### Imvelaphi

Ukongeza ekubeni neempahla zedijithali ngokuthe ngqo, abantu abaninzi abacebileyo kunye neofisi zeentsapho babela inxalenye yotyalo-mali lwabo olutsha kwiimali ezityala kwiimpahla zedijithali ezithile okanye kwiiprojekthi zeempahla zedijithali. Ngelixa ii-bitcoin spot ETFs ezintsha zivumela ukuba ube notyalo-mali olulandelela ukusebenza kwe-bitcoin ngaphandle kokuthwala umngcipheko wokugcina impahla ngokwayo, eyona ndlela ikhuselekileyo yokukhusela ixabiso lexesha elide kukufunda indlela yokugcina impahla ngokwakho.

Okukhuthazayo, kubonakala ngathi abalawuli bamkela ngokubanzi ukuba iimpahla zedijithali ezifana ne-bitcoin ziza kuhlala, ngoko ke, endaweni yokucinga ukuba kufuneka zithintelwe okanye hayi, ngoku bagxile ekufumaneni ibhalansi phakathi kophuhliso lweshishini kunye nokukhusela abatyalomali. Kubalulekile ukufumana ingcebiso epheleleyo yendawo kunye nembono ebanzi malunga nonyango lomthetho kwiindawo ezininzi zotyalo-mali lweempahla zedijithali kunye nendlela imigaqo eyahlukeneyo echaphana ngayo.

Imimandla eyahlukeneyo nayo inendlela ezahlukeneyo yokujonga iimpahla zedijithali kunye nenzuzo evela kuzo kwirhafu. Kufuneka kusetyenziswe indlela efanayo yokuhlalutya imigaqo njengoko kuchaziwe ngasentla kwirhafu, kufunyanwe iingcebiso zerhafu zengingqi kunye nembono ebanzi yamazwe ngamazwe.

Imiba ejikeleze ukhuseleko lwedatha ifuna ukusetyenziswa kwamanyathelo aqinileyo okhuseleko lwe-cyber ukuqinisekisa ukuba zombini iimpahla zezemali kunye nolwazi lomntu zikhuselwe ngokwaneleyo.

Uninzi lweeofisi zeentsapho zigcina umgcini okhethekileyo we-crypto wesithathu ukuze agcine i-cryptocurrency yabo kunye nezitshixo zabucala ezifanelekileyo. Umnikezeli wenkonzo kufuneka abe neenkqubo zokhuseleko eziqinileyo ezikhoyo ngoku, afune imiqobo emininzi yokuqinisekisa kubasebenzi abavela ngaphakathi ukuze benze naluphi na urhwebo, kwaye abe ne-inshurensi egubungela ilahleko yempahla xa kukho ubuqhophololo okanye uhlaselo lwe-cyber.

##### Amathuba

Abacebisi beeofisi zeentsapho kunye nabantu abacebileyo banokunikela ngeenkonzo ezahlukeneyo ngokusekelwe kule meko yentengiso:

* Ulawulo lokhuseleko lweempahla zedijithali
* Ingcebiso yokhuseleko lwe-cyber
* Ingcebiso yokugcina iBitcoin ngokhuselekileyo
* Iziphumo zerhafu
* Bitcoin njengenxalenye yolawulo lwephothifoliyo iyonke
* Uphuhliso lweprotocol entsha yomthetho kunye nokuthotyelwa, ezifana nemisitho yokusayina izitshixo xa kuthunyelwa



#### Ingcebiso yokuthengisa

##### Imvelaphi

Kukho iinkampani ezininzi zeenkonzo zobungcali ezigxile ekuboneleleni ngeenkonzo zokuthengisa, ukusuka ekuncedeni abathengi ekuchazeni isicwangciso nasekwenzeni umxholo, ukuya ekwakheni iiwebhusayithi nasekutsaleni iindwendwe kuzo.

Nayiphi na isicwangciso sokuthengisa sedijithali kufuneka siquke isicwangciso sokusebenzisa amajelo akwi-intanethi ukwakha ubukho kwi-intanethi kunye nokufezekisa iinjongo ezithile zokuthengisa. Ekugqibeleni, injongo kukunyusa ukubonakala kweshishini kwaye kutsalwe abathengi abatsha kusetyenziswa amajelo ahlukeneyo.

Umzekelo omnye wejelo elitsha elisondelelene neBitcoin yiNostr. NjengeBitcoin, eli liqonga elizimeleyo nelivulekileyo apho umsebenzisi agcina ubunini bedatha ngokusebenzisa isitshixo sabucala. Ulwakhiwo lukaNostr olunganyangekiyo kulawulo lwenkqubo luqinisekisa ukuba unxibelelwano lweshishini, imiyalezo yokuthengisa, kunye nentsebenziswano nabathengi akuphazanyiswa ngabantu bangaphandle. Oku kubaluleke kakhulu kumashishini asebenza kumashishini apho imigaqo iqatha okanye apho inkululeko yokuthetha ingumcimbi. Ukongeza, ukusetyenziswa kukaNostr kwesitshixo sabucala esinye ukufikelela kwiindawo ezininzi kwenza kube lula ukuphatha iiakhawunti, kunika uxolo lwengqondo ngokukwazi ukutshintsha phakathi kwezicelo ezahlukeneyo ngokulula. Udibaniso neBitcoin kunye neLightning kuvumela iintengiselwano zehlabathi kunye neentlawulo ezincinci – uphawu lweLightning oluvula iindlela ezintsha zokuthengisa.

##### Ulawulo lweeDatha eziKhuselekileyo – umzekelo Nostr

Ubunini kunye nokwabelana ngokukhuselekileyo ngedatha yesigulana yinto ephambili ekuxhalabelwa ngayo, kunye nemigangatho efana ne-HIPAA eUnited States ebekwe ukuqinisekisa ukuba idatha ikhuselekile ngokwaneleyo.

Njengoko kugxininiswe kwiprojekthi yovavanyo yakutshanje e-El Salvador, iNostr-based SALUD protocol ijolise ekuguquleni indlela idatha yesigulana elawulwa kwaye yabelwana ngayo. Ngokusasaza idatha yezempilo, iNostr iqinisekisa ukuba izigulana zigcina ubunini bamarekhodi azo ezonyango ngelixa ivumela abanikazi beenkonzo zempilo ukufikelela kwidatha echanekileyo nengatshintshiyo xa kufuneka.

Le ndlela ijongana nemiba emikhulu kwiinkqubo zangoku zolawulo lwedatha yezempilo, apho ulwazi lwesigulana luhlala lulawulwa ngamaqela aphakathi anokuthi enze imali okanye aphathe kakubi idatha. NgeNostr, umngcipheko wokophulwa kwedatha okanye ukufikelela okungagunyaziswanga uncitshisiwe, kwaye izigulana zingakhetha ukuphuma ekwabelaneni ngedatha ukuba ziyafuna ngaphandle kokuphulukana nolawulo lolwazi lwazo.

Umzekelo wesibini wendlela iBitcoin enokudityaniswa ngayo kwiindlela ezintsha kwezempilo yiCrowdHealth. Yenze iqonga elisebenzisa izibonelelo zeendleko kunye nokusombulula kweBitcoin ngokudityaniswa kwi-Lightning wallet ukwenza nokufumana iintlawulo. Ezi ntlawulo ziphethwe ngabanikeli, abaza kuthi bakwazi ukucela uncedo kuluntu xa bejamelene nendleko enkulu yezonyango.

Le yimizekelo yendlela i-ecosystem yeBitcoin ekhula ngokukhawuleza ephazamisa ishishini elithile, kwaye imizekelo emininzi ingafumaneka kwamanye amashishini. Imibutho yeenkonzo zobungcali ejolise kwishishini elithile inokuba nakho ukuxhamla ngokunceda elo shishini lamkele ezi zisombululo, okanye ibe ngumphambili ozisa ezi ntlobo zezixazululo kumashishini amatsha njengoko imarike ikhula.



#### 4.5.3 Ukwakha i-ecosystem yeBitcoin

I-Bitcoin ibe yikhonkco eliphambili kwaye ihlala injalo, kodwa ugxininiso oluyimfuneko kwinqanaba lokuqala kukhuseleko kunye nokusasazeka kwashiya umsantsa kwezinye iimpawu kwimarike, apho ezinye izisombululo ezisekwe kwi-blockchain zavela ukuzama ukuzalisa loo msantsa. Ngezizathu ezahlukeneyo, ezi azizange zibe yimpumelelo enkulu kwezorhwebo kule minyaka idlulileyo, kwaye i-ecosystem yeBitcoin ngoku ikhule kwinqanaba apho ezi ntlobo zintsha zeempawu zinokwakhiwa kuyo ngaphandle kokuchaphazela kakubi itekhnoloji esisiseko yenqanaba lokuqala.

Ukongeza, uninzi lwabasebenzisi beBitcoin ngabagcini bexesha elide, belibona njengeyindawo yokugcina ixabiso okanye igolide yedijithali. Ama-maximalist eBitcoin yinxalenye enkulu yolu luntu abakholelwa ukuba yiye kuphela i-cryptocurrency eya kuthi ihlale ixesha elide. Baphikisa ukuba ubume bayo obusasazekileyo, ukhuseleko, kunye nenzuzo yokuba yokuqala kwenza ibe ngcono kunezinye zonke iimali zedijithali, nto leyo eyahlula kwezinye ii-blockchain.

Kukho nexabiso elikhulu elikhulayo elikhuselwe ngaphakathi kwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin ngaba bagcini, ngoko ukukhulisa i-ecosystem yemveli yeBitcoin akuyi kuba ngumcimbi wokutsala abasebenzisi abatsha. Endaweni yoko, iiprojekthi ziya kufuna ukuxhamla kuluntu olukhulu lwabasebenzisi, abaphuhlisi, kunye nemali sele ikwi-network. Oku kuzisa amathuba amaninzi okwandisa i-ecosystem yeBitcoin apho iinkonzo zobungcali zinokwakhiwa khona:

1. **Phucula Ukukhula:** Sele zikhona izisombululo ezininzi zokukhulisa ezisebenza kwi-network yeBitcoin, ezifana ne-Lightning Network. Nangona kunjalo, ezinye nazo ziyakhiwa ukujolisa kwiingxaki ezahlukeneyo. Umzekelo, ukuhambisa izisombululo zokukhulisa ezibanzi ezibonelela ngamapulatifomu akhuselekileyo, asasazekileyo kwaye asebenzayo kwi-smart contracts.
1. **Yenza kube lula i-Smart Contracts:** Cinga ngesivumelwano esizisebenzelayo ngokuzenzekelayo, ngaphandle kwesidingo samagqwetha okanye abantu abaphakathi. Yiloo nto iBitcoin smart contracts eyenzayo. IBitcoin smart contracts zizivumelwano ezizisebenzelayo apho imiqathango yesivumelwano ibhalwe ngqo kwikhowudi yedijithali. Zizisebenzisa ngokuzenzekelayo kwaye zinyanzelise isivumelwano xa iimeko ezichaziweyo zifezekisiwe, ngaphandle kwesidingo sabalamli.
1. **Khuthaza Ukuhambelana:** Ukwakha iibhulorho kunye nezidibanisi ezivumela unxibelelwano olungenamthungo phakathi kweBitcoin kunye nezinye ii-blockchain. Oku kungavumela abasebenzisi ukuba bahambise iiasethi phakathi kwee-chains ngokulula kwaye basebenzise ezona mpawu zibalaseleyo zenethiwekhi nganye. Umzekelo ungaba yi-USDT – i-stablecoin enokudluliselwa kwi-blockchain ezininzi ezahlukeneyo apho abasebenzisi bahlala bekhetha ixabiso elisezantsi. Ukuvumela i-USDT egcinwe kwenye yezi blockchain ukuba idluliselwe kwi-Lightning kunye neBitcoin kunokwenza ukuhambelana kunye nokufuduswa kexabiso kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin ekhuselekileyo.
1. **Dibanisa Amava alula kuMsebenzisi:** Njengoko i-ecosystem ikhula, kukho imfuneko yezixhobo ezenza lula inkqubo yophuhliso kunye nokwazisa kubaphuhlisi. Oku kubandakanya ii-wallets ezingcono, iindlela zophuhliso, kunye nezixhobo zokulungisa iimpazamo. Ngaphandle koko, ubulula bokusebenzisa bubalulekile kubo bonke abasebenzisi abangazaziyo kakhulu itekhnoloji ukuze bakwazi ukunxibelelana kwaye bathathe inxaxheba kwi-ecosystem.
1. **Imfundo kunye noKwakha uluntu:** Uluntu olomeleleyo lusisiseko sako nasiphi na isicwangciso se-blockchain esiphumeleleyo. Ukutyala imali kwimfundo, iindibano zocweyo, kunye neenzame ezikhokelwa luluntu kunokunceda abasebenzisi bafumane, abaphuhlisi bakhe, kunye nabatyali-mali badibane.
