# 8.1 Indalo yeLedger kaBitcoin

Irejista yeentengiselwano zeBitcoin (ekwabizwa ngokuba yi-timechain okanye i-blockchain) yirekhodi efikeleleka esidlangalaleni, enexesha elibekiweyo, yazo zonke iintengiselwano ezisemthethweni ezake zenzeka kwinethiwekhi. Kwinkqubo yemali yakudala, iintengiselwano zangaphakathi zibonakala kuphela kubathathi-nxaxheba abagunyazisiweyo, njengamabhanki, abalawuli, okanye abasebenzi bedatha abafana ne-SWIFT, BACS okanye SEPA. Ukufikelela kwidatha yeentlawulo kwiinkqubo zemveli kunokuthintelwa kakhulu kwaye kubize kakhulu.

Ngokwahlukileyo, kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin, nabani na onoqhagamshelo lwe-intanethi angajonga yonke intengiselwano ukusuka kwixabiso elikhulu ukuya kwiSatoshi enye. Abathathi-nxaxheba banokulandelela inani elipheleleyo lebitcoin ngexesha langempela, bajonge imisebenzi yeedilesi kunye neewallet, kwaye babone imivuzo yabadobi (miners) kunye nendlela yokuhlawula imali yezintengiselwano. Nangona imisebenzi ebonakalayo kwirejista inxulunyaniswa needilesi ze-public key hayi kwizazisi zabantu, kunokwenzeka ukuqokelela iiseti ezinkulu zedatha malunga nokuziphatha kokuchitha imali, okuvumela wonke umntu ukuba aqokelele kwaye aphande imisebenzi yezoqoqosho ngexesha langempela. Njengoko inethiwekhi ikhula kwaye yamkelwa ngakumbi njengomthombo wenyaniso yezoqoqosho, sinokubona ukuncipha kokuxhomekeka kumaziko karhulumente kunye nababoneleli besithathu kwimveliso yohlalutyo lwedatha kunye neengxelo zokuziphatha kokuchitha imali.



#### 8.1.1 Iinodi kunye nabahloli beBlock

Nabani na ofuna ukuqinisekisa ngokuzimeleyo irejista yeBitcoin kunye nokufikelela kwidatha yayo kufuneka asebenzise i-node epheleleyo. I-node epheleleyo ichazwa rhoqo njengendlela esisiseko yokuthatha inxaxheba kunye nokuqinisekisa uqoqosho lweBitcoin. Ifumaneka kwihlabathi lonke njengesoftware evulekileyo (open-source) ethi, xa isetyenziswa, ikhuphele kwaye iqinisekise yonke irejista yeBitcoin ukusuka kwi-'Genesis Block', epapashwe ngoJanuwari 2009, ukuya kuthi ga namhlanje. Ikwaxhasa ukhuseleko lwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin ngokunceda ukuqinisekisa iintengiselwano ezintsha ezongezwa kwirejista. Ngokufikelela kwirejista yeBitcoin ngale ndlela, i-node epheleleyo isebenza njengomthombo wenyaniso kubaphandi nabahloli benethiwekhi. Kwaye, kubasebenzisi beBitcoin, i-node epheleleyo isebenza njengendlela 'yokuzimela' yokufikelela kulwazi lweentengiselwano zoqoqosho lweBitcoin kuba iphucula ubumfihlo nokhuseleko ngokususa ukuxhomekeka kwiinkonzo zomntu wesithathu.

Ngelixa iinodi ezipheleleyo zikhuphela idatha ecocekileyo, abahloli beblock abafana ne-mempool.space okanye i-blockstream.info banikezela ujongano olubonakalayo lokukhangela nokutolika imisebenzi kwirejista. Umhloli weblock uvumela ukuba iintengiselwano nganye zilandelwe kwaye ibhalansi kunye nembali yeewallet zibonwe. Ikwabonisa imilinganiselo yemisebenzi yabadobi (miners) efana nemivuzo yeeblock kunye nedatha yemali yeentengiselwano.

Zonke kunye, iinodi ezipheleleyo kunye nabahloli beblock zizixhobo ezenza ukuba ukucaca kwenethiwekhi yeBitcoin kusebenziseke.



#### 8.1.2 Umsebenzi: Ukuhlola iRejista yeBitcoin

1. Vula [mempool.space](https://mempool.space) kwaye uhlolisise iphepha lasekhaya.
  * Yintoni ukuphakama kwebhloko yakutshanje?
  * Yimalini imali yeentlawulo zangoku (Ezisezantsi, Ezisemaphakathi, Ezisezantsi kakhulu)?
  * Zingaphi iintengiselwano ezilinde kwi-mempool zebhloko elandelayo?
1. Fikelela kwibhloko yakutshanje kwirejista.
  * Zingaphi iintengiselwano ezifakiweyo?
  * Chaza igama lomdobi (miner) webhloko?
  * Yayiyintoni imivuzo yebhloko?
1. Fikelela kwintengiselwano kwibhloko.
  * Zingaphi i-inputs kunye ne-outputs ezikwiintengiselwano?
  * Yintoni ixabiso lentengiselwano kwiBTC nakwiUSD?

Xoxa umahluko phakathi kwendlela imali ehamba ngayo kwinkqubo yakudala kunye nendlela ishishini okanye urhulumente angayisebenzisa ngayo le ndlela yokucaca.
