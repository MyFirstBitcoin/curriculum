# 8.3 Ikamva le-On-Chain Metrics

> Abantu abaninzi bakholelwa ukuba ukungabikho kombuso kuthetha ukulahlekelwa lulawulo. Ayisiyonyaniso leyo. Unokuphucula ulawulo ukuba ujonga ulawulo njengolawulo lweziganeko hayi abantu. Xa kunjalo, kokukhona unabantunabantu abaninzi abalawula iziganeko - kokukhona unabantunabantu abaninzi abakhathalela ukulawula iziganeko, kokukhona unabantunabantu abaninzi abasebenza ngokuzimisela ukwenza iziganeko ezilungileyo.  
_Sipho L. Creech_

Ikamva leemetriki ezisekwi-chain ekwenzeni idatha yezoqoqosho-mali libambe ithemba elikhulu, ngakumbi njengoko uqoqosho lweBitcoin luqhubeka nokukhula ngobukhulu nangokubaluleka.

Ngokungafaniyo neziphumo zezoqoqosho zemveli ezixhomekeke kwiingxelo ezilibazisekileyo, ezisekwiimibuzo, okanye ezingacacanga, idatha esekwi-chain inika iirekhodi zexesha langempela, ezicacileyo nezingenakuguqulwa zokudluliselwa kwealthu. Njengoko iBitcoin isiya idityaniswa ngakumbi kurhwebo lwehlabathi, umthamo wotshintshiselwano oluse-chain, umsebenzi wabasebenzisi, kunye nentsebenziswano yenethiwekhi kungasebenza njengemiqondiso yexesha langempela yokuchitha kwabathengi, ukuthumela imali ekhaya kunye nokuhamba kwemali.

Ubume obuvulekileyo belidger yeBitcoin buthetha ukuba nabani na angayisebenzisa idatha yayo ukuhlanganisa nokunika ingxelo ngeendlela zoqoqosho-mali okanye ukwahluka ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kwiingxelo ezivela kwimithombo karhulumente egxile embusweni okanye 'ezisemthethweni'. Ngelixa iimetriki ezichazwe kweli candelo sele zisebenza njengemiqondiso ephambili yemvakalelo yabatyali-mali kunye nomnqweno womngcipheko, kwixesha elizayo zingasebenza njengemithombo ebalulekileyo kwiimodeli zoqoqosho ezibanzi zokuziphatha kwemarike kunye nabathengi. Ezi zinokwenziwa ngexesha langempela nangokujonga ukusuka ezantsi phezulu, zixhaphaza iindlela zemveli zokwakha iimodeli zoqoqosho.
