# 8.0 Intshayelelo

> Ukuze uqonde iBitcoin, kufuneka uqonde ingxaki eyisombululayo  
_Wences Cesares_

Inkqubo yehlabathi yezenzo zoqoqosho iyakhiwa malunga nenethiwekhi yeBitcoin kunye nokwamkelwa kwayo njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso kunye nendlela yokutshintshiselana. Le nkqubo yenziwe ziimarike zokugcina, iinkonzo zokuhlawula, izixhobo kunye neenkonzo ngaphakathi kumashishini emigodi kunye namandla, kwakunye neenkonzo zezezimali ezibanzi ezifana norhwebo kunye ne-derivatives. Abathathi-nxaxheba kwezi marike baquka abantu ngabanye kunye nemibutho emikhulu, bonke badibana kwinethiwekhi enye eyakhelwe kwiinkqubo zesoftware evulekileyo.

Entliziyweni yale nkqubo kukho i-ledger esasazeke kwihlabathi lonke, enokuhlolwa esidlangalaleni, egcina ngokucacileyo irekhodi lotshintshiselwano ngalunye. Uhlobo olusasazekileyo nolubonakalayo lwaloo ledger kunye nedatha yayo esisiseko luvumela uhlalutyo loqoqosho ngexesha langempela kwinqanaba lehlabathi ngabantu ngabanye kunye nemibutho ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kubalamli. Ngenxa yoko, ngokungafaniyo nenkqubo yemali yesintu, uninzi lwemisebenzi yangaphakathi yeBitcoin iyabonakala ngokupheleleyo.
