# 8.4 Intengiselwano yoSuku lwePizza

Kule module ukuza kuthi ga ngoku, kugxilwe ekusebenziseni ubume obuvulekileyo belidger yeBitcoin ukuhlanganisa imilinganiselo eluncedo evela kwidatha yeentengiselwano ezidibeneyo. Nangona kunjalo, kunokwenzeka ukusebenzisa idatha yelidger kunye nabahloli beebhloko ukujonga iintengiselwano ezenzeka ebomini bokwenyani nokulandelela ukuhamba kwemali ngaphakathi kwenethiwekhi.

Minyaka le ngomhla wama-22 kuMeyi, uluntu lweBitcoin lubhiyozela uLaszlo Hanyecz, owayengumntu wokuqala okhankanyiweyo ukusebenzisa i-bitcoin ukuthenga iimpahla ezibonakalayo. Ngomhla we-18 kuMeyi 2010, uHanyecz wabhengeza kwiforum yeBitcointalk.org ukuba wayefuna ipizza kwaye wayezimisele ukuhlawula ngeBTC. Wanika 10,000 BTC kuye nabani na owayezimisele ukwenza loo ntengiselwano. Wasilinda iintsuku ezimbalwa, de umfundi oneminyaka eli-19 ubudala ogama linguSipho wavuma kwaye wathumela iipizza ezimbini ezinkulu.
