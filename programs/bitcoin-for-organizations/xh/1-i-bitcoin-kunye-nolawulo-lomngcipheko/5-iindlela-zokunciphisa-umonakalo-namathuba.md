# 1.5 Iindlela zokunciphisa umonakalo namathuba

Injongo yokuchonga imingcipheko kunye nokuvavanya iziphumo kukuba umbutho wakho ukwazi ukuthatha amanyathelo okunciphisa izoyikiso okanye ukusebenzisa amathuba, ukuba uhlalutyo luxhasa olo nyathelo.

* Yamkela / Qwalasela
* Nciphisa
* Yamkela / Sebenzisa

Ukubeka phambili kuncediswa kukujonga umbala weeseli kwiitafile zakho zeziphumo. Xa isiphumo sinombala obomvu kufuneka sibekwe phambili xa kuthelekiswa nezinye iziphumo ezinemida yexesha efanayo okanye ende, iziphumo ezikwibhokisi eluhlaza zilungele ngakumbi ukwamkelwa nokujongwa.

Qaphela umngeni wokufuna ukujonga kuphela, endaweni yokuthatha amanyathelo, kuyo yonke imingcipheko, kuba oko kungathetha ukuba izinto ezininzi zingenzeka ngaxeshanye kwixesha elizayo.

Kuphephe ukoyika ilahleko. Ukoyika ilahleko yindlela yokucinga ebonisa ukuba kubantu, intlungu yokulahlekelwa inamandla aphindwe kabini ngokwengqondo kunovuyo lokufumana. Gxila ubuncinane kangangoko ekuchongeni nasekusebenziseni amathuba njengoko usenza ekuphepheni izoyikiso.

Kuphephe ukuthatha izigqibo ngokukhawuleza kakhulu. Ungazilungiselela iziphumo ezingahambelaniyo kwaye oku kunokuba yindlela eluncedo yolawulo lwemingcipheko.

Kolu candelo sigxininisa amanye amaqela amakhulu amanyathelo ukukunceda uguqule uhlalutyo lube ngamanyathelo. Olu luhlu aluphelelanga, kodwa luyilelwe ukukunceda uqale.


> **Light – Iindlela ze-P&L**
>
> * Khusela ingeniso ekhoyo kunye nesabelo sentengiso
>   * Nciphisa ukubonakaliswa kwiimarike ezichaphazeleka kakubi
>   * Gxila kwimisebenzi yokukhula kwiimarike ezilindelekileyo ukuba zibe nenzuzo
> * Nciphisa iindleko zokusebenza kwaye uphucule ukusebenza kakuhle
>   * Tsala kwaye ugcine italente engcono
>   * Nciphisa iindleko nobunzima bentlawulo (ngakumbi ezamazwe ngamazwe) ezingenayo neziphumayo
> * Qhamuka neemveliso ezintsha, imijelo kunye neemarike
>   * Chonga imijelo yokukhula kunye neemarike
>   * Jolisa kubathengi abatsha, abakhulayo
>   * Nxibelelana kwaye wenze iimveliso neenkonzo zabathengi abatsha
>   * Hlola amanyathelo abakhenkethi, kunye nabangena abatsha. Qwalasela amandla abo kunye nobuthathaka babo.



> **Light – Iindlela zeBhilanse Sheet**
>
> * Qhuba ixabiso kwi-balance sheet yakho
>   * Nciphisa ukubonakaliswa kwiiasethi ezingaphumeleli ngaphezulu kweendleko zenkunzi, ngaphandle kokunciphisa amandla enkampani
> * Phucula imilinganiselo yokuxela ye-ESG
>   * Funda le ngxelo evela kwi-KPMG US echaza indlela ukunxibelelana ne-Bitcoin enokuphucula amanqaku akho e-ESG kwixesha elizayo
>   * [https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2023/bitcoin-role-esg-imperative.html](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2023/bitcoin-role-esg-imperative.html)
> * Nciphisa umngcipheko we-equity
>   * Yakha kwakhona i-balance sheet ngokwaneleyo ukuze uqinisekiswe ngokuchasene nemingcipheko emide enxulumene nayo.
