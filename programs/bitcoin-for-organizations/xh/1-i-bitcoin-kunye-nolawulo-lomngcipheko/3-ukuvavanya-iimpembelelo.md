# 1.3 Ukuvavanya iimpembelelo

Ukusebenzisa itheyibhile efana nomzekelo ongezantsi kunokunceda ukuvavanya ifuthe elinokubakho kumbutho xa umngcipheko uphumelele.

![Evaluate Impacts using a Table](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/816c58a56d039bc639121358282870e23be6b4e1-492x221.svg)

_Qwalasela amathuba okwenzeka kwexesha elithile (umz. iminyaka eyi-0-2, 3-5, 6-10, 10+)_

Izinto ezimbini ekufuneka ziqwalaselwe ngamathuba okuba umngcipheko wenzeke kunye nobukhulu befuthe kumbutho. Zombini ezi zinto zixhomekeke kwixesha elithile eliqwalaselwayo, ngoko kufanelekile ukwenza itheyibhile kwifuthe ngalinye elichongiweyo kwiixesha ezahlukeneyo.

La manqaku mathathu adibene ngandlela ithile, umzekelo, amathuba okuba utshintsho olukhulu lwenzeke kwangoko aphantsi kakhulu, kodwa kwixesha elide okanye xa ifuthe lingekho likhulu, amathuba angaphakama.

Umbala weeseli kwitheyibhile unceda ekubekeni phambili amanyathelo, oku kuchazwe kwiCandelo 3.5.

Kubalulekile ukuqaphela ukuba lo msebenzi kufuneka uqhubeke rhoqo. Njengoko ikamva elingaqinisekanga litshintsha libe lixesha elidlulileyo elaziwayo, amathuba, ubukhulu kunye namaxesha azayo kuya kufuneka atshintshwe kwaye aphuculwe. Xa kuvela iinkcukacha ezintsha, buyela kwizigqibo zakho zangaphambili.



#### 1.3.1 Amathuba Okuba Kwenzeke

> Ulawulo, ngaphezu kwayo yonke into, luyinzululwazi apho ubugcisa, inzululwazi, kunye nobuchule kudibana khona  
_Henry Mintzberg_

Ucinga ukuba amathuba okuba umngcipheko wenzeke, aze abangele ifuthe, angakanani kwixesha eliqwalaselwayo? Kwaye ubukhulu befuthe obuya kubakho bungakanani?

Akukho mpendulo 'ichanekileyo' apha; kukho nje izigqibo ezahlukeneyo ezisekelwe kwiingcamango zomntu ngamnye kunye nombutho malunga nefuthe lesiganeko ngasinye. Ukuva iincoko ezahlukeneyo kunokunceda ukuphucula uqikelelo ngokusekelwe kumgaqo wokubuza abaphulaphuli, nangona iimbono kufuneka zixhomekeke kubuchule kunye nolwazi olufanelekileyo.

#### 1.3.2 Ubukhulu

Ubukhulu befuthe naluphi na lunokunyuka njengoko ixesha lokubhekisa linde. Oku kuthetha ukuba kusenokwenzeka ukuba kufuneka kuthathwe amanyathelo xa ixesha libanzi. Ukulinganisa ubukhulu kumaxesha ahlukeneyo kunika ithuba lokubeka phambili amanyathelo kuwo onke umngcipheko.

#### 1.3.3 Ixesha

Ngokwesiqhelo, xa amathuba kunye nobukhulu befuthe bulingana, ixesha elifutshane lifuna ingqwalasela ephezulu ekuthatheni amanyathelo.
