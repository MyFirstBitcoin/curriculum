# 1.2 Ukuchonga iimpembelelo

> Impembelelo ye-Intanethi kuqoqosho ayizange ibe nkulu kunaleyo yomshini wefax…. kwiminyaka elishumi ezayo, igama elithi “uqoqosho lolwazi” liza kuvakala lihlekisa.  
 _Paul Krugman_

Emva kwesi sicatshulwa singasentla, kwiminyaka elishumi kamva ii-smartphone zazisanda kuvela, kwaye impembelelo yazo ekuphuhliseni iinkonzo ezintsha ze-intanethi kunye nee-apps yayingalindelekanga nangowe-2007. Ubungqina bubonisa ukuba iingcali zangoku azisoloko ziba zezona zingcali xa kujongwa emva kwexesha kutshintsho olukhulu; kudla ngokuphuma udidi olutsha lweengcali. Isicatshulwa esilandelayo sichaza ukuba kutheni 'iingcali' ngaphakathi kwindlela yakudala zingakwazi ukubona utshintsho olukhulu – kuba lusenzeka ngaphandle kolawulo lwazo.

> Awusoze watshintsha izinto ngokulwa inyani ekhoyo. Ukuba ufuna ukutshintsha into, yakha imodeli entsha ethi yenze imodeli ekhoyo ingasasebenzi.  
 _R. Buckminster Fuller_

Isifundo – ukuqikelela phambili kunokuba buthathaka kakhulu xa kuhlalutyiwa impembelelo yetekhnoloji enokutshintsha izinto ngokupheleleyo. Endaweni yoko, sicebisa ukuba uzame ukwenza iimeko-bume zendlela eza kuba ngayo ihlabathi xa iBitcoin iba yimali yehlabathi, uze emva koko uzame ukujonga ukuba singafika njani apho ukusuka namhlanje. Le yindlela esiyichazileyo xa sisenza uluhlu lweengozi kwi-1.1.

Ukuze uchonge impembelelo, thatha iingozi ezichongiweyo nganye nganye uze uzijonge ngaphakathi nangaphandle kwenkampani yakho. Ukwenzeka kwengozi kunokudala umngcipheko, ithuba okanye zombini, kwaye kunokwahluka kwiinkampani ezahlukeneyo. Le yingcamango ephambili kuba umngcipheko kwinkampani yakho unokuba lithuba kwinkampani ekhuphisana nawe okanye kwishishini elitsha.

Qaphela ukuba ukwenzeka kwengozi kunokudala iimpembelelo ezininzi kwinkampani.

#### Imizekelo

##### Umngcipheko wokuba iBitcoin iba yeyona mali iphambili, igcinwe kwiindawo zokugcina imali zehlabathi

* Iinkampani ezibona oku kwangethuba ziya kuxhamla, kwaye ezo zilibazisekayo zinokubandezeleka (Ithuba / Umngcipheko ?)

##### Umngcipheko wokuba inethiwekhi yeBitcoin iba linqanaba lokuhlala kweentengiselwano zorhwebo lwe-intanethi

* Uyilo lwemveliso kunye nendlela yokuhanjiswa zingatshintsha njani xa kukho iintlawulo ezincinci? (Amathuba okuhlawula ngokusasaza / Imingcipheko kwiimodeli zokubhalisa)
* Iinkampani ezisebenza kakhulu kwimarike yeenkqubo zokuhlawula zakudala zinokufuna ukutshintsha (Umngcipheko)

##### Umngcipheko wokuba ixabiso letyala linokunyuka kakhulu kwixesha elizayo

* Impembelelo kwimfuno yemveliso xa kukho utshintsho lwexesha elide ekufumanekeni / kwixabiso letyala (Umngcipheko?)
* Impembelelo kwisakhiwo sebhilansi yenkampani (Ithuba / Umngcipheko?)

##### Umngcipheko wokuba ubume babantu abanemali kunye nengeniso bungatshintsha

* Impembelelo kwintengiso yeendlela zokuthengisa xa kukho utshintsho lwexesha elide kwindlela yokwabiwa kwemali / ingeniso (Ithuba / Umngcipheko?)
* Impembelelo kwindibaniselwano yotyalo-mali lwabatyalizimali (Ithuba / Umngcipheko?)

##### Umngcipheko wokuba wonke umntu eMhlabeni asebenzise imali enye

* Impembelelo yokusebenza xa kubalwa kwiimali ezintsha (Ithuba)
* Impembelelo yokusebenza kwindlela yokulawula imali xa kukho imali enye kuphela (Ithuba)
* Impembelelo yokusebenza kwiindleko zokuhlawula ngaphakathi nelizwe nakwamanye amazwe xa kusetyenziswa imali enye (Ithuba)
* Impembelelo kwintengiso xa amaxabiso esiba lula ukuwathathela umgangatho ngokweendawo ezahlukeneyo (Ithuba / Umngcipheko?)
