# 1.0 Intshayelelo

> Ukujonga ikamva kukuvumela ukuba ubone kancinci kwixesha elizayo ngokubukela iziqwenga ezikhoyo ngoku.  
_Roger Spitz_

Zicinge usekunyaka ka-1995, usebenza emsebenzini owawunawo ngelo xesha (okanye abazali bakho) kodwa unolwazi onalo ngoku. Xa ujonga emva, loluphi uhlobo lomngcipheko owawujongene nawo emsebenzini wakho, kwishishini lakho, okanye kwimarike yakho ngenxa yokunyuka okuzayo kwe-intanethi? Ngaba kwakukho imingcipheko ekufuneka ithintelwe kwakunye namathuba ekufuneka athatyathwe? Okanye cinga ukuba usebenze kwaBlockbuster. Ukuba wawusazi ngelo xesha oko usaziyo ngoku, kwaye wawuyisikhulu esiphezulu kwinkampani kawonke-wonke, ubuya kube ungazifezekisi izibophelelo zakho njengomgcini wemali yabatyali-zimali ukuba awuzange uzame ukongeza umngcipheko wokunyuka kwe-intanethi kwiRisk Register yenkampani yakho.

I-intanethi yayiyingozi entsha ngo-1995, njengoko kunjalo neBitcoin namhlanje. Ngo-1995, i-intanethi yayisaziwa kakhulu kwiincoko; yayingelilo ifihlo lobuchwepheshe. Nangona kunjalo, yayiqondwa kancinci okanye isetyenziswa zizoshishino, kwaye bambalwa, ngaphandle kwezo ziqalayo kwi-intanethi, ababenomdla wokuyifunda okanye bayilandele njengomngcipheko.

Njenge-intanethi ngo-1995, iBitcoin iyaziwa kakhulu, kodwa ayiqondwa kakuhle okanye isetyenziswa zizoshishino, ngaphandle kwezo zisebenza kakhulu kwiBitcoin economy. Ngo-1995, i-intanethi yayisele iphuhliswe ukusuka kwimvelaphi yayo kwiminyaka yoo-1960s ngokudityaniswa kwemigangatho emitsha de yafikelela kwinqanaba lokusetyenziswa elakhokelela ekukhuleni kwayo okukhulu.

I-Bitcoin iphuhlile ngendlela efanayo ukusukela oko yaqalwa ngo-2009, iphucula kancinci indlela yokusetyenziswa kwaye ithomalalisa ubunzima. Njengoko kunjalo ne-intanethi, inani labasebenzisi beBitcoin likhule kancinci ([https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/an-objective-look-at-bitcoin-adoption](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/an-objective-look-at-bitcoin-adoption)) ngexesha ezi ziphuculo zobuchwepheshe zenzeka. Njenge-intanethi, asazi ukuba loluphi uphuculo oluzakuba yinqaku eliqhuba ukwamkelwa okukhulu de kube sekugqibeleni, kodwa sele sinedatha ukusukela ngo-2022 ebonisa "iziqwenga ezikhoyo ngoku" (Roger Spitz).
