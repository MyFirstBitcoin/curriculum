# 5.2 Yintoni iBitcoin Treasury

> I-Bitcoin, njengemali ye-cryptocurrency esetyenziswa kakhulu emhlabeni, yindawo ethembekileyo yokugcina ixabiso kwaye iyimpahla etyaliweyo enomtsalane enokukhula ixesha elide ngakumbi kunokugcina imali. Ukususela oko yaqalayo kwiminyaka elishumi eyadlulayo, i-Bitcoin iye yaba yinxalenye ebalulekileyo kwinkqubo yezezimali yehlabathi, inezinto eziluncedo kubantu ngabanye nakwiinkampani. I-MicroStrategy ibone i-Bitcoin njengempahla etyaliweyo esemthethweni enokuthi ingcono kunemali, kwaye ngenxa yoko yenze i-Bitcoin yaba yeyona nto iphambili kwisicwangciso sayo sokugcina imali kwitrejari.  
_Michael Saylor_



#### 5.2.1 Intshayelelo

Nangona i-bitcoin ingekho njengemali eqhelekileyo egcinwa ngabaququzeleli be-treasury yeenkampani, ukuqonda okukhulu nge-bitcoin kunokunceda ukuchaza ukuba kutheni abaququzeleli be-treasury beenkampani ezinkulu ezidweliswe esidlangalaleni nabanye beqale ukuyamkela kwiminyaka yakutshanje. Uninzi lweempawu ze-bitcoin, ezifana nomda ophezulu wokubonelela nge-21 million tokens kunye nokunqongophala okuqinisekisiweyo kwi-blockchain kawonke-wonke, kuyenza ibe yindawo etyebileyo yokugcina ixabiso. Le nxalenye ibalulekileyo yephothifoliyo inokuba ngumkhuseli obalulekileyo ngokuchasene nokunyuka kwezikweletu zikarhulumente, ukwehla kwexabiso lemali, kunye nemingcipheko yezopolitiko. Njengoko abaququzeleli be-treasury beenkampani bejamelene nemiceli mngeni emitsha yezoqoqosho, iimpawu ezizodwa ze-bitcoin ziye zaba luncedo.

Ngokwesiko, i-treasury yeenkampani ibisoloko ilawula imali ngokukhuselekileyo ngokuyabela uninzi lwemali kwizinto ezithathwa njengezisezantsi emngciphekweni (umzekelo, iidiphozithi zebhanki, iimali zemarike yemali, amaphepha etreasury, amaphepha orhwebo, kunye nezivumelwano zokuthenga nokuthengisa). Nangona kunjalo, izinto ezingaqinisekanga zoqoqosho ezifana nokunyuka kwamaxabiso, amanani enzala, kunye nemingcipheko yezopolitiko eninzi zinokubangela ukuba iinkampani ziqwalasele kwakhona ukuba ezi zicwangciso zisasebenza na. Ukwamkela i-bitcoin njengempahla yokugcina imali kwitrejari kunokunceda ukujongana nale mingeni.



#### 5.2.2 Kutheni i-Bitcoin?

Imali yimpahla okanye iseti yeempahla abantu abanokuzisebenzisa ukuthenga iimpahla okanye iinkonzo, ukuhlawula amatyala okanye ukugcina ixabiso, ngoko ke ibandakanya enye okanye ezingaphezulu kwezi mpawu zilandelayo:

* Iyunithi yokubala: Imali isetyenziswa ukuseta ixabiso lempahla kunye neenkonzo
* Isixhobo sotshintshiselwano: Imali ingasetyenziswa ukuncedisa urhwebo lweempahla kunye neenkonzo
* Indawo yokugcina ixabiso: Ixabiso elinikezelwa ngendlela yeempahla okanye iinkonzo ezinikwe imarike yinkampani okanye ngumntu linokugcinwa ukuze lisetyenziswe kamva

Ayizizo zonke iimpahla ezimele zibonelele zonke ezi mpawu zintathu – umzekelo, ubugcisa obuxabisekileyo okanye ipropathi ebizayo ingaba yindawo yokugcina ixabiso, kodwa ayinakutshintshiselwa lula kwimarike.

I-Bitcoin iyaguquka ukuze ikwazi ukuhlangabezana nazo zonke ezi misebenzi zintathu, kodwa kugxilwe apha kwindlela enokusetyenziswa ngayo njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso kwitrejari yenkampani, nokuba inkulu okanye incinci. Iphumelele kakhulu kule misebenzi ukusukela oko yaqalayo, kwaye oku ngoku kukhokelela ekwamkelweni kwayo njengempahla yetrejari kubantu ngabanye, iinkampani kwanamazwe, njengoko icatshulwe ngasentla.

* Amazwe afana ne-El Salvador ne-Bhutan sele ethenga okanye emba i-Bitcoin ixesha elithile kwaye eyongeza kwiindawo zawo zokugcina imali, kodwa ingcamango yokuba ilizwe linendawo yokugcina i-Bitcoin yafumana udumo lwehlabathi lonke xa umgqatswa wobongameli wase-US wabhengeza isicwangciso sokudala indawo yesizwe yokugcina i-Bitcoin.
* Iinkampani nazo ziye zaqala ukudala iindawo zazo zokugcina i-bitcoin ukusukela ngo-2020 xa zombini i-Square kunye ne-MicroStrategy zabhengeza izicwangciso zazo zokwenza njalo, kwaye iinkampani ezincinci zilandele umkhondo wazo.

> Saziva ngathi sikwi-€500M eqala ukunyibilika, xa inzala yokwenyani kwitrejari yethu yehle yaya ngaphezulu kwe -10%, saqonda ukuba yonke into esiyenzayo kwi-P&L ayibalulekanga.  
_Michael Saylor_

Ukwenza i-treasury ye-Bitcoin kusombulule ingxaki eyayijamelene ne-MicroStrategy kwaye kuye kwanceda ukonyusa ixabiso kubanini zabelo ukusukela oko baqalisa esi sicwangciso ngo-2020. Le ndlela inokuvelisa izibonelelo ezifanayo kwiinkampani ezinkulu nezincinci naphi na emhlabeni.

Kutheni iinkampani kwanamazwe zinokuxhamla kwi-treasury ye-Bitcoin? Imali ese-bhankini, ethi yehle ixabiso ngenxa yokwenziwa kwemali, ayizange ibe yindawo elungileyo yokugcina ixabiso. Iinkampani ziye zanyanzelwa ukuba zisebenzise loo mali ukufumana ukukhula ngokuthenga ezinye iinkampani, okanye ukuyibuyisela kubanini zabelo ngendlela yokuthenga izabelo kwakhona.

Iinkampani, oorhulumente kunye nabantu ngabanye abakwazi ukugcina ixabiso ngemali ye-fiat, nto leyo ebabangela ukuba bakhangele ezinye iindlela, ezifana namabhondi, ipropathi okanye iimpahla ezinzima. Ayizizo zonke ezi zinokongezwa lula kwitrejari.

I-Bitcoin imele isisombululo kunqongophala lwedijithali kwaye ikwimeko yokutsala ixabiso lemali kwezinye iimpahla ezifana nezi. Oku kuye kwanceda ukuba ibe yeyona mpahla iphumelele kakhulu ukusukela oko yaqalayo, kwaye kulindeleke ukuba iqhubeke nokudlula ezinye iimpahla kwixesha elizayo.

Ngokufika kwemithetho emitsha ye-accounting, wonke umntu onoxanduva lokulawula imali ngoku unoxanduva lokuqonda i-bitcoin, ukubona ukuba inendawo na kwitrejari, kunye nokuqonda imingcipheko yokuyamkela okanye ukungayamkeli kwishishini.

##### Square

Ngomhla we-7 ku-Okthobha, 2020, i-Square, Inc. yathenga malunga ne-4,709 bitcoins ngexabiso elipheleleyo le-€50 million. I-Square ibiyinkokeli kwishishini le-bitcoin ukusukela ngo-2018 nge-Cash App yethu, enika abathengi ithuba lokuthenga nokuthengisa i-bitcoin. Njengabakholelwa ekukhuleni okuqhubekayo kwe-bitcoin kwixesha elizayo, inkampani yaseka i-Square Crypto, iqela elizimeleyo eligxile kuphela ekunikeni igalelo kumsebenzi we-bitcoin open-source ukuze kuxhamle wonke umntu. I-Square ikwazise i-Cryptocurrency Open Patent Alliance (COPA), umbutho ongenzi nzuzo okhuthaza ukusungulwa kwe-crypto, uvula ukufikelela kwizinto ezilungiselelwe i-crypto, kwaye unceda iinkampani kunye nabantu abazikhusela kubahlaseli be-patent.

##### Elon Musk - Tesla kunye ne-Space X

Uhambo lwe-Tesla kwi-Bitcoin lwaqala ngoFebruwari 2021 xa inkampani yatyalomali i-€1.5 billion. Inkampani yathi ukuthengwa kwe-bitcoin kwakukukunceda ukwahlula-hlula ngakumbi kunye nokwandisa ingeniso yemali yayo. I-Tesla yathi iya kuqalisa ukwamkela iintlawulo nge-Bitcoin xa kuthengwa iimveliso zayo, ngokuhambelana nemithetho esebenzayo. U-Elon Musk naye wabhengeza ukuba i-SpaceX inayo i-Bitcoin ngo-2021 kwi “The B Word” inkomfa ye-intanethi._Ndine-Bitcoin, i-Tesla ine-Bitcoin, i-SpaceX ine-Bitcoin_,” watsho uMusk.

Uhlalutyo lwamva nje lwe-on-chain lubonise ukuba ukusukela kwi-Q4 2024, iimpahla ezidityanisiweyo zezi nkampani ziyi-19,788 Bitcoin.

Ukusukela ekupheleni kuka-2024, abanini zabelo bezinye iinkampani ezinkulu ezifana ne-Microsoft ne-Amazon baqala ukuqwalasela izindululo ze-treasury ye-Bitcoin, ngoko ke umkhwa uyakhula.

##### Tahini’s

Omnye wabanini be-Tahini’s, ikhonkco leevenkile zokutyela ezizinze eKhanada, wayesele enomdla omkhulu kwi-Bitcoin xa i-MicroStrategy yabhengeza izicwangciso zayo ze-Bitcoin Treasury kwaye inkampani yagqiba ekubeni ilandele, yafaka yonke imali engabalulekanga (imali yokusebenza yeenyanga ezi-6) kwi-Bitcoin. Iye yagcina kwaye yaqhubeka nokongeza kwi-treasury yayo ngexesha le-peak ka-2021 nasekwehleni kwexabiso, icacisa ukuba nawuphi na umshishino ofuna ukusebenzisa le ndlela kufuneka uthathe umbono weminyaka emine ubuncinane.

Kwi minyaka emine ukusukela oko baqalisa le ndlela, ikhonkco liye ladlula uninzi lwe-Wall Street ngokubhekiselele kwingeniso. Ngokufaka ii-ATM ze-Bitcoin kunye nokuqhuba ii-Meetup ze-Bitcoin, ishishini likwazile ukwakha abalandeli abathembekileyo be-Bitcoin.

Ngoko kwaba yinto enengqiqo ukufaka imali yethu kwi-bitcoin kwaye oko kuya kudlula naliphi na inqanaba lokunyuka kwamaxabiso esiza kulibona kule minyaka ilandelayo.

#### Ukwamkelwa kwamazwe

Njengoko umfanekiso ubonisa, amazwe ngoku agcina i-Bitcoin, rhoqo ngenxa yokuthathwa kweempahla, iindlela ezifihlakeleyo ezifana nokumba okanye ngeefandesi zempahla yesizwe, kwaye kwimeko ye-El Salvador njengomgaqo-nkqubo ocacileyo.

##### El Salvador

Ngo-2019, iqela labaxhasi be-cryptocurrency kunye namalungu oluntu asekuhlaleni kwidolophana encinci eselunxwemeni i-El Zonte, e-El Salvador, baqalisa ukwenza uqoqosho oluphumelelayo olusekwe kwi-Bitcoin kuluntu lwabo. Ngokufundisa abahlali ngezibonelelo ze-Bitcoin, ukubonelela ngezixhobo ezifunekayo kunye nezixhobo zokuyamkela, kunye nokukhuthaza ukusetyenziswa kwayo ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo, iprojekthi ye-Bitcoin Beach yakhula ngokukhawuleza, yaguqula i-El Zonte yaba yindawo ephilayo ye-Bitcoin.

Impumelelo ye-Bitcoin Beach yatsala umdla kukamongameli wase-El Salvador, uNayib Bukele, owabona amandla okutshintsha kwe-cryptocurrency ekusombululeni ezinye zeengxaki ezinzima zoqoqosho nezentlalo kweli lizwe. Ephefumlelwe le projekthi isuka ezantsi, uBukele waqala ukukhangela iindlela apho urhulumente wase-El Salvador angamkela kwaye akhuthaze ukusetyenziswa kwe-Bitcoin kwilizwe lonke.

NgoJuni 2021, uBukele wenza isibhengezo esikhulu sokuba i-El Salvador iza kuba lilizwe lokuqala emhlabeni ukwamkela i-Bitcoin njengemali esemthethweni, kunye nedola yase-US.

Oku akuzange kunike kuphela abantu base-El Salvador isixhobo esitsha, esiyintsusa kwezezimali kodwa kwenze ilizwe laba yindawo ephambili yehlabathi yokwamkelwa kunye nokuqanjwa kwe-cryptocurrency.

Kutsha nje, uBukele uye wadibana noJavier Milei wase-Argentina ukuxoxa ngentsebenziswano kulo lonke elaseLatin America kuphuhliso lweempahla zedijithali. Ilizwe sele lithenga i-bitcoin enye ngosuku ukusukela oko lamkela i-bitcoin njengempahla yokugcina imali kunye nemali esemthethweni, utyalo-mali oluye lwaba nenzuzo ukuza kuthi ga ngoku.

##### United States of America

Kwi-Q3 2024, urhulumente kaTrump wenza isibhengezo esikhulu eNashville, ebhengeza izicwangciso zokwabela inxalenye yeendawo zokugcina imali ze-US Treasury kwi-Bitcoin. Le nyathelo yayijolise ekwahluleni iimpahla zesizwe kunye nokusebenzisa izibonelelo ezibonwayo zeempahla zedijithali. Emva kophumelela iphulo lakhe lokhetho, indawo yesizwe ye-Bitcoin kulindeleke ukuba ibe ngumgaqo-nkqubo karhulumente wase-US.

Amazwe amaninzi aseMelika abhengeze izicwangciso ezifanayo zokudala iindawo zokugcina iBitcoin, nto leyo enokwenzeka ngokuzimeleyo ngaphandle kwenjongo echaziweyo yokuba ibe yindawo yokugcina ye-USA njengelizwe.

##### Bhutani

IBhutani ngumzekelo welizwe elisebenzisa ugodi-mali ukwenza iBitcoin ngqo.

Uhambo lweBhutani ekugodeni iBitcoin lwaqala ngo-Epreli 2019, xa imali ye-cryptocurrency yayixabisa malunga ne-$5,000. Ingalo yotyalo-mali karhulumente, iDruk Holding & Investments, yaqinisekisa kwiphephandaba lasekuhlaleni iThe Bhutanese ukuba "ingene kwindawo yogodi-mali" ngelo xesha.

Ubukumkani baseBhutani basebenzise izixhobo zalo ezininzi zamandla ombane avela emanzini ukuxhasa imisebenzi yogodi-mali.

La mandla acocekileyo amaninzi avumela iBhutani ukuba ixhase imisebenzi yogodi-mali yeBitcoin emikhulu ngelixa igcina ukuzibophelela kwayo ekukhuseleni indalo.

Isigqibo seBhutani sokungena kwimarike ye-cryptocurrency sakhuthazwa yimfuneko yezoqoqosho. Ilizwe lajamelana nokwehla kwemali engenayo evela kubakhenkethi kwaye lafuna ukwandisa imithombo yengeniso. Ubhubhane lwe-COVID-19, olwaluchaphazele kakhulu ingeniso yeBhutani ye-$88.6 yezigidi ngonyaka evela kubakhenkethi, lwaqinisekisa ngakumbi esi sicwangciso.

Ukusukela nge-16 Septemba 2024, iBhutani inemali yeBitcoin efikelela kwi-$750 yezigidi, iyenza ibe sesikweni sesine ngobukhulu phakathi korhulumente abaninzi kwiqonga le-Arkham. Oku kubeka iBhutani njengomdlali obalulekileyo kwimarike yehlabathi ye-cryptocurrency, ngakumbi xa kuthathelwa ingqalelo inani labemi elingaphantsi kwe-800,000.

Ukugcina iBitcoin kunye nemisebenzi yogodi-mali imele umthombo omtsha wengeniso, onokunciphisa ukwehla kwemali engenayo evela kubakhenkethi kwaye ujongane nengxaki yokungalingani kurhwebo lwelizwe. Urhulumente ucinga ukusebenzisa iBitcoin ukuxhasa ukunyuswa kwemivuzo kwicandelo likarhulumente, nto leyo ebonisa ukubaluleka kwayo kwezemali zelizwe.

Ukungena kweBhutani kugodi-mali yeBitcoin kunye notyalo-mali kwi-cryptocurrency kunika izifundo ezixabisekileyo kwamanye amazwe amancinci.

##### Iinkampani Zogodi-mali yeBitcoin

Iinkampani zogodi-mali yeBitcoin, njengazo zonke iinkampani, zineendleko ekufuneka zihlawule, ezifana na:

* Iindleko zotyalo-mali kunye nentlawulo yezixhobo zogodi-mali
* Izakhiwo
* Iindleko zamandla ombane
* Ugcino
* Iindleko zangaphakathi (iintlawulo zabasebenzi njl.)

Nangona bengathanda ukugcina iBitcoin njenge-asethi, kuba iyona ndlela ephambili yokufumana ingeniso kwinkampani, bahlala bethengisa inxenye okanye yonke iBitcoin abayigodileyo.

Indlela entsha eqhutywa yiMicroStrategy yokungena kwimarike yetyala ukufumana imali yokuthenga iBitcoin ngqo ivula isicwangciso esitsha nakwezinye iinkampani zogodi-mali yeBitcoin ezisesidlangalaleni ukuba zenze okufanayo. Ngokwenza oku, banokuthi 'bathenge kwangaphambili' iminyaka yeBitcoin abayigodileyo ngokukhupha ityala kwimarike kwaye bathenge iBitcoin ngexabiso langoku ukuze bayongeze kwibhala yesicwangciso-mali. Njengoko iBitcoin isiya ithambekele kwimirhumo yokuthengiselana njengoko imivuzo yogodi-mali incipha kwiimijikelo ezizayo, le ndlela ivula umceli-mngeni omtsha kunye namathuba kwiinkampani zogodi-mali ukuba zongeze iBitcoin ngexabiso langoku baze bahlawule iindleko zabo ngemirhumo yokuthengiselana kwixesha elizayo. Ukufikelela kule marike kunokubeka iinkampani zogodi-mali ezisesidlangalaleni kwindawo engcono kunezo zikwimfihlo. Ukuqonda izibonelelo ezinokubakho kunye neziphumo zale ndlela kufuna ukuqonda iBitcoin kunye neshishini lemali yemveli.

#### Izizathu zokwamkelwa

Izizathu zokwamkelwa kweBitcoin njenge-asethi yokugcina imali yeTreasury zingasishwankathelwa ngolu hlobo:

##### Amazwe

* Amazwe angasebenzisa izixhobo zendalo, ezifana namandla ombane avela emanzini, kwiitekhnoloji ezisakhulayo, anike amandla asekuhlaleni kwaye agode iBitcoin
* Kukho amathuba okwahlula-hlula uqoqosho, kuncitshiswe ukuxhomekeka kumacandelo emveli afana nokhenketho.
* Oku kunokufezekiswa kusetyenziswa imodeli yokufumana ngokuzolileyo ngaphandle kokutsala ingqalelo engadingekiyo (njengoko iBitcoin igodwa ngqo endaweni yokusebenzisa i-Exchange)

##### Amashishini

* Ukwahlula-hlula iiasethi: Iasethi enye yokunciphisa umngcipheko kwiTreasury
* Ukuzikhusela kukunyuka kwamaxabiso: Iinkampani ezamkele iBitcoin zibonile ukuba iindawo zabo zokugcina imali zidlule kumaxabiso okunyuka asemthethweni nangasemthethweni
* Njengenxalenye yesicwangciso esibanzi seBitcoin, ukufumana ukuqonda iBitcoin njenge-asethi, ukwakha uluntu nokunceda abanye bafumane izibonelelo ezifanayo.

#### 5.2.4 Imicimbi yoLawulo kunye noThotyelo-mthetho

##### Imeko yoMthetho weHlabathi

Iziganeko ezininzi zomthetho kwihlabathi jikelele malunga nee-asethi zedijithali zinike abatyali-mali ukuzithemba okukhulu kwiBitcoin njengotyalo-mali. Ngokwanda kwedatha yemarike kunye nembali yamaxabiso, imithetho enobuhlobo nee-asethi zedijithali efana ne-European Union's (E.U.) Markets in Crypto Assets (MiCA) legal framework, kunye nemvume ye-U.S. SEC ye-spot bitcoin exchange-traded product ngoJanuwari 2024 zinike abatyali-mali kunye neenkampani ukuqinisekiswa kunye nokucaca abebekufuna.

Ukugcina iBitcoin njenge-asethi yeTreasury kuneziphumo ezahlukeneyo zerhafu kuxhomekeke kwilizwe apho inkampani ise khona. Nazi izinto eziphambili ekufuneka ziqwalaselwe, ezichaneke ngexesha lokubhala kodwa zinokutshintsha:

##### Unyango lweAccounting

* **IFRS (Yamazwe ngamazwe)**
  * I-Bitcoin nayo kufuneka ithathwe njenge-asethi engabonakaliyo, nangona amanye amazwe evumela ukuba ithathwe njenge-inventri ukuba inkampani igcina iBitcoin ngenjongo yokurhweba.
* **Utshintsho kwiMithetho yeFASB yeAccounting**
  * NgoDisemba 2023, iBhodi yeMigangatho yeAccounting yezeMali (FASB) yahlaziya imigaqo yayo malunga nendlela iinkampani ekufuneka zibala ngayo kwaye zixele iBitcoin nezinye ii-asethi zedijithali kwiibhala zazo zemali phantsi kwe-USGAAP. Le mithetho intsha inceda iinkampani ezigcina iBitcoin ngokubavumela ukuba basebenzise i-fair value accounting, ekugqibeleni ivumela iinkampani ukuba zonyuse ixabiso le-asethi ngokwemarike. Ngaphambili, phantsi kwe-US GAAP, iinkampani zazivumelekile kuphela ukwehlisa ixabiso leendawo zabo ze-asethi zedijithali. Imigaqo emitsha inokuveza imeko yemali yenkampani kunye nempilo yezezimali ngokubonisa ixabiso leBitcoin elichanileyo eligcinwayo.

##### Irhafu kwiNzuzo kunye nokuLahleka

* **Irhafu kwiNzuzo yeNtloko:**
  * Kumazwe amaninzi, inzuzo evela ekuthengiseni iBitcoin ithathwa njenge nzuzo yentloko.
  * Ixesha elifutshane vs. Ixesha elide: Ukuba igcinwe ixesha elingaphantsi kwexesha elithile (umz., unyaka e-US), inzuzo irhafiswa njenge nzuzo yentloko yexesha elifutshane, rhoqo ngexabiso eliphezulu kuneleyo yexesha elide.
* **Ukuncitshiswa kokuLahleka**:
  * Ilahleko evela kwiBitcoin inokuba yancitshiswa kwezinye iinzuzo zentloko, kuxhomekeke kwilizwe.

* **Ulwelo:**Ulwelo lithetha amandla okuguqula isixhobo sibe yimali ngokukhawuleza ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa kakhulu. Okukhona ulwelo lukhulu, kokukhona impilo yesixhobo ingcono. IBitcoin yenye yezona digital assets zine-ulwelo oluphezulu kwihlabathi, kunye neetriliyoni kwivolumu yokurhweba ngonyaka. INkampani ingathengisa iBitcoin ngokukhawuleza, nangona iintengiselwano ezinkulu zinokuchaphazela amaxabiso emarike.
* **Ukhuseleko:** Iiasethi kufuneka zibe nomngcipheko omncinane wokungahlawuli okanye wokuncipha kwexabiso. Iiasethi ezinomngcipheko ophezulu wokungahlawuli okanye ezichaphazelekayo kwiimarike eziguquguqukayo azilungelanga. IBitcoin ayilawulwa kwaye ayinakuthintelwa, inika ukhuseleko xa kukho ingxaki yezopolitiko okanye yezoqoqosho. Nangona kunjalo, imingcipheko ibandakanya uhlaselo lwe-cyber kunye nesidingo sokhuseleko olomeleleyo lokugcina.
* **Uzolile:** Iiasethi zeNkampani akufuneki zibonise ukuguquguquka okukhulu kwixabiso lazo. Inqanaba lokuguquguquka kweBitcoin ngoku lihlala liyeyona ngxaki inkulu.
* **Inzuzo:** Nangona ukhuseleko lubalulekile, ukufumana ingeniso encinci kunceda ukugcina ukusebenza kweshishini. Ngokungafaniyo neasethi zemali yeNkampani zemveli, iBitcoin ayivelisi inzala. Kodwa ukunyuka kwexabiso layo kule minyaka ilishumi idlulileyo kuyenza ibe lukhetho olomeleleyo lokufumana inzuzo, igqitha iiasethi zemveli.

Ukuphumelela, kungafuneka uqonde ezi nkxalabo kunye nendlela yokuzinciphisa kwaye oku kufuna ukuqonda okuhle kweBitcoin, zombini kwinethiwekhi nakwixabiso elilondoloziweyo.

* Ubungqina ukuza kuthi ga ngoku kwiinkampani ezithengiswa esidlangalaleni ezamkele isicwangciso se-bitcoin treasury bubonisa ukuba ixabiso lezabelo zazo libonakala likhuphuka ngenxa yemarike.

* Ukungazinzi kweBitcoin kungaqhubeka okanye kunyuke, okanye ingeniso yemali ingapheli ukuba luncedo, nto leyo enokuthi ibe neziphumo kwezemali kwishishini.
* I-Bitcoin inokuba nengxaki enkulu yezobugcisa enokuchaphazela ixabiso layo.
* I-treasury inokuba sengozini ngenxa yokophulwa kokhuseleko.
