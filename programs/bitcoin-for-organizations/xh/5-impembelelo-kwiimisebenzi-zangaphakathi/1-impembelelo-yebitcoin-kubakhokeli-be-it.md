# 5.1 Impembelelo yeBitcoin kubakhokeli be-IT

> Wonke umntu onolwazi kufuneka azi ngeBitcoin kuba inokuba yenye yezona zinto zibalulekileyo ezenzekileyo ehlabathini.  
_Lwazi_



#### 5.1.0 Intshayelelo

Iinkokeli ze-IT zinoxanduva kwishishini, zisebenzisa itekhnoloji ukukhuthaza izinto ezintsha kunye nokhuphiswano kwiinkampani zabo, kwakunye nokufumana iindlela zokuphucula ukusebenza ngaphakathi ukuze kuncitshiswe iindleko.

Kukho imingcipheko eqhelekileyo kunye nokuqonda okungachanekanga malunga neBitcoin okubalulekileyo ukuqonda nokunika isikhokelo:

* Idla ngokubonwa njengenxalenye yomzi-mveliso omkhulu we-‘crypto’, kunye nezisombululo ezisekelwe kwenye yezinto ezintsha eziphambili ezisetyenziswa, i-blockchain.
* Ibonwa njengento ‘echitha amandla’ ukuqhuba inethiwekhi.
* Kukho umngcipheko kubasebenzisi bePublic Cloud wokuba imeko-bume ingahlaselwa kwaye isetyenziswe ‘ukumba’ iBitcoin okanye ezinye iimali ze-crypto ngumntu onobungozi, nto leyo enokukhokelela kwityala elikhulu nelingalindelekanga, kwakunye nefuthe ekusebenzeni kwezicelo zeshishini.
* Kukho ukuswela ulwazi ngetekhnoloji esetyenziswa yiBitcoin.

Kwicala elilungileyo, iBitcoin inokuba luncedo kuyo nayiphi na inkampani ngokuthi:

* Yongezwe kwithala-mali njengempahla, nokuba ikumbiwa ngqo okanye ithengwe kwimarike evulekileyo.
* Ukusebenzisa izixhobo ezingasetyenziswanga ukumba iBitcoin yenkampani.
* Ukubonelela ngesizathu sokutyala imali kwizisombululo ze-AI ezidinga izixhobo zokubala ezinamandla afanayo.
* Ukongeza indlela yokuhlawula enye ekuthengeni iinkonzo okanye iimveliso zenkampani.
* Ukunciphisa iindleko zokuguqula imali zamazwe ngamazwe.
* Ukubonelela ngelinye inqaku lomenzi wenzuzo kubasebenzi ngokusebenzisa iintlawulo ze-Lightning.
* Ukwakha imithombo emitsha yengeniso yenkampani esekwe kwiBitcoin.

Nayiphi na inkokeli ye-IT kufuneka ithathe ixesha lokuqonda iBitcoin, nefuthe elinokubakho kunye nemingcipheko enxulumene nayo, kwakunye neenzuzo ezinokubakho, ukuze ikwazi ukunika isikhokelo nobunkokeli kwishishini.

> I-Bitcoin yimpumelelo enkulu kwicandelo le-cryptography. Ukukwazi ukudala into engenakuphindwa kwihlabathi ledijithali kunexabiso elikhulu. Abantu abaninzi baza kwakha amashishini phezu koko.  
_Eric Schmidt_



#### 5.1.1 Imingcipheko kunye nokuqonda okungachanekanga malunga neBitcoin

##### I-Bitcoin njengenxalenye yomzi-mveliso omkhulu we-‘crypto’.

I-Bitcoin yayiyinzame yokuqala ephumeleleyo yokudala impahla yedijithali enomda kwaye iye yazala umzi-mveliso opheleleyo we-‘alt-coins’ ezizama ukusebenzisa eminye yetekhnoloji esisiseko ukuzama ‘ukuphucula’ iBitcoin okanye ukwakha isisombululo sokujolisa kwezinye iimarike ezinokubakho.

Ababoneleli be-cloud yoluntu baye badala amaqonga e-blockchain ukuvumela iinkampani zakhe ezi zisombululo, kodwa umdla kwezi uhlala unyuka kwaye wehle ngokuhambelana nokukhankanywa kweshishini, eneneni iMicrosoft yayeka inkonzo yayo ye-blockchain ngo-2021.

* Kude kube ngu-2017, iBitcoin yayimela ukuya kuthi ga kwi-95% yomthamo wemali we-crypto iyonke.
* Izaqhwithi zokuqala ze-‘alternative’ crypto ICOs zabangela ukuba ulawulo lweBitcoin lwehle lufikelele kwelona nqanaba lisezantsi le-37.6%.
* Njengoko ezi zingazange zize nezibonelelo zeshishini ezibonakalayo kwaye zaqala ukuphela, ulawulo lweBitcoin lwaqala ukunyuka kwakhona.
* Ngo-2021, njengoko ixabiso leBitcoin lanyuka, kwavela izaqhwithi ezintsha zezisombululo ezisekwe kwi-NFT ezaphinda zanciphisa ulawulo lweBitcoin.
* Emva kokuba olu khankanyo luphinde lwasilela ukuvelisa izibonelelo ezibonakalayo, ulawulo lweBitcoin luqalile ukunyuka kwakhona.
* Xa iBitcoin ingena kwenye imarike yokunyuka, kunokwenzeka ukuba kuvele izaqhwithi ezintsha ze-‘alt-coins’ ukuzama ukuzuza kwindlela entsha, kodwa kusenokwenzeka ukuba ziya kufumana isiphelo esifanayo.

Ukuba ishishini liza kwinkokeli ye-IT nezinto ezintsha ezikhuthazwa njengesisombululo esithile se-blockchain, okanye njengenye indlela kwiBitcoin, kubalulekile ukukhumbula le ndlela kwaye ubuze:

* Yintoni injongo ye-blockchain?
* Ngaba i-blockchain iyafuneka okanye iyathandeka kwaphela, xa kuthelekiswa nokusebenza kwayo xa ithelekiswa ne-database yerelational egxile embindini?
* Ngubani onokutshintsha iprotocol kwaye yintoni impembelelo yayo kwisombululo?
* Zeziphi izinto ezinikwe umngcipheko ukhuseleko okanye ukungagqibekanga ukuze kufezekiswe ukusebenza okukhankanyiweyo?
* Yeyiphi inzuzo enokubonelelwa engakwazi ukunikwa yiBitcoin kunye neprotocols ezinxulumene nayo, ezifana ne-Lightning?

##### Iziphumo zokuqhuba iBitcoin

Kuxhomekeke kwicandelo elisebenza kulo ishishini, kunokubakho izibonelelo ezivela kumsebenzi wokumba iBitcoin, ngakumbi apho ubushushu obuveliswayo bunokusetyenziswa ngendlela eluncedo. Nangona kunjalo, rhoqo kukho ukuchasa ngenxa yokuba iBitcoin ibonwa njengengalunganga kwindalo, ichitha amandla okanye ingenanzuzo.

Njengenkokeli ye-IT, ukuqonda le meko kunye nokuba kukho izibonelelo ezinokubakho kwinkampani kuyimfuneko ukuze unike isikhokelo esisebenzayo. Imbono eqhelekileyo ibikukuba iBitcoin ayilunganga kwindalo kwaye ichitha amandla. Kodwa oku kuyatshintsha ngokukhawuleza, iingxelo ezininzi ezilungileyo ziyapapashwa, kwaye eli bali kulindeleke ukuba litshintshe libe yile apho ukumba iBitcoin kunokubonwa njengeluncedo kwindalo nakutshintsho oluya kwimithombo yamandla ehlaziyekayo. Le yinto iinkampani ezininzi ezijolise kuyo kwiimfuno zabo ze-ESG.

##### Ukuqonda imingcipheko enokubakho yokumba i-crypto

Kube nemizekelo kwixesha elidlulileyo apho imeko-bume yePublic cloud elawulwa yinkampani ithathiwe ngabaqhacker, abakwazi ngokukhawuleza ukuvulela izixhobo zokubala ezongezelelweyo ukuze bakumbe i-crypto. Okukhona izixhobo ezinamandla aphezulu (ezibiza kakhulu) zivuliwe, kokukhona amathuba omqhacker okumba iBitcoin okanye enye i-crypto. Oku kubizwa ngokuba yi-‘Crypto-Jacking’ - ngokwe-AWS:

‘Le yintlobo yolwaphulo-mthetho lwe-cyber equka ukusetyenziswa okungagunyaziswanga kwezixhobo (iikhompyutha ezikwi-edge, iiselfowuni, iitafilethi, okanye iiseva) ukumba i-cryptocurrency. Njengoko amaxabiso e-cryptocurrency enyuka kwaye izixhobo ezinamandla ezise-edge ezine-GPU zisetyenziswa ukuqhuba iimeko zokusebenzisa i-Machine Learning kwi-edge, umngcipheko wokuba abaphangi basebenzise iziphene zokhuseleko kwizixhobo ze-edge uyanda. Xa oku kwenzeka, izixhobo zokubala ze-edge zisetyenziswa ukumba i-crypto currency, nto leyo ekhokelela ekusebenziseni okuphezulu kwe-CPU/GPU kunye nokuncipha kokusebenza kwezicelo ze-edge kunye nokunyuka kwexesha lokuqhuba i-ML kwi-edge.’

Kubalulekile ke ngoko ukuba nasiphi na ukusetyenziswa kwezixhobo ze-public cloud kuyilwe kakuhle ngokulandela ezona ndlela zibalaseleyo. Ezi zihlala zichazwe ngendlela ethile ye-Cloud Adoption Framework, echaza iingcebiso malunga nokhuseleko, ukusebenza, ukongamela, ukomelela kunye nemisebenzi. Inkokeli ye-IT kufuneka iqinisekise ukuba ezi ziyalandelwa, kwaye ukuba uhlobo oluthile lokongamela ngexesha langempela luvuliwe ukuze kuchongwe kwaye kuthintelwe olu hlaselo phambi kokuba kubekho iindleko ezinkulu.

##### Ukuswela ulwazi ngetekhnoloji esetyenziswa yiBitcoin

Kukho ukuqonda okungachanekanga okuninzi malunga netekhnoloji esetyenziswa yiBitcoin, okuholela kwimibuzo yokuba ingaba inokugqunywa, amandla asetyenziswa, okanye ukuba ingagqithwa nguhlobo olutsha njengoko kusoloko kwenzeka ngetekhnoloji. Njengomphathi wezobuchwephesha, ukuba nolwazi ngetekhnoloji esisiseko kuya kuba luncedo ukubeka iBitcoin kakuhle ngaphakathi kwenkampani ukuze kuthotywe ezi nkxalabo.



#### 5.1.2 Kwicala elilungileyo.

##### I-Bitcoin njengempahla yethala-mali

Kukho izibonelelo zemali ezinokubakho kwinkampani efaka iBitcoin kwiincwadi zayo njengempahla yethala-mali.

* Ukugcina ixabiso kunye nokuzikhusela kukunyuka kwamaxabiso
* Ukuncipha kokuthembela kwiimali ze-fiat njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso
* Umngcipheko womntu wesithathu kwicandelo leebhanki
* Uncedo lokuqala kwishishini ekongezweni kweBitcoin kwimali yalo yokugcina

Nangona iziphumo zemali zokwenza oku ngokwembono ye-accountancy zingeloxanduva lomphathi we-IT, ukuqonda indlela oku kunokusebenza ngayo kunye nendlela iBitcoin enokuthengwa, igcinwe kwaye ikhuseleke kuya kuba yinxalenye yomsebenzi wakhe.

Zikhona iinkonzo ezikhoyo kwimarike eziza kunceda ekuthengeni, ekugcineni nasekubolekiseni ngokuchasene neempahla ezigciniweyo. Ukuba le ngxoxo iza phakathi kwenkampani, kwaye kufuneka, umphathi we-IT anganceda ekuqinisekiseni iinkampani zomntu wesithathu ezinikezela ngezi nkonzo. Ukuqinisekisa ukuba iinkonzo ezinikezelwayo zihlangabezana neemfuno zokhuseleko, ukucaca kunye neempawu zenkonzo kuya kunceda ekukhetheni iqabane elithembekileyo.

##### Izibonelelo zokumba iBitcoin

Ukusetyenziswa kweendawo zokugcina idatha kulindeleke ukuba kunyuke kwixesha elizayo, kwaye inxalenye enkulu yeendleko zokusebenza kweendawo zokugcina idatha kukuphelisa ubushushu obuveliswayo. Oku kuyinyani ngakumbi kwizicelo ezisebenzisa iikhompyutha ezinamandla aphezulu, ezifana ne-AI/ML kunye nokumba iBitcoin.

Iinkampani kumacandelo ahlukeneyo kwihlabathi liphela ziye zafumanisa indlela le ntshukumo yobushushu enokuthi ibe luncedo kwishishini endaweni yokuba yindleko ngokusebenzisa ubushushu obuvelisiweyo kwi:

* Iipuli zokuqubha/iiSpas
* Iziko lemidlalo yamanzi
* Ukukhulisa iintyatyambo/izityalo kwiigreenhouse
* Ukufudumeza izakhiwo zeshishini kunye neenkqubo zamanzi ashushu

Oku kunokufezekiswa ngokusebenza kunye nenkampani yokumba iBitcoin esebenzisa izakhiwo zeshishini ukumba iBitcoin ngenjongo yenzuzo kwaye inike ubushushu bokusetyenziswa ngokubanzi, okanye

ishishini linokwenza lo msebenzi ngokwalo ukuze lakhe imali yokugcina yeBitcoin ngqo. Le ndlela inokunceda inkampani nakwiinzame zayo ze-ESG.

Indlela engathanga ngqo yokufumana ezinye zezi zibonelelo kukuhambisa iimfuno zokubala kwiindawo zokugcina idatha ezisebenzisa le ndlela kwaye zinike indawo yeerakhi okanye iziseko njengenkonzo elawulwayo kwaye ezidlulisela ezinye zezi ndleko ezigcinwayo kwishishini.

Njengomphathi we-IT, ukuhlala usesikhathini kweli candelo kuya kukunceda ukhokelise inkampani ekwamkeleni izisombululo ezinokunceda ishishini.

##### Bitcoin kunye ne-AI

Ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI kunye ne-ML kulindeleke ukuba kunyuke kakhulu kwiminyaka ezayo. Indibaniselwano yeBitcoin kunye noBukrelekrele bokwenziwa ikhokelela kwixesha elitsha leentshukumo zedijithali, ngakumbi ngokudityaniswa kwe-AI kunye neLightning Network yeBitcoin. Le manyano ilindeleke ukuba itshintshe imiba ye-intanethi, ukusuka kwiintlawulo ezincinci ukuya kwi-arhente zoqoqosho ezikhokelwa yi-AI kwi-intanethi.

* Ukulungisa iimodeli ze-AI kakuhle yinyathelo elibalulekileyo kuphuhliso lwe-AI – iintlawulo ezincinci usebenzisa iLightning zinokwenza abantu kwihlabathi liphela bakwazi ukuhlawulwa ngomsebenzi ngamnye kwiBitcoin, kukhuthaza ukuthatha inxaxheba.
* Kwiimeko apho iiarhente ze-AI zenza iintengiselwano ngeenkonzo, iLightning network sisixhobo esibalulekileyo kwimisebenzi yoqoqosho ekhokelwa yi-AI apho isantya sibalulekile.
* Xa inkqubo ye-AI sele iphuhliswe, iintlawulo ezincinci zinokunceda ekudaleni imodeli yokuhlawula ngokusetyenziswa kuphela apho abasebenzisi behlawula kuphela ngezixhobo ze-AI abazisebenzisayo.

Nakuphi na ishishini elicinga ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI, nokuba njengenkonzo ekufuneka ihlawulwe okanye kuphuhliso lwesisombululo sayo se-AI, kubalulekile ukuqonda indlela iBitcoin kunye neLightning edityaniswa ngayo kwaye yongeza ixabiso kuso nasiphi na isisombululo.

##### Iindlela ezintsha zokuhlawula ezithengiswayo

Nasiphi na ishishini esamkelayo iintlawulo ngeenkonzo, nokuba ngqo kwiindawo zokuthengisa okanye kwi-intanethi, sinokuxhamla ekwamkeleni iintlawulo zeBitcoin ngezi ndlela zilandelayo:

* Ukonyuka kwabathengi kunye nokukhula kweshishini ngokujolisa kubathengi beBitcoin
* Iintlawulo eziphantsi kakhulu okanye ezingenazo kwaphela
* Intlawulo ekhawulezayo
* Akukho zimbuyekezo zentlawulo (Chargebacks)

Ukusuka kumbono we-accounting, zikhona iindlela ezahlukeneyo zokuphatha iBitcoin efunyenweyo, ukuyigcina njengemali yokugcina okanye ukuyiguqulela ngqo kwi-fiat, okanye indibaniselwano njengoko kuvunyelwene. Kuxhomekeke kwishishini, kungabakho imiba yezobugcisa ekufuneka yenziwe, njengamatheminali amatsha ePOS okanye ukudityaniswa nesoftware yokuhlawula kwi-intanethi, ekufuneka umphathi we-IT aqonde imiphumo yezobugcisa ukuba oku kuvunyelwene njengenjongo yeshishini.

##### Ukunciphisa iintlawulo zokuguqula imali zamazwe ngamazwe

Kwiinkampani ezidlulisela imali eninzi kumazwe ngamazwe, iindleko kunye nobunzima bokwenza oku zihlala zinzima. Izisombululo ezintsha ezisekelwe kwiBitcoin ziye zanikezelwa kwimarike ezinciphisa ezi ndleko kwaye zinike intlawulo ekhawulezayo nengxamisekileyo. Ukwamkela ezi nkonzo kunokuzisela ishishini izibonelelo, kwaye ukuqonda le marike, iinkonzo ezinikezelwayo kunye nokusebenza neqela le-accounting ukufaka isisombululo esingcono kufuna inqanaba lolwazi lobugcisa kunye nokuqonda onokuthi umphathi we-IT anike.

##### Izibonelelo zabasebenzi

Uninzi lweenkampani zijonge indlela ezinokuthi zinike izibonelelo kunye neenkuthazo kubasebenzi bazo ukuphucula ukuqesha nokugcina abasebenzi. Kutshanje, abadlali abaziwayo bebhola kunye nezopolitiko babhengeze ukuba bafumana imivuzo yabo yonke okanye inxenye kwiBitcoin. Ukukwazi ukuhlawula inxenye yomvuzo kwiBitcoin kuya kuba yinto ebalulekileyo kumqeshi xa oku sele kuba ngumahluko ekufumaneni italente ephambili, okanye xa amalungu aphambili eqela eqala ukucela oku okanye aqale ukukhangela abaqeshi abaninika le ndlela kwenye indawo.

* Ukudibanisa ukhetho lokuhlawula umvuzo wonke okanye inxenye kwiBitcoin kunika umbutho ithuba lokhuphiswano lokuba phambili. Izisombululo zomvuzo weBitcoin zenza le nkqubo ibe lula ukuyidibanisa.
* Iinkampani ezifana neMicroStrategy zijonge iindlela zokukhuthaza ukusebenza kakuhle okanye nokuzimasa iintlanganiso ngeentlawulo ezincinci ezisekelwe kwiLightning.

Nangona isigqibo sokuphumeza ezi zibonelelo zabasebenzi singekho ngqo kumphathi we-IT, ukubonelela ngokuqonda ukuba kutheni oku kungaba luncedo, izisombululo ezikhoyo zokufezekisa oku, kunye nemiphumo yezobugcisa yokuphumeza kunokuba ngumsebenzi wakhe. Abalawuli be-IT abaphakamisa izimvo kwangaphambili koogxa babo banokubonisa ixabiso labo elibanzi kwisicwangciso seshishini.

##### Amathuba amatsha emarike

Njengoko isicatshulwa esivela kuGoogle sitshilo, amashishini amaninzi aza kujonga ukwakha imithombo emitsha yengeniso esekelwe kwinkqubo ekhula yeBitcoin. Oku kunokuvulela ishishini imarike ezintsha ekufuneka ziqwalaselwe, apho umphathi we-IT angaba yinxalenye ebalulekileyo ekuvavanyeni ukufaneleka, imingeni yezobugcisa kunye namathuba anokubakho.



#### 5.1.3 Isishwankathelo

Kulindeleke ukuba iBitcoin iqhubeke nendlela yayo yokwamkelwa, ibe yinto ebalulekileyo kumashishini ngokuhamba kwexesha, ichaphazela ishishini, izicwangciso zobugcisa kunye neenzame kwiindawo ezininzi. Njengomphathi wezobugcisa kwinkampani, umphathi we-IT uya kuxhamla ngokuba phambili kwezi ntshukumo, ebonelela ngengcebiso kwinkampani, kwaye eqinisekisa ukuba ifumana izibonelelo ezinkulu ekuphumezeni izisombululo zeBitcoin kulo lonke umbutho.
