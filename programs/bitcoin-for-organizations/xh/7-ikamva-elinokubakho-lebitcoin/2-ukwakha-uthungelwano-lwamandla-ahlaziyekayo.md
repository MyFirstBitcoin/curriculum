# 7.2 Ukwakha uthungelwano lwamandla ahlaziyekayo

#### 7.2.0: Intshayelelo

I-Bitcoin ixhomekeke kumandla ombane kwindlela yayo ye-‘proof of work’ yokuvumelana, nto leyo enceda ukuqinisekisa ukuba ihlala iyimali engaphantsi kolawulo, engenamvume. Umnatha wombane ujongene nemingeni yokudibanisa iindlela ezintsha zamandla avela kwimithombo ehlaziyekayo, nto leyo ebeka uxinzelelo kwiinfrastructures ezikhoyo ngoku. Eli sahluko linika intshayelelo emfutshane kule mingeni kunye nenkcazo yezinto ezibalulekileyo malunga neBitcoin phambi kokubonisa indlela enceda ngayo kuguquko lomnatha wamandla ohlaziyekayo.


> **Dark – I-Bitcoin njengemali yamandla**
>
> Ngomhla we-4 kuDisemba 1921, iNew York Tribune yapapasha inqaku elichaza umbono kaFord wokutshintsha igolide ngemali yamandla awayekholelwa ukuba ingaphula ulawulo lwabantu abakhulu bamashishini ebhanki kubutyebi behlabathi kwaye iphelise iimfazwe. Wayehlela ukwenza oku ngokwakha "eyona planti yamandla inkulu ehlabathini" kunye nokudala inkqubo entsha yemali esekwe "kwiiyunithi zamandla."


Njengoko uHenry Ford wayebonile, iBitcoin isebenzisa amandla ombane ukudala nokukhusela imali ngokuzimeleyo ngaphandle kolawulo lukarhulumente okanye amashishini amakhulu. Oku kuyenza ibe yimali yokuqala yokwenene yehlabathi engaphantsi kolawulo. Indlela iBitcoin mining esebenza ngayo – igama elinikezelwa kwinkqubo yokudala nokongeza iibhloko ezintsha kwinethiwekhi – inemidlalo enkulu kwaye ikhuthaza uluntu lweBitcoin mining ukuba lufune imithombo yamandla engabizi kakhulu. Ikwakwazi nokuzivumelanisa ngokukhawuleza ngokunyusa okanye ukunciphisa ukusetyenziswa kwamandla kwiindawo ezithile. Le mpawu inokuba luncedo kakhulu kumnatha wombane osebenzisa imithombo yamandla ehlaziyekayo.

##### Ukubaluleka koPhuhliso loMnatha waMandla oHlaziyekayo

Uxinzelelo lokusebenzisa imithombo yamandla ehlaziyekayo luzisa imingeni emitsha kubalawuli bomnatha wombane, njengokungaqinisekanga kunye nokusasazeka kwemithombo yamandla, iindawo ezixineneyo zokuhambisa umbane kunye nemida yokugcina amandla ekhoyo ngoku. Oku kongeza ubunzima ekusebenzeni komnatha wombane, into eyayingekho xa kusetyenziswa kuphela imithombo yamandla eziko ezithembekileyo nezilawulwayo. Ukuze bakwazi ukujongana nale mingeni, abalawuli bomnatha kufuneka baphonononge itekhnoloji ye-smart grid kunye neetekhnoloji zokubikezela ezisekwe kwi-AI ukuphucula ukusebenza. Enye indlela esetyenziswa namhlanje ziinkqubo zokuphendula kwimfuno, ezidinga umthombo wamandla onokuzivumelanisa ngokukhawuleza phantse ngexesha langempela ukuze kulungelelaniswe imfuno kunye nokubonelela. Apha ke iBitcoin mining inokuba luncedo.



#### 7.2.1 Imingeni yoDibaniso lwaMandla oHlaziyekayo

Abalawuli bomnatha kufuneka bahlale belungelelanisa ukubonelela kunye nemfuno yombane. Ukuba imfuno yombane inyuka kakhulu, umnatha unokungasebenzi, nto leyo ekhokelela ekucimeni kombane okungxamisekileyo okanye ukucima kombane okukhulu.

Ukuba umbane ungaphezulu kakhulu kunoko kufunekayo kumnatha, oku nako kungabangela iingxaki ezifana nokunyuka kobushushu kunye nomonakalo kwi-infrastructure. Kwimeko ezinzima, oku kungakhokelela ekucinyweni kombane ngokuzenzekelayo, okubangela uchungechunge lweengxaki kumnatha kwaye kudale ukucima kombane okungxamisekileyo okanye okukhulu. Ukucima kombane okukhulu ziziganeko ezinzima kakhulu ezibiza amashishini izigidi zeerandi. Zikwabiza nobomi babantu.

##### Imeko ye-Infrastructure yangoku

I-infrastructure yomnatha wombane yanamhlanje ilungiselelwe imithombo yamandla yemveli efana namalahle, igesi okanye i-nuclear enokubonelela ngokuhamba kombane okuzinzileyo, okuphakathi kunye nokulawulwayo ukuze kuhlangatyezwane nemfuno. Oku kwenza ukuba kube lula ukulinganisa ukubonelela kunye nemfuno. Ngokungeniswa kwamandla ohlaziyekayo, umnatha ngoku kufuneka ulawule iintlobo ezahlukeneyo zamandla ezisasazekileyo, zonke ezisebenza ngendlela eyahlukileyo kwimithombo yamandla awayeyilwe ngayo. Imithombo ehlaziyekayo efana nomoya kunye nelanga ivelisa umbane ngamaxesha athile kuphela. Umzekelo, xa kungekho moya, ifama yomoya inokuvelisa umbane omncinane okanye ingavelisi kwaphela, kanti xa kukho imimoya emikhulu, iiturbine zinokugcwalisa umnatha ngombane ongaphezulu. Iinkqubo zomnatha zangoku azilungelanga ukujongana nale mingeni yokuguquguquka kakuhle.

##### Impendulo kwimfuno

Zimbini iindlela ezinokuthathwa ngabalawuli bomnatha ukuze bajongane nokuguquguquka kokubonelela kunye nemfuno:

* Yakha izikhululo zamandla zemveli (ezisekwe kumalahle okanye igesi) ezinokugcinwa zimi ngelixa zixabisa imali. Xa imfuno inyuka ngokungalindelekanga, ezi zikhululo zingavulwa ukuze zibonelele ngombane owongezelelweyo.
* Yakha imithombo yamandla ohlaziyekayo engaphezulu koko kufunekayo uze usebenzise izicwangciso zokunciphisa ukuveliswa kombane ukuze ungagcwalisi umnatha xa kuveliswa umbane omninzi ngaphandle kwemfuno eyaneleyo.

Enye indlela kukuzama ukunciphisa imfuno ngexesha apho kusetyenziswa umbane kakhulu**.** Nangona kunjalo, abalawuli bomnatha babengazange bafumane indlela ethembekileyo, ekhawulezayo, enokwandiswa yokunciphisa imfuno phambi kokuba kufike iBitcoin mining, nto leyo ebashiya bengekho kwenye indlela ngaphandle kokutyala imali kwizityalo ezimi ngelixa okanye ukuhlawula imithombo yamandla ohlaziyekayo ukuba icime – zombini ezi ndlela zibiza kakhulu.

##### Amandla ashiyekileyo

Imingeni yokudibanisa iifama zomoya kumnatha wombane idla ngokubandakanya amanyathelo amaninzi; uphononongo lokufikelela, uhlalutyo oluneenkcukacha zempembelelo, isicwangciso sokuphumeza kunye nesivumelwano sokudibanisa. Le nkqubo inokuba neminyaka emininzi. Umzekelo, umzobo ongezantsi ubonisa umthamo opheleleyo weefama zomoya ezilinde uvavanyo lomnatha phakathi konyaka ka-2024.

##### Uqhakamshelwano lomnatha

Emva kokuba imithombo yamandla ohlaziyekayo yakhiwe, kuhlala kukho ulibaziseko lokuyidibanisa ngenxa yokungabikho komthamo owaneleyo kumnatha. Oku kukhokelela ekubeni amandla ashiyekileyo achitheke de kube kudityaniswa, ngeli xesha amandla anokuthi avele asetyenziswe ukuqhuba iBitcoin miners kwaye kuveliswe ingeniso.

##### Ukuveliswa okungaphezulu kunye nokunciphisa

Xa lo mthamo sele wongezwe kumnatha, ingxaki ijika ibe kukunciphisa ukuveliswa kombane. Xa umoya uvelisa amandla amaninzi kunoko kufunekayo ngexesha elithile, akukho teknoloji ikhoyo ngoku yokuwugcina, ngoko la mandla achitheka nje. Ukuze bathathe umngcipheko wokwakha iifama zomoya, abalawuli bafumana ixabiso eliqinisekisiweyo kuyo yonke amandla avelisiweyo, ngoko ke ukuze bangagcwalisi umnatha, bahlawulwa ukuba bacime iiturbine zomoya. Umzekelo, abathengi base-UK bahlawule i-£1bn ngo-2024 ukuze "kuncitshiswe" i-6.6 Gwh yomthamo.

Enye indlela yokunciphisa ukuveliswa kombane kukusebenzisa izityalo zamandla ezisebenzisa igesi (gas peaker plants). Le yiplanti yamandla esebenzisa igesi yendalo ukuvelisa umbane ngexesha lemfuno ephezulu. Zikwasebenza ukulinganisa umnatha wombane ngokuvula umbane xa imfuno iphezulu okanye ukubonelela kusezantsi. Njengoko igama lazo libonisa, zisetyenziswa kuphela ngexesha lemfuno ephezulu, kodwa kufuneka zihlale zifakelwe kwaye zigcinwe, ngoko ke zihlala 'zincitshisiwe' ixesha elininzi zimi ngelixa. Xa imfuno iphezulu, umphathi womnatha angazisebenzisa ukunyusa ukubonelela. Umzekelo, ukusetyenziswa kweBitcoin mining endaweni yokuthenga nokusebenza kwezi zityalo kuthiwa kugcine iTexas i-$18Bn.

##### Uhlaziyo lomnatha wombane

I-Smart grids zakhiwa ukuze zilawule olu hlobo lwemithombo yamandla ehlukeneyo, zidityanisa ngokungenamthungo imithombo yemveli efana namafutha kunye neendlela zale mihla zamandla ohlaziyekayo kumnatha omnye osebenzayo. Ngokusebenzisa itekhnoloji ephambili efana nokugcinwa kwamandla kwiibhetri, i-smart grids zinokugcina umbane ongaphezulu kwaye ziwukhuphe xa kufuneka, nto leyo enokunceda ekulawuleni ukuguquguquka kwamandla ohlaziyekayo njengamaxesha okuveliswa okuninzi okanye ukusilela xa kuveliswa kancinci. Ngexesha lokubhala oku, oku kusekwiqela lokuqala lophuhliso.

##### Inkqubela phambili yetekhnoloji

Ulawulo kunye nohlalutyo zibalulekile ekuphunyezweni kwe-smart grids kwinqanaba elikhulu. Oku kuqala ngokufakwa kweesensor kunye netekhnoloji yokubeka iliso kwisiza apho umbane uveliswa khona. Isofthiwe yohlalutyo emva koko ihlalutya kwaye ibikezele iindlela esekwe kwidatha eqokelelwe ngolu lawulo, inike iziluleko kwiingxaki zempilo yesityalo ezifana nokucima okanye ukusilela ukuze ilungiselele i-smart grid kwezi meko. Iimetre ezikrelekrele (smart meters) zisisiphelo sokuqokelelwa kwedatha, zibeka iliso ekusetyenzisweni kombane ngabathengi kwindawo yemvelaphi. Ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI kulindeleke ukuba kuncede ekulawuleni obu bunzima, ngoko abalawuli bomnatha kufuneka baphucule izakhono zabo kule ndawo.

##### Isishwankathelo

Ukukhawuleza kukarhulumente ukufaka amandla ohlaziyekayo kumnatha wombane kubeka uxinzelelo kuyilo lomnatha okhoyo, kwaye kufuna utyalo-mali olukhulu ukuze kujongwe imvelaphi esasazekileyo kunye nokuguquguquka kwemithombo yamandla ohlaziyekayo. Uyilo lwangoku luchitha umbane kakhulu, lonyusa iindleko kumashishini nakubathengi. Uninzi lweetekhnoloji ezifunekayo ukuze oku kuphumelele zisaphuhliswa ngexesha lokubhala. Kufuneka kufunyanwe isisombululo esingcono.



#### 7.2.2 Intshayelelo kwiBitcoin Mining

##### Yintoni iBitcoin mining?

I-Bitcoin mining yinkqubo esetyenziswa ukuvelisa iiyunithi ezintsha ze-bitcoin kunye nokuqinisekisa iintengiselwano ezintsha. Ibandakanya inethiwekhi yeekhompyutha kwihlabathi lonke eziqinisekisa kwaye zikhusele i-blockchain – incwadi yedijithali egcina zonke iintengiselwano kwaye isombulula ingxaki ye-‘double-spend’ apho imali efanayo inokusetyenziswa kabini.

Abavukuzi beBitcoin ziikhompyutha ezisebenzisa ii-ASICs ezikhethekileyo (application specific integrated circuit) ukudala iibhloko ezintsha ezinokubakho kwaye bafumane ithuba lokongeza ibhloko entsha kwincwadi ngokwenza isisombululo se-cryptography esihlangabezana nemigangatho ethile. Okukhona abavukuzi besebenza kwinethiwekhi, kokukhona kunzima ukufumana esi sisombululo, nto leyo elungiswa ngokuzenzekelayo yinxalenye yeprotocol ebizwa ngokuba yi-difficulty adjustment. Umvuzo wokongeza ibhloko entsha kukwabelwa ngeemali ezintsha kunye nemirhumo yebhloko kumvukuzi ophumeleleyo.

Olu khuphiswano lokudala ibhloko elandelayo kunye nokufumana imivuzo lwenze inethiwekhi enkulu, esasazekileyo yabavukuzi abafuna umbane ongabizi ukuze bakhuphisane, kwaye luzisa umdla omtsha kumzamo wokongeza amandla ohlaziyekayo kumnatha wombane.

##### Ingxoxo malunga nokusetyenziswa kwamandla

Njengoko kuchaziwe kwintshayelelo, iBitcoin mining inxulunyaniswa nokusetyenziswa kwamandla kwihlabathi lokwenyani. Oku kusetyenziswa kwamandla sele kubekwe kwiindaba iminyaka emininzi. Ihlala igxekwa ngokusebenzisa amandla amaninzi, ingawasebenzisi kakuhle, okanye kwezinye iimeko, ithathwa njengengxaki enkulu yemozulu/nombane. Nangona kunjalo, inethiwekhi yeBitcoin iya kuhlala iyimpazamo encinci xa kuthelekiswa nokusetyenziswa kwamandla kwihlabathi, nokuba iyaphumelela okanye ayiphumeleli, kwaye ukusetyenziswa kwayo kwamandla akuyi kudlula ixabiso layo lexesha elide (kungakhathaliseki ukuba liphezulu okanye lisezantsi). Njengoko siza kubona, iimpawu ezithile zokusetyenziswa kwayo kwamandla zinokunceda ekwamkelweni kwamandla ohlaziyekayo.

Le nethiwekhi yamandla yehlabathi ikhulula izixhobo ezishiyekileyo kwaye yenza ezintsha zibe semthethweni. Ngalo naliphi na ixesha, oomatshini bokumba iBitcoin kwihlabathi lonke bajonge ukwenza ibhloko elandelayo, kwaye kuba eyona ndleko inkulu kubagcini-migodi yiombane, oku kudala ukhuphiswano phakathi kwabagcini-migodi ukuba bafumane kwaye basebenzise amandla avela kwimithombo yombane engabizi naphi na bakhoyo. Abantu bahlala becinga ukuba abagcini-migodi beBitcoin bakhuphisana namanye amashishini ngombane, ngathi ukumba iBitcoin kufuneka kukhuphe ezinye iindlela zokusebenzisa umbane ukuze kusebenze... Nangona kunjalo, kuba abagcini-migodi beBitcoin bafuna imithombo yombane engabizi kakhulu ngokwendalo, _abakwazi_ukukhuphisana ngokuqhelekileyo nabanye abasebenzisi bombane. Ngenxa yoko, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin bafuna iindawo ezineengxaki kwihlabathi apho umbane ungasebenzisi kakuhle kwaye ulahlekwa. Oku kuchazwe kakuhle ngo-2018 nguNic Carter.

##### Ukuguquguquka kwemfuno kwiMisebenzi yoKumba

> Abagcini-migodi beBitcoin ngabathengi bamandla abahlukileyo kuba banikezela ngemfuno eguquguqukayo kwaye kulula ukuyimisa, bahlawula ngemali ye-cryptocurrency evakalayo kwihlabathi, kwaye abaxhomekekanga ndawo, bafuna kuphela unxibelelwano lwe-intanethi. Ezi mpawu zidibeneyo zenza ukuba babe luhlobo olungaqhelekanga lwabathengi bamandla, abathengi bamandla bokugqibela abanokucinywa okanye bavulwe nangaliphi na ixesha naphi na emhlabeni.  
_Jack Dorsey_

Ukongeza ekubeni banokuhamba naphi na, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin banokubonelela ngokuguquguquka kwemfuno. Ukumba iBitcoin kwenza kube yinzuzo ukwakha imithombo yamandla ahlaziyekayo egqithisileyo, kuba oku kuvumela loo mandla angasetyenziswanga ukuba afumane ingeniso. Wonke uluntu olufuna umbane othembekileyo lufuna amandla agqithisileyo, kwaye kumoya, ilanga kunye namanzi kubaluleke ngakumbi kuba ayaguquguquka. Kodwa, ukwakha ngaphezulu akuhlali kuyinzuzo, ngaphandle kokuba ungawusebenzisa loo mandla xa ungadingeki kwenye into enenzuzo. Abagcini-migodi beBitcoin sisombululo esikhethekileyo kule ngxaki, banokwenza ukwakha ngaphezulu kube yinzuzo, kwaye ngaloo ndlela badlale indima engathanga ngqo njengesisombululo sokugcina amandla.

Kwiimeko ezininzi xa kukho umbane ongaphezulu kunemfuno, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin babe ngabanye babathengi bombane kuluntu abakwaziyo ukuqhuba oomatshini babo, bafumane ingeniso, kwaye bahlawule iindleko zombane. Ukuba kukho ukunyuka kwemfuno yombane okanye ukwehla kokubonelela okungakhokelela ekunqongopheni kombane kummandla, abo bagcini-migodi banokucima oomatshini babo okwethutyana.

Isivumelwano samaxabiso esilungiselelwe kakuhle sinokwenza oku kusebenze kakuhle. Inkampani yombane inganika umgodi ixabiso elisezantsi kakhulu kummandla, ngokutshintshiselana ngokuba babe nomonde omkhulu kwiinguqu zexabiso kunye nezinye iimeko zesivumelwano.

Ngamafutshane, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin bahlukile kuba:

* Phantse zonke iindleko zabo zokusebenza ziyiombane
* Bakwazi ukunyamezela ukusetyenziswa okungaqhelekanga
* Baguquguquka kwindawo, ngoko banokuphepha iindleko zokuhambisa umbane ngokuzisa isixhobo kufutshane nomthombo wombane.

Ngenxa yoko, banokunikela ngezinye izinto ezingenakwenziwa ngamanye amashishini, ukuze bafumane amaxabiso ombane asezantsi kakhulu xa umbane uninzi. Oku kuthetha ukuba ngokumba iBitcoin ngoku sinomthengi kuwo onke amawathi ombane aveliswayo, naphi na emhlabeni, imini nobusuku.



#### 7.2.3 Iimeko zophando

Ngokwe-theory, siyabona ukuba ukumba iBitcoin kunokudlala indima enkulu ekukhawuleziseni ukusetyenziswa kwamandla ahlaziyekayo. Masijonge eminye imizekelo yokusetyenziswa kwanamhlanje.

##### Amandla ombane avela emanzini angasetyenziswa

Izityalo zombane ezisebenzisa amanzi zivelisa umbane rhoqo, onokuthi uguquguquke unyaka wonke kuxhomekeke kwindawo nexesha lonyaka. Oku kuthetha ukuba umbane ulahlekwa ebusuku xa wonke umntu elele, okanye xa imveliso inyuka ngexesha lemvula, njengaseTshayina. Kuba abagcini-migodi beBitcoin banokuhamba ukuya apho kukho umbane, babe beqokelela eSichuan ngexesha lemvula ukuze basebenzise loo mandla ulahlekayo. Abazange benze oku ngenxa yokuba bengabakhathaleli indalo, koko kuba bekubiza kancinci kwaye akukho mntu wumbi uwusebenzisayo. Xa iTshayina ivale ukumba iBitcoin, bavele bapakisha baphuma.

Iidolophu okanye iilali ezikufutshane nemithombo yamandla ombane avela emanzini zihlala zingakwazi ukuhlawula utyalo-mali oluyimfuneko lokwakha izixhobo zokuhambisa umbane. Kule meko, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin banokufumana imali yokwakha isityalo, banike umbane ongabizi kubahlali basekuhlaleni baze basebenzise umbane oseleyo ukumba iBitcoin. Kwakhona, oku akwenziwa ngenxa yokunceda, koko ngenxa yengeniso; yinzuzo kubagcini-migodi nakuluntu lwasekuhlaleni.

##### Ukumba iBitcoin ukuzinzisa iGridi

Iigridi zombane kufuneka zilungelelanise izinto ezimbini: utshintsho kwinqanaba lokubonelela kunye notshintsho kwinqanaba lemfuno. Eminye imithombo yombane izinzile kakhulu, njengamandla enyukliya, anokusebenza iiyure ezingama-24/7. Eminye imithombo, njengamandla omoya, ilanga, kunye namanzi, iguquguquka ngokuxhomekeke kwindalo. Ngenxa yale nguquguquko, kufuneka kubekho umbane ogqithisileyo ukuze naxa imveliso iphantsi kube kusekho umbane owaneleyo kuluntu. ETexas, isicwangciso esiqhelekileyo yayikukwakha izityalo zamandla ezisebenzisa ifosili ukuze zilungelelanise ukunyuka kwemfuno. Enye indlela eyathathwayo yayikukongeza ukuguquguquka kwemfuno ngokudibanisa abagcini-migodi beBitcoin kwinethiwekhi. Le ndlela yongise iTexas izigidi ezininzi zotyalo-mali kwaye yanika enye indlela ekhathalela indalo.

##### Ezinye izibonelelo ezikufutshane

Nangona kungahambelani ngqo nengqalasizinda yamandla ahlaziyekayo, zikhona ezinye iindlela zokusebenzisa amandla apho ukumba iBitcoin kunokunceda khona:

* Igesi etshisiweyo: ukuthintela igesi ekuphunyeni okanye ekutshisweni ngokuyisebenzisa ukumba iBitcoin kwindawo.
* Igesi evela kumgqomo wenkunkuma: ukubamba imethane kumgqomo wenkunkuma kwaye uyisebenzise ukuvelisa umbane ukunciphisa ukukhutshwa kwegesi eyingozi
* Uphuhliso lwetekhnoloji entsha: Ukuguqulwa kwamandla obushushu bolwandle (OTEC) yindlela eyaziwayo yokusebenzisa umahluko wobushushu phakathi komphezulu nolwandle oluphantsi ukuvelisa umbane. Oku bekungazange kube yinzuzo ngaphambi kweBitcoin.
* Ukuqalisa uphuhliso lombane kumazwe asakhulayo: Njengoko kuchaziwe ngaphambili, abagcini-migodi beBitcoin banokuba 'ngabathengi abaphambili' abaya kuhlala besebenzisa umbane ovelisiweyo, benze utyalo-mali lube nentsingiselo baze bahambe xa uluntu lwasekuhlaleni luphuhlile kwaye lufuna umbane ngaphezulu.

##### Isishwankathelo

Ukumba iBitcoin kunokunceda ukuxhasa utyalo-mali kunye nokuqinisekisa uzinzo lwengqalasizinda yamandla ahlaziyekayo:

* Ukufunxa umbane ongaphezulu ngexesha lemfuno ephantsi
* Ukuqinisa igridi ngokulinganisa ukubonelela kunye nemfuno
* Ukunika umthombo wengeniso kubaphuhlisi bamandla ahlaziyekayo
* Ukuxhasa ngemali iiprojekthi zamandla ezikude okanye ezingafikeleliyo
* Ukutyhala imida yokusebenza kakuhle kwamandla
* Ukusebenza njengomthengi wokugqibela wombane oza kulahlekwa naphi na emhlabeni nangaliphi na ixesha



#### 7.2.4 Ukuphendula iinkxalabo

Sibonile ukuba ukumba iBitcoin kunokunceda ekukhuleni kwamandla ahlaziyekayo, kodwa zithini izithintelo koku?

##### Impembelelo kwindalo kunye neengcamango ezingachanekanga

Ukuze iBitcoin idityaniswe ngempumelelo kwinto ebalulekileyo njengegridi yombane, naziphi na iinkxalabo malunga nemiphumo kwindalo kunye neengcamango ezingachanekanga njengokusetyenziswa kwamandla kufuneka ziphendulwe. Imibutho efana neBitcoinpolicy.uk isebenza nzima ukuphendula ezi nkxalabo namashishini afanelekileyo kunye namagosa, kodwa oku kudla ngokuba ngumzabalazo onzima. Ukunceda ukufundisa imarike ngezibonelelo ezinokubakho zokwenza ingeniso kumandla angasetyenziswa okanye ukusetyenziswa kwamandla angaphezulu kubalulekile ukuze kufezekiswe ukusetyenziswa okuphumelelayo.

##### Imigaqo kunye neenkuthazo zokumba okukhathalela indalo

Amazwe anokuba neendlela ezahlukeneyo zokwamkela ukumba, ukusuka kumazwe afana neBhutan aqhuba ukumba iBitcoin ngokuthe ngqo, ukuya kwiindawo ezifana neTexas eMelika ezivumela ukumba ngaphandle kokuzama ukuyimisa, ukuya eTshayina eyayivale ngokupheleleyo ukumba iBitcoin.

Amanye amazwe afana ne-UK anokuhlawula imali eninzi kubanini bamafama omoya ukuze bayeke ukuvelisa umbane ngexesha lomoya omkhulu. Inkuthazo yokudibanisa ukumba iBitcoin incinci kwezi meko, nangona ingatshintsha imodeli yeshishini ukusuka ekubeni yindleko kumthengi ukuya ekubeni yingeniso enokunciphisa iintlawulo zombane.

##### Izithintelo ezingathanga ngqo zomthetho

Ezinye izithintelo zomthetho ezingathanga ngqo zinokubakho ezingabhekiseli ngqo kwiBitcoin kodwa zinokuchaphazela. Umzekelo, ingqalasizinda ekufuneka yakhiwe ukuze amafama omoya olwandle adityaniswe negridi inokuba ayivumelekanga ukwabelana nengqalasizinda yeendawo zedatha ezifunekayo ukumba iBitcoin.



#### 7.2.5 Isiphelo kunye nesimemo sokwenza

* I-Bitcoin ibonelela ngenkonzo abantu abanokuyisebenzisa ukugcina nokuthumela ixabiso. Ukuza kuthi ga ngoku, imarike enabathathi-nxaxheba abaninzi igqibe ekubeni le nethiwekhi inexabiso, kwaye njengayo nayiphi na into enexabiso isebenzisa amandla.
* Ukumba iBitcoin kusebenzisa ngaphantsi kwe-0.1% yamandla ehlabathi, kwaye iinkxalabo malunga nokusetyenziswa kwamandla ngokungachazekanga sele ziphenduliwe ngokupheleleyo kwimarike.
* Inxalenye enkulu yamandla asetyenziswa kukumba iBitcoin ingamandla awayeza kulahlekwa. Oku kungenxa yokuba abagcini-migodi beBitcoin banesakhono esikhethekileyo sokuhamba kwiindawo ezikude kwaye basebenze ngamandla angazinzanga angasetyenziswa ngabanye abathengi.
* I-Bitcoin inganceda ukuzinzisa igridi, ibe ngumthengi wokuqala ngokusebenzisa nokuhlawula umbane de idityaniswe negridi kwaye isetyenziswe kwenye indawo, kwaye inike impendulo kwimfuno ngokucima ngokukhawuleza ngexesha lemfuno ephezulu.

Imarike yeBitcoin kunye namandla ziya zisondelelana, kwaye ubunini bezixhobo buza kusondela. Kukho nendawo yokudibana ne-AI, efuna izakhono kunye nengqalasizinda efanayo neBitcoin kwaye iza kusetyenziswa kulawulo lwegridi ehlakaniphile. Iinkampani ezilungelelanisa uphuhliso lwezicwangciso zazo ukuze ziquke ezi ndlela zintsha ziya kuba kwindawo engcono yokuxhamla kwezi nguqu zintsha.
