# 7.1 Ikamva Elinokubakho leBitcoin

> Ingxaki ephambili ngemali yesiqhelo kukuba kufuneka sithembe kakhulu ukuze isebenze. Ibhanki ephambili kufuneka ithembeke ukuba ayiyi kuthoba ixabiso lemali, kodwa imbali yeemali ze-fiat izele ziziganeko zokungathembeki. Iibhanki kufuneka zithembe ukuba zigcine imali yethu kwaye ziyidlulisele ngombane, kodwa ziyiboleka rhoqo kwiimeko zokunyuka kwamatyala zize zishiyeke nezinto ezimbalwa kuphela zigcinwe njengemali egciniweyo.  
_Satoshi Nakamoto_



#### 7.1.0 Intshayelelo

Injongo yale module kukucetyisa ikamva elinokwenzeka leBitcoin kunye nefuthe elinokuthi libe nalo kuqoqosho lwethu. Xa sicinga ngemeko yekamva, kuyanceda ukuqwalasela ingxaki iBitcoin eyayizama ukuyisombulula xa yakhululwa okokuqala. Njengoko isicatshulwa esingasentla sibonisa, uSatoshi Nakamoto wayeqonda kakuhle ingxaki yokuthotywa kwamandla okuthenga kwemali ye-fiat. IBitcoin yadalwa njengesisombululo esiyilwe ngobunjineli.

IBitcoin yadalwa ukuze yenze imisebenzi emithathu ephambili yemali. Oko kukuthi, ukugcina ixabiso ngokuhamba kwexesha nendawo, ukusebenza njengendlela yokutshintshiselana kwimarike yeempahla neenkonzo, kunye nokusebenza njengoyunithi yokubala ukuze kulinganiswe kwaye kuthelekiswe ixabiso loqoqosho.

Ngoko ke, ukuze sihlolisise ikamva elinokwenzeka leBitcoin, kufuneka siqwalasele nganye kwezi misebenzi zemali ngokwahlukeneyo.



#### 7.1.1 Ukugcina Ixabiso

Ukusukela ngoMatshi 2025, iBitcoin isesigabeni sokuqala sokuzimisa njengempahla yegolide yexesha elide kwiinkampani, iifandesi zomhlala-phantsi, oomasipala kwanemimandla karhulumente ngokusebenzisa iifandesi zobutyebi belizwe. Kuyinto eqhelekileyo ukubona iBitcoin ichazwa njenge ‘igolide yedijithali’ kwimithombo yeendaba yorhwebo eqhelekileyo. Njengoko lo msebenzi uqala ukuqondwa ngokubanzi, kufuneka silindele ukuba abalawuli beepropathi kunye neebhanki baqale ukunikela ngezisombululo ezijikeleze iBitcoin kwaye ukugcina iBTC kwiibhile zenkampani kube yinto eqhelekileyo kwiinkampani zabucala nezikarhulumente ngokufanayo.

Njengoko iBitcoin iqala ukwamkelwa ngakumbi kwicandelo labucala kwihlabathi liphela, kusenokwenzeka ukuba oorhulumente kunye neebhanki eziphambili kuya kufuneka bamkele itekhnoloji ngokusebenzayo, nto leyo enokubangela ukuba ibe yimpahla yokugcina ixabiso efana negolide. Urhulumente omtsha wase-US uchaze isicwangciso se-Strategic Bitcoin Reserve kwaye, nangona iinkcukacha ze-SBR zisasebenza, injongo yokugcina iBTC kwinqanaba likarhulumente icacile.

##### Ukuphela koMthengi Oqhutywa Ngetyala?

Kwiqoqosho le-fiat elikhula ngexabiso lemali, ubuninzi bamatyala angabizi kakhulu bukhuthaza ukuchitha ngokugqithisileyo, nto leyo ekhokelela abathengi abaninzi ekubeni bangene kwiityala ezinzima njengoko bephila ngaphezu kwamandla abo. Le meko ichaphazela kakhulu abo bangenamali eninzi kuluntu. Iqoqosho eliqhutywa yiBitcoin, apho imali igcina okanye inyusa amandla okuthenga ngokuhamba kwexesha, liya kukhuthaza abathengi ukuba baboleke kancinci kwaye bagcine kwiBitcoin.

Njengoko iBitcoin iqala ukwamkelwa njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso jikelele, sinokubona utshintsho olukhulu kwindlela abathengi baziphatha ngayo. IBitcoin ikhuthaza ingcinga yexesha elide okanye yokulibazisa iminqweno, nto leyo ekhuthaza ingqondo yokulinda ukuze ufumane umvuzo. Oku kuya kukhuthaza uluntu ukuba lugcine imali yekamva kwaye lwaleke kwiindlela zokuziphatha ezinxulumene nokwenza izigqibo zexesha elifutshane, ezikhokelela ekuchitheni ngokugqithisileyo nasekuchitheni okungadingekiyo.

##### Ukuziphatha koMthengi kunye neNdalo

Amashishini nawo aza kufuneka alungelelanise le ngcinga intsha. Okwangoku, uqoqosho oluqhutywa yimali ye-fiat lukhuthaza abathengi ukuba bachithe imali kwizinto abangazidingiyo kuba amandla okuthenga kwemali ehla ngokuhamba kwexesha. Le meko ikhuthaza amashishini ukuba avelise iimveliso ezisemgangathweni ophantsi apho ukuguga kwazo kucewangwe kwangaphambili. Uhlobo lweBitcoin oluthanda ukugcina ixabiso (olukhuthaza abathengi ukuba bagcine imali endaweni yokuyichitha) lwenza amashishini anyanzeleke ukuba avelise iimveliso ezisemgangathweni ophezulu nezihlala ixesha elide.

Olu tshintsho kwindlela abathengi namashishini abacinga ngayo lunokubangela utshintsho olukhulu kuqoqosho nakuluntu lwethu. Imveliso kunye nokusetyenziswa okucwangcisiweyo kuya kutshintsha kakhulu indlela esijongana ngayo nokugcinwa, ukuphinda kusetyenziswe kunye nokurisayikilisha, nto leyo eya kunciphisa kakhulu inkunkuma. Oku kulindeleke ukuba kube nefuthe elihle kakhulu kwindalo njengoko amashishini eqala ukugxila kwimveliso ezinzileyo kunye nomgangatho endaweni yobuninzi. Ukwehla okukhulu kwimveliso yeempahla ezingabizi nezilahlwayo kukhokelela ekunciphiseni kakhulu inkunkuma yendalo.

##### Ukuchasana Kwezopolitiko Okunokwenzeka

Nangona iziphumo ezilungileyo ezinxulumene nolu tshintsho zibonakala ngokucacileyo, kungathatha iminyaka ngaphambi kokuba ziqondwe luluntu ngokubanzi. Abapolitiki abaninzi nabathetha ngemiba yezoshishino bathetha kakuhle malunga nesidingo sokusuka kuqoqosho oluqhutywa kukuchitha ukuya kwiziphumo ezizinzileyo ngenxa yezizathu zokukhusela indalo. Nangona kunjalo, enyanisweni akukho nto itshintshileyo kuba utshintsho olunjalo luza kuzisa utshintsho olukhulu olungafuna, okona kungcono, ukungazinzi koqoqosho lwexesha elifutshane okanye, okona kubi, ixesha elide lokuncipha kwemizi-mveliso. Uluntu oluchaphazelekayo kakubi (mhlawumbi ngenxa yokuphulukana nemisebenzi kwicandelo labathengi) luza kucela oorhulumente ukuba bazame ukulawula okanye bade babuyise umkhwa. Abapolitiki kunye neebhanki eziphambili ezidume ngokucinga ixesha elifutshane nelijolise kwiivoti baza kuzama ukukhuthaza abathengi ukuba bachithe imali ngakumbi kwaye benze ukuba ukufumana amatyala kube lula, ngokunyusa inani lemali esetyenziswa kunye nokwehlisa inzala.



#### 7.1.2 Indlela Yokutshintshiselana

Okwangoku, iBitcoin ayisetyenziswa kakhulu njengendlela yokutshintshiselana. Uluhlu oluphambili alusebenzi kakuhle kwiintlawulo zemihla ngemihla. Nangona kunjalo, ‘izisombululo zomaleko wesibini’, ezifana neLightning neLiquid, ziyakhula kwaye zibonisa ithemba, kwaye abanikeli beqonga abafana neLightspark baphuhlisa izisombululo ezijolise ekwandiseni intlawulo kwihlabathi liphela besebenzisa iLightning Network njengeqonga.

Ngelixa kufuneka silindele ukuba amashishini aphambili aqale ukwamkela iBitcoin kwiintlawulo eMntla Melika naseYurophu, amathuba akufutshane ezisombululo zentlawulo yemihla ngemihla akwiindawo ezisakhulayo. Ezi ziindawo ezinezakhiwo zebhanki ezibuthathaka kunye nokungabikho kokuthatha inxaxheba ebhankini phakathi koluntu. Kuloo bantu, ukufikelela kwi-smartphone kunye nonxibelelwano lwe-intanethi kuphela okudingekayo ukuze bathathe inxaxheba kuqoqosho lwehlabathi ngeBitcoin network.

Iidola zase-US ezizinzileyo, ezifana neTether, sele zibonisa ukukhula okukhulu kumazwe asakhulayo okanye kumazwe ajongene nokunyuka okukhulu kwamaxabiso. Urhulumente wase-US ubonakalise inkxaso engathanga ngqo yokukhula kwezi mali zizinzileyo kwihlabathi liphela njengoko zinqinisa ulawulo lwedola yase-US. Kumhlali ojongene nokunyuka okukhulu kwamaxabiso kwimali yasekhaya, inzuzo yokuba neakhawunti yedola yase-US icacile kwaye into engekho lula ukufumaneka kwinkqubo yebhanki yasekhaya.

Nangona abemi bamanye amazwe asakhulayo banokuthembela ngakumbi kwidola yase-US kunemali yabo yasekhaya, idola yase-US isengaphantsi kokuthotywa, nangona kungenjalo ngokukhawuleza. Njengoko abasebenzisi beemali zizinzileyo beqhelana ngakumbi nokuzigcina nokuzisebenzisa kwiintlawulo, kufuneka silindele ukuba abanye baqalise ukusebenzisa iBitcoin njengendlela yokugcina okanye ukwandisa amandla okuthenga. Ngale ndlela, sinokubona ukunyuka kokusetyenziswa kweemali zizinzileyo namhlanje njengenyathelo eliya ekwamkelweni okukhulu kweBitcoin kumazwe asakhulayo.

##### Izinto Ezinkulu Zexabiso

Kumazwe aphuhlileyo, apho iinkonzo zebhanki zikhona ngokulula kubemi nakwiinkampani, akukho sizathu sininzi sokusebenzisa iBitcoin kwiintlawulo zemihla ngemihla. Nangona kunjalo, kunokubakho izibonelelo ezinkulu xa kuthelekiswa neendlela zemveli zentlawulo kwizivumelwano ezinkulu, ezifana nokuthengiselana kwezindlu, ukuthenga iinqanawa okanye iinqwelo-moya ezininzi, ngakumbi xa kukho umba wamazwe ngamazwe.

Iibhanki zemveli zihlala zityhila iintlawulo eziphezulu (ngamanye amaxesha amashumi okanye amakhulu amawaka eerandi) kwiintlawulo zamazwe ngamazwe kwizivumelwano ezinkulu, ngakumbi xa ziquka utshintshiselwano lwemali. Kukho kwanokulibaziseka okukhulu kweentsuku ezininzi ngelixa kuqhutywa uphicotho lwamaqela, kwaye, nakweyiphi na imeko, iintlawulo zihlala zenzeka kuphela ngexesha leeyure zomsebenzi hayi ngeempelaveki.

Ngokwahlukileyo, intengiselwano yeBitcoin equka izigidi zeerandi ingenzeka nangaliphi na ixesha, emini okanye ebusuku, ngeempelaveki okanye ngeeholide zebhanki. Kwaye, ngokuxhomekeke kwinqanaba lomsebenzi wenethiwekhi, loo ntengiselwano ingenzeka ngemizuzu embalwa ngendleko ezincinci kwaye ibe nesigqibo esipheleleyo. Ukuqinisekiswa kokudluliselwa kwemali kungenziwa kwangoko.

##### Umnyhadala Wokutyikitya

Kwiimali zemveli, intengiselwano yamazwe ngamazwe equka izinto ezixabisa kakhulu (izindlu, inqanawa okanye inqwelo-moya) ngoku ibandakanya abalamli abaninzi, kuquka iibhanki, amagqwetha kunye neenkonzo zokugcina imali. Kungabakho imigaqo-nkqubo enzima ekufuneka ilandelwe, equka amazwe ngamazwe amaninzi kunye neemfuno zomthetho ezinzima kumazwe amaninzi, nto leyo eyongeza ixesha kunye neendleko.

Intengiselwano efanayo equka iBitcoin ingaba lula kakhulu, kuba inokususa abalamli abaninzi bemveli kwaye ibandakanye amagqwetha kuphela avela kumacala omabini. La magqwetha angalandela umnyhadala wokutyikitya othethwene ngawo kwangaphambili oza kudlulisa imali kusetyenziswa i-wallet ye-multisig lula ngemizuzu nangaliphi na ixesha. Ukudluliselwa kungabandakanya nesivumelwano esihlakaniphile esiza kukhulula ngokuzenzekelayo imali okanye iintlawulo ezinxulumeneyo xa umthengi egqibezele iimeko ezithile zokuhanjiswa. Le ndlela isusa amanyathelo amaninzi kunye nenani labalamli abathembekileyo abathatha inxaxheba kwintengiselwano, nto leyo enciphisa kakhulu ixesha, iindleko kunye nomngcipheko.

Ukongeza, kuba i-ledger yeBitcoin ixhaswe yindawo ekhuselekileyo kakhulu kwihlabathi, intengiselwano iba yirekhodi engaguqukiyo nengapheliyo. Oku kuqinisekisa ukucaca okupheleleyo kunye nokuba lula ukuphonononga, hayi kuphela kwabo bathatha inxaxheba kwintengiselwano, kodwa nakubabukeli bangaphandle ngaphandle kokuba kufuneka baqeshe abantu besithathu ukuba bajonge imeko yobunini. Le ndlela inokuba luncedo koorhulumente abafuna ukuqinisekisa ukuba irhafu efanelekileyo ihlawulwe.

##### Iintlawulo Ezincinci & Ezincinci Kakhulu

Kuyaziwa ukuba umaleko ophambili wenethiwekhi yeBitcoin awulungelanga iintlawulo ezincinci zemihla ngemihla ngenxa yokuxinana kunye nokulibaziseka kwexesha xa kudityaniswa iintengiselwano ezintsha kwi-ledger yehlabathi rhoqo emva kwemizuzu elishumi, ngokomndilili.

Okwangoku, iLightning Network iyahlangabezana nezinye zeemfuno zentlawulo ezincinci ngexesha langempela kwaye kufuneka silindele ukusetyenziswa kwale nethiwekhi kunye nezinye iilayer 2s kuqhubeke kukhula. Iinkqubo ziya kwakhiwa kwiilayer 2s ezenza iintlawulo zibe lula ngakumbi kunye namava omsebenzisi aphucukileyo. Iinkampani ezifana neLightspark zisebenza ekudibaniseni iLightning Network kwizicelo zoshishino kwaye kufuneka silindele ukuba amakhadi etyala, iMastercard neVisa, adibanise lo msebenzi nawo, ukuba bafuna ukuhlala befanelekileyo.

Ukukhula kweentlawulo ezincinci ezikhawulezayo kuya kuvumela ukukhula kweemodeli zokuhlawula ngokusetyenziswa kweenkonzo. Umzekelo, endaweni yokubhalisela ‘intlawulo yenyanga’ kwiTV, iimuvi okanye umxholo wezemidlalo, iintlawulo ezincinci zeBitcoin zinokwenziwa ngexesha lokusetyenziswa komxholo. Oku kuya kudibanisa iindleko ngokufanelekileyo nokusetyenziswa, kwenze kube nobulungisa phakathi komnikezeli kunye nomthengi.



#### 7.1.3 Iyunithi Yokubala

Umsebenzi wemali njengomlinganiso wexabiso uvela kwimpumelelo yayo, kuqala njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso, emva koko njengendlela yokutshintshiselana. Xa iBitcoin sele isetyenziswa ngokubanzi kuqoqosho kwaye abathengisi beqala ukukhetha okanye ukufuna ukuhlawulwa ngeBTC endaweni yemali yasekuhlaleni, kufuneka silindele ukubona iimpahla neenkonzo zixabiswa ngale ndlela. Le yinto ebizwa ngokuba yinqanaba le-hyperbitcoinization. Kule meko, iBitcoin iba uzinzile ngakumbi kwaye ingashukumi kakhulu xa ithelekiswa nemali yasekuhlaleni njengendlela yokuxabisa.

Nangona i-hyperbitcoinization ingathatha iminyaka emininzi okanye amashumi eminyaka, sinokubona uqoqosho oluhamba ngokufanayo luvela kwiimarike eziphuhliweyo apho iBitcoin ikhona kunye nemali ye-fiat. Kule meko, iBTC ingasetyenziswa ukugcina imali ixesha elide, ngelixa imali ye-fiat isasetyenziswa kakhulu ekutshintshiselaneni kwemihla ngemihla. Ukongeza, amashishini aza kugcina iBTC kwiibhuku zawo ngelixa eqhubeka esebenzisa i-fiat kwimisebenzi eqhelekileyo. Oku kuhambelana nomthetho kaGresham, othi 'imali embi ikhupha imali elungileyo' kwaye oku kukhokelela ekubeni imali elungileyo (iBitcoin) igcinwe kwaye imali embi (i-fiat) ichithwe.

Kuhamba kwexesha, kwaye njengoko abathengisi beqhelana ngakumbi neBitcoin, kusenokwenzeka ukuba siza kubona ukwanda kwamashishini afuna ukuhlawulwa ngeBitcoin endaweni ye-fiat kwiintengiselwano zemihla ngemihla. Oku kungakhawuleza xa ukwehla kwamandla emali ye-fiat kuqhubeka kwaye ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuyaqhubeka. Okukhona iBitcoin isetyenziswa kuqoqosho, ukungazinzi kwexabiso layo kuya kuncipha kwaye amandla ayo okuthenga aza kuzinza ngakumbi. Oku kuya kukhuthaza abathengisi abaninzi ukuba bajoyine uqoqosho lweBitcoin kwaye siza kubona iimpahla neenkonzo ezininzi zixabiswa ngeBitcoin.

Njengoko abathengisi abaninzi betshintshela kwiBitcoin njengomlinganiso wexabiso abakhethayo, kufuneka sibone ukuncipha kobukhulu boqoqosho obuphakathi kwemali ye-fiat. Olu tshintsho lunokunyusa ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kwi-fiat (ngaphandle kokuba kukho ukuncitshiswa okucacileyo kwimali ye-fiat), kubangele ukwehla kwamatyala kunye nokuwa kwamandla okuthenga kwemali ye-fiat. Isizathu sokunyuka kwamaxabiso kwi-fiat kukuba ukuba inani lempahla, iinkonzo kunye nomsebenzi onokuthengwa ngemali ye-fiat liyancipha, kodwa imali ye-fiat ihleli injalo, imali efanayo ijongene nezixhobo ezimbalwa, nto leyo ekhokelela ekunyukeni kwamaxabiso kwi-fiat.

Njengoko inethiwekhi yeBitcoin ikhula, uqoqosho oluhamba ngokufanayo lunokukhokelela kwi-hyperbitcoinization kwiminyaka ezayo.



#### 7.1.4 Ukuhlanganiswa nezeMali zesiNtu

Singalindela ukuba iBitcoin idityaniswe ngakumbi neenkonzo zemali zesiNtu. Ukongeza ekuphuculeni ukusebenza kwamashishini akhoyo kwinqanaba elikhulu, iya kudala amathuba amatsha oshishino ngelixa ezinye zishiywa zingasasebenzi.

Iibhanki nabalawuli bee-asethi kuya kufuneka badibanise iBitcoin kwiinkonzo zabo ukuze bahlale bekhuphisana. Amanye amashishini anokuthi ancitshiswe okanye avalwe ngokupheleleyo. Kukho imizekelo kwimbali kwishishini lehlabathi lonke le-telecoms ngexesha le-1990s xa ukunyuka kwe-intanethi kwehlisa kakhulu iindleko zokufowuna kumgama omde. Ngelo xesha, iinkampani ezininzi ze-telecom zatshintsha zaba ngababoneleli beenkonzo ze-intanethi. Ngokufanayo, kufuneka silindele ukuba iinkampani zemali zesiNtu zibe ngababoneleli benethiwekhi yeBitcoin ukuze zisinde.

##### Ugcino lweZiko

Njengoko iBitcoin isetyenziswa ngokubanzi ngabantu, imibutho kunye nemibutho karhulumente, kufuneka silindele ukuba kubekho imfuno enkulu yezisombululo zokugcina eziguquguqukayo nezikhuselekileyo ezakhelwe phezu kwamandla eBitcoin protocol.

Iibhanki nabalawuli bee-asethi abajolise kugcino lwe-asethi zesiNtu ngoku, kusenokwenzeka ukuba baza kwandisa iinkonzo zabo ukuze babandakanye nokugcina iBTC. Ezi zisombululo ziya kwahluka ngobunzima kwaye, kumgangatho ophezulu, ziya kubandakanya imisebenzi exhasa izisombululo ezisebenzisa i-multi-signature apho izitshixo zabucala zigcinwa ngamaqela amaninzi alawulwayo. Ukongeza, kuba ukucaca kwe-ledger yeBitcoin sele kuvumela abanini ukuba baqinisekise ukuba iBTC abayilawulayo ikhona kwaye ikhuselekile, kufuneka silindele ukuba lo msebenzi unikezelwe ngabagcini abaqhelekileyo kwaye ube yimfuno enkulu kubanini-mali abakhulu. Umzekelo, abaninzi bamasheya kunye nabanye abanomdla kwinkampani kufuneka bakwazi ukuqinisekisa ngokuzimeleyo ixabiso leBitcoin elichazwe kwiingxelo zezezimali, ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kwiingqinisekiso zomhloli-mali wesithathu.

Izicelo zogcino olucacileyo zineziphumo ezilungileyo kurhwebo lwamazwe ngamazwe kuba inxalenye yokuthembana ingancitshiswa ngakumbi. Ukugcinwa kwebitcoin ukuhlawula izivumelwano kunokugcinwa kwi-escrow enokuthi iqinisekiswe ngamaqela amaninzi nangaliphi na ixesha.

Kufuneka silindele ukuba nababoneleli behlabathi beenkonzo ze-inshurensi baqale ukunomdla kwiBitcoin. Njengoko ixabiso lebitcoin ligqitha, ababoneleli beenshurensi baza kubona ithuba lokufumana imivuzo emikhulu ngokubhalela abanini-mali. Ngo-2025, iqela kwi-marike yeLloyd’s yaseLondon langena kweli shishini ngokusebenzisana nomnikezeli wokugcina iBitcoin uOnramp ukubonelela ngesisombululo se-inshurensi kubanini beBitcoin.

Njengoko i-inshurensi yeBitcoin isiba yinto eqhelekileyo, kufuneka silindele ukuba imigangatho yoshishino lwamazwe ngamazwe ivele malunga nogcino lwabantu kunye nemibutho. Le migangatho iya kuqinisekisa ukuba imigaqo ethile kunye neenkqubo ziyagcinwa kwaye zihlolwe rhoqo, ukuze ababoneleli beenshurensi bakwazi ukuthembela ngakumbi ekubhaleni kwabo.

##### I-Bitcoin njengeSibambiso: IiMarike zeTyala

IiMarike zetyala zixabisa malunga ne-$300T kwihlabathi liphela kwaye, kuba abanini beBitcoin baza kufuna ukufumana inzala kwiindawo zabo, kufuneka silindele ukuba iimarike zetyala zinike izisombululo eziguquguqukayo. Kwicala elinye, kufuneka silindele ukuba abanikazi bamatyala baphendule kwimfuno yokufumana iBitcoin ngezinto ezintsha zemveliso.

Sele sibonile inkampani yase-US ebizwa Strategy (MSTR) ikhokelela ekudaleni izisombululo ezintsha zabathengi bamatyala abathanda ukufumana iBitcoin, kubandakanya iibhondi eziguqukayo kunye nesitokhwe esikhethekileyo. Kukho inxalenye yokuvavanya imarike ngezi mveliso ukuze kufumaneke ezona zisombululo zisebenzayo. Nangona kunjalo, xa imarike yezi mveliso sele iqinisekisiwe, kufuneka silindele ukuba iimveliso zamatyala ezinxulumene nebitcoin ziqale ukubanjwa ngokubanzi kwiipotifoliyo ezijolise kwi-fixed-income, ezifana neefandesi zomhlala-phantsi.

Sikwabonile ukuqala kwabanikezeli bamatyala abavavanya ukusebenzisa iBitcoin njengesiqinisekiso kwiimali-mboleko zezakhiwo. Ukubandakanya iBitcoin njengesiqinisekiso kwimali-mboleko kunokunceda zombini umbolekisi nomthengi ukuba baxhamle ekunyukeni kwexabiso leBitcoin ngexesha lemali-mboleko.

Kukho abanye abanikazi abancinci beenkonzo zemali-mboleko ezixhaswa yiBitcoin okanye iimveliso zenzala, ezifana neLEDN. Nangona abanikazi bamatyala abakhulu bengakangenanga kweli shishini, kufuneka silindele ukuba bafike kungekudala.

##### Ulawulo loTyalo-mali: IBitcoin kunye ne-'Hurdle Rate'

Ukusuka kwicala lotyalo-mali, sele sibonile abanye abadlali kushishino bekhankanya ukukhula konyaka kweBitcoin ngexabiso lemali njengendleko yethuba lotyalo-mali okanye 'hurdle rate' yotyalo-mali. Oku kukhuthaza ingcinga yokuba ukuze utyalo-mali luthathelwe ingqalelo, imbuyekezo yalo yonyaka okanye edityanisiweyo kufuneka (okungenani ngokwethuba) idlule eyebitcoin. Xa kuthelekiswa utyalo-mali lwesiNtu nebitcoin ngale ndlela, kubeka umda ophezulu wokuba imali isuswe kwiBitcoin, nangona kufuneka silindele ukuba imbuyekezo yonyaka yeBitcoin iya kuncipha ekuhambeni kwexesha njengoko le asethi ikhula.

Ukuba le ngcinga iyamkelwa kwaye ukukhula konyaka kweBitcoin kuba 'risk free rate' entsha yotyalo-mali, inokuba nefuthe elikhulu kwiindidi zee-asethi zesiNtu. Umzekelo, sinokubona ukunyuka okukhulu kweembuyekezo ezilindelweyo kwiimarike zetyala ukuze zikhuphisane neBitcoin. Kwiimarike zesitokhwe, sinokubona utshintsho olukhulu kwiimethrikhi ezifana ne-price-to-earnings ratios okukhokelela ekwehleni kwamaxabiso. Kwaye, iimarike zezakhiwo nazo zinokubona utshintsho olukhulu lwamaxabiso kuba, ukuze zibe lukhetho olufanelekileyo kutyalo-mali xa kuthelekiswa nokuba neBitcoin, imbuyekezo yerenti kufuneka inyuke ngokufanelekileyo. Ukuba oku kuyenzeka, ukuba ixabiso lerenti alitshintshi kakhulu, amaxabiso ezakhiwo angawa, mhlawumbi kakhulu.

Elinye ifuthe lolu tshintsho kukuba izigqibo zomgaqo-nkqubo wemali ye-fiat ezenziwa ngabaLawuli-mali abaphakathi ziya kuba nefuthe elincinci kwiimarike zotyalo-mali. Kwiminyaka ezayo, iziphumo zeentlanganiso rhoqo ze-US Federal Reserve kunye ne-symposium yaseJackson Hole ziya kuba nefuthe elincinci kubanini-mali.

##### Iimali zeSizwe

Kwihlabathi elizayo apho iBitcoin kunye ne-dollar stablecoins zingagcinwa kwaye zisetyenziswe lula njengemali yasekuhlaleni, imfuno yemali yasekuhlaleni ingehla kakhulu. Okwangoku, iimali ezininzi zesizwe zikhuselwe ziibhanki zasekuhlaleni ezenza kube nzima kubemi ukuba basebenzise iimali zangaphandle. Kuba iBitcoin kunye ne-dollar stablecoins azidingi imvume yeebhanki zomntu wesithathu ukugcina nokuthumela imali, ukusetyenziswa kwazo kungakhula, ngakumbi kumazwe apho ixabiso lemali yasekuhlaleni lingazinzanga okanye lisengozini yokwehla kwamandla okuthenga ngenxa yokunyuka kwamaxabiso.

Ezinye iimali zasekuhlaleni zingaphelelwa kukusebenza. Iimali ezibuthathaka zingachaphazeleka kuqala kodwa nezo zomeleleyo azikhuselekanga. Umzekelo, eYurophu, ukuba kuya kuba lula kwaye kukhawuleze ukuthumela imali nge-dollar stablecoins kunokusebenzisa i-Euro ngokwayo (ngakumbi kwiintlawulo zamazwe angaphandle), imfuno ye-Euro ingehla njengoko abemi bengakhetha ukugcina i-dollar equivalents endaweni yayo. Ngale ndlela, i-dollar stablecoins zinokunceda ukuqinisekisa ukusasazeka kwe-dollar kunye nokugcina i-US njengomnikezeli wemali yokugcina yehlabathi. Izimvo zamva nje ezivela kubagqabazi base-US zibonisa ukuba le ngcinga yamkelwa ngurhulumente wangoku wase-US.



#### 7.1.5 Ukuhlanganiswa ne-AI

Indibaniselwano yeBitcoin kunye noBukrelekrele bokuZenzekelayo (AI) idala ithuba lentsha yokuqamba izinto ezintsha kwidijithali, ngakumbi ngokudityaniswa kwe-AI kunye neBitcoin's Lightning Network. Olu manyano lujolise ekuguquleni imiba ye-intanethi, ukusuka kwiintlawulo ezincinci ukuya kwi-arhente zoqoqosho ezisebenza nge-AI kwi-intanethi. Iindlela zokuhlawula ezixhomekeke kwiiplatifomu ze-AI namhlanje zindala, zidlulisela iindleko kubasebenzisi kwaye zinciphisa ukusetyenziswa kunye nokufikelela, kwaye zisebenzisa iindlela ezizimeleyo nezibiza kakhulu. Zisebenza kakuhle kwiintlawulo ezinkulu okanye iimodeli zokubhalisa, kodwa kwiintlawulo ezincinci iindleko zenza zingabi luncedo, apho nokuba ziisenti ezimbalwa ngentengiselwano kunokuba yinkinga. Iiarhente ze-AI azinazazisi zomthetho ezinokusetyenziswa ukufumana ii-akhawunti zebhanki okanye iinkonzo zokuhlawula kwinkqubo yeebhanki zesiNtu, kwaye ezingasebenzi iiyure ezingama-24/7. IBitcoin ayifuni isazisi somthetho kwaye ke inika indlela yezinto ezingengomntu ezifana neearhente ze-AI ukugcina ixabiso, ukuthumela nokufumana intlawulo. Eminye imizekelo yeenkonzo ezinokwenziwa oku kubandakanya:

1. Ukuhlanganiswa kweearhente ze-AI kunye nezixhobo ze-IoT ngokusebenzisa iinethiwekhi ezizimeleyo zengqalasizinda kunokukhokelela kwiinkqubo ezizimeleyo ezilawula izixhobo, ziphucule iinkqubo, kwaye zisebenze kuqoqosho ngokuzimeleyo.
1. Kwicandelo lomxholo, iinkqubo ze-AI zingazenza zodwa, zipapashe, kwaye zifumane ingeniso kwizinto ezenziweyo, zilawula ingeniso ngaphandle kongenelelo lomntu.
1. Kwiinkonzo zezeMali, ii-arhente ze-AI zingakwazi ukwenza iintengiselwano ngexesha langempela iiyure ezingama-24 ngosuku, iintsuku ezisi-7 ngeveki egameni leenkampani ezinkulu zezezimali, ngaphandle kwemfuneko yokungenelela komntu. Imali eninzi ingabandakanyeka, mhlawumbi yokudluliselwa komngcipheko okubandakanya iintlobo ngeentlobo zezixhobo kunye neempahla, kwaye kusetyenziswa indibaniselwano yomaleko wesibini kunye nomaleko osisiseko ekusulungekisweni. 1. Kumashishini emveliso, ii-arhente ze-AI zingazenzela ngokuzenzekelayo inkqubo yokuthenga, zifumane kwaye zithenge izinto ezifunekayo ngokuzimeleyo.
