# 7.3 Ukubonelela ngeenkonzo zebhanki kwabo bengenazo okanye abangazifumani ngokwaneleyo

> Ububanki, ngokubanzi, luthotho lweemigangatho zomthetho nezobuchwepheshe abantu abaye bayiphuhlisa phezu kwemali yezinto ezithengiswayo.  
_Lynn Alden_



#### 7.3.1 Intshayelelo

> Uhlobo lwemali yedijithali olusekwe ngokupheleleyo kubantu abathengiselana ngokuthe ngqo lungenza ukuba intlawulo kwi-intanethi ithunyelwe ngokuthe ngqo ukusuka kumntu omnye iye komnye ngaphandle kokudlula kwibhanki okanye umbutho wezezimali.  
_Satoshi Nakamoto_

Isicatshulwa esingentla esivela ekuqaleni kwe-abstract kwi-whitepaper yeBitcoin sichaza ukuba kutheni iibanki zingeyomfuneko kwiintlawulo kwihlabathi apho kukho iBitcoin.

Esi sahluko sigxininisa indlela iBitcoin esombulula ngayo izizathu ezininzi ezenza ukuba abantu abazizigidi ezi-1.4 behlabathi bengenazo iiakhawunti zebhanki ngokwe-Global Findex database yeBhanki yeHlabathi. Ngokujongana nale mingeni, iBitcoin inamandla okunciphisa kakhulu eli nani kwaye ngaloo ndlela ivumele ukubandakanywa kwezigidi zabantu abakhutshiweyo kuqoqosho lwehlabathi.

Siyalumisa uhlalutyo kwinkonzo ezisisiseko zobubanki zokusebenzisa nokugcina imali ezivumela ukuthatha inxaxheba ngokufanelekileyo kuqoqosho lwehlabathi olungaphaya kwendawo yasekhaya okanye ixesha elifutshane apho imali ephathekayo okanye ezinye iindlela zinokwaneleka. Kulesi sahluko, sichaza abantu abangaphantsi kobubanki njengabantu abadala abathintelwe ekuthatheni inxaxheba njengabantu ngabanye kuqoqosho lwehlabathi ngenxa yokungabinayo iakhawunti yebhanki ebonelela ngeenkonzo zokuhlawula nokugcina imali abayidingayo ukuze bathathe inxaxheba ngendlela abayifunayo.

Ukusetyenziswa kwethu kwegama elithi ‘abangaphantsi kobubanki’ kusivumela ukuba sidlule kwicandelo elilula leBhanki yeHlabathi elithi banked vs unbanked. Olu khetho lwamagama luvela kwingqwalasela yokuba nangona abaninzi bengenaso isiseko sobubanki, mhlawumbi abaninzi ngakumbi bakwimeko ethile yokungabikho ngokupheleleyo kobubanki kuba imeko yabo ingatshintsha ngokungaqhelekanga nangaphandle kolawulo lwabo, sithi aba ‘bangaphantsi kobubanki’.

Sikwabonisa imfuneko yokuba iziko elingengomntu lifumane inkqubo yemali, umzekelo imibutho. Kwaye imfuneko elindelekileyo yokuba izidalwa ezingengomntu nezingengomthetho, umzekelo ii-agent ze-Artificial Intelligence (AI), zifumane ukufikelela.



#### 7.3.2 Izinto ezichaphazela ukufikelela kububanki

> Ukuze sikwazi ukuhlangabezana neemfuno zethu kunye nokonwabela amalungelo ethu asisiseko kunye nenkululeko, sifuna ukufikelela kwinkqubo yemali. Sifuna ukutya, impahla, kunye nendawo yokuhlala, kunye nokufikelela kwimpilo kunye nococeko. Sifuna imali ukuze sikwazi ukuhlawula ezi zinto.  
_Resistance Money, Andrew M Bailey, Bradley Rettler, Craig Warmke_

Ezona zinto ziphambili ezimisela ukuba abantu bangaphantsi kobubanki kangakanani zinokwahlulwa zibe ziindidi ezi-5 :

* Uqoqosho - eyona nto iphambili xa kujongwa abantu abazizigidi ezi-1.4 abachongwe yiBhanki yeHlabathi njengabangenaso isiseko sobubanki
* Ukufumaneka kwendlela yokusebenzisa ibhanki
* Ukuqinisekiswa kwesazisi
* Ukuthembana
* Isidima

Ukusuka kwicala lomthengi ezinye izinto zimiselwa yibhanki, kwaye ezinye zichaphazeleka kukhetho lomthengi. Izinto ezimiselwa yibhanki, idatha abayigcinayo, kunye nendlela abayichaza ngayo le datha zingatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha. Ukhetho lomthengi nalo lungatshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha. Ngoko ke, inqanaba apho umntu angaba phantsi kobubanki litshintsha ngokuhamba kwexesha kwaye alimiselwa kuphela kukuba unayo okanye akanayo iakhawunti yebhanki.

Kufanelekile ukuqaphela ukuba nangona umhlathi wokugqibela ubonisa ukuba umthengi ubona imithombo emibini kuphela - ibhanki okanye yena - eneneni uninzi lwendima yebhanki lukhulu lusekwe kwisiseko somthetho kunye nemigaqo-nkqubo apho amalungelo kunye noxanduva lwebhanki lusekwa khona. Ngokugqibela, kukho imiba emininzi enzima kwaye edla ngokudityaniswa ejongene neebhanki kunye nabathengi bazo etshintsha ngokweendawo nakwixesha. Oku kudala inkqubo yemali yehlabathi eqhekezayo apho kukho umahluko omkhulu kwinqanaba lokufikelela kububanki kwihlabathi lonke.

##### Uqoqosho

Iindleko zokubonelela ngenkonzo zobubanki yeyona nto iphambili ichongwe yiBhanki yeHlabathi ebangela ukuba abantu abazizigidi ezi-1.4 bangenazo iiakhawunti zebhanki. Iibhanki kufuneka zenze imisebenzi enenzuzo.

Ukuba ixabiso lempahla yemali, inzuzo enokufumaneka kwimali mboleko kunye nemirhumo yokuthengiselana ingaphantsi kweendleko zokubonelela ngeenkonzo kwabanye abathengi, kusenokwenzeka ukuba aba bathengi baya kuhlala bengenazo iiakhawunti zebhanki. Iindleko zokubonelela ngeenkonzo zinokuba phezulu kwezinye iindawo ngenxa yokungasebenzi kakuhle kweenkqubo zokuhlawula, iziseko zebhanki, iindleko zabasebenzi kunye nokuthotyelwa kwemigaqo-nkqubo.

Ukuba iinkonzo zebhanki zinokubonelelwa ngexabiso elizisa inzuzo, abanye abathengi banokugqiba ukuba iindleko ziphezulu kakhulu kubo baze bakhethe ezinye iindlela. Ezi zinokubandakanya ukwabelana ngeakhawunti yebhanki phakathi kwamalungu osapho okanye ukusebenzisa ii-apps zenkonzo yokuhlawula, umzekelo WeChat, CashApp okanye M-Pesa. Ezinye iindlela ngoku ziquka iintlawulo nge-stablecoin okanye iBitcoin. Xa abantu befikelela kuphela kwiinkonzo zebhanki ngokusebenzisa abanikazi beenkonzo zokuhlawula, banokuthathwa njengabangaphantsi kobubanki.

Ubungakanani kunye nobutyebi jikelele babemi kwindawo ethile yemali bunokuchaphazela uqoqosho lwezinga elikhulu kwishishini lobubanki.

Iindleko, ixesha kunye nokuthembeka kweentlawulo zamazwe ngamazwe zinokuba zimbi kakhulu xa kuthelekiswa neentlawulo ezenziwa ngaphakathi kwilizwe elinye lemali.

##### Ukufumaneka

Abathengi kufuneka babe nendlela yokusebenzisa ibhanki. Le ndlela ingaba yindawo ebonakalayo okanye ubukho bedijithali ngefowuni, iwebhusayithi okanye i-app yeselfowuni. Ngokugqibeleleyo, ukuba zonke ezi zikhona kubonelela ukufumaneka okuphezulu. Kukho iindawo ezininzi zehlabathi ezingenayo okanye ezinesithintelo kwezinye okanye kuzo zonke ezi zixhobo. Nakumazwe aphuhlileyo amazwe amaninzi abone ukwehla okukhulu kwamanani amasebe eebhanki okunciphisa ukufumaneka kweenkonzo zebhanki kwabanye abathengi.

Ukuba akukho masebe afikelelekayo kubathengi, ukusilela kwezixhobo zedijithali, okanye abathengi ngokwabo abanayo itekhnoloji yeselfowuni, ke ukufikelela kweenkonzo zebhanki kunokuthintelwa kakhulu.

##### Isazisi

Ukukwazi ukuvula iiakhawunti zebhanki, kwaye kwezinye iimeko ukuzisebenzisa buqu, kufuna amaxwebhu esazisi njengesiqinisekiso sobuwena, ipaspoti, ilayisensi yokuqhuba, okanye amaxwebhu aqinisekisa idilesi. Kwezinye iimeko kungafuneka nobungqina bobutyebi okanye ingeniso. Abantu abaninzi abanaloo maxwebhu kwaye ke bayakhutshelwa ngaphandle kwenkqubo. Kwezinye iimeko ukusilela kwezi ngxelo kunokuba ngenxa yeendleko zazo, ezikwamanye amazwe zinokuba phezulu kubathengi abanokubakho.

Ii-agent ze-Artificial Intelligence (AI) zinokufuna ukusebenzisa nokufumana iintlawulo ukuze zifezekise imisebenzi yazo. Okwangoku akukho siseko sokuba ii-agent ze-AI zinikeze isazisi kuba azinayo ifomu esemthethweni.

Ezinye izithintelo kwiinkonzo zebhanki ezipheleleyo okanye ezinxalenye ezinokuthi zivele kwabanye abantu kwamanye amazwe ngexesha elithile ziquka :

* Imigaqo-nkqubo, yokwenene okanye echazwe ngendlela ethile
* Isini - kwamanye amazwe kuphela izini ezithile ezivumelekileyo ukuba zibe neeakhawunti zebhanki
* Ezentlalo - abanye abantu bajongana nenkohlakalo yoluntu kwaye bathintelwa zizalamane ekubeni babe neeakhawunti zebhanki
* Ubuzwe - ukuvula iakhawunti yebhanki kwilizwe ongasosakhamuzi kulo akuvumelekanga kwiindawo ezininzi
* Ukuchaphazeleka kwezopolitiko - kwamanye amazwe unokwala ukufumana iinkonzo zebhanki ukuba ibhanki igqiba ukuba usengozini ephezulu ngenxa yokubandakanyeka kwezopolitiko.
* Izohlwayo zezopolitiko kunye nehlabathi elinemimandla emininzi. Kukho iinethiwekhi ezininzi zebhanki kwihlabathi ezingasebenzi kakuhle okanye ezinganiki iinkonzo ezikhawulezayo nezisebenzayo.

##### Isimilo

Abantu banokubamba iinkolelo zokuziphatha ezisekelwe kwizimvo zonqulo okanye zefilosofi ezingahambelani nezenzo zeebhanki ezikhoyo kubo. Isiphumo soku kukuba bahlala bengabikho ebhankini.

#### 7.3.3 Indlela iBitcoin enokunciphisa ngayo iziphumo

I-Bitcoin inezinto ezininzi ezinokunciphisa ukutshintshiselana phakathi kwezinto ezichongiweyo. Nokuba iBitcoin ayithethi ukuba bonke abantu abangama-1.4 bhiliyoni abangekho ebhankini emhlabeni banokuyisebenzisa, inani elikhulu liza kukwazi. Kwabanye abangaphantsi kokufikelela kwiibhanki, iBitcoin inika enye indlela enokuzalisa ezinye, ukuba ayizizo zonke, izikhewu. Ngenxa yoko kulindeleke ukuba iBitcoin inciphise kakhulu iziphumo zokungabikho (okanye ukungonelanga) kwebhanki ebantwini bayo kwaye ngaloo ndlela noqoqosho lwehlabathi.

##### Uqoqosho

Kukho ukwanda kwezixazululo zentlawulo ezidibanisa iBitcoin. Ezinye izisombululo zisebenza ngendlela yokugcina imali egameni lomntu, njengamabhanki akhoyo, kwaye ezinye zivumela umntu azigcinele ngokwakhe. Nangona i-timechain (irekhodi leentengiselwano) yeBitcoin ingabi yongcono kwezo ntengiselwano zincinci, ixabiso layo lisezantsi kakhulu kwiintengiselwano ezinkulu. Imirhumo ingatshintsha kwi-timechain ukusuka ngaphantsi kwe-euro enye ukuya kumashumi e-euro kuxhomekeke kwimfuno yendawo kwelo xesha kunye nokungxamiseka komthumeli.

Nangona kunjalo, ixabiso lentengiselwano alichaphazeli iindleko zayo, ngoko ke kwiintengiselwano ezinkulu iindleko zizonke zinokuphantsi kakhulu ngokwepesenti. Oku kukuvumela ukuthumela nayiphi na imali naphi na emhlabeni, ngokuqhelekileyo ngaphantsi kweyure, kwaye rhoqo ngaphakathi kwemizuzu eli-15.

Kwiintengiselwano ezincinci, izisombululo zentlawulo zomaleko wesibini ziye zavela. Inethiwekhi ye-lightning ngumzekelo osebenzayo mihla le kwihlabathi liphela. Imirhumo kwi-lightning network ihlala iphakathi kwe-<0.1% ukuya kwi-0.2% yexabiso lentengiselwano kwaye inika intlawulo ekhawulezayo ngaphandle kokubuyiselwa kwemali. Izicelo ezininzi ngoku ziyafumaneka kwiivenkile zee-apps ezizisa ukufikelela lula kwi-lightning network kuye nabani na one-smartphone, kuxhomekeke kwimigaqo yendawo. Njengenjongo yokwenza iintlawulo zemihla ngemihla kwihlabathi liphela, i-lightning network isebenza ngokufanelekileyo, ithembekile kwaye ikhawuleza kakhulu phantse kubo bonke abantu kunokwenza iintlawulo zamazwe ngamazwe ngeenethiwekhi zebhanki ezikhoyo.

I-Bitcoin izisa ukhuphiswano olutsha kuzo zonke iinethiwekhi zentlawulo kwaye kulindeleke ukuba inciphise impembelelo yezizathu zoqoqosho ezibangela ukungabikho (okanye ukungonelanga) kwebhanki.

##### Ukufumaneka

Kwiindawo zehlabathi apho ukufikelela kwiibhanki zomzimba kunqongophele, iBitcoin inika indlela entsha yokuthengiselana kwihlabathi liphela. Ezona ndlela zilula zokufikelela kwiimpawu zeBitcoin zisese-desktop, ilaptop, ithebhulethi okanye i-smartphone.

Nangona kunjalo, apho uluntu lunezixhobo zedijithali eziphantsi, ezinye izisombululo ziye zaveliswa phezu kweBitcoin. Umzekelo, kwamanye amazwe aseAfrika abantu baye baphuhlisa izisombululo zentlawulo ngeBitcoin ezifana neMachankura ngokusebenzisana nababoneleli beenkonzo zonxibelelwano ezivumela iintlawulo ze-lightning ukuba zithunyelwe ngeSMS kwizixhobo ezindala zeselula.

Umgangatho ovulekileyo obizwa ngokuba yiBolt 12 uphuhliswe ukuvumela amakhadi eBitcoin debit omzimba apho umthumeli angahlawula umntu ngaphandle kwesidingo sesixhobo esixhunyiwe.

Imali yedijithali enokuthwalwa isetyenziswa ngakumbi ngezixazululo ze-ecash ezivulekileyo ezifana neCashu ([cashu.space](https://cashu.space/)), ezisebenzisa itekhnoloji yeChaumian ecash kwiBitcoin. Ezi zisombululo zandisa ngakumbi ukufumaneka kokugcina kunye nokwenza iintlawulo ezakhiwe kwiBitcoin.

Ukufika kwezixazululo ezakhiwe phezu kweBitcoin kwenza ukuba umsebenzi wentlawulo ufumaneke kubantu abaninzi ngakumbi kunabo basele bekhonza, kwaye kudala iindlela ezintsha kwabo basele bekhona. Oku kulindeleke ukuba kunciphise impembelelo yezizathu zokufumaneka ezibangela ukungabikho (okanye ukungonelanga) kwebhanki.

##### Isazisi

Ukusebenzisa iBitcoin ngendlela yokuzigcinela akufuni sazisi semthethweni. Le yinzuzo enkulu kwabo bajongene nemingeni yokuchongwa ngenxa yezizathu zoqoqosho okanye zentlalo.

Ezinye izisombululo zentlawulo ezigcinwe egameni lomntu zinokuxhasa abasebenzisi abangenako ukufikelela kwiibhanki ngenxa yokungabi nendawo yokuhlala. Amakhadi agcina isitshixo sabucala se-wallet ye-lightning angasetyenziswa ukwenza iintlawulo. Umgangatho ovulekileyo ophuhliswe kwiBitcoin ukuxhasa la makhadi okucofa uhlawule ubizwa ngokuba yi‘Bolt’ ([boltcard.org](https://www.boltcard.org/)). Umbutho wosapho wasekuhlaleni unokukhupha amakhadi eBolt kubantu abangenamakhaya abavumela ukuba bathenge evenkileni. Le ndlela inganceda abamkeli bakhe ukuzithemba kwakunye nezakhono zokulawula imali njengendawo yenkxaso abayifumanayo. Esi sisombululo sinokusetyenziswa nakwimeko yongxamiseko ukuqinisekisa ukuba iinkonzo zentlawulo ziyafumaneka kwiindawo ezifunekayo ngokukhawuleza nangexabiso eliphantsi.

Amandla okwenza nokufumana iintlawulo ngaphandle kwemfuneko yesazisi semthethweni aya kuphucula nokusebenza kwamaGosa obuNgqondi (AI) kwaye akhawulezise izibonelelo zoqoqosho lwehlabathi oluvela kwezi teknoloji zintsha. La magosa anokufuna ukusebenzisa nokufumana iintlawulo ukuze enze imisebenzi yawo. Okwangoku akukho siseko sokuba amaGosa obuNgqondi abonise isazisi kuba awanalo ifomu esemthethweni.

I-Bitcoin inika indlela kubantu abajongene nemigaqo-nkqubo enzima, engenelela okanye ecinezela ukuba bathathe inxaxheba kuqoqosho lwehlabathi kwaye ngaloo ndlela bandise inkululeko yabo yoqoqosho.

I-Bitcoin ayifuni nto malunga nesini, kuquka nokuba akukho sifo kwimeko yamaGosa obuNgqondi. Sele bekho imizekelo apho abantu abacinezelweyo ngenxa yesini sabo bekwazile ukusebenzisa iBitcoin ukulwa nolu cinezelo.

Ubunini beBitcoin abufuni ukuba bubonakale ngokwasemzimbeni, kwaye oku kunceda abantu abajongene nokuxhatshazwa kwentlalo kwaye bavinjwe zizalamane ukuba babe neakhawunti yebhanki.

I-Bitcoin ayinayo ingcamango yejografi kwaye ngenxa yoko urhwebo nalo naliphi na ilizwe emhlabeni, nokuba ngowuphi umthumeli okanye umamkeli, lunokwenzeka ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka. Izohlwayo zopolitiko, ezihlala zenzakalisa abona bangenamandla kuluntu, zinokuphepheka ngabemi abavelisayo besenza imisebenzi yabo yemihla ngemihla besebenzisa iBitcoin. Xa ezopolitiko zehlabathi zivelisa iindawo ezininzi zebhanki ezithintela ukukhula koqoqosho, ukusebenzisa iBitcoin kunokugcina urhwebo luqhuba kakuhle.

Ukuba umntu akavunyelwanga iinkonzo zebhanki kuba ibhanki yakhe igqibe kwelokuba uyingozi ngenxa yokuzibandakanya kwezopolitiko, ukufikelela kwi-bitcoin kunye nezisombululo zentlawulo zeBitcoin kunokuba yipolisi yokuqinisekisa elungileyo.

Imvume engenamda yeBitcoin inika amathuba amaninzi kubantu abajongene okanye abanokujongana nemiba yeebhanki. Oku kulindeleke ukuba kunciphise impembelelo yezizathu zesazisi ezibangela ukungabikho (okanye ukungonelanga) kwebhanki.

##### Uthembano

Ngokungafaniyo nokusebenzisa iibhanki okanye iinkonzo zentlawulo, iBitcoin ayifuni kuthembana nabanye abantu, iinkampani okanye iziko. IBitcoin ayifuni kuthembana kwaye ayifuni mvume. Xa umntu eqonda indlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo, angagqiba kwelokuba kuphela kuthembana okufunekayo kukwimathematika nasefiziksi. Ungaba yibhanki yakho ngokwakho.

Ungakhetha ukugcina enye ye-bitcoin yakho ebhankini, kodwa unenketho. Oku kuthetha ukuba ukuba unengxaki yokuthembana ngebhanki ekhoyo kuwe, ubuncinane unokwahlula loo ngozi ngokugcina ubuncinane enye ye-bitcoin yakho kwibhanki yakho wedwa.

Ukuba ukungathembani kubangelwa lixhala lokhuseleko lwedatha, iBitcoin inika enye indlela ngenxa yokuba ayifuni mvume. Akukho mfuneko yokunika naluphi na ulwazi lomntu ukuze usebenzise iBitcoin kwaye ngenxa yoko akukho ngxaki yokuthembana ivela.

Xa ukungathembani kuvela kwixabiso elingaqinisekanga okanye ukwamkelwa kwentlawulo kwemali yendawo, iBitcoin inika uncedo kuba yimali yehlabathi. Akukhathaliseki ukuba umsebenzisi ukweyiphi indawo emhlabeni, bonke babelana nge-bitcoin enye kwaye banokugcina, bathumele kwaye bamkele ngendlela efanayo nanabanye abasebenzisi beBitcoin kwihlabathi liphela.

Ixabiso leBitcoin linokwahluka, kodwa liyahluka naphi na ngexesha elinye. Ukungazinzi kweBitcoin kuya kuncipha ngokuhamba kwexesha njengoko inethiwekhi yabasebenzisi ikhula. Ayigqibelelanga njengendawo yokugcina ixabiso okanye indlela yokutshintshiselana, kodwa ngokuqinisekileyo yindlela enye ethe ngcono rhoqo. Ngenxa yezizathu ezi, iyimbangi ekhoyo kwaye ekhula kwiinkqubo zebhanki ezikhoyo kunye neemali abasebenza ngazo.

##### Isimilo

I-Bitcoin yimali enesimilo. Oko kukuthi, akukho mntu, iqela labantu, iziko okanye urhulumente onelungelo elikhethekileyo lokuphazamisa ukusebenza kwayo ngenxa yenzuzo yabo. IBitcoin ayinayo nengcamango yemali-mboleko enenzala. Iinkolo ezininzi kunye neembono zefilosofi zikholelwa ukuba ukuboleka imali ngenzala akulunganga. Kubantu abanezizimvo, iBitcoin inika indlela yokuba bavelise ngokwezoqoqosho, benze kwaye bamkele iintlawulo kwaye bagcine imali ngaphandle kokungena kwinkqubo yebhanki abayicinga ukuba ayilunganga.

Umahluko wokuziphatha kweBitcoin unika amathuba kwabanye abantu abakhoyo ngoku beziva bekhutshelwe ngaphandle koqoqosho lwehlabathi ngenxa yeenkolelo zonqulo okanye zefilosofi ukuba bathathe inxaxheba. Oku kulindeleke ukuba kunciphise impembelelo yezizathu zokuziphatha ezibangela ukungabikho (okanye ukungonelanga) kwebhanki.

Ukungabikho ebhankini kugxile ikakhulu kwiMazantsi eHlabathi, nangona amanye amalungu oluntu nakwamazwe aphuhlileyo engabikho ebhankini. Nangona kunjalo, ukungonelanga kwebhanki yingxaki ejamelwa phantse ngumntu wonke emhlabeni.

Kakhulu xa kuthelekiswa ne-stablecoins, iBitcoin inikezela ngezinye izibonelelo. IBitcoin inokuphelisa iingxaki zokuziphatha ezinxulumene nemboleko kunye nenzala ezisekela imali ye-fiat kunye ne-stable coins. Ukungakhethi cala kwayo kukwanika ithuba elingadibananga nenkxaso-mali yetyala karhulumente othile kunye nemiba yokuziphatha kunye nezopolitiko enokuthi ivele. IBitcoin ikwanika nendlela yokugcina ixabiso ixesha elide ngendlela engadingi mvume.

Indibaniselwano yeBitcoin yeempawu ezintsha kuzo zonke iindawo ezichongiweyo ezibangela ukungabikho kweebhanki (okanye ukungabikho ngokupheleleyo) ngoku, inokwazisa izisombululo ezintsha kunye nokunyusa ukhuphiswano. Kucacile ukuba oku kuya kukhokelela ekwehleni kokungabikho kweebhanki (okanye ukungabikho ngokupheleleyo) kubantu abaninzi kwihlabathi lonke.
