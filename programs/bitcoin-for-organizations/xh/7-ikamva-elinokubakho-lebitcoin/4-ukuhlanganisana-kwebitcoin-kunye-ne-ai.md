# 7.4 Ukuhlanganisana kweBitcoin kunye ne-AI

#### 7.4.0 Intshayelelo

![HTTP/1.1](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/aaa601f5809d610ab73750a71d4a3db5b63b06b8-744x462.png)

UTim Berners-Lee wabona urhwebo kwiwebhu njengento engenakuphepheka, kwaye waqala ukumisela iinkqubo zokuyixhasa phambi kokuba imarike ikhona. Kwisicatshulwa sokuqala se-RFC (Request for Comment) echaza i-HTTP, ikhowudi yemposiso 402 yafakwa ukuze ijongane nezicelo zentlawulo. Nangona le nto ingazange isetyenziswe kangangeminyaka, ubukho bayo bubonisa ukuba abayili bokuqala bewebhu babebona urhwebo njengengxenye ephambili yalo mbono.

Kube nezamalinge ezininzi kule minyaka idlulileyo zokwenza ukuba kube lula ukuhlawula imali encinci kwi-intanethi, kodwa ziye zaphumelela ngenxa yezizathu ezahlukeneyo. Kodwa ukuqonda kwangaphambili kukaTim Berners-Lee kubeka isiseko sokuba oonozakuzaku be-Artificial Intelligence bakwazi ukusebenza ngokuzimeleyo besebenzisa inethiwekhi ye-Lightning.

Nangona kukho iindlela ezininzi zokuhlawula ngoku, umzekelo iPaypal kunye neApple okanye iGoogle Pay, azamkelwa kwihlabathi liphela kwaye zixhomekeke kwimigaqo yeebhanki. Ezi zithintelo zithetha ukuba ukuhlawula imali encinci okanye ukuhlawula rhoqo akukwazi ukwenzeka ngokwezoqoqosho kusetyenziswa iinkqubo zemali zangoku. Imigangatho ephezulu yeBitcoin efana ne-Lightning Network inokuzisa ezi zakhono ngendleko ezifikelelekayo. Kukho imimandla emininzi apho uphuhliso lwezi zisombululo zentlawulo lunokuzisa impembelelo enkulu ekukhuleni. Enye yale mimandla kukuhlawula imali encinci kwi-Artificial Intelligence.

Oonozakuzaku be-Artificial Intelligence abakwazi ukwenza okanye ukufumana iintlawulo kwinkqubo ekhoyo ngoku kuba abanazazisi zabantu okanye zomthetho. Kukho ithuba lokukhokela nokwenza iimveliso eziza kunceda oonozakuzaku be-AI ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele iintlawulo. Iibhanki ezikhoyo kunye nababoneleli beenkonzo zentlawulo banokuphuhlisa itekhnoloji enokuphucula oku. Kuba le marike ayikabikho, ayisosoyikiso, kodwa inokuba lithuba eliphuthelweyo. IBitcoin ayifuni zazi zabantu ukuze isebenze, ngoko ke kunokwenzeka ukuba oonozakuzaku be-AI baya kusebenzisa itekhnoloji eyakhelwe kwiBitcoin ukuze bafumane, bathumele kwaye bamkele imisebenzi yentlawulo ngexesha elithile.



#### 7.4.1 Ithuba

Indibaniselwano yeBitcoin kunye ne-Artificial Intelligence idala ithuba lentsha yokuqamba izinto ezintsha kwidijithali, ngakumbi ngokudityaniswa kwe-AI kunye ne-Lightning Network yeBitcoin. Olu manyano lujolise ekuguquleni imiba ye-intanethi, ukusuka kwiintlawulo ezincinci ukuya koonozakuzaku beqoqosho abaqhutywa yi-AI kwi-intanethi. Eli candelo lijonga ukudibana okukhulayo phakathi kwengqalasizinda yeBitcoin kunye netekhnoloji ye-Artificial Intelligence (AI), ligxininisa imimandla ephambili yokudibana kwengqalasizinda yetekhnoloji kunye nokusetyenziswa okusebenzayo. Oku kubandakanya:

* Zombini iinkampani zemigodi yeBitcoin kunye nababoneleli beenkonzo ze-AI baxhomekeke kwimfuno etshintshayo yamandla okusebenza; abavukuzi ngexesha lokungabi nenzuzo okanye ukuhla kwemarike, kunye neenkonzo ze-AI ezihlala zixhomekeke kumsebenzi othile okanye iprojekthi kwaye azidingi ukusebenza imini nobusuku. Utyalo-mali kwiziko ledatha kunye nengqalasizinda yokupholisa yemigodi yeBitcoin okanye i-AI lunokubuyiselwa lula ukuba luyafumaneka kuzo zombini iintlobo zokusetyenziswa.
* I-AI kunye nemigodi yeBitcoin emaphethelweni: izinto ezintsha zetekhnoloji ezifana nokupholisa ulwelo zivumela imigodi yeBitcoin ukuba yehle iindleko, de kube iseti encinci inokudityaniswa kwindawo ukuze ihlangabezane nemfuno yeshishini okanye yoluntu yokufudumeza, njengamachibi okuqubha kawonke-wonke, iinkqubo zokufudumeza zoluntu, amagreenhouse okanye amaziko ezilwanyana zaselwandle. Njengoko ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI kukhula, ukukwazi ukusasaza amandla okusebenza kufutshane nabasebenzisi kuya kunceda ukuhambisa umgangatho ofunekayo wokusebenza ngokwempendulo ekhawulezayo.
* Njengoko iinkonzo ze-AI zikhula, kulindeleke ukuba kubekho imfuno enkulu yokulawula iintlawulo ezincinci ukuze oonozakuzaku be-AI bagqibe imisebenzi. I-ecosystem yehlabathi yeengcali ze-AI kwiinkonzo ezifana nokuguqulela ulwimi okanye ukuguqula umbhalo ube lilizwi iyaphuhliswa kwaye iya kufuna imali yedijithali, yehlabathi, engenamvume yokurhweba ngayo, eyi-bitcoin. Abayili bokuqala bomgaqo-nkqubo we-intanethi babebone le mfuno kwaye bakha iinkqubo kumgaqo-nkqubo wokuqala we-HTTP, ongakhange ufumane ukusetyenziswa kude kube ngoku.
* Iitekhnoloji ze-AI zinokunceda iBitcoin ngokwayo, ziphucule ukhuseleko lwenethiwekhi kunye nomgaqo-nkqubo ngokuchonga imisebenzi enetyhefu, mhlawumbi kwi-blockchain ngokwayo okanye kwimisebenzi yeepuli zemigodi.

Iinkampani ezijolise kwi-AI kunye noluntu lweBitcoin ziya kuxhamla ekuqondeni olu didi lutsha phakathi kwezi teknoloji zimbini.



#### 7.4.2 Ukudibana kwengqalasizinda yokubala

Zombini iinkampani zemigodi yeBitcoin kunye neenkonzo ze-AI zixhomekeke kwizixhobo ezikhethekileyo ezingahambelani ngqo, kodwa zombini zisebenzisa amandla amaninzi zifuna iinkqubo zokupholisa ezisebenzayo kunye nolawulo lwamandla, kunye nonxibelelwano lwenethiwekhi kunye nolawulo lwezixhobo zomzimba. Olu didi lukhokelele ekubeni ishishini lemigodi yeBitcoin lijikele kwimisebenzi ye-AI. Iinzuzo ezinokubakho ziquka:

* **Ukusebenza ngexesha lokungasebenzi**: Izixhobo zemigodi yeBitcoin zihlala zineendawo zokungasebenzi, ngakumbi ngexesha lokungabi nenzuzo okanye ukuhla kwemarike. Xa imigodi inganenzuzo, iinkampani zinokujika zisebenze kwiiprojekthi ze-AI, ngaloo ndlela ziqinisekisa ukuba izixhobo zazo zihlala zisetyenziswa. Izixhobo zemigodi yeBitcoin zinokusebenzisa amaxesha okungasebenzi xa iinkonzo ze-AI zingasetyenziswa, kwaye zinokucinywa kwangoko xa imfuno inyuka.
* **Ukwahlula imithombo yengeniso**: Ngokongeza iinkonzo ze-AI kumzekelo weshishini labo, iinkampani zemigodi yeBitcoin zinokudala umthombo omtsha wengeniso. Ukubonelela ngeenkonzo zokubala ze-AI kumashishini okanye abaphandi kunokulinganisa ukungazinzi kwengeniso evela kwimigodi yeBitcoin.
* **Uzinzo kunye nokusebenza kakuhle**: Imisebenzi ye-AI ihlala isebenzisa amandla amancinci kunemigodi kwaye inokwenziwa ngexesha apho amaxabiso ombane enyuka okanye xa ingeniso yemigodi iphantsi. Oku kunceda ukunciphisa iindleko zamandla kunye nokunciphisa umkhondo wekhabhoni onxulumene nemigodi.
* **Imbuyekezo yotyalo-mali kwengqalasizinda**: Utyalo-mali kwiziko ledatha kunye nengqalasizinda yokupholisa yemigodi yeBitcoin lunokubuyiselwa lula ukuba lusetyenziswa nakwi-AI, lwenza ingqalasizinda ibe nengeniso ngakumbi ekuhambeni kwexesha.

Iinkampani ezifana ne-Applied Digital kunye ne-Iris Energy ziqalile ukwandisa kakhulu kwi-AI cloud computing kunye neziko ledatha elisebenza kakuhle, ngokwahlukeneyo, nto leyo ebonisa ukushukuma ukuya kwimisebenzi eqhutywa yi-AI. Olu tshintsho aluboniwa njengokushiya imigodi yeBitcoin kodwa njengendlela yokwahlula imithombo yengeniso, kuncitshiswa ukuxhomekeka kwiimeko zemarike yeBitcoin kwaye kwamkelwa icandelo le-AI elikhulayo. I-Hut8 yenye inkampani etyale imali kwiziko ledatha elixhotyiswe ngeeGPU zeNvidia, ezikwaziyo ukuphatha imisebenzi eyahlukeneyo kubandakanya i-AI kunye nokufunda koomatshini, kunye nezixhobo zabo zemigodi yeBitcoin. Olu didi lwemigodi kunye nemisebenzi yeziko ledatha lubonisa amandla obudlelwane bokusebenzisana phakathi kweBitcoin PoW kunye ne-AI, apho amandla omabini anokuxhamla ekukhuthazeni ukuqamba izinto ezintsha kunye nokuqina koqoqosho lwedijithali.

Ngendlela efanayo, ababoneleli be-AI banokuxhamla ekwamkeleni imigodi yeBitcoin njengokulandelayo:

* **Ukusebenzisa amandla angasetyenziswanga**: Imisebenzi ye-AI ihlala isekwe kwiprojekthi kwaye ayidingi ukusebenza imini nobusuku kuzo zonke izixhobo. Ngexesha lokungasebenzi, ababoneleli be-AI banokusebenzisa amandla okusebenza angasetyenziswanga kwimigodi yeBitcoin ukuze bafumane ingeniso eyongezelelweyo.
* **Ukunciphisa iindleko zengqalasizinda**: Iindleko zokuqala zokwakha ingqalasizinda ye-AI ziphezulu, kodwa imigodi ngexesha lokungasebenzi inokunciphisa ezi ndleko. Imigodi ingaba ngumthombo wesibini wengeniso, inike umkhusane xa imfuno ye-AI okanye izivumelwano zabathengi zitshintsha.
* **Ukusebenzisa ii-chip ze-ASIC**: Njengoko imisebenzi ye-AI ikhula, kwaye uphuhliso lwee-chip luqhubeka, ezinye ii-ASIC ezenzelwe ukufunda nzulu zinokukwazi ukuxhasa imisebenzi enxulumene neBitcoin. Kwezi meko, ababoneleli be-AI banokusebenzisa izixhobo zabo kuzo zombini, nangona oku kuya kufuna izixhobo eziguquguqukayo, ezingekabikho okwangoku.

Iimodeli zeshishini ezinokwenziwa oku:

* **Iziko ledatha elinemisebenzi emibini**: Iinkampani zinokwakha iziko ledatha elilungele zombini i-AI kunye nemigodi, kunye nengqalasizinda eguquguqukayo yokwamkela imisebenzi eyahlukeneyo ngokuxhomekeka kwimfuno, ingeniso, kunye nokufumaneka kwezixhobo.
* **I-AI kunye nemigodi njengeNkonzo (AMaaS)**: Ukubonelela ngenkonzo yokubala ye-AI kunye nemigodi yeBitcoin kubathengi bangaphandle kunokwenza ukusetyenziswa kwengqalasizinda kube ngcono ngelixa kwahlulwa imithombo yengeniso. Iinkampani zinokwenza ngokuzenzekelayo utshintsho lwemisebenzi ngokuxhomekeka kwinzuzo, iimfuno zabathengi, okanye iimeko zemarike.
* **Iinyathelo zokubala eziluhlaza**: Iinkampani ezigxile kuzinzo zinokusebenzisa amandla avuselelekayo kwiziko labo elinemisebenzi emibini, bazibeke njengabakhathalela indalo kuzo zombini i-AI kunye neBitcoin.

Ukudibanisa imigodi yeBitcoin kunye nokubala kwe-AI kunzima kodwa kunokwenzeka xa kukho ingqalasizinda efanelekileyo kunye neendlela ezifanelekileyo. Ukudibanisa le misebenzi kunokwandisa ukusetyenziswa kwezixhobo, kuphucule uzinzo, kwaye kwahlule imithombo yengeniso kwiinkampani ezilungele ukujongana nemingeni yetekhnoloji kunye nokusebenza okuhambelanayo.



#### 7.4.3 I-AI kunye nemigodi yeBitcoin emaphethelweni

Iziko ledatha elixineneyo nelincinci elisasazwe emaphandleni endaweni yokugxilwa kwiziko ledatha elikhulu linokuzisa izibonelelo ezininzi:

* Abavukuzi beBitcoin bakhangela imithombo yamandla etshiphu nethembekileyo kwaye banokubekwa ngqo apho la mandla aveliswa khona. Ngamanye amaxesha, ubushushu obuveliswa bunokusetyenziswa ngamashishini ahlukeneyo njengamachibi okuqubha asekuhlaleni, amagreenhouse kunye neenkqubo zokufudumeza zoluntu, buyitshintshe ukusuka kwindleko bube luncedo.
* Ukushenxisa imisebenzi ye-AI kufutshane nomsebenzisi endaweni yokugxilwa kwiziko ledatha ezimbalwa ezinkulu kunokuphucula ukusebenza ngokusasaza amandla okusebenza kunye nokunciphisa ukulibaziseka. Ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI kwimisebenzi efana nohlalutyo lwevidiyo ye-CCTV, iimoto ezizihambelayo kunye nokubeka iliso kwengqalasizinda ye-IoT kunokuphucula amandla eenkonzo kunye nokusebenza.

Iinkampani ezakha ingqalasizinda yokusasaza imigodi yeBitcoin okanye iinkonzo ze-AI zinokuxhamla xa ziqwalasela ukudityaniswa kwezi zisombululo zombini kuyilo lwengqalasizinda, zisusa amandla okusebenza kufutshane nabasebenzisi kunye nokuxhamla kwimithombo yamandla avuselelekayo etshiphu.



#### 7.4.4 Ulawulo lwee-micropayments kwiinkonzo ze-AI usebenzisa iBitcoin

Okokuqala imbali encinci: Yintoni i-402 Payment Required?

**HTTP Status Code**: Ikhowudi ye-HTTP 402 Payment Required yinxalenye yomgaqo-nkqubo we-HTTP, ochaza indlela imiyalezo efomathwa ngayo kwaye ithunyelwa kwiwebhu. Yayiyenzelwe ukuba ibe yindlela yokuba iseva yewebhu ibonise ukuba umxhasi kufuneka agqibe intlawulo ukuze afumane imithombo ayicelileyo. Nangona iyinxalenye yomgaqo-nkqubo we-HTTP, ikhowudi 402 ayikaze isetyenziswe kakhulu. Okwangoku isagcinelwe iimeko ezinokuphuma kwixesha elizayo, ngakumbi njengoko iimodeli zentlawulo kwi-intanethi ziguquka. Iintlawulo zedijithali kunye ne-microtransactions zinokubonelela ngeempendulo ezisemgangathweni xa umsebenzisi ezama ukufikelela kwinkonzo okanye ukwenza ukuthenga okuncinci engenamali eyaneleyo, okanye ingasetyenziswa kwakhona ukuphatha iintlawulo kwinkqubo esekwe kwindawo ezininzi ezifana nokuphumeza isivumelwano esihlakaniphile. Esikugxile apha kukusetyenziswa kwe-micropayments yeBitcoin kwimisebenzi ye-AI.

##### Umngeni weshishini

Iindlela zokuhlawula ezixhaphakileyo ezisetyenziswa ngamapulatifomu e-AI namhlanje zindala, zidlulisela iindleko kubasebenzisi kwaye zinciphisa iimeko zokusebenzisa kunye nokufikelela, kwaye zisebenzisa iindlela ezizodwa nezibiza kakhulu. Zisebenza kakuhle kwiintlawulo ezinkulu okanye iimodeli zokubhalisa, kodwa kwiintlawulo ezincinci iindleko ezongezelelweyo zenza ukuba zingabi nenzuzo, apho nokuba yimali encinci kakhulu ngentengiselwano ingaba yinqobo.

Kumazwe aphuhlileyo, imodeli yokubhalisa esekwe kwikhadi letyala ukuze kufumaneke inkonzo ephezulu ingasebenza, kodwa oku akufumaneki kwamanye amazwe. Oku kungaba ngumngeni xa usebenza kwiqela lehlabathi elidinga ukufikelela kwiinkonzo zokubhalisa ukuze likwazi ukunceda. Iintlawulo nazo zingaphikiswa ngumsebenzisi kamva, nto leyo ekhokelela ekubuyisweni kwemali sele isetyenzisiwe kwizixhobo zokubala.

Ama-ejenti e-AI nawo awanazo izazisi zomthetho ezinokusetyenziswa ukufumana iiakhawunti zebhanki okanye iinkonzo zokuhlawula kwinkqubo yemali yesintu, kwaye engasebenzi iiyure ezingama-24 ngosuku. I-Bitcoin ayifuni sazisi yomthetho kwaye ke inika indlela yokuba izidalwa ezingengomntu ezifana nama-ejenti e-AI zigcine ixabiso, zithumele kwaye zamkele iintlawulo.

Lightning Labs – yinkampani yeziseko ze-Lightning – isungule iseti yezixhobo ezijolise ekoyiseni ezi zithintelo ngokudibanisa iintlawulo ezincinci ze-Bitcoin ezisebenza ngobuninzi be-Lightning kwiilayibrari ze-AI ezidumileyo, ivula amathuba amatsha:

##### Iimodeli ze-AI ezihlawulwa ngokwemibuzo.

Ngokwenza isoftware ye-AI ikwazi ukuhlawulisa ukufikelela kwi-API. Ama-ejenti e-AI angasebenzisa i-Lightning ukuhlawula ukufikelela kwi-API xa ebuzisa kwamanye ama-ejenti. Ama-ejenti e-AI aqhuba iintlawulo kuphela emva kokufumana impendulo eyanelisayo, eqinisekisa iintengiselwano ezifanelekileyo nezisebenzayo. Ezi ntlawulo zikwangokugqibela.


> **Definition – Retrieval Augmented Generation**
>
> **Retrieval Augmented Generation** (RAG) yindlela entle yokuthi "hamba kwenye indawo ukufumana iinkcukacha kwaye uzidibanise nempendulo ye-AI chatbot yam"


##### Iinkonzo zokwenza umxholo nge-AI

I-AI eyenza umxholo inokuvelisa zombini umbhalo nomfanekiso wokuthengisa, apho abathengisi bokukhula banokungena kwiGoogle okanye kwiFacebook's advertising centre, balayishe umfanekiso nombhalo, basete uhlahlo lwabiwo-mali lwemihla ngemihla, kwaye bacinezele iqhosha lokuqala ukuzama ukufumana abantu ukuba bathenge imveliso okanye inkonzo yabo. Oku kusebenzisa uhlobo lwe-AI agent, kodwa lukhawulelwe kule meko yokusebenzisa kuphela.

Ukwandisa le ngcamango kwezinye iimeko zokusebenzisa kufuna iintlawulo ezincinci ukuze kuvunyelwe eminye yale misebenzi kwaye kunokwenzeka kufune iintlawulo ezihamba ngexesha lokwenyani.

Lightning HTTP 402 Protocol, eyaziwa njenge L402, yindlela yokuhlawulisa iinkonzo kunye nokuqinisekisa abasebenzisi kwiinethiwekhi ezisasazekileyo. Idibanisa izixhobo ezimbini ezinamandla — iiMacaroons, kunye ne-Lightning Network.

IiMacaroons ziithokheni ezizodwa ezisetyenziswa ukuqinisekisa. Ziquka iimvume kwaye zingaqinisekiswa kusetyenziswa isitshixo sengcambu. Uxwebhu luthi oku kubalulekile kwiinkqubo apho sifuna ukuphepha okanye asikwazi ukukhangela ukuba ithokheni nganye isebenza na.

I-Lightning sisombululo seLayer 2 sokwenza iintlawulo ze-bitcoin ezikhawulezayo nezikhuselekileyo. I-L402 isebenzisa amandla eeMacaroons kunye ne-Lightning ukwenza indlela evumela abasebenzisi ukuba baqinisekise kwaye benze iintlawulo ngaphandle kwesidingo sedatha ephakathi.

Kwi-L402, iMacaroon iquka i-payment hash. Ukuze isebenze, umsebenzisi kufuneka anike iMacaroon kunye ne-preimage ehambelana ne-payment hash kwiMacaroon. I-preimage ifumaneka ngokuhlawula i-invoice ye-Lightning Network.

Isofthiwe entsha ebizwa ngokuba yi-Aperture isebenza njengomlamli phakathi komsebenzisi kunye ne-API yenkonzo. Idlulisa izicelo ezine-L402 esebenzayo kwi-API endpoint efanelekileyo kwaye inokukhupha iiMacaroons ezintsha kunye nee-invoice ze-Lightning kubasebenzisi abatsha.

I-L402 ivumela ii-API ezilinganisiweyo, apho iinkonzo zinokuhlawulisa ukusetyenziswa kwazo ngaphandle kwesidingo sokungena okanye iipassword. IMacaroon, kunye ne-preimage, iqinisekisa ukuba umhlawuli wenze intlawulo.

Le ngcamango ibaluleke kakhulu kwimeko yeentengiselwano phakathi kwama-AI. Ama-ejenti e-AI angakwazi ukwenza iintlawulo ezincinci ngokufanelekileyo, evula amathuba amatsha oqoqosho. Umzekelo, i-AI ingahlawula ngokuzenzekelayo imali encinci ukufikelela kulwazi, izixhobo zokubala, okanye iinkonzo ezikhethekileyo kwamanye ama-ejenti e-AI. Oku kungakhokelela kulwabiwo lwezixhobo olusebenzayo, iimodeli zoshishino ezintsha, kunye nokukhula koqoqosho olukhawulezayo kuqoqosho lwedijithali.

##### Iimeko Zokusebenzisa Ezisebenzayo

1. Umdibaniso wama-ejenti e-AI kunye nezixhobo ze-IoT ngokusebenzisa iinethiwekhi zezixhobo zomzimba ezisasazekileyo unokukhokelela kwiinkqubo ezizimeleyo ezilawula izixhobo, zisebenzise iinkqubo, kwaye zisebenze kuqoqosho ngokuzimeleyo.
1. Kwicandelo lomxholo, iinkqubo ze-AI zingazenza, zipapashe, kwaye zifumane ingeniso kumxholo, zilawula ingeniso ngaphandle kongenelelo lomntu.
1. Iinkonzo zemali: Ama-ejenti e-AI angathengiselana ngexesha lokwenyani iiyure ezingama-24 ngosuku egameni leenkampani zemali ezinkulu, ngaphandle kwesidingo sokungenelela komntu. Imali eninzi ingabandakanyeka, mhlawumbi yokudluliselwa komngcipheko phakathi kweentlobo ezininzi zezixhobo kunye nezixhobo zemali, kwaye kusetyenziswa indibaniselwano ye-layer 2 kunye ne-base layer yokuhlala. I-Bitcoin (okanye i-stablecoin) ingasetyenziswa kuba inokuqwalaselwa ngama-ejenti e-AI ngokweemfuno zawo.
1. Icandelo lezothutho linokubona ukuvela kweemoto ezizimeleyo ngokupheleleyo ezikwaziyo ukubonelela ngeenkonzo zika-taxi ngokuzimeleyo, zamkele abakhweli, zamkele iintlawulo, kwaye zahlawule ukugcinwa kwazo.
1. Kwimveliso, ama-ejenti e-AI angazenzekelayo inkqubo yokuthenga, efumana kwaye ethenga izinto ezifunekayo ngokuzimeleyo.
1. Kwicandelo lezemisebenzi, iinkqubo ze-AI zingaqesha kwaye zihlawule abaqeshwa ngokuzimeleyo.
1. Amakhaya akrelekrele angazithengela ngokuzenzekelayo iimpahla kunye neenkonzo ezifunekayo.

##### Ukucinga ngekamva

Umphuhlisi we-AI angadala uthotho lwemisebenzi ekhethekileyo ye-AI, umzekelo uguqulelo kulwimi oluthile olungasetyenziswa kakhulu, okanye umbhalo ukuya kwisandi kunye nokuveliswa komxholo kwicandelo elithile. Aba ma-ejenti e-AI banokujonga iiwebhusayithi okanye amagumbi encoko befuna izicelo ezihlangabezana nemfuno ethile baze bakhuphisane ngomsebenzi - bakhulule umxholo owenziweyo kuphela emva kokuba uphononongwe wamkelwe kwaye emva kokuhlawulwa.

Eli kamva likufuphi kunokuba sicinga ngenxa yokunyuka okukhulu kokusebenza kwe-AI kunye namandla ayo - kodwa kuya kufuna i-bitcoin ukuze iphumelele.

Ukulungisa iimodeli ze-AI, inyathelo elibalulekileyo kuphuhliso lwe-AI, nako kungazuza kwi-Lightning Network. Ngokwenza iintlawulo ezincinci nezikhawulezayo, abantu kwihlabathi liphela banokuthatha inxaxheba ekulungiseni i-AI, behlawulwa ngomsebenzi ngamnye nge-bitcoin. Le nkqubo isebenzisa ukufikelela kwehlabathi kwe-intanethi, apho malunga nezigidi ezi-4.32 zabasebenzisi be-intanethi abaseselfowuni banokubandakanywa kwinkqubo yophuhliso lwe-AI.

I-Bitcoin ikwangumsindisi kumazwe amaninzi asakhulayo apho inokuba yindlela yokugcina imali, ukuvulela abantu abangenaso isixhobo semali, kunye nokwenza ukuba abasebenzi abafudukayo bathumele imali ekhaya ngexabiso eliphantsi. Kumazwe anenkqubo yemali esebenzayo, ezi misebenzi zinokwenziwa, kodwa kungasebenzi kakuhle kwaye kubize kakhulu. Nangona kunjalo, amandla okwenza iintlawulo ezincinci zemali encinci ngexesha lokwenyani kunye nokuqinisekiswa kokugqibela kwentlawulo yeenkonzo ze-AI ayinakufezekiswa ngenye itekhnoloji. I-Bitcoin kuphela kwendlela enokwenzeka yokuvumela olu hlobo lonxibelelwano lwe-AI, iyenza ibe yinxalenye ebalulekileyo yokukhula kwe-AI kwixesha elizayo.



#### 7.4.5 Ukhuseleko Lwenethiwekhi

Njengoko i-Bitcoin isetyenziswa ngakumbi kwaye ixabiso layo liqhubeka nokunyuka, inokuba yijelo elula kubagculeli kunye nabaphuli-mthetho be-cyber. Ukugculelwa kweewallets kunye neendawo zokutshintshiselana kudale inkxalabo kwaye kugxininisa imfuneko yokuphucula ukhuseleko. Kukho idatha eninzi ekhula eqokelelwa kwinkqubo ekufuneka i-AI ikwazi ukuyihlalutya kwaye ichonge izoyikiso ze-cyber ezinokubakho. Ngokuhlalutya imijelo yedatha ngexesha lokwenyani, i-AI ingaqaphela ukuziphatha okungaqhelekanga kwaye mhlawumbi ibonise izoyikiso ezinokubakho phambi kokuba zenzeke. Ukuchonga iphethini ebonwe kuhlaselo lwe-ransomware lwangaphambili olujolise kwiindawo zokutshintshiselana umzekelo, okanye ukwanda komthamo wetrafikhi ukusuka kuluhlu lwe-IP oluchongwe njengoluyingozi kunokunika amaqela okhuseleko ixesha lokuphendula kwaye athathe amanyathelo okuthintela uhlaselo olunjalo.

I-AI inokuba nako ukongeza imilinganiselo yokuziphatha kwizixhobo zokhuseleko ezifana ne-MFA ukuze ichonge iingxaki ezinokubakho. Iialgorithm ze-AI zingasebenzisa idatha yangaphandle efana nendlela umsebenzisi abamba ngayo isixhobo, indlela abachwetheza ngayo kunye neminye imiba ukuze ichonge ukuziphatha okungaqhelekanga kwaye ifune inqanaba eliphezulu lokuqinisekisa kumsebenzisi.

Njengoko i-AI ikhula, ukudibanisa ezi ntlobo zamandla kwi-wallets kunye neendawo zokutshintshiselana kunokuphucula ukhuseleko lwenethiwekhi ngokusebenzisa iialgorithm zokufunda ngomatshini eziphambili kunye nokuzenzekelayo okuqhutywa yi-AI ukuze kuphendulwe ngokukhawuleza kwizoyikiso ezinokubakho.

##### Ukusetyenziswa kwe-AI kwiipuli zokumba i-Bitcoin

Njengoko kuchaziwe ngaphambili, kukho izibonelelo ezinokubakho kwinkampani xa idibanisa iinkonzo ze-AI kunye nokumba i-Bitcoin, kodwa oku kuza nemingeni ethile. Amandla e-AI okufunda, ukuziqhelanisa kunye nokwenza iinkqubo zisebenze kakuhle anokwenza ukuba kube lula kwiziko ledatha, avumele abavukuzi ukuba benze izigqibo ezizizo malunga nokuba bangena nini emsebenzini wokumba ngokusekelwe kutshintsho lwamaxabiso ombane. Ukwenza ukusetyenziswa kwamandla kusebenze kakuhle kunokunceda ekunciphiseni iimfuno zamandla ngokubanzi kwaye ke kunciphise umkhondo wekhabhoni. Ngokwengxelo yakutshanje ye-KPMG, ukumba i-bitcoin kunegalelo ekuzinziseni iigridi zombane kwaye kunokusebenzisa amandla avuselelekayo awayeza kulahlekwa. Ukusebenzisa i-AI kule nkqubo kunokunceda ukwenza le nkqubo isebenze ngakumbi.

Nangona kunjalo, okwangoku kukho imida ethile ekufuneka ithathelwe ingqalelo:

* **Izithintelo Zesixhobo**: I-ASICs zokumba i-Bitcoin azihambelani nemisebenzi ye-AI, ngoko inkampani yokumba kufuneka ityalomali kwi-GPU okanye i-TPU ze-AI. Ngokuchaseneyo, iziseko ze-AI ezisekwe kwi-GPU okanye i-TPU azizukusebenza kakuhle ekumbini kune-ASICs ezizinikeleyo kwaye akulindelekanga ukuba zisebenze kakuhle ngolu hlobo lwetekhnoloji yangoku.
* **Ulawulo Lwamandla**: Ukumba kunye ne-AI zombini zifuna amandla amaninzi, kwaye ukuqhuba zombini ngobuninzi kunokuxinzelela izixhobo zalapha. Inkampani kufuneka ibe nesicwangciso esiphuhliswe kakuhle solawulo lwamandla ukuphepha iindleko eziphezulu okanye iingxaki zomthetho.
* **Ukulinganisa Imithwalo yomsebenzi kunye Nezinto Ezibalulekileyo**: Imisebenzi yokubala ye-AI ihlala inamaxesha okugqiba kunye ne-SLA ekufuneka ihlangatyezwane nazo, ngelixa ukumba i-Bitcoin kuyinkqubo eqhubekayo. Ukulinganisa imithwalo yomsebenzi kufuna ishedyuli enenkathalo kwaye kunokukhokelela kutshintsho ekusebenzeni okanye ekufumanekeni.
* **Iimfuno zenethiwekhi kunye neziseko zokugcina**: I-Bitcoin ifuna i-bandwidth encinci ukudibanisa kwinethiwekhi, i-AI compute iza kufuna ukuhambisa idatha eninzi efuna unxibelelwano olukhawulezayo. Iimfuno zokugcina ziya kwahluka - i-Bitcoin ilungiselelwe ukufuna indawo encinci yokugcina ukuze nabani na akwazi ukuthatha inxaxheba ngesixhobo esisezantsi. Imithwalo yomsebenzi ye-AI iza kufuna indawo enkulu yokugcina.
