# 8.0 Gabatarwa

> Don fahimtar Bitcoin, dole ne ka fahimci matsalar da yake warwarewa  
_Wences Cesares_

Ana gina wata babbar hanyar tattalin arziki ta duniya a kusa da hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin da kuma karɓuwarsa a matsayin wurin ajiya na ƙima da kuma hanyar musayar kudi. Wannan tsarin ya ƙunshi kasuwanni na ajiya, ayyukan biyan kuɗi, kayan aiki da ayyuka a cikin masana'antar hakar ma'adinai da makamashi, da kuma manyan ayyukan kuɗi kamar ciniki da kayan musaya. Masu shiga cikin waɗannan kasuwanni sun haɗa da mutane da manyan cibiyoyi, duk suna mu'amala da wata hanyar sadarwa guda ɗaya da aka gina bisa tsarin software na buda-tushen lamba.

A tsakiyar wannan tsarin akwai wani littafin bayanai da aka rarraba a duniya baki ɗaya, wanda kowa zai iya duba shi kuma yana rubuta kowace mu'amala a fili. Halayen rashin tsaka-tsaki da kuma iya gani na wannan littafi da bayanan da ke ƙasa da shi suna ba da damar yin nazarin tattalin arziki a ainihin lokaci a matakin duniya ga mutane da cibiyoyi ba tare da dogaro da masu shiga tsakani ba. Saboda haka, sabanin tsarin kuɗi na gargajiya, yawancin ayyukan cikin gida na Bitcoin ana iya gani a fili.
