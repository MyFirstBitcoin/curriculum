# 8.2 Ma'aunai don Binciken Littafin Lissafi

Saboda gaskiyar bayanai a Bitcoin ya bambanta da tsarin kudi na gargajiya — inda yawancin kudade ke yawo a bayan ƙofofin cibiyoyi — hakan ya haifar da fagen nazarin bayanan sarkar kai tsaye, inda bayanan matakin cibiyar sadarwa ke zama madubi don fahimtar halayen masu amfani, yawo da kudi da kuma dogon lokaci. Wadannan ma'auni na iya taimakawa wajen amsa tambayoyi na musamman, kamar yadda ake amfani da cibiyar sadarwar, ko ana tara ko ana sayar da tsabar kudi, da kuma ko cibiyar sadarwar na kara samun tsaro.

Fahimtar wadannan ma'auni yana da amfani ba kawai ga masu amfani da Bitcoin ba, har ma ga masu bincike ko masu tsara manufofi da ke neman fahimta game da wannan tsarin kudi mai gaskiya.

Wannan sashe yana dauke da wasu daga cikin ma'auni da ake yawan amfani da su wajen nazarin ayyukan Bitcoin, an rarraba su zuwa ƙananan rukuni. Ba cikakken jerin bane. Ziyarci [www.bitcoinmagazinepro.com/charts](https://www.bitcoinmagazinepro.com/charts) don samun cikakken jerin da bayanai.



#### 8.2.1 Ma'aunin Adireshi

Ma'aunin adireshi yana da amfani a duba a tsawon lokaci domin yana nuna matakin aiki a cikin cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin. Misali, yayin da Bitcoin ke kara karbuwa, yawan adiresoshin da ke aiki yana karuwa. Za mu iya zurfafa wannan ta hanyar ware yawan adiresoshin da ke rike da akalla wani adadin Bitcoin, misali 0.1 BTC, a cikin wani lokaci, kamar shekara guda. Duk da yake wannan yana nuna yadda karbuwa ke gudana a tsawon lokaci, ba cikakke bane domin mutum daya na iya rike adiresoshi da dama. A daya bangaren, kasuwanni ko ETFs na iya bayyana a matsayin daya yayin da suke rike kudaden mutane da yawa.

![Bitcoin: Addresses Hodling > X BTC by Year](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/b88a9239820e45ed50ce00812170e2bb4d02b5d2-1407x766.png)

_Adiresoshi masu riƙe da Bitcoin > X BTC a kowace shekara. Tushe: Bitcoin Magazine Pro._

Ta hanyar kwatanta adiresoshi da farashin BTC na yanzu, ana iya ganin kashi na adiresoshin Bitcoin gaba ɗaya da ke samun riba. Wannan yana bamu damar bibiyar yanayin kasuwa domin za mu ga kaso na kasuwa da ke rike BTC da riba ko asara.

Misali, **Kashi na Ribar da Ba a Fitar ba** a jadawalin da ke ƙasa yana nuna kaso na dukkan adiresoshin ledger da ke da ribar da ba a fitar ba, auna da dalar Amurka. Lura cewa, tun da jadawalin da ke ƙasa an dauka kusa da mafi girman farashin Bitcoin, kaso na adiresoshin da ke nuna ribar da ba a fitar ba yana kusa da dari bisa dari. Hakanan za mu iya ganin cewa dogon lokaci na Kashi na Ribar da Ba a Fitar ba kasa da daya daga cikin matsakaita ba abu ne da aka saba ba. Saboda haka, sauka kasa da wannan layi na iya nuna lokaci mai kyau ga masu saye.

#### 8.2.2 Alamomin Sakar Kai Tsaye

Alamomin sakar kai tsaye suna da amfani domin suna ba da haske game da halayen cibiyar sadarwa, fiye da abin da farashi da ma'aunin adireshi kadai za su iya nuna. Suna taimakawa masu nazari su fahimci ayyuka da yanayin masu shiga daban-daban, kamar masu riƙe na dogon lokaci da 'yan kasuwa na gajeren lokaci ta hanyar bibiyar yadda ake riƙe, motsa ko darajar tsabar kudi a tsawon lokaci. Wadannan alamomi suna amfani da gaskiyar bayanan ledger don bayyana abubuwan da ke faruwa a kasuwa kamar tara kudi, rarrabawa, ko ma yakinin masu zuba jari. Wannan yana sa su zama masu amfani musamman wajen gano yanayin tsarin kasuwa, tantance ko kasuwa ta yi zafi ko ta yi ƙasa da kima, da kuma hango lokacin da kasuwa za ta canza.

Misali, ta hanyar duba darajar BTC tun lokacin da aka yi mu'amala da su na ƙarshe, za mu iya fahimta ko kasuwa na cikin damuwa (kamar yadda zai iya faruwa a lokacin mafi ƙasƙanci na zagaye). Wannan ma'auni ana kiransa da **Farashin da Aka Gane** kuma yana bamu 'matsakaicin farashin saye' na duk BTC da ke yawo. Idan farashin kasuwa ya sauka kasa da Farashin da Aka Gane, hakan yana nuna cewa mafi yawan adiresoshi suna rike da asara a takarda.

Ta hanyar rarraba bayanan ledger zuwa rukunan shekaru, za mu iya nuna yadda adadin BTC ke motsawa tsakanin adiresoshi a tsawon lokaci, wanda ke haifar da zane mai kama da igiya a jadawali da ake kira **HODL igiyoyi**.

![Bitcoin HODL Waves](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/ce108e45a1a7217e081101e4a276ee2d9e95a22e-1129x577.png)

_Bitcoin HODL Waves. Tushe: Bitcoin Magazine Pro._

HODL igiyoyi suna nuna abin da masu riƙe na dogon lokaci, matsakaici da gajeren lokaci ke yi da BTC dinsu. Misali, a jadawalin da ke sama, masu riƙe na gajeren lokaci ana nuna su da ja da orange kuma za mu ga tashin hankali a ayyuka yayin da wannan rukuni ke gaggauta saye kusa da kololuwar kasuwa. A daya bangaren, za mu ga cewa masu riƙe na dogon lokaci sosai (a shudi da purple) suna kara yawan su a cikin cibiyar sadarwa, wanda ke nuna yakini mai ƙarfi a cikin wannan rukuni. Jadawalin ba cikakke bane domin wasu tsabar kudi na iya motsawa daga tsofaffin zuwa sababbin adiresoshi a hannun mai amfani guda. Duk da haka, yana ba da kyakkyawan hangen nesa game da yakinin masu riƙe na dogon lokaci.

Wani hanya na duba 'kudin masu wayo' na masu riƙe na dogon lokaci shine duba **Ranar Tsabar Kudi da Aka Lalata** (CDD). Ma'anar 'Ranar Tsabar Kudi' ita ce adadin BTC da aka ninka da kwanakin da suka wuce tun bayan da aka motsa tsabar kudin ƙarshe. Misali, 5 BTC da bai motsa ba tsawon kwana 100 ya tara 500 ranakun tsabar kudi, kuma 10 BTC da bai motsa ba tsawon kwana 10 ya tara 100 ranakun tsabar kudi. Ta haka, muna ba da ƙarin nauyi ga tsabar kudi da aka riƙe na dogon lokaci. Idan aka motsa wadannan tsabar kudi, ranakun tsabar kudin sun 'lalace'. Wannan alama tana nuna karuwa a CDD a lokutan manyan motsin farashi, wanda ke ba masu nazari hanyar ware ayyukan kasuwa na yau da kullum daga manyan sauye-sauye a yanayin masu riƙe na dogon lokaci.

Wani ma'auni da zai iya taimakawa wajen gano ko kasuwa na ƙasa da kima ko sama da kima ga BTC shine Market-Value to Realised Value ko **MVRV**. Ana lissafa shi ne ta hanyar raba Market Value (adadin BTC da ke yawo da ninka da farashin kasuwa) da Realised Value (jimillar duk BTC tun bayan da aka motsa su ƙarshe). MVRV mai yawa yana nuna cewa yawancin tsabar kudi na da riba (ana gani kusa da kololuwar kasuwa) kuma MVRV mai ƙasa yana nuna da yawa na tsabar kudi na cikin asara (ana gani kusa da ƙasan kasuwa).



#### 8.2.3 Ma'aunin Ma'adinai

Ma'aunin ma'adinai yana da amfani wajen fahimtar tsaro, ribar tattalin arziki da lafiyar cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin gaba ɗaya. Ma'auni kamar hashrate, kudin shiga na masu hakar ma'adinai, wahala, da rabon kudin shiga daga kudin mu'amala suna bayyana yadda ake amfani da ƙarfin kwamfuta wajen kare blockchain da yadda masu hakar ma'adinai ke samun lada daga ayyukansu.

**Hashrate** na cibiyar sadarwar Bitcoin wataƙila shine mafi yawan ambato a matsayin alamar lafiyar cibiyar sadarwa da ƙarfinta. Tun da aikin hakar ma'adinai ke kare cibiyar sadarwa da tabbatar da sahihancin mu'amaloli a ledger, mafi girman matakin kwamfuta (ko hashing) da ake da shi, sai ya fi wahala ga mai mugunta ya mamaye ko kai hari ga cibiyar sadarwar.

![Bitcoin Hashrate](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/fcb4a24c431a37580d3d6c4ec62b664e7e41c362-1134x584.png)

_Bitcoin Hashrate. Tushe: Bitcoin Magazine Pro._

Jadawalin da ke sama yana nuna cewa, a watan Mayu 2025, jimillar ƙarfin kwamfuta na cibiyar sadarwa yana kusan 900 TeraHash/s (900 Tiriliyan lissafin 'hash' na lissafi a kowane dakika). Idan hashrate yana ƙaruwa, yana nuna cewa cibiyar sadarwa na ƙara samun tsaro, wanda ke kwantar da hankalin masu amfani.

Puell Multiple (wanda David Puell ya kirkiro) yana duba zagayen kasuwa daga hangen masu hakar ma'adinai da kudin shigar su. Ana lissafa ma'aunin ne ta hanyar raba adadin BTC da ake fitarwa kullum (a cikin dalar Amurka) da matsakaitan adadin da ake fitarwa kullum na kwanaki 365. Wannan ma'auni yana taimakawa wajen gano lokutan da masu hakar ma'adinai ke cikin matsin lamba ko sauki. A tarihi, adadi sama da 3 ya saba zuwa kafin saukar farashin BTC, domin yana nuna cewa masu hakar ma'adinai na samun riba sosai. Adadi kasa da 0.5 yana nuna matsin lamba kuma a tarihi yana nuna ƙasan kasuwa ga darajar BTC.
