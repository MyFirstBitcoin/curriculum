# 8.4 Ma'amalar Ranar Pizza

Zuƙowa yanzu, wannan bangare ya mayar da hankali ne kan amfani da bude tsarin rajistar Bitcoin don tattara muhimman bayanai daga bayanan hada-hadar da aka tara. Duk da haka, yana yiwuwa a yi amfani da bayanan rajista da masu binciken bulok don duba ainihin hada-hadar da aka yi a rayuwa da kuma bin diddigin motsin kudade a cikin hanyar sadarwa.

Kowace shekara a ranar 22 ga Mayu, al'ummar Bitcoin tana tunawa da Laszlo Hanyecz, wanda ya zama mutum na farko da aka ruwaito ya yi amfani da bitcoin wajen sayen kaya na zahiri. A ranar 18 ga Mayu 2010, Hanyecz ya sanar a dandalin Bitcointalk.org cewa yana neman pizza kuma yana son biya da BTC. Ya bayar da 10,000 BTC ga duk wanda zai amince da yin wannan mu'amala. Ya jira na wasu kwanaki, har sai wani dalibi mai shekaru 19 mai suna Musa ya amsa, ya aika masa da manyan pizza guda biyu.

Hadin **Pizza Day** na iya dubawa daga kowa kuma yana da wannan lambar mu'amala:

`a1075db55d416d3ca199f55b6084e2115b9345e16c5cf302fc80e9d5fbf5d48d`
