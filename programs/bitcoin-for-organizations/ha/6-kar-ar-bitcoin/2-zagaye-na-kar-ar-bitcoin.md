# 6.2 Zagaye na Karɓar Bitcoin

#### 6.2.0 Gabatarwa

**To, ina da Bitcoin. Me zan iya yi da shi?**

Yawancinmu mun taɓa jin irin wannan tambaya (watakila da ɗan dariya) daga waɗanda ke da shakku ko Bitcoin zai samu karɓuwa a matsayin kuɗi. Wannan ra'ayi ne da aka saba ji daga masana harkar kuɗi na gargajiya da kafafen yaɗa labarai, inda ake cewa har yanzu fasahar ba ta samu karɓuwa sosai ba, duk da cewa ta shafe fiye da shekaru 15 tana aiki ba tare da tsayawa ba.

Shin wannan yana nufin Bitcoin ta rasa damar ta na samun karɓuwa sosai? Ko kuwa har yanzu muna farkon zagayen karɓuwar wannan fasaha ne? Za mu iya duba yadda wasu sabbin fasahohi suka samu karɓuwa a tarihi don mu auna matsayin Bitcoin a yanzu da kuma hasashen abin da zai faru nan gaba? Shin akwai wata hanya da ake amfani da ita don taimakawa wajen amsa waɗannan tambayoyi?



#### 6.2.1 Samfurin Kirkira na Rogers

A shekarar 1962, Farfesa na zamantakewa Everett Rogers ya gabatar da wani samfurin karɓar kirkira a cikin littafinsa,_Diffusion of Innovations_. Ra’ayinsa ya zama sananne cikin gaggawa a tsakanin masana da ‘yan kasuwa, kuma har yanzu ana yawan ambatonsa.

![Adoption curve](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/8c05a22d4ada0aa6dd25bd252a61a194ab01bbaa-500x190.svg)

_Dangantaka tsakanin nau’ikan masu karɓar kirkira bisa ga irin yadda suke da sabbin tunani da kuma matsayinsu a kan layin karɓuwa (Tushen: Everett M. Rogers, Diffusions of Innovations)_

Samfurin Rogers yana gabatar da muhimman abubuwa guda biyar na karɓar fasaha, inda ya raba su zuwa nau’ikan masu amfani da ke karɓar sabuwar kirkira kuma yana nuna su a kan layin rarraba mai siffar ƙaho. Rabe-raben masu karɓa guda biyar na Rogers sun danganta da matsayi a cikin al’umma. Waɗannan su ne:

* **Masu Farko** (2.5% na masu amfani) – Waɗannan su ne masu ƙirƙirar fasahar da kansu da kuma waɗanda ke da jarumtar ɗaukar babban haɗari saboda suna da isasshen kuɗi ko kuma suna kusa da tushen fasaha ko wasu masu kirkira.
* **Masu Karɓa na Farko** (13.5% na masu amfani) – Ana ɗaukar su a matsayin shugabannin ra’ayi. Suna amsa sauye-sauyen fasaha cikin sauri saboda suna da hangen nesa a zamantakewa ko kuma suna da isasshen kuɗi fiye da masu karɓa na baya.
* **Yawancin Masu Karɓa na Farko** (34% na masu amfani) – Wannan rukuni yana shirye ya karɓi fasaha da wuri, amma sai an tabbatar da amfaninta. Wannan rukuni na iya ƙunsar wasu shugabannin ra’ayi, amma gaba ɗaya suna da taka-tsantsan fiye da masu karɓa na farko.
* **Yawancin Masu Karɓa na Baya** (34% na masu amfani) – Wannan rukuni yana da taka-tsantsan sosai kuma suna iya nuna shakku fiye da masu amfani na baya.
* **Masu Jinkiri** (16% na masu amfani) – Wannan rukuni ne mafi tsoron sauyi. Suna iya karɓar sabuwar fasaha ne kawai idan dole ko kuma saboda tsoffin hanyoyi sun daina aiki.

![The chasm](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/0eaae1a95661a488ec185db32b39ad3ebf79652a-530x354.svg)

Sauyawa daga Masu Karɓa na Farko zuwa Yawancin Masu Karɓa na Farko wani lokaci ana kiran shi da**Tsallaka Tazara**. Wannan ra’ayi ya shahara ne ta Geoffrey A. Moore a cikin littafinsa mai suna iri ɗaya, wanda aka fitar a 1991. Wannan sauyi yana nuna canjin masu amfani daga masu sha’awar fasaha da masu hangen nesa, zuwa masu amfani da ke karɓar fasaha saboda buƙata da sauƙi. Moore ya ce tsallaka tazara shi ne mataki mafi wahala ga sabuwar fasaha, amma idan an samu nasara, yana kawo sabon zamani inda fasahar ke shiga cikin al’umma da karfi.



#### 6.2.2 Tarihin Karɓar Intanet

A wannan lokaci yana da amfani mu duba baya mu kwatanta ci gaban Bitcoin da na intanet kanta. Wannan kwatancen yana da amfani saboda, kamar intanet, tsarin My First Bitcoin yana dogara ne da manhajar buda-tushen kuma ana iya samun damar shiga ta daga ko’ina a duniya muddin mutum na da kayan aiki da ya dace.

Intanet kamar yadda muka sani a yau ta fara ne da ƙirƙirar ARPANET a cikin Ma’aikatar Tsaron Amurka a shekarun 1960. Fasahar ta ci gaba a cikin shekaru goma masu zuwa ta hanyar haɓaka tsarin TCP/IP da kuma fara amfani da imel. A 1983, ƙirƙirar tsarin Domain Name System (DNS) ya nuna sauyawa zuwa intanet na zamani, sannan a 1990 aka ƙirƙiri World Wide Web, wanda aka gina a kan tsarin HTTP. A tsakiyar shekarun 1990 aka fara samun masu lilo na farko da kuma fara sabis na intanet na kasuwanci, kamar AOL. A wannan lokaci, lilo na asali da imel (wanda aka gina a kan tsarin SMTP) suna ƙara shahara a cikin al’ummar masu fasaha.

A 1997, tashin kasuwancin dot-com ya fara, kuma dandamalin kasuwanci na intanet kamar Amazon da eBay suka ƙara shahara. Masu binciken intanet na farko ma sun samu karɓuwa sosai a wannan lokaci. Faduwar kamfanonin intanet da dama a farkon shekarun 2000 (wanda aka fi sani da dot-com crash) ya rage zuba jari a wannan fanni amma ya ƙarfafa kamfanonin da suka dace da kuma masu riba.

Bayyanar intanet mai sauri (broadband) a tsakiyar shekarun 2000 ya ba da damar haɗin kai cikin sauri sosai kuma ya ba da damar haɓaka aikace-aikacen da ke buƙatar sauri kamar wasannin intanet da kallon fina-finai ta intanet. A wannan lokaci, dandamalin sada zumunta na farko kamar Facebook da Twitter sun jawo sabbin masu amfani da intanet da dama, sannan fitowar iPhone ta kawo sabbin aikace-aikacen wayar hannu.

A shekarun 2010, amfani da girgije (cloud computing) ya yadu, wanda ya haifar da sabbin hanyoyin amfani da manhaja, sabis na yawo da kuma aikace-aikacen wayar hannu. Kuma, yayin da aka ci gaba da haɓaka hanyoyin sadarwa na wayar hannu masu sauri (3G, 4G da sauransu), yankuna da dama da ba su da saurin intanet sun samu damar haɗuwa.



#### 6.2.3 Kwatanta Bitcoin da Tsarin Intanet

##### Bitcoin a Matsayin Tsarin Asali

Tunda Bitcoin tsarin asali ne ga ‘intanet na daraja’, yana da amfani mu kwatanta shi da TCP/IP, wanda shi ne tsarin asali na sadarwar intanet. Bitcoin, kamar TCP/IP, yana samar da matakin farko ga tsarin aikace-aikace da sababbin hanyoyin adanawa da musayar daraja.

Tsarin Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP) tsarin aikace-aikace ne da ke saman TCP/IP kuma yana sauƙaƙa musayar shafukan yanar gizo tsakanin sabar da mai lilo. Idan muka kwatanta, hanyar Lightning Network ta Bitcoin tana aiki a matsayin hanyar biyan kuɗi, tana ba da damar mu’amala cikin sauri da arha wanda za a iya daidaita su a matakin asali na Bitcoin daga baya.

Sauran hanyoyin aikace-aikace kamar hanyar Liquid suna ba da damar mu’amala cikin sauri da sirri da kuma fitar da wasu takardun shaida na dijital. Wasu hanyoyin da ba su bayyana ba za su iya ba da damar kyauta, ba da tukwici, biyan saƙo ko yawo da daraja don abun cikin kafofin watsa labarai.

Duk da wasu kamanceceniya tsakanin hanyoyin da aka gina a kan Bitcoin da na intanet kafin shi, kusan shekaru 17 ne suka wuce tsakanin fitowar TCP/IP (1974) da HTTP (1991). Wannan ya bambanta da hanyoyin aikace-aikace a kan Bitcoin (Lightning da Liquid) wanda aka fitar da su cikin kasa da shekaru goma bayan kafuwar Bitcoin – yana nuna cewa karɓuwa na faruwa cikin sauri. Wannan ba abin mamaki ba ne tunda intanet da kanta ta ba da damar yaduwar bayanai na dijital, wanda ya sa ilimin game da hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin ya yadu cikin sauri a duniya.

##### Bitcoin a Matsayin Tsarin Aikace-aikace

A madadin haka, maimakon mu ɗauki Bitcoin a matsayin tsarin asali kamar TCP/IP, za mu iya ɗauka yana da matsayi na musamman a cikin tsarin intanet na yanzu, yana faɗaɗa shi don sauƙaƙa musayar daraja. Ta haka za mu iya ɗaukar Bitcoin a matsayin tsarin asali na ‘musayar daraja’, kamar yadda HTTP ke matsayin daidaito don isar da abun cikin yanar gizo. Dukansu suna zaune a saman TCP/IP a matsayin matakin asali na sadarwar bayanai.

Yayin da bitcoin (dukiya) ke kafa kanta a matsayin ajiyar dukiya ta duniya, Bitcoin (tsari) na iya zama daidaiton duniya don daidaita kasuwancin intanet a ko’ina.

Duk yadda muka zaɓa mu kwatanta Bitcoin da ci gaban intanet na zamani, a bayyane yake cewa har yanzu muna matakin farko a ci gaban Bitcoin.



#### 6.2.4 Bitcoin da Zagayen Karɓar Fasaha

A lokacin da aka ƙirƙiri ‘Genesis Block’ na Bitcoin a watan Janairu 2009 (kuma watakila har tsawon watanni bayan haka), fasahar ta kasance sananniya ne kawai ga ƙananan ‘yan cypherpunk’. Idan muka duba yau, manyan masu kula da dukiya a Wall Street suna ba da kayayyakin kasuwanci da hanyoyin adanawa da ke kasuwanci da miliyoyin Yuro kowace rana.

Idan muka koma samfurin Rogers na karɓuwa, a wane mataki ne Bitcoin ke ciki a yanzu? Don amsa wannan tambaya, ya kamata mu duba tarihin Bitcoin da yadda ake karɓar ta.

* Amfani da matakai da ranakun da ke ƙasa shawarwari ne kuma masana za su iya da ra’ayinsu da fassarar su!

##### Masu Farko (2009-2015)

Masu Karɓa: ‘Yan cypherpunk’ na farko ko kwararrun masu ilimin lissafi da waɗanda ke sha’awar ra’ayin kuɗi mara tsakiya, wanda ya samo asali daga intanet. Wannan mataki ya haɗa da masu ra’ayin ‘yanci da masu sha’awar fasaha ko intanet. Wasu masu zuba jari na farko ma sun shiga tare da kamfanonin farawa da ke binciken damar Bitcoin ko fasahar da ke ƙasa don adanawa da biyan kuɗi.

**Muhimman Abubuwa**:

* 2009: Fitar da takardar bayanin Bitcoin daga Satoshi Nakamoto.
* 2010: Ƙirƙirar ‘Genesis Block’ ta hanyar tsarin Proof-of-Work da kuma mu’amala ta kasuwanci ta farko na 10,000 BTC don pizzas biyu.
* 2012: Halving na farko wanda ya aiwatar da tsarin raguwar fitar Bitcoin.
* 2011-2013: Tasowar kasuwanni kamar Mt. Gox da amfani a ‘dark web’ (Silk Road).
* 2013-2015: Tashin farashi mai yawa ya taimaka wajen yada sanin Bitcoin.

##### Masu Fara Karɓa (2016-2022)

Masu Karɓa: Masana fasahar bayanai da suka gina da kuma inganta kayayyakin da suka shafi Bitcoin, kamar na’urar hakar Bitcoin da walat. Kasuwanni masu sauƙin amfani sun taimaka wajen ƙara yawan masu amfani da Bitcoin. Kamfanonin saka jari na farko sun shiga (Microstrategy, Tesla) kuma babban mai kula da kadarori (Fidelity) ya fara ba da ajiyar Bitcoin. Duk da haka, shakku ya ci gaba a cikin tsarin kuɗi na gargajiya, musamman saboda rashin tabbas na dokoki a mafi yawan ƙasashe masu ci gaba da kuma rahotannin kafafen watsa labarai na gargajiya, suna nuna yawan amfani da makamashi na Bitcoin da kuma zargi da alaƙarsa da laifuka. Wasu ƙasashe sun fara binciken Bitcoin da fasahar da ke ƙasa da shi don fitar da kuɗin dijital a nan gaba.

**Muhimman Abubuwa**:

* 2016: Rarrabuwar kai mai tsanani tsakanin masu amfani dangane da hanyar ci gaban fasahar Bitcoin (Yaƙin Girman Bulo).
* 2017: Kafafen watsa labarai na gargajiya sun ruwaito tashin farashi zuwa kusan €20,000 ga kowanne BTC.
* 2018: An ƙaddamar da hanyar Lightning don biyan kuɗi cikin sauri.
* 2020: Kamfanin software na kasuwanci Microstrategy ya sanar da dabarar ajiyar Bitcoin.
* 2021/2022: Tashin farashi ya kai BTC sama da €60,000.
* 2021: El Salvador ta zama ƙasa ta farko da ta amince da Bitcoin a matsayin kuɗin doka.

##### Yawancin Masu Karɓa / Tsallake Gibin (2023-2029)

Masu Karɓa: Manyan cibiyoyin kuɗi na gargajiya sun fara ba da kayayyakin da suka shafi Bitcoin saboda samun ƙarin tabbas daga dokoki. Mutane da kamfanoni suna saka jari don dalilai na amfani ko kariyar haɗari. Ƙasashe suna ci gaba da binciken amfani da Bitcoin a matsayin wani ɓangare na ajiyar kuɗi da manufofin kuɗi, wasu ma suna yin manyan jarin. Tsayayya daga tsarin kuɗi na gargajiya yana fara raguwa, duk da cewa har yanzu akwai manyan ƙalubale na dokoki da ilimi ga mutane da kamfanoni.

**Muhimman Abubuwa** (zuwa yanzu):

* 2023/2024: Microstrategy ta ƙara saurin shirin sayen BTC kuma tana binciken sabbin dabarun kuɗin kamfani.
* 2024: Wasu manyan cibiyoyin kuɗi na gargajiya sun ƙaddamar da Bitcoin ETFs a Amurka, waɗanda suka zama kayayyakin ETF mafi saurin girma a tarihi.
* 2023/2024: Ƙalilan daga cikin asusun fansho a Amurka/Birtaniya da Kanada sun fara saka jari.
* 2024: Rahotannin kafafen watsa labarai na gargajiya sun fi kyau kuma suka rage kai hari ga Bitcoin.
* Karshen 2024: Ɗan takarar shugaban ƙasa mai goyon bayan Bitcoin ya lashe zaɓen Amurka.

##### Yawancin Masu Karɓa na Ƙarshe / Masu Jinkiri (2030 zuwa gaba)?

Masu Karɓa: A lokacin wannan mataki na Yawancin Masu Karɓa na Ƙarshe, Bitcoin na iya zama abin karɓuwa sosai a matsayin ajiyar kadarar kuɗi. A wannan lokaci, masu ruwa da tsaki a tsarin kuɗi na gargajiya za su iya amincewa cewa samun ‘Dabarar Bitcoin’ ya zama dole don tsira – kalmar da za ta zama ‘daidaita ko lalacewa’ a wannan lokaci.

Tsarin kuɗin fiat yana ƙara zama maras tabbas yayin da jarin ke fita daga tsohon tsarin kuma dokoki sun fi bayyana sosai, inda masu tsara dokoki suka amince da buƙatar daidaitawa da sabon yanayi.

Babban ƙasashe sun karɓi Bitcoin a matsayin ajiyar kadarar gwamnati da kuɗin doka kuma fasahar kuɗi mai amfani da AI, 24x7, ta ƙara yaduwa a duniya, tana motsa tattalin arziki zuwa Bitcoin, domin ita ce kaɗai kuɗin da ke da tsaro, ba da iko ga kowa, kuma ana iya amincewa da ita, wadda aka gina bisa buɗaɗɗen tushe mai iya shirya.

Bitcoin ta zama muhimmin kadarar kuɗi da ake amfani da ita wajen sauya makamashi zuwa sabuntawa kuma ta zama ginshiƙi a harkar kuɗi ta duniya, kamar yadda intanet ko wayar salula suka zama ruwan dare.

A wannan lokaci, Bitcoin ba kawai ana kallonta a matsayin ajiyar daraja ba, amma amfani da ita zai iya yaduwa a matsayin hanyar musayar kaya da auna darajar kayayyaki da ayyuka, saboda kuɗin fiat ba a fi so gaba ɗaya.

##### Sabanin Samfurin Rogers

Abin da ke sama yana nuna cewa Bitcoin tana (a lokacin rubutu) tsallake gibin zuwa matakin Masu Fara Karɓa. Duk da haka, a cikin Bitcoin, akwai sabani da samfurin Rogers wanda ke cewa dole ne fasaha ta kai kusan kashi 15% na kasuwar da ake nufi a wannan lokaci. A lokacin rubutu, [BiTBO](https://bitbo.io/how-many-users/), yana nuna cewa akwai sama da mutane miliyan 100 da ke amfani da Bitcoin a duniya, wanda ke wakiltar kashi kaɗan cikin dari. Kimantawa daga [Triple-A](https://www.triple-a.io/cryptocurrency-ownership-data) sun fi da tabbaci, suna nuna cewa kusan mutane miliyan 560 a duniya suna da cryptocurrency. Wannan zai nuna kashi 7% na yawan mutanen duniya.

A madadin haka, za mu iya ɗauka cewa jimillar kasuwa ita ce mutane biliyan 5 a duniya da ke da damar shiga intanet. Wannan adadi yana nuna kusan kashi 11% suna da alaƙa da cryptocurrency, kusa da kashi 16% da samfurin Rogers ke nunawa.

A ƙarƙashin babban adadi, ya kamata mu sa ran bambance-bambancen al’umma. Misali, akwai yiyuwar yaduwar amfani da Bitcoin ya fi yawa a tsakanin matasa ƙasa da shekaru 25, kuma ƙasa sosai a tsakanin masu shekaru sama da 45, inda karɓuwa ke ƙasa sosai.

Ta wannan hanya, za mu iya duba samfurin Rogers a kan ƙananan kasuwanni masu bambanci da halayensu. Waɗannan na iya kasancewa bisa wurin zama, shekaru ko matsayin arziki. Hakanan za mu iya duba kasuwar ‘Ajiyar Daraja’, inda Bitcoin ke ƙara karɓuwa a ƙasashe masu ci gaba, daban da kasuwar ‘Hanyar Musanya’ wadda ke samun karɓuwa a ƙasashe masu tasowa ko wuraren da ke ƙarƙashin mulkin kama-karya.



#### 6.2.5 Shin Bitcoin Ta Tsallake Gibin?

Bayan amincewar da Hukumar Tsaro da Musayar Hannun Jari ta Amurka ta yi da kuma ƙaddamar da su a watan Janairu 2024, Bitcoin Exchange Traded Funds sun karya tarihin shigowar jari a shekarar farko. Jimillar darajar kadarorin da ETFs ke rike da su yanzu ya haura €100 biliyan. Za mu iya duba wannan ci gaba a matsayin babban canji ga wannan fanni. Zai iya zama lokacin ‘Tsallake Gibin’ da ke nuna fara karɓuwar Bitcoin a matsayin gama gari, kamar yadda fitowar burauzar intanet ta Netscape a watan Oktoba 1994 ta taimaka wajen shahara da amfani da ‘World-Wide-Web’.

Wannan yana nuna muhimmancin hanyar amfani da fasaha wajen karɓuwarta. Masu sha’awar fasaha ne ke mamaye matakan Masu Kirkira da Masu Fara Karɓa saboda suna jin daɗin mu’amala da tsarin IT mai wahala kuma ba sa jin tsoro da wahalar amfani da fasaha, ta hanyar hanyar amfani da ba ta cika ba ko ba ta da sauƙi. Inganta hanyar amfani da fasaha da ke sauƙaƙa samun damar amfani da fasalulluka za ta jawo masu amfani daban-daban. Ƙaddamar da ETFs na iya zama wannan ingantawa ga Bitcoin.



#### 6.2.6 A Hankali, Sai Da Gaggawa: S-Kurvan Karɓuwa

Duk da cewa samfurin Rogers yana da amfani wajen fahimtar tsarin karɓuwar fasaha, babban gazawarsa shi ne ba ya bayyana saurin ko, mafi muhimmanci, hanzarin karɓuwa.

Misali, idan muka ɗauka muna shiga matakin Masu Fara Karɓa bayan shekaru 15 na aikin Bitcoin, za mu iya ɗauka cewa za mu ci gaba da tafiya a kan layin samfurin Rogers daidai wannan sauri a cikin shekaru 15 masu zuwa. Idan haka ne, Bitcoin za ta ci gaba da kasancewa a matakin Masu Fara Karɓa har tsawon shekaru goma masu zuwa.

Amma, nazarin wasu fasahohin da suka kawo sauyi yana nuna mana cewa karɓuwa ba ta tafiya a layi madaidaici ba kuma matakan Yawancin Masu Karɓa da Masu Karɓa na Ƙarshe na iya faruwa cikin gajeren lokaci yayin da karɓuwa ke ƙaruwa da sauri. Saboda haka aka ce ‘A hankali, sai da gaggawa’.

Saboda haka, amfani da samfurin S-Kurva wajen nazarin karɓuwar fasaha mai kawo sauyi yana da amfani.

![The S-Surve of Adoption](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/vje9ehw2/staging/b970da812607d282a96c8b9fe7e27a14dad74a04-493x291.svg)

_S-Kurvan Karɓuwa (Asali: Investaura)_

Yana da muhimmanci a lura cewa gangaren jadawalin ƙiyasta ne kuma saurin karɓuwa na kowanne zagaye na fasaha zai bambanta. Duk da haka, S-Kurva tana nuna cewa tsawon matakai ba daidai ba ne, inda Yawancin Masu Karɓa da Masu Karɓa na Ƙarshe ke ɗaukar ƙasa da lokaci idan aka kwatanta da Masu Kirkira da Masu Fara Karɓa. A misalin da ke sama, Masu Kirkira da Masu Fara Karɓa sun kai kusan kashi 40% na jimillar lokaci. Wannan ya bambanta da kusan kashi 25% na jimillar lokaci ga Yawancin Masu Karɓa da Masu Karɓa na Ƙarshe, duk da cewa waɗannan matakan suna da kashi 80% na shiga kasuwa gaba ɗaya.

Akwai kamanceceniya da ci gaban intanet, wadda ta samu ‘Lokacin Burauza’ a tsakiyar shekarun 1990 lokacin da Netscape da Microsoft Internet Explorer suka fara samun karɓuwa a kasuwa. Kafin fitowar waɗannan, intanet na hannun ƙalilan daga cikin masu sha’awar fasaha ne tsawon shekaru. Cikin shekaru biyar bayan fitowar burauzan intanet, kusan kowa ya shiga ‘Babbar Hanyar Bayanai’ kamar yadda ake kiranta a wancan lokaci. Za mu iya ganin irin wannan ci gaba a tarihin wasu fasahohi, kamar wayar salula, talabijin, rediyo da mota.



#### 6.2.7 Kammalawa

Daga hangen nesa na wanda ke kusa da sabuwar fasaha kamar Bitcoin, karɓuwa tana da jinkiri kuma yana da sauƙi a yi tunanin cewa karɓuwar gama gari tana da nisa. Wannan ra’ayi galibi sakamakon tunani ne mai layi madaidaici kuma yana ƙarfafa masu shakku da ke cewa Bitcoin ta ‘gaza cimma’ alkawuran farko.

Ko da wasu tsofaffin masu amfani da Bitcoin na iya tunani da layi madaidaici. Wasu sun ji takaici cewa karɓuwar cibiyoyin saka jari ba ta yadu sosai ba a zagayen rabin Bitcoin na baya (2020-2024). Yanzu da dama na hasashen hakan zai faru a wannan zagaye (2024-2028), inda ake sa ran karɓuwar ƙasashe ba za ta faru ba sai zagaye na gaba (2028-2032). Duk da haka, S-Kurvan karɓuwa na nuna cewa za mu iya ganin waɗannan abubuwa sun faru cikin gajeren lokaci.

Yana da muhimmanci kada a raina ƙarfin lambobin da ke ƙaruwa da sauri wajen karɓar kasuwa. Idan muka duba auna amfani da Bitcoin a kasuwannin jama'a, kamar yawan adireshin walat ko asusun musanya, ko kuma yawan kamfanoni da ke ɗaukar dabarar Bitcoin, a bayyane yake cewa shiga kasuwa bai yi yawa ba. Duk da haka, watakila ba mu da wuri sosai idan aka auna da lokacin da ya shige.

Farkon shekarar da ta gabata, ƙaddamar da Bitcoin ETFs da ta samu gagarumar nasara ta buɗe kasuwa ga wani sabon rukuni na masu amfani kuma watakila wannan ne 'Lokacin Browser' ko kuma lokacin da Bitcoin ta tsallaka gibin. Idan haka ne, za mu iya ganin karɓuwa ta ƙaru sosai cikin ɗan gajeren lokaci.
