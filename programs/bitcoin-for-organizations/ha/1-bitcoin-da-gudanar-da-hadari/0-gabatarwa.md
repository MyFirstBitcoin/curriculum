# 1.0 Gabatarwa

> Binciken hangen nesa yana ba ka damar ganin abin da zai faru a gaba ta hanyar lura da wasu alamu da ke cikin abin da ke faruwa yanzu.  
_Roger Spitz_

Ka yi tunanin kanka a shekarar 1995, kana yin aikin da kai (ko iyayenka) kake yi a wancan lokacin, amma da ilimin da kake da shi yanzu. Idan ka duba baya, wane irin hadari ne aikinka, kungiyarka, ko kasuwarka ke fuskanta saboda zuwan intanet da ke gabatowa? Shin akwai barazana da za a kauce wa su da kuma damar da za a iya amfani da su? Ko kuma ka yi la’akari da cewa kana aiki a Blockbuster. Idan ka san abin da ka sani yanzu a wancan lokacin, kuma kai babban jami’i ne a kamfani na jama’a, za a dauke ka da sakaci da nauyin da aka dora maka na kula da kudaden masu hannun jari idan ba ka nemi saka hadarin zuwan intanet a cikin Rijistar Hadarin kungiyarka ba.

Intanet wata sabuwar barazana ce a shekarar 1995, kamar yadda Bitcoin yake a yau. A shekarar 1995, an riga an san intanet sosai a cikin tattaunawa; ba wani sirrin fasaha bane. Amma, 'yan kasuwa da dama ba su fahimta ko amfani da shi ba, sai dai sabbin kamfanonin intanet, kuma kadan daga cikinsu ne suka yi kokarin koyon game da shi ko ma lura da shi a matsayin hadari.

Kamar yadda intanet yake a 1995, Bitcoin an san shi sosai, amma 'yan kasuwa kadan ne suka fahimta ko amfani da shi, sai dai wadanda ke aiki sosai a cikin tattalin arzikin Bitcoin. A shekarar 1995, intanet ta samo asali tun daga shekarun 1960 ta hanyar kara sabbin matakan ka'idoji har ta kai wani mataki na saukin amfani wanda ya haifar da gagarumin ci gabanta.

Bitcoin yana ci gaba da sauyawa a irin wannan hanya tun daga kafuwarsa a 2009, yana kara saukin amfani da rage wahalar fahimta. Kamar yadda aka samu a intanet, yawan masu amfani da Bitcoin yana karuwa a hankali ([https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/an-objective-look-at-bitcoin-adoption](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/an-objective-look-at-bitcoin-adoption)) yayin da wadannan ci gaban fasaha ke faruwa. Kamar intanet, ba za mu san wane ci gaba ne zai zama abin da zai tayar da gagarumin karbuwa ba sai daga baya, amma tuni muna da bayanai tun daga 2022 da ke nuna "alamun da ke cikin abin da ke faruwa yanzu" (Roger Spitz).
