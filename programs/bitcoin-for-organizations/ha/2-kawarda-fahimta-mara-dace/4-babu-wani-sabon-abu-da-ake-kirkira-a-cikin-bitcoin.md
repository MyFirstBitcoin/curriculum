# 2.4 Babu wani sabon abu da ake kirkira a cikin Bitcoin

> Ƙirƙirar daji dubu yana cikin ƙwayar acorn guda ɗaya.  
_Ralph Waldo Emerson_

Masu sukar sau da yawa suna ƙoƙarin cewa Bitcoin fasaha ce 'tsohuwa' ko 'matacciya' saboda ba ta canza tsarin asali na yarjejeniya kamar yadda wasu blockchain ke yi. Wannan zargi yana watsi da dalilan da yasa ake ɗaukar lokaci kafin a canza Bitcoin da kuma yawan ƙirƙira da ake yi don faɗaɗa hanyar sadarwa a matakai mafi girma, kamar Lightning Network. Hakanan yana watsi da cewa yawancin fasahohinmu mafi sassauci da ɗorewa ma ba sa canzawa da sauri a matakin asali.

Misali, babu wani sabon ƙirƙira da ake yi a cikin Transmission Control Protocol (TCP), wanda ke ƙarkashin intanet. An fara ƙirƙirar TCP a shekarar 1974. Karon ƙarshe da aka sabunta TCP shine a 1982. Yana yin abin da ake buƙata. Ba cikakke bane, kuma akwai muhawara ko ya kamata a sabunta IPv4 don tallafawa ci gaban intanet na gaba. Duk da haka, cewa babu wani ƙirƙira da aka yi a intanet tun 1982 zai zama zargi mai ban mamaki. Duk wannan ƙirƙira ana yin shi 'a kan' TCP, ba 'ciki' TCP ba.

Yawancin ƙirƙira da ake yi ba 'ciki' Bitcoin ba ne, amma 'a kan' Bitcoin. Wata rana, watakila ba za a sake samun ƙirƙira 'ciki' Bitcoin ba, kuma hakan ya kamata ya zama buri ba zargi ba, domin hakan zai nuna yadda ya zama ginshiƙi wajen tallafawa tattalin arzikin duniya ta hanyar samar da tushe ga kuɗi mai ƙarfi, mara son kai kuma mara izini. Kuɗi mai ƙarfi duka a ma'anar tattalin arziki cewa akwai iyaka a yawan su da kuma rajista mai ɗorewa, amma kuma mai ƙarfi a fannin fasaha saboda ba ya canzawa kuma abin da ke tafiya yana da shekaru da dama ba tare da katsewa ba. Bitcoin ta riga ta cimma 100% uptime cikin shekaru 10 da suka gabata.

Duk da haka, zai zama abin damuwa idan babu ƙirƙira da ake yi 'a kan' Bitcoin. Mu duba hakan cikin shekaru 10 da suka gabata:



#### 'Ciki' Bitcoin

An aiwatar da Segregated Witness (SegWit) a 2017 don karewa daga sauya bayanan mu'amala da kuma ƙara ƙarfin block. SegWit kuma ya zama mataki na farko da ake buƙata don lightning da wasu side chains su yi aiki yadda ya kamata.

An aiwatar da Taproot a 2021 don baiwa damar haɗa da tantance sa hannun mutane da yawa ta hanyar amfani da Schnorr signatures, da gabatar da harshen rubutun don ƙarin ayyuka masu rikitarwa da kuma ƙara sirri da juriya ga tsangwama a mu'amaloli.



#### 'A kan' Bitcoin

##### Liquid Sidechain

An aiwatar da Liquid sidechain a 2018. Liquid, kamar sauran sidechains, wata rajistar blockchain ce daban wadda aka haɗa da babban blockchain na Bitcoin, bisa wasu ƙa'idoji da aka riga aka tsara. Waɗannan ƙa'idojin suna da sassauci don baiwa Liquid damar haɓakawa da haɗa sabbin tsari da faɗaɗa a tsawon lokaci. Duk da haka, haɗin da ke tsakanin Liquid da Bitcoin yana tabbatar da cewa iyakar 21 miliyan na bitcoin yana daidai a kowanne sarkar.

Dukiyar da ke cikin Liquid, L-BTC, ana ɗaure ta da bitcoin a babban sarkar. Akwai bambance-bambancen farashi, sauri, sirri da tsaro wanda ke sa L-BTC ya dace da wasu amfani. Farashi, sauri da sirri duk sun fi kyau da L-BTC, amma sai an dan dogara da ƙungiyoyin da ke cikin Liquid Federation, waɗanda ke sarrafa tsarin multisig 11 daga 15 don canza L-BTC zuwa bitcoin da akasin haka.

##### Lightning Network

An aiwatar da Lightning network a 2018. Lightning an tsara ta ne don zama hanyar biyan kuɗi kai tsaye tsakanin mutane a matsayin hanyar nodes da aka haɗa ta tashoshi; ba blockchain ba ce. Ana kulle Bitcoin ta hanyar node a babban blockchain don a iya amfani da ita a Lightning Network, hakan yana tabbatar da cewa ana amfani da 'gaskiya' bitcoin ne kawai. Nodes za su iya buɗe tashoshin ruwa ta hanyar kwangilar smart contract na multisig da juna. Biyan kuɗi yana samun hanya ta hanyar hanyar sadarwa daga tushe zuwa inda ake nufi, yana daidaita farashi da buƙatar ruwa mai isasshe a kowanne mataki. Lightning Network yana ƙara inganta farashi, sauri da sirri sosai, amma da rashi tsaro (ko ƙarin dogaro) da ƙarin rikitarwa. Duk da haka, an tsara shi ne don biyan kuɗi masu yawa amma ƙananan kudi na yau da kullum, don haka wannan musayar yana da ma'ana sosai ga miliyoyin mu'amaloli na kullum (majiyar: River, 2023).

##### Chaumian eCash Mints

Fedimints za a iya ɗauka a matsayin hanyar Lightning Network da ke iyakance ga al'umma. An tsara su ne don amfani da amincewar da ke tsakanin wasu al'ummomi (misali: iyalai, ƙauyuka, ƙungiyoyin abokai) don sauƙaƙa rikitarwa da ƙara sirri ga masu amfani. Su ne tsarin bude tushe, masu sassauci don adanawa da mu'amala da bitcoin a cikin al'umma. Suna iya aiki tare da Lightning Network kanta.

**Cashu** wata alama ce da za a iya adanawa a kan na'ura kamar wayar hannu; an tsara ta ne don kwaikwayon amfanin kuɗin hannu amma a cikin dijital. Cashu misali ne na Chaumian eCash da aka gina a kan Bitcoin kuma yana ƙara sirri da juriya ga tsangwama da rage rikitarwa a maimakon dogaro da mint ɗin eCash da ake amfani da shi. Cashu mints suna fitar da alamu na eCash, masu wakiltar bitcoin, waɗanda masu amfani za su iya kashewa ba tare da bayyana su wanene ba. Cashu na iya aiki tare da Lightning Network.

Mai yiyuwa ne a samu ƙarin aikace-aikacen mataki na 2 da za a gina a nan gaba, tare da aikace-aikacen mataki na 3 da za a gina a kan kowanne daga cikinsu.

A matsayin misali na yawan aikace-aikacen da ake ginawa a kan Lightning, ga wani ɓangare daga rahoton bincike na Lightning Network daga River.
