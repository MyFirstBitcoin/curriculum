# 5.1 Bitcoin’s impact on IT leaders

> Every informed person needs to know about Bitcoin because it might be one of the world’s most important developments.  
_Leon Luow, Nobel Peace Prize nominee_



#### 5.1.0 Introduction

IT leaders have responsibilities to the business, using technology both to drive innovation and competitiveness for their companies, as well as finding ways to improve internal efficiencies to drive down costs.

There are several common risks and misconceptions around Bitcoin that are useful to understand and provide guidance on:

* It is often seen as part of a larger ‘crypto’ industry, and solutions based around one of the key innovations used, the blockchain.
* It is perceived as ‘wasting energy’ to run the network.
* There is a risk for Public Cloud users that the environment could be hacked and used to ‘mine’ Bitcoin or other crypto currencies by a nefarious actor, leading to a very large and unexpected bill, as well as performance impacts on business applications.
* There is a lack of knowledge of the technology behind Bitcoin.

On the positive side, Bitcoin could benefit any company by:

* Being added to the treasury as an asset, either being mined directly or purchased on the open market.
* Using otherwise idle resources to mine Bitcoin for the company.
* Providing justification for investments in AI solutions which require similar high performance compute resources.
* Adding an alternative payment method for purchasing company services or products.
* Reducing global FX transaction fees.
* Providing an additional employee benefit through incentives based on Lightning payments.
* Building new revenue streams for the company based on Bitcoin.

Any IT leader needs to take the time to understand Bitcoin, the potential impact and risks associated with it, as well as potential benefits, to be able to provide guidance and leadership for the business.

> Bitcoin is a remarkable cryptographic achievement… The ability to create something which is not duplicable in the digital world has enormous value… Lots of people will build businesses on top of that.

#### 5.1.1 Risks and misconceptions around Bitcoin

##### Bitcoin as part of a larger ‘crypto’ industry.

Bitcoin was the first successful attempt to create a finite digital asset and has spawned an entire industry of ‘alt-coins’ that are trying to use some of the underlying technology to either ‘improve’ on Bitcoin or to build a solution to address other potential market opportunities.

The public cloud providers have created blockchain platforms to enable companies to build these solutions, however the interest in these tends to rise and fall in line with industry hype, indeed Microsoft discontinued its blockchain service in 2021.

* Until 2017, Bitcoin accounted for as much as 95% of the overall crypto market cap.
* The first wave of ‘alternative’ crypto ICOs saw the dominance fall to an all-time low of 37.6%.
* As these failed to deliver real business benefits and began to fade away, Bitcoin dominance started moving higher again.
* In 2021, as the Bitcoin price increased, a new wave of NFT based alternative solutions appeared on the market which pushed the Bitcoin dominance down again.
* After this hype also failed to deliver real benefits, the dominance of Bitcoin has started climbing again.
* As and when Bitcoin enters another bull market, it is possible a new wave of ‘alt-coins’ emerge to capitalise on a new trend, but will likely meet a similar fate.

If the business comes to the IT leader with the latest ‘shiny object’ being promoted for a particular application of the blockchain, or as an alternative to Bitcoin, it is important to keep this trend in mind and ask:

* What is the purpose of the blockchain?
* Is a blockchain needed or desirable at all, given its performance drawbacks when compared to a centralised relational database?
* Who can change the protocol and what is the impact on the solution?
* What sacrifices have been made for security or decentralisation to provide the claimed performance?
* What benefit can it provide which cannot be delivered using Bitcoin and the associated protocols, such as Lightning?

##### The implications of running Bitcoin

Depending upon the sector the business runs in, there may be potential benefits from Bitcoin mining activity, typically where the heat generated can be put to some good use. However, there is often pushback due to the perception that Bitcoin is bad for the environment, wastes energy or has little benefit.

As an IT leader, understanding this dynamic and whether there are potential benefits to the company is necessary to provide effective guidance. The general default has been that Bitcoin is not ‘good for the environment’ and ‘wastes energy’. However this is changing quite rapidly, more positive reports are being published, and this narrative is expected to change to one where Bitcoin mining can be seen as positive for the environment and the transition to renewable energy sources. This is something that many companies focus on in their ESG initiatives.

##### Understanding the potential risks from crypto mining

There have been examples in the past where the Public cloud environment managed by a company has been taken over by hackers, who can very quickly enable additional compute resources to mine crypto. The more resources enabled of the highest performance profile (most costly) profile, the better the chances the hacker has of mining Bitcoin or any other crypto. This is referred to as ‘Crypto-Jacking’ - according to AWS:

‘This is a type of cybercrime that involves the unauthorised use of devices (edge computers, smartphones, tablets, or even servers) to mine for cryptocurrency.. As cryptocurrency prices rise and more powerful edge devices with GPU capabilities are used to run Machine Learning at the edge use cases, there is an increasing threat of cryptojackers exploiting security vulnerabilities on edge devices. When this happens, edge computing resources are used to mine crypto currency, resulting in higher CPU/GPU usage and a degradation in performance of edge applications and an increase in ML at the edge inference processing times.’

It is therefore critical that any use of public cloud resources is correctly designed following best practices. These are typically outlined in some form of Cloud Adoption Framework, describing recommendations around security, performance, monitoring, resilience and operations. The IT leader should ensure these are followed, and that some form of real time monitoring is enabled to identify and mitigate any such attacks before any large bills are incurred.

##### Lack of knowledge of the technology used by Bitcoin

There are many misconceptions around the technology behind Bitcoin, leading to questions about whether it can be hacked, the energy that is used, or if it will be overtaken by a new version as has often happened with technology. As the technical lead, having an understanding of the underlying technology would be useful to correctly position Bitcoin internally to mitigate these concerns.



#### 5.1.2 On the positive side.

##### Bitcoin as a treasury asset

There are potential financial benefits to a company putting Bitcoin on the books as a treasury asset.

* Store of value and inflation hedge
* Waning trust in fiat currencies as a store of value
* Counterparty risk in the banking sector
* First mover advantage for companies in the addition of Bitcoin to their treasury

Whilst the financial implications of doing this from an accountancy perspective is not the role of the IT leader, understanding how this might work and how the Bitcoin can be purchased, stored and secured will be.

There are services available in the market which will help to purchase, custody and offer lending services against the stored assets. If this becomes a discussion within the company, and it should, then the IT leader can help to qualify any potential third-party companies offering these services. Due diligence to ensure that the service(s) being offered meet the necessary security needs, transparency and service features will help to ensure the selection of a reliable partner.

##### Bitcoin mining benefits

Datacenter usage is only expected to grow in the future, and a large percentage of the costs for running a datacenter is dissipating the heat generated. This is especially true for applications that use high performance computing, such as AI/ML and Bitcoin mining.

Companies in different sectors globally have identified how this increased heat output can become a net benefit to the business instead of a cost by using the generated heat for:

* Swimming pools/Spas
* Aquatics centres
* Growing flowers/vegetables in greenhouses
* Heating the business’ own premises and hot water systems

This can be achieved by either working in conjunction with a Bitcoin mining company that uses the business facilities to mine Bitcoin for profit and provides the heat for general use, or

the business could carry out this function itself to build up a Bitcoin treasury directly. This approach can also help a company with any ESG initiatives.

A more indirect method of gaining some of these benefits can be achieved by moving compute requirements to datacenters that take this approach and offer rack space or infrastructure as part of a managed service and who pass on some of these cost savings to the business.

As the IT leader, staying on top of this area will help you provide guidance to the company in the adoption of solutions that may help the business.

##### Bitcoin and AI

The use of AI and ML is expected to grow substantially in the next few years. The intersection of Bitcoin and Artificial Intelligence is leading a new era of digital innovation, particularly highlighted by the integration of AI with Bitcoin's Lightning Network. This union is poised to revolutionise aspects of the internet, from micropayments to AI-driven online economic agents.

* Fine tuning AI models is an essential step in AI development – micropayments using Lightning can enable individuals worldwide to be paid per task in Bitcoin, encouraging participation.
* In scenarios where AI entities are transacting for services, the Lightning network is an indispensable tool for AI-driven economic activities where speed is key.
* Once the AI system is developed, micropayments could facilitate a more equitable pay-per-use model where users pay only for the AI resources that they use.

For any company considering the use of AI, either as a service it needs to pay for or in the development of its own AI solution, it is important to understand how Bitcoin and Lightning integrates into and adds value to any solution.

##### New retail payment options

Any business that accepts payments for services, either directly in retail outlets or online, could benefit from accepting Bitcoin payments in the following ways:

* Increased footfall and business growth by targeting Bitcoin customers
* Low to zero payment fees
* Instant settlement
* No Chargebacks

From an accounting perspective, there are different ways of handling the Bitcoin received, keeping it as part of a treasury or directly converting to fiat, or some combination as agreed. Depending on the business there may be some technical implications to achieving this, such as new POS terminals or integration with a software solution for online payments, which the IT leader needs to understand the technical implications of if it’s agreed to as a goal for the business.

##### Reducing global FX transfer fees

For companies that transfer large amounts of money globally, the costs and complexity of doing this can often be challenging. New solutions based on Bitcoin are being offered in the market which reduce these costs and provide faster and immediate settlement. Adopting these services could potentially be of benefit to the business, and having an understanding of this market, the services offered and working with the accounts team to implement the best solution requires a level of technical knowledge and understanding which the IT leader can provide.

##### Employee benefits

Most companies are looking at how they can provide benefits and incentives to their employees to improve recruitment and retention. Recently, several high-profile professional athletes and politicians have announced they were taking full or partial salaries in Bitcoin. The ability to pay portions of salary in Bitcoin will matter to employers when it starts to be the difference maker in key recruitments, or when core team members start asking for this accommodation or begin looking for employers who are offering it elsewhere.

* Integrating the option for full or partial compensation in Bitcoin gives an organisation a competitive edge in getting ahead of the curve. Bitcoin payroll solutions make integrating this process simple.
* Companies like MicroStrategy are looking at ways to incentivise performance or even attendance to meetings with micro-payments based on Lightning.

Although the decision to implement such benefits for employees doesn’t land directly with the IT leader, providing an understanding of why this might be of benefit,the solutions available to achieve this, and the technical implications of implementation may well do so. IT directors that proactively raise ideas to peers can demonstrate their wider strategic value to the business.

##### New market opportunities

As the quote from Google stated, many businesses will be looking to build new revenue streams based on the growing Bitcoin ecosystem. This may open up new markets for the business to consider, which the IT leader can be instrumental in evaluating the suitability, technical challenges and potential opportunity these may offer.



#### 5.1.3 Summary

Bitcoin is expected to continue in its adoption journey, becoming more important to businesses over time, affecting both business, technical strategies and initiatives in several areas. As the technical lead for the company, the IT leader will benefit from getting ahead of the curve on these developments, providing guidance to the company, and ensuring it obtains the greatest benefit from implementing Bitcoin solutions across the organisation.
