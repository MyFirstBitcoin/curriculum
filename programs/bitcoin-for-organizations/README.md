# Bitcoin for Organizations

**Bitcoin for Organizations** is an educational curriculum designed to help organizations understand Bitcoin in a **strategic, operational, and educational context**.

The curriculum explores how Bitcoin may affect organizations as:

- institutions
- employers
- service providers
- long-term actors in the global economy

## 🎯 Educational objective

A concise summary objective for the curriculum would be:

**To provide organizations with a foundation for understanding why Bitcoin should be included in strategic thinking, risk analysis, and long-term planning.**

⚠️ We strongly recommend that all readers begin with **Module 1: Bitcoin and Risk Management** before exploring the rest of the curriculum.

The risk-based perspective introduced in the first module serves as a foundation for the modules that follow.

## 👥 Target audience

This curriculum is intended for:

- senior leaders and decision-makers
- educators and program managers
- nonprofit and mission-driven organizations
- finance, operations, and technology teams
- technical and non-technical professionals seeking a serious understanding of Bitcoin

## 🧭 Program themes

Across the curriculum, several themes appear repeatedly:

- Bitcoin as a strategic issue, not only a technical or financial one
- risk management as a starting point for organizational analysis
- the relationship between Bitcoin, energy, and infrastructure
- the relevance of Bitcoin to different sectors and internal functions
- long-term thinking in the face of technological change
- the value of public data and open systems for economic analysis

## Notes

- This curriculum is educational in nature.
- It is intended to support understanding, discussion, and critical thinking.
- It should not be interpreted as financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
