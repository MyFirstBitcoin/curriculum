# Educator Guide

The **Educator Guide** is a teaching companion for the Bitcoin Diploma.

It is designed to help educators lead each module with clarity, confidence, and flexibility. It provides structure, pacing, discussion prompts, and classroom-tested approaches — but it is not a script. The real work of teaching happens in the classroom, and this guide is in service of that.

This guide is for educators who take their work seriously and want to go beyond content delivery to genuine learning.

## 🎯 Purpose

The Educator Guide supports educators in:

- preparing each lesson with clear objectives and the right materials,
- leading discussions that build critical thinking rather than answer recall,
- adapting timing, examples, and activities to their specific students and context,
- identifying and addressing common misconceptions before and during class,
- connecting across the My First Bitcoin Education Network.

## 👥 Who this guide is for

This guide is designed for:

- educators and trainers delivering the Bitcoin Diploma,
- facilitators running community or workshop sessions,
- anyone supporting learners through the curriculum in any format.

No prior Bitcoin expertise is required. Serious preparation is.

## 📚 Guide structure

Each module guide follows a consistent structure:

- **Learning Objectives** — the skills and understandings students must develop; these are non-negotiable across all classrooms
- **Duration & Core Idea** — 90-minute lessons with a one-sentence anchor
- **Tools & Resources** — materials, handouts, and visual aids to prepare in advance
- **Preparation** — what to do before class begins
- **Procedure** — step-by-step classroom moves, broken into numbered segments
- **Notes** — what good looks like, common misconceptions, intervention strategies, online teaching adaptations, and time management guidance

## Notes

- This guide is a support tool, not a script. Adapt the method; hold the learning objectives.
- It is intended to support understanding, discussion, and critical thinking.
- It should not be interpreted as financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
