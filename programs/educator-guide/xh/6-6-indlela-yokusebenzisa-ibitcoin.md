# 6 - Indlela Yokusebenzisa iBitcoin

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcamango Ephambili: Ukusebenzisa iBitcoin kwi-chain kufundisa abafundi indlela yokusebenza kobunini, ukuzigcina, kunye nokuqinisekisa ngokwenene, kutshintsha ithiyori ibe yintshukumo yemali esebenzayo.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi kufuneka bakwazi:

* Chonga iindlela eziqhelekileyo zokufumana nokutshintshiselana nge-bitcoin, kubandakanya iindlela zobuso ngobuso kunye neendawo zokutshintshiselana eziphakathi.
* Cacisa umahluko phakathi kwe-wallet ozigcinelayo kunye ne-wallet egcinwa ngomnye umntu, kunye nesizathu sokuba ukuzigcina kubalulekile kwiBitcoin.
* Chaza injongo ye-private keys, public addresses, seed phrases, kunye ne-interface ye-wallet.
* Thelekisa iindidi ezahlukeneyo ze-wallet kwaye uhlalutye umahluko wazo ngokhuseleko, lula, ubumfihlo, kunye nolawulo.
* Misela i-wallet yeBitcoin ephathwayo kwaye uchaze inkqubo esisiseko yokubuyisela.
* Bonisa indlela yokufumana nokuthumela i-bitcoin kwi-chain.

Sebenzisa umgaqo othi "Musa Ukuthemba, Qinisekisa" ekukhetheni i-wallet, kwiintengiselwano, nakusetyenzisweni olubanzi lweBitcoin.

##### 6.0 Intshayelelo, imizuzu esi-8

Qala ngokudibanisa esi sahluko nesidlulileyo:

* Ukuba iBitcoin yimali, abantu bayifumana kwaye bayisebenzisa njani ngokwenene?
* Kuthetha ukuthini ukulawula i-bitcoin yakho ngokwenene?
* Kutheni ukusetyenziswa kweBitcoin kuhluke ekusebenziseni i-app yasebhankini?

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko simalunga nokusebenzisa ngokwenene. Abafundi abasafundi nje ukuba yintoni iBitcoin, bafunda indlela yokusebenzisana nayo ngokuthe ngqo.

##### 6.1 Ukufumana kunye nokutshintshiselana ngeBitcoin, imizuzu eli-12

Cacisa ukuba abantu banokufumana i-bitcoin ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo, kubandakanya:

* ukuhlawulwa nge-bitcoin
* ukumba i-bitcoin
* ukutshintshela imali eqhelekileyo kwi-bitcoin ubuso ngobuso
* ukutshintshela imali eqhelekileyo kwi-bitcoin kwi-intanethi

Emva koko gxila kwiindlela ezimbini eziphambili zokufumana ezixoxwa kwesi sahluko:

* ubuso ngobuso, ngokwasemzimbeni
* peer-to-peer, kwi-intanethi
* iitshintshiselwano eziphakathi nendawo

Yenza kube kucacile ukuba kukho izinto ezifunekayo ukutshintsha.

Kwii-P2P ebusweni, gxininisa utshintshiselwano oluthe ngqo ngaphandle kwebhanki okanye umphakathi ophakathi, kodwa ukhankanye nemingcipheko yokwenene yokudibana nabantu xa kutshintshiselwa imali.

Kwii-P2P kwi-intanethi, chaza i-escrow ngendlela elula, njengendlela yokunciphisa umngcipheko wokungathembani ngelixa kusavumela utshintshiselwano oluthe ngqo phakathi kwabantu.

Kwizitshintshiselwano eziphakathi nendawo, yenza kucace ukuba zilula, kodwa zifuna abasebenzisi ukuba bathembe inkampani, badla ngokwabelana ngolwazi lwabo lomntu, kwaye bayeke imali yabo iphantsi kolawulo lomntu wesithathu de bayirhoxise. Le yindawo efanelekileyo yokuphinda uqaqambise ukuba lula kuhlala kuhamba nezinto ezifunekayo ukutshintsha kubucala kunye nenkululeko.

##### 6.2 Intshayelelo kwiBitcoin Wallets, imizuzu engama-35

**Yintoni iBitcoin Wallet Ngokwenene**

Cacisa ngoko nangoko impazamo eqhelekileyo: i-bitcoin ayigcinwanga ngaphakathi kwi-app ye-wallet njengemali ephathekayo esesakeni.  
I-bitcoin ikhona kwi-ledger egcinwa linethiwekhi. Oko kulawulwa ngumsebenzisi yindlela yokuyisebenzisa ngokusebenzisa iiprivate keys.

Emva koko chaza izinto ezimbini abantu abaqhele ukuziqonda xa besithi "wallet":

* inkqubo ye-private key, apho idilesi ziveliswa khona
* i-app okanye ujongano olusetyenziswa ukunxibelelana nenethiwekhi

Sebenzisa umzekelo we-imeyile wesi sahluko ukuba uyanceda:

* idilesi kawonke-wonke = njengedilesi ye-imeyile onokuyabelana ngayo
* private key = njengephasiwedi omele uyikhusele

Yiba ngqongqo apha: nabani na olawula iiprivate keys ulawula i-bitcoin. Lilo eli ngcamango ephambili abafundi abamele bayiqonde.

**Self-Custodial vs Custodial Wallets**

Le yenye yezona ndawo zibalulekileyo kwesi sahluko.

Chaza umahluko ngokucacileyo:

* Self-custodial wallet: umsebenzisi ulawula iiprivate keys
* Custodial wallet: umntu wesithathu ulawula iiprivate keys egameni lomsebenzisi

Emva koko chaza izinto ezifunekayo ukutshintsha:

Self-custodial

* ulawulo olupheleleyo lwemali
* akukho nkqubo yokuvunywa
* ukhuseleko ngokuchasene nokuthathwa kwemali ngokungekho sikweni
* uxanduva olukhulu
* akukho lula ukufumana kwakhona ukuba ilahleke i-seed phrase

Custodial

* kulula ukufumana kwakhona kunye nenkxaso
* kulula kubantu abaqalayo
* uchaphazeleka ngakumbi kwi-account freezes, iihacks, kunye nolawulo lomntu wesithathu
* umsebenzisi akabambi ngokwenene i-bitcoin

Le lixesha elifanelekileyo lokugxininisa esi sivakalisi:

"Not your keys, not your coins."

Abafundi kufuneka baphume kweli candelo beqonda hayi nje isilogeni, kodwa nento ethetha yona ngokwenene ekusebenzeni.

**Iindidi ezahlukeneyo zeWallets kunye nendlela yokukhetha enye**

Yazisa iindidi ze-wallets ezixoxwa kwesi sahluko:

* i-wallet ye-intanethi
* i-wallet yeselula
* i-wallet ye-desktop
* i-wallet ye-hardware
* i-wallet yephepha

Ungayithathi nanye njengegqibeleleyo. Endaweni yoko, chaza ukuba nganye inezinto ezifunekayo ukutshintsha phakathi kwe:

* ukhuseleko
* ubucala
* ukulunga
* ukuhambelana
* imirhumo
* ulawulo
* igama elihle

Kwakhona cacisa ukuba sicebisa ukuba abantu bahlale beqwalasela ukuba isoftware ye-wallet ivulelekile (open-source), kuba izixhobo ezivulekileyo zinokuhlolwa, zihlolwe, kwaye ziqhutywe luluntu. Oku kudibanisa ngqo nomgaqo wokungathembi kodwa uqinisekise kwiBitcoin.

##### 6.3 Ukuseta iWallet yeBitcoin ephathwayo, imizuzu eli-10

Khokela abafundi kwinkqubo esisiseko eboniswe kwesi sahluko:

* khuphela i-wallet
* yenza i-wallet entsha
* velisa kwaye ubhale phantsi ibinzana lokubuyisela (recovery phrase)
* qinisekisa ibinzana lokubuyisela
* yongeza ukhuseleko olongezelelweyo ukuba lukhona
* vula i-wallet uze ufumane umsebenzi wokufumana imali (receive function)

Yenza isilumkiso sebizwana lokubuyisela sicace gca:

* ukuba ibinzana lokubuyisela lilahlekile, ukufikelela kwiimali nako kungalahleka
* ukuba omnye umntu ufumana ibinzana lokubuyisela, angathatha imali

Ukuba abafundi benza oku ngezandla, umfundisi kufuneka ame kwinyathelo ngalinye aze aqinisekise ukuba wonke umntu uyayiqonda into abayenzayo. Ukuba iklasi ingaphezulu kwengqiqo, le ndawo ingachazwa njengokuhamba kwenkqubo endaweni yokuyenza bukhoma. Ukhetho lokubuyisela oluboniswe kwesi sahluko luyanceda ukuchaza ukuba ii-wallet zinokubuyiselwa ukuba ibinzana lokubuyisela ligcinwe kakuhle.

##### 6.4 Ukufumana kunye nokuthumela iiNtengiselwano, imizuzu eli-17

**Ukufumana kunye nokuthumela iiNtengiselwano kwiBlockchain**

Ngoku chaza indlela ezisebenza ngayo iintengiselwano kwi-blockchain.

Ukufumana i-bitcoin:

* vula i-wallet
* cofa 'fumana' okanye 'deposit'
* khuphela idilesi, wabelane ngekhonkco, okanye ubonise i-QR code

Ukuthumela i-bitcoin:

* vula i-wallet
* khuphela okanye skena idilesi yomamkeli
* ngenisa isixa
* phinda uqinisekise zonke iinkcukacha
* thumela intengiselwano (broadcast the transaction)
* linda ukuqinisekiswa

Yenza ezi ngongoma zicace:

* intengiselwano idlulisa ubunini, hayi iinkozo zemali ezibonakalayo
* iintengiselwano azinakuguqulwa
* ii-node ziqinisekisa ukuba zisemthethweni
* abavukuzi (miners) bafaka iintengiselwano kwiibhloko
* imirhumo ichaphazela indlela yokukhethwa kokuqinisekiswa
* iintengiselwano kwi-blockchain zikhuselekile ngokubanzi, kodwa zicotha kwaye zihlala zibiza kakhulu kuneeNtengiselwano zeLightning

Umzobo wokuhamba kwentengiselwano okwisahluko uluncedo kakhulu apha, kuba unceda abafundi babone indlela ukusuka kwisicelo se-wallet ukuya kokuqinisekiswa kwenethiwekhi.

**Iintengiselwano Zisebenza kunye noQeqesho lweNdima**

Sebenzisa indlela yokuzilolonga ngokubambisana esesahlukweni ukuqinisekisa ukuqonda. Chaza indima ezine ezibandakanyekayo:

* umthumeli
* umamkeli
* umvukuzi (miner)
* umqhubi we-node

Indlela elula yeklasi kukwabela indima aze umfundisi ahambe nentengiselwano inyathelo ngenyathelo. Oku kunceda abafundi babone ukuba intengiselwano yeBitcoin ayilulo umlingo, yinkqubo ehambelanayo equka imvume, ukuqinisekiswa, ukufakwa kwi-block, kunye nohlaziyo lwe-ledger.

Injongo apha ayikokungena nzulu kwezobuchwephesha. Injongo kukunceda abafundi baqonde ukuba ngubani owenza ntoni kwintengiselwano kwaye kutheni ukuqinisekisa kubalulekile.

##### 6.5 Musa Ukuthembela, Qinisekisa, imizuzu esi-8

Chaza ukuba oku kusebenza ku:

* ii-wallet
* iitshintshiselwano (exchanges)
* ii-apps
* iinkcukacha zentengiselwano
* iimangalo malunga 'nengeniso elula'
* iiprojekthi ezizenza ngathi zifana neBitcoin

Cacisa ukuba iBitcoin ifuna abasebenzisi bacinge nzulu, baqinisekise into abayisebenzisayo, kwaye baphephe ukuthembela ngokungaboni. Chaza kwakhona ukuba kutheni izixhobo ezivulekileyo zibalulekile kule meko: zenza ukuba kube nokwenzeka ukuqinisekisa ngokuzimeleyo.

#### Amanqaku oTitshala

Isahluko esi sisebenziseka kakhulu, ngoko ke phambili ekucaciseni, ukhuseleko, kunye nokuphindaphinda.

Abafundi abafuni ukufunda zonke iindidi ze-wallet kwiklasi enye. Iinjongo eziphambili zezi:

* ukuqonda izinto ezisisiseko ze-wallet
* ukuqonda ukuzigcina wedwa (self-custody)
* ukufunda indlela esebenza ngayo i-transaction
* ukwamkela ingqondo yokuhlola ngokuzimeleyo

Qaphela kakhulu xa uthetha nge-seed phrase kunye nokuseta i-wallet. Abafundi mabaphume beqonda ukuba ezi azizizo iinkcukacha ezincinci, kodwa zisisiseko sobunini beBitcoin.

Ezona mifanekiso nemisebenzi iluncedo kweli sahluko zezi:

* uthelekiso phakathi kwe-wallet egcinwe ngumntu wesithathu kunye ne-wallet ozigcinelayo
* itafile yokuthelekisa iintlobo ze-wallet
* umsebenzi wokuseta i-wallet inyathelo ngenyathelo
* umzobo wokuhamba kwe-transaction
* umsebenzi wokuhamba kwe-transaction ngokweendima

##### Umzekelo oLungileyo

* Kubalulekile ukuba abafundi benze ngokwenene ukuseta i-wallet okanye babukele umboniso ochanekileyo, benze i-seed phrase ibe yinto ephambili nge-'La magama ali-12 YI-Bitcoin yakho,' bavavanye iimeko ezifana ne-'Kwenzeka ntoni xa ulahlekelwa yifowuni yakho?', kwaye baqhelise ukuqaphela ubuqhophololo (phishing).
* Ootitshala mababe ziikhokelo ezisebenzayo ezakhe zakwenza oku ngaphambili, babe noxanduva lokhuseleko ngaphandle kokoyika kakhulu, kwaye bathethe inyaniso ngobunzima kunye nemfuno yokufunda.
* Abafundi baziva ukuba bafumene isakhono sokwenene abanokusi sebenzisa, baqonde ukuba i-seed phrase iyinyani kwaye ibalulekile hayi nje ingcamango, bazive bekulungele ukubamba iBitcoin yabo, kwaye baqonde ukuba ukungagcini ndawo kufuna uxanduva lomntu.
* Iziphumo zoFundo ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi bayakwazi ukuseta i-wallet kwaye baqonde umahluko phakathi kwezitshixo zikawonke-wonke nezabucala, baqonde umahluko wokugcina phakathi kwe-wallet egcinwe ngumntu wesithathu kunye ne-wallet ozigcinelayo, bachaze indlela i-transaction esebenza ngayo kubandakanya i-inputs, outputs, kunye neefizi, babonise ukuqonda ukhuseleko kubandakanya ukukhusela i-seed phrase, kwaye babuze imibuzo ebalulekileyo malunga nobunini kunye nolawulo.

##### Ulawulo lwexesha

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili ku:

* Ukuqonda izinto ezisisiseko ze-wallet
* Ukuqonda ukuzigcina wedwa
* Ukufunda indlela esebenza ngayo i-transaction
* Ukwamkela ingqondo yokuhlola ngokuzimeleyo

Ukuba ixesha likhona, chitha ixesha ku:

* Itheyibhile yokuthelekisa phakathi kwe-wallet egcinwe ngumntu wesithathu kunye ne-wallet ozigcinelayo
* Itheyibhile yokuthelekisa iintlobo ze-wallet
* Umsebenzi wokuseta i-wallet inyathelo ngenyathelo kunye nomboniso ophilayo
* Umzobo wokuhamba kwe-transaction kunye nokubala iifizi
* Iindlela zokhuseleko eziphambili kunye nokuthathela ingqalelo i-hardware wallet

##### Ukuba Abafundi Banengxaki
