# 3 - Yintoni Imali yeFiat?

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcinga Ephambili: Imali ye-fiat yinkqubo yemali elawulwa embindini esekwe kumthetho, ukuthembana, kunye nokwandiswa kwetyala, inika amaziko aphambili amandla amakhulu ekudaleni nasekulawuleni imali.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi baya kuba nako:

* Chaza indlela imali ye-fiat eyavela ngayo kunye nendlela eyahlukileyo ngayo kwimali exhaswa zizinto ezixabisekileyo.
* Chaza iimpawu eziphambili zenkqubo ye-fiat, kubandakanya imali esemthethweni, ukuthembana, kunye nolawulo oluphakathi.
* Qonda indlela iibhanki ezisebenzisa ngayo inkqubo ye-fractional reserve ukwandisa inani lemali ngokusebenzisa ityala.
* Chonga abadlali abaphambili abachaphazela kwaye baxhamla kwinkqubo ye-fiat, kubandakanya oorhulumente, iibhanki eziphakathi, iibhanki, kunye nabantu abacebileyo abanempahla.
* Chaza ukuba yintoni i-Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), kunye nesizathu sokuba zibe yindlela elawulwa ngakumbi yemali ye-fiat.
* Vavanya ezinye zeziphumo zoqoqosho nezentlalo zolawulo lwemali oluphakathi.

#### Ukufundisa kwi-Intanethi

* Sebenzisa isilayidi esinye esicacileyo sokuthelekisa imali exhaswa zizinto ezixabisekileyo kunye nemali ye-fiat.
* Fundisa indlela yokudala imali usebenzisa umzekelo omnye olula wenani endaweni yengcaciso ende engacacanga.
* Zoba inkqubo ye-fiat bukhoma kwi-whiteboard yedijithali ukuze abafundi babone ukuba ngubani olawula ntoni.
* Gqiba ngokubuza abafundi ukuba bachaze imali ye-fiat ngesivakalisi esinye kwi-chat.

#### Ulungiselelo

* Lungiselela umzobo wengcaciso yeMonopoly money kunye netshathi yokuthelekisa "ngubani oxhamlayo kwi-fiat" (urhulumente, iibhanki, abantu abacebileyo, ibhanki ephakathi).
* Lungiselela umfanekiso wengcaciso ye-fractional reserve banking kunye nezixhobo zomzekelo "weebhayisikile/IOUs".
* Lungiselela iikopi zeKhadi leNgcaciso yeGama ukuze wabelane ngazo.

#### Inkqubo

Esi sifundo sichaza indlela ilizwe elahamba ngayo ukusuka kwimali exhaswa zizinto ezixabisekileyo ukuya kwimali ye-fiat, kunye nendlela inkqubo ye-fiat esebenza ngayo kwihlabathi langoku. Ulwakhiwo ngoku lulandela iDiploma ngokuthe ngqo ukuze amacandelo aphambili ahambelane nezinto zabafundi ngelixa kugcinwa inkxaso enzulu kumfundisi ngaphakathi kwicandelo ngalinye.

##### 3.0 Intshayelelo, imizuzu eli-10

Qala ngokudibanisa esi sahluko nesidlulileyo:

* Yintoni eyatshintsha xa imali ingasaxhaswanga yigolide?
* Kuthetha ukuthini ukuba imali ixhaswa kukuthembana kuphela?
* Ucinga ukuba ngubani olawula imali yale mihla?

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko sichaza indlela inkqubo ye-fiat eyakhayo, indlela esebenza ngayo, kunye nabantu abanamandla amakhulu kuyo

##### 3.1 Imbali emfutshane yeMali ye-Fiat, imizuzu engama-20

Khokela abafundi kwinkqubela-ntle yembali yesahluko:

* igolide nesilivere njengemali ezinzileyo
* amaphepha obungqina bokugcina kunye neebhanki zokuqala
* iibhanki zikhupha amabango amaninzi kunoko zinokubuyisa ngako
* ukusekwa kweFederal Reserve ngo-1913
* uMyalelo kaRoosevelt 6102 ngo-1933
* uMthetho weGold Reserve ngo-1934
* iBretton Woods ngo-1944
* uNixon waphelisa ukutshintshwa kweedola ngegolide ngo-1971

Gcina ingqwalasela kwipateni: imali yahamba ukusuka kubunini obuthe ngqo bezinto ezinqabileyo ukuya kumaphepha amabango, emva koko kwinkqubo ye-fiat engasaxhaswanga yigolide. Olu lutshintsho oluphambili abafundi ekufuneka baluqonde. Umgca wexesha kwesi sahluko uluncedo kakhulu ekubancedeni babone olu luhlu ngokucacileyo.

##### 3.2 Inkqubo ye-Fiat, imizuzu engama-45

**Yintoni Inkqubo yeFiat**

Cacisa ukuba imali ye-fiat yimali eyamkelwa ngumthetho, hayi kuba ixhaswe yimpahla enqabileyo. Cacisa la manqaku:

* Fiat ithetha "ngomthetho"
* Imithetho yemali esemthethweni ifuna abantu bayamkele
* Ixabiso layo lixhomekeke kuthembeko kurhulumente nakwibhanki ephakathi
* Zonke iimali eziphambili zamazwe namhlanje ziyiimali ze-fiat

Ungasebenzisa umzekelo osebhukwini othelekisa imali exhaswe yimpahla kunye neFiat money ukuze abafundi babone umahluko ngokucacileyo. Into ebalulekileyo yokufundisa apha kukuba imali ye-fiat ayixabisekanga ngenxa yezinto ezenziwe ngayo, kodwa kuba abantu kufuneka bayisebenzise kwaye bathembe inkqubo ejikeleze yona.

**Inkqubo yeBhanki yeFractional Reserve**

Le yenye yezona ndawo zibalulekileyo kwesi sahluko.

Cacisa kakuhle ukuba kwinkqubo ye-fractional reserve:

* iibhanki zigcina kuphela inxenye encinci yeediphozithi njengeyogcino
* ziboleka yonke enye imali eseleyo
* imali entsha ingena ekujikelezeni ngokubolekwa
* oku kwandisa inani lemali ejikeleza kwaye kwandisa nemboleko kuqoqosho lonke

Sebenzisa umzekelo ka"Dax kunye neBhayisikile eziQikelelweyo" ukuba kuyanceda. Lo mzekelo wenza ingongoma ephambili icace: izithembiso ezininzi zenziwa, kodwa isiseko sokwenyani singaphantsi kakhulu. Oku kunceda abafundi baqonde ukuba kutheni kuvela i-bank run kwaye kutheni inkqubo ixhomekeke kuthembeko.

Ukuba ixesha likhona, cacisa nalo mkhuba ubanzi wokunyuka nokuhla koqoqosho:

* ukwandiswa kwetyala
* imali eninzi ijikeleza
* amaxabiso anyuka kunye notyalo-mali olugqithisileyo
* ukungakwazi ukuhlawula kunye noloyiko
* ukungenelela kwebhanki ephakathi kunye noncedo-mali
* phinda

Injongo ayikokuba abafundi bafunde onke amanyathelo ngentloko, kodwa baqonde ukuba le nkqubo ixhaswa ngetyala kwaye ayizinzanga.

**Ngubani Owalawula Inkqubo yeFiat Kwaye Ngubani Ozuza Kuyo**

Khokela abafundi kwiindima ezine eziphambili ezikhankanyiweyo kwesi sahluko:

* urhulumente
* ibhanki ephakathi
* icandelo lezezimali, ngakumbi iibhanki
* abantu abacebileyo abanokufikelela kwiimpahla kunye nemali-mboleko enexabiso eliphantsi

Cacisa ingongoma ephambili yokufundisa: ulawulo lokudala imali kunye ngetyala aluhanjiswa ngokulinganayo. Amanye amaqela asondele kakhulu kule nkqubo kwaye azuza ngakumbi kuyo, ngelixa abanye bethwala iindleko, ngakumbi xa amaxabiso enyuka kwaye ugcino lwemali luphulukana namandla okuthenga. oku kunganceda ukomeleza le ngxoxo.

##### 3.3 Iimali Zedijithali zeBhanki Ephakathi, 15 imizuzu

Cacisa ukuba Iimali Zedijithali zeBhanki Ephakathi ziyimali ezizeleyo zedijithali ezikhutshwe ngurhulumente. Cacisa ukuba kutheni esi sahluko sizibona njengophuhliso olubalulekileyo:

* zizeleyo zedijithali
* zikhutshwa kwaye zilawulwa ziibhanki eziphakathi
* zinokunyusa ukongamela, ukulandelela, kunye nolawulo lwezithengiselwano
* zenza umgaqo-nkqubo wemali ube ngqo ngakumbi kwaye uchane ngakumbi

Le nxalenye kufuneka incede abafundi babone ukuba iiCBDC azilohlobo "olutsha" lwemali ezinzileyo. Zingumgangatho wedijithali ngakumbi nowugxile ngakumbi kulawulo olufanayo lwefiat.

###### Isishwankathelo kunye nokuJonga uQonde

Vala ngemibuzo emifutshane enje:

* Yintoni eyenza imali ye-fiat yahluke kwimali exhaswe yimpahla?

Ukuba kuyimfuneko, cela abafundi bachaze inxalenye yesahluko ngamagama abo endaweni yokuphinda iinkcazelo. Oko kuya kukunika umbono ongcono wokuba baqonde ntoni ngokwenene.

#### Amanqaku oTitshala

Esi sahluko siqulethe umxholo obalulekileyo kakhulu, ngoko injongo kufuneka ibe kukucacisa, ukulandelelana, kunye nemizekelo ecacileyo.

Qhubeka ubuyisela abafundi kumxholo ophambili: imali ye-fiat ilawulwa embindini, ixhaswa ngetyala, kwaye ixhomekeke kuthembeko kwiinkokeli ezinamandla.

Zama ukuphepha ukulahleka kwiimini ezininzi okanye iinkcukacha zezopolitiko. Injongo enzulu kukuba abafundi baqonde ulwakhiwo lwenkqubo.

Amanqaku abalulekileyo okufanele agxininiswe, ukuba ixesha lifutshane, ngala:

3.2 indlela esebenza ngayo inkqubo yeebhanki ezisebenzisa i-fractional reserve

3.4 kutheni ii-CBDC zibalulekile

##### Umzekelo Olungileyo

* Kubalulekile ukucacisa imali ye-fiat kwangoko nge "Urhulumente uthi njalo KWAYE wonke umntu uyavuma," wenze imizekelo efana nemali yeMonopoly kunye neemeko zokuboleka, ubonise inkqubo ye-fractional reserve banking ngokubonakalayo.
* Ootitshala kufuneka bacacise kakuhle, bachaze izibonelelo kunye neengxaki, basebenzise amanani okwenyani ukuxhasa incoko, kwaye bathi "abantu abacinga kakuhle banokungavumelani."
* Abafundi bayaqonda indlela ibhanki yabo esebenza ngayo ngokwenene, baziveleliswe kwiindawo ezahlukeneyo kwinkqubo (ababolekayo bayaphumelela, abagcina imali bayaphulukana), kwaye baqonde ukuba yonke into inezinto ezilungileyo nezimbi.
* Iziphumo zokufunda ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi bayakwazi ukuchaza umahluko phakathi kwemali ye-fiat kunye nemali exhaswa zizinto ezithile, baqonde indlela i-fractional reserve banking eyenza ngayo imali ngokuboleka, bachonge ukuba ngubani ongenelayo kwaye ngubani ohlawulayo kwiinkqubo ze-fiat, kwaye bakwazi ukuqikelela iziphumo zokunyuka kwamaxabiso (inflation) kumandla okuthenga.

##### Ulawulo Lwexesha

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili kwezi:

* Yintoni imali ye-fiat
* Indlela esebenza ngayo i-fractional reserve banking
* Ngubani ongenelwa yile nkqubo
* Kutheni ii-CBDC zibalulekile

Ukuba uhamba phambili, thatha ixesha kwi:

* Ukungena nzulu kwiinkqubo ezithile zeebhanki eziphakathi
* Uhlalutyo lwamanani okunyuka kwamaxabiso kumazwe ahlukeneyo
* Uphando ngezinye iindlela zokulawula imali

##### Ukuba Abafundi Banengxaki

* Imali ye-fiat (ayixhaswanga) → Umzekelo wemali yeMonopoly; "wonke umntu uyavuma."
* Fractional reserve → Umzekelo weebhayisikile; wonke umntu ufuna iibhayisikile ngaxeshanye.
* Ngubani ongenelayo/ohlawulayo → Imibuzo yomntu siqu malunga nomvuzo wabo kunye namaxabiso.
