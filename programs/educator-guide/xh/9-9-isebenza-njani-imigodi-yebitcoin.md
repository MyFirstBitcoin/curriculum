# 9 - Isebenza Njani Imigodi yeBitcoin

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcamango Ephambili: Ukumba kweBitcoin kunye nokuqinisekisa kwenode kusebenza kunye ukukhusela inethiwekhi, ukuqinisekisa iintengiselwano, kunye nokunyanzelisa imithetho yenkqubo ngokusebenzisa Ubufakazi Bomsebenzi (Proof of Work).

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi kufuneka bakwazi:

* Chaza umahluko phakathi kwendima yeenode zeBitcoin kunye nendima yabavumbi beBitcoin.
* Chaza indlela iinodes eziqinisekisa ngayo iintengiselwano, ukwabelana ngolwazi, kunye nokunceda ukunyanzelisa imithetho yeBitcoin.
* Chaza into abayenzayo abavumbi, kubandakanya ukukhetha iintengiselwano, ukwakha iibhloko ezilindelekileyo, kunye nokhuphiswano lokufumana i-hash efanelekileyo yebhloko.
* Chaza i-mempool kwaye uchaze ukuba kutheni isebenza njengegumbi lokulinda leentengiselwano ezingekaqinisekiswa.
* Chaza indlela imirhumo yeentengiselwano echaphazela ngayo ukhetho lwabavumbi kunye nesantya sokuqinisekiswa.
* Chaza Ubufakazi Bomsebenzi (Proof of Work) njengendlela ekhuseleka ngayo iBitcoin ngokwenza uhlaselo lube luxabisa kakhulu.
* Chaza indlela ulungelelwaniso lobunzima olunceda ukugcina ixesha lebhloko eliphakathi kwemizuzu eli-10.
* Hamba nomfundi kwinkqubo epheleleyo yentengiselwano yeBitcoin, ukusuka ekudalweni nasekutyikityweni ukuya ekuqinisekisweni kwi-bhloko.

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko sichaza indlela inethiwekhi eqhuba ngayo iintengiselwano ngokwenene kunye nendlela ukumba okukhusela ngayo inkqubo ngaphandle komphathi ophakathi.

##### 9.1 Iinodes zeBitcoin kunye nabavumbi, imizuzu engama-47

**Iinodes kunye nabavumbi, Iindima Ezahlukeneyo**

Qala ngokwahlula ngokucacileyo ezi ndima zimbini.

Iinodes zeBitcoin:

* zigcina ikopi ye-blockchain
* ziqinisekisa ukuba iintengiselwano zilandela imithetho
* zabelana ngolwazi nezinye iinodes
* zinceda ii-wallets kunye nezinye iisoftware ukufikelela kwidatha ye-blockchain
* zingala iintengiselwano ezingavumelekanga okanye iibhloko ezingavumelekanga

Isahluko sichaza ii-node njengabalindi bokuvavanya, kwaye siyayandisa loo nto ngomzekeliso womlindi wendlela wedijithali. Oku kuyanceda kuba kubonisa ii-node njengabahloli kunye nabalungelelanisi, hayi njengookumkani. Umzobo ukwakhona uqinisekisa ukuba ii-node ezininzi zigcina iikopi zencwadi-mali kwihlabathi lonke.

Abavukuzi beBitcoin:

* baqokelela iintengiselwano ezisemthethweni
* bahlanganisa iibhloko ezizakungeniswa
* bakhuphisana ukufumana i-hash yebhloko evumelekileyo
* basasaza iibhloko ezisemthethweni xa bephumelele
* bafumana imivuzo yebhloko kunye nemirhumo yeentengiselwano

Eyona nto ibalulekileyo efundwayo kwesi sahluko kukuba injongo yokumba ayisosokudala i-bitcoin entsha kuphela, kodwa kukusasaza ukhuseleko lweBitcoin. I-bitcoin entsha yinkuthazo, ngelixa inkqubo yokumba ngokwayo iyindlela yokhuseleko.

**Okwenziwa Zizithuthi Ngokwenene**

Yakha kwicandelo le-node usebenzisa uluhlu lwemisebenzi ye-node esahlukweni:

* Abalindi bokuvavanya: bajonga ukuba iintengiselwano kunye neebhloko zilandela imithetho
* Iziko lonxibelelwano: zixhuma omnye komnye kwaye zabelana ngedatha yeentengiselwano
* Umhloli womgangatho: ziwala ulwazi olungasebenziyo
* Umazisi we-blockchain: zinika idatha kwezinye izixhobo ezifana neewallet
* Umamkeli we-node entsha: zinceda ii-node ezintsha ukufumana i-blockchain, ngelixa i-node nganye entsha isaqinisekisa idatha ngokuzimeleyo

Lixesha elifanelekileyo lokugxininisa ukuba ukuqhuba i-node kunika umsebenzisi inkululeko engakumbi. Endaweni yokuxhomekeka ngokupheleleyo kwiinkonzo zangaphandle ukubaxelela imeko yenethiwekhi, banokuyiqinisekisa ngokwabo.  yenza le ngongoma icace, kuquka nokukhankanywa kweBitcoin Core njengomzekelo wesoftware abasebenzisi abanokuyiqhuba.

**Okwenziwa Ngabavukuzi Ngokwenene**

Ngoku chaza inkqubo yokumba ngokucokisekileyo.

Abavukuzi:

* baqokelela iintengiselwano eziqinisekisiweyo kodwa ezingekaqinisekiswa
* bazidibanisa zibe yibhloko ezizakungeniswa
* baphinda-phinda i-hash yedatha yebhloko bejonga i-hash evumelekileyo
* basasaza ibhloko ephumeleleyo kwinethiwekhi
* bafumana imivuzo ukuba ibhloko yamkelwe

Sebenzisa umzekeliso wesahluko 'isikhwama esikhulu sezitshixo' ukuba kuyanceda. Inika abafundi umfanekiso ocacileyo womdyarho wokumba. Ingcamango ephambili ayikokuba abavukuzi basombulula ingxaki yezibalo encedayo ngokwesiqhelo, kodwa kukuba babonisa ukuba basebenzise amandla okwenyani kunye nokubala ukukhusela inkqubo.

Apha kukwakho nethuba lokuchaza imivuzo yabavukuzi:

* umvuzo webhloko: i-bitcoin entsha ekhutshelweyo
* imirhumo yeentengiselwano: imirhumo eqhotyoshelwe kwiintengiselwano abafuna ziqinisekiswe

Cacisa ukuba abavukuzi badla ngokukhetha iintengiselwano ezinemirhumo ephezulu, kuba oko kwandisa umvuzo wabo. Isahluko sichaza nokuqhawulwa komvuzo (halving) apha, ngoko ungakhankanya ngokufutshane ukuba umvuzo webhloko wehla rhoqo kwiibhloko ezili-210,000, malunga neminyaka emine, ngokwecebo lolawulo lwesibonelelo seBitcoin. Amakhasi 5 no 6 aquka ishedyuli yesibonelelo kunye netafile yokuncitshiswa okulandelayo, enokunceda ukuqinisekisa ukukhutshwa okuqikelelweyo kweBitcoin.

**I-Valid Block Hash, Proof of Work, kunye noLungiso lobunzima**

Esi sahluko sesona sixhobo siphambili sesahluko.

Cacisa ukuba abavukuzi bafuna i-hash yebhloko evumelekileyo, oko kuthetha i-hash yebhloko edibana nenjongo yenethiwekhi. Isahluko sichaza oku njengokufumana inani elingaphantsi kwenjongo emiselwe yenethiwekhi.

Emva koko chaza iProof of Work ngokucacileyo:

* abavukuzi kufuneka benze umsebenzi wokubala ophindaphindiweyo
* owokuqala ukufumana i-hash evumelekileyo ubonisa ukuba wenze loo msebenzi
* oku kwenza kubize kakhulu ukuphinda kubhalwe okanye ukuhlasela incwadi-mali
* ii-node emva koko ziqinisekisa ibhloko phambi kokuyamkela

Umhlathi onamandla wokufundisa ngulo:

IProof of Work ikhusela iBitcoin ngokwenza ubuqhetseba bubize kwaye ukuqinisekisa kube lula.

Chaza nolungiso lobunzima:

* inethiwekhi ilungisa ubunzima bokumba rhoqo kwiibhloko ezili-2,016
* oku kwenzeka malunga neentsuku ezilishumi elinesine
* injongo kukugcina ixesha eliphakathi kwebhloko kufutshane nemizuzu eli-10
* xa amandla okumba enyuka kwinethiwekhi, ubunzima buyenyuka
* xa amandla okumba ehla, ubunzima buhla

Amakhasi 7 no 8 achaza le nkqubo kwaye abonisa indlela ekuya kuba nzima ngayo ukufumana i-hash xa injongo inzima. Oku kunceda abafundi baqonde ukuba ixesha leBitcoin alilawulwa ligunya eliphakathi kodwa yimigaqo yeprotocol ephendula ngokuzenzekelayo kwiimeko zenethiwekhi.

##### 9.2 Yintoni iMempool?, imizuzu eli-15

Ngoku qhubeka uye kwi-mempool.

Cacisa ukuba i-mempool ligumbi lokulinda leentengiselwano ezisemthethweni, ezingekaqinisekiswa. Xa umsebenzisi esasaza intengiselwano, ii-node ziqinisekisa kuqala. Ukuba ivumelekile, bayongeza kwi-mempool yabo kwaye bayabelana ngayo nezinye ii-node. Emva koko abavukuzi banokukhetha kwezo ntengiselwano zilindileyo xa besakha ibhloko. Amakhasi 10 no 11 achaza le nkqubo ngokuthe ngqo.

Amanqaku abalulekileyo okufanele ugxininise:

* i-mempool ayisosetyenziswa lwe-blockchain
* iintengiselwano ezikhoyo azikakaqinisekiswa
* isikhululo ngasinye sigcina eyaso i-mempool
* akukho mempool enye yelizwe lonke
* iintengiselwano ezineemali zefizi eziphezulu zinethuba elingcono lokukhethwa ngokukhawuleza

Isahluko sikwachaza nezizathu eziqhelekileyo zokuba intengiselwano ingahlala ingaqinisekiswanga ixesha elide:

* imali yefizi ephantsi
* uxinzelelo lwenethiwekhi
* umzamo wokuphinda kusetyenziswe imali enye kabini
* idatha engachanekanga okanye engaphelelanga
* intengiselwano engalunganga

Ukuba kuyanceda, khankanya umsebenzi usebenzisa i-mempool.space njengendlela ebonakalayo yokujonga iintengiselwano ezingakaqinisekiswa kunye namanqanaba efizi. Kwakhona cacisa ukuba i-mempool.space ngumkhangeli nje, hayi i-mempool ngokwayo.

##### Umzekelo Omhle

* Kubalulekile ukucacisa kwangoko ukuba Abavukuzi ≠ Izikhululo, ubonise ukumba njengomsebenzi wezoqoqosho oneendleko zezixhobo kunye nombane, usebenzise ulungiso lobunzima kunye neProof of Work ukuchaza indlela yokhuseleko, kwaye uvavanye ukuqonda ngemizekelo malunga notshintsho kwinethiwekhi.
* Ootitshala kufuneka basebenzise amanani okwenyani ukuxhasa iingxoxo, bacacise gca baze baphinde-phinde umahluko phakathi kwabaSebenzisi-migodi (Miners) kunye neeNode, babe nenyani malunga nemiceli-mngeni yokugxila kwamandla kwiindawo zokumba (mining pools), kwaye bahloniphe ubunzima bokwenene obubandakanyekayo.
* Abafundi bayaqonda ukuba ukumba (mining) kwenziwa ngabantu abahlakaniphile abenza umsebenzi onzima kuba bafumana iBitcoin, bayabona ukuba imivuzo ikhuthaza ukuziphatha ngokunyanisekileyo kuba ingeniso yabasebenzi-migodi ixhomekeke ekuphumeleleni kweBitcoin, babona indlela inkqubo izilawula ngayo ngokuzenzekelayo ngokuhlengahlengisa ubunzima, baqonda ukuba ukumba yishishini lokwenene hayi uncedo, kwaye bayayixabisa into yokuba ukhuseleko lweBitcoin lubiza umbane nemali yokwenene.
* Iziphumo zokufunda ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi bayakwazi ukwahlula abasebenzi-migodi abenza iibhloko kwi-node eziqinisekisa, baqonde iProof of Work njengesixhobo sokhuseleko esenza uhlaselo lubize kakhulu, baqaphele ukuba uhlengahlengiso lobunzima lugcina ixesha lebhloko malunga nemizuzu eli-10, baqonde imivuzo yabasebenzi-migodi malunga nemivuzo yeebhloko kunye nemirhumo, bachaze ukuba kutheni uhlaselo lwe-51% lungasebenzi, kwaye babone ukumba njengezenzo zoqoqosho ezineendleko kunye neenzuzo zokwenene.
