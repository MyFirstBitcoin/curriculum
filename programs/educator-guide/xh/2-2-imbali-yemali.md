# 2 - Imbali yeMali

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcinga Ephambili: Imali yaphuhliswa ngokuhamba kwexesha njengoko uluntu lwalufuna iindlela ezingcono zokusombulula iingxaki zotshintshiselwano, ukuthembana, ukuthwaleka, kunye nokulungelelanisa.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi baya kukwazi uku:

* Landela indlela imali eyaphuhliswa ngayo ukusuka kwiinkqubo zotshintshiselwano ukuya kwimali yale mihla kunye nemali yedijithali.
* Chaza ukuba kutheni utshintshiselwano luba lusilela njengoko uluntu lukhula, ngakumbi ngenxa yengxaki yokungafani kweminqweno phakathi kwabantu ababini.
* Chaza indlela imali yezinto (commodity money) eyavela ngayo ngokwendalo ngenxa yokurhweba kwimarike.
* Chonga amanyathelo aphambili kuphuhliso lwemali yeengqekembe, imali yephepha, kunye nemali yedijithali.
* Hlola utshintsho ukusuka kwimali ezinzileyo ukuya kwimali engazinzanga kunye nendlela oko kwachaphazela ngayo ukuthembana, uzinzo, kunye nobomi bemihla ngemihla.
* Cinga ngendlela utshintsho kwimali oluchaphazela ngayo uluntu, izikhuthazo, kunye nokuziphatha kwezoqoqosho kwabantu.

#### Ukufundisa kwi-Intanethi

* Sebenzisa isilayidi sethayimlain esabelwana ngaso ukuze abafundi balandele ulungelelwaniso ukusuka kutshintshiselwano ukuya kwimali yedijithali.
* Veza inqanaba ngalinye ngalinye endaweni yokubonisa ithayimlain yonke ngaxeshanye.
* Buza abafundi kwincoko ukuba yeyiphi ingxaki utshintsho ngalunye lwemali oluzama ukuyisombulula.
* Sebenzisa ibhodi emhlophe esabelwana ngayo ukuze ubonise ingxaki, isisombululo, kunye nengxaki entsha.

#### Ukulungiselela

* Lungiselela ithayimlain (utshintshiselwano → imali yezinto → imali yeengqekembe → iphepha → idijithali) ukuze iboniswe.
* Phanda kwaye ulungiselele imizekelo emi-2 ukuya kwemi-3 yembali yemali eyasetyenziswa kummandla wakho kunye nemifanekiso/inkcazo.
* Lungiselela izixhobo zokuchaza ingxaki "yokungafani kweminqweno phakathi kwabantu ababini" kunye nokuba kutheni utshintshiselwano lusilela.

#### Inkqubo

Esi sifundo sinceda abafundi baqonde ukuba imali ayizange ivele ngexesha elinye, kwaye ayizange yenziwe ngurhulumente kuphela. Endaweni yoko, yaphuhliswa ngokuhamba kwexesha njengoko abantu babefuna iindlela ezingcono zokutshintshiselana ngexabiso. Isahluko ngoku silandela ulungelelwaniso olufanayo neDiploma, namanyathelo embali afakwe ngaphakathi kwiindawo eziphambili endaweni yokwahlulwa kwiintloko eziphambili ezizimeleyo.

##### 2.0 Intshayelelo, imizuzu eli-10

Qala ngokubuza:

* Nicinga ukuba abantu basebenzisa ntoni phambi kokuba kubekho imali?
* Kutheni utshintshiselwano lunokuba nzima?
* Nicinga ukuba imali yenziwa ngurhulumente, okanye yavela ngokwendalo?

Chaza ukuba imali yaphuhliswa ngokutshintshiselana kwabantu ngokuhamba kwexesha njengoko babesoloko befuna iindlela ezingcono zokutshintshiselana ngexabiso

##### 2.1 Ukusuka kuTshintshiselwano ukuya kwiMali yale Mihla, imizuzu engama-60

**Utshintshiselwano kunye neMida zalo**

Chaza utshintshiselwano njengotshintshiselwano oluthe ngqo kwaye wazise ingxaki yokungafani kweminqweno phakathi kwabantu ababini. Sebenzisa umzekelo weziqhamo osesahlukweni, okanye wenze umzekelo olula kwigumbi lokufundela, ukuze ubonise indlela urhwebo oluba nzima ngayo xa umntu ngamnye efuna into eyahlukileyo.

**Ukusuka kwiMali yezinto ukuya kwiMali yeengqekembe**

Khokela abafundi ngendlela uluntu olwamkela ngayo kancinci kancinci iimpahla ezamkelwa ngumntu wonke njengemali. Gxininisa imizekelo efana neenkomo, amagobolondo, ityuwa, isilivere, kunye negolide, uze uchaze ukuba kutheni izinyithi ezixabisekileyo zaba ngaphezulu. Chaza ngokufutshane izibonelelo kunye neengxaki zemali yeengqekembe, kubandakanya iingxaki zokuthwala kunye nobuqhetseba bokunciphisa ixabiso.

**Uphuhliso lweMali yePhepha**

Chaza indlela amaphepha aqinisekisiweyo egolide okanye isilivere enza urhwebo lwaba lula ngayo. Gxininisa ukuba imali yephepha ekuqaleni yayimele into ebonakalayo kwaye inokutshintshwa. Eli lixesha elifanelekileyo lokunceda abafundi babone utshintsho ukusuka kwimali yezinto ebonakalayo ukuya kwimali emele into.

**Utshintsho ukusuka kwiMali ezinzileyo ukuya kwiMali engazinzanga**

Gxininisa ukuba iibhanki norhulumente baqale ukukhupha amaphepha angaphezulu kunegolide ababenayo. Emva koko chaza, ngendlela elula:

* Ukushenxwa kwemali enokutshintshwa
* Bretton Woods
* Ukuphela komgangatho wegolide ngo-1971
* Iziphumo zokuphila ngemali engazinzanga

Icandelo eli kufuneka ligxile kancinci ekufundeni imihla nemihla kwaye ngakumbi ekuqondeni utshintsho olubanzi kwithemba, umlinganiselo, ityala, amaxabiso, kunye namandla okuthenga. Umzekelo "womlinganiso oguquguqukayo" osesahlukweni uluncedo kakhulu apha.

##### 2.2 Imali Yedijithali, imizuzu engama-20

Vala umxholo ophambili ngokubonisa ngokufutshane indlela iinkqubo zokuhlawula ezaphucuka ngayo ngakumbi:

* amakhadi etyala
* ibhanki ekwi-intanethi
* imali yedijithali

Nceda abafundi baqonde ukuba namhlanje uninzi lwemali sele luyidijithali, nangona lusese yinxalenye yenkqubo yemali ye-fiat.

###### Isishwankathelo nokuJonga uQondiso

Buza imibuzo embalwa ekhawulezayo:

* Kutheni utshintshiselwano lwezinto lungasebenzi kakuhle?
* Yintoni imali yezinto ezithengiswayo?
* Kutheni uluntu lwashiya iinkozo lwasebenzisa imali yephepha?
* Yintoni eyatshintsha xa imali ingasaxhaswanga yigolide?
* Yintoni eyahlukileyo ngemali ye-fiat yedijithali xa ithelekiswa neendlela zangaphambili zemali?

#### Iincwadi zoTitshala

Gcina isahluko sigxile kuphuhliso kunye nesizathu nesiphumo, hayi nje iziganeko zembali.

Abafundi abafuni ukufunda ngentloko yonke imihla okanye iziganeko zemali, kufuneka baqonde ukuba kutheni utshintsho ngalunye lwenzeka.

Eyona nto ibalulekileyo yokufundisa kweli sahluko yile:  
abantu bajongene neengxaki zotshintshiselwano, benza izisombululo, emva koko kwavela iingxaki ezintsha kwakhona.

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili:



1. Utshintshiselwano lwezinto kunye nengxaki yokufuna okufanayo
1. Imali yezinto ezithengiswayo kunye nemali yephepha
1. Imali ezinzileyo vs. imali engazinzanga

##### Umzekelo oLungileyo

* Kubalulekile ukuxela imbali njengesizathu nesiphumo endaweni yemihla, ubuze "Yeyiphi ingxaki le nto eyayisombulula?" kutshintsho ngalunye, kwaye uphande imbali yemali yengingqi.
* Ootitshala kufuneka bazise amandla ebali, baqhubeke bebuza "Kutheni batshintshe?", bavume ukuba abazi yonke into, kwaye baphande kunye nabafundi.
* Abafundi bafumana amava okuba imali yadalwa kuba abantu bayifuna, baqonde ukuba nabo babenokuyiqamba, kwaye babone imizekelo yokusombulula iingxaki ebantwini.
* Iziphumo zoFundo ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi banokuchaza indlela imali ephuhla ngayo beqonda _kutheni_ utshintsho ngalunye lwenzeka, bachonge ingxaki yokufuna okufanayo njengengxaki ephambili, bachaze izibonelelo kunye neengxaki zemali yezinto ezithengiswayo, kwaye badibanise nembali yoluntu lwabo.

##### Ulawulo lwexesha

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili:

* Utshintshiselwano lwezinto kunye nengxaki yokufuna okufanayo
* Imali yezinto ezithengiswayo kunye nemali yephepha
* Imali ezinzileyo vs. imali engazinzanga

Ukuba phambili, thatha ixesha kwi:

* Imizekelo eneenkcukacha yokuphazamiseka kwemali kunye nembali yeemali zemali
* Uthelekiso lweemali zezinto ezithengiswayo kwiinkcubeko ezahlukeneyo
* Ingxoxo ende malunga nokophuka kweBretton Woods

##### Ukuba Abafundi Banengxaki

* Imbali okanye imihla → Tshintshela ku "Yeyiphi ingxaki le nto eyayisombulula?"
* Umkhwa kwimbali → Zoba: Ingxaki → Isisombululo → Ingxaki Entsha.
* Ingxaki yokufuna okufanayo → Yenze ngokudlala nabafundi.
