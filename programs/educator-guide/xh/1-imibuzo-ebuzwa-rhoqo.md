# Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

#### Phambi kokuba Uqale

##### Yintoni le ncwadana isetyenziselwa yona?

Le ncwadana ikunceda ukufundisa iDiploma ye-My First Bitcoin ngokucacileyo, ngokuzithemba, nangokuguquguquka. Inika ulungelelwaniso, iingcebiso zokufundisa, inkxaso yokucwangcisa ixesha, kunye neengcebiso ezisebenzayo zokukunceda ukhokele iklasi kakuhle.

##### Ngaba kufuneka ndifunde yonke incwadana phambi kokufundisa?

Hayi. Kodwa kufuneka ubuncinane ufunde intshayelelo, isingeniso, kunye nesahluko oza kusifundisa. Ukutsiba yonke into uthembele ethamsanqeni asiyiyo indlela eyomeleleyo.

##### Ngaba le ncwadana yinkqubo yokuthetha?

Hayi. Sisixhobo sokuxhasa, hayi inkqubo yokuthetha. Awulindelekanga ukuba uyifunde igama negama phambi kwabafundi.

##### Ngaba kufuneka ndibe yingcali kwiBitcoin ukuze ndisebenzise le ncwadana?

Hayi. Kodwa kufuneka uzilungiselele kakuhle, uqonde iingcamango eziphambili zesahluko, kwaye uthethe inyaniso malunga nezinto ozaziyo nezingaziyo.

##### Kuthekani xa abafundi bebuzwa umbuzo endingakwazi ukuwuphendula?

Yithethe ngokucacileyo. Musa ukuzama ukuxoka. Bhala phantsi umbuzo, ubuyele kuwo kamva, kwaye usebenzise eli thuba ukubonisa ukuthembeka kwengqondo.

##### Ngaba kufuneka ndifundise yonke into kanye njengoko ibhaliwe?

Hayi. Iingcamango eziphambili kunye neenjongo zokufunda kufuneka zihlale zingatshintshanga, kodwa imizekelo yakho, imisebenzi, indlela yokuhamba, kunye nendlela yokuhambisa unokuyilungisa ngokwemeko yakho.



#### Malunga ne-Open Source

##### Ngaba le nto ivulelekile (open source)?

Ewe. Oko kuthetha ukuba yenzelwe ukwabelwana, ukusetyenziswa, ukuphuculwa, nokulungiswa endaweni yokugcinwa ifihlakele.

##### Ngaba ndingakopa okanye ndilungise amacandelo encwadana?

Ewe, ngaphakathi kwemigaqo yelayisensi. I-Open source ayithethi ukusetyenziswa ngokungenangqondo. Ithetha ukusetyenziswa ngokuxandileyo, ukulungisa, kunye nokunikela.

##### Kutheni i-open source ibalulekile apha?

Kuba imfundo kufuneka ifikeleleke, iphuculwe, kwaye ingalawulwa liqela elincinci labagcini-masango. I-Open source ivumela ootitshala abaninzi bafundise, bavavanye, baphucule, kwaye balungelelanise imathiriyali.



#### Malunga nokuguquguquka

##### Ngaba ndingayilungiselela ilizwe lam, isikolo, okanye uluntu lwam?

Ewe. Enyanisweni, kufuneka wenze njalo. Imizekelo yasekuhlaleni, imbali yasekuhlaleni, iingxaki zemali zasekuhlaleni, kunye namava abafundi basekuhlaleni zibalulekile.

##### Ngaba oku kungafundiswa kwi-intanethi okanye ebusweni?

Ewe. Incwadana yenzelwe ukuxhasa ukufundisa kwiimeko ezahlukeneyo, kodwa kungafuneka ulungelelanise kancinci kuxhomekeke kwindlela ofundisa ngayo, ubungakanani beqela, kunye nezixhobo ezikhoyo.

##### Ngaba ndinganciphisa okanye ndandise imisebenzi?

Ewe, logama nje isifundo sisafikelela kwinjongo ephambili. Musa ukusika kakhulu de iklasi ilahlekelwe yinjongo yayo.

##### Kuthekani ukuba abafundi bam bancinci, badala, bathule, banamandla, okanye baphantsi kunokuba bendilindele?

Lungisa indlela yakho, imizekelo, kunye nesantya. Ukufundisa kakuhle kuphendula kubafundi abaphambi kwakho, hayi kwiklasi egqibeleleyo engabonakaliyo.



#### Malunga neNcwadana Ngokwayo

##### Ngaba le ncwadana igqibelele kwaye iphelele?

Hayi. Iyanceda, kodwa ayigqibelelanga. Akukho ncwadana igqibeleleyo.

##### Kutheni amanye amacandelo eneenkcukacha ngakumbi kunezinye?

Kuba ezinye izifundo kulula ukuzifundisa kunezinye, kwaye amanye amacandelo asafuna ukuphuculwa ngakumbi. Le ncwadana sisixhobo sokuxhasa esisebenzayo, hayi incwadi engcwele yokugqibela.

##### Kuthekani ukuba ndiphawula into engacacanga, ebuthathaka, engekho, okanye engahambi kakuhle?

Kulungile. Oko kuthetha ukuba uyabakhathalela abafundi. Bhala phantsi kwaye wabelane ngengxelo ukuze imathiriyali iphucuke ekuhambeni kwexesha.

##### Ngaba incwadana ithatha indawo yokugweba kotitshala?

Hayi. Ukugweba kwakho kusabalulekile. Incwadana inokuxhasa ukufundisa kakuhle, kodwa ayinakuthatha indawo yokulungiselela, ukulalela, okanye ukuqonda okuqhelekileyo.



#### Imibuzo Esebenzayo

##### Yintoni endifanele ndiyenze phambi kweklasi?

Funda isahluko, jonga izixhobo, qonda injongo yesifundo, kwaye ucinge apho abafundi banokuthi banqabe khona.

##### Yintoni endifanele ndigxile kuyo kakhulu?

Ukucaca, isantya, ukuthatha inxaxheba kwabafundi, kunye nokuba abafundi bayayiqonda na ingcamango enkulu.

##### Kuthekani ukuba ndinexesha elincinci?

Nika ingqwalasela kwinjongo ephambili yesifundo. Kungcono ukufundisa ingcamango enye ebalulekileyo kakuhle kunokukhawuleza yonke into kakubi.

##### Yeyiphi indlela ephambili endifanele ndibe nayo njengotitshala?

Yiba ulungile, uzolile, unomdla, kwaye uthethe inyaniso. Umsebenzi wakho ayikokubonisa. Umsebenzi wakho kukunceda abafundi bacinge.
