# Indlela Yokusebenzisa Esi Sikhokelo

#### Wamkelekile

Olu xwebhu luyilelwe ukuba lube liqabane lokucinga—hayi iskripthi. Icandelo ngalinye linika ulwakhiwo, ixesha, iinjongo zokufunda, kunye neendlela ezivavanyiweyo eklasini, kodwa umsebenzi wokwenene wokufundisa wenzeka eklasini yakho.

Esi siqalo sikukhokelela kwicandelo ngalinye lesikhokelo soTitshala ukuze uqonde indlela yokusebenzisa into nganye.



#### Indlela Yokufunda Esi Sikhokelo

Phambi kokuba ungene kwiimodyuli, qonda indlela yokusondela kulo mxwebhu:

* **Okokuqala:** Funda yonke imodyuli phambi kokungena eklasini ukuze ufumane umbono opheleleyo wendlela yokuhamba kunye nenkqubela yokufunda.
* **Ukulungiselela:** Gxila kwiNjongo zokuFunda, Izixhobo & neeReshosi, kunye neCandelo lokuLungiselela.
* **Ukufundisa:** Sebenzisa icandelo leNkqubo njengesiqhelo sakho, kodwa ugcine ingqalelo yakho kubafundi bakho.
* **Emva kweklasi:** Phonononga 'Oko Kubonakala Kukuhle' kunye 'Ukuba Abafundi Bayasokola' ukuze uthathe amanyathelo alandelayo.
* **Ngokuqhubekayo:** Njengoko ufundisa, phawula esi sikhokelo. Bhala emacaleni. Phawula oko kusebenze, oko kungasebenzanga, noko utshintshe.

**Inqaku ngamava:** Ukuba umtsha ekufundiseni esi sifundo, sicebisa ukuba ulandele isikhokelo ngokungqongqo. Ulandelelwano, ixesha, kunye neendlela ziye zavavanywa kwiiklasi zokwenene. Njengoko ufumana amava kunye nokuzithemba, uya kuphuhlisa iingcamango zokulungelelanisa. Zithembeke kuloo kuzithemba—kodwa ziqale zithembe ulwakhiwo.



#### Ukuqonda Ulwakhiwo lweModyuli

##### Iinjongo zokuFunda

Ezi zizakhono kunye nokuqonda abafundi ekufuneka bakuphuhlise ukuze baphumelele kwidiploma ye-My First Bitcoin. Zicacile, ziyabonakala, kwaye zixhomekeke kwiziphumo eziphambili zokufunda—ukucinga okunzulu ngeenkqubo, imali, kunye nokukhetha.

**Ezi azinakutshintshwa.** Umfundi ngamnye ogqiba le modyuli kufuneka afumane ezi ziphumo. I-_yintoni_ kunye _kutheni_ kuhlala kungatshintshi kuyo yonke iklasi nakwiimeko ezahlukeneyo.

I-_indlela_—indlela, imizekelo, ixesha, kunye neendlela zokufundisa—zingatshintshwa kwaye kufuneka zilungelelaniswe nguwe ngokusekelwe kubafundi bakho kunye nemeko. Kodwa indawo ekuyiwa kuyo (iNjongo zokuFunda) iyafana kuye wonke umntu.

Yabelana ngale njongo nabafundi bakho ekuqaleni kwesifundo kwaye uyisebenzise ukukhokela uvavanyo lwakho lonke ixesha.

##### Ixesha & Ingcamango Ephambili

Imodyuli nganye yenzelwe ukuba ingene kwisifundo esiqhelekileyo semizuzu engama-90. Ingcamango Ephambili sisishwankathelo semgca omnye wento imodyuli ejolise kuyo. Yifunde kuqala ukuze uzilungiselele kwaye uqinisekise ukuba iyahambelana nekharityhulam yakho.

##### Izixhobo & neeReshosi

Eli candelo lichaza izinto ezifunekayo, amaphepha, izixhobo zedijithali, kunye nemifanekiso efunekayo kwisifundo, kubandakanya nezixhobo ezivela kwi-My First Bitcoin repository. Jonga olu luhlu phambi kweklasi kwaye uqokelele into oyidingayo.

##### Ukulungiselela

Eli candelo lichaza into omele uyenze phambi kokuba iklasi iqale—imisebenzi yokulungiselela, ukulungiselela engqondweni, okanye ukufunda okungqongqo okuzakunyusa ukuzithemba kwakho ngexesha lesifundo. Thatha oku ngokubaluleka. Imizuzu eli-15 oyichitha ulungiselela umdlalo okanye uphonononga imibuzo yengxoxo iya kukugcina imizuzu engama-30 yokudideka ngexesha lesifundo.



#### Isifundo: Icandelo leNkqubo

Icandelo leNkqubo yintliziyo yemodyuli. Yahlulwe yangamacandelo anamanani (1.1, 1.2, 1.3, njl.), ngalinye line njongo ecacileyo, indlela, kunye nemiyalelo inyathelo ngenyathelo.

**La macandelo ayilwe ukuba alandelane.** Icandelo ngalinye lakha kulwazi olufunyenwe kwicandelo elidlulileyo, lwenza inkqubela eya kwiNjongo zokuFunda. Walandele ngokulandelelana ukuze ufumane iziphumo zokufunda ezomeleleyo.

Oko kuthethwayo, xa usazi kakuhle isifundo, unokufumanisa ulandelelwano okanye iindlela ezizezinye ezisebenza kakuhle (okanye ngcono) kubafundi bakho ngelixa usafikelela kwiNjongo zokuFunda. Ukuba ufumana indlela esebenzayo ngakumbi, yisebenzise—kodwa qinisekisa ukuba abafundi basafikelela kwiziphumo ezifunwayo.

##### Injongo

Kutheni likho eli candelo? Yintoni ukufunda elikuvulayo? Ukuqonda injongo kukuvumela ukuba wazi xa lisebenza kwaye xa kufuneka utshintshe.

##### Indlela

Indlela ichaza uhlobo lomyalelo: Ingxoxo, Umsebenzi, Ingcaciso, okanye Umdlalo. Indlela nganye ivuselela iindawo ezahlukeneyo zengqondo zabafundi.

##### Ulwakhiwo

Ulwakhiwo lukuxelela indlela abafundi abahleli ngayo: Ikilasi yonke, amaqela amancinci, Cinga–Bini–Yabelana, okanye umsebenzi ngamnye. Ulwakhiwo olwahlukeneyo lusebenza ngeenjongo ezahlukeneyo—ingxoxo yeklasi yonke yakha ukuqonda okudibeneyo; amaqela amancinci avumela ukucubungula okunzulu; umsebenzi wobabini unciphisa uxinzelelo. Sebenzisa olu lwakhiwo njengoko luyilwe. Ukuba ingxoxo yeklasi yonke ayihambi kakuhle, yehla uye kubabini. Ukuba amaqela amancinci athatha ixesha elininzi, buyisela wonke umntu kunye imizuzu emi-2 yokushwankathela. Qwalasela abafundi bakho. Isikhokelo asibaboni; nguwe obabona.

##### Inkqubo Inyathelo ngenyathelo

Ezi zezona zenzo zicacileyo ozenzayo eklasini. Zifunde, uzifake engqondweni, uze uthethe ngelizwi lakho.

##### Ukujonga Phakathi kwesifundo

Ngaphakathi kwenkqubo, uya kufumana uHlolo Phakathi kweSifundo—umbuzo okhawulezayo okanye inqaku lokujonga eliyilelwe ukukunceda uqonde ukuba abafundi bayalandela na umxholo ophambili. Apha kulapho utshintsha khona kwangoko, hayi emva koko. Sebenzisa oku ukuze uthathe isigqibo: Ndiyahamba phambili? Ndiyama ndichaze kwakhona? Ndiyatshintsha indlela endisebenzisa ngayo?

##### Isishwankathelo & Uvavanyo Lokugqibela Lokuqonda

Isahluko ngasinye siphela ngesishwankathelo esifutshane esivalela ukufunda kunye novavanyo lokugqibela—ihlala iyitikit exit ekhawulezayo, ukubhala umzuzu omnye, okanye impendulo yomlomo. Oku kukwazisa wena nabafundi bakho—kukunceda wazi ukuba abafundi bathatha ntoni kwaye kufuneka uphinde uphindephi. Ayisosigxina, kodwa lulwazi olubalulekileyo.



#### Izahluko Zenkxaso

Icandelo leZimvo ekupheleni kwesahluko ngasinye linika isikhokelo esongezelelweyo.

##### Into Ebonisa Ubuchule

Esi sahluko sichaza imiqondiso yomgangatho wesahluko—kubonakala njani ukubandakanyeka kwabafundi okusemgangathweni? Kufanele abafundi bakwazi ukuchaza ntoni ekupheleni?

##### Iingcamango Ezixhaphakileyo Ezilungileyo

Abafundi baza kufika neengcinga ngemali, ixabiso, kunye neenkqubo ezingaphelelanga okanye ezingachanekanga. Eli candelo libonisa ezona zixhaphakileyo kwaye licebisa indlela yokuzilungisa ngaphandle kokuphoxa iingcinga zabafundi.

##### Ukuba Abafundi Banengxaki

Nangona ulungiselele kakuhle, abanye abafundi baya kufumana iingcamango zinzima. Eli candelo linika amacebo oncedo—iindawo zokuphinda ungene, iindlela ezizezinye, okanye imibuzo yokwakha ukuqonda ngelixa ugcina iiNjongo Zokufunda engqondweni.

##### Imisebenzi

Ezi ziindlela zokwandisa, imidlalo, okanye imidlalo yokulinganisa ezinzulu zokufunda ngokwenza. Ezinye zifakwe kwisifundo esiphambili; ezinye zingongezwa ukuba unexesha okanye abafundi bakho bafuna ukufunda ngokwenza.

##### Ukufundisa Kwi-Intanethi

Ukuba ufundisa kwi-intanethi ngexesha elifanayo, eli candelo linika isikhokelo esithile sokuba ungazilungisa njani izakhiwo (amagumbi okuphuma endaweni yamaqela amancinci) kunye neendlela (amaxwebhu wabelwana ngawo endaweni yeebhodi ezimhlophe) ngelixa ugcina ukufunda kunye nokufezekisa iiNjongo Zokufunda.

##### Ulawulo Lwexesha

Ukufundisa akuhlali kuhamba njengoko kucebiwe. Eli candelo linika iindlela ezimbini: **Ukuba Ixesha Lifutshane** licebisa ukuba ngawaphi amacandelo anokuncitshiswa okanye adityaniswe ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa ngumxholo ophambili okanye iiNjongo Zokufunda.**Ukuba Uqhuba Phambili** linika iindlela zokwandisa ukufunda okanye ukudibanisa nezahluko zangaphambili.



#### Lungisa Indlela, Hayi Umxholo

Esi Sikhokelo soTitshala sinika isakhiwo nendlela. Wena unika imeko, isigwebo, kunye nokulungelelanisa.

Uyabazi abafundi bakho. Uyaqonda uqoqosho lwakho lwasekhaya, imbali, kunye nezopolitiko. Uyazi ukuba zeziphi iimeko eziza kuchaphazela, zeziphi imibuzo eziza kuvusa, kwaye nini kufuneka uqhubeke okanye ume. Sebenzisa olo lwazi.

Umemelelekile ukuba:

* **Lungisa imizekelo** kwiimeko zangoku kunye neenyani zasekhaya kwilizwe lakho
* **Yandisa ixesha** ukuba abafundi bakho bayalidinga; nciphisa ukuba abalidingi
* **Tshintsha izakhiwo** ukuze zilungele indlela abafundi bakho abafunda ngayo kakuhle
* **Yongeza izixhobo** ezivela kuluntu lwakho—izazi zembali zasekhaya, abanini bamashishini, iingcali zoqoqosho
* **Yenza imisebenzi emitsha** ethetha nobomi kunye nemibuzo yabafundi bakho

Izahluko ziyahlengahlengiswa. Ungazifundisa ngokulandelelana okanye uzidibanise ukuze zilungele isemesta yakho. Ungahlala kwiSahluko 3 iiveki ezine ukuba apho kulapho umdla wabafundi bakho ukhoyo.

Kodwa soloko ugcina iiNjongo Zokufunda engqondweni. Yonke into oyenzayo kufuneka isebenzele ezo ziphumo.

Into esiyakhayo kunye—ngomsebenzi wakho eklasini—asingomsebenzi ogqityiweyo. Yinkqubo yokufunda ephilayo etshintsha njengoko wena nabafundi bakho nisebenza nayo.



#### Igama Lokugqibela

Awukho apha ukuhambisa esi sikhokelo. Ukhona apha ukukhokelela abafundi bakho kwiincoko ezibalulekileyo ngemali, iinkqubo, nenkululeko. Esi Sikhokelo soTitshala sikunceda kuloo msebenzi.

Zithembe. Uyabazi abafundi bakho. Uyazi ukuba bafuna ntoni.

Wamkelekile emsebenzini.
