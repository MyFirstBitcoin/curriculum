# 1 - Yintoni Imali?

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcamango Ephambili: Imali sisixhobo esinceda abantu ukugcina ixabiso, ukutshintshiselana ngokulula, nokwenza izigqibo zoqoqosho kwihlabathi elinokunqongophala kunye nokutshintshiselana.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi baya kukwazi:

* Ukucamngca ngendima yemali kuluntu.
* Ukuchonga iingxaki ezisombululwa yimali.
* Ukuchaza imali nokuchaza imisebenzi yayo.
* Ukuhlalutya iimpawu zemali elungileyo.
* Ukwahlula phakathi kweendidi ezahlukeneyo zemali.
* Ukuphonononga iingcamango zokunqongophala kunye nokutshintshiselana kwizigqibo zoqoqosho.

* Sebenzisa incoko kwimibuzo yokuqala ukuze wonke umfundi anikele ngaphambi kokuba kuqale ingxoxo.

##### 1.2 Ingcaciso yeMali, imizuzu eyi-8

Khokelela abafundi kumbono wokuba imali sisixhobo esinceda abantu ukuba batshintshe iimpahla neenkonzo ngendlela esebenzayo ngakumbi.

##### 1.3 Imisebenzi yeMali, imizuzu eyi-15

Emva koko chaza imisebenzi emithathu ephambili yemali:

* ukugcina ixabiso
* indlela yokutshintshiselana
* iyunithi yokubala

Sebenzisa imizekelo elula yobomi bemihla ngemihla kuyo yonke imisebenzi ukuze abafundi bakwazi ukudibanisa ingcamango nobomi babo bemihla ngemihla.

##### 1.4 Iimpawu zeMali, imizuzu eyi-15

Yazisa ngeempawu eziphambili zemali elungileyo:

* ukuhlala ixesha elide
* ukwahlulahlula
* ukuthwaleka
* ukwamkeleka
* ukufumaneka okulinganiselweyo
* ukufana (fungibility)

Endaweni yokuchitha ixesha elininzi kuyo yonke imizekelo, umfundisi angagxila ekuncedeni abafundi baqonde ukuba kutheni ezi mpawu zibalulekile xa uluntu lukhetha okanye lwamkela imali.

##### 1.5 Iindidi zeMali, imizuzu eyi-15

Chaza ngokufutshane umahluko phakathi kwe:

* imali yezinto (commodity money)
* imali emele into (representative money)
* imali ye-fiat
* imali yedijithali

Le nxalenye mayihlale isisiseko, yanele ukuba abafundi baqonde ukuba ayingomali yonke ifana kwaye imali iye yatshintsha ngeendlela ezahlukeneyo.

##### 1.6 I-Psychology yeMali, imizuzu eyi-22

Yazisa ngombono wokuba izixhobo zilinganiselwe kwaye abantu bahlala benza izigqibo malunga nendlela yokuzisebenzisa. Sebenzisa umzekelo olula, njengokuthi:

* ukuthatha umvuzo omncinane ngoku okanye umvuzo omkhulu kamva
* ukusebenzisa imali ngoku okanye ukuyigcina kwixesha elizayo

Ukuba kunokwenzeka, fakela umdlalo we-sweet okanye umvuzo olibazisekileyo ngendlela emfutshane. Le nxalenye mayincede abafundi badibanise imali nokuziphatha, izikhuthazo, kunye nokwenza izigqibo.

###### Isishwankathelo kunye nokuJonga uQonde

Vala ngemibuzo embalwa yokuphinda uphonononge, enjengale:

* Yeyiphi ingxaki esonjululwa yimali?
* Zeziphi imisebenzi emithathu yemali?
* Zeziphi iimpawu ezimbini ezibalulekileyo zemali elungileyo?
* Yeyiphi umzekelo wokwenza ukhetho phakathi kwezinto ezimbini?

Unokumema abafundi ukuba babuyele kwenye yemibuzo yabo yokuqala kwaye bajonge ukuba ingaba ingcinga yabo itshintshe na ngexesha lesifundo.

#### Amanqaku oMfundi

##### Into eBonakala iLungile

* Kubalulekile ukubuza imibuzo yokuqala nokuphulaphula, uvumele abafundi bazame izimvo kunye, bafumane iimpendulo, kwaye benze unxibelelwano nobomi bemihla ngemihla.
* Abafundisi kufuneka babonise umdla wokwenene, bahlale benomonde xa kungathethwa, bavume xa bengazi, kwaye bahlale bedibanisa iingxaki nezisombululo.
* Abafundi baziva bethembekile ukuba bangazilungiselela imali ngokwabo, banomdla wokuba kutheni iinkqubo zisebenza, kwaye baqonde ukuba iimpawu zemali zibalulekile ngezizathu ezibonakalayo.
* Iziphumo zoFundo ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi banako ukuchaza ukuba kutheni imali ibalulekile, bahlule imisebenzi emithathu ngemizekelo yokwenyani, bahlalutye ukuba kutheni iimpahla zisebenza okanye zingasebenzi njengemali, kwaye babonise umdla wokwenene kwiinkqubo.

##### Ulawulo lwexesha

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili:

* Kutheni sifuna imali
* Imisebenzi yemali
* Ukufumaneka okulinganiselweyo kunye nokwenza ukhetho phakathi kwezinto ezimbini

Ukuba ixesha likhona, chitha ixesha kwi:

* Uphando olwandisiweyo lweempawu (ukuhlala ixesha elide, ukwahlulahlula, njl.)
* Iimeko ezineenkcukacha zeendidi zemali
* Ukungena nzulu kumxholo wokukhetha ixesha kunye nokulibazisa ukwaneliseka

##### Ukuba Abafundi Banengxaki

* Kutheni imali ibalulekile → Imeko yokutshintshiselana ngezinto; mabayifumane ngokwabo.
* Imisebenzi emithathu → Imizekelo yobomi bokwenene ebomini babo.
* Kutheni iimpawu zibalulekile → Bonisa ukuba kutheni izinto zingasebenzi xa zilahlekile.
