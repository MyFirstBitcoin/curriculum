##### 10.1 Yintoni i-Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)?, imizuzu engama-27

**CBDCs, Yintoni Ziyiyo Kwaye Ngubani OziLawulayo**

Chaza ngokucacileyo ukuba i-CBDCs ziinguqulelo zedijithali zemali ye-fiat ekhutshwa kwaye ilawulwa ziibhanki eziphakathi. Zilandele umgaqo ofanayo nemali ye-fiat, ulawulo oluphakathi, unikezelo olunokwandiswa, kunye nokuxhomekeka kugunyaziwe wombuso, kodwa ngendlela edijithali ngokupheleleyo.

Gqamisa amanqaku aphambili okufundisa kweli candelo:

* I-CBDCs azilolutsha lwemali yedijithali engenamkhethe
* zinika urhulumente ukubonakala ngokuthe ngqo kwiintengiselwano
* zinokwandisa ulawulo lombuso ngendlela imali esetyenziswa ngayo
* zinokwenza kube lula ukuvala, ukuvimba, okanye ukunciphisa iintengiselwano
* zigxininisa ukongamela kunye nokunyanzeliswa ngokuthe ngqo ngakumbi kunemali yephepha

Le yindawo elungileyo yokugxininisa ukuba isahluko sibonisa i-CBDCs njengolwandiso olukhulu lolawulo lwemali, hayi njengendlela eya ekuzimeleni kwezezimali. Imizekelo ekubhalweni, enjengeentlawulo ezivaliweyo okanye ukuthengwa okuthintelweyo, inceda ukwenza iziphumo zibe sezantsi ngakumbi kubafundi.

Ukuba kuyanceda, unokukhankanya ukuba isahluko sikhankanya uphando olubanzi lwehlabathi malunga ne-CBDCs, nto leyo egxininisa ukuba le yindlela yomgaqo-nkqubo yangoku, hayi umxholo wengcinga nje.

**Bitcoin vs CBDCs, Iikamva Ezimbini Ezahlukileyo Ngokugqithisileyo**

Ngoku yenza umahluko ube ngowacacileyo.

Sebenzisa umfanekiso othelekisayo, othelekisa i-CBDC kunye ne-Bitcoin kwiindidi ezifana nokubonelelwa, ulawulo, ukucaca, imvume, umngcipheko wokuthathwa kweeakhawunti, ubumfihlo, kunye nokumelana nokucinezelwa. Lo mfanekiso uqinile kakhulu kuba unceda abafundi babone ngokukhawuleza ukuba nangona zombini ziyi-digital, zimele iinkqubo zemali ezahlukeneyo kakhulu.

Ulwakhiwo olucacileyo lokufundisa apha lolu:

I-CBDC

* ubonelelo olungenamda okanye olunokwandiswa
* ulawulo olugxile embindini
* ufikelelo olufuna imvume
* umngcipheko omkhulu wokubekwa phantsi kolawulo
* kulula ukucinezelwa kunye nemida kwiakhawunti
* umngcipheko ophezulu wokuthathwa kweeakhawunti

Bitcoin

* ubonelelo olusisigxina lwe-21 million
* ukunyanzeliswa kwemithetho okungagxilwanga embindini
* ufikelelo oluvulekileyo nolungafuni mvume
* ukumelana nokucinezelwa okomeleleyo
* umngcipheko omncinci wokugcina imali ngokwakho
* ulwazi olungakumbi ngobumfihlo ngenxa yoyilo olungaziwayo ngokupheleleyo

Yenza kucace ukuba umahluko ophambili asiyotekhnoloji kuphela. Kukuba ngubani olawula imithetho, ngubani onokuyitshintsha, kunye nokuba umsebenzisi unenkululeko engakanani ngaphakathi kwenkqubo.

##### 10.2 Ifilosofi ye-Bitcoin, imizuzu eli-15

Guqulela kwisiseko sefilosofi sale sahluko.

Cacisa ukuba ifilosofi ye-Bitcoin isekwe ku:

* ukomelezwa kwamandla
* inkululeko
* uzimele kwezezimali
* ukucinga okunzulu
* ukuzimela ekwenzeni izigqibo
* ukuthatha inxaxheba kwinkqubo apho imithetho ingalawulwa ligunya elinye eliphakathi

Isahluko sikwadibanisa oku nokusebenza kwenode, sicacisa ukuba abantu abaqhelekileyo banokunceda ukukhusela imithetho yenethiwekhi yemali ngokuthatha inxaxheba ngqo kuyo. Elo ngqwalaselo ibalulekile: i-Bitcoin asisixhobo nje abantu abasisebenzisayo, yinkqubo abantu abanokuyikhusela kwaye bayiqinisekise.

Ungayenza le thelekiso ngolu hlobo:

* kwiinkqubo zemali eziqhelekileyo, amandla emali agxile embindini
* ku-Bitcoin, ukunyanzeliswa kwemithetho kusasazwa phakathi kwabathathi-nxaxheba
* kwiinkqubo zemali eziqhelekileyo, amaziko anokutshintsha imithetho
* ku-Bitcoin, akukho mntu mnye onokutshintsha inkqubo yemali yedwa

Le ndima kufuneka incede abafundi babone ukuba i-Bitcoin asiyiyo nje i-app entsha yokuhlawula. Ibonisa umbono ohlukileyo wamandla, intsebenziswano, kunye nenkululeko yomntu.

##### 10.3 Iingenelo ze-Bitcoin, imizuzu eli-18

Ngoku hamba neengenelo eziphambili ezidweliswe kwesi sahluko xa kusetyenziswa i-Bitcoin ngokubanzi.

Akufuneki uzibonise njengezinto eziqinisekisiweyo. Zibonise njengeziphumo ezibalulekileyo ezichazwe kwesi sahluko ukuba i-Bitcoin inokuzenza ukuba yamkelwe ngokubanzi.

Ulwakhiwo oluncedisayo lolu:

Ikamva lokuzimela

* abantu ngabanye banolawulo olukhulu kwimali nakwiimpahla zedijithali
* abangaphakathi abambalwa baphakathi kwabantu kunye nempahla yabo
* abantu bafumana uxanduva oluthe ngqo kunye nokuzimela ngakumbi

Indawo yokugcina ixabiso ethembekileyo

* ukungabikho kwe-Bitcoin kunceda ukonga ixesha elide
* ubonelelo olusisigxina lutshintsha indlela abantu abacinga ngayo ngokusebenzisa, ukonga, kunye nokucwangcisa

Utshintsho kwimithetho yemali

* urhulumente wayenokungabi namandla amaninzi okwandisa ubonelelo lwemali nanini na efuna
* uluntu lunokuhamba luselele kumandla okuthenga aqinileyo kunye nokucwangcisa ixesha elide

Ukucaca okuphuculweyo kunye nokulandeleka

* incwadi kawonke-wonke yenza ukuhamba kwealth kubonakale kwaye kube lula ukukhangela
* oku kunokunyusa uxanduva xa kuthelekiswa neenkqubo zemali ezingacacanga

Uguquko kwiintlawulo ezithunyelwa kwamanye amazwe

* I-Lightning ivumela ukuthunyelwa kwemali ngokukhawuleza nangexabiso eliphantsi ngaphaya kwemida yamazwe
* oku kubaluleke kakhulu kwiintsapho ezithumela imali encinci kwamanye amazwe

Amandla amaninzi

* Ukumba iBitcoin kungasebenzisa amandla ashiyekileyo okanye angasetyenziswa
* isahluko sibonisa ukumba njengekhuthazo elinokubakho lokusebenzisa amandla ngcono kunye nophuhliso

Amanqaku aphambili ngowona mgangatho ucacileyo kweli candelo, kwaye akunika indlela ecocekileyo yokushwankathela ingxoxo enkulu yezentlalo noqoqosho malunga neBitcoin.

##### 10.4 Ikamva Elinamandla, imizuzu engama-22

**Ikamva Elinamandla**

Sebenzisa eli candelo ukudibanisa umyalezo.

Ingxoxo yesahluko kukuba imali imisela indlela uluntu olusebenza ngayo kuba inceda ukulungelelanisa oko abantu bakuxabisayo, abakuphehlayo, nabakubeka phambili. Xa imali ilawulwa embindini, ezo mpawu ziyaphazamiseka. Xa imali inobuninzi obusisigxina kunye nemithetho engenakutshintshwa liqela elinye, uluntu lufumana isiseko esahlukileyo sokucwangcisa, ukonga, kunye nokutshintshiselana.

Inqaku elibalulekileyo lokufundisa apha leli:

I-Bitcoin itshintsha hayi kuphela indlela imali ehamba ngayo, kodwa ikwatshintsha nabantu abafumana ulawulo kwimigaqo yemali kwasekuqaleni.

Lixesha elifanelekileyo lokukhumbuza abafundi ukuba ikhosi ibisoloko isakhela esi sigqibo ukusukela ekuqaleni:

* kutheni sifuna imali
* yintoni eyenza imali ibe mhle okanye imbi
* indlela esebenza ngayo imali ye-fiat
* kutheni iBitcoin yadalwa
* indlela yokuyisebenzisa
* indlela esebenza ngayo ngokwezobugcisa
* kwaye ngoku, luhlobo luni lwekamva enokulunceda ukudala

##### Kubonakala Njani Okulungileyo

* Kubalulekile ukwenza ingxoxo ibe yeyobuqu ngokubuza "Luhlobo luni lwemali ofuna ukuba nalo, kwaye kutheni?", uthelekise ikamva elahlukileyo ngokufanelekileyo ubonise ukuba omabini anezinto ezilungileyo nezimbi, xhasa ingxoxo kwiimeko zokwenyani ezifana nokuthumela imali ekhaya kunye nokufikelela kwabangenayo imali ebhankini, kwaye ubuyele kwimibuzo evulwe kwiSahluko 1 ukuze ubonise indlela ingcinga etshintshe ngayo.
* Ootitshala kufuneka babe ngabakhokeli abacingayo abakwazi ukubuza imibuzo hayi nje ukuphendula, babe nehlonipho xa beqikelela ikamva, bahloniphe ukuba abantu abaqhelekileyo banokungavumelani, badibanise amabali okwenyani avela kwihlabathi liphela, kwaye banike amandla abafundi ukuba baqonde ukuba banendima ekwakheni ikamva.
* Abafundi baziva benoxanduva ngekamva hayi nje ukwamkela into ekhoyo, baqonde umahluko beqonda ukuba omabini la maqonga anezinto ezilungileyo nezimbi kwaye akukho mpendulo igqibeleleyo, babone unxibelelwano olucacileyo phakathi koko bakufundileyo kunye nentshukumo yokwenyani, baphuhlise ithemba elisekelwe kwinyani, kwaye baqonde amandla abo ekwakheni iinkqubo zemali.
* Iziphumo zokufunda kufuneka zifezekiswe ukuba abafundi bayakwazi ukuchaza iindlela ezahlukeneyo zekamva lemali kubandakanya ulawulo lwe-CBDC, ukwamkelwa kwe-My First Bitcoin, kunye neendlela ezidibeneyo, bahlalutye i-CBDC xa ithelekiswa ne-My First Bitcoin ngokusekelwe kwizinto ezifana nolawulo, ubumfihlo, kunye nokuchasana nokucinywa, baqonde iimeko zokwenyani apho i-My First Bitcoin inceda ekuthumeleni imali ekhaya nasekuphepheni ukuhla kwemali, baqonde i-My First Bitcoin njengenye yeendlela ezininzi hayi isisombululo kuyo yonke into, bathathe inxaxheba ngokucinga kwimibuzo evulekileyo malunga nohlobo lweenkqubo zemali esifuna ukuba nazo, kwaye benze unxibelelwano lobuqu malunga nendlela iinkqubo ezahlukeneyo eziza kuchaphazela ngayo ubomi babo.
