# 5 - Yintoni iBitcoin?

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcamango Ephambili: I-Bitcoin yadalwa njengothungelwano lwemali olungaphantsi kolawulo lwesebe elinye, eyenzelwe ukusombulula iingxaki zokuthembana, ulawulo, kunye nokunqongophala kwedijithali ngaphandle kokuxhomekeka kugunyaziwe ophakathi.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi kufuneka bakwazi:

* Chaza ukuba ngubani uSatoshi Nakamoto kumxholo wokudalwa kweBitcoin, kwaye kutheni iBitcoin yaphunyezwa njengempendulo kwiimpazamo zemali eziphakathi kunye nengxaki yezezimali ka-2008.
* Chaza iBitcoin njengothungelwano lwemali olungaphantsi kolawulo lwesebe elinye, olusekelwe kwimigaqo ekwabelwana ngayo endaweni yogunyaziwe ophakathi.
* Chaza izisiseko zeNakamoto Consensus, kubandakanya nesizathu sokuba kutheni imvumelwano ibalulekile kwinkqubo engaphantsi kolawulo lwesebe elinye.
* Chonga abathathi-nxaxheba abaphambili kwinethiwekhi yeBitcoin, kubandakanya abavukuzi, iinodi, abasebenzisi, abaphuhlisi, kunye neeprojekthi, kwaye uchaze indima yomntu ngamnye.
* Vavanya iBitcoin njengemali yedijithali ethembekileyo ngokuthelekisa iimpawu zayo zemali nemali ye-fiat kunye negolide.
* Chaza ukuba kutheni iBitcoin inika abasebenzisi ulawulo olungakumbi ngemali yabo, kwaye kutheni oko kufuna uxanduva lomntu siqu ngakumbi

##### 5.0 Satoshi Nakamoto kunye noDalo lweBitcoin, imizuzu engama-20

**Ukuvula kunye noQhagamshelo oluvela kwiSifundo 4**

Qala ngokudibanisa esi sahluko nesangaphambili:

* Ukuba iinkqubo zemali yedijithali zangaphambili zasile, yintoni eyenza iBitcoin yahluke?
* Kutheni abantu bengafuna inkqubo yemali engenagunyaziwe ophakathi?
* Zaziziphi iingxaki iBitcoin ezazama ukuzisombulula?

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko sichaza ukuba yintoni iBitcoin, kutheni yadalwa, isebenza njani kwinqanaba elisisiseko, kwaye kutheni abantu abaninzi beyibona njengemali ethembekileyo.

**Satoshi Nakamoto kunye noDalo lweBitcoin**

Cacisa ukuba iBitcoin ayizange ivele nje. Yayingumphumo weminyaka emininzi yophando kwi-cryptography, isayensi yekhompyutha, imali yedijithali, kunye neenkqubo peer-to-peer. Umgca wexesha unceda ukubonisa ukuba iBitcoin yakhiwe kumsebenzi wangaphambili ofana ne-public key cryptography, DigiCash, HashCash, B-money, Bit Gold, kunye nereusable proof of work.

Emva koko khokela abafundi kumaxesha abalulekileyo:

* Ngo-Okthobha 2008, uSatoshi Nakamoto wapapasha i-whitepaper yeBitcoin
* NgoJanuwari 3, 2009, kwaqulunqwa i-genesis block
* I-Bitcoin yaqalwa njengenkqubo yemali ye-peer-to-peer engaphantsi kolawulo lwesebe elinye
* USatoshi kamva wasuka, eshiya iprojekthi ezandleni zabanye

Cacisa ukuba iBitcoin yadalwa njengempendulo kukonakaliswa, ubuthathaka, kunye nolawulo oluphakathi kwinkqubo yemali ye-fiat, ngakumbi emva kwengxaki yezezimali ka-2008.

##### 5.1 Isebenza Njani iBitcoin?, imizuzu engama-40

**Indlela iBitcoin esebenza ngayo kunye neNakamoto Consensus**

Chaza iBitcoin ngendlela elula:

I-Bitcoin yimithetho esetyenziswa ngokufanayo ngabo bonke abathathi-nxaxheba kwinethiwekhi.

Sebenzisa umzekelo weMonopoly okhankanywe kwesi sahluko ukuba kuyanceda. Njengokuba umdlalo webhodi usebenza kuphela xa wonke umntu evumelana ngemithetho efanayo, iBitcoin isebenza kuba abathathi-nxaxheba bavumelana ngemithetho yeprotocol efanayo. Ukuba umntu uzama ukophula imithetho, umzekelo ngokwenza i-bitcoin engaphezulu, inethiwekhi iyala eso senzo.

Amanqaku aphambili okufanele ugxininise kuwo:

* I-Bitcoin ayinagosa eliphambili
* imithetho ibhalwe kwikhodi
* wonke umntu osebenzisa isoftware uqinisekisa ukuba imithetho iyalandelwa
* akukho mntu unokutshintsha inani le-bitcoin okanye akhohlise inkqubo ngokulula
* ukuvumelana (consensus) yinto evumela abantu abangazaziyo, abangathembaniyo, ukuba basebenzisane

Ukuba kuyanceda, nxuluma ngokufutshane lo msebenzi, ochaza uvumelwano njengomceli-mngeni wokufikelela kwisivumelwano kwinethiwekhi ye-peer-to-peer engenamphathi.

**Abathathi-nxaxheba kwiNethiwekhi yeBitcoin**

Ngoku chaza indima eziphambili kwinkqubo yeBitcoin. Injongo apha ayikokufunda ubuchwephesha nzulu, kodwa kukuqonda indlela i-decentralization esebenza ngayo ebomini bokwenyani.

Ulwakhiwo olucacileyo lolu:

* Abavukuzi (miners) bakhusela inethiwekhi ngokusebenzisa ubungqina bomsebenzi (proof of work) kwaye bayakhuphisana ukongeza iibhloko ezintsha.
* Iinodi (nodes) ziqinisekisa iintengiselwano kwaye ziqinisekisa ukuba imithetho iyalandelwa ngokusebenzisa isoftware yeBitcoin kwaye zigcina ikopi yencwadi-mali.
* Abasebenzisi bathumela, bamkela, kwaye bagcine i-bitcoin ngaphandle kokufuna abalamli.
* Abaphuhlisi (developers) baphakamisa uphuculo, banegalelo kwikhodi, kwaye banceda ukugcina i-protocol.
* Iiprojekthi zakha izixhobo, iinkonzo, kunye nemigudu yemfundo exhasa ukwamkelwa kunye nokusetyenziswa ebomini bokwenyani.

Umzekeliso 'we-symphony' okhankanywe kwesi sahluko uyanceda apha. Ubonisa ukuba iBitcoin isebenza ngenxa yokuba abathathi-nxaxheba abaninzi basebenza ngokuzimeleyo kodwa ngaphakathi kwenkqubo yemithetho efanayo, ngaphandle kokuba kukho igunya elilawulayo. Umzobo othelekisa ulwakhiwo olugxile embindini, olwahluliweyo, nolusasazekileyo unokunceda abafundi baqonde umahluko.

##### 5.2 IBitcoin njengoMali yoMdla kwiDijithali, imizuzu engama-30

**I-Bitcoin njengoMali yoMdla kwiDijithali**

Guquka uye kumbuzo othi: Kutheni abantu abaninzi becinga ukuba iBitcoin yimali enexabiso elizinzileyo?

Okokuqala, cacisa ukuba iBitcoin yimali, hayi nje into yokutyala imali, kwaye yenzelwe ukuba ibe yinkqubo yemali yedijithali, engenamda, kwaye inqabileyo. Isahluko sikwabonisa iBitcoin njengento evulekileyo, yehlabathi lonke, kwaye ifikeleleka kubantu abanefowuni kunye nonxibelelwano lwe-intanethi.

Emva koko khokela abafundi ekuthelekiseni iimpawu zemali:

* Ukuhlala ixesha elide: IBitcoin yedijithali kwaye ayonakali ngokwasemzimbeni
* Ukuhlulahlula: i-bitcoin ingahlulwa ibe ziisatoshi
* Ukuthwaleka: inokudluliselwa kwihlabathi lonke ngokukhawuleza nangempumelelo
* Ukwamkelwa: ukwamkelwa kwayo kuyakhula, nangona kusezantsi xa kuthelekiswa nemali yesiqhelo
* Ukunqaba: inani le-bitcoin lilinganiselwe kwi-21 yezigidi
* Ukufana: iiyunithi ze-bitcoin ziyatshintshiselana ngokulinganayo

Umzobo othelekisa iBitcoin, igolide, kunye nedola yaseMelika uluncedo kakhulu apha. Unceda abafundi babone ukuba kutheni iBitcoin idibanisa ezinye iingenelo zegolide kunye neenzuzo zedijithali ezingekho kwigolide okanye kwimali yesiqhelo.

Unokuchaza nenkqubela yemali enamanqanaba amathathu echazwe kwesi sahluko:

* indawo yokugcina ixabiso
* indlela yokutshintshiselana
* iyunithi yokubala

Cacisa ukuba isahluko sibonisa iBitcoin njengesele isisixhobo sokugcina ixabiso, ikhula njengenendlela yokutshintshiselana, kwaye isaqhubeka ikhula ukuze isetyenziswe ngokubanzi njengeyunithi yokubala.

**Uxanduva Lomntu kunye noLawulo lwezeMali**

Vala umxholo ophambili ngomnye wemfundiso ezibalulekileyo ezisebenzayo kwesi sahluko:

I-Bitcoin inika abasebenzisi ulawulo olungakumbi, kodwa olo lawulo luza noxanduva.

Chaza umahluko ngokucacileyo:

Kwinkqubo yemali yesiqhelo, abantu baxhomekeke kwiibhanki, oorhulumente, kunye nabanikezeli bentlawulo ukuphatha ii-akhawunti, ukulungisa iimpazamo, kunye nokumisela imithetho.

KwiBitcoin, abasebenzisi bagcina izitshixo zabo kwaye bathatha uxanduva ngqo lokufikelela kunye nokhuseleko. Ukuba umntu ulahlekelwa kukufikelela kwi-wallet yakhe, akukho mnxeba wenkxaso yabathengi onokubuyisela ezo mali.

Lixesha elifanelekileyo lokuqaqambisa ukuba iBitcoin inika amandla, kodwa ayisebenzi ngokuzenzekelayo. Ifuna abantu bafunde, baqinisekise, kwaye bathathe uxanduva ngemali yabo.
