# 4 - Indlela Iingxaki Ezikhokelela Ngayo Kwiisisombululo

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcinga Ephambili: Ukungaphumeleli kunye noxinzelelo kwinkqubo yemali ye-fiat kwabangela abantu ukuba bafune ezinye iindlela, nto leyo ekhokelele kwiingcinga ezintsha malunga nokuzimelela, ubumfihlo, kunye nemali yedijithali.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi kufuneka bakwazi uku:

* Chaza indlela ukunyuka kwemali (inflation) okunciphisa ngayo amandla okuthenga ngokuhamba kwexesha.
* Chaza indlela inkqubo ye-fiat echaphazela ngayo abantu abaqhelekileyo ngokunyuka kwamaxabiso, imivuzo enganyuki, amatyala, kunye noxinzelelo lokucinga ixesha elifutshane.
* Hlaziya indlela amatyala kunye nokungalingani kobutyebi okukhula ngayo ngaphakathi kwenkqubo esekwe kwi-fiat.
* Chaza ukuba babangobani amaCypherpunks kwaye kutheni basebenza ekudaleni ezinye iindlela ezizimeleyo.
* Thelekisa iinkqubo eziphakathi (centralized) nezozimeleyo (decentralized) usebenzisa imizekelo ecacileyo yobomi bokwenyani.
* Chaza iinzame zokuqala zemali yedijithali kunye nokuba kutheni ezo nkqubo zingazange zisombulule ngokupheleleyo ingxaki.

##### 4.0 Intshayelelo Kwengxaki, imizuzu eli-10

Qala ngokudibanisa esi sahluko neSahluko 3:

* Kwenzeka ntoni kubantu xa imali ihlala ilahlekelwa lixabiso?
* Ukuba inkqubo ihlala idala amatyala kwaye inyusa amaxabiso, ngubani ochaphazeleka kakhulu?
* Loluphi uhlobo lwezisombululo abantu abanokuzifuna xa bebona ukuba inkqubo ayisebenzi?

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko sijolise kwiziphumo kuqala, emva koko iimpembelelo. Abafundi kufuneka babone ukuba iBitcoin ayizange ivele nje ngaphandle kwesizathu. Yavela emva kokuba abantu abaninzi beqaphele iingxaki ezinzulu kwinkqubo ye-fiat.

##### 4.1 Ukuhla kwamandla okuthenga, imizuzu engama-30

**Ukuhla kwamandla okuthenga**

Cacisa kakuhle ukuba ukunyuka kwemali kuthetha ukwanda kwesixa semali esikhoyo, kwaye xa imali eninzi ilandela iimpahla ezifanayo, amaxabiso athanda ukunyuka. Sebenzisa umzekelo olula osebhukwini wokuba abahlobo abathathu bayathengiselana ngebhotile enye yamanzi emva kokuba ngamnye efumene i-euro eyongezelelweyo. Oku kunceda abafundi babone ukuba imali eninzi esetyenziswayo ayithethi ukuba uluntu lutyebile ngokuzenzekelayo.

Yenza ingongoma ephambili icace:

* imali eninzi ayithethi iimpahla ezininzi
* xa inani lempahla lihlala lingatshintshi, imali eyongezelelweyo itshintsha amaxabiso
* oku kunciphisa into enokuthengwa yimalini nganye

Ungaqinisa umahluko phakathi kokunyuka kwemali (monetary inflation) kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso (price inflation), kuba isahluko sikhankanya ngokucacileyo ukuba azifani.

**Iziphumo Zobomi Bokwenyani Kubantu Ngamnye**

Qhubeka nomzekelo kaSipho ukubonisa indlela ukunyuka kwemali kuchaphazela ngayo ubomi bemihla ngemihla. Cacisa ukuba nokuba umvuzo womntu ubonakala ufana okanye unyuke kancinci, imali yakhe inokuba ithenga izinto ezimbalwa kunangaphambili. Incwadi yokubhalisa kunye nokuthelekiswa kweendleko kwesi sahluko kuyanceda apha kuba kwenza ilahleko yamandla okuthenga ibonakale icacile. Isahluko sibonisa ukuba uSipho ufuna imali eninzi kunyaka wesibini ukuze agcine umgangatho wobomi ofanayo, kwaye itshathi ibonisa ukuhla kwexesha elide kwamandla okuthenga kwe-dollar yaseMelika.

Gxininisa iziphumo ezisebenzayo:

* irenti, ukutya, kunye nezinto ezifunekayo ziba nexabiso eliphezulu
* imivuzo ayihlali inyuka ngesantya esifanayo
* abantu kufuneka basebenze ngakumbi ukuze bagcine umgangatho wobomi ofanayo
* ukugcina imali kuba nzima ngakumbi
* ukucwangcisa ikamva kuba nzima ngakumbi

Le yindawo elungileyo yokudibanisa kwakhona nomxholo wokukhetha ixesha. Xa abantu bevakalelwa kukuba imali yabo ilahlekelwa lixabiso ngokukhawuleza, abaninzi bagxila ngakumbi ekusindeni kwangoko kunokucwangcisa ixesha elide.

##### 4.2 Umthwalo weTyala leHlabathi kunye nokungalingani kweNtlalo, imizuzu eli-15

Ngoku jonga umfanekiso omkhulu ukusuka kumntu ngamnye ukuya kuluntu.

Cacisa ukuba isahluko sibonisa imali ye-fiat hayi kuphela njengengxaki yezoqoqosho, kodwa njengenkqubo echaphazela amandla, imivuzo, kunye nokuziphatha kuluntu lonke. Gxininisa ezi ziphumo zilandelayo:

* urhulumente uthatha amatyala amakhulu kakhulu
* abantu abaqhelekileyo baxhomekeke ngakumbi kwityala ukuze baphile
* ubutyebi buqokelelwa kwizandla zabo bakufuphi nokuveliswa kwemali
* ukunyuka kwinqanaba lobomi kuba nzima ngakumbi
* ukungathembani, ukungazinzi, kunye nokungazoli kwezentlalo kuyanda

Isahluko sikwathi inkqubo ye-fiat _ikhuthaza_ ukucinga kwexesha elifutshane, uxhomekeko, kunye nokuthenga okungapheliyo. Ungakuchaza oku kubafundi njengempembelelo yenkqubo: xa isixhobo sokulinganisa singasathembeki, abantu batshintsha indlela abaziphatha ngayo.

Ukuba kuyanceda, sebenzisa umbuzo omnye wengxoxo yeklasi ovela kwincwadi:

* Zeziphi iziphumo zenkqubo ye-fiat ozibonayo kwilizwe lakho okanye kuluntu lwakho?

Oko kunokwenza isifundo sibe semngciphekweni ngakumbi kwaye sixhaswe ziimeko zobomi bokwenyani.

##### 4.3 Ukufuna imali engaphantsi kolawulo, imizuzu engama-35

**AmaCypherpunks kunye nokufuna isisombululo**

Guqula isifundo uye kwindlela yokuphendula kunye nokuqamba izinto ezintsha.

Cacisa ukuba amaCypherpunks ayengamagorha, abaprograma, iingcali zokhuseleko lwedatha, kunye nabakhuseli babucala ababeqonda imingcipheko yolawulo oluphakathi kunye namandla ekhompyutha, i-intanethi, kunye nokufihla ulwazi. Injongo yabo yayingekokugxeka inkqubo kuphela, kodwa yayikukwakha izixhobo ezinokukhusela ubumfihlo, ukuzimela, kunye nenkululeko kwixesha ledijithali.

Amanqaku aphambili okufundisa:

* babekholelwa ukuba ukufihla ulwazi kungakhusela inkululeko yomntu
* babefuna unxibelelwano kunye notshintshiselwano lwemali lwenziwe ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa ngulawulo oluphakathi
* babekhathazekile ngokuhlolwa kunye nento ebizwa kwisahluko ngokuba 'likamva lika-Orwell'
* banegalelo ekwakheni isiseko sengcinga kunye nobuchwephesha bemali yedijithali engaphantsi kolawulo

Awudingi ukungena nzulu kuwo onke amagama, kodwa abafundi kufuneka bashiye besiqonda isizathu sokuba le ntshukumo ibalulekile.

**Iinkqubo eziphakathi kolawulo vs. Iinkqubo ezingaphantsi kolawulo**

Sebenzisa le ndawo ukwenza umahluko ube ngowacacileyo.

Cacisa ukuba iinkqubo eziphakathi kolawulo zibeka izigqibo kunye nolawulo ezandleni zeliqela elincinci okanye igunya elinye, ngelixa iinkqubo ezingaphantsi kolawulo zisasaza amandla phakathi kwabathathi-nxaxheba abaninzi. Isahluko sinika imizekelo kunye neengozi/izibonelelo zazo zombini.

Ulwakhiwo olulula lokufundisa:

Iinkqubo eziphakathi kolawulo

* indawo enye yolawulo
* kulula ukuvala okanye ukunciphisa inkululeko
* indawo enye yokusilela
* abalamli abaninzi
* inkululeko encinci kubasebenzisi

Iinkqubo ezingaphantsi kolawulo

* akukho ndawo enye yokusilela
* ukomelela ngakumbi
* inkululeko enkulu yomsebenzisi
* ukubonakala okukhulu
* kunzima ukuvala inkululeko

Umzekelo wokugatywa kweakhawunti yasebhankini eKhanada kwisahluko uluncedo kwiinkqubo eziphakathi kolawulo, kwaye inethiwekhi yeTor ngumzekelo oluncedo kwiinkqubo ezingaphantsi kolawulo. Injongo apha kukuba abafundi baqonde ukuba ukungaphantsi kolawulo kutshintsha ukuba ngubani onamandla kunye nokuba inkqubo ichasene kangakanani nolawulo.

**Iinzame zokuqala zemali yedijithali**

Cacisa ngokufutshane ukuba phambi kweBitcoin, kwenziwa iinzame ezininzi ezibalulekileyo zokudala imali yedijithali. Itheyibhile kwisahluko ibandakanya imizekelo efana ne-E-Cash, DigiCash, B-Money, HashCash, Bit Gold, kunye ne-e-Gold.

Endaweni yokuchitha ixesha elininzi kweyinye nganye, gxila kumfanekiso omkhulu:

* ezinye iinkqubo zaxhomekeka kugunya oluphakathi
* ezinye zashiyeka kwinqanaba lengcinga kuphela
* abanye basombulule ingxaki enye kodwa abanye abazange bakwazi ukusombulula ezinye
* abaninzi baphoxekile kuba bengenakudibanisa ukhuseleko, ukungabikho kwendawo enye yolawulo, kunye nokuphunyezwa okusebenzayo

Oku kwakha ibhulorho eqinileyo ukuya kwicandelo elilandelayo. Abafundi kufuneka baqonde ukuba iBitcoin yakhiwe kwiminyaka emininzi yemizamo yangaphambili, hayi ukuba ivele nje ngaphandle kwembali.

#### Amanqaku oTitshala

Esi sahluko sigubungela izinto ezininzi, ngoko gcina umgca ophambili webali ucocekile:  
Imali ye-fiat izisa iingxaki ezinzulu, ezo ngxaki zichaphazela uluntu kakhulu, kwaye ezo meko zakhokelela abantu ekukhangeleni iindlela ezingalawulwa embindini.

Kuphephe ukwenza isahluko sibe kukukhalaza kuphela ngemali ye-fiat. Injongo yemfundo kukubonisa unobangela kunye nempembelelo.

Amacandelo aqinileyo okufanele aphambili, ukuba ixesha lifutshane, ngala:

* amandla okuthenga
* ityala kunye nokungalingani
* Abantu beCypherpunk
* iinkqubo ezilawulwa embindini vs ezingalawulwa embindini

Umzobo kunye netafile yeemali zedijithali zangaphambili zixhasa kakuhle lo mhlathi ngokubonakalayo.

##### Umzekelo oLungileyo

* Kubalulekile ukuxhasa yonke into ngamava abantwana abawaziyo, usebenzisa idatha yokwenyani yokunyuka kwamaxabiso, umsantsa wemivuzo, kunye neendlela zetyala, uchaze abantu beCypherpunk njengabasombululi bengxaki, kwaye uvumele abafundi bafumane iingxaki kuqala phambi kokuba banikwe izisombululo.
* Ootitshala kufuneka bathethe inyaniso malunga nemingeni ngaphandle kokuyithoba, kwaye bahlale benethemba ngezindlela kunye nezisombululo ezakhiwayo.
* Abafundi bayavuma ukuba inkqubo yemali inengxaki ezichaphazela iintsapho zabo, baqonde ukuba abantu abahlakaniphile basebenze ukusombulula ezi ngxaki amashumi eminyaka, kwaye bazive ukuba isizukulwana sabo siya kuzidla kwaye siyilungise le mikhetho.
* Iziphumo zoFundo kufuneka zifezekiswe ukuba abafundi banokuchaza iingxaki ezintathu eziphambili zemali ye-fiat ngemizekelo yokwenyani, baqonde indlela amandla okuthenga ancipha ngayo ngokuhamba kwexesha, babone abantu beCypherpunk njengabasombululi bengxaki hayi abavukeli, bathelekise nobomi babo, kwaye babone iBitcoin njengesindululo sesisombululo kwiingxaki ezithile hayi indlela yokufumana ubutyebi ngokukhawuleza.

##### Ulawulo lwexesha

Ukuba ixesha lifutshane, phambili:

* Amandla okuthenga kunye nokunyuka kwamaxabiso
* Ityala kunye nokungalingani
* Abantu beCypherpunk njengabasombululi bengxaki
* Inkqubo elawulwa embindini vs engalawulwa embindini

Ukuba ixesha likhona, thatha ixesha kwi:

* Izifundo zetyala zokunyuka kwamaxabiso kwingingqi (eVenezuela, eZimbabwe, eTurkey)
* Imbali yeengxaki zoqoqosho kunye neempendulo zomgaqo-nkqubo
* Uphando olwandisiweyo lwabavulindlela bokhuseleko lwedijithali

##### Ukuba abafundi banengxaki
