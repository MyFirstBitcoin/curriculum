# 7 - Sebenzisa iBitcoin Kubomi Bemihla Ngemihla

Ixesha: Imizuzu engama-90

Ingcinga Ephambili: I-Lightning Network yenza ukuba iBitcoin isebenze ngakumbi kwiintlawulo zemihla ngemihla ngokuvumela iintengiselwano ezikhawulezayo, ezingabizi mali kakhulu ngelixa igcina iBitcoin njengesiseko.

#### Iinjongo Zokufunda

Ekupheleni kwesi sifundo, abafundi kufuneka bakwazi uku:

* Chaza ukuba yintoni i-Lightning Network kwaye kutheni yakhiwe phezu kweBitcoin.
* Thelekisa iintengiselwano ezise-chain kunye ne-Lightning ngokubhekiselele kwisantya, iindleko, kunye nokutshintshiselana ngokhuseleko.
* Yahlula phakathi kwe-Lightning wallets eziphethwe ngabanye (custodial) kunye nezoziphetheyo (self-custodial), kwaye uchaze ukuba kutheni kubalulekile ukuziphethela.
* Misela i-Lightning wallet kwaye chaza indima ye-seed phrase ekubuyiseni i-wallet.
* Bonisa indlela iintlawulo ze-Lightning ezihamba ngayo kwinethiwekhi, nokuba abasebenzisi ababini abanalo ijelo ngqo phakathi kwabo.
* Chonga iindlela ezisebenzayo zokusebenzisa iBitcoin ebomini bemihla ngemihla nge-Lightning, kubandakanya ikofu, iivenkile zokutya, iintlawulo zorhwebo, kunye nokusebenzisa imali kwindawo ohlala kuyo.
* Chaza indlela izixhobo ezifana ne-BTCPay Server, BTCMap, kunye ne-gift cards ezandisa ngayo ukusetyenziswa kweBitcoin ebomini bokwenyani.
* Chaza ukuba yintoni uqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin (circular economy) kwaye kutheni i-Lightning iyenza ibe yinyani ngakumbi.

##### 7.0 Intshayelelo, imizuzu esi-8

Qala ngokudibanisa esi sahluko nesidlulileyo:

* Ukuba iBitcoin isebenza kwi-chain, kutheni kufuneke omnye umaleko?
* Kwenzeka ntoni xa abantu befuna ukwenza iintlawulo ezininzi ezincinci ngokukhawuleza?
* Loluphi uhlobo lwenkqubo yokuhlawula olungasebenza ngcono kwikofu, iivenkile zokutya, okanye ukuhlawula umhlobo?

Cacisa ukuba esi sahluko sigxile kwiBitcoin yokusetyenziswa kwemihla ngemihla, ngakumbi xa isantya kunye neefizi eziphantsi zibalulekile. Yenza kucace ukuba i-Lightning yakhiwe phezu kweBitcoin, ayahlukanga kuyo.

##### 7.1 I-Lightning Network, imizuzu engama-25

**Yintoni i-Lightning Network**

Chaza ukuba i-Lightning Network yinkqubo yokuhlawula eyakhiwe phezu kweBitcoin evumela abasebenzisi ukuba bathumele kwaye bamkele i-bitcoin ngokukhawuleza nangexabiso eliphantsi. Isebenza ngokuhambisa iintlawulo ezininzi ezincinci ngaphandle kwe-blockchain engundoqo kwaye kuphela isiphumo sokugqibela sibuyiselwa kwi-chain kamva.

Indlela eluncedo yokuyichaza kukusebenzisa umzekelo we-café tab okhankanywe kwesi sahluko:

* endaweni yokuhlawula into nganye enye nganye kwi-chain
* amaqela amabini avula ijelo
* bahlaziya ibhalansi zabo xa besenza iintengiselwano
* kuphela ibhalansi yokugqibela ebhalwa kwi-blockchain xa bevala ijelo

Oko kwenza i-Lightning ikhawuleze kwaye ingabizi mali kakhulu kwiintlawulo ezincinci eziphindaphindiweyo. Cacisa kwakhona ukuba iintlawulo ze-Lightning zinokudlula kwinethiwekhi, ngoko abasebenzisi abafuni ijelo ngqo nomntu ngamnye abamhlawulayo.

**On-chain vs Lightning**

Ngoku yenza umahluko ube cacile kakhulu.

Iintengiselwano ze-On-chain

* zenzeka ngqo kwi-blockchain ye-Bitcoin
* ngokubanzi zicotha
* zixhomekeke ekufakweni nasekuqinisekisweni kwebhloko
* zisoloko zikhuselekile ngakumbi
* zinokubiza kakhulu kuxhomekeke kwiimali zokusebenza

Iintengiselwano ze-Lightning

* zenzeka kumaleko wesibini owakhiwe ngaphezulu kwe-Bitcoin
* zigqitywa ngokukhawuleza kakhulu
* ngokuqhelekileyo zibiza kancinci kakhulu
* ziluncedo kwiintlawulo ezincinci nezisoloko zisenziwa
* zineengxaki ezithile xa kuthelekiswa nokuhlala kwe-on-chain

Gcina ingongoma ephambili ilula: i-on-chain yomelele ngakumbi xa kufikwa ekuhlaleni kokugqibela, i-Lightning yomelele ngakumbi kwisantya kunye nokusetyenziswa kwemihla ngemihla okungabizi. Olu thelekiso luncedo kakhulu apha.

##### 7.2 Iindidi ezahlukeneyo zeeWallet ze-Lightning, 10 imizuzu

Cacisa ukuba i-wallet ye-Lightning isebenza njengendlela efanayo ne-wallet ye-Bitcoin, ifumana kwaye ithumele i-bitcoin, kodwa yenzelwe ukusetyenziswa kwi-Lightning Network. Emva koko chaza umahluko ophambili kwiincwadi zale sahluko:

* eyakho ngokupheleleyo: umsebenzisi ulawula izitshixo
* egcinwe ngomnye umntu: omnye umntu ulawula izitshixo

Cacisa umahluko ophambili:

* ii-wallet ezigcinwe ngomnye umntu zinokuziva zikhululekile kwaye zilula
* kodwa umsebenzisi uxhomekeke kwimvume nolawulo lomnye umntu
* ii-wallet ezilawulwa ngumsebenzisi zinika ubunini kunye nenkululeko engakumbi

Kwakhona qinisa ingcebiso yalesi sahluko yokukhetha ii-wallet ezivulekileyo (open-source), kuba izixhobo ezinjalo zingaphononongwa, zaphuculwa, kwaye ziqinisekiswe luluntu.

##### 7.3 Ukuseta iWallet ye-Bitcoin Lightning, 10 imizuzu

Khokela abafundi kwindlela esisiseko yokuseta:

* khuphela i-wallet ye-Lightning
* yenza i-wallet entsha
* bhala phantsi amagama okubuyisela (recovery phrase)
* qinisekisa amagama ngokulandelelana afanelekileyo
* yongeza ukhuseleko olongezelelweyo ukuba i-wallet iyakuvumela
* qalisa ukusebenzisa i-wallet

Yiba necaciso ngakumbi malunga ne-seed phrase:

* yiyo evumela umsebenzisi ukuba abuyise ukufikelela
* ukuba ilahlekile, ukufikelela kwiimali kunokulahleka
* ukuba omnye umntu uyifumana, angalawula imali

Le ndawo kufuneka igxininise kakhulu uxanduva kunye nokuphatha ngokhuseleko, njengoko kwenziwa kwicandelo le-on-chain.

##### 7.4 Ukuthumela nokufumana iintengiselwano ze-Lightning, 17 imizuzu

**Indlela iintengiselwano ze-Lightning ezisebenza ngayo kwindlela yokwenyani**

Sebenzisa umzekelo kaNoluthando, Jabu, no-Eva ukuchaza indlela yokudlulisela. Noluthando akadingi umjelo ngqo no-Eva. Intlawulo yakhe ingadlula kuJabu, odibanisa nenethiwekhi, kwaye isafikelela ku-Eva ngokukhuselekileyo.

Yenza ezi ngongoma zicace:

* Iintlawulo ze-Lightning zingadlula phakathi kwabalamli
* abalamli banceda ekudluliseni iintlawulo
* inkqubo yokudlulisela ayithethi ukuba abasebenzisi bathembele ebhankini okanye kumphathi wentlawulo ophakathi
* inethiwekhi isebenzisa i-cryptography ukuze intlawulo ifike kumamkeli ochanekileyo

Oku kunceda abafundi baqonde ukuba i-Lightning isasebenza phakathi kwabantu ababini, nangona iintlawulo zidlula kwinethiwekhi ebanzi. Ukuba kuyimfuneko, khankanya ukuba isahluko sikwachaza ukuba abaqhubi beenodi banokufumana imali yokusebenza kwaye bomeleze inethiwekhi ngokudlulisela iintlawulo.

**Ukuxhasa Imijelo kunye nokusebenzisa i-Lightning amaxesha amaninzi**

Cacisa umzekelo kaNoluntu ngakumbi:

* Noluntu uhambisa i-bitcoin esuka kwi-wallet yakhe ye-on-chain ukuya kwi-wallet yakhe ye-Lightning
* oku kuxhasa umjelo wentlawulo
* angakwazi ke ukwenza iintlawulo eziphindaphindiweyo ngaphandle kokuvula kwakhona inkqubo rhoqo ngexesha ngalinye
* xa isikhululo sivaliwe, ibhalansi yokugqibela ibuyiselwa kwi-blockchain

Cacisa umda obalulekileyo: imali evaliweyo kwisikhululo esisebenzayo isetyenziswa kwi-Lightning kwaye ayifumaneki ngokukhululekileyo ukuze isetyenziswe kwi-blockchain ngexesha elifanayo. Oku kunceda abafundi baqonde ukuba i-Lightning inamandla, kodwa inendlela eyahlukileyo yokwenza iintlawulo.

##### 7.5 Ukuthenga Ikofu kunye neGrosari ngeBitcoin, imizuzu engama-20

**Iimeko Zokusetyenziswa Kwemihla Ngemihla**

Guquka ukusuka kwindlela yokusebenza uye ebomini bokwenyani.

Chaza ukuba i-Lightning iluncedo ngokukodwa xa kusenziwa:

* ukuthenga ikofu
* iigrosari
* ukuthenga
* ukuhlawula abahlobo
* iintengiselwano ezincinci zemihla ngemihla

Umzekelo kaMina kwesi sahluko unceda ukubonisa ukuba kutheni i-Lightning ilungele ngakumbi kuneendlela zemveli zokuhlawula kwiimeko ezininzi: iyakhawuleza, iindleko ziphantsi, ayinamingcele, kwaye ifikeleleka nakubantu abangenaso isikhwama-mali ebhankini. Itheyibhile yokuthelekisa kunye nomzobo wokuhamba kwentlawulo zizixhobo ezinamandla zokufundisa apha, ngakumbi ekuboneni ukuba bangaphi abaphakathi abakhona kwiintlawulo zamakhadi zemveli.

**Izixhobo Zabarhwebi kunye Nokusebenzisa iBitcoin Ebomini Bokwenyani**

Ngoku chaza indlela amashishini kunye nabasebenzisi abanokwenza ngayo i-Lightning isebenze ebomini bemihla ngemihla.

Gubungela izixhobo ezintathu eziphambili okanye iindlela ezikwisahluko:

BTCPay Server

* umhlinzeki wentlawulo ovulelekileyo
* ivumela abarhwebi ukuba bamkele i-bitcoin ngqo
* akukho mntu ophakathi olawula imali
* iluncedo kwiintlawulo zeshishini ezikwi-intanethi nakwiindawo zokuthengisa

BTCMap

* inceda abasebenzisi bafumane abarhwebi noluntu olwamkela i-bitcoin
* ivumela abantu bakhangele kufutshane
* inokuhlaziywa luluntu

Amakhadi ezipho kunye neevawutsha

* izixhobo zethutyana zokusebenzisa i-bitcoin apho ukwamkelwa ngqo kungekabikho
* zinceda ukudibanisa umsantsa ngelixa ukwamkelwa kukhula

Le ndawo ibalulekile kuba ibonisa abafundi ukuba ukusetyenziswa kweBitcoin akusiyonto yethiyori kuphela. Sele zikhona izixhobo ezisebenzayo abantu abanokuzisebenzisa namhlanje.

**Uqoqosho Olujikelezayo kunye neBitcoin NjengeSixhobo Sokutshintshiselana**

Vala umxholo ophambili ngokuchaza ukuba uqoqosho olujikelezayo luluntu apho abathathi-nxaxheba bazama ukuthenga nokuthengisa omnye komnye kangangoko kunokwenzeka. Xa kusetyenziswa kwiBitcoin, oku kuthetha ukuba abarhwebi, abasebenzi, kunye nabasebenzisi bakhetha ukwenza iintengiselwano ngebitcoin kwaye baxhase omnye nomnye kwezoqoqosho.

Cacisa ukuba kutheni i-Lightning ibalulekile apha:

* iintlawulo zikhawuleza phantse kwangoko
* iindleko ziphantsi
* iintlawulo ezincinci ziba lula
* urhwebo lwasekuhlaleni luba lula ukulugcina

Ungakhankanya ukuba isahluko sibhekisa kwimizekelo efana neArnhem neBitcoin Beach, ukubonisa ukuba uqoqosho olujikelezayo aluyonto eqikelelwayo. Sele lukhona kwaye luyaqhubeka nokukhula. Umgca wexesha obonakalayo uluncedo kakhulu apha

#### Amanqaku oTitshala

Gcina umgca ophambili wokufundisa ucocekile: iBitcoin linqanaba elisisiseko, i-Lightning inceda ukwenza iintlawulo zemihla ngemihla zikhawuleze kwaye zibe sezantsi iindleko.

Esi sahluko kufuneka sizive sisebenziseka kwaye sicace, hayi sobuchwephesha kakhulu.

Nika ingqwalasela ekuqondeni kunokungena nzulu kwiindlela zokusebenza kwezikhululo.

Amanqaku anamandla okufanele anikwe ingqwalasela, ukuba ixesha lifutshane, ngala:

* yintoni i-Lightning
* ukhetho phakathi kokusebenzisa on-chain kunye ne-Lightning
* ulawulo lwe-wallet kunye nendlela yokumisela
* iintlawulo kwihlabathi lokwenyani
* uqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin

Ezona mifanekiso zinceda kakhulu kweli sahluko zezi:

* uthelekiso phakathi kwe-on-chain kunye ne-Lightning
* umaahluko phakathi kweentlobo ze-wallet
* umzekelo wokuhanjiswa kwentlawulo phakathi kukaSipho, uThandi, noLindiwe
* itheyibhile yokuthelekisa kunye nomzobo womthamo
* umzobo wendlela yemveli yokuhlawula
* umgca wexesha loqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin

##### Oko Kukubonakala Kakuhle

* Kubalulekile ukuqala ngengxaki ethi "iBitcoin ithatha imizuzu eli-10 kwaye ibiza i-R40," uchaze i-Lightning njengendlela ekhawulezayo phezu kweBitcoin, usebenzise imizekelo yokwenyani evela kubathengisi kunye neendlela zokuthumela imali, kwaye wenze imithi yokuthatha izigqibo xa kusetyenziswa i-on-chain okanye i-Lightning.
* Ootitshala kufuneka babe nendlela yokusebenza ngokwenene malunga nento i-Lightning eyisombululayo, babelane ngamabali avela kwindawo apho iBitcoin isetyenziswa khona, bacacise ngokuthe ngqo ngezinto ezifunekayo ukuba zitshintshwe, kwaye bahlale benyanisekile malunga nokwamkelwa kwayo ngelixa bonwabile ngamathuba.
* Abafundi babona iBitcoin isebenza ngokwenene kwiintlawulo zokwenyani kwiindawo zokwenyani, baqonde ukuba isantya kunye neendleko zibalulekile kwiintlawulo, bacinge ngoqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin apho iBitcoin ihlala kwindawo, baqonde ukuba i-Lightning ≠ Bitcoin (izixhobo ezahlukeneyo ngeenjongo ezahlukeneyo), kwaye baqalise umdla kwiinkqubo zoqoqosho ezakhiwe kwiintlawulo zeBitcoin.
* Iziphumo zokufunda ziya kufezeka ukuba abafundi banokuchaza i-Lightning Network njengomaleko ophezulu kweBitcoin, baqonde indlela yokusebenza kweendlela zokuhlawula kunye nendlela yokuhanjiswa, babone imizekelo yokwenyani yokusebenzisa i-Lightning, bathelekise i-on-chain kunye ne-Lightning kwiimeko ezahlukeneyo, baqonde umxholo woqoqosho olujikeleze iBitcoin, kwaye baqaphele izinto ezithile ezifunekayo ukuba zitshintshwe kwindlela nganye.
